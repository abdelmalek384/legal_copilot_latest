**Potential conflict/precision checks:** the secondary report uses the phrase “35% فأكثر” for expected permanent disability and “حروق من الدرجة الثالثة أو أكبر”. Do not silently standardize these expressions. Preserve source wording and compare Arabic tokens against the official text. The title says “نماذج وإحصائيات”; ensure attached forms/schedules are included, not only the prose articles.

**Additional cross-source issue identified 2026-10-30 — Article 260 vs Decision 248 reporting triggers:** The official parent-law Article 260 excerpt states 24-hour notification for each serious accident occurring at the establishment and for the appearance of occupational-disease symptoms. The secondary summaries of Decision 248 report 24 hours for an accident inside the establishment, 48 hours for an accident outside its boundaries, and occupational-disease notification upon diagnosis/confirmation by an approved medical report. This is not yet a confirmed legal conflict: the parent-law clause expressly refers to accidents occurring at the establishment, while the decision summary appears to cover off-site events too. The disease trigger also needs exact comparison. Obtain Decision 248's official Article 4 and forms; have counsel determine whether the additional scope/timing is authorized and how it interacts with Article 260. **Do not answer that off-site accidents may wait 48 hours, or that occupational-disease notice can wait for diagnosis, from secondary text alone.** Secondary references: Al-Youm Al-Sabea (2026-10-28), https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/10/28/حماية-أرواح-العمال-أولوية-وزارة-العمل-تحدد-مهلة-24-ساعة/7590406; EgyIn (2026-10-19), https://www.egyin.com/article/154732/.

### New official Ministry clarification — Decision 249 (published 2026-10-27)

Official source: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/بيان-توضيحي-هام-بشأن-القرار-الوزاري-رقم-249-لسنة-2026-الخاص-بالسلامة-والصحة-المهنية-وزارة-العمل-لن-ي-ضار-أي-عامل-يعمل-بمهنة-أخصائي-السلامة-والصحة-المهنية-ولا-مساس-بالمراكز-القانونية-المكتسبة-وأهمية-المهنة-تستوجب-منتهى-الدقة-في-اختيار-شاغليها/

The Ministry's clarification is important evidence about administrative interpretation, but it is not itself a substitute for the decision or a later binding circular. It says that current OSH specialists are not to be harmed or excluded solely due to the decision; previously recognized qualifications and acquired legal positions are to be respected; other university graduates with specialized postgraduate diplomas, extended experience or recognized certificates may be considered under level-specific rules; and the Ministry's central administration was directed to issue periodic circulars to standardize implementation and transitional arrangements.

Add these checks:
- **249-H — incumbent workers/acquired positions:** locate the exact decision transitional clauses and verify the Ministry's clarification does not conflict with the operative text. Record the clarification as an official administrative interpretation, not as a decision article.
- **249-I — future circulars:** search for the promised periodic circulars and any implementation/regularization guidance. Until located, the detail of transition rules remains open; do not infer that all current workers are automatically exempt from every qualification.
- **249-J — specialist classification:** distinguish the decision's required establishment-level staffing rules from professional licensing/qualification categories. Do not conflate “أخصائي” with “استشاري” without textual support.

## Cross-instrument and currentness checks

1. Verify each decision’s exact issue, publication date and commencement. The secondary pages say supplement to issue 225 (A), published 2026-10-12; Decision 250’s Article 2 is reported to take effect the following day. Confirm the same rule in the official text.
2. Search for correction notices, subsequent amendments, suspension or replacement decisions after 2026-10-12. Search absence alone does not prove none exist.
3. Cross-reference each decision to the enabling Labour Law 14/2025 articles, but do not assume that the decision can add criminal penalties or override other OSH legislation.
4. Extract all annexes, forms, thresholds and numerical tables as separate records. Keep exact Arabic, units, conditions, exceptions, source pinpoint and transcription status.
5. Check all licensing fees and deadlines with particular care; these have direct compliance and financial consequences.
6. Ask qualified Egyptian labour/OSH counsel to approve each instrument and each high-impact rule independently.

## High-value article pinpoints from the transcription

- **Article 2 (secondary page lines 134–164):** employers using **10 or more workers** must prepare workplace rules-of-work regulations; the required subjects include hiring, training, contracts, worker files, performance evaluation, promotion, transfers, wage protection, working time/rest/overtime, leave, safety, termination, investigations and discipline.
- **Article 7 (lines 185–199):** the submission package includes **three copies** of the proposed workplace regulation, signed by the responsible manager and stamped on every page.
- **Article 8 (lines 203–208):** the Labour Directorate sends a copy to the competent trade-union organization/worker representative within **3 working days** after receipt; the union has **15 days** after receipt to provide its written opinion, otherwise it is deemed to have approved the draft.
- **Article 9 (lines 210–212):** where the Directorate objects in writing, the establishment must address the objections within **5 working days** at most; if the Directorate neither approves nor objects within **30 days** after receiving the regulation, it is treated as effective from the following day.
- **Article 21 (lines 277–280):** a probation period, if used, must be stated in the employment contract, may not exceed **3 months**, and may not be imposed more than once by the same employer.
- **Article 66:** the decision takes effect the day after publication; exact official Gazette page/visual pinpoint remains pending.

## Interaction with Law 14/2025 Article 137

Law Article 137 already has its own deadlines for employers to prepare regulations and for the union/administrative authority to respond. Decision 162/2026 adds procedural details for the submission/ratification workflow. Do not mechanically merge the deadlines without checking the precise triggering event and whether the provision is a distinct step in the workflow. In particular, the Article 137 60-day preparation period and the Decision 162 Articles 7–9 review steps may run from different events.

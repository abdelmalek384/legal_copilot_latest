## Important caution about exact rules

Some secondary reporting provides specific numbers (for example, incident reporting deadlines, workforce thresholds, retention periods, consultant qualification/experience, licensing fees and processing times). These are potentially high-impact compliance rules. They must **not** be imported as verified statutory rules based only on news articles or partial excerpts. Keep any extracted facts in a separate secondary-source comparison layer with fields for the exact quote, URL, publication date, article number if shown, and a primary-source-match status of `not_checked`.

The Ministry's announcement is a strong official discovery source for the existence and broad subject of the five decisions, but it is not a replacement for the decision texts. The Gazette catalogue's metadata likewise does not establish the provisions' full wording.

## High-value candidate facts visible in the image set

These are discovery notes only. All thresholds, hours, fees, test percentages, dates and qualifications require transcription from the original Gazette before legal use:

1. **Articles 1–3:** define the training programmes, training provider, licensed trainer and competent committees; state the decision's purpose and scope.
2. **Articles 4–5:** prohibit training activity without a Ministry licence and distinguish in-person/electronic training, with electronic training subject to the specified controls.
3. **Articles 6–9:** identify categories of eligible training providers, set provider/activity conditions, regulate establishment-run training and require compliance with approved programmes.
4. **Articles 10–13:** establish the licensing committee and set the application/decision workflow.
5. **Articles 14–15:** set detailed provider documentation and training-centre facility requirements. The page images include candidate area/room-space requirements; transcribe every measurement and exception exactly.
6. **Articles 16–20:** cover inspection/licensing fees, licence term/renewal, provider duties, pre-training conditions and suspension/revocation.
7. **Articles 21–23:** provide oversight/administrative controls and establish a committee for programme standards/curricula.
8. **Articles 24–27:** regulate trainer qualifications, trainer licensing, term/renewal and revocation.
9. **Articles 28–30:** set programme admission requirements, approved training categories/hours and annual training plans.
10. **Articles 31–35:** govern tests, assessment, attendance, training quality, records and certificates.
11. **Article 36:** states publication and commencement. Confirm the exact effective date from the original Gazette.
12. **Article 29 tables:** contain basic, advanced, specific and specialist training categories with hours. These are safety-critical and must be transcribed cell-by-cell, including footnotes and role categories.

## Workflow caution

Decision 162/2026 is not simply a replacement for Article 137 of Law 14/2025. The statute's 60-day preparation period and Decision 162's submission, union-review, objection-correction, and approval periods have different triggers and stages. Implement a state machine only after a lawyer confirms the exact sequence, start events, holiday/day-count rules, and effects of incomplete submissions.

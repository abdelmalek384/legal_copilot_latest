# Egyptian official legal documents — Article 141 source-use route

**Research date:** 2026-10-30  
**Primary legal reference:** WIPO Lex, Egyptian Law No. 82/2002 on the Protection of Intellectual Property Rights, consolidated Arabic text amended through Law No. 178/2020: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/ar/legislation/details/22066  
**Relevant provision:** Book Three, Article 141  
**Supporting English text (2002 version, reference only):** https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/1301

## Finding

The WIPO Lex record identifies the Arabic consolidated version as amended through Law No. 178/2020 and states that its text is current through 5 September 2020. The reproduced Article 141 says copyright protection does not cover official documents, regardless of source or target language, including laws, regulations, resolutions and decisions, international conventions, court decisions, arbitral awards and decisions of administrative committees with judicial competence.

Article 141 also says that collections of those documents may enjoy protection if the selection is creative by virtue of arrangement or another personal effort deserving protection. Article 138(6) separately defines derivative works to include creative compilations/databases whose arrangement and selection make them created works.

## Application to the Egyptian legal corpus

1. **Underlying official legal text:** There is a strong statutory basis to treat the text of an Egyptian law or ministerial decision, as an official document, as outside copyright protection under Article 141(1). This is a legal research lead supported by the WIPO Lex consolidated text; it is not a final opinion on every proposed use.
2. **Official Gazette scan/PDF:** Do not assume that the scan file, typography, annotations, editorial additions, or a publisher's compiled package can be copied wholesale merely because the underlying decision text is an official document. Prefer extracting the underlying official text and retaining a link to the official source; avoid redistributing the scan unless its status is separately reviewed.
3. **Third-party transcriptions and databases:** A third-party site's creative selection, arrangement, annotations, editorial corrections, translations, or database may have separate protection. Article 141's official-document exclusion is not blanket clearance to copy the site's whole page, database, images, or compilation.
4. **Access controls and terms:** Copyright status and access permission are separate questions. The Amiri Press DSpace catalogue's Download control routes to a request-a-copy form. This project must not bypass that control or automate restricted retrieval. Use ordinary publicly accessible official text routes where available.
5. **Attribution and provenance:** Even where official text itself is not protected by copyright, the corpus must retain issuing authority, instrument number/date, Gazette issue/date/page, source URL, retrieval timestamp, actual-file hash when original bytes are obtained, and the exact text/page evidence used.

## Practical route that avoids permission outreach

- Prefer public official sources: Amiri Press searchable legal text, Ministry of Labour public decision/announcement pages, official ministry or government publications, and openly accessible Official Gazette pages.
- For a publicly accessible official text, capture only the legal document text and legally relevant metadata, not a third-party site's surrounding editorial material or creative compilation.
- Keep the source-use status as `provisional_official_document_exclusion_candidate` until qualified Egyptian IP/labour counsel confirms the intended commercial ingestion, storage, transformation, and display model.
- Where only a third-party transcription is accessible, keep it as discovery-only evidence. Do not treat Article 141 as permission to reproduce the third-party page or as proof that its transcription is accurate.

## Currentness limitation and follow-up search — 2026-10-30

The WIPO Lex Arabic consolidated record expressly says it incorporates amendments through **Law 178/2020**, effective 5 September 2020; it is not a consolidation certified current through 30 October 2026. The official GAFI-hosted Arabic PDF of Law 82/2002 also reproduces Article 141, but the indexed file is the original law text rather than proof of a complete 2026 consolidation.

Targeted searches for amendments to Law 82/2002 / Article 141 during 2021–2026 did not identify a later amendment to Article 141. A 10 January 2026 State Information Service report describes a broader IP-system overhaul and the Egyptian IP Authority; that policy/institutional development is **not itself proof that Article 141 was amended**. Search absence is not proof of non-existence.

Accordingly, record the currentness state as:
- Article 141 wording supported by the WIPO consolidated version through 2020 and the original government-hosted 2002 text.
- No later Article 141 amendment located in this targeted search.
- Full Official Gazette amendment/corrigendum search through 2026 and qualified counsel confirmation remain outstanding.
- Do not label the Article 141 commercial-use route “verified current” or treat it as a blanket commercial licence.

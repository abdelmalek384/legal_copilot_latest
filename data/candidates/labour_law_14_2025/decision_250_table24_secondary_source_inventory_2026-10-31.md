### Article52 — chemical-control obligations visible in the transcription

The secondary transcription's Article52 states that establishments must control chemical hazards from production, use, handling, transport, storage, treatment and disposal, without exceeding Table24 limits. Its visible clauses include:
1. Maintain an updated inventory of all chemicals/hazardous substances, quantities and locations, available to relevant workers, using record form 4.
2. Keep hazardous inventories within Table23 threshold quantities; exceeding a threshold triggers major-hazard establishment controls.
3. Store chemicals/waste according to chemical/physical properties, compatibility, container integrity, fire/explosion prevention and safe loading/unloading.
4. Label all containers with chemical/trade names, manufacturer details, hazard classification, acute/chronic warnings, precautions, first aid/emergency instructions and standard symbols.
5. Provide and make SDS available to relevant workers.
6. Provide ventilation, emission controls, separation of incompatible substances, fire protection and containment.
7. Assess chemical risks and monitor airborne concentrations; apply substitution, safer technology, engineering controls/local exhaust, safe work procedures, health-surveillance programmes, PPE and warning signs.
8. Maintain safe storage practices and periodic measurement of emissions/contaminants.
9. Prohibit mixing chemicals without a prior risk assessment for heat, toxic gas, explosive reaction or spontaneous ignition.
10. Select respirators/filters by contaminant type and concentration; use full-face protection where eyes/mucous membranes need protection; apply the specified limits and checks to supplied-air/SCBA equipment.
11. Inspect respiratory equipment before use, train workers in fit/use/removal and replace filters under manufacturer instructions or when warning signs occur.

The list above is a paraphrased locator aid, not a replacement for the full Article52 wording. Exact clause numbering/wording and all later clauses must be checked against the Gazette original.

### Table 23 / Table 24 must remain distinct

- **Table23:** threshold quantity of a hazardous substance that can classify a site as a major-hazard establishment; units are mass (kg and lb) and CAS/reference identity.
- **Table24:** occupational exposure threshold limit values for hazardous chemicals; includes time-weighted average (TWA), short-term exposure limit (STEL), ceiling limit (C), units and notations.
- Do not use a Table23 stock threshold as a worker-exposure limit, or a Table24 exposure limit as a major-accident inventory threshold.

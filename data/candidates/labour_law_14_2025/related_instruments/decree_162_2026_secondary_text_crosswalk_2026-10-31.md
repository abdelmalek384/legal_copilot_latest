## 2. Provision-level crosswalk to Labour Law 14/2025

This maps subjects and apparent operational relationships; it does not conclude that the decree amends or overrides the statute.

### Interpretive boundaries

1. Articles 38–41 appear to regulate what an establishment's internal work rules must contain. They do not, on this secondary text alone, amend statutory limits or premiums in Law Articles 117–123 or leave entitlements in Articles 124–129.
2. Article 2's ten-worker threshold is consistent with Law Article 137. Exact commencement/transition deadlines for each establishment require separate verification.
3. The Article 46 / Article 141 investigation-period wording is a reconciliation question, not proof of conflict. The statute remains controlling unless a valid legal interpretation establishes otherwise.
4. Article 47 must not be read to remove statutory procedural safeguards, including the union's right to designate a representative to attend an investigation.
5. The transcription contains possible OCR/typographic irregularities. No article text should be copied into the authoritative legal corpus from this note.

## 2. Provision-level crosswalk to Labour Law 14/2025

This is a mapping of subjects and apparent operational relationships, not a conclusion that the decree amends or overrides the statute.

Confirm the Gazette publication event and exact date from the original issue before recording an effective date. |

### Important interpretive boundaries

1. Articles 38–41 appear to regulate what an establishment's internal work rules must contain. They do not, on the secondary text alone, amend the statutory limits or premiums in Labour Law Articles 117–123 or the leave entitlements in Articles 124–129.
2. Article 2's ten-worker threshold is consistent with the threshold in Labour Law Article 137, but the exact commencement/transition deadlines for each establishment must be checked against the statute and the original decree.
3. The Article 46 / Article 141 investigation-period wording is a potential reconciliation question, not proof of conflict. The statute remains controlling unless a valid legal interpretation establishes otherwise.
4. The Article 47 investigator provisions must not be read to remove any statutory procedural safeguard, including the union's right to designate a representative to attend an investigation.
5. The secondary transcription includes a number of OCR/typographic irregularities. No article text should be copied into the authoritative legal corpus from this note.

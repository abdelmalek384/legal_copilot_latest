## 2. Legal relationship and retrieval controls

The secondary transcription's preamble cites Labour Law No. 14/2025. The decision's stated subject is the general rules for workplace work-regulations. Its Article 2 requires employers using ten or more workers to prepare a work-regulations document, and the decision then describes required content, approval, workplace rules, investigation, sanctions, termination, and later updates.

This is a related implementing instrument, not evidence that the text of Labour Law No. 14/2025 itself has been amended. Do not represent any provision of this decision as a formal amendment to the statute without a separately verified legal basis.

## 4. High-priority legal comparison work

1. **Article 2 threshold and scope:** confirm the “10 workers or more” trigger, counting method, and whether the law or other instruments specify exceptions.
2. **Articles 7–9 approval workflow:** validate the three-working-day referral, 15-day union opinion period, five-working-day correction period, and 30-day no-action rule. Confirm how each period is calculated and when the clock begins.
3. **Articles 14 and 19 preservation of better terms:** distinguish statutory minimum rights, contractual benefits, established workplace practice and the rule on more favourable provisions.
4. **Articles 38–41 working time and leave:** build a separate clause-by-clause comparison against the exact Labour Law provisions and every relevant operative ministerial decision. No entitlement calculator should rely on the secondary transcription.
5. **Articles 46–48 investigation and dismissal:** independently validate the seven-day, three-month and 30-day periods, the five-day deduction limits, written investigation rule and labour-court dismissal requirement.
6. **Articles 60–63 termination:** compare each termination route with the statute and relevant decisions; preserve the distinctions between express resignation, deemed resignation, notice and disciplinary dismissal.
7. **Article 66 commencement:** inspect the Gazette page itself; retain 14 August 2026 as candidate-only until verified.

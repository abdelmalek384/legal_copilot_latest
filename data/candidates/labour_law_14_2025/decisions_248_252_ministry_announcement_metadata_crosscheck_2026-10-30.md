## What is corroborated

The Ministry of Labour announcement dated 20 October 2026 states that the Minister issued the five executive decisions numbered 248, 249, 250, 251 and 252 of 2026 as part of implementation of Labour Law No. 14 of 2025 and occupational safety and health protections.

The Amiri Press DSpace catalogue record independently identifies the same five decisions in *Al-Waqa’i al-Misriya*, Supplement No. 225 (A), issued 12 October 2026, with the Ministry of Labour as contributor and Amiri Press as publisher.

The catalogue abstract supplies these instrument subjects:

| Decision | Catalogue-described subject |
|---|---|
| 248/2026 | Forms and statistics for ordinary, chronic and occupational diseases, serious accidents and injuries |
| 249/2026 | Establishments/branches required to establish occupational safety and health functional units and relevant committees |
| 250/2026 | Workplace safety rules, standards, safety limits, conditions and precautions to prevent risks |
| 251/2026 | Conditions, rules and procedures for licensing occupational safety and health expertise and consultancy |
| 252/2026 | Rules for licensing and training systems in occupational safety and health |

The Ministry announcement additionally describes subject areas across the package, including noise, heat, radiation, excavation, demolition, scaffolding and cranes. It does not map every subject to a specific decision article or provide the complete operative wording.

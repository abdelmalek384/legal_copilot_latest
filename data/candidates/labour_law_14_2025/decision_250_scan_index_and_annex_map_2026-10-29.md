## Numeric candidate leads from inspected pages

- Definition of noise: 20–20,000 Hz appears in the definition on p. 51. This is a definition of audible-frequency range, not a workplace permissible exposure limit.
- Definition of cold stress references a body-temperature fall below 36°C; do not treat this as a standalone workplace exposure threshold.
- UGR definition appears on p. 51; the scan references a later table for values by work type.
- The heat-stress chapter refers to tables 1–3 in the decision's third annex; exact values must be transcribed from those tables.
- Cold-stress chapter refers to tables 4–6 in the third annex; exact values must be transcribed.
- Noise chapter refers to tables 7–8; mechanical vibration refers to tables 9–10; illumination refers to table 11; radiation chapter refers to table 12. This table numbering is visible in the page-image text and is a concrete guide for targeted extraction.

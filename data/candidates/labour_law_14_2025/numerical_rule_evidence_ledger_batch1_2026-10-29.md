## High-risk ambiguity / exception queue

1. **Hours:** Art. 117 actual work hours, Art. 119 daily presence span, and Art. 121 exceptional total-presence cap are different measures. Never merge them into a single “maximum hours” field.
2. **Leave:** Art. 124 base leave entitlements and the extra 7 days depend on different criteria. The list of qualifying hazardous jobs/remote areas is determined by a separate instrument and remains a separate verification task.
3. **Wage percentages:** Arts. 113–114, 121, and 129 use different wage bases and contexts. Store each percentage with its base, triggering condition, and exception.
4. **Discipline and disputes:** Arts. 143, 145–149 have different caps and deadlines with distinct triggering events. A deadline calculator must identify the exact event and article before calculating.
5. **Termination:** Arts. 154–166 distinguish fixed-term from indefinite-term contracts, notice, compensation, and absence-related resignation. Do not combine these into a generic termination rule.

## Method and limitations

The uploaded Gazette PDF was treated as the comparison reference at the user's direction. The visual comparison used rendered page images; automated OCR/fuzzy matching was not accepted as proof. The local comparison checklist records per-row notes and printed-page references.

This result establishes only that the 120 quantitative checklist entries were compared and no numerical discrepancy was recorded. It does not establish:
- verbatim identity or completeness of all 311 JSONL rows;
- correct paragraph boundaries, punctuation, headings, omissions, or cross-references throughout the statute;
- authenticity beyond the user's supplied reference characterization;
- legal currentness, amendments, corrigenda, repeal/supersession, or operative effect;
- rights to store, index, embed, display, redistribute, or commercially reuse the text;
- Egyptian lawyer review or approval.

Keep every JSONL row's `verified=0` unchanged. Do not infer legal status from incoming `in_force=1` values. The Gazette publication date (3 May 2025) is not itself the general commencement date. No corpus activation or search-eligibility change follows from this comparison.

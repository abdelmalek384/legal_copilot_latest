## Key operative provisions located in the official publisher's searchable text

- **Article 1:** allows specialized subcommittees and requires balanced representation of the three labour-market parties.
- **Article 2:** sets the National Council for Wages' competencies, including setting national minimum wages, reviewing minimum wages at least annually or when needed, setting the annual periodic-increment minimum at **3% of the insurance wage**, considering employer applications to reduce or exempt payment of the annual increment, and notifying relevant ministries of decisions.
- **Article 2(4):** the Council decides applications to reduce/exempt annual-increment payment within **30 days** from referral to the Council.
- **Article 3:** the Council meets at least once every **six months**, or when needed; quorum is a majority and decisions are by majority of members present.
- **Articles 4–5:** provide for a technical secretariat and its functions; the secretariat meets at least once every **three months**, and its chair submits a **semiannual** report to the Council chair.
- **Article 6:** Council chair sets attendance allowances within legal rules and with Ministry of Finance approval.
- **Article 7:** repeals Prime Minister's Decree 2659/2020 and conflicting provisions.
- **Article 8:** says the decree is published in the Official Gazette and takes effect on the day after publication. The exact printed page and publication artifact should be checked before operational date calculation.

## Relation to Law 14/2025

- Law Article 102 provides for a Prime Minister's decision defining the Council's work system and other competencies within six months from the law's commencement. Law 14/2025's general commencement candidate is 2025-09-01; Decree 3165 was issued 2026-10-12. This apparent timing gap is a legal issue to refer to qualified Egyptian counsel. Do not infer invalidity, retroactive effect, or a remedy from the dates alone.
- Law Article 103 states that the Council meets at least every six months. Decree Article 3 provides matching cadence.
- Law Article 106 contains a 30-day challenge period for specified Council decisions. The exact trigger depends on the case; do not calculate it without identifying whether the relevant date is the increment due date or notification of the decision.
- Decree 3165 expressly repeals Decree 2659/2020; it is not thereby an amendment to Law 14/2025. Represent the relationship as implements/replaces prior decree, not statutory amendment.

# Decisions 251/252 — candidate fee cross-check against Labour Law 14/2025

**Checked:** 2026-10-30  
**Parent statute source:** ETA-hosted Official Gazette PDF for Labour Law 14/2025, PDF page indices 96–97 (printed Gazette pages 98–99), Articles 261–262.  
**Decision candidate sources:** Almasdar watermarked Gazette-page reproductions; candidate JSON files named below.  
**Classification:** arithmetic/scope cross-check only; not official decision verification, fee approval or legal opinion.

## Parent-law ceilings

- Article 261: the licence fee for the training systems described in that article must not exceed **EGP 50,000**.
- Article 262: the licence fee for occupational safety and health expertise/consultancy must not exceed **EGP 50,000**.

These are ceilings in the parent statute. They do not establish the amount charged by the implementing decisions or the legal character of every administrative charge.

## What this cross-check does and does not establish

**It establishes only:**
- The cited parent-law articles contain EGP 50,000 statutory ceilings for the licence categories described in Articles 261 and 262.
- The amounts in the candidate transcription, when compared as numbers, are below EGP 50,000 for the listed Decision 251/252 licence candidates.
- The Decision 252 non-licence charges require separate classification rather than automatic inclusion under the cap.

**It does not establish:**
- That the candidate fee amounts are correct or verbatim.
- That the categories, exemptions, renewals, disability reductions or fee triggers are correctly understood.
- That the decisions' provisions are current, valid, unamended or enforceable.
- That every charge in Decision 252 is subject to Article 261's cap.
- That either decision is approved for authoritative retrieval.

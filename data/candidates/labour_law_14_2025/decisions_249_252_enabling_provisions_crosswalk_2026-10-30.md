## Purpose and limits

This is a cross-reference between express ministerial-decision delegations in the official searchable text of Labour Law No. 14/2025 and the catalogue-described subjects of Ministry of Labour Decisions 249–252/2026. It helps prioritize legal review and detect scope mismatches. It does not reproduce the decisions' operative clauses and does not prove that the later decisions were made entirely within the delegation, remain in force, or have been correctly transcribed.

## Decision 248 — unresolved statutory basis

The Gazette catalogue identifies Decision 248/2026 as concerning forms and statistics for ordinary, chronic and occupational diseases, serious accidents and injuries. The Ministry's announcement confirms that broad subject description. The currently captured searchable-text excerpt does not establish the exact enabling article or articles for Decision 248. Do not assign an article number by inference. Locate the full law text around its occupational injury/notification provisions and compare the decision's preamble and operative articles once the actual decision text is available.

## Statutory numerical caps to verify against the decision text

The searchable Law 14/2025 text states:
- Article 261: the licence fee for the training systems described there must not exceed EGP 50,000.
- Article 262: the licence fee for occupational safety and health expertise/consultancy must not exceed EGP 50,000.

These are statutory ceilings in the law text, not a claim about the fees actually charged under Decisions 251 or 252. The decision's exact fee schedule, exemptions and any later amendment must be read from its operative text.

## Test and attendance thresholds visible on printed Gazette pages 395–396

The table on p.395 (image URL https://www.almasdar.com/WebP/libfiles/46/4/92.webp) contains separate theoretical/practical test percentages by trainee programme. Several cells are visible but require a second image-quality/official-file pass before they are stored as legal rules. The following should be recorded as candidate values only:

- OSH specialist: theoretical pass threshold appears 60%; practical threshold appears 70%.
- OSH supervisor: theoretical and practical thresholds appear 85% each.
- OSH technician: theoretical and practical thresholds appear 85% each.
- OSH committee members: theoretical threshold appears 100%; practical cell is shown as not applicable.
- Awareness/specialized programmes: visible thresholds appear to differ by programme type; do not generalize the same percentage to all programmes.
- Other programmes: table shows a different theoretical/practical pair from specialist/supervisor/technician; confirm exact row labels and cells before promotion.

Article 33 on p.396 (image URL https://www.almasdar.com/WebP/libfiles/46/4/93.webp) appears to state:
- trainee is barred from the exam if absence exceeds 20% of total theoretical lectures and practical training;
- trainee is considered to have passed the training programme if total evaluation score is at least 65%, with attendance not less than 80% of total programme duration;
- a trainee may repeat an exam at most twice, at the end of a later training programme, on payment of the stated fee (the amount must be transcribed from the original text before use);
- training records and evaluation materials are retained for at least five years after programme completion.

These are high-impact numeric rules; treat as provisional scan transcription pending official PDF comparison.

# Decision 294/2025 — paid-holiday list evidence and remaining verification

**Prepared:** 2026-10-29  
**Status:** Two holiday items are cross-checked through official Ministry announcements; the complete decision and its full list are not yet reviewed from the Official Gazette.

## Sources

- **Official Gazette archive metadata:** Egyptian Proceedings supplementary issue 291 (supplement), dated 2025-12-25, lists Labour Ministry Decision 294/2025 on holidays, feasts and occasions treated as fully paid leave: https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/37add437-802d-4848-8a24-8e064db22fcc.
- **Official Ministry announcement dated 2026-01-05:** says Decision 294/2025 Article 1 item 5 includes Christmas Day (7 January): https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-الأربعاء-المقبل-إجازة-مدفوعة-الأجر-للعاملين-بالقطاع-الخاص-بمناسبة-عيد-الميلاد-المجيد-جبران-وحدة-المصريين-والعمل-المشترك-أساس-قوة-الوطن/.
- **Official Ministry announcement dated 2026-10-01:** says Decision 294/2025 Article 1 item 12 includes Armed Forces Day (6 October): https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/لتوحيد-مواعيد-الإجازات-الرسمية-في-قطاعات-الدولة-المختلفة-وزير-العمل-الخميس-المقبل-إجازة-مدفوعة-الأجر-للعاملين-بالقطاع-الخاص-بمناسبة-ذكرى-نصر-أكتوبر-المجيد/.

## Supported statements

1. Decision 294/2025 exists and is indexed in Egyptian Proceedings supplementary issue 291 dated 2025-12-25.
2. The Ministry's 2026-01-05 announcement identifies 7 January (Christmas Day) as item 5 in Article 1.
3. The Ministry's 2026-10-01 announcement identifies 6 October (Armed Forces Day) as item 12 in Article 1.
4. Law 14/2025 Article 129 provides the general fully-paid-holiday right by reference to the minister's designated list and provides double wage or an alternative day at the worker's written request where work is required on such a day.

## Not established yet

- The complete set of holidays and occasions in Decision 294/2025, including any items not referenced in the two Ministry announcements.
- The exact full decision text, Gazette printed page numbers, source PDF SHA-256, and any later amendment/correction.
- Whether later official circulars move a holiday date for a specific year. A one-year government announcement should not be treated as permanently changing the underlying designated holiday list.

## Safe retrieval behavior

Until the complete list is reviewed, a question such as “Is date X a statutory paid holiday for this worker?” must not be answered by inferring from the two examples above. Retrieve the full Decision 294/2025 and any date-specific official circular, cite the source and date, and distinguish the recurring holiday designation from a one-year substitute date. Keep the holiday list non-authoritative until primary-source and lawyer review pass.

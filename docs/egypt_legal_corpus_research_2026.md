# Egyptian Legal Corpus: Platform and Source Research (2026-10-23)

This is a research and collection manifest for Legal Copilot. It is **not** a downloaded legal corpus and does not certify the accuracy of any provider's marketing claims. Counts and features below are claims shown by the named websites when checked; verify directly with providers before purchasing, exporting, or ingesting data.

## Executive recommendation

Build a broad legal collection from lawful primary sources, and use specialist AI platforms to discover issues, citations and missing materials. Do not ingest platform-generated answers as if they were authoritative legal texts. Before importing provider data, obtain written confirmation of bulk-export, storage, commercial reuse and AI-processing rights.

### Suggested vendor due-diligence email

Subject: Egyptian legal database coverage, source provenance and data export

Hello,

I am evaluating Egyptian legal research platforms for an Egyptian-law research application. Could you please confirm:

1. The current number and date range of legislation and judicial decisions available for Egypt, broken down by court and subject.
2. Whether each result provides the original source, Gazette issue/page or case/appeal number, judgment date, and full text.
3. Whether there is an API or bulk export, and whether the export can include Arabic text, metadata, citations and original documents.
4. The licence terms for storing exported texts in a private legal research database, indexing them, generating embeddings, and using them in a commercial application.
5. Whether amendment history, repeal status and effective dates are tracked, and how quickly updates are added.
6. Whether a trial account can be used to test a set of 25 known questions and verify the cited source texts.

Please share pricing, technical documentation, and any written data-use terms.

Thank you.

## C. Subject-area collection matrix

For each subject, collect: (a) governing statute, (b) executive regulations, (c) amending instruments, (d) relevant ministerial/regulator decisions and circulars, (e) judgments and legal principles, (f) official forms/guidance, and (g) licensed commentary. This is a research checklist, not a claim that the items below have already been downloaded or verified.

### Foundational / constitutional / public law
- Constitution of the Arab Republic of Egypt and amendments.
- Constitutional declarations and transitional instruments, where still relevant.
- Supreme Constitutional Court judgments and principles.
- Administrative law; State Council law and procedure; administrative decisions and judicial principles.
- Public employment, civil service, public procurement and public property legislation.
- Treaties and instruments ratified by Egypt.

### Civil law / litigation
- Civil Code (Law 131/1948) and amendments.
- Civil and Commercial Procedure Code (Law 13/1968) and amendments.
- Evidence Law (Law 25/1968) and amendments.
- Arbitration in Civil and Commercial Matters Law (Law 27/1994).
- Registration and real-estate/publicity laws, land-registration rules and related regulations.
- Lease legislation and relevant historical/special laws.
- Consumer Protection Law (Law 181/2018) and regulations.
- Civil Court of Cassation principles, organised by doctrine and article.

### Criminal law / criminal procedure
- Penal Code (Law 58/1937) and amendments.
- Criminal Procedure Code (Law 150/1950) and amendments; track replacement/transition legislation if enacted.
- Anti-Cyber and Information Technology Crimes Law (Law 175/2018).
- Anti-money laundering, narcotics, weapons, trafficking, corruption and special criminal laws.
- Criminal Court of Cassation principles and Supreme Constitutional Court decisions.
- Public Prosecution circulars and instructions only where lawfully accessible; label internal/restricted materials appropriately.

### Labour / social insurance
- Labour Law (Law 14/2025), its commencement/transitional provisions and executive regulations/decisions.
- Earlier labour laws and repeals for historical matters.
- Trade union legislation.
- Social Insurance and Pensions Law (Law 148/2019) and amendments.
- Occupational safety and health, work permits, foreign employment and wage-related decisions.
- Labour and insurance Court of Cassation principles; ILO conventions ratified by Egypt.

### Companies / commercial / investment / insolvency
- Commercial Code (Law 17/1999) and amendments.
- Companies Law (Law 159/1981) and executive regulations.
- Investment Law (Law 72/2017) and executive regulations.
- Bankruptcy/restructuring law (Law 11/2018) and related rules.
- Commercial registry, trade names, agency, competition, consumer protection and capital-market instruments.
- GAFI and regulator decisions, circulars, model forms and official guidance.
- Commercial/economic court decisions and Cassation principles.

### Tax / customs / banking / finance
- Income Tax Law (Law 91/2005) and amendments.
- VAT Law (Law 67/2016) and amendments.
- Unified Tax Procedures Law (Law 206/2020) and amendments.
- Customs Law (Law 207/2020) and executive regulations.
- Central Bank and Banking Sector Law (Law 194/2020).
- Financial leasing, factoring, microfinance, capital markets and financial-regulatory instruments.
- Annual tax amendments, finance laws, official tax authority instructions and customs decisions.
- Tax dispute and economic court materials where public and reusable.

### Family / personal status / nationality / civil status
- Applicable personal-status statutes and amendments, separated by legal topic and personal-status regime.
- Marriage, divorce, maintenance, custody, guardianship, inheritance and execution provisions.
- Nationality law, civil-status law, birth/death registration and related executive decisions.
- Personal-status Court of Cassation and Supreme Constitutional Court decisions.
- Clearly identify historical provisions and amendments; do not assume one consolidated text is current.

### Technology / privacy / intellectual property / media
- Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151/2020) and executive regulations/decisions when issued.
- Anti-Cyber and Information Technology Crimes Law (Law 175/2018).
- Telecommunications Regulation Law (Law 10/2003) and amendments.
- Intellectual Property Rights Protection Law (Law 82/2002), executive regulations and amendments.
- Copyright, patents, trademarks, industrial designs, domain names, e-signatures and e-commerce rules.
- Press/media and audiovisual legislation.
- NTRA, IP authority and relevant regulator decisions; WIPO treaty materials.

### Other sectors to expand into
- Real estate, construction, urban planning, tenancy, mortgages and land registration.
- Environment, agriculture, water, food safety and animal health.
- Health, medicines, medical devices and professional practice.
- Transport, aviation, maritime, ports and road traffic.
- Education, universities, professional licensing and research.
- Public utilities, electricity, gas, water and telecommunications.
- Public procurement, concessions, public enterprises and local administration.
- Tourism, antiquities, banking employment regulations and sector-specific company bylaws.

## D. Minimum metadata and status policy

Recommended document-level fields:
- document_id; document_type; title_ar; title_en; instrument_number; year; issuing_authority; subject_tags
- gazette_name; gazette_issue; publication_date; page_start; page_end; official_source_url; archive_reference
- original_file_url; retrieval_timestamp; source_sha256; file_format; language; translation_status
- effective_date; end_date; legal_status (in_force / amended / repealed / unknown); status_checked_at
- amendment_links; affected_articles; supersedes; superseded_by
- extraction_method; OCR_status; extraction_quality; article_count; parser_report
- verification_status (unreviewed / source_checked / text_checked / status_checked / lawyer_approved)
- reviewer; review_date; licence_basis; allowed_storage; allowed_commercial_use; allowed_model_processing

Judgment fields:
- court; chamber/circuit; case_number; appeal_number; judgment_date; publication_source; full_text_or_headnote; cited_articles; legal_issue; disposition; anonymization/status.

## E. Collection protocol

1. Register source and terms before downloading.
2. Download only by permitted means; use conservative rate limits and no access-control bypass.
3. Preserve the original source file and its hash.
4. Extract text to UTF-8 while retaining page references and Arabic numbering.
5. Detect duplicates and changed versions using hashes.
6. Split articles/sections and create a completeness report; never silently drop preambles, schedules, tables or issuing provisions.
7. Leave legal status unknown until verified; do not infer in-force status from the fact that a page is accessible.
8. Link each amendment and judgment to the relevant law/articles without overwriting history.
9. Put secondary commentary, templates, translations and AI-generated summaries in distinct trust classes.
10. Run a human review queue and a benchmark of known questions/citations before enabling legal answers in production.

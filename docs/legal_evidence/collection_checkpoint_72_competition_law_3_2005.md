## 1. Law 3/2005 — competition prohibitions and institutions
- Article 1 requires economic activity to be conducted without preventing, restricting or harming competition.
- Article 2 defines persons, products, economic efficiency, sectoral regulators and (after Law 175/2022) economic concentration, control, material influence, corrective and behavioural measures.
- Article 3 defines relevant market by product and geographic scope.
- Article 4: market control threshold is a share exceeding 25%, plus effective ability to influence prices/supply without competitors' ability to constrain it.
- Article 5 applies to foreign conduct that restricts/h harms competition in Egypt and constitutes an offence under the law.
- Article 6 prohibits horizontal agreements among competitors to fix/raise/lower prices, allocate markets/customers/geographies, coordinate tenders/bids, or restrict production/distribution/marketing.
- Article 7 prohibits vertical agreements with suppliers/customers that restrict competition.
- Article 8 prohibits abuse of dominant position, including restricting production/supply, refusal to deal, exclusive distribution, tying, discriminatory terms, refusal to supply scarce product, denial of access to essential facilities, predatory pricing and exclusivity obligations.
- Article 9 excludes directly State-managed public utilities, subject to statutory exceptions.
- Article 10 allows Cabinet to set price of essential product for limited period after ECA opinion.
- Articles 11 onward establish ECA and its powers: receive complaints/notifications, investigate, collect evidence, inspect, request data, issue reasoned decisions, approve exemptions/settlements and refer criminal matters.
- Law 175/2022 added economic-concentration controls (merger/acquisition/joint venture), notification and review framework, corrective measures and new sanctions.

## 2. Law 175/2022 — economic concentration / merger control
- Gazette issue 30 (supplement A), 28 December 2022 (some sources cite 29 December; verify Gazette issue/date).
- Adds the definition of economic concentration, control, material influence, corrective measures and behavioural measures.
- Requires prior notification to ECA for specified economic concentrations meeting turnover/asset thresholds; standstill obligation and ECA review apply.
- ECA may approve, conditionally approve or prohibit transaction; may require structural/behavioural remedies.
- ECA may impose penalties for failure to notify, gun-jumping, misleading data or breach of conditions.
- The exact thresholds, calculation methods, filing timelines and review periods are in Law 175/2022 and Executive Regulation; extract them from the official consolidated booklet and current ECA guidelines before use.

## 3. Executive Regulation Decision 1316/2005 — official consolidated text
- Official ECA PDF includes the full regulation (approximately 30+ pages) and amendments/footnotes.
- Definitions:
  - Relevant product market: practical/objective substitutes from consumer perspective, including product similarity and ability to switch due to relative price or other competitive factors.
  - Relevant geographic market: area with homogeneous competitive conditions, considering buyers' and sellers' ability to shift geographically, transportation/insurance/time costs and customs/non-customs restrictions.
  - Control: market share above 25% plus effective price/supply influence and inability of competitors to constrain it.
- The regulation details:
  - Relevant market definition and market-share calculations.
  - Horizontal/vertical agreements and exemptions for economic efficiency benefiting consumers.
  - Dominance and abuse.
  - ECA complaint, investigation, information requests, inspection, hearing and decision procedures.
  - Notification/assessment of economic concentration, turnover/assets calculation, filing documentation, review phases and remedies.
  - Administrative settlement and enforcement.
- The 2024 official consolidated text includes amendments by PM Decisions 2509/2016, 2590/2016, 2595/2016 and 1120/2024; compare each original Gazette text.

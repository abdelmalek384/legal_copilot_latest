وعلى القرار الجمهورى رقم 379 لسنة 2015 بشأن التشكيل الوزارى الحالى وتعديلاته؛

## Scope and crosswalk

- This amendment is specifically to the **model regulation for residential nurseries** issued by Decision 277/1998. It changes Article 5(1) to require the child age to be between three months and six years.
- It is not evidence that the general model nursery regulation Decision 244/2012 (cited in Social Solidarity Decision 756/2022) was amended.
- Decision 48/2026 on workplace nurseries cites Decision 44/2012. The difference between 44/2012 and 244/2012 remains unresolved and must be checked against original Gazette copies; do not treat them as interchangeable.
- The decision's preamble confirms that Executive Regulation 2075/2010 to Child Law 12/1996 had amendments by 2017, but the complete amending decisions 208/2014, 1783/2018 and 1143/2020 have not yet been acquired as full texts in this pass.

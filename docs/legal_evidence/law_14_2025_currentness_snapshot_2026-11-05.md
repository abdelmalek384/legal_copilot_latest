## 2. Related post-enactment instruments found

The following are related implementing/operational instruments, **not amendments to the text of Law 14/2025** based on the material located:

- **Prime Minister Decision 982/2026:** the Official Printing Authority text says remote work applies on Sundays during April 2026 to specified sectors and eligible administrative functions (including legal affairs/advisory work), starting 5 April 2026. The decision's own period is April; later extensions are separate instruments. Record: https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=X+ynf8GR0jI%3D
- **Prime Minister Decisions 1305/2026, 1572/2026, 2033/2026 and 2377/2026:** the later records describe extensions of Decision 982/2026 through May, June, July and August 2026 respectively. Decision 2377 record: https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=OHEywmUwNf8%3D
- **Prime Minister Decision 2590/2026:** the Official Printing Authority record states that Decision 982/2026 continues during September 2026. The targeted search in this snapshot did not surface an October or November extension; that absence is not proof that none exists. Record: https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=HAJdFlN7HCw%3D
- **Ministerial Decision 49/2026:** the Ministry of Labour announced on 26 March 2026 that this decision identifies dangerous/difficult work and remote areas for the additional seven days of annual leave under Law 14/2025. The signed decision/Gazette text was not captured in this snapshot; the ministry announcement is a discovery lead, not the operative instrument. Ministry notice: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/لتحقق-التوازن-بين-متطلبات-الإنتاج-وصون-حقوق-القوى-العاملة-وزير-العمل-يصدر-قرار-ا-وزاري-ا-بتحديد-الأعمال-الخطرة-والمناطق-النائية-التي-ت-زاد-فيها-الإجازات-السنوية-7-أيام-للعاملين/
- **General workplace-regulation rules:** the Ministry of Labour announced on 29 August and 5 September 2026 the text of general rules for workplace regulations in private-sector establishments, describing them as an important implementing decision under Law 14/2025 and applying to establishments with 10 or more workers. The full signed/published instrument was not captured here. Notices: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/تنفيذ-ا-لتوجيهات-الرئيس-السيسي-بتوفير-بيئة-عمل-لائقة-ومنتجة-وزير-العمل-يعقد-مؤتمر-ا-صحفي-ا-وي-طلق-القواعد-العامة-للائحة-تنظيم-العمل-بشركات-القطاع-الخاص/ and https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-يعلن-نص-القواعد-العامة-للائحة-تنظيم-العمل-بالقطاع-الخاص/

## 3. Currentness conclusion and limits

- The official Ministry listing and Official Printing Authority record continue to identify Law 14/2025 as the labour statute. The search conducted for this snapshot did not locate a later statute expressly amending or repealing Law 14/2025.
- This is **not proof that no amendment, corrigendum, implementing decision, or later court interpretation exists**. The related 2026 instruments above show that operational rules continue to develop.
- The 2026 instruments have not been ingested or article-mapped in this comparison package. Decision 982/2026 and extensions through September were identified; no October/November extension was surfaced in this targeted search. Decision 49/2026 and the general workplace-regulation rules were found through Ministry announcements, but their full signed/published texts were not captured. Exact Gazette publication, operative text, commencement, and interactions with individual articles require separate capture and legal review.
- The Ministry's announcement of general workplace-regulation rules is a notice, not a substitute for the complete signed/published instrument.
- Effective-date handling remains separate: promulgation Article 13 provides commencement from the first day of the month following 90 days after publication, and specialized labour courts from the following 1 October. The Gazette publication date is 3 May 2025; do not treat the metadata `as_of=2025-05-03` as a commencement date.
- Source reuse rights and the user-uploaded PDF's original download URL/capture chain remain unverified.

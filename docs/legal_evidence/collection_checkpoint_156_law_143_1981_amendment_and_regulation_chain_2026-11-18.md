https://moa.gov.eg/media/sf1heyme/143.pdf

4. **Law 96/1995** — replaced Article 4(1); Official Gazette issue 22 bis, 2 June 1995.
5. **Law 72/1996** — replaced Article 4(1) again; Official Gazette issue 15 bis, 16 April 1996.
6. **Law 11/2024** — replaced Article 11(2) and Article 12(2); Official Gazette issue 8 bis (A), 24 February 2024; effective 25 February 2024.

Laws 205/1991, 96/1995, 72/1996 and 11/2024 were already registered. New records EG-0236 and EG-0237 add Laws 12/1984 and 55/1988. The article graph must preserve Article 21's repeal, the two successive Article 4(1) versions, and the successive Article 11(2)/12(2) versions.

## 2. Constitutional invalidation

SCC Case **101/26** (1 February 2009) declared Article 22(1) of Law 143/1981 unconstitutional. The consolidated statute footnote cites Official Gazette issue 7 bis (A), 15 February 2009. The official SCC judgment page is:
https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/3204_26_101_1_2.html

This is a judicial invalidation, not an amendment law. Preserve it in the judicial-effects layer and mark the affected provision's status separately.

## 3. Executive Regulation Decision 198/1982 and identified amendments

The Executive Regulation was issued by Ministerial Decision **198/1982**, published in Egyptian Proceedings issue 113, 16 May 1982. The full text is available in a secondary consolidated transcription:
https://lawyeregypt.net/اللائحة-التنفيذية-للقانون-رقم-143-لسنة-1981-في/

Its amendment chain identified in the consolidated text is:

| Instrument | Gazette publication | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Decision 305/1983 | Egyptian Proceedings issue 185, 14 Aug 1983 | Repeals Articles 27–30. |
| Decision 105/1986 | Issue 151, 5 Jul 1986 | Replaces Article 45. |
| Decision 139/1990 | Issue 43, 19 Feb 1990 | Replaces Article 50. |
| Decision 305/1990 | Issue 135, 12 Jun 1990 | Replaces Article 41. |
| Decision 1230/1990 | Issue 242, 25 Oct 1990 | Replaces Article 45 again. |
| Decision 1057/1995 | Issue 123, 5 Jun 1996 | Replaces Article 42; decision number/year is 1995 although publication is 1996. |
| Decision 1012/2009 | Issue 223, 28 Sep 2009 | Replaces Article 15, including a ten-year tax exemption framework for qualifying reclaimed/cultivated land. |

New acquisition rows EG-0238–EG-0244 record these seven regulatory amendment decisions. The source texts are from Laweg and secondary consolidated regulation footnotes; original Gazette scans are not acquired. The order of amendments to Article 45 is important: Decision 105/1986 was later superseded by Decision 1230/1990. Decision 1057/1995's issue year differs from its decision number/year and must not be normalized to 1996.

Decision 632/1982 (presidential decision on specified recognition-of-ownership cases) appears in the preamble to Decision 1057/1995. It is a related implementing instrument, but is not yet registered in this pass because its exact scope and relation to the current article graph have not been established.

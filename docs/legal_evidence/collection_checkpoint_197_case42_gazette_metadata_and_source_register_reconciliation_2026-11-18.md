## 1. SCC Case 42/17 — new publication metadata leads

The full secondary judgment transcription remains available at MKSEgypt:
https://mksegypt.org/ar/judgments-of-the-supreme-constitutional-court/15553

The judgment dated 6 June 1998 held:
- Article 37(2) of Law 142/1964 unconstitutional insofar as it prohibited acquisition by prescription contrary to the real-estate register.
- Article 38 fell consequentially.
- Article 37(1), giving the register evidentiary force, was not invalidated.

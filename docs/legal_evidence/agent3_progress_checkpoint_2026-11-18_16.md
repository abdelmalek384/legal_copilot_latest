## New primary-source acquisition — FRA Chairperson Decision 2872/2026
- Official six-page Gazette scan acquired: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/alamiria_2026_2872_-R-2.pdf
- Gazette: issue 216 supplement (أ), 30 September 2026, printed pages 2–7; decision dated 13 September 2026.
- Captured all six Arabic operative articles manually from page images because the PDF text layer is not searchable, plus the attached eight-step Baridy onboarding procedure.
- All FRA-licensed NBFI companies/entities must register and activate a registered electronic-mail account (Baridy) for receiving official FRA correspondence.
- Existing entities: complete registration within three months from commencement. New licensees/registrants: within 30 days of licence/registration/approval and before commencing activity, as applicable.
- The mailbox becomes an official digital address for FRA notices; electronic proof of dispatch/receipt or failed receipt is recognized; failure to read a delivered message is not a defence. Maintaining an active mailbox and updating its data are continuing obligations.
- Mandatory receipt service is free; sending correspondence to FRA through Baridy is optional and subject to postal service subscription/fees; other accepted channels remain available.
- Effective 1 October 2026.

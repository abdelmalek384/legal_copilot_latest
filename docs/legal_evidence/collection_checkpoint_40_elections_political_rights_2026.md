# Collection checkpoint 40 — elections, political rights and National Election Authority (2026-11-17)

## 1. Core election-law framework

The National Election Authority's 2025 decisions cite the following core statutes:
- Law 45/2014 regulating the exercise of political rights.
- Law 46/2014 issuing the House of Representatives Law.
- Law 198/2017 on the National Election Authority.
- Law 174/2020 on division of House of Representatives electoral constituencies.
- Law 141/2020 issuing the Senate Law.
- Law 22/2014 regulating Presidential Elections.
- Law 40/1977 regulating political parties is an additional foundational statute; obtain its amendments and current constitutional review history.

These instruments must be treated as separate statutes, not one generic "election law".

## 2. National Election Authority — 2025 official decisions

- NEA Decision 71/2025 ordered re-running individual-seat elections in specified House of Representatives constituencies after Supreme Administrative Court judgments of 29 November 2025, with a detailed timetable:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=tJsXZOnE2po%3D
- The decision cites Laws 45/2014, 46/2014, 198/2017 and 174/2020, NEA Decisions 37/2025 and 38/2025, and the Supreme Administrative Court judgments.
- It lists affected constituencies in Giza, Fayoum, Minya, Assiut, New Valley, Sohag, Luxor, Aswan, Alexandria and Beheira.
- The decision provides overseas voting 8–9 December 2025, domestic voting 10–11 December 2025, repeat voting 31 December 2025–1 January 2026, and final announcement by 10 January 2026 for the listed constituencies.
- This is a historical election-specific decision; preserve it as an event record, not a permanent rule.

### NEA Decision 40/2025 — legal representative of electoral lists
- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=rE7sCIKSnnI%3D
- Requires each electoral list to have one or more legal representatives from outside the list who are registered in the voter database; a certificate is issued by the NEA.
- Dated 4 October 2025.
- It is a procedural rule under Laws 45/2014, 46/2014, 198/2017 and 174/2020.

### NEA Decisions 1, 4 and 14/2025 — civil society/election observation database
- Decision 1/2025 registers/renews Egyptian civil-society organizations in the database for monitoring elections/referenda:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=nqUAQ9tGw%2FI%3D
- Decision 4/2025 registers/renews organizations and national councils:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=KEsuNQesXfk%3D
- Decision 14/2025 registers/renews further organizations and the National Council for Persons with Disabilities:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=+nAXeHi3bwI%3D
- These are individual database/observer accreditation decisions, not amendments to election statutes.

## 3. NEA institutional governance

- Presidential Decision 426/2023 completes the composition of the NEA Board, with members from Court of Cassation, Courts of Appeal, State Council, State Lawsuits Authority and Administrative Prosecution:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=TPFyw1dXIPM%3D
- Law 198/2017 and its Executive Regulation/NEA decisions should be the primary institutional source set for voter registration, polling, counting, complaints, observation, campaign finance, candidate lists and results.

## 4. Presidential election rules

- NEA Decision 5/2023 opened candidacy for the 2024 Presidential Election:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=VG32PDL84%2FA%3D
- It cites Presidential Election Law 22/2014, Political Rights Law 45/2014 and NEA Law 198/2017.
- This is a historical 2024 election timetable/procedure; the underlying statutory rules remain relevant, while the event decision does not.

## 5. Political parties

- Law 40/1977 regulates political parties.
- Acquire the base law, amendments, Political Parties Affairs Committee rules, party registration/merger/dissolution procedures, financing/disclosure and relevant SCC judgments.
- Distinguish the statutory framework from individual Committee decisions on party registration or status.

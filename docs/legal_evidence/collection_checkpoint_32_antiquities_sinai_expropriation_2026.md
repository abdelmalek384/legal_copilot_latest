# Collection checkpoint 32 — antiquities, Sinai development, expropriation and desert land regimes (2026-11-17)

## 1. Antiquities Protection Law 117/1983 — enforcement instrument and acquisition lead

- Base statute: Law 117/1983 issuing the Antiquities Protection Law.
- It is cited in the preamble to PM Decision 200/2026 (Executive Regulation of State Private Property Law 168/2025) and in the official tourism/hotel regulatory framework.
- Justice Minister Decision 3586/2024:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=SlJuBtiMAIU%3D
- Decision 3586/2024 grants specified Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities personnel judicial-officer status for offences under Tourism and Hotel Establishments Law 8/2022 and Law 117/1983 (Antiquities Protection).
- The decision includes inspectors for the Grand Egyptian Museum and surrounding area and repeals earlier judicial-officer decisions 2467/2010, 2417/2010, 7927/2011, 8046/2012 and 1121/2014.
- This is an enforcement/competence record, not a substantive amendment to the Antiquities Law.
- Acquire Law 117/1983, all amendments (including Law 91/2018 if confirmed by primary source), Executive Regulation and antiquities-zone/designation/permit decisions.

## 2. Integrated Sinai Development Law 14/2012

- Law 14/2012 governs integrated development in the Sinai Peninsula, including ownership, usufruct, investment and approvals in designated areas.
- Official Presidential Decision 128/2022:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=37B2gztNoYs%3D
- Decision 128/2022 exempts Sharm El-Sheikh, Dahab and the Gulf of Aqaba tourism sector in South Sinai from all provisions of Law 14/2012, subject to the conditions and arrangements in the decision.
- It repeals Presidential Decision 28/2021, which had provided a partial exemption for Sharm El-Sheikh and Dahab.
- The decision transfers relevant land/property/project/company applications to South Sinai Governorate, competent land authorities, GAFI or FRA as applicable, and provides that certain companies operating only in exempted areas must amend their articles of association/constitutive documents after approvals from the Ministry of Defence, Ministry of Interior and General Intelligence.
- This is a territorial/scope exception and must be encoded as a geographically scoped rule, not as a repeal of Law 14/2012 throughout Sinai.
- Obtain the base Law 14/2012, Executive Regulation and PM Decision 48/2017 on ownership/usufruct conditions for occupants of land in Sinai, plus all later exceptions and implementing decisions.

## 3. Expropriation Law 10/1990

- Law 10/1990 governs expropriation of real estate for public benefit and is cited in many 2025–2026 PM decisions declaring projects to be public-benefit works or renewing expropriation decisions.
- Official Amiri Press examples:
  - PM Decision 3127/2026 amending the land area in an earlier school-expropriation decision:
    https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=1Y4UAHo%2F5CY%3D
  - PM Decision 3129/2026 renewing the public-benefit designation for land to establish a youth centre, with reference to the three-year period under Article 12 as amended by Law 187/2020:
    https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=A+nXQmGF2X8%3D
  - PM Decision 3503/2022 concerning expropriation for Cairo historic-area redevelopment:
    https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=EmptuKbiP9Q%3D
- These examples show that public-benefit decisions are individual parcel/project instruments with maps, coordinates, owner notices and time limits. Store each as a separate administrative act linked to Law 10/1990.
- Obtain base law, Executive Regulation, Law 187/2020 amendment to Article 12, compensation valuation rules, notification/deposit requirements and Supreme Court/Cassation principles.

## 4. Desert Lands Law 143/1981 and State-land allocation

- Law 143/1981 on desert lands is cited in PM Decision 200/2026, State Private Property Law 168/2025 implementation and many presidential land-allocation decisions.
- Official Presidential Decree 410/2022 allocates 581.13 feddans of State-owned private land in Beheira to the General Authority for Reconstruction and Agricultural Development for reclamation/cultivation:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=nvYwcPlH5Os%3D
- Presidential Decree 569/2022 adjusts land allocations in the northwest coast:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=9tjg9c3ha5k%3D
- Presidential Decree 113/2022 allocates land for grain silos in Qena, Giza and Sharqia:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=dWbgkAQMU0I%3D
- Presidential Decree 96/2023 adjusts administrative boundaries of governorates and cites the desert-land/state-land framework:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=W1z6ZooR7uU%3D
- Acquire Law 143/1981, Executive Regulation, amendments and competent-authority decisions. Link with State Private Property Laws 144/2017 and 168/2025, Sinai Law 14/2012, New Urban Communities Law 59/1979 and Investment Law 72/2017.

## 5. Public Roads Law 84/1968 and related land/utility controls

- Law 84/1968 on public roads is cited in PM Decision 200/2026 and Waste Executive Regulation 722/2022.
- It governs public roads and related occupation/works/rights-of-way. Obtain the full official text, Executive Regulation, amendments and road-occupation/utility decisions.
- Keep it distinct from Public Roads Occupation Law 140/1956, which is a separate instrument also cited in waste regulation.

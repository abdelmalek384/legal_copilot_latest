## 2. Water Resources and Irrigation Law 147/2021 — official Ministry PDF

- Official Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation PDF:
  https://www.mwri.gov.eg/spa/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/قانون-147-لسنة-2021.pdf
- Law 147/2021 issues the Water Resources and Irrigation Law.
- Promulgation Article 2 repeals Law 12/1984 on Irrigation and Drainage and inconsistent provisions.
- Promulgation Article 3 requires an Executive Regulation; existing regulations/decisions continue insofar as they do not conflict until the new regulation is issued.
- Promulgation Article 4 states publication in the Official Gazette and commencement on the day after publication.
- Law issued 16 October 2021; locate the Executive Regulation, PM decision, ministerial decisions, water-use permits, groundwater, irrigation/drainage, Nile waterways, encroachments, fees and penalties.
- This is an official Ministry-hosted Arabic PDF locator; store bytes/hash and page mapping before marking source-matched.

## 3. Nuclear and Radiological Activities Law 7/2010 — major 2026 amendment

- Official Amiri Press text of Law 10/2026:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=rmB3E25O+II%3D
- Law 10/2026 amends Law 7/2010 regulating nuclear and radiological activities.
- Gazette: issue 20 bis, 16 May 2026; effective 17 May 2026 according to metadata:
  https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/16-05-2026/no-10?id=5638945&type=1
- Another Gazette PDF:
  https://gate.ahram.org.eg/media/News/2026/5/19/2026-639147821628570612-857.pdf
- It replaces the definition of radiological facilities in Article 3 and provisions in Articles 13(2), 32, 55 and 108; adds definitions and Articles 14 bis, 26 bis, 62 bis and 110.
- Updated Article 3 expands/clarifies radiological facilities, including irradiation facilities, radioactive-waste handling/treatment/storage/disposal, radioisotope production, mining/processing facilities emitting ionizing radiation, and electron/ion accelerators, with stated exclusions.
- Updated Article 55 restricts import, customs release, export or transport of radioactive materials/sources or radioactive components/products without Authority approval, except X-ray devices for medical use.
- Article 108 revises penalties for listed violations.
- This is a major current 2026 statutory amendment and must be integrated into Law 7/2010 article versions and the Nuclear and Radiological Regulatory Authority's licensing decisions.

## 5. Private State Property Disposal Law 168/2025 and Executive Regulation 200/2026

- Law 168/2025: Gazette issue 32 bis (w), 13 August 2025; secondary metadata reports it becomes effective after 30 days:
  https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/13-08-2025/no-168?id=2174188&type=1
- Secondary full-text locator:
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=15446
- Promulgation Article 2 repeals Law 144/2017 and inconsistent provisions.
- Promulgation Article 3 directs issuance of the Executive Regulation within 30 days.
- Promulgation Article 4 provides commencement after 30 days from publication.
- PM Decision 200/2026 issues the Executive Regulation:
  - Official Amiri Press text: https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=cEpwOo6BfmY%3D
  - Official Gazette PDF (issue 3 bis (a), 18 January 2026): https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/xmlui/bitstream/handle/123456789/118973/OG3_20260118_2885.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2
- Decision 200/2026 provides definitions and rules for regularizing private-state-property possession/disposal; it also allows a new regularization application for a person whose application under Law 144/2017 was rejected, subject to the new Regulation.
- This is a high-impact land/property law version transition: do not continue treating Law 144/2017 as current without linking the repeal and transitional rules under Law 168/2025.

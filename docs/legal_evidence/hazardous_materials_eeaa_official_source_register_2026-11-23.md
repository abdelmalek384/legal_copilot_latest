## 2. Verbatim Arabic text captured from the official EEAA page

### 2.1 Definitions (page section: التعريفات)

**المواد الخطرة:**

> المواد ذات الخواص الخطرة التي تضر بصحة الانسان أو تؤثر تأثيرا ضارا علي البيئة مثل المواد المعدية أو السامة أو القابلة للانفجار أو الاشتعال أو ذات الإشعاعات المؤينة.

**النفايات الخطرة:**

> مخلفات الأنشطة والعمليات المختلفة أو رمادها المحتفظة بخواص المواد الخطرة التي ليس لها استخدامات تالية أصلية أو بديلة مثل النفايات الإكلينيكية من الأنشطة العلاجية والنفايات الناتجة عن تصنيع أي من المستحضرات الصيدلية والأدوية أو المذيبات العضوية أو الأحبار والأصباغ والدهانات.

These are page definitions, not represented as a substitute for statutory definitions in Law 202/2020 or its Executive Regulation.

### 2.2 Customs-certificate procedure (page section: الإجراءات المطلوبة لإبداء رأى جهاز شئون البيئة فى الشهادات الجمركية الواردة من الجمارك المصرية)

The official page states the applicant must submit a company-letterhead request addressed to the Executive Director, identifying the customs certificate and date, invoice and date, bill of lading, chemical names and quantities, and customs office; the request also includes applicant national ID/telephone, authorization and ID copy.

It further states:
- the customs certificate must bear a referral to EEAA and the exports/imports stamp;
- originals are presented for inspection and copies are submitted;
- supporting documents include customs certificate, bill of lading, invoice, certificate of origin, manufacturer-issued chemical safety data identifying scientific name and CAS number, tax card, commercial register (and industrial register for a factory), and importers register / investment record;
- EEAA reviews the papers through a committee of researchers and sends its formal opinion to customs and exports/imports;
- the page states the letter is issued within 3–5 working days and collected from the Ministry's general archive by an authorized representative.

This is a transcription/structured capture of the current public EEAA service page. It is operational guidance, not an independently verified statutory deadline or a replacement for the applicable licensing rules.

## 3. Relevant legislative text and chain

### 3.1 Executive Regulation of Waste Management Law 202/2020 — PM Decision 722/2022, Article 47

Official Amiri Press searchable text: https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=4dvIKCtGaRc%3D

The following Article 47 text is captured from the official publisher's searchable text:

> تختص اللجنة المشار إليها فى المادة السابقة بوضع وإصدار ومراجعة القوائم الموحدة للمواد والمخلفات الخطرة، ووضع ضوابط واشتراطات التداول والإدارة المتكاملة للمواد والمخلفات الخطرة وتحديد أسلوب الحد من تولدها. كما تختص بتحديد الوزارة أو الجهة المعنية المسئولة عن إصدار تراخيص التداول والإدارة المتكاملة للمواد والمخلفات الخطرة. ويصدر كل وزير أو مسئول عن الوزارات والجهات المبينة فى المادة السابقة، كل فى نطاق اختصاصه، قرارًا يحدد فيه الجهة المسئولة عن هذا الشأن داخل هذه الوزارات أو الجهات. ويقوم الجهاز بالتنسيق مع تلك الجهات فى وضع وتنظيم آليات وإجراءات مراقبة تداول المواد والمخلفات الخطرة، واتخاذ ما يلزم من تدابير فى هذا الشأن، كما يتولى تسجيل الشركات التى تمارس أى نشاط من أنشطة الإدارة المتكاملة للمخلفات الخطرة.

Article 47 then enumerates committee functions, including:
- allocating ministry/authority responsibility for each hazardous and conditionally hazardous waste type;
- setting concentration, quantity and hazardous-property thresholds;
- classifying large and small hazardous-waste generators;
- setting maximum on-site storage periods;
- specifying required licenses for collection, transport, temporary/permanent storage and final disposal;
- adopting classification methods for hazardous substances.

**Relationship:** Article 47 of the 2022 Executive Regulation describes the committee's legal remit. The 2025 Decisions 121 and 151 operationalize export controls and approve a unified list, respectively. The complete text of the committee's constituting Decision 30/2023 remains a separate acquisition target.

### 3.2 Ministerial Decision 150/2025 — internal-facility hazardous-material / waste handling fees

Official legal-memory index entry: https://mksegypt.org/ar/nashras/33  
The index records Environment Minister Decision 150/2025 in Egyptian Proceedings issue 163, 27 July 2025, describing it as setting fees for WMRA approval of activities handling hazardous materials and waste **inside the facility**.

This is a newly identified companion instrument to Decision 151/2025 in the same issue. The complete Gazette text and fee table have not yet been retrieved in this capture. Do not ingest the title/summary as if it were the complete decision.

### 3.3 Ministerial Decision 151/2025 — unified list

Official SIS Gazette catalogue record: https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/handle/123456789/104996  
It identifies Egyptian Proceedings issue 163 (supplement), 27 July 2025, and a 92.21 MB PDF named `WQ163_20250727_1555.pdf`. Secondary report: https://www.almasdar.com/print.aspx?id=158999.

Only Articles 1–2 are currently transcribed in the related repository tranche. The annexed unified list itself is not acquired here. The Gazette PDF was not readable through the available retrieval path in this pass; do not treat this as a completed list acquisition.

### 3.4 Ministerial Decision 30/2023 — technical committee formation

The official EEAA announcement dated 4 January 2025 confirms that the Technical Committee for Hazardous Materials and Waste was formed by Environment Minister Decision 30/2023 and describes its remit as preparing, issuing and reviewing unified lists, setting handling/integrated-management controls, and reducing generation:
https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/News/22401/Details

This official announcement is corroborative evidence of the decision's existence and role, not its constituting text. The complete decision, membership schedule, amendments/reconstitution and current membership remain unacquired.

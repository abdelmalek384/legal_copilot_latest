# Collection checkpoint 45 — nationality, foreign residence and immigration administration (2026-11-17)

## 1. Egyptian Nationality Law 26/1975 and amendment Law 28/2023

- Base Law 26/1975 regulates Egyptian nationality.
- Law 28/2023 official Amiri Press full text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=EPTqQ0aVE6Y%3D
- Law 28/2023 replaces:
  - Article 4, items First and Third.
  - First paragraph of Article 4 bis 2.
  - Second paragraph of Article 6.
- It adjusts naturalization eligibility for:
  - persons born in Egypt to a father or mother of Egyptian origin, subject to ordinary residence in Egypt and adulthood at application;
  - certain foreign-born-in-Egypt applicants whose foreign parent was also born in Egypt and belongs to a majority population in a country whose language is Arabic or religion is Islam, subject to application within one year after reaching adulthood.
- It revises the naturalization application fee to USD 10,000, transferred from abroad or deposited through the Central Bank route, with customs evidence that the funds entered Egypt.
- It deletes wording in Article 4 bis concerning property owned by the State or other public legal persons.
- Issued 21 May 2023; effective the day after publication.
- Obtain the full base Law 26/1975, Law 154/2004 and all other amendments, plus constitutional review and administrative procedures.

## 2. Naturalization for investment — PM Decision 3099/2019 and amendment 876/2023

- PM Decision 3099/2019 regulates cases in which Egyptian nationality may be granted to foreigners under Law 26/1975.
- PM Decision 876/2023 amends Decision 3099/2019:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=QVIE69iwhpI%3D
- Decision 876/2023 replaces the opening paragraph and updates investment routes, including:
  - purchase of State/public-person property for at least USD 300,000, subject to transfer/entry evidence and available property designation;
  - other investment/deposit routes set by the amended decision.
- PM Decision 647/2020 organizes the Naturalization Application Review Unit, its powers, work system and application procedures; the decision has later amendments.
- Individual naturalization approvals (e.g. PM Decisions 835/2025, 836/2025, 2799/2025 and 2800/2025) are case-specific administrative acts, not amendments to the general nationality law.
- Store applicant-specific nationality approvals with appropriate privacy controls; do not index personal identity details into general public legal answer content.

## 3. Entry, residence and exit of foreigners — Law 89/1960

- Law 89/1960 regulates entry, residence and exit of foreigners in Egypt.
- Ministry of Interior Decision 977/2023 amends Decision 8180/1996 regulating foreigners' residence:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=nC0LqOuFqQY%3D
- Decision 977/2023 replaces Article 2 bis of Decision 8180/1996 and provides temporary non-tourist residence routes including:
  - five-year renewable residence for a foreigner who owns one or more Egyptian properties worth at least USD 200,000;
  - other routes and requirements are set out in the full text.
- The decision's preamble cross-references Law 230/1996 on non-Egyptian ownership of built property/vacant land, Sinai Development Law 14/2012, Investment Law 72/2017 and Central Bank Law 194/2020.
- Obtain the base Law 89/1960, Executive Regulation, Ministerial Decision 180/1964, Visa Decision 31/1960, Residence Decision 8180/1996, amendment Decision 977/2023 and all subsequent amendments.

## 4. Residence through property and investment — cross-law controls

- Law 230/1996 regulates non-Egyptian ownership of built property and vacant land.
- Law 14/2012 applies special ownership/usufruct restrictions in the Sinai Peninsula, subject to territorial exceptions and competent approvals.
- Investment Law 72/2017 and PM Decision 3099/2019 provide separate investment/naturalization routes.
- Residence Decision 977/2023 provides an immigration residence route linked to property ownership.
- These regimes are distinct:
  - owning property does not itself grant nationality;
  - a residence permit does not itself grant property ownership;
  - a naturalization application does not automatically confer residence or citizenship;
  - Sinai restrictions and security approvals must be assessed separately.
- Obtain Law 230/1996, its Executive Regulation and current Ministry of Interior/land registration/foreign ownership decisions.

## 5. Illegal Migration Law 82/2016

- Law 82/2016 on Combating Illegal Migration and Smuggling of Migrants is separately recorded in checkpoint 24.
- PM Decision 2528/2026 amends the Fund for Combating Illegal Migration and Protecting Migrants and Witnesses under Decision 369/2023.
- Do not conflate regular entry/residence rules under Law 89/1960 with criminal offences and victim-protection provisions under Law 82/2016.

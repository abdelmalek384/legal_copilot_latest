## 1. Law 175/2018 — scope and key definitions
- Regulates information technology crimes, digital evidence, service-provider obligations and competent authorities.
- Article 1 defines electronic data/information, personal data, government data, information technology, service provider, user, software, information system, network, website, website manager, private account, email, interception, hacking, content, digital evidence, traffic data, computer, electronic support, critical information infrastructure and national security.
- Service provider includes natural/legal person supplying IT/communications services, including processing/storage directly or through another.
- Critical information infrastructure includes essential systems/networks/assets whose compromise can affect State services, utilities or national economy; sectors include electricity, gas/oil, telecom, finance/banking, industry, transport, education, broadcasting, water/wastewater, health, government and emergency services.

## 2. Service-provider obligations — Article 2
- Service providers must retain and store specified user data, traffic data, content, subscriber information and related records for at least 180 days from creation, subject to the statutory scope.
- They must preserve confidentiality/security and not disclose stored data except under lawful orders/authorizations.
- They must maintain technical capacity to provide competent authorities with required data under law and preserve data integrity.
- The exact statutory categories, access authorization, emergency procedure and service-provider definitions must be extracted verbatim; do not generalize to every business that uses cloud software.

## 3. Substantive offences and penalties
- Articles 13–17: unauthorized access to accounts/systems/networks, exceeding authorized access, unlawful interception, unlawful access to government systems, disabling/altering data, damaging/deleting systems/data and attacks on critical infrastructure.
- Articles 18–24: offences involving website/account creation or use, impersonation, fraud, unlawful content, privacy violations, misuse of electronic payment instruments and other specified conduct.
- Articles 25–27: violation of privacy, publication of personal/family information, threats/extortion and use of IT systems for specified unlawful acts.
- Articles 28–30: responsibility of website managers/service providers and aggravating circumstances.
- Articles 31–34: criminal investigation, confiscation, legal-person liability and related provisions.
- Exact offence elements and penalty ranges require article-level extraction; the law must be read with Penal Code 58/1937, Criminal Procedure Law 174/2025 and Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020.

## 4. Executive Regulation 1699/2020 — technical and evidentiary requirements

### Article 1 — definitions
- Defines encryption, encryption key, asymmetric encryption, critical information infrastructure and related terms.

### Article 2 — service-provider technical/organizational controls
- Encryption standard at least AES-128 (symmetric or asymmetric) for confidentiality and integrity, with encryption-key security.
- Anti-malware systems/software and updates.
- Secure protocols such as HTTPS.
- Logical access permissions for networks, files and databases.
- Inventory of devices/equipment with unique/serial numbers and models, and systems/software/applications/databases.
- Further controls require detailed extraction from the complete regulation; do not treat this partial list as a complete security baseline.

### Remaining regulation topics
- Critical information infrastructure classification and security.
- Technical and organizational controls for service providers.
- Expert registration/qualification for digital evidence.
- Collection, extraction, preservation, authentication and chain of custody of digital evidence.
- Requests and orders for access to stored data and related records.
- Settlement/conciliation mechanisms for specified offences.
- The complete regulation PDF and 2020 Gazette source are available; extract all articles and annexes exactly.

## 3. Relationship to existing hazardous-waste evidence

- Law 202/2020 Article 53 provides the statutory context for the technical committee.
- Environment Minister Decision 30/2023 is the previously identified committee-formation instrument; its currentness must not be inferred solely from the later decision title.
- Environment Minister Decision 121/2025, published in Proceedings issue 127 on 11 June 2025, states in its export controls that existing hazardous-material lists issued by competent ministries are to be used while observing the unified lists that the Article 53 committee will issue. This is a primary searchable text from Amiri Press, but it does not itself identify the contents of any later unified list.
- Decision 151/2025 is the previously identified unified-list adoption lead. Its large Gazette annex remains unacquired in the current evidence record.
- Decision 346/2025 may be material to the committee's later decisions, but its exact resolutions and annexes cannot be mapped without the Gazette PDF or another authorized complete primary text.

Do not infer that Decision 346/2025 repeals Decision 30/2023, adopts Decision 151/2025, or itself publishes the unified list. Those propositions require the operative text and annexes.

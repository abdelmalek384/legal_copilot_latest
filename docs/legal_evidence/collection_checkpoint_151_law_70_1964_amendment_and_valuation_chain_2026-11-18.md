## 2. Law 70/1964 — consolidated amendment graph

The base statute is Law 70/1964, Official Gazette issue 67, 22 March 1964 (EG-0151). Its identified substantive amendment chain is now:

1. **Law 94/1980** — Article 21/22 valuation and fee-table substitutions.
2. **Law 6/1991** — major amendments to Articles 19, 21, 25–27, new Articles 24 bis, 34 bis, 34 bis(1), repeal of Article 20.
3. **Law 224/1996** — Article 24 bis(1) and Article 34 bis replacements.
4. **Law 225/1996** — repeal of Article 21(8).
5. **Law 9/2003** — insertion of Article 34 bis(2).
6. **Law 3/2004** — insertion of Article 34 bis(3).
7. **Law 83/2006** — Article 19 replacement; also affects Law 142/1964.
8. **Law 163/2019** — new Table B item.
9. **Law 67/2021** — Table B terminology substitution.

This is the chain identified by the footnotes of the current secondary consolidated text and cross-checked against the available MKSEgypt, Manshurat, Laweg, EastLaws and Ministry of Finance records. The original Gazette issue pages for these amendment laws have not all been acquired/visually verified. Keep the source-tier distinction in the amendment graph.

## 3. Constitutional judgment effects must be a separate layer

The Law 70 consolidated text identifies two constitutional judgments:

- **SCC Case 2/24, 14 December 2003:** declared Article 30 of Law 70/1964 unconstitutional; published in Official Gazette issue 52 bis, 31 December 2003. Full text source located at Qadaya is secondary; direct SCC page/original Gazette scan not yet located.
- **SCC Case 10/16, 18 May 2014:** declared Article 21(9) (first section) unconstitutional and consequentially removed item (3) of Table 2 attached to Justice Minister Decision 2936/1992; published in Official Gazette issue 21 bis (A), 24 May 2014. Direct official SCC judgment page located: https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/4946_16_10_1_2.html

Neither judgment is a statutory amendment. The article-version graph must show constitutional invalidation and consequential fall separately from enacted amendments. These two judgments are not newly added as acquisition rows in checkpoint 151; a dedicated judicial-source update remains next.

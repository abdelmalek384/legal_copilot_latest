## New core capital-market acquisition — FRA Decision 66/2026
- Official two-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/alamiria_2026_66.pdf
- Gazette issue 63 supplement (أ), 17 March 2026, printed pages 5–6; decision dated 26 February 2026.
- Amends Decision 11/2014, replacing Article 7 bis(7) and Article 8(2).
- SPAC/acquisition-company rule: complete the acquisition within two years from listing; acquire 100% or another percentage of target capital/voting rights; after acquisition the target may merge or remain as a continuing company/partly owned company according to the extraordinary general assembly resolution.
- Free-float rule: at least 10% of total shares or value equal to 1/8 per thousand of the Exchange free-float market cap, subject to a 5% minimum shareholding float; at least 300 shareholders.
- Effective 18 March 2026.
- Full record: [Decision 66/2026 Arabic text](agent3_fra_decision_66_2026_spac_acquisition_and_free_float_arabic_text.md).

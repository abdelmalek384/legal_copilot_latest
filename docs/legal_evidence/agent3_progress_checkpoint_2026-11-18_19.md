## New core capital-market acquisition — FRA Decision 26/2026
- Official 20-page Gazette PDF: https://www.fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/alamiria_2026_26.pdf
- Gazette issue 27 supplement (ج), 3 February 2026, printed pages 2–20.
- Amends FRA Board Decision 11/2014 on Egyptian Exchange listing and delisting rules.
- Captured the decision preamble and the operative amendment set: 19 replacement rule units under Article 1 and 9 added/new rule units under Article 2, plus transition and commencement clauses.
- Core amendments: cumulative voting; board nomination and remuneration committee; minority-shareholder definition; board self-evaluation and succession; major shareholder lock-ups; new listing pathway for recently established companies; stronger financial/accounting systems; auditor-change oversight; quarterly capital-increase use disclosure; insider dealing controls; independent valuation for material asset sales/acquisitions; capital changes and disclosure; delisting and minority exit protections.
- Three-month transition for Article 6(14) and new Article 37 bis requirements.
- Effective 4 February 2026.
- Full record: [Decision 26/2026 Arabic text](agent3_fra_decision_26_2026_listing_delisting_governance_arabic_text.md).

## Relationship to Decision 195/2026
- Decision 26/2026 is the broad February amendment to Decision 11/2014.
- Decision 195/2026 is a separate September amendment that replaces Article 38 and adds Articles 39 bis and 39 bis 1 on insider blackout and conflicts of interest.
- These must be read cumulatively; neither directly amends Companies Law 159/1981, although both cross-reference it and implement listed-company governance requirements.

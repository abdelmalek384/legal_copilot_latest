## Important legal and product implications
- The decision requires a formal licence from FRA for the listed key positions; these are not merely recommended corporate job titles.
- For companies licensed in multiple NBFI activities, FRA may permit one person to hold the same key position across activities, but where one managing director is shared, the company must appoint an executive director for each other activity.
- A vacancy in a required key role generally requires replacement within three months; the company must notify FRA and maintain an interim arrangement.
- Companies must maintain a key-position register, notify FRA of changes within 15 days, report the roster annually in January, retain supporting records for at least five years after employment ends, and maintain a board-approved succession plan.
- Decision 44/2026 separately requires regional risk and credit officers in its branch network framework. Decision 45/2026's Annex 1 and Annex 2 must be read with Decision 44/2026 as amended by Decision 100/2026.
- AML/CFT role eligibility cross-refers to the separate FRA AML/CFT registration rules; do not infer that the general seven-year requirement applies to that role.

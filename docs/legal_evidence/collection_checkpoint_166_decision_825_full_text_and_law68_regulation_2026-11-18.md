# Collection checkpoint 166 — Decision 825/1975 full-text recovery and Law 68 regulation amendment text
**Date:** 18 November 2026

## New substantive acquisition

### Decision 825/1975 — Executive Regulation of Law 142/1964
- Recovered a searchable Arabic full-text transcription for all **136 articles**, described by its publisher as text copied from the Court of Cassation legislation portal and updated through 6 February 2016.
- Cross-source metadata: Manshurat scan record identifies Decision 825/1975, Minister of Justice, dated 23 June 1975, published and effective 16 August 1975, Egyptian Gazette issue 189.
- Added working text for Articles 1–10, 11–22 (selected), and 126–129. These cover registry administration, legal/survey review, paper/digital work, office functions, conflicts of interest, application windows, public notice, first registration, settlement forms, survey maps, boundaries and preservation of register pages.
- The 136-article source is now a substantive text source, not merely a reference. It still needs complete article-by-article extraction, Gazette-image comparison, and amendment redlines.

### Amendment Decision 11264/2016
- Full searchable decision text located; published in Egyptian Gazette issue 30, 6 February 2017, signed 26 December 2016.
- Adds final paragraphs to Articles 3, 98 and 116 of Decision 825/1975: digital/paper processing; more than two change forms where required; and a note directing users to the underlying instrument for contractual conditions.
- Repeals the final paragraph of Article 11, Article 12 and the final paragraph of Article 13 of Decision 825/1975.
- This clarifies the version boundary and confirms that a raw 1975 text cannot be treated as the current regulation.

### Decision 1532/1976 — amendment to Law 68/1947 Executive Regulation
- Full text located in Laweg; Gazette issue 101, 1 May 1977; signed 20 September 1976.
- Adds a requirement to read foreign marriage certificates and adds Article 18 bis for deposit of sealed wills/post-mortem dispositions and foreign-notarized documents.
- Replaces regulatory Articles 5, 7 and 34, including capacity evidence, identity verification and certificates of date-proof/annotation.

2. Decision 825/1975 amendments 948/1976, 8171/2009, 2645/2015 and 11264/2016 require full redline. Correction: Decision 2332/2022 amends Decree 2/1946 (Law 114/1946 Executive Regulation), not Decision 825/1975.
3. Law 142 Article 37 scope has now been resolved from the full Case 42/17 judgment: only Article 37(2)'s prohibition on acquisitive prescription contrary to the register is invalid, and Article 38 fell consequentially. LAW HUB's shorthand that Article 37 is unconstitutional is overbroad. Separately, Case 10/35 (decided 2 June 2018; published 6 June 2018) invalidated Articles 21 and 24 and caused Article 23 and Decision 553/1976 to fall.

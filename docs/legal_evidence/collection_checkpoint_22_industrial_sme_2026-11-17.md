## 2. Industrial Licensing Facilitation Law 15/2017 and Executive Regulation 1082/2017

- IDA official index identifies both the Law and Executive Regulation.
- IDA official explanatory PDF:
  https://www.ida.gov.eg/uploads/files/pdfs/QR_PDF/about%20IDA%20%28arabic%29.pdf
- The IDA guide explains two industrial licensing routes:
  - Notification-based licensing for low-risk industrial activities.
  - Prior licensing for high-risk industrial activities.
- For low-risk activities, the guide states the IDA inspects and issues the operating licence within 20 days of receiving the application.
- For high-risk activities, inspection occurs within 30 days to confirm compliance before licensing.
- The framework recognizes registered accreditation offices to review requirements and issue certificates accepted by the IDA.
- The law provides facilitation for small and micro projects and a grievance committee chaired by a State Council judge.
- This guide is explanatory, not the statute. Retrieve the complete Law 15/2017 and Decision 1082/2017 original Gazette copies.

## 4. Unlicensed industrial establishments — Law 19/2023

- Industry Minister Decision 378/2024 expressly cites Law 19/2023 on regularizing the status of unlicensed industrial establishments.
- Law 19/2023 should be collected as a separate temporary/regularization statute with deadlines, eligibility and transitional provisions; do not treat it as part of Law 15/2017.
- Need official Gazette text, executive rules and subsequent extensions/decisions.

## 5. Industrial Development Authority Law 95/2018

- IDA official index lists Law 95/2018 issuing the Authority's statute.
- IDA guidance describes it as allowing serviced industrial land to be provided free or at nominal prices to manufacturers in development-priority regions by competent authority decision.
- Retrieve full Law 95/2018, Executive Regulation, amendment decisions, IDA board decisions and land allocation rules.

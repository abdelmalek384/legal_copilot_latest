# Research Agent 3 — FRA Chairman Decision 77/2019: standard financial-leasing and factoring contract templates

**Instrument type:** FRA Chairman decision approving mandatory minimum contract templates
**Decision date:** 12 July 2019 (as cited in preamble)
**Official FRA-hosted six-page Gazette/template PDF:** https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/UG45644_UG45647.pdf

## Exact Arabic operative text — decision

### ديباجة القرار

بعد الاطلاع على القانون رقم (10) لسنة 2009 بشأن تنظيم الرقابة على الأسواق والأدوات المالية غير المصرفية؛ وعلى قانون التأجير التمويلي والتخصيم رقم (176) لسنة 2018؛ وعلى قرار رئيس الجمهورية رقم (192) لسنة 2009 بإصدار النظام الأساسي للهيئة العامة للرقابة المالية؛ وعلى قرار مجلس إدارة الهيئة رقم (139) لسنة 2018 بشأن أحكام وإجراءات القيد في سجل عقود التأجير التمويلي؛ وعلى قرار مجلس إدارة الهيئة رقم (163) لسنة 2018 بشأن الضوابط التنفيذية لممارسة نشاط التخصيم؛ وعلى مذكرة الإدارة المركزية للإشراف والرقابة على شركات التمويل المؤرخة في 2019/7/12؛

### المادة الأولى

تحرر عقود التأجير التمويلي والتخصيم وفقًا للنماذج المرفقة، وتلتزم الشركات كحدود دنيا من البيانات الواردة بنماذج هذه العقود عند ممارستها للنشاط.

### المادة الثانية

ينشر هذا القرار في الوقائع المصرية وعلى الموقع الإلكتروني للهيئة ويعمل به من تاريخ صدوره.

## Attached template A — financial leasing contract (11 clauses)

### Clause 1 — preamble
يعتبر التمهيد السابق جزءًا لا يتجزأ من هذا العقد.

### Clause 2 — asset selection, delivery and liability
- If the lessee directly negotiates specifications with supplier/contractor, the lessee is responsible for errors in selecting/specifying the leased asset.
- Lessor remains responsible for its own acts that cause a mistake in asset selection or enable supplier/contractor/third parties to interfere with the lessee's use.
- From delivery, lessee bears civil/criminal liability for accidents/damage caused by the asset to third parties and damage caused to the asset during possession/use.

### Clause 3 — purchase option
للمستأجر الحق في شراء الأصل المؤجر محل التعاقد كله أو بعضه وفقًا للشروط المتفق عليها. ولا تنتقل ملكية الأصل المؤجر إلى المستأجر إلا بعد قيامه بسداد كامل الثمن والقيم الإيجارية المتفق عليها، ويلتزم المؤجر بتحرير عقد بيع الأصل المؤجر إلى المستأجر.

### Clause 4 — lessee obligations
- Use, maintain and repair the asset for its intended purpose and in accordance with technical standards/instructions.
- Notify lessor of defects preventing all or part of use.
- Pay rent on time.
- Do not dispose of the asset without written lessor consent; unauthorized disposal is void and lessor may recover the asset.
- Continue rent/payment where asset is destroyed due to lessee fault, taking insurance proceeds into account.

### Clause 5 — lessor obligations
- Deliver asset or authorize lessee to receive it under a signed delivery record describing condition, defects and identifying details.
- Do not interfere with lessee's possession/use.
- Notify lessee of assignments/transfers of rights under the contract.
- Preserve lessee data confidentiality, subject to FRA, judicial, regulatory, rating, credit-information, securitization and refinancing disclosures required by law.

### Clause 6 — asset marking and inspection
- Lessor marks ownership, contract number and registration date visibly on the asset; lessee may not conceal/remove the marking.
- Lessor may inspect periodically at the agreed monthly/quarterly/semiannual/annual interval.

### Clause 7 — assignment
- Lessor may assign the contract to another lessor, effective against lessee only after notice and without impairing lessee's rights/guarantees.
- Lessor may assign financial rights to another lessor, same-activity entity, local CBE-registered bank or securitization licensee.
- Lessee may assign the contract to another lessee with written lessor consent, subject to Article 24 of Law 176/2018.

### Clause 8 — automatic termination
- Non-payment despite reminder and passage of 30 days after due date, unless parties agree otherwise.
- Death of lessee or relevant joint-liability partner, subject to the model's 30-day continuation mechanism.
- Bankruptcy/insolvency or dissolution, subject to trustee/liquidator notice and continuation where permitted.
- Contract may include additional agreed termination events.
- Termination is recorded in the leasing-contract register; leased asset does not form part of lessee's general creditor security.

### Clause 9 — dispute resolution
تختص المحاكم الاقتصادية بنظر كافة المنازعات التي تنشأ نتيجة الإخلال بأي بند من بنود هذا العقد بما فيها منازعات التنفيذ الوقتية والموضوعية. ويستبدل بالنص التالي عند الاتفاق على التحكيم: يكون الاختصاص لهيئات التحكيم بالفصل في المنازعات الناشئة بين أطراف هذا العقد.

### Clause 10 — elected domicile and notices
- Contract addresses are elected domiciles; address changes must be notified by registered letter with acknowledgment; otherwise notices at the stated address remain effective.

### Clause 11 — copies
- Three copies: one for each party and a third for registration in the FRA financial-leasing contracts register.

## Attached template B — factoring contract (13 clauses)

### Clause 1 — preamble
يعتبر التمهيد السابق جزءًا لا يتجزأ من هذا العقد.

### Clause 2 — assignment/discounting of receivables
يوافق الطرف الأول المخصم على شراء الحقوق المالية (الحالية و/أو المستقبلية) موضوع هذا العقد والتي يمتلكها الطرف الثاني البائع والتي نشأت أو آلت إليه نتيجة ممارسته للنشاط، وتحدد الحقوق وشروط تخصيمها والخدمات المرتبطة بها في ملحق العقد.

### Clause 3 — transfer of rights
تنتقل الحقوق المالية المخصمة وفقًا لأحكام القانون المدني ومن خلال حوالة الحق أو الحلول الاتفاقي المرفق بالعقد، وتنتقل معها كافة الحقوق والضمانات التي يمتلكها البائع في مواجهة المدين.

### Clause 4 — seller warranties
- Seller warrants existence and due-date validity of receivables.
- Seller warrants accuracy/validity of contracts, transactions and documents issued by seller/debtors.
- Receivables must be commercial, arise from genuine commercial agreements/supply orders and be assignable.
- Receivables must be free from in rem/accessory rights for buyer or third parties, including retention, attachment, pledge or set-off.
- Information/documents supplied to factor must be accurate when provided.

### Clause 5 — term
- Contract has a specified start/end date and renews automatically for similar periods unless notice is given within the agreed notice period; factor may terminate immediately in specified agreed events.

### Clause 6 — finance limit, advance and charges
- Contract sets maximum financing, advance payment, discount/return, commissions and expenses. Preserve the signed form's blanks; do not infer values.

### Clause 7 — seller obligations
- Provide transaction/receivable information, supporting purchase orders, invoices, delivery notes and security documents.
- Reimburse/repay disputed receivables where seller breach causes debtor refusal; factor may pursue collection and legal action.
- Warrant receivables have not been previously assigned and not reassign or encumber them without written factor consent.
- Seller remains responsible to debtor for quality/specifications of goods/services under the underlying sale/service contract; factor is not responsible for those underlying obligations.
- Notify debtor of transfer of receivables; if seller fails to notify, factor may recourse against seller.

### Clause 8 — factor obligations
- Purchase receivables within agreed credit limits and after satisfying acceptance conditions.
- Maintain customer data confidentiality, subject to lawful disclosure to FRA, regulators, courts, rating agencies, credit-information companies, securitization/refinancing entities and financing institutions.
- Notify debtor if assigned factoring rights are transferred to another factor, with sufficient identifying details.

### Clause 9 — factor's recourse against seller
- Recourse where debtor non-payment is caused by seller's breach of commercial/contractual obligations.
- Recourse where receivable ceased/existed no longer before assignment, is extinguished, has priority/rights of another, is non-assignable or previously transferred.
- Recourse for seller's breach of contract/annexes; parties may specify additional cases.
- Seller warrants debtor has no defences or set-off rights against the receivable, subject to the contract.

### Clause 10 — assignment by factor
- Factor may assign the factoring contract or all/part of receivables to the entities specified in the contract, without impairing seller's contractual rights/guarantees.

### Clause 11 — dispute resolution
تختص المحاكم الاقتصادية بنظر كافة المنازعات التي تنشأ نتيجة الإخلال بأي بند من بنود هذا العقد بما فيها منازعات التنفيذ الوقتية والموضوعية. ويستبدل بالنص التالي عند الاتفاق على التحكيم: يكون الاختصاص لهيئات التحكيم بالفصل في المنازعات الناشئة بين أطراف هذا العقد.

### Clause 12 — elected domicile and notices
- Contract address is elected domicile; address changes must be notified by registered letter with acknowledgment; otherwise notices at stated address remain legally effective.

### Clause 13 — copies
- Two signed copies, one for each party.

## Legal significance and limitations
- The templates establish minimum data fields, not a complete substitute for negotiated contracts or mandatory statutory requirements under Law 176/2018.
- Leasing template includes asset description, ownership, rent, down payment, term, purchase option, maintenance, liability, assignment, termination, economic-court/arbitration clause and contract-register copy.
- Factoring template includes current/future receivables, assignment/subrogation, seller warranties, debtor notice, recourse, factor confidentiality, assignment, disputes and copies.
- Blank form fields are not substantive legal text. The exact arbitration alternative and optional clauses must be populated only where the parties agree and law permits.

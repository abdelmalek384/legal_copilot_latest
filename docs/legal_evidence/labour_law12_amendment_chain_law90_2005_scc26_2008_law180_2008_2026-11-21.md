### Operative scope

Article 1 of Law 90/2005 replaced Labour Law 12/2003 Articles **70, 71, 72 and 248**. Article 2 provided publication and commencement the day after publication.

| 2003 Law article | 2005 change | Later event |
|---|---|---|
| Article 70 | Individual labour dispute settlement: request to administrative authority within 7 days; 10-day settlement window; referral to judicial committee within 45 days of dispute; limitation wording | The Constitutional Court struck the 10-day/45-day referral-and-forfeiture phrase in Case 26/27; Law 180/2008 replaced Article 70 again |
| Article 71 | Created judicial labour committees of two judges plus labour administration, union and employer representatives; exclusive individual-dispute jurisdiction | Declared unconstitutional by Case 26/27 and replaced by ordinary labour courts under Law 180/2008 |
| Article 72 | Committee decision by majority, chair casting vote, enforceability as first-instance judgment and appeal route | Declared unconstitutional by Case 26/27 and replaced by ordinary appellate procedure under Law 180/2008 |
| Article 248 | Replaced employer penalty provision; fines EGP500–1,000 for listed breaches (including Articles 73(2), 74, 75, 89, 90, 99, 100, 101, 102), multiplied by affected workers and doubled on recidivism | Not among the provisions replaced by Law 180/2008; preserve the 2005 text in the historical consolidated version until Law 14/2025 repealed Law 12/2003 |

Do not treat the 2005 Article 70–72 committee regime as an operative current-law process. The Constitutional Court ruling below invalidated the core committee mechanism.

## 2. Constitutional Court Case 26/27 Judicial Year — invalidation of the 2005 committee regime

**Court:** Supreme Constitutional Court of Egypt.  
**Case:** القضية رقم ٢٦ لسنة ٢٧ قضائية دستورية.  
**Judgment date:** 13 January 2008.  
**Gazette publication:** الجريدة الرسمية، العدد ٤ مكرر، ٢٧ يناير ٢٠٠٨.  
**Official SCC source reproducing the dispositive holding and its effect:** https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/3234_30_14_6_2.html  
**Secondary legal reproduction:** https://ahmedmossa.com/ar/يتبع-في-الطعن-على-أحكام-المحاكم-العمال/  
**Additional secondary reproduction:** https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2021/11/72.html

### Holding / legal effect

The Court:
1. Declared unconstitutional Articles 71 and 72 of Labour Law 12/2003, as amended by Law 90/2005, because they assigned individual labour disputes to committees with judicial jurisdiction rather than the constitutionally competent ordinary courts.
2. Struck the phrase in Article 70 beginning with the 10-day failed-settlement condition and continuing through the 45-day committee-referral/forfeiture mechanism.
3. Struck Minister of Justice Decision 3539/2003 forming the five-member labour committees.
4. The judgment was published 27 January 2008; no different commencement date is identified in the reproduced holding. The ordinary-court system therefore became the operative route from the day after publication, subject to the judgment's treatment of final judgments and accrued procedural positions.

**Exact Arabic source excerpt — official SCC source (short excerpt):**

> بعدم دستورية نص المادتين (71) ، (72) من قانون العمل

### Consequence for the version graph

- The 2005 amendment did not remain fully operative until Law 180/2008.
- The unconstitutional Articles 71–72 and the specified Article 70 phrase must be tagged “invalidated by SCC Case 26/27, published 27 Jan 2008”, not simply “repealed by Law 180/2008”.
- The remainder of Law 90/2005, including its amendment to Article 248, was not invalidated by this holding.
- The prior judicial labour committees formed under Decision 3539/2003 lost their statutory basis. Do not model them as a current court or dispute-resolution body.

## 3. Law 180/2008 — replacement of Articles 70–72

**Arabic title:** قانون رقم ١٨٠ لسنة ٢٠٠٨ بتعديل بعض أحكام قانون العمل الصادر بالقانون رقم ١٢ لسنة ٢٠٠٣  
**Gazette:** الجريدة الرسمية، العدد ٢٥ مكرر، ٢٢ يونيو ٢٠٠٨.  
**Effective:** ٢٣ يونيو ٢٠٠٨.  
**Official Ministry of Labour PDF:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/1d3psheo/تعديل-قانون-العمل-بالقانون-رقم-180-لسنه-2008.pdf  
**Official Ministry of Finance catalogue:** https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-5fe869647bfc600007cbf35f  
**Secondary full-text record:** https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/22-06-2008/no-180?id=689934&sub=4499434&type=1  
**Additional combined text:** https://www.elsayyad.net/UploadFiles/LegislationDetails/2003-012r.pdf

### Operative changes

- Article 1 substituted Articles 70, 71 and 72 with the ordinary labour-court model:
  - Article 70: individual labour disputes and the administrative conciliation request; the new statutory window is 10 days to request conciliation, 21 days for conciliation, then referral/access to the Labour Court within 45 days from expiry of the conciliation period.
  - Article 71: one or more circuits of the Court of First Instance constitute the Labour Court; union and employer representatives are notified to express views; urgent termination applications and interim compensation receive special treatment.
  - Article 72: appeals against Labour Court judgments follow the Civil and Commercial Procedure Law.
- Article 2 replaced the term “committee” with “Labour Court” wherever it appeared in Law 12/2003.
- Article 3 required committees/courts to transfer pending disputes and cases that had become Labour Court jurisdiction, preserving the new jurisdictional arrangement.
- Article 248 remained in the 2005-amended form unless separately amended by another later instrument; no separate replacement by Law180/2008 is identified in the retrieved text.

### Law 125/2010 — separate priority-of-worker-rights statute

Law 125/2010 granted a privilege to amounts owed to workers arising from employment and prescribed a payment deadline in dissolution, liquidation, final closure or bankruptcy. Its Executive Regulation was issued by PM Decision 2736/2010, and Ministry of Manpower Decision 290/2010 issued implementing instructions. Law14/2025 issuance Article12 expressly repeals Law125/2010. Preserve Law125/2010, Decision2736/2010 and Decision290/2010 for historical insolvency/liquidation events and claims arising under the old regime; obtain Gazette copies and analyze accrued rights/transitional treatment before deciding whether any subordinate provision has residual effect.

### Newly acquired exact Arabic text — Law90/2005 Articles 71 and 72

The full reasoned judgment transcription at Legal Reform reproduces the challenged Law90/2005 wording in its reasons (lines 70–74). This supplies the missing article text while the original Law90 Gazette page remains outstanding.

**Article 71 opening (secondary full judgment transcription):**

> تشكل بقرار من وزير العدل بالاتفاق مع الجهات المعنية لجان ذات اختصاص قضائي من

**Article 72 opening (same source):**

> يصدر قرار اللجنة بأغلبية أعضائها الحاضرين

The full Article71 text describes a five-member committee with two judges and three non-judicial members (labour administration, union and employer representatives), exclusive jurisdiction over individual labour disputes, a 60-day decision period, special 15-day dismissal applications and interim compensation. Article72 governs majority decisions, chair casting vote, enforceability as a first-instance judgment and appeal to the appellate court. These are historical provisions only; the SCC invalidated them.

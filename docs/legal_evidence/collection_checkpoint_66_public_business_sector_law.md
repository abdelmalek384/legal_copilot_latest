## 1. Law 203/1991 — scope
- Governs public business sector holding companies and subsidiaries.
- Both company types take joint-stock company form; Companies Law 159/1981 applies where Law 203/1991 has no special rule and does not conflict.
- Public Sector Authorities and Companies Law 97/1983 does not apply to companies under Law 203/1991.
- Holding companies replace former public-sector authorities and subsidiaries replace supervised companies from commencement.
- Establishment decrees, legal personality, capital, board, general assembly, employee participation, financial management, auditors, transfer of ownership, mergers, liquidation and State ownership are governed by special provisions.

## 2. Executive Regulation Decision 1590/1991
- Promulgates Executive Regulation of Law 203/1991.
- Article 1: Companies Law 159/1981 Executive Regulation Decision 96/1982 applies to matters not specially regulated by Decision 1590/1991, insofar as not inconsistent.
- Regulation applies to holding/subsidiary companies established under Law 203/1991 and those replacing former public-sector authorities/companies.
- It covers establishment, share capital, boards, general assemblies, employee representatives, company bylaws, audit, financial statements, reserves, asset disposals, mergers, restructuring and liquidation.
- Full regulation text is accessible on FRA PDF and MKSEgypt transcription; full article-by-article extraction and amendments remain outstanding.

## 3. Law 185/2020 — major amendment
- Replaces Articles 3, 5 first paragraph, 8, 9, 16 first paragraph, 18, 19, 20 first paragraph, 21, 25, 32 fourth paragraph, 33, 34, 42, 48 and 55 of Law 203/1991.
- Reworks corporate governance and board composition, shareholder/State representation, appointment of board members, employee representation and company-management structures.
- Adds/revises provisions on performance evaluation, remuneration, general assemblies, board accountability and restructuring.
- The official 16-page Gazette PDF is searchable; exact replacement wording should be transcribed article by article before production use.

## 4. Cross-law relationships
- Companies Law 159/1981 and Executive Regulation 96/1982 apply as gap-filling law.
- Public Business Sector Law 203/1991 is distinct from Public Sector Authorities Law 97/1983.
- Capital Market Law 95/1992, Commercial Code 17/1999, Public Contracts Law 182/2018 and Unified Public Finance Law 6/2022 may apply to specific transactions/companies.
- Labour Law 14/2025 and Social Insurance Law 148/2019 govern employee relationships unless special provisions apply.
- State ownership and public assets rules, public procurement and audit legislation are important cross-references.

It is useful as an independent base-text lead and publication-metadata cross-check, not as a verbatim transcription source without image review.

The Notaries Union and LAW HUB texts are searchable and expose the full article sequence, but are secondary reproductions. Their text can bridge the missing Gazette page while retaining a provenance flag.

## 3. Commencement and promulgation cross-check

- The original Gazette page image (page 0 / printed page 311) shows Law 25/1968, the issuing provisions, and the Gazette header.
- Issuing Article 2 states that the law operates six months after publication.
- WIPO Lex records publication on 30 May 1968 and entry into force on 30 November 1968.
- The Ministry's PDF and Notaries Union transcription both show the six-month commencement rule.
- The apparent commencement is therefore **30 November 1968**.

### OCR discrepancy in issuing Article 1

The WIPO PDF OCR renders the repealed Civil Code chapter as “الباب الرابع”, while the Ministry page-image and Notaries Union transcription show “الباب السادس”. The WIPO text layer is therefore not safe to quote for that line. The image-backed Ministry PDF and Notaries transcription support “الباب السادس من الكتاب الأول من القسم الأول من القانون المدني” and “الباب السابع من الكتاب الأول من قانون المرافعات المدنية والتجارية”.

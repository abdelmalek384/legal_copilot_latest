The full consolidated text is available online but has not been downloaded, normalized or independently compared article-by-article with the primary Court of Cassation portal or Gazette pages.

## Amendment leads visible in the consolidated text
The secondary page's article footnotes identify these changes:
- **Decree-Law 48/2011:** replaces Article 5; Gazette issue 24 bis, 19 June 2011.
- **Law 184/2008:** adds Article 44 bis; Gazette issue 25 bis (A), 22 June 2008.
- **Law 91/2015:** repeals paragraph/item (1) of Article 44 bis; Gazette issue 30 supplement (A), 25 July 2015.
- **Law 137/2021:** adds Articles 27 bis and 33 bis; Gazette issue 32 bis (A), 15 August 2021.
- **Law 168/1998:** amends the third paragraph of Article 49; the exact Gazette issue/date and current text must be collated.
- The secondary text also cites Law 17/2007 in relation to age/retirement wording in Articles 14 and 24; its exact effect and any later changes need review.

These are amendment leads derived from secondary article footnotes, not independent primary-source verification. Do not use them as a complete amendment chain.

**Research date:** 18 November 2026  
**Base instrument:** Law 70/1964 on notarization and registration fees; Official Gazette issue 67, 22 March 1964.  
**Sources:** Egypt Notaries Union full text https://www.notariesofegypt.org/article.php?id=312 ; Al-Hasswa consolidated text https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-70-1964 ; Manshurat base-law scan catalogue https://manshurat.org/node/33668 . ## 2. Statutory amendment chain

| Instrument | Date / effective date | Effect on Law 70/1964 |
|---|---|---|
| Law 6/1991 | 13 Mar 1991; effective day after Gazette publication | Replaced Articles 21, 25, 26 and 27; amended Article 19; raised caps in Article 32; inserted Articles 24 bis, 34 bis and 34 bis(1); repealed Article 20 |
| Law 224/1996 | 14 Jul 1996; effective day after publication | Replaced first paragraph of Article 24 bis and Article 34 bis |
| Law 225/1996 | 14 Jul 1996; effective day after publication | Repealed Article 21(8) |
| Law 9/2003 | 1 Feb 2003; effective 2 Feb 2003 | Inserted Article 34 bis(2): additional 25% reduction on the relative fee, subject to its temporal and extension rules |
| Law 3/2004 | 26 Feb 2004; effective 27 Feb 2004 | Inserted Article 34 bis(3): additional one-third reduction after the prior reductions, subject to its temporal and extension rules |
| Law 83/2006 | Gazette issue 22 bis, 6 Jun 2006; effective 7 Aug 2006 | Capped registration-related charges at EGP 2,000 per transaction/subject/unit; capped fixed fees at EGP 30 per act and EGP 15 per document/page; replaced Article 19; also amended Law 142/1964 Article 30 |
| Justice Minister Decision 5424/2006 | effective 7 Aug 2006 | Set the implementing fee categories and schedules under Law 83/2006; includes fixed, preservation and relative fees |
| Law 163/2019 | issued/published 12 Dec 2019; effective 13 Dec 2019 | Added a Table B item for experience certificates and balance-sheet approvals of contracting companies for overseas work: 0.5%, maximum EGP 100,000 |
| Law 67/2021 | issued/published 13 Jun 2021; effective 14 Jun 2021 | Replaced the phrase “contracting companies” with “companies” in the relevant Table B item, extending the special treatment beyond contracting companies |

### Important interpretation from 2026
The General Assembly of the Fatwa and Legislation Sections of the State Council issued Fatwa 453/2026, file 37/2/964, at its sitting on 25 March 2026, on certification of company balance-sheet declarations under Law 70/1964 as amended by Laws 163/2019 and 67/2021.

A 10 June 2026 secondary report says the question concerned declarations bearing the wording “used inside and outside the country”. The report's reasoning at paragraphs around lines 42–45 says that such declarations are not within the special Table B cap because domestic use remains included, while the final sentence at line 46 says they are subject to the maximum cap. The reported reasoning and conclusion are internally inconsistent. **Do not encode this opinion as a settled rule until the original signed Fatwa 453/2026 is acquired and the inconsistency resolved.**

## 3. Constitutional Court effects

### SCC Case 10/16 — 18 May 2014
Official full judgment: https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/4946_16_10_1_2.html  
Published Official Gazette issue 21 bis (أ), 24 May 2014.

Operative effect:
- Article 21, first section, item (9) of Law 70/1964, as amended by Law 6/1991, was declared unconstitutional.
- Item (3) of Table 2 attached to Justice Minister Decision 2936/1992 fell consequentially.
- Do not mark all of Article 21 unconstitutional; the invalidation is limited to item (9) in the first section and the consequential schedule item.

### SCC Case 2/24 — 14 December 2003
Secondary full judgment transcription: https://qadaya.net/?p=12446  
Consolidated footnote also appears in https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-70-1964 . Gazette publication is reported as issue 52 bis, 31 December 2003.

Operative effect: Article 30 of Law 70/1964 was declared unconstitutional. The original SCC direct page and original Gazette judgment scan remain outstanding.

## 4. Annual vehicle valuation decisions — temporal versions

- Justice Minister Decision 278/2025, published in Egyptian Proceedings issue 39, 17 February 2025, set minimum vehicle values under Article 21(II) and stated the attached values apply through 31 December 2025. It is expired for 2026.
- Justice Minister Decision 7657/2025, issued 25 November 2025, applies the attached minimum vehicle values from 1 January through 31 December 2026. Official Amiri Press searchable text: https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=99IsDvXE8+o%3D . Secondary publication report: https://www.cairo24.com/2356386 .
- The attached 2026 vehicle schedule values have not yet been transcribed into the repository. Do not use the 2025 Decision 278 schedule for 2026.
- Decision 8073/2023 is cited as a predecessor in the 2025 decisions, but its direct text and exact schedule validity period remain unlocated.

## 5. Schedule and currentness status

- The 11-page secondary-hosted 1964 Gazette scan was visually inspected. It includes schedules A, B and C; schedule pages and tables have not been fully transcribed.
- Schedule A concerns instruments requiring registration; Schedule B concerns instruments not requiring registration; Schedule C includes civil-status and related instruments.
- Law 83/2006 and Decision 5424/2006 changed the fee structure and tables; Laws 163/2019 and 67/2021 later changed Table B.
- The original base-law fee amounts in Articles 1–4 are historical, not current tariffs.
- 2026 vehicle values under Decision 7657/2025 remain to be extracted from its attached table.
- The current schedule for all other categories and the 2026 Council of State Fatwa 453/2026 require authoritative source acquisition.

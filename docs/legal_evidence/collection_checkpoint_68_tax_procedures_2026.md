# Collection checkpoint 68 — Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020 and 2025–2026 tax administration updates

## 1. Law 206/2020 — official full-text PDF

- Official Egyptian Tax Authority PDF:
  https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.206-2020.pdf
- Official PDF: 43 pages, 876 extracted line references.
- Official ETA tax legislation index:
  https://www.eta.gov.eg/ar/content/qwanyn-aldrybt-ly-aldkhl
- The searchable PDF extraction is partially garbled due encoding; preserve the raw PDF and visually extract the Arabic text rather than relying on the text layer alone.
- Law 206/2020 establishes unified tax procedures and applies across taxes administered by the Egyptian Tax Authority, subject to exclusions/special statutes.
- The law repeals/updates several prior tax-procedure provisions and provides the framework for tax registration, returns, assessment, audit, records, electronic systems, objections, appeals, collection, penalties, limitation and tax disputes.
- Executive Regulation: PM Decision 286/2021.
- Obtain original Gazette PDF and compare the full text against subsequent amendments.

- Separate tax returns, e-invoice/e-receipt, electronic notices, tax-audit powers, information exchange, penalties, administrative settlement and judicial appeal as distinct workflows.

## 7. Critical procedural safeguards

- Verify the precise legal basis for electronic service and notices; do not infer service validity from an email/SMS without the statutory and regulatory conditions.
- Distinguish tax audit, tax assessment, objection, internal committee, tax appeal committee, settlement committee and court proceedings.
- Preserve filing deadlines, suspension/extension rules, proof of service and the exact version of the procedural law.
- Taxpayer data and financial records require purpose-limited access and confidentiality under applicable tax secrecy, CBE and Personal Data Protection rules.

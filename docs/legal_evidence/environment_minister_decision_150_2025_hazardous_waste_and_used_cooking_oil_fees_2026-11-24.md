## 2. Article 1 — annual approval fees for hazardous-material / hazardous-waste handling within facilities

The secondary transcription states that, in addition to applicable VAT, the fees for WMRA approval to handle hazardous materials and hazardous waste within a facility are:

### Healthcare facilities

| Facility type | Annual fee (EGP) |
|---|---:|
| Hospitals, 1–50 beds | 2,000 |
| Hospitals, 51–100 beds | 3,500 |
| Hospitals, 101–200 beds | 5,000 |
| Hospitals, 201 beds or more | 7,500 |
| Private clinics | 1,000 |
| Private dispensaries | 2,000 |
| Medical centres (diagnostic, oncology, dialysis, cosmetic, dental), each branch | 5,000 |
| Medical analysis laboratories, each branch | 5,000 |
| Pharmacies, each branch | 2,000 |
| Primary healthcare centres / health units (family medicine units) | 100, including online application fee |
| Charitable non-profit healthcare facilities | Exemption / fee treatment must be read with exact Gazette table layout |
| Government non-profit healthcare facilities | Exemption / fee treatment must be read with exact Gazette table layout |

**Caution:** The last two categories appear in the extracted table without a clearly associated amount in the available text. Do not assign them a zero fee until the Gazette table layout is checked.

### Projects by size

| Project category | Annual fee (EGP) |
|---|---:|
| Micro-enterprises | 250 |
| Small enterprises | 500 |
| Medium enterprises | 10,000 |
| Above-medium / large enterprises | 25,000 |

The source text describes the fee as the fee for issuance of the approval for one year. The fees are stated in addition to applicable VAT.

## 3. Article 2 — used cooking oil licensing and export permit fees

The secondary transcription states that, in addition to applicable VAT, WMRA fees for used cooking oil are:

| Annual quantity | Collection / transport / storage licence fee for one year (EGP) |
|---|---:|
| 1–10,000 litres | 7,500 |
| 10,001–30,000 litres | 20,000 |
| 30,001–50,000 litres | 35,000 |
| 50,001 litres or more | 50,000 |

Export permit: 1% of the value of the annual quantity, capped at EGP 200,000.

This Article 2 concerns used cooking oil, not all waste oils or all hazardous wastes. Do not conflate it with the separate export-fee provision in Environment Minister Decision 120/2025.

## 4. Article 3 — application fee

A contemporaneous independent news report describes Article 3 as imposing EGP 500 when an application is submitted for the first time or on renewal, for study of applications for licences, permits or approvals relating to integrated management activities for materials and waste.

The complete operative wording and precise application categories should be checked against the Gazette PDF before treating this as a production rule.

## 5. Article 4 — revenue destination

The contemporaneous news report states that the proceeds of the prescribed fees are credited to the Waste Management Regulatory Authority account, while VAT is remitted to the Egyptian Tax Authority. This remains a secondary-source summary pending direct article/page comparison.

## 6. Relationship to other WMRA fee decisions

- **Decision 113/2022:** foundational fee schedule for integrated waste-management licences, hazardous-waste approvals and related services. Official searchable text: https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=YdNQg7Ye2lQ%3D
- **Decision 120/2025:** inserts Article 3 bis into Decision 113/2022 and adds five categories, including export approvals and Basel notifications. It is a separate amendment and must not be merged with Decision 150/2025.
- **Decision 150/2025:** establishes facility-level hazardous-material/waste approval fees and used-cooking-oil activity fees; includes an application fee and revenue-allocation provisions.
- **Decision 279/2025:** later 2025 WMRA service-fee decision; an official Gazette PDF excerpt shows a separate EGP 2,000 fee for studying applications to amend existing licences/approvals/permits. This is a later instrument and requires a separate article-by-article capture/currentness analysis.

Do not infer that Decision 150/2025 repeals Decision 113/2022 or Decision 120/2025. The complete operative clauses and later amendments must be reviewed together.

## 7. Currentness and implementation cross-check lead

The WMRA online service page currently displays EGP 200,000 for an approval for safe disposal of hazardous waste. Decision 113/2022's searchable text also lists EGP 200,000 for issuance of safe-disposal approval. That apparent consistency does not establish the complete current fee schedule or whether Decision 150/2025's categories apply to that distinct service.

A later Cairo Governorate / Ahram Online government report dated 15 August 2026 says the national waste information system is being used for electronic licences, approvals and permits and that facilities are inspected before approvals. This is operational context, not a legislative amendment.

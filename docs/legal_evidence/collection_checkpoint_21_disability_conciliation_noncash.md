## 1. Rights of Persons with Disabilities Law 10/2018

- Official Gazette PDF: https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/qanwn_rqm_10_lsnt_2018_e755416bf4.pdf
- Gazette issue 7 bis (c), 19 February 2018.
- Searchable PDF text inspected (514 extracted lines).
- Promulgation:
  - Applies to rights of persons with disabilities and obligations under the attached law, including foreign persons with dwarfism residing in Egypt subject to reciprocity.
  - Repeals Law 39/1975 on rehabilitation of persons with disabilities and conflicting provisions.
  - Executive Regulation to be issued by Prime Minister within six months; entities were required to regularize within one year from commencement.
  - Effective the day after Gazette publication.
- Attached law, Articles 1–3:
  - Aims to protect rights, secure full enjoyment of human rights/fundamental freedoms on an equal basis, dignity, inclusion and a decent life.
  - Defines a person with disability by a stable total/partial physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairment that, in interaction with barriers, prevents full/effective participation on an equal basis.
  - Defines National Council for Persons with Disabilities, impairment, dwarfism (height not exceeding 140 cm after puberty), monitoring, empowerment, social protection, care, communication, disability discrimination, accessibility, reasonable accommodation, inclusion and rehabilitation.
- Article 5 (as defined in Article 3) identifies the disability and integrated-services card as the sole approved means of proving disability, its type and degree, and as a means to access public services/benefits.
- The rest of the 514-line PDF contains detailed rights and duties; article-level extraction and Executive Regulation 2733/2018 must be linked and reconciled.
- Official Ministry of Finance record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-67863067a19d140007b8606e
- Official NCPD copy: https://ncpd.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/10_21.pdf

## 2. Law 7/2000 — conciliation committees for disputes involving public entities

- Official Gazette PDF hosted by Ministry of Petroleum: https://www.petroleum.gov.eg/ar-eg/investment/rules-decisions/LawList/قانون%207%20لجان%20فض%20المنازعات.pdf
- Gazette issue 13 bis, 4 April 2000.
- Searchable full text inspected (88 extracted lines).
- Article 1 establishes one or more committees in each ministry, governorate, public authority and other public legal person to conciliate civil, commercial and administrative disputes between these entities and their employees or private persons/legal persons.
- Article 2 provides for committee composition: chair from current/former judicial members at adviser grade or above, administrative representative at director-general grade or above, and the other party/representative.
- Article 4 excludes disputes involving Ministry of Defence/Armed Forces, real rights in immovable property, matters governed by special systems or mandatory judicial/administrative committees, and disputes agreed to arbitration; access is free.
- Article 8 requires full committee attendance; recommendations are by majority, deliberations confidential.
- Article 9: committee recommendation due within 60 days; recommendation is sent within 7 days; if approved by competent authority and accepted in writing by other party within 15 days, record is an enforceable instrument.
- Article 10: if recommendation rejected/not accepted or not issued within 60 days, parties may litigate; submission suspends limitation/forfeiture and filing periods until statutory milestones.
- Article 11: subject to exceptions (urgent matters, execution disputes, provisional orders, payment orders and annulment challenges with stay requests), initial court claims in covered disputes are inadmissible unless prior committee request is filed and statutory period has elapsed.
- Article 14: effective 1 October 2000.
- Important procedural application must be checked against current constitutional rulings, amendments and scope exceptions.

## 3. Non-Cash Payment Law 18/2019 — Executive Regulation PM Decision 1776/2020

- Executive Regulation Gazette PDF: https://serviceapi.egyptianlaws.com/Images/Index?T=1&folder=10&path=2020-9-7-2020-1776.pdf
- Gazette issue 36 bis (z), 7 September 2020.
- Searchable text inspected (232 extracted lines).
- Decision 1776/2020 promulgates the Executive Regulation of Law 18/2019 and takes effect the day after publication.
- Article 1 defines non-cash payment as methods resulting in credit to a beneficiary's bank account, including deposits, transfers, debits, credit/debit cards, mobile payments and other methods approved by CBE Governor.
- Article 2:
  - State authorities/public legal persons and companies wholly/majority owned by State must pay financial entitlements to members, employees, experts, board/committee members and social-insurance contributions by non-cash means, except overseas travel allowances.
  - Private legal persons/establishments must pay employee/expert/board/committee entitlements and social-insurance contributions non-cash when they have more than 25 employees or monthly payroll exceeds EGP 100,000.
- Article 3 lists payment categories subject to maximum cash thresholds, including supplier/contractor/service-provider payments, cash financing, company/investment-fund profit distributions, union member entitlements, private insurance benefits/compensation, charitable aid/donations and real-estate/vehicle purchase/lease consideration.
- Article 4 requires entities providing public services or operating public utilities to provide non-cash acceptance methods at all service-collection outlets without additional cost, including universities/private schools, electricity, water, natural gas, fuel stations, telecom and air/land/sea/metro/technology-enabled passenger transport.
- Article 5 prescribes thresholds for non-cash collection of taxes/customs/fees/fines, government-service claims, tuition, electricity, water, gas, telecom, fuel, air/sea transport and metro subscriptions.
- Note: the extracted table in Article 3 has a missing/corrupted numeric threshold in one row; inspect original Gazette page image before using that threshold. Do not infer it.
- Need acquire the full base Law 18/2019, amendments, CBE decisions and current thresholds/implementation rules. Thresholds may be amended and should not be treated as current solely from the 2020 regulation.

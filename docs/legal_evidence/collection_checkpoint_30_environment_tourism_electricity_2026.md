# Collection checkpoint 30 — nature reserves, Nile protection, tourism facilities and electricity regulation (2026-11-17)

## 1. Nature Reserves Law 102/1983 — official EEAA full text

- Official EEAA PDF:
  https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/Uploads/Laws/Files/20221017140256340.pdf
- Official EEAA "other laws" index:
  https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/Laws/57/index
- Search-indexed Arabic full text is available.
- Article 1 defines a natural reserve as a land or coastal/inland water area with living organisms or natural phenomena of cultural, scientific, tourism or aesthetic value, designated by PM decision on EEAA proposal.
- Article 2 prohibits acts that destroy/damage/degrade natural environments or harm wildlife, marine life or plants. Prohibited acts include hunting, capture, transport or disturbance of wild/marine organisms; removing shells/coral/rocks/soil; destroying or removing plants; damaging geological formations or habitats; introducing alien species; and polluting soil, water or air in the reserve.
- Construction, roads, vehicle use and agricultural/industrial/commercial activities inside a reserve require permission from the competent authority under PM-set rules.
- EEAA reports Law 102/1983 provides for a Protectorates Fund; later environmental institutional changes must be checked before using old references to the fund.
- Extract all remaining articles, penalties, enforcement, reserve designation decisions and amendments. Do not rely only on the first two provisions.

## 2. Nile River and Watercourses Protection Law 48/1982

- EEAA official "other laws" index identifies Law 48/1982 and links to the Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation:
  https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/Laws/57/index
- Official Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation remains the primary source to obtain the statute and Executive Regulation.
- Historical EEAA explanatory material identifies the law as protecting fresh and non-fresh water surfaces, including the Nile and branches, major canals, drains and lakes:
  https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/Uploads/Reports/Files/Matrooh%20Des.pdf
- EEAA states the law restricts discharge of solid/liquid/gaseous waste from buildings and commercial/industrial/tourism facilities into watercourses without Ministry of Irrigation permit and compliance with prescribed standards.
- Environment Law 4/1994 strengthens penalties for violations of Law 48/1982, according to EEAA explanatory material.
- A separate older executive instrument, Minister of Irrigation Decision 38/1983, is identified as the original implementing regulation in EEAA national disaster-plan material; currentness and amendments must be checked.
- Justice Minister Decision 4804/2025 grants named Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation staff judicial-officer status for violations of Law 48/1982 and Water Resources Law 147/2021:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=SqFQdTy7On0%3D
  The 2025 decision states validity through 31 August 2026; do not assume it remains effective after that date without a renewal.

## 4. Electricity Law 87/2015 and enforcement decisions

- Electricity Law 87/2015 is a separate sectoral statute; obtain official Gazette text, Executive Regulation and Electricity Regulatory and Consumer Protection Agency decisions.
- Justice Minister Decision 961/2025:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=gvD2u3vcNUs%3D
- Decision 961/2025 grants named personnel of the Electricity Regulatory and Consumer Protection Agency judicial-officer status for offences under Law 87/2015.
- It revokes earlier Decisions 3606/2018 and 8626/2019.
- Validity is stated through 28 February 2027.
- This is an enforcement/competence decision, not an amendment to the Electricity Law.
- Link electricity legislation to energy planning, licensing, grid codes, consumer protection, renewable energy, environment and public utility regulation.

## Actual text inspected in secondary transcription (selected article-level provisions)

### Promulgation articles
- Article 1: applies attached Executive Regulation to Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020.
- Article 2: annual income-tax return payment is calculated after deducting advance payments already paid and interest on advance payments, computed using CBE discount/credit rate on 1 January before the relevant year and period held.
- Article 3: repeals listed provisions of the Stamp Tax Executive Regulation 525/2006, Income Tax Executive Regulation 991/2005 and VAT Executive Regulation 66/2017.
- Article 4: preserves Income Tax Executive Regulation Articles 99 bis (1)–(4) until Finance Minister/delegate decides the e-invoice system is complete.
- Article 5: published in Al-Waqa'i and effective the day after publication.

### General rights, electronic notices and confidentiality
- Article 2: notices/announcements filed by taxpayers through ETA portal count as filed with competent office; non-cash payment counts as payment to competent office.
- Article 3: ETA may accept tax data, records and documents in any language, but may require translation into Arabic by accredited office/entity.
- Articles 4–5: public tax-law awareness; taxpayers may obtain free forms, circulars, instructions and manuals.
- Articles 6–7: taxpayer/rightful representative may inspect tax file within three working days; includes registration data, inspection/interview records, audit memorandum, notices, assessment and collection forms, seizure records; copies may be requested.
- Article 8: ETA must respond in writing by traditional/electronic means to inquiries about tax status.
- Article 9: ETA must preserve confidentiality; disclosure only within statutory exceptions.
- Article 10: field tax audit generally occurs in taxpayer/representative presence after notice, subject to exceptions under Law 206/2020.

### Transfer pricing and related parties
- Article 14 lists controlled transactions including goods/services, assets, reimbursements, royalties, loans/credit facilities, securities, contracts and intangible assets.
- If taxpayer fails to provide required documentation, ETA may set transfer-pricing rules based on available information; taxpayer may challenge and bears proof burden.
- Exemption threshold for related-party transactions is calculated on gross revenue/expenses during the tax year, not net transactions.
- Articles 15–20 deal with master file/local file/country-by-country reporting, non-disclosure payments, deadlines and the Minister's guidance.

### Registration and tax cards
- Article 24: registration application by natural/legal person with prescribed forms and supporting documents, including ID/passport, tax card, incorporation documents, commercial register, lease/title, importer/exporter card, power of attorney and professional syndicate registration where applicable.
- Incomplete registration application: notice to complete within 15 days.
- If taxpayer does not submit application, competent office may register based on available data and notify.
- Article 25: tax card issued within five working days of complete application.
- Article 26 lists card data: tax registration number, office code, name/address/activity, business address, social insurance number, commercial register/professional licence, branches/warehouses, start date, legal form, return/invoice details, exemptions, advance-payment status, issue/expiry dates.
- Article 29: tax card/registration certificate valid five years and renewable.

### VAT, payroll and electronic invoices
- Article 30: VAT return filed within month after each tax period with payment; supporting sales/purchase invoice data submitted electronically; electronic return without invoice data is not relied upon.
- Article 31: quarterly payroll-tax return through ETA portal/other electronic channel; employer is responsible for data; annual settlement return also required.
- Articles 37–41: service-provider licence for e-invoice system, including Egyptian joint-stock company wholly owned by Egyptians, fees, technical conditions, digital seal security, SLA, financial guarantee, financial statements and registration. Licence lasts three years; renewal application at least three months before expiry; ETA may revoke for breach, bankruptcy, non-compliance or repeated warnings; grievance within 30 days.
- Article 42: e-invoice/e-receipt data fields include unified product/service code, CBE exchange rate for foreign-currency invoice, buyer type, activity/branch code, buyer ID/passport above prescribed threshold, UUID, quantity, tax types, QR code; e-receipt also includes POS serial, payment method, refund/utility reference; professional receipt includes provider identity, national ID, syndicate number, customer identity, service date/type/value and table tax.
- Article 43: e-invoice format, codes, branch ID, buyer registration/national ID and electronic signature requirements.

### Assessment, limitation, collection and execution
- Article 50: ETA may adjust a return where tax differs; if no return or invalid return, ETA estimates based on available data; additional undisclosed revenue triggers adjusted notices.
- Article 51: limitation is interrupted by notice of assessment elements, demand for payment, referral to appeal committees, and Civil Code causes such as judicial claim, seizure, creditor claim in bankruptcy and express/implied taxpayer acknowledgment.
- Article 52: unpaid tax, delay consideration, additional tax and other sums collected under enforceable demand form.
- Article 53: execution seizure order after tax is due, normally after warning unless tax debt collection is at risk.
- Article 54: administrative seizure rules require fair market valuation and seizure limited to enough assets to cover debt; business inventory only seized where other assets insufficient; garnishment of third-party receivables/bank accounts is controlled.
- Article 55: provisional seizure amount may not exceed 1.5 times estimated tax debt/other amounts.
- Article 56: setoff requires taxpayer credit final and undisputed and tax debt payable; tax debts are set off first, then other Finance Ministry revenue debts.
- Article 57: taxpayer may request clearance certificate; ETA issues within 40 days.
- Article 58: forced execution must leave taxpayer/heirs income not below income-tax zero-rate bracket, calculated using CBE credit/discount rate at execution date.
- Article 60: taxpayer may challenge assessment forms within 30 days of knowledge; failure after proof of notice makes assessment final.
- These excerpts are not the full 838-line regulation; use the complete source and Gazette version for legal advice.

## Amendment graph found in the transcription/official indexes

- Decision 286/2021 base Executive Regulation.
- Decision 188/2022 adds Article 23 bis on post-employment conflicts and restricted handling of former tax files for five years.
- Decision 20/2023 amends Article 31 payroll return.
- Decision 42/2023 amends Article 50 notice/adjustment paragraphs and Article 52 collection form.
- Decision 188/2023 amends Article 42 invoice/receipt fields.
- Decision 518/2023 adds Article 52 bis on VAT payment in foreign currency.
- Decision 538/2023 repeals a paragraph of Article 52 bis.
- Decision 309/2023 is officially listed by Ministry as amending the regulation (retrieve its exact affected articles).
- Decision 300/2025 is a later amendment lead (Eastlaws listing; official Gazette PDF still required).
- Do not assume the amendment list is complete; inspect all ministerial decisions and current ETA forms.

# Collection checkpoint 168 — Law 114/1946 Executive Regulation text recovered; correction of 825/1975 attribution
**Date:** 18 November 2026

## Major new full-text acquisition

### Decree 2/1946 — Executive Regulation of Law 114/1946
- Located a full searchable Arabic consolidated page with 38 numbered provisions at LAW HUB, https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12044. Manshurat scan catalogue exists at https://manshurat.org/node/72161.
- The searchable text includes amendments/annotations through Decision 2332/2022, including decisions 948/1976, 3003/1979, 9310/2020 and 2332/2022.
- Captured substantive Arabic wording for Articles 1–7; 3 bis; the 7 bis, 7 bis 1, 7 bis 2 and 7 bis 3 series; Articles 10, 11, 13, 14, 14 bis, 16, 17, 19, 20, 21 bis, 21 bis (A), 21 bis (B), 32 bis and 33.
- New Article 7 bis procedures include receipt timestamp/number, first-in-time priority within 24 hours, SMS notifications and handling of earlier pending applications. Article 7 bis 1 allows electronic submission. Article 14 bis provides the possession/factual-event registration process, public notice, objections, site inspection and decision workflow. Articles 21 bis series establish temporary/final numbers and objection procedures for registration of judgments. Article 32 bis sets a 37-day processing/decision-or-refusal period.
- Decision 2332/2022 also repeals regulatory Articles 8, 9 and 12; modifies digital/physical map provisions and registry records.
- Decision 2332/2022 official DSpace metadata identifies Egyptian Proceedings issue 75 supplement (ج), 30 March 2022. Original DSpace PDF remains request-only; the searchable text is secondary and requires Gazette page-image verification.

## Critical correction to the instrument graph

A previous acquisition row misattributed Decision 825/1975 to Law 114/1946. This is incorrect:
- **Decision 825/1975 is the Executive Regulation of Law 142/1964** (real-estate register system), 136 articles; its full searchable text was acquired in checkpoint 166.
- **Decree 2/1946 is the Executive Regulation of Law 114/1946** (real-estate publicity), 38 numbered provisions; its full searchable consolidated text is newly acquired here.
- **Decision 2332/2022 amends Decree 2/1946**, not Decision 825/1975.
- Decision 948/1976 also amends the Law114/1946 Decree 2/1946 regulation. Decision 11264/2016 amends the Law142/1964 Decision 825/1975 regulation.
- The acquisition register has been corrected: EG-0148 now links Decision 825/1975 to Law142/1964; new EG-0246 records Decree 2/1946 under Law114/1946. New inventory record FT-0031 added.

### Core law and Law 90/2025 changes
- Governs eligibility to practise pharmacy, registration, training, examination of foreign qualifications, licensing and management of pharmacies, pharmaceutical establishments, manufacture, compounding, storage and distribution.
- Law 90/2025 replaces Articles 2, 3, 5, Article 19 paragraph 1 and Article 37; repeals Articles 4, 24 and 87.
- Article 2 now requires pharmacy degree from Egyptian university or equivalent degree approved by Supreme Council of Universities, and one year compulsory training in approved hospitals/pharmacies/training units, under the specified accreditation route.
- Article 3 provides monthly training allowance of EGP 2,500 (may be increased by competent minister) during compulsory training; exact amended text and implementation decision must be transcribed.
- Article 5 addresses registration/examination and recognition of foreign qualifications.
- Article 19 paragraph 1 changes conditions for opening/operating pharmaceutical establishments.
- Article 37 changes statutory rules for pharmacy management/ownership (extract exact final wording from Gazette).
- The 2025 amendment creates a significant currentness change; old Law 127 text cannot be used without applying it.

### Core provisions
- Article 1 prohibits organ/part/ tissue transplant except in accordance with the law, Executive Regulation and implementing decisions.
- Article 2 permits transfer from living donor only where necessary to preserve recipient life or treat serious illness, and transfer is the only means; it must not expose donor to grave risk.
- Article 3 prohibits transplant from Egyptian donors to foreigners except spouses where one is Egyptian and marriage has lasted at least three years and is officially documented; other narrowly defined exceptions for children of Egyptian mother/foreign father and same-nationality foreigners under statutory controls.
- Article 5 requires free, informed, written consent; children and incapacitated persons cannot donate, subject to limited stem-cell exceptions to parents/children/siblings where no other donor exists and statutory consent requirements are met.
- Article 6 prohibits trading in human organs/tissues for sale or purchase or any material consideration; donor/heirs cannot receive material or in-kind benefit.
- Article 7 requires a tripartite committee to explain nature/risks and obtain consent before procedure.
- Article 15 requires licensed transplant facilities to have a responsible medical transplant team.
- Article 16 onward prescribes criminal penalties, including aggravated cases and legal-person liability.
- Article 26 repeals specified Penal Code paragraphs while preserving Blood Collection/Storage/Distribution Law 178/1960 and Eye Banks Law 103/1962.
- Executive Regulation 93/2011 governs committees, facilities, donor consent, medical criteria, transplant approvals, records and oversight.

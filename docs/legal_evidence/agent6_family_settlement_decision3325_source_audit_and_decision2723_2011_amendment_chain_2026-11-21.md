## New source-quality improvement for Decision 3325/2004
A full 14-article Arabic reproduction was independently found in a UNDP Arabic annex PDF, labelled as a copy of Justice Minister Decision 3325/2004, Egyptian Proceedings issue 154, 11 July 2004. The text-layer transcription includes all Articles 1–14.
Source (PDF): https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2024-12/undp_annex_arabic.pdf
Relevant PDF text begins page/lines in source extraction around L2–L55.
This provides a separate reproduction/provenance check against the secondary text already in the repo; it is not an original Gazette page image and does not constitute official authentication.
Key clauses confirmed in the new independent reproduction:
- Arts 1–3: office composition and settlement panels; chief's administration duties.
- Arts 4–6: request format, required personal/family/dispute data, same-day registration and assignment.
- Arts 7–9: notice, attendance, and settlement counseling.
- Arts 10–11: settlement record/executive-form procedure; unsuccessful settlement record and reports forwarded within seven days after request.
- Art 12: separate register for court-referred disputes.
- Arts 13–14: implementation and publication/effective date.

## Decision 6383/2011 — amendment text lead, not full instrument
LawEgypt transcription:
https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?ItemID=69779&NID=87203&Type=6&action=ViewActivePages
The reproduced operative text states, in substance and part verbatim:
(المادة الرابعة) يُعدل قرار وزير العدل رقم 2723 لسنة 2004 بشأن الإدارة العامة لمكاتب تسوية المنازعات الأسرية، وذلك بإلغاء المادتين الأولى والثالثة، وتلحق الإدارة المذكورة - باعتبارها إدارة فرعية - بإدارة المحاكم المختصة وتمارس اختصاصاتها الواردة بقرار وزير العدل المذكور فيما لا يتعارض مع أحكام هذا القرار.
The source page is headed by Decision 6383/2011 and cites Decision 10960/2007. The original Gazette issue and complete decision 6383/2011 remain unacquired. This excerpt is a clear structural amendment to Decision 2723/2004: Arts 1 and 3 repealed, administration subordinated to specialized-courts administration; preserve remaining compatible functions.

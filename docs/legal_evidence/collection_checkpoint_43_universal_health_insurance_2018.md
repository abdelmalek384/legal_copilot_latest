## 1. Law 2/2018 — scope and institutions
- Mandatory application to all citizens resident in Egypt; optional for Egyptians working abroad and their families abroad; foreign residents covered according to law.
- Geographic implementation is phased by governorate according to the statutory schedule and PM decisions, subject to financial sustainability and actuarial balance.
- State continues to provide public health, preventive, emergency, family-planning, disaster/epidemic services and specified free services for those unable to pay outside the insured package.
- Establishes:
  1. General Authority for Comprehensive Health Insurance (financing/purchasing).
  2. General Authority for Healthcare (service provision).
  3. General Authority for Healthcare Accreditation and Regulation (quality/accreditation).
- Core coverage includes health-care services for illness and work injuries across primary, secondary and tertiary care, subject to statutory exclusions and service lists.
- Insured person may choose contracted provider within referral levels; Authority finances care through contracts and approved prices/quality standards.
- Establishes permanent medical-service pricing committee and regulates contracts with public/private providers.
- Provides for transition of assets/personnel from existing health-insurance and Health Ministry entities in phased governorates.

## 2. Contributions and financing
- Law sets employer/employee contributions, contributions for pensioners and other categories, Treasury contributions for persons unable to pay, and additional sources of finance.
- Contributions are linked to wages/income and are collected with social-insurance contributions for covered workers.
- The system is financed through earmarked contributions, Treasury allocations, co-payments and other statutory sources; exact rates/ceilings and exemptions must be extracted from current text and subsequent decisions.
- Article 41 requires employers to remit the relevant employer and employee shares to the National Organization for Social Insurance on the same timetable as social-insurance contributions, for onward transfer to the Universal Health Insurance Authority.

## 3. Executive Regulation Decision 909/2018
- Defines the system, Authority, healthcare provider, accreditation/regulation authority, primary/secondary/tertiary care, insured person, dependents, inability to pay and other terms.
- Establishes operational procedures for enrolment, registration, family files, contribution calculation/collection, exemption/inability-to-pay verification, benefit packages, referral, provider contracting, service pricing, quality, complaints and appeals.
- Detailed eligibility, family-member rules, contribution calculations and provider payment arrangements require article-level extraction.

## 4. Implementation and related instruments
- Geographic rollout is phased; collect each PM decision designating start date/operational commencement for each governorate, and separate administrative preparation from actual system operation.
- Link Law 2/2018 to Law 148/2019 Social Insurance and Pensions (contribution collection), Law 151/2026 Income Tax amendment (comprehensive health-insurance contribution reference), Public Procurement Law 182/2018 and Unified Procurement Authority Law 151/2019.
- Health-sector standards and accreditation decisions issued by GAHAR and the healthcare authority must be classified separately from statute/regulation.

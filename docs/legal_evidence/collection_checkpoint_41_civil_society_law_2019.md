## 1. Law 149/2019 — scope and transition
- Regulates practice of civil-society work by associations, foundations, regional/foreign NGOs and unions, subject to special legal regimes and international agreements.
- Promulgation requires covered entities to align their bylaws, activities and funding with Constitution, Egyptian laws and ratified international agreements.
- Repeals NGO Law 84/2002 and conflicting provisions.
- Effective 20 August 2019 (day after publication).
- Core structure:
  1. Definitions.
  2. Associations: establishment, objectives, rights/obligations, general assembly, board, suspension/dissolution.
  3. Public-benefit associations.
  4. Civil foundations.
  5. Foreign NGOs.
  6. Central Unit for Associations and Civil Work and its sub-units.
  7. Fund for Supporting Projects of Associations and Civil Foundations.
  8. General, regional and specialized federations.
  9. Volunteering.
  10. Penalties.
- Article 2: association is established by notification to administrative authority with required documents; legal personality arises upon notification, subject to completeness.
- Other core provisions address domestic/foreign funding, project notification/approval, governance, accounting, reporting, inspection, public-benefit status, branches and dissolution.
- Full article-level extraction is still required; do not rely on a summary for funding/approval advice.

## 2. Executive Regulation Decision 104/2021
- Promulgates the attached Executive Regulation and repeals the former Executive Regulation under Minister of Insurance and Social Affairs Decision 178/2002.
- The regulation has 188 articles.
- Official explanatory summaries state that existing entities were given one year from commencement to regularize their status; administrative units must maintain paper/electronic records of regularization notifications.
- Regulation covers association notification/registration, statutes, founders, boards/general assemblies, governance, foreign funding, grants, fundraising, project activity, branch operations, financial records, inspection, public-benefit status, foreign NGOs, federations, volunteering and the supporting fund.
- Exact article-by-article extraction and currentness check remain outstanding.

## 3. Related legal and administrative decisions
- PM Decision 702/2021 delegates to Minister of Social Solidarity certain PM powers under Articles 55/56 of Law 149/2019.
- Ministry of Social Solidarity Decision 27/2024 confers public-benefit status on named associations/foundations; example of individual administrative acts under the statute.
- Numerous registration, public-benefit, funding and permit decisions are published in Al-Waqa'i and Al-Amiriya; classify individual entity decisions separately from general rules.
- Law 149/2019 must be linked to FRA Law 10/2009, AML/CFT rules, tax law, Labour Law and applicable foreign funding rules.

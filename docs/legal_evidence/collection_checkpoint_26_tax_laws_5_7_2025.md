# Collection checkpoint 26 — Laws 5/2025 and 7/2025: tax regularization and Unified Tax Procedures amendments

**Primary source:** Egyptian Tax Authority official PDFs  
- Law 5/2025: https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2025-02/law_no.5.of_.2025.pdf  
- Law 7/2025: https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2025-02/law_no.7.of_.2025.pdf

**Gazette:** issue 6 bis (w), 12 February 2025. Both PDFs were fully text-extracted and inspected.

## 1. Law 7/2025 — amendments to Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020

- PDF: 2 pages; 39 extracted lines.
- Adds Articles 45 bis, 75 bis and 75 bis 1 to Law 206/2020.

**Article 45 bis — cap on additional tax/delay consideration**
- In applying tax laws, delay consideration or additional tax may not exceed 100% of the original tax amount on which the delay consideration/additional tax is due.

**Article 75 bis — settlement of specified tax-law crimes**
- Minister or delegate may settle offences under the Unified Tax Procedures Law or tax law where the underlying obligation is not itself a tax due, by paying compensation not less than half and not more than twice the minimum fine provided by law, before criminal proceedings are brought.
- If the compensation paid equals the minimum fine, the right to bring criminal proceedings is extinguished.
- After a judgment on the merits but before it becomes final, settlement may be made by paying compensation equal to three times the minimum fine.
- After a final judgment, settlement may be made by paying compensation equal to four times the minimum fine, not exceeding the maximum fine.
- Payment is made to the Authority's treasury or to a person authorized by the Minister.

**Article 75 bis 1 — settlement for Article 135 of Income Tax Law 91/2005**
- Settlement by paying compensation equal to:
  1. 12.5% of amounts that should have been deducted/withheld but were not.
  2. 12.5% of amounts deducted/withheld/collected but not remitted.
- Plus the principal amounts and delay consideration.

- Article 2: effective the day after publication.
- Cross-links: Law 206/2020, Income Tax Law 91/2005 Article 135, criminal procedure and fine/settlement rules.

## 2. Law 5/2025 — settlement of positions of certain taxpayers and tax-liable persons

- PDF: 5 pages; 124 extracted lines.
- Article 1 definitions: Tax Authority; tax law includes Income Tax, VAT, Stamp Tax or State Financial Resource Development Fee.
- Article 2 — unregistered persons:
  - No tax accounting for prior periods before the law's effective date for persons not registered with the Tax Authority in relation to income tax, VAT, stamp tax or State Financial Resource Development Fee.
  - For purposes of Income Tax Law 91/2005 and VAT Law 67/2016, the date of commencement of activity is treated as the law's effective date, subject to conditions:
    1. Submit registration application (for income tax and VAT where registration is legally required) within three months from effective date; Finance Minister may extend once.
    2. No prior enforcement/administrative action taken by the Tax Authority against the applicant before the law's effective date.
    3. Submit all documents needed for registration in the Authority's electronic systems according to mandatory phases.
- Article 3 — late/missing returns from 2020:
  - Taxpayers who did not submit returns for any tax period beginning 2020 and ending before the law's effective date may submit them.
  - Taxpayers who submitted returns may submit amended returns to correct omissions/errors or add data, without additional tax or delay consideration for the period between original and amended returns.
  - The Article 3 relief applies only if the specified returns are submitted within six months from the law's effective date; otherwise ordinary penalties/financial sanctions apply.
- Article 4 — pre-2020 audited disputes:
  - For periods ending before 1 January 2020 where the Authority audited and a dispute is pending at any stage, taxpayer may request settlement.
  - Route 1: pay 30% of tax due according to the return for each disputed period, without prejudice to paying tax due under the return.
  - Route 2: pay tax equal to 40% of tax due based on the last prior agreement, for periods where no return was filed, a return showed no tax due, or return showed tax losses.
  - Tax and delay/additional tax may be paid in four equal instalments at three-month intervals, without delay/additional tax on those instalments.
- Article 5 — regular books and accounts:
  - For pre-2020 periods audited on the basis of regular books/accounts and disputed at any stage, settlement may be requested by waiving 100% of delay consideration/additional tax and amounts, if the taxpayer pays the principal tax debt in full within three months of the settlement request.
- Article 6 — application procedure:
  - Applicants under Articles 4 and 5 must submit a settlement request within three months from the law's effective date, including name, registration number, lawsuit/appeal number, tax types and disputed periods.
  - The Authority records the request and notifies the court registry, Tax Appeal Committee secretariat or internal committee secretariat within 30 days.
  - Notification suspends consideration of the dispute by operation of law for three months from the day after expiry of the five-day notice period.
  - Once the relevant body is notified of payment, the dispute ends by operation of law.
  - Finance Minister may extend the period for a similar further period.
- Article 7 — natural persons and prior real-estate/unlisted-securities disposals:
  - Natural persons who during the five years before commencement disposed of real estate or unlisted securities, and do not carry on other income-taxable activities, may request tax accounting for real-estate disposal tax or capital-gains tax on those unlisted securities.
  - Payment within six months from effective date results in 100% waiver of delay consideration.
  - Authority may not account for disposals more than five years old.
  - Natural persons with existing disputes over such disposals may request settlement at any dispute stage and pay tax within three months; 100% delay consideration is waived.
- Article 8: settlement does not create a right to refund sums already paid.
- Article 9: Tax Authority Head determines forms for applications under Articles 4–7.
- Article 10: Finance Minister issues implementing decisions within one month.
- Article 11: effective the day after publication.

## 3. Cross-law map and caution

- Law 5/2025 is a temporary transitional/regularization statute; it is not a general repeal of historical tax liability. Conditions, periods, eligibility and exclusions are critical.
- Law 7/2025 permanently amends the Unified Tax Procedures Law by adding a delay-charge cap and settlement provisions, subject to later amendments.
- Law 6/2025 establishes a separate simplified regime for eligible projects up to EGP 20m annual turnover.
- Law 150/2026 further amends Law 206/2020 and must be layered after Law 7/2025.
- Law 152/2026 separately extends Law 79/2016 tax dispute committees through 31 December 2026.
- Need verify implementing decisions, subsequent amendments and exact effective-date calculations.

# Collection checkpoint 2 — social protection, insurance and non-bank finance (2026-11-17)

## Newly inspected primary-source legal texts

### Law 12/2025 — Social Security Law
- Ministry of Finance record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-685d75f1d16c6e0008d1ff9f
- Official-hosted PDF (23 pages; Official Gazette issue 14, supplement, 3 April 2025):
  https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/qanwn_aldman_alajtmaey_2025_12_22544853c7.pdf
- Text extraction is available for the promulgation provisions and substantive chapters.
- Promulgation text states it repeals Social Security Law 137/2010 and Article 49 of the Child Law 12/1996, subject to the transitional provisions in the new law.
- Scope includes conditional and unconditional cash support (Takaful/Karama), eligibility categories, application and administration rules.
- The promulgation clause provides for commencement after publication with a three-month period (read the complete official Gazette and implementing instruments before operationalizing any dates).
- The Ministry portal labels it in force but explicitly says its online texts are guidance and the Official Gazette is the legal reference.

### Law 155/2024 — Unified Insurance Law
- Ministry of Finance record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-6a731e6b63496600085dd85c
- Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA) updated-text page: https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قانون-رقم-155-لسنة-2024/
- Official-hosted FRA PDF (95 pages; Official Gazette issue 27 bis (c), 10 July 2024):
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/alamiria_2024_155.pdf
- Searchable PDF extraction is available (2,571 extracted lines).
- Promulgation provisions state that it governs insurance/reinsurance and related insurance services/professions/activities, with FRA as the competent licensing/supervision authority.
- Repeals Laws 54/1975, 10/1981 and 72/2007 and Civil Code Articles 747–771, as well as inconsistent provisions. These repeal relationships need to be represented explicitly in the amendment/repeal graph.
- FRA page identifies predecessor instruments including Laws 98/2015, 118/2008, 72/2007, 156/1998, 91/1995, 30/1989, 10/1981 and 54/1975.

## Social Insurance Law 148/2019
- Ministry of Finance record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-5fe868b37bfc600007cbec80
- Official-hosted PDF endpoint was exposed from the Ministry record (156 pages):
  https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/2076_aad99154bd.pdf
- Related Prime Minister decisions listed by the Ministry include Decision 1218/2020 and Decision 2165/2019 concerning implementation of Article 111.
- Ministry record labels Law 148/2019 in force but warns that its online texts are guidance; compare against Gazette and later amendments and implementing decisions.
- Distinguish Social Insurance and Pensions Law 148/2019 from Social Security Law 12/2025; they are different regimes.

## Collection controls

- These are official source locators and text-inspection notes; no assumption of a blanket commercial-reuse licence is made.
- The FRA source is an official regulator page and official-hosted Gazette PDF. Record the terms/rights analysis separately from legal authenticity.
- Do not mark any law or decision lawyer-reviewed or verified solely because it appears on an official portal.
- Preserve each source PDF as received, SHA-256, Gazette issue/date/page, extraction method/version, and article/page mappings once acquisition is permitted and carried out.

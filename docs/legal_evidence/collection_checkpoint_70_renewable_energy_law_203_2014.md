## 1. Law 203/2014 — core mechanisms
- Establishes legal framework to stimulate electricity generation from renewable energy sources.
- Defines renewable energy sources, feed-in tariff, energy-source certificate, eligible subscribers, relevant ministry, EgyptERA and NREA.
- Article 2 sets four project models:
  1. NREA tenders renewable generation stations to be operated by NREA; electricity sold to Egyptian Electricity Transmission Company at price proposed by regulator and approved by Cabinet.
  2. Egyptian Electricity Transmission Company tenders investors to build/own/operate renewable stations; output sold under agreed price/terms.
  3. Investors build/own/operate renewable stations and sell to transmission/distribution licensees under power-purchase agreement at feed-in tariff for up to 25 years, subject to law.
  4. Investors contract directly with consumers to sell renewable electricity using transmission/distribution networks at agreed price/term.
- Article 3: land for renewable generation allocated by usufruct under Cabinet rules; Law 11/2022 amended the payment basis from 2% of project electricity value to a revised formula (exact amendment text must be transcribed).
- Article 4: investor establishing a renewable station above 500 kW must establish Egyptian project company under Investment Law, subject to Cabinet controls.
- Article 5: licence required from EgyptERA for generation/sale, with temporary permits for preliminary works; self-consumption and projects at/below 500 kW may be exempt under regulator controls.
- Article 6: transmission/distribution companies must connect renewable stations at producer's expense; network expansion costs borne by utilities under allocated State funds; utility must purchase/pay for available energy if unable to transmit under regulator rules.
- Article 7: Cabinet sets annual consumption levels for mandatory renewable-energy subscriber list and mandatory percentage, before each fiscal year.
- Article 8: Cabinet issues rules on feed-in tariff, required capacity, regularization of pre-law projects, land-use rules, subscriber lists/mandatory percentages and energy-source certificates.
- Article 9: PM issues implementing regulations/decisions.

## 2. Law 11/2022 amendment
- Amends Law 203/2014 and Law 102/1986 establishing NREA.
- Adds/changes terminology from electricity/thermal energy to energy in the definition of renewable energy sources.
- Amends Article 3 land usufruct consideration and other NREA statutory provisions.
- Also abolishes the Authority for Implementation of Hydropower Projects established under Law 14/1976, transferring relevant functions/assets/obligations as specified.
- Exact Gazette article-by-article changes must be captured before consolidating the 2014 law.

## 3. PM Decision 54/2023 — land usufruct licensing rules
- Issued 11 September 2023; effective the day after publication.
- Establishes regulatory rules for licensing usufruct of land owned by NREA for wind/solar electricity projects.
- Covers two project models: (1) station with transmission company purchase; (2) station selling directly to consumers.
- Includes stages: preparation, land usufruct licence for construction, construction, commercial operation, end of project life and general provisions.
- Regulates project land allocation, construction milestones, commercial operation, cancellation/termination of usufruct licence and end-of-life obligations.
- Full Gazette text and schedules should be normalized into milestones, deadlines, fees and licence conditions.

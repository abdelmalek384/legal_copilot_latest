## 1. SCC Case 10/35 — Real-Estate Register Law 142/1964

- Manshurat judgment record: https://manshurat.org/node/65064
- PDF: https://manshurat.org/file/75813/download?token=E2jG1O_x
- Judgment: Constitutional Court Case 10 of Judicial Year 35, decided **2 June 2018**.
- Gazette publication: **Official Gazette issue 22 bis (ط), 6 June 2018**, printed pages **16–25**.
- PDF: 10 pages; all ten page images visually inspected (zero-indexed 0–9). The masthead and judgment number/date are visible on page 16.

## 2. SCC Case 24/20 — Old-rent legislation, Law 136/1981

- Manshurat judgment record: https://manshurat.org/content/dm-dstwry-tthbyt-ljr-fy-nzm-l-ijr-lqdym-lqnwn-136-lsn-1981
- PDF: https://manshurat.org/file/89251/download?token=pfFXgSgF
- Judgment: Constitutional Court Case 24 of Judicial Year 20, decided **9 November 2024**.
- Gazette publication: **Official Gazette issue 45 bis (A), 10 November 2024**, printed pages **17–30**.
- PDF: 14 pages; all fourteen page images visually inspected (zero-indexed 0–13). Page 17 carries the Gazette masthead and the Court's judgment caption; page 30 carries the operative conclusion/signatures.
- Operative result: unconstitutional the first paragraph of each of Articles **1 and 2** of Law 136/1981 insofar as those paragraphs fixed the annual rent for premises licensed for residential use from the date the Law took effect.
- The Court deferred the effect of its ruling to the day following the end of the then-current ordinary legislative session of the House of Representatives. Preserve this temporal qualification exactly. Do not infer a specific operative date without verifying the official parliamentary session-ending record and subsequent legislation (including Laws 164/2025 and 165/2025).
- The decision is not a blanket invalidation of all rent-control provisions or all old-rent contracts. Its exact subject and temporal qualification must be retained.

# Collection checkpoint 18 — Traffic Law 66/1973 and amendments

## 1. Traffic Law 66/1973 — official Ministry/Gazette copy

- Ministry of Finance record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-5fe86ad27bfc600007cc018c
- Official Gazette PDF: https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/1395_4d0b5d7a68.pdf
- PDF: 14 pages, scanned/image-only; page 0 visually inspected.
- Gazette scan page identifies Law 66/1973 issuing the Traffic Law; Gazette issue 34 dated 23 August 1973.
- Promulgation clauses visible:
  - Article 1 repeals Law 449/1955 on motor vehicles and road rules, the law regulating operation of passenger and cargo transport vehicles issued by Law 449/1955, and the executive regulations/ministerial decisions specified in the text, to the extent inconsistent.
  - Article 2 authorizes Minister of Interior to issue the Executive Regulation and necessary implementing decisions; existing regulations/decisions continue insofar as not inconsistent until the new regulation issues.
  - Article 3: publication in Official Gazette and commencement one month after publication.
- Attached law begins with Chapter One, regulating road traffic and use of public roads. Article 1 provides that use of public roads is governed by the Traffic Law; the law applies to roads open to public traffic unless otherwise specified.
- Article 2 addresses international conventions and licensing requirements for vehicles/driver categories.
- The rest of the 14-page scan requires Arabic OCR and human article checking.

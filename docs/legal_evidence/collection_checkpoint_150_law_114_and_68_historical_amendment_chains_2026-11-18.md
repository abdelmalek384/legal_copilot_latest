## 2. Law 114/1946 — current amendment map

### Substantive statutory chain

1. **Law 69/1950** — Article 24 replacement.
2. **Law 222/1956** — inserted Article 3; **Law 181/1957** later repealed that inserted provision.
3. **Law 136/1957** — replaced first paragraph of Article 24.
4. **Law 82/1957** — a consolidated text attributes a further insertion (identified there as Article 12 bis), but publication metadata is inconsistent and the original law is not acquired.
5. **Law 5/1964** — organizational/repeal effects on Law 114 (Article 2 and paragraph 2 of Article 1).
6. **Law 25/1976** — changes to Articles 16, 21, 23 bis, 24, 48, 49 and 57 are attributed in the consolidated text; it inserted Article 23 bis.
7. **Law 223/1996** — replaces Article 21(1).
8. **Law 186/2020** — inserts Article 35 bis.
9. **Law 5/2021** — defers Law 186/2020 operation to 30 June 2023.
10. **Law 9/2022** (already registered as EG-0146) — replaces portions of Article 9 and Articles 21, 22, 23 bis, 28, 33, 35, 36 bis, 48, 49, 50 and 57; adds Articles 10 bis and 22 bis; repeals Articles 24, 26, 29, 34 and Article 9 paragraph 5.

### Executive-regulation chain

- Decision **825/1975** is the principal Executive Regulation (EG-0148).
- Decision **948/1976** (new EG-0211) added Articles 14 bis and 36 bis and replaced Articles 16, 17, 20 and 30.
- Decision **9310/2020** (new EG-0212) added Articles 21 bis, 21 bis A and 21 bis B to implement Law 186/2020 Article 35 bis. Its effectiveness was tied to Law 186/2020; Law 5/2021 deferred the underlying statute until 30 June 2023.
- Decision **2332/2022** is already registered as EG-0147; it replaces several provisions, adds new provisions and repeals provisions in the 1946 Executive Regulation, effective with Law 9/2022.
- Decision **11264/2016** is already registered as EG-0149; its complete article-level changes and original Gazette scan remain open.

## 3. Law 68/1947 — amendment map

- **Law 5/1964** (EG-0204) repealed Article 1 according to the consolidated text.
- **Law 629/1955** (EG-0209) replaced Article 3 and changed the allocation of notarization for specified personal-status instruments.
- **Law 103/1976** (EG-0210) replaced Articles 2, 5, 6, 9 and 12.
- **Decision 1532/1976** (EG-0214) amended the Executive Regulation, adding a paragraph to Article 10 and new Article 18 bis. Although numbered as a 1976 decision, it was published in Egyptian Proceedings issue 101 on 1 May 1977.
- **Decision 4730/2020** (EG-0213) added Article 34 bis (b) to the Executive Regulation, establishing electronic registers in specified manual offices.
- The original Executive Regulation is separately catalogued (Manshurat metadata: 3 November 1947, published 10 November 1947, effective 1 January 1948); original issue scan and all subsequent amendment/version effects still require full verification.

## 4. Commencement and metadata conflicts to resolve

### Law 25/1976

Manshurat metadata records Law 25/1976 as issued 23 March 1976, published in Official Gazette issue 14 on 1 April 1976, and effective 1 May 1976. A Court of Cassation case reproduces a passage treating 23 March 1976 as the relevant commencement date. Keep both readings pending the original Gazette issue and commencement clause. Do not silently normalize the effective date.

### Law 82/1957

A consolidated statutory text attributes an inserted Article 12 bis to Law 82/1957, but a secondary annotation contains an implausible/conflicting Gazette year. No original law or Gazette page has been obtained. Keep this as an unresolved chain lead, not verified operative wording.

### Law 222/1956 and Law 181/1957

The addition/repeal relationship is presently supported by the Court of Cassation-attributed consolidated text only. Original act numbers, exact text, issue references and commencement must be independently verified.

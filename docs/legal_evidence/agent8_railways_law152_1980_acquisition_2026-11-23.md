## 1. Base law and amendment chain

### Law 152/1980 — Egyptian National Railways Authority
**Arabic:** قانون رقم 152 لسنة 1980 بإنشاء الهيئة القومية لسكك حديد مصر  
**Gazette:** Official Gazette issue 28 bis (ب), 14 July 1980; commencement 15 July 1980 (Manshurat catalogue metadata).  
**Sources:**
- Manshurat catalogue and PDF locator: https://manshurat.org/node/13916
- Full secondary Arabic text with article-by-article amendment footnotes: https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-152-1980
- LAW HUB consolidated Arabic text: https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12552

Secondary transcription states the statute contains 28 articles and has the following amendment chain:
1. Law 149/2006 adds a second paragraph to Article 4 permitting concessions to private investors for constructing and operating new railway lines/networks, under competition/publicity and with a maximum 99-year concession term.
2. Law 20/2018 replaces Articles 2 and 4. It removes the former exclusive ENR network monopoly and broadens concession possibilities, while limiting concession term to 15 years. Official DSpace metadata for the Arabic and English amendment PDFs: https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/a408d578-36f6-4be9-8d57-dbea28be1b75. Gazette metadata in secondary consolidated source: issue 13 bis (ج), 2 April 2018.
3. Law 144/2020 replaces Article 7 and adds Article 7 bis. Gazette issue 27 bis (ب), 6 July 2020. A secondary-hosted Gazette issue PDF reproduces the amendment: https://www.elmodawanaeg.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/قانون-رقم-144-لسنة-2020.pdf. It classifies railway operational facilities, tracks, rights-of-way and related structures as public funds/public utilities and addresses release/reallocation of certain ENR land/assets for investment subject to exclusions, national-security coordination and Cabinet approval.

**Text status:** full 28-article base-law transcription read from secondary legal copies; official Manshurat PDF locator found but not independently authenticated against the original Gazette page images. Amendment chain is identified; original Gazette page images for Laws 149/2006 and 20/2018 remain outstanding. No lawyer review or reuse rights claimed.

## 2. Contracting regulation and amendments

### Minister of Transport Decision 191/2021
**Title:** إصدار لائحة تنظيم التعاقدات التي تبرمها الهيئة القومية لسكك حديد مصر  
**Publication:** Egyptian Proceedings, issue 87 (تابع أ), 14 April 2021.  
**Official repository metadata:** Middle East Library for Economic Services / DSpace, publisher Amiri Press, with a 550.77 KB original-bundle PDF listed: https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/30d317aa-1815-4ccd-961a-62ef4f259f62  
**Secondary catalogue:** https://manshurat.org/node/74139  
The regulation is the authority-specific contracting framework; the 2021 decision's issuing clauses state that Law 182/2018 and its Executive Regulation apply where the authority regulation contains no special provision. Full 2021 annexed regulation not transcribed in this pass.

### Minister of Transport Decision 577/2022
Secondary report identifies Decision 577/2022 as amending Articles 88 and 96 of Decision 191/2021, concerning contract execution duration and delay penalties: https://almalnews.com/1408321/النقل-إجراء-تعديل-على-لائحة-تنظيم-التعاقدات/  
Exact issue/date, signed text and article replacement wording remain uncollected. Do not rely on this as a verified amendment until the Gazette issue is obtained.

### Minister of Transport Decision 342/2023
Amends numerous provisions of the Decision 191/2021 contracting regulation. Official Amiri Press searchable text: https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=swCjt6g+P5U%3D  
The text states it substitutes a long list of regulation provisions, including Articles 4, 24–26, 29, 32–34, 41–42, 50, 52, 56, 58, 61, 77, 82, 93–96, 102, 121–131, 143–144, 149, 152, 157, 162, 174 and 175. Gazette issue 151, 12 July 2023, is reported by a scan reproduction: https://www.cairo24.com/Upload/libfiles/107/3/341.pdf. The official searchable page was opened as a search result but the full amendment text has not been captured locally or page-image checked.

## 3. Freight concession — PM Decision 3452/2023
**Date:** 6 September 2023.  
**Source:** secondary full searchable text at MKSEgypt: https://mksegypt.org/ar/decisions/16999  
The decision grants a 15-year concession to a named consortium to manage and operate ENR's freight transport sector under Law 152/1980 and an attached contract. The page reproduces four issuing articles and extensive contract text/annex references. It is a distinct implementing concession decision, not an amendment to Law 152/1980. The exact Official Gazette issue and official Cabinet/Gazette PDF were not acquired in this pass.

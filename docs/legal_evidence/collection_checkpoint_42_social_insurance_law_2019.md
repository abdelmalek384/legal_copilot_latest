## 1. Law 148/2019 — scope and system
- Applies to four broad categories:
  1. Employees working for others: public administration, public authorities, public institutions, public-sector/business-sector units, private-sector workers under Labour Law, temporary/seasonal workers, domestic-service workers subject to exclusions, and certain family members working for employer.
  2. Employers/self-employed and persons conducting commercial/industrial/professional activities.
  3. Egyptian workers abroad and specified foreign workers in Egypt, subject to conditions.
  4. Other categories defined by law/Executive Regulation.
- The law creates a unified national social insurance and pension framework and transfers payment of social-insurance entitlements to the National Organization for Social Insurance, with Treasury funding for specified liabilities.
- It preserves special-cadre benefits where stated and repeals inconsistent provisions for covered persons.
- Repeals/transition rules require mapping prior social-insurance statutes and the treatment of accrued rights.

## 2. Contribution rates — initial statutory framework
- For employees (category one):
  - Employer share: 12% of insured monthly wage.
  - Insured employee share: 9% of insured monthly wage.
- For self-employed/other categories under the relevant law provisions: aggregate 21% of chosen monthly contribution income, subject to statutory tables.
- For specified categories:
  - Insured person's share 9% of minimum contribution wage.
  - Treasury contribution 12% of minimum contribution wage.
- Contribution rates increase every seven years from commencement by 1%, shared equally between employer and insured person, subject to total contribution cap of 26%.
- Exact rates, contribution-wage ceilings/floors and later amendments must be checked against current annual decisions and the Executive Regulation; do not hard-code the 2019 base rates as current without verification.

## 3. Benefits and coverage structure
- Old-age, disability and death insurance.
- Work injury insurance, occupational disease, temporary disability and medical care.
- Sickness insurance.
- Unemployment insurance.
- Pension calculation, minimum/maximum pension, annual increases, family/dependent benefits, survivor pensions and lump-sum compensation.
- Voluntary contribution/crediting of periods, buyback/combination of service periods and settlement of contribution debts.
- Employer obligations: registration, wage declarations, contribution payments, records, reporting changes, and penalties for non-compliance.
- Exact formulas, eligibility ages, minimum contribution periods, actuarial adjustments, pension floors/ceilings and transition rules require article-by-article extraction.

## 4. Executive Regulation Decision 2437/2021
- Regulation index from PDF:
  - Articles 1–26: definitions, insurance coverage, social-insurance file and subscription procedures.
  - Remaining chapters address insured wage, contribution calculations, old-age/disability/death benefits, work injury, sickness, unemployment, payment of pensions, records, employer procedures and enforcement.
- Regulation's Article 1 defines law, insured person, employer and other key terms.
- Full 2021 Executive Regulation text is available in official Gazette PDF and secondary searchable transcription; full extraction and amendments are outstanding.

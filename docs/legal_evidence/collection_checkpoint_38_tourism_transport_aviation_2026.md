# Collection checkpoint 38 — tourism, river transport, maritime ports and civil aviation (2026-11-17)

## 1. Tourism and Hotel Establishments Law 8/2022 — Executive Regulation 705/2023

- Official Amiri Press full text of PM Decision 705/2023:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=%2FpVh28ctjNw%3D
- Decision 705/2023 issues the Executive Regulation of Law 8/2022.
- Dated 21 February 2023; commencement the day after publication.
- The decision states existing licences continue if not inconsistent, and licensed establishments must regularize and satisfy the new requirements within one year from publication of the Tourism Minister's special requirements; PM may extend up to two additional years.
- The Regulation covers licensing, hotel/tourism establishment classification, operating requirements, capacity, food/beverage services, temporary-event establishments, licence data, inspections, management and compliance.
- Article 7 lists core data in a tourism/hotel licence, including establishment name/type/activity, tourist grade, licensee, legal representative, responsible manager, room/bed count, capacity, facilities and other tourism licences.
- Article 8 allows permits for temporary establishments at events/holidays/exhibitions for up to three months, subject to requirements and fees.
- The preamble cross-references a broad set of statutes: Civil Code, Criminal Procedure, Real Estate Registration, Internal Navigation Law 10/1956, public health, Civil Defence, Tourism Chambers, Local Administration, Nile Protection 48/1982, Nature Reserves 102/1983, Environment 4/1994, Labour Law 12/2003 (now replaced by 14/2025), Building 119/2008, Sinai Development 14/2012, NFSA 1/2017, Non-Cash Payments 18/2019, Public Shops 154/2019, Waste 202/2020, Customs 207/2020, Water Resources 147/2021, Tourism Law 8/2022 and River Transport Authority Law 167/2022.
- Obtain Law 8/2022 full Gazette text and all permanent/special requirements, Ministry decisions and amendments; the Executive Regulation is not the whole tourism-law corpus.

## 2. River Transport Authority Law 167/2022

- Law 167/2022 reorganizes the General Authority for River Transport.
- It is cited as the enabling statute in the Tourism Executive Regulation 705/2023.
- The base law and its Executive Regulation need official Gazette acquisition; collect navigation licences, vessel registration, safety, passenger/freight transport, river ports, waterways, inspection and penalties.
- Link to Water Resources Law 147/2021, Nile Protection Law 48/1982, Internal Navigation Law 10/1956, Environmental Law 4/1994 and Tourism Law 8/2022.

## 3. Civil Aviation Law 28/1981 and implementing decisions

- Law 28/1981 regulates civil aviation and is cited in Ministerial Decisions 60/2022, 744/2022 and 965/2024.
- Minister of Civil Aviation Decision 60/2022 defines civil aviation activities that companies/establishments may carry on under applicable laws, conventions and decisions:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=O57urxzJsL4%3D
- Minister Decision 965/2024 amends Article 3 of Decision 744/2022, which amended the Executive Regulation of Law 28/1981 (Ministerial Decision 1/1989):
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=MQFczxQNHbI%3D
- Decision 965/2024 provides a compliance period from 3 August 2024 to 1 February 2025 for licensed civil aviation companies to regularize their status under the amended requirements; the Minister may extend for up to two years.
- Obtain Law 28/1981, Executive Regulation Decision 1/1989, Decision 744/2022, Decision 965/2024 and current Civil Aviation Authority licensing/operational rules.
- Link to Chicago Convention, airport companies, aircraft operations, airworthiness, safety, airport security, air transport, passenger rights, customs, immigration and environmental regulation.

## 4. Maritime commerce, ports and navigation — statutory map

The following primary instruments are identified in official transport decisions:
- Maritime Commerce Law 8/1990.
- Dry and Specialized Ports Law 1/1996.
- Internal Navigation Law 10/1956.
- Law 63/1961 on coastal maritime transport.
- Law 12/1964 establishing the Egyptian General Maritime Transport Corporation (as amended by Law 1/1998).
- Law 6/1967 establishing Alexandria Port Authority.
- Law 30/1975 on the Suez Canal Authority.
- Law 88/1980 establishing Port Said Port Authority.
- Presidential Decision 217/1978 establishing Red Sea Ports Authority.
- Presidential Decision 317/1985 establishing Damietta Port Authority.
- Presidential Decision 349/1996 establishing the General Authority for Land and Dry Ports.
- Presidential Decision 399/2004 establishing the Egyptian Authority for Maritime Safety.
- Law 83/2002 on Special Economic Zones.
- Presidential Decision 330/2015 establishing the Suez Canal Economic Zone.

These statutes/decisions form a sectoral transport/port network. Obtain each base text, amendments, Executive Regulations, port authority statutes, tariffs, safety/security rules, pilotage, towage, port dues, vessel registration and maritime labour requirements.

  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=SRdGzEoCDLA%3D

  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=oFi4ofL6%2F2k%3D

- Minister of Transport Decision 800/2016 issues rules for practicing maritime transport-related activities and user charges; Decision 81/1999 issues the Executive Regulation of the Specialized Ports Law.
- Capture all instruments and appendices separately, especially tariffs, licence conditions and safety standards.

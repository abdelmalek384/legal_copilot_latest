### Legal relationship to capture when the file is acquired
Article 137 of Law 14/2025 requires employers with ten or more workers to prepare workplace regulations and submit them to the competent administrative authority for review and ratification. The competent authority must obtain the competent trade-union organisation's opinion; the union has 15 days to respond, and the authority has 30 days to ratify or object, failing which the regulation is deemed effective under the statutory wording.

Decision 162/2026 sets the general substantive/procedural framework for workplace regulations. Decision 254/2026, if confirmed, appears to address the institutional machinery for ratification. These should be modelled as separate instruments and linked by an implementation relationship only after the signed text confirms the legal basis and scope.

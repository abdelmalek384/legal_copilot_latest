## 1. Law 95/2018 — IDA institutional statute
- Establishes/organizes Industrial Development Authority as public economic authority with legal personality and independent budget, responsible for industrial development and coordination.
- Article 1: applies the attached law to IDA.
- Article 2: does not prejudice Investment Law 72/2017, Sinai Development Law 14/2012 or Special Economic Zones Law 83/2002.
- Article 3: repeals inconsistent provisions.
- Article 4: industry minister issues Executive Regulation within six months; prior regulations/decisions continue insofar as consistent.
- Article 5: effective day after Gazette publication.
- Core powers include industrial development planning, industrial land allocation, industrial zones, investor service, industrial licensing coordination, industrial registry, industrial statistics, promotion and facilitation of industrial projects.
- Law must be linked to Industrial Licensing Law 15/2017, which separately regulates the licensing procedure.

## 2. Executive Regulation Decision 198/2021 — 58 articles
- Issued by Minister of Trade and Industry, Decision 198/2021.
- 58 articles in full secondary transcription, updated through 2024.
- Defines IDA, industrial zone, industrial park, industrial activity, industrial land, project, investor, service centre and related terms.
- Regulates:
  - IDA board and internal governance.
  - Industrial land allocation and usufruct/sale procedures.
  - Industrial zone development and management.
  - Industrial project establishment, expansion, relocation and regularization.
  - Investor service centres and coordination with other authorities.
  - Industrial register, data and statistical reporting.
  - Industrial project support and investment incentives.
  - Fees, inspections, grievances and administrative measures.
- The SIS Gazette record establishes publication and public availability; compare the complete 58 articles with the original Gazette PDF before source-matched promotion.

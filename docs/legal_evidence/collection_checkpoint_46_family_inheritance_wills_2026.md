# Collection checkpoint 46 — family courts, inheritance, wills and family insurance (2026-11-17)

## 1. Family Courts Law 10/2004 — official Gazette PDF

- Official Ministry of Finance-hosted Gazette PDF:
  https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/1100_a5e6ce8477.pdf
- Gazette issue 12 supplement (A), 18 March 2004.
- Searchable official PDF text identifies Law 10/2004 issuing the Family Courts Law.
- Promulgation Article 2 requires first-instance partial/primary courts to transfer pending cases that become Family Court jurisdiction, in their existing state and without fees, subject to exceptions for cases already decided or reserved for judgment.
- The Law establishes Family Courts and Family Dispute Settlement Offices, assigns family/personal-status jurisdiction, and sets special procedures and appeals.
- Secondary text and metadata:
  https://manshurat.org/node/7336
- Obtain full text and all amendments/implementing decisions; map each subject-matter jurisdiction and procedural rule.

## 2. Personal Status Litigation Procedures Law 1/2000

- Law 1/2000 regulates certain conditions and procedures for litigation in personal-status matters.
- Secondary full-text record:
  https://manshurat.org/node/27318
- Amendment Law 91/2000:
  https://manshurat.org/node/12574
- Ministry of Social Solidarity's official catalogue lists the 2000 personal-status litigation statute and Family Courts Law as separate instruments:
  https://www.moss.gov.eg/الإصدارات/القوانين-والتشريعات/
- It is a procedural statute and must be linked to substantive personal-status laws, Evidence Law 25/1968, Family Courts Law 10/2004 and the Civil/Commercial Procedure Law where applicable.

## 3. Family Insurance System Fund Law 11/2004

- Ministry of Finance official record:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-5fe869f17bfc600007cbf87c
- Official record labels Law 11/2004 in force and identifies it as establishing the Family Insurance System Fund.
- Secondary full text:
  https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?ItemID=135&NID=48876&Type=14&action=ViewActivePages
- Article 1 establishes a non-profit public legal person with its own budget, headquartered in Cairo and affiliated with Nasser Social Bank.
- Article 2 requires family contributions to the insurance system under Article 71 of Law 1/2000.
- Ministry of Social Solidarity catalogue lists it as a family-law instrument.
- Obtain the Gazette original, amendments, fee schedules, payment rules, enforcement and Nasser Social Bank decisions.

## 4. Personal Status Law 25/1929 and Law 25/1920

- Personal Status Law 25/1929 is a foundational statute covering marriage/divorce and related personal-status issues; secondary full-text lead:
  https://manshurat.org/node/12369
- Law 25/1920 is a distinct statute governing maintenance and certain personal-status obligations; obtain and track separately.
- The amendment graph includes amendments such as Laws 33/1926, 103/1958, 100/1985, 33/1992, 4/2005 and 2/2006 (secondary guide; verify each against Gazette).
- Constitutional Court judgments may invalidate or interpret particular provisions; the base text alone is not a complete current personal-status rule set.
- Egypt does not have one single all-inclusive personal-status code covering all subjects and communities; maintain a statute-by-statute scope map.

## 5. Inheritance Law 77/1943

- Law 77/1943 regulates inheritance.
- Secondary full-text lead:
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11346
- Alternate secondary PDF record:
  https://manshurat.org/node/12494
- The opening provisions state that inheritance arises on death or judicial declaration of death, and the heir must be alive at the relevant time; rules for an unborn child are addressed in Article 43.
- This is a core substantive statute. Obtain original Gazette text, amendments and relevant SCC/Cassation principles before answering inheritance shares or disqualification questions.

## 6. Wills Law 71/1946

- Law 71/1946 regulates wills and testamentary dispositions.
- Secondary full-text records:
  - https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-71-1946
  - https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11414
  - https://www.notariesofegypt.org/article.php?id=148
- The full secondary text includes rules on formation, capacity, beneficiaries, conditions, revocation, limits beyond one-third of the estate, wills to heirs, unborn beneficiaries, usufruct and periodic payments.
- A secondary law library reports an article-by-article version through 2026 and says it is copied from the Court of Cassation legislation portal; verify the original source and Gazette version before ingestion.
- Keep the Will Law distinct from Inheritance Law 77/1943; the two statutes interact but govern different legal events.

## 7. Family Court operational decisions

- Justice Minister Decision 6099/2022 creates the Shebin El-Kom Full Family Prosecution and specifies subordinate family prosecution offices:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Fpet0SM33Kg%3D
- It cites Law 1/2000 and Family Courts Law 10/2004 and allocates territorial family-prosecution responsibilities.
- Justice Minister Decisions 4462/2025 and 7021/2025 relocate family court/prosecution offices:
  - https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=TsfFzY4kimM%3D
  - https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=y%2Fm4l6KZkRU%3D
- These are administrative venue/location decisions, not changes to substantive family law.

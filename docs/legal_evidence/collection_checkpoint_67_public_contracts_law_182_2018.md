## 1. Law 182/2018 — scope and structure
- Applies to public entities within the State General Budget, ministries/departments/agencies with special budgets, local administration, public service/economic authorities, affiliated special-purpose units and special funds, subject to exclusions and special statutes.
- Gazette issue 39 bis (d), 3 October 2018; effective 30 days after publication.
- Repeals Tenders and Auctions Law 89/1998 and inconsistent provisions, with transition for existing contracts/procedures.
- Related provisions preserve PPP Law 67/2010, local content preference Law 5/2015 and Investment Law 72/2017.
- The law's 95? articles and chapters regulate procurement planning, contract methods, tendering, practice, auctions, direct agreement, framework agreements, evaluation, award, performance, guarantees, penalties, disputes and Government Services Authority.

## 2. Executive Regulation 692/2019 — full searchable Gazette text

### Promulgation and repeal
- Article 1 applies the attached Executive Regulation to entities covered by Law 182/2018.
- Article 2 repeals Executive Regulation Decision 1367/1998 under old Law 89/1998.
- Article 3: published in Al-Waqa'i and effective the day after publication.

### Definitions and institutional structure
- Article 1 definitions include contracting administration, beneficiary/requesting administration, business community, tender documents, financial appropriation, bidder, winning bid, procurement operation, contracting methods, award committee, contract, life-cycle cost and sustainable procurement.
- Article 2 gives General Authority for Government Services responsibilities: public procurement portal, policy proposals, instructions, review of agency procurement, data analysis/publication, training, standard tender documents/contracts, contractor performance evaluation, central contractor register, property databases and related tasks.
- Article 3 requires each administrative entity to establish a separate procurement/contracting department, staffed by trained/certified personnel with integrity and no conflict of interest.
- Articles 4–5 require transparency, free competition, equality, equal opportunity, integrity and referral of suspected bid-rigging/anti-competitive arrangements to Egyptian Competition Authority.
- Article 6 requires complaints study and reasoned report to competent authority within five days.
- Article 7 Arabic is official language of tender documents/contracts, with controlled exceptions for technical specifications or foreign tenders.
- Article 8 requires written/documented official correspondence and equal treatment in communications.
- Articles 10–13 cover procurement planning, need assessment, life-cycle cost, market study, sustainable procurement and annual needs plans.

### Procurement methods and process
- Regulation sets detailed requirements for public tender, limited tender, local/international tender, two-envelope procedures, two-stage tender, practice/negotiation, auctions, direct agreement, framework contracts, prequalification, requests for information and expressions of interest.
- It prescribes announcement/publication on the public procurement portal, minimum time periods, tender document content, bid security, technical/financial opening, evaluation criteria, committee composition, minutes, award, notification and reasons for rejection/cancellation.
- It governs price analysis, abnormally low bids, equal bids, preference for Egyptian products, local content, contractor classification and experience, conflicts of interest and disqualification.
- Contract performance provisions address advance payments, guarantees, insurance, delivery/acceptance, variations, price adjustment, delay penalties, termination, subcontracting, assignment, final settlement and claims.
- Public property disposal and sale/lease procedures are also included.
- The complete 2,857-line source should be extracted article-by-article; this checkpoint records selected opening articles and the full source locator, not a full transcription.

## 3. Amendment graph

### Law 188/2020
- Gazette issue 36 bis (b), 5 September 2020.
- Replaces Article 92: contractor may not assign contract or amounts due, except assignment of receivables to licensed banks or non-bank financial companies; contractor remains responsible for performance and administrative entity retains its rights.
- Adds Article 15 bis: permits administrative entities, with Minister/competent Governor approval and for economic/development goals, to use alternative procurement procedures subject to statutory conditions.
- Adds second paragraph to Article 93: administrative entities may not contract with persons convicted by final judgment of specified Penal Code public-funds crimes or tax/customs evasion offences until rehabilitation.
- Full Law 188 text available in official Gazette PDF and secondary transcription.

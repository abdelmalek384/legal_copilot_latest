# Collection checkpoint 76 — Law 11/2026 amends Social Insurance Law 148/2019

## 1. Official Law 11/2026 full text

- Official Amiri Press full text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=s6iZUpH%2FRi4%3D
- Law 11/2026 amends Article 111 of Social Insurance and Pensions Law 148/2019.
- Signed 4 June 2026; effective the day after publication.
- It replaces Article 111 in full and governs the annual instalment paid by the State Treasury to the National Social Insurance Authority in consideration for the Social Insurance Fund assuming specified legacy liabilities.

## 2. New Article 111 — State Treasury annual instalment

- For FY 2025/2026, the first annual instalment is EGP 238.55 billion.
- Instalment increases by 6.4% compounded annually starting 1 July 2026.
- From 1 July 2027, the annual increase rate rises by 0.2 percentage points each year until it reaches 7% compounded from 1 July 2029.
- An additional EGP 1 billion is added annually starting 1 July 2026 for five years.
- Instalments continue for 50 years.
- In consideration, the Social Insurance Fund assumes the enumerated legacy obligations, including:
  1. State Treasury pension obligations accrued up to commencement of Law 148/2019.
  2. Specified Treasury obligations under Law 148/2019 promulgation provisions and Articles 19, 23, 27, 28, 29, 35, 159 and 163.
  3. Treasury contribution for specified insured categories.
  4. Amounts deposited for the two social-insurance funds with the National Investment Bank.
  5. Treasury bonds previously issued to the funds.
  6. Entire outstanding State Treasury indebtedness to the funds.
  7. Actuarial deficit in the social-insurance system up to the commencement date.
  8. Treasury bonds with total nominal value EGP 100 billion, identified by a decision of the Authority's Board Chair, without prejudice to coupons due up to commencement.
  9. Debt arising under Law 8/2020 amending Police Authority Law 109/1971.
  10. Entire debt owed by the General Authority for Supply Commodities.
  11. Entire debt owed by the Egyptian Tax Authority, excluding insurance contributions and additional amounts due on them.
- The annual instalment excludes:
  - exceptional pensions under Law 71/1964 granted after commencement;
  - new/additional benefits granted after commencement and charged to the Treasury.
- After the 50-year instalment period, the Treasury must pay the financial entitlements due under the Law.
- PM decision, based on Finance Minister and competent insurance minister proposals, sets implementation rules.

## 5. Important legal and financial distinctions

- Article 111 is a long-term financing arrangement between the State Treasury and the Social Insurance Fund; it does not itself set individual pension entitlements or annual pension increases.
- The 50-year instalment schedule must not be presented as the period for paying an individual pension claim.
- Distinguish accrued Treasury obligations, annual State contributions, actuarial deficit, bond assets and new benefits granted after commencement.
- For individual pension calculation, use the operative benefit/insured-wage/retirement provisions of Law 148/2019 and its regulations, not Article 111's Treasury funding schedule.

# NFSA Chair Decision 269/2022 — reduced sampling for white-list food manufacturers

**Acquisition date:** 24 November 2026  
**Instrument:** NFSA Board Chair Decision No. 269 of 2022 on reducing sampling rates for production inputs imported by white-list food-manufacturing establishments  
**Official Customs catalogue date:** 14 November 2022  
**Status:** Six-page signed Arabic scan hosted by the Egyptian Customs Authority. All six page images inspected, including the implementation mechanism and two application/notification forms.

## 1. Primary source

- Egyptian Customs Authority-hosted six-page PDF: https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace531843308.pdf
- Official Customs catalogue listing: https://customs.gov.eg/Legislations/Manshorat?categoryId=1
- The signed decision page states Decision 269/2022 and refers to NFSA Decisions 6/2020 and 7/2020; the implementation annex refers to the pre-arrival-notification mechanism.

## 2. Main decision and attached mechanism

- **Article 1:** definitions include NFSA, food, food-manufacturing establishments, food operators, importer, production inputs and food handling.
- **Article 2:** establishes a reduced sampling regime (less than 100%) for imported production inputs used by eligible food-manufacturing establishments for their own production/service activity, not for sale in the market; the reduction is risk-based.
- **Article 3:** applies to white-list food-manufacturing establishments importing production inputs for their own account, not for market sale, under the prior-notification system associated with Decision 7/2020.
- **Article 4:** implementation is through the attached mechanism and forms.
- **Attached implementation mechanism:** eligible establishments must be registered on the NFSA white list, production inputs must be used in their own manufacturing process and not sold as inputs, the establishment requests enrolment, submits product/input and manufacturer information, supplies prior-notification and supporting documentation, and cooperates with NFSA risk assessment and sampling. The annex describes an ongoing review/monitoring mechanism and includes a programme-enrolment form and prior-notification form.

## 3. Operational boundaries

- The decision does not create a universal 25% sampling rate. It authorizes a rate below 100% determined by risk for qualifying white-list establishments and qualifying inputs.
- Inputs must be for the establishment's own production/service activity and not for direct market sale.
- The annex uses the Decision 7/2020 prior-notification system and the Decision 6/2020 importer-licensing framework.
- Decision 270/2024 later establishes a more detailed fast-release regime and expressly cites Decision 269/2022. Read them together; do not substitute the older reduced-sampling mechanism for the 2024 fast-release eligibility and obligations.

## New core investment-fund acquisition — FRA Decision 154/2026
- Official four-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alamiria_2026_154.pdf
- Gazette issue 210 supplement (ج), 23 September 2026, printed pages 2–5; decision adopted 5 August 2026.
- Captured exact Arabic Articles 1–7.
- Establishes Egypt's first specific hedge-fund framework under the Capital Market Law regime: new hedge funds and conversion of existing funds; permitted asset classes; disclosure requirements; manager expertise, systems, leverage, stress testing, collateral and reporting obligations; FRA 15-day review period.
- Permits hedge-fund borrowing without applying the general Article 160 Executive Regulation limit, subject to fund-specific disclosures/limits and other requirements in the decision.
- Effective 24 September 2026.

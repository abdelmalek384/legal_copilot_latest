# Administrative judiciary and Administrative Prosecution amendment-chain acquisition — 2026-11-23 (batch 2)

## New instruments located
This batch adds four amendment instruments not previously represented as individual records in the candidate registry:
- Law171/1981 — amends Law117/1958, Articles12 and14.
- Law12/1989 — major amendment to Law117/1958, including Articles1,2,4,36,38 bis,39,40,47 bis and new provisions.
- Law17/2007 — related to Law47/1972 and several judicial statutes; exact affected provisions not yet mapped.
- Law13/2017 — amends Law117/1958 Article35 and Law47/1972 Article83; the Article83 appointment mechanism is later replaced by Law77/2019.

### Law12/1989
EastLaws metadata identifies publication in Official Gazette issue16, 20 April 1989, effective 21 April 1989:
https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/20-04-1989/no-12?id=4763&sub=1716133&type=1

The instrument's Article1 replaces Law117/1958 Articles1,2,4,36,38 bis,39,40 and47 bis. Article2 adds Articles2 bis,2 bis 1 and35 and further new provisions; Article3 adds the post of Assistant Administrative Prosecution Member to the salary/rank schedule annexed to Law88/1973. The EastLaws record is not a Gazette scan. The consolidated LAW HUB record https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11722 shows the amended text and identifies the later amendments.

A significant exact consolidated clause now visible in LAW HUB:
«تتولى النيابة الإدارية إقامة الدعوى التأديبية ومباشرتها أمام المحاكم التأديبية. ولرئيس هيئة النيابة الإدارية الطعن فى أحكام المحاكم التأديبية. ويباشر الطعن أمام المحكمة الإدارية العليا أحد أعضاء النيابة بدرجة رئيس نيابة على الأقل.»
The page annotates this provision as substituted by Law12/1989.

## Amendment-chain cautions
- Law117/1958 is not a single 2019-amended instrument: at minimum the chain now includes Law171/1981, Law12/1989, Law13/2017 and Law77/2019. Other amendments still need tracing.
- LAW HUB's current consolidated Law117 page identifies the 2023 Supreme Constitutional Court judgment invalidating part of Article40 bis-1 (case47/44 constitutional), which is a judicial invalidation, not a legislative amendment. Record the judgment separately when its official judgment text is acquired.
- Law47/1972 amendment chain includes at least Law136/1984, Law17/2007, Law13/2017 and Law77/2019. Manshurat also lists Laws3/2002 and159/2003 as judicial retirement-age amendments; add and map these in a subsequent pass.
- Law13/2017 Article83 text is superseded by Law77/2019; the chain must preserve temporal versions.
- No source in this batch is promoted to production corpus. Secondary rights remain unknown; official MOF pages are guidance only; the original Gazette scans for 171/1981 and12/1989 remain missing.

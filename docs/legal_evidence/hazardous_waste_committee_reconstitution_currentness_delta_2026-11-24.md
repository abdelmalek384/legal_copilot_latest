# Hazardous-waste technical committee — currentness delta (official EEAA notices)

**Research date:** 24 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Record newly confirmed official evidence that the Technical Committee for Hazardous Materials and Waste created under Environment Minister Decision 30/2023 was subsequently reconstituted. This closes a currentness-discovery gap only; it does not acquire the reconstitution instrument or the original Decision 30/2023 text.

## 1. Official evidence

### A. EEAA notice — 4 January 2025

Official page: https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/News/22401/Details  
SIS mirror and attached PDF/images: https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/handle/123456789/85815  
PDF: https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/85815/MOE20250104_1130.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1

The EEAA notice identifies the Technical Committee for Hazardous Materials and Waste as formed by Environment Minister Decision 30/2023. It describes its remit as:
- preparing, issuing and reviewing unified lists of hazardous materials and waste;
- setting controls and requirements for handling and integrated management of hazardous materials and waste; and
- determining methods to reduce generation.

The notice reports an eleventh committee meeting and discusses work on slaughterhouse waste. It is an official agency report about committee activity, not the constituting decision or minutes themselves.

### B. EEAA notice — 2 January 2026

Official page: https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/News/22821/Details

The EEAA report says the Technical Committee for Hazardous Materials and Waste (Committee 53) had been **reconstituted**:
- chaired by the Executive President of the Waste Management Regulatory Authority (جهاز تنظيم إدارة المخلفات);
- with representatives of 23 competent administrative authorities;
- with a remit to establish regulatory frameworks and standards for safe, integrated management of hazardous materials and waste;
- including unified lists of prohibited materials, materials permitted under licence, and hazardous wastes; allocation of roles/responsibilities; controls for handling and integrated management of certain waste classes; and waste-generation reduction, including electronic waste.

This is material evidence that the 2023 formation record alone is insufficient to establish current committee membership in January 2026 or later.

## 2. What can and cannot be concluded

**Supported by official agency reporting:**
1. Decision 30/2023 formed the committee.
2. The committee was active by January 2025 and had held at least eleven meetings by the 4 January 2025 notice.
3. EEAA reported a later reconstitution by 2 January 2026, chaired by the Waste Management Regulatory Authority's Executive President and including 23 competent administrative authorities.
4. The committee's reported functions include unified hazardous-material/waste lists and controls for integrated management.

**Not established by these notices:**
- the number/date/title of the later reconstitution decision;
- whether the later decision expressly repealed, amended or replaced Decision 30/2023;
- the exact names and appointing instruments of all 23 represented authorities;
- membership terms, quorum, voting rules, secretariat composition or delegated powers;
- whether there were further reconstitutions after 2 January 2026;
- the current membership as of 24 November 2026;
- the full text or annexes of Decision 30/2023;
- the full texts of committee minutes or technical standards discussed in the notices.

Do not infer that the later reconstitution legally repealed Decision 30/2023. Treat the original formation decision and later reconstitution instrument as separate records until the legal instrument is obtained.

## 3. Retrieval and legal-research implications

- Queries about current membership should not return Decision 30/2023 alone as a complete answer.
- For historical legal basis, Decision 30/2023 remains the identified formation instrument, pending full text.
- For current organizational status, surface the dated EEAA notice and explicitly label it **official agency report — reconstitution instrument not yet located**.
- A notice about a committee meeting or work product is not itself a binding technical standard or a ministerial decision.
- Decision 151/2025's unified list and Decision 346/2025's adoption of technical committee decisions must be linked to the committee as separate instruments; do not treat the EEAA report as a substitute for either.
- Keep committee membership/currentness answers abstaining where a user asks for exact current names, legal appointment authority or current legal composition and the primary reconstitution instrument is absent.

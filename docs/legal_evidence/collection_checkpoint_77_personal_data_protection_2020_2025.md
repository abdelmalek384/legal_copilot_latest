## 1. Law 151/2020 — scope and legal basis
- Applies to electronic processing, wholly or partly, of personal data relating to natural persons, by any holder, controller or processor.
- Territorial reach includes Egyptian offenders inside/outside Egypt; non-Egyptian residents in Egypt; and non-Egyptians abroad where conduct is criminal where committed and data relates to Egyptians or foreign residents in Egypt.
- Exclusions include personal-use data held by natural persons, official statistical processing/legal mandate, and purely journalistic processing subject to accuracy and other conditions.
- Establishes Personal Data Protection Center as regulator; controller, processor, holder, data subject, sensitive data, cross-border transfer and direct electronic marketing are defined.
- 56 articles in full text; key rights, duties, licensing, penalties and transitional provisions are covered.

## 2. Key substantive provisions
- Article 1 defines personal data, sensitive personal data, processing, controller, processor, holder, data subject, data breach, electronic marketing, cross-border transfer, profiling and related terms.
- Articles 2–3 establish data-subject rights and principles: lawful/transparent processing, specific purposes, data minimization, accuracy, retention limitation, confidentiality, security and accountability.
- Article 4 requires controller to obtain licence/permit from Personal Data Protection Center for specified processing; processor must obtain permit where required.
- Article 5 requires processor to act under written instructions and law, use security controls, limit purpose/duration, assist controller and preserve confidentiality.
- Article 6 regulates data collection/notice, lawful basis, consent and disclosure of purpose/rights.
- Article 7 requires controller to notify Center of a personal-data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware; if breach threatens rights/freedoms, notify data subjects within three working days after notifying Center, subject to statutory rules.
- Articles 8–10 address data subject access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability/availability and requests to controller/processor.
- Article 11 gives digital evidence derived from personal data evidentiary force equivalent to written evidence if technical criteria in Executive Regulation are satisfied.
- Article 12 prohibits collecting, transferring, storing, preserving, processing or making available sensitive personal data without Center licence; written explicit consent is required unless law provides otherwise; child data requires guardian consent.
- Article 13 requires Data Protection Officer and security policies for sensitive data.
- Articles 14–18 govern data protection officer, direct electronic marketing, consent and restrictions.
- Articles 19 onward establish Personal Data Protection Center, licensing/permit authority, inspections, cross-border transfer, complaints, enforcement and criminal penalties.
- Exact licence/permit classes, fees, forms, exemptions and thresholds must be extracted from the 2025 Executive Regulation and later Center decisions.

## 3. Executive Regulation 816/2025 — 43 articles
- Implements Law 151/2020 and sets operational controls for controllers, processors, holders and data subjects.
- Article 1 adopts statutory definitions.
- Regulation covers:
  - Controller/processor registration and licensing/permits.
  - Privacy notices and consent mechanisms.
  - Lawful processing bases and records of processing.
  - Security programmes and technical/organizational measures.
  - Data Protection Officer duties.
  - Breach notification to Center within 72 hours and affected persons within three working days, subject to risk/conditions.
  - Sensitive data and children's data.
  - Direct electronic marketing licences/consent.
  - Cross-border transfer, storage, sharing and access.
  - Data-subject requests and response procedures.
  - Local representative requirements for foreign controllers/processors.
  - Inspection, audit, enforcement and transitional arrangements.
- Article 15: handlers/controllers/processors of data of children under 15 must obtain prior explicit written (paper/electronic) parental consent for collection/processing; consent must specify period. For children aged 15–18, consent is required from child or parent as specified by Center rules.
- Regulation's Article 5 covers breach reporting and affected-person notification; exact trigger and clock must be read with law Article 7.
- Full 43-article text should be extracted article-by-article; preserve exact Arabic wording and all technical standards.

## 4. Transition and current implementation status
- Law's promulgation provisions grant covered entities one year from issuance of the Executive Regulation to regularize.
- Decision 816/2025 published 1 November 2025 and effective 2 November 2025. The one-year transition period therefore appears to have expired in early November 2026; calculate exact legal expiry from the original wording and any later Center decisions/extensions.
- As of 17 November 2026, do not assume the transition remains open.
- Personal Data Protection Center has issued/announced 2026 guidance:
  - Electronic Direct Marketing Guidelines v1.1.
  - Data Subject Consent Guidelines v1.1.
  - Lawful Bases of Processing Guidelines v1.1.
  - Privacy Notice Guidelines v1.1.
- These guidelines are administrative guidance; record as separate documents, not as legislation.
- Need verify licensing portal opening, actual licence forms, fees, DPO accreditation and any extension decisions from official Center sources.

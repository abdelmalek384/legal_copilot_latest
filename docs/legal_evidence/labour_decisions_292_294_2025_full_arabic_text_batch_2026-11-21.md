**Official-adjacent Ministry/SIS record:** https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/handle/123456789/117216?locale-attribute=ar  
**Ministry/SIS-hosted PDF:** https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/117216/MOM20251226_0805.pdf?sequence=4

**Gazette bundle catalogue:** https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/37add437-802d-4848-8a24-8e064db22fcc

The SIS-hosted PDF text layer independently reproduces the four operative articles. It confirms the 12 paid national/religious occasions, the Minister's power to substitute a holiday date, paid leave not deducted from annual leave, compensation or a substitute day when work is performed, and a separate religious-holiday entitlement for non-Muslim workers. The Ministry/SIS record dates the public statement 26 December 2025 and provides a PDF attachment.

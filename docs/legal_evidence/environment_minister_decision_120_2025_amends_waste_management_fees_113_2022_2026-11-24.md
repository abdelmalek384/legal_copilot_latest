## 1. Operative text captured from Amiri Press

The searchable Amiri Press text identifies the decision as Environment Minister Decision 120/2025, dated 13 May 2025, amending Environment Minister Decision 113/2022 on categories and fees for Waste Management Regulatory Authority licences, approvals and permits for integrated management activities involving materials and waste.

The preamble cites, among other things:
- Environment Law 4/1994 and its Executive Regulation;
- Waste Management Law 202/2020 and its Executive Regulation;
- Decision 113/2022;
- Waste Management Regulatory Authority Board approval at meeting 9 on 12 March 2024;
- Cabinet approval at meeting 41 on 29 April 2025.

### Article 1 — insertion of new Article 3 bis

The decision adds a new Article 3 bis to Decision 113/2022. In addition to applicable VAT, the following fees are prescribed:

| Item | Approval / service | Fee |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Approval to export hazardous materials/waste and non-hazardous waste | 1% of the value of the quantity permitted for annual export, capped at EGP 200,000 |
| 2 | Licence or approval for companies conducting any stage of integrated waste-management activity | EGP 5,000 annually |
| 3 | Approval for an additional certificate or replacement of a damaged/lost certificate | EGP 2,000 |
| 4 | Approval to conduct valuation of hazardous materials/waste, issued to expert valuers for waste-sale activities | EGP 25,000 annually |
| 5 | Approval to issue Basel Convention notifications or extend validity for waste transit | EGP 12,000 per notification, for one year |

### Article 2 — deletion

Article 2 repeals item 3 of Article 3 of Environment Minister Decision 113/2022.

This is a targeted deletion. Do not treat it as repeal of Decision 113/2022 as a whole or as cancellation of the newly inserted Article 3 bis.

### Article 3 — publication and commencement

The decision is to be published in Egyptian Proceedings and takes effect on the day following publication.

## 2. Legal and operational interpretation boundaries

- The 1% export fee is subject to the stated EGP 200,000 legal maximum; it is not an uncapped percentage.
- The five fee items are stated in addition to applicable VAT.
- The EGP 5,000 amount is annual and applies to licences/approvals for companies working in any stage of integrated waste-management activity.
- The EGP 25,000 annual amount concerns the specified expert valuers / hazardous-material and waste valuation activity; do not generalize it to all waste operators.
- The EGP 12,000 amount is per Basel notification and the text says for one year.
- Article 2 deletes one identified item from Article 3 of Decision 113/2022. The deleted item's former wording and any consequential administrative practice should be checked against the original Decision 113/2022 before explaining what service/fee ceased.
- Decision 120/2025 is distinct from Decision 121/2025, which concerns export conditions and procedures. Their shared publication issue does not merge their legal functions.

# Collection checkpoint 10 — e-signature, cybercrime, consumer protection and consumer finance (2026-11-17)

## 1. Electronic Signature Law 15/2004 — official ITIDA Gazette text

- Official ITIDA English PDF: https://www.itida.gov.eg/English/Documents/2.pdf
- Official ITIDA Arabic PDF: https://itida.gov.eg/English/Documents/1.pdf
- Gazette: Official Journal issue 17 (Supplement D), 22 April 2004.
- The Arabic indexed text begins with definitions:
  - الكتابة الإلكترونية: letters, numbers, symbols or signs fixed on an electronic, digital or optical support or similar means and giving perceptible meaning.
  - المحرر الإلكتروني: a data message containing information created, integrated, stored, sent or received wholly or partly by electronic/digital/optical means.
  - التوقيع الإلكتروني: marks placed on an electronic document, with unique characteristics allowing identification of the signer and distinguishing them from others.
- ITIDA's official overview: https://itida.gov.eg/Arabic/Pages/E-Signature.aspx
- ITIDA confirms that it is the competent authority for regulating electronic signatures under Law 15/2004.
- An official Gazette text dated 23 April 2020 was also located:
  https://itida.gov.eg/English/Documents/3.pdf
  It includes technical security requirements for signature-creation systems, including exclusive control/secrecy, resistance to deduction/forgery/manipulation, protection of document content, signer awareness and detection of later alteration.
- Collect the full executive regulation and all ITIDA licensing/technical decisions. Do not infer a qualified/secure signature from the mere presence of an electronic mark.

## 4. Consumer Finance Law 18/2020 — FRA official updated version

- Official FRA updated-law page:
  https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قانون-رقم-18-لسنه-2020-باصدار-قانون-تنظيم-نشا/
- Official Gazette PDF:
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/UG50279UG50280.pdf
- Gazette issue 11 bis (k), 17 March 2020.
- The PDF text identifies Law 18/2020 issuing the law regulating consumer finance activity.
- Promulgation Article 2 states that companies licensed by FRA to practice consumer finance under the law are companies providing non-bank financial market services under Law 10/2009.
- FRA updated page records Law 158/2022 as prior/amending legislation; preserve this amendment link.
- FRA regulatory index exposes implementing instruments including:
  - FRA Chair Decision 869/2021 on consumer finance contract forms.
  - FRA Board Decision 83/2021 (updated) on goods/services within the law.
  - FRA Chair Decision 457/2020 on standard consumer-finance contract.
  - Additional consumer-finance regulatory decisions and circulars.
- FRA 2026 update: on 5 September 2026, FRA announced a comprehensive guide collecting licensing, governance, internal control, solvency, financial reporting, contract forms and branch registration rules under Law 18/2020 and the decisions/circulars issued to implement it:
  https://fra.gov.eg/fra_news/الرقابة-المالية-تصدر-أول-دليل-شامل-لق/
- The guide is a valuable regulatory research index, but must not be mistaken for legislation itself.

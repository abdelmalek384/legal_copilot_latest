## 1. State Council Law No. 47/1972 — Article 10 and jurisdiction structure

**Full searchable secondary transcription:** https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11397  
**Official Gazette identity:** issue 40, 5 October 1972. The Gazette issuance pages are reproduced at https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?ItemID=36190&NID=45787&Type=6&action=ViewActivePages.  
**Source status:** verbatim text below is from a secondary consolidated transcription. Article amendments, statutory corrections, and later judgments must be reconciled before treating it as current.

### المادة (10) — اختصاص محاكم مجلس الدولة

«تختص محاكم مجلس الدولة دون غيرها بالفصل فى المسائل الآتية:

أولاً: الطعون الخاصة بانتخابات المجالس المحلية.

ثانياً: المنازعات الخاصة بالمرتبات والمعاشات والمكافآت المستحقة للموظفين العموميين أو لورثتهم.

ثالثاً: الطلبات التي يقدمها ذوو الشأن بالطعن فى القرارات الإدارية النهائية الصادرة بالتعيين فى الوظائف العامة أو الترقية أو بمنح العلاوات.

رابعاً: الطلبات التى يقدمها الموظفون العموميون بإلغاء القرارات الإدارية الصادرة بإحالتهم إلى المعاش أو الاستيداع أو فصلهم بغير الطريق التأديبى.

خامساً: الطلبات التى يقدمها الأفراد أو الهيئات بإلغاء القرارات الإدارية النهائية.

سادساً: الطعون فى القرارات النهائية الصادرة من الجهات الإدارية فى منازعات الضرائب والرسوم وفقاً للقانون الذي ينظم كيفية نظر هذه المنازعات أمام مجلس الدولة.

سابعاً: دعاوى الجنسية.

ثامناً: الطعون التى ترفع عن القرارات النهائية الصادرة من جهات إدارية لها اختصاص قضائى، فيما عدا القرارات الصادرة من هيئات التوفيق والتحكيم فى منازعات العمل، وذلك متى كان مرجع الطعن عدم الاختصاص أو عيباً فى الشكل أو مخالفة للقوانين واللوائح أو الخطأ فى تطبيقها أو تأويلها.

تاسعاً: الطلبات التى يقدمها الموظفون العموميون بإلغاء القرارات النهائية للسلطات التأديبية.

عاشراً: طلبات التعويض عن القرارات المنصوص عليها فى البنود السابقة سواء رفعت بصفة أصلية أو تبعية.

حادي عشر: المنازعات الخاصة بعقود الالتزام أو الأشغال العامة أو التوريد أو بأى عقد إدارى آخر.

ثاني عشر: الدعاوى التأديبية المنصوص عليها فى هذا القانون.

ثالث عشر: الطعون فى الجزاءات الموقعة على العاملين بالقطاع العام فى الحدود المقررة قانوناً.

رابع عشر: سائر المنازعات الإدارية.

ويشترط فى طلبات إلغاء القرارات الإدارية النهائية أن يكون مرجع الطعن عدم الاختصاص أو عيباً فى الشكل أو مخالفة القوانين أو اللوائح أو الخطأ فى تطبيقها أو تأويلها أو إساءة استعمال السلطة.

ويعتبر فى حكم القرارات الإدارية رفض السلطات الإدارية أو امتناعها عن اتخاذ قرار كان من الواجب عليها اتخاذه وفقاً للقوانين واللوائح.»

### Adjacent jurisdiction provisions

**المادة (11):** «لا تختص محاكم مجلس الدولة بالنظر فى الطلبات المتعلقة بأعمال السيادة.»

**المادة (12):** requires personal interest and, for specified categories under Article 10 (items 3, 4 and 9), prior grievance to the issuing or hierarchical administrative authority and waiting for the statutory decision period.

**المادة (13):** assigns the Administrative Court the Article 10 matters not allocated to administrative or disciplinary courts and appellate review of administrative-court judgments, with a sixty-day period stated in the transcription.

**المادة (14):** allocates certain employment annulment, salary/pension/bonus and lower-value administrative-contract disputes to administrative courts.

**المادة (15):** allocates financial and administrative disciplinary proceedings involving the listed public employees and other specified categories to disciplinary courts.

**Use note:** Article 10 is the central subject-matter jurisdiction gateway, but the individual subparagraphs are not self-applying without the allocation rules in Articles 13–15, procedural conditions in Articles 12 and 24 onward, and later amendments. The sixty-day annulment period in Article 24 must not be conflated with other statutory time limits or special-law regimes.

## 2. Local Administration Law No. 43/1979 — local council, asset and governor powers

**Full searchable secondary transcription:** https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11568  
**Official Gazette issuance reproduction:** https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?NID=51030&action=viewactivepages&itemid=39949&type=6  
**Source status:** substantive articles below are copied from the secondary consolidated text. Several articles were amended by Laws 50/1981, 145/1988, 84/1996, 96/2003 and Decree-Law 115/2011; the amendment annotations must be checked against Gazette copies.

### المادة (12) — اختصاصات المجلس الشعبي المحلي للمحافظة

The secondary text provides that the governorate local council supervises local facilities and works within the governorate, oversees local development plans, and within the approved plan and budget may:
- approve the governorate's economic/social development plan, annual budget project and final account;
- set popular-participation plans;
- approve public projects for housing, construction and urban development;
- approve public-benefit facilities;
- approve local productive projects, especially food-security projects;
- propose local-character taxes;
- impose or amend local-character fees, shorten their term, exempt from them or repeal them, subject in the text to Cabinet approval;
- prepare and monitor literacy and family-planning programs;
- issue recommendations on local security and public order; and
- propose free zones, joint investment companies and joint projects, subject to planning and investment-law requirements.

The transcription labels Article 12 as substituted by Law 50/1981. **This is a historical statutory text, not proof that elected local councils are presently constituted or exercising those powers.** Constitutional Articles 180 and 242 and the actual implementation status must be read separately.

### المادة (14) — disposal or lease of local-government assets

The secondary text permits the governorate local council, for a public-benefit purpose, to dispose of or lease immovable or movable property free of charge, for nominal rent, or below market rent to specified public entities and public-benefit organizations.

For private legal persons or foreign entities, the text requires approval of the Prime Minister within the stated annual-value ceiling and Cabinet approval above it; it also imposes a maximum thirty-year lease term, continued dedication to the approved purpose, and an automatic termination/recovery mechanism if the purpose ceases or the recipient breaches the conditions.

The transcription annotates Article 14 as substituted by Law 50/1981. The historical monetary threshold and approval framework must be checked against later legislation, current public-property rules, and Gazette amendments before operational use.

### المادة (15) — local borrowing

The secondary transcription permits the governorate local council, within the approved plan and budget, to borrow for productive or investment projects necessary for the governorate or local units, subject to a debt ceiling of 40% of annual own-source revenues; exceeding that percentage or borrowing from a foreign entity requires Prime Minister approval. The text is annotated as substituted by Law 50/1981. Do not use the historical percentage as a current borrowing authorization without checking the full amendment and public-finance framework.

### المادة (25) — governor appointment and oath

The transcription states that each governor is appointed and removed by presidential decision, may not be a member of Parliament or local councils, receives ministerial treatment for salary/pension, and takes an oath before the President. The provision is annotated as substituted by Law 26/1982.

### المادة (26) — governor as executive authority

The transcription describes the governor as representative of the executive authority in the governorate, responsible for implementing state policy and supervising local service/production facilities. It also states powers to protect public and private State property and remove encroachments administratively. The first paragraph is annotated as amended by Laws 50/1981 and 145/1988.

### المادة (27) — delegated ministerial powers and supervision

«يتولى المحافظ بالنسبة إلى جميع المرافق العامة التى تدخل فى اختصاص وحدات الإدارة المحلية وفقا لأحكام هذا القانون جميع السلطات والاختصاصات التنفيذية المقررة للوزراء بمقتضى القوانين واللوائح ويكون المحافظ فى دائرة اختصاصه رئيساً لجميع الأجهزة والمرافق المحلية.»

The article further describes the governor's powers in relation to boards of public authorities operating local services and supervision of national facilities/ministerial branches not transferred to local units. The transcription annotates Article 27 as substituted by Law 50/1981.

### SCC effects recorded by the secondary source

- SCC Case 2/16, 3 February 1996: invalidity was limited to the specific mixed individual/party-list electoral design in Article 3 and Article 10; do not mark the entire law or the entirety of those articles invalid.
- SCC Case 36/18: the secondary page annotation contains a date typo in one location. The project's existing court acquisition records the judgment date as 3 January 1998 and the relevant invalidity concerning the unified local-fee decision and inseverable provisions of Article 4 of Law 43/1979 and Decision 870/1990. Confirm against the official SCC judgment and Gazette before any legal application.

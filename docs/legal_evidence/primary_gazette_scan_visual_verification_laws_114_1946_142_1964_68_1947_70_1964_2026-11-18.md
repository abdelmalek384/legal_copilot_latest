# Visual verification of historical Official Gazette page scans — four priority statutes

**Research date:** 18 November 2026  
**Host classification:** Manshurat (secondary legal archive). The downloaded PDF objects are scans of printed Official Gazette pages, but the files are not hosted by the Amiri Press and no publisher-certified hash/authentication is available. Do not label them as official Amiri Press digital originals. They are materially stronger than text-only transcriptions because the Gazette masthead, issue/date and printed page numbering are visible in the scans.

## 1. Law 114/1946 — Real Estate Publicity Law

- Manshurat source record: https://manshurat.org/node/69796
- PDF: https://manshurat.org/file/81500/download?token=G8bK0_YZ
- PDF length: **6 pages**.
- Visually inspected all six PDF pages (zero-indexed pages 0–5).
- Printed Gazette masthead on page 1: **الوقائع المصرية، العدد 85، في 24 أغسطس سنة 1946**.
- The law begins on the first scanned page and continues through the sixth; the scan is a six-page Gazette-page reproduction, not an official consolidated text. The full statutory article sequence in the current text has 70 numbered articles, while the 1946 original is the base version and later amendments are not included in that original issue.
- Metadata on the Manshurat record: law issued 11 August 1946, published 24 August 1946, effective 1 January 1947.

## 2. Law 142/1964 — Real-Estate Register Law

- Manshurat source record: https://manshurat.org/node/33844
- PDF: https://manshurat.org/file/44021/download?token=5Zwo9ziS
- PDF length: **7 pages**.
- Visually inspected all seven PDF pages (zero-indexed pages 0–6).
- Each page carries the Gazette masthead **الجريدة الرسمية، العدد 69، الصادر في 24 مارس سنة 1964** and printed page numbers **781–787**.
- The first page contains the promulgating provisions and beginning of the statute; the sequence continues through the final page. The scan is a printed Gazette-page reproduction hosted by Manshurat; no direct Amiri Press binary or certified hash is available.
- Metadata: issued and published 24 March 1964. This corrects any uncertainty about the likely issue number/date: the scan itself confirms issue 69.

## 3. Law 68/1947 — Notarization Law

- Manshurat source record: https://manshurat.org/node/70547
- PDF: https://manshurat.org/file/82270/download?token=fuDVQjIu
- PDF length: **2 pages**.

- Printed Gazette masthead: **الوقائع المصرية، العدد 58، في 3 يولية سنة 1947**; printed page numbers 2–3.

- Manshurat source record: https://manshurat.org/node/33668
- PDF: https://manshurat.org/file/43842/download?token=z-q7csZN

- Each page carries the Gazette masthead **الجريدة الرسمية، العدد 67، الصادر في 22 مارس سنة 1964**; printed page numbers **644–654**.

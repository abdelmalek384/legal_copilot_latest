## 2. Article map (seven Gazette pages inspected)

- **Article 1 — definitions:** food-manufacturing establishment, food imports, production inputs, prior notification, fast-release system and multiple-criteria analytical methodology.
- **Article 2 — scope:** applies to food-manufacturing establishments/importers on NFSA's white list that import production inputs for their own production/service activity, not for sale in the market, under Decision 6/2020.
- **Article 3 — eligibility:** application to the competent NFSA department; compliance with Decision 6/2020; active white-list registration with no suspension; inputs must be used in the establishment's own manufacturing and not marketed as inputs. NFSA examines the application and notifies acceptance/rejection within no more than three days.
- **Article 4 — enrolment documents:** item list, manufacturing processes to be performed on the inputs, list of products incorporating those inputs, stamped undertaking not to trade the inputs before manufacturing or place them on the market, and registration in NFSA's food-manufacturing-establishment register as an importer under the fast-release system.
- **Article 5 — establishment obligations:** follow NFSA requirements; provide complete consignment data; submit prior notification at least three days and no more than three months before arrival; enable NFSA sampling and control of the final product; pay relevant fees/service costs; retain records of manufacturing processes, input quantities, finished products and inventory.
- **Article 6 — risk-based sampling:** inputs accompanied by prior notification are subject to random sampling. Risk assessment considers origin country, risk of the production input, conformity evidence and the importer's prior compliance record. Non-compliance discovered after final release permits NFSA to take appropriate measures, including destruction, withdrawal and recall at the establishment's expense.
- **Article 7 — release channels:** documentary conformity, visual inspection, or visual inspection plus sampling.
- **Article 8 — technical committee:** a permanent technical committee reviews and adjusts risk criteria and analytical methodology in light of scientific evidence and additional data.
- **Article 9 — suspension/cancellation:** NFSA may stop or cancel fast-release participation for material non-compliance, suspension from the white list, trading inputs before manufacturing, destruction/withdrawal/recall decisions, or failure to pay fees/service charges.
- **Article 10:** publication in the Official Gazette and commencement the following day.

## 3. Crosswalk

- Decision 270/2024 expressly cites Decision 269/2022 on reduced sampling for white-list manufacturers, Decisions 6/2020 and 7/2020, Decision 11/2020, Decision 9/2021 on temporary release, and Decision 10/2021 on recognition of exporting-country competent authorities.
- The 2024 fast-release scheme is limited to production inputs for own use by qualifying white-list food-manufacturing establishments; it is not a general exemption from NFSA import controls.
- The decision requires prior notification, sampling, final-product controls and records; the three release paths are documentary check, visual inspection, or visual inspection plus sampling. Its cited Decisions 9/2021 (temporary release) and 10/2021 (recognition of exporting-country control performance) now have separate full-text evidence records in the repository.
- The Ministry of Finance repository currently labels the decision "in force". Still check later NFSA Board decisions, implementation circulars, white-list criteria, risk schedules, and any amendments before operational advice.

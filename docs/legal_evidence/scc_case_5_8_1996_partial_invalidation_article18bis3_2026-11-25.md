# Supreme Constitutional Court judgment — Case 5/8 (constitutional), 6 January 1996

## Source
- **Court:** Supreme Constitutional Court of Egypt.
- **Case:** No. 5 of Judicial Year 8, Constitutional.
- **Judgment date:** Saturday, 6 January 1996.
- **Subject:** Constitutional challenge to Article 18 bis 3 of Decree-Law 25/1929, added by Law 100/1985.
- **Official judgment page:** https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/592_8_5_1_2.html
- **Publication:** Official Gazette issue 3, 18 January 1996, as quoted by a later official SCC decision in Case 95/36 (2016): https://www.manshurat.org/file/76980/download?token=-JOSRGKb

## Operative disposition — exact scope
The Court declared Article 18 bis 3 unconstitutional **only insofar as it provided for**:

1. An absolute obligation on the divorced father to prepare suitable accommodation for his children and their custodian, even where the children had sufficient present funds for housing or the custodian had a dwelling in which she lived, whether rented or owned.
2. A restriction on the divorced father's right, where the marital home was rented, requiring preparation of suitable alternative accommodation for the children and custodian within a period that could not extend beyond the end of the former wife's waiting period (idda).

The judgment does **not** declare the whole of Article 18 bis 3 unconstitutional. The remaining portions of the article are not invalidated by this disposition. A later official SCC decision in Case 95/36 (2016) quotes the same two-part disposition and confirms publication in Official Gazette issue 3 on 18 January 1996.

## Effective date
Article 49 of Law 48/1979 provides the general effect of constitutional judgments from the day after publication unless a different date is specified. The event register provisionally records 19 January 1996. The original Gazette issue 3 page image and a check for any express date in the judgment remain outstanding.

## Why this matters for the corpus
Do not mark Article 18 bis 3 as wholly repealed, deleted or unusable. A consolidated version must preserve the surviving text and clearly annotate the two invalidated portions, with the judgment citation and exact scope. The 1985 text and the 1996 constitutional disposition must be represented as separate version/effect records.

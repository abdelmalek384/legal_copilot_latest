## 3. Relationship to Decision 662/2025 and the 50-micron statement

- Decision 662/2025 expressly cites Decision 559/2022 as the instrument requiring producers and importers of plastic shopping bags to comply with Egyptian Standard 3040.
- Decision 662/2025 Article 1 applies its EPR designation to shopping bags produced/imported in accordance with Standard 3040.
- A later official SIS government communication states that Decision 559/2022 / Standard 3040 requires manufacturers of single-use plastic bags to produce bags with a thickness of at least 50 microns.
- That 50-micron statement is an official government implementation statement, but the searchable fragment of Decision 559/2022 captured here does **not** reproduce the technical standard or its thickness clause. Do not cite Decision 559/2022's table fragment as if it contains the full 50-micron specification.
- Standard 3040 itself is a technical standard distinct from the ministerial decision that makes compliance obligatory. Its complete authoritative text has not been acquired in this pass.

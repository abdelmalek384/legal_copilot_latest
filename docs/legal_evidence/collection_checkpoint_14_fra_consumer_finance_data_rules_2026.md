# Collection checkpoint 14 — FRA 2026 consumer-finance data and branch controls

**Date:** 2026-11-17. FRA primary-source Gazette PDFs and regulator publications inspected. Preserve each decision as a separate instrument.

## 1. FRA Chair Decision 2864/2026 — consumer-finance database connectivity

- Official Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alamiria_2026_2864_-R.pdf
- Gazette: issue 210 (supplement A), 23 September 2026.
- Decision dated 9 September 2026.
- Full searchable 2-page PDF text inspected.
- Requires licensed consumer-finance companies to provide the technological infrastructure needed to connect their databases to the FRA database under FRA-set controls.
- Requires electronic systems to make available:
  1. Customer data immediately after approval to grant financing.
  2. Real-time transactions involving customer purchases of goods/services or obtaining services.
  3. Classification of financed goods and services.
  4. Customer classification by repayment regularity/delinquency.
  5. Company solvency-related data and indicators.
  6. Any other data requested by FRA.
- Article 2 requires FRA to issue implementation controls within six months from the decision's commencement.
- Article 3: published in Al-Waqa'i and FRA website; effective the day after publication.

## 2. FRA Chair Decision 2863/2026 — digital verification and alternative-data behavioral scoring

- Official Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alamiria_2026_2863_-R.pdf
- Gazette: issue 210 (supplement A), 23 September 2026.
- Decision dated 9 September 2026.
- Full searchable 2-page PDF text inspected.
- Applies to licensed consumer-finance companies.
- Before granting or renewing consumer finance, companies must digitally verify customer data and identify customer assessment based on behavioral scoring using alternative data, through the credit-information company.
- Companies must consider the resulting information when making their credit decision.
- Does not displace existing customer-data verification rules or digital identity controls under FRA Board Decisions, including Decision 186/2024 as amended by Decision 133/2026.
- Does not displace existing credit-information rules for customer dealings with finance providers.
- General provisions commence the day after Gazette publication; provisions relating to behavioral-scoring assessment take effect from 1 April 2027, subject to readiness of the dedicated system at the credit-information company.
- Model these as separate commencement dates within one instrument.

## 3. FRA Board Decision 44/2026 and Decision 100/2026 — non-bank finance branch rules (regulator publication summary)

- FRA Decision 44/2026 official page: https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قرار-مجلس-إدارة-الهيئة-رقم-44-لسنة-2026/
- FRA official news summary of Decision 100/2026 amending Decision 44/2026: https://fra.gov.eg/fra_news/هيئة-الرقابة-المالية-تعدّل-ضوابط-فروع-شركات-التمويل-غير-المصرفي-قبل-انتهاء-فترة-توفيق-الأوضاع/
- The official FRA news publication (25 June 2026) says Decision 100/2026 amends Decision 44/2026. Decision 44/2026 gave companies six months, ending 25 August 2026, to regularize.
- The FRA summary says companies against which judicial judgments, criminal-prosecution requests or administrative measures have been issued and which seek branch registration must have executed the judgment/settled the violation/removed the administrative-measure grounds and waited three months.
- Where the underlying cause is not removable, a period of at least three months and no more than three years is required, depending on severity as FRA assesses.
- Applicants must submit regulatory reports and annual/periodic financial statements on time and address inspection/compliance observations.
- Risk and credit officers may supervise up to four regional areas, each consisting of up to five financing branches, subject to portfolio concentration/risk considerations.
- Decision 44/2026's branch framework covers marketing branches (promotion/document collection only), mobile branches and seasonal branches; it also addresses organizational structures, credit decision authority, documents, registration conditions, inspection/study fees and administrative measures.
- This section is a regulator's official explanatory publication, not the full text of Decisions 44/2026 and 100/2026. Retrieve Gazette PDFs and compare before article-level ingestion.

## 4. FRA Circular 7/2026 — monthly data reporting to FRA (image-only circular)

- Official FRA index lists Circular 7/2026 as requiring licensed non-bank financial activity providers to submit activity-related data to FRA's data platform.
- Official image: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/كتاب-دوري-رقم-7-لسنة-2026-1.jpg
- Image visibly states date 7 October 2026.
- It requires licensed non-bank finance entities to provide monthly data to the FRA data platform, with the platform period running from day 1 through day 15 of each month.
- It requires data to be reviewed for accuracy/completeness and consistency with the entity's own records, with corrections/explanations as needed.
- The regulator says the platform will reopen for all relevant entities from 12 October through 29 October 2026 to collect/review data previously entered since the start of 2026.
- Executive management responsible for the activity must take necessary action and is responsible for non-compliance.
- The circular is a raster image, not searchable text. This transcription is visual/manual, not machine OCR; compare carefully with the image before operational use.

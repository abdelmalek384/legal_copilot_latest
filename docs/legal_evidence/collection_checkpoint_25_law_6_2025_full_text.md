## Law No. 6 of 2025
**Concerning certain tax incentives and facilities for projects whose annual turnover does not exceed EGP 20 million**

### Chapter One — Definitions and General Provisions

**Article 1 — Definitions**
For purposes of this law:
1. Projects subject to the law: projects whose annual turnover does not exceed EGP 20 million and which request to benefit from its provisions, including professional activities, whether tax-registered on the law's effective date or not.
2. The Authority: Egyptian Tax Authority.
3. Tax law: Income Tax Law or VAT Law.

**Article 2 — Determining annual turnover**
Without prejudice to rules/procedures in the tax law, annual turnover is determined using any of:
1. Last final tax assessment data for a project registered with the Authority on the law's effective date.
2. Last tax return filed by a registered project that had not been tax-assessed by that date.
3. The return filed by a project that registers after the law's effective date.
4. Data available through the e-invoice or e-receipt system.

**Article 3 — Eligibility conditions**
To benefit from the incentives/facilities:
1. Submit the tax returns required by Article 12 within statutory deadlines.
2. Join the Authority's electronic systems, including e-invoice or e-receipt, according to mandatory phases set by the Authority Head, and issue required invoices/receipts.

**Article 4 — Exclusions**
The law does not apply to:
1. Professional consulting activities where at least 90% of annual turnover is derived from professional consulting to one or two persons.
2. Projects that take any action/behavior to enter the law's umbrella unlawfully, including dividing or splitting an existing activity without an economic justification; burden of proof is on the Tax Authority.
The Finance Minister may, by decision, exempt certain activities from paragraph 1.

**Article 5 — Withdrawal from the regime**
A project subject to the law may not withdraw its request to benefit from the law before five years have elapsed from the day following submission of the application.

**Article 6 — Gap-filling**
For matters not specifically provided for in this law, the applicable tax law or Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020 applies, as appropriate.

### Chapter Two — Tax Incentives

**Article 7 — Exemptions**
Projects subject to the law are exempt from:
- State financial-resource development fee.
- Stamp tax.
- Notarization/registration fees for incorporation contracts of companies and establishments, credit facilities and mortgage contracts related to their activities, and other guarantees submitted to obtain financing.
- The referenced taxes/fees for contracts to register land necessary for establishing those projects.

**Article 8 — Capital gains exemption**
Capital gains arising from disposal of fixed assets or production machinery/equipment of projects subject to the law are exempt from the tax due on those gains.

**Article 9 — Dividend distributions**
Profit distributions arising from the activity of projects subject to the law are not subject to the tax otherwise imposed on those distributions under the Income Tax Law.

**Article 10 — Turnover-based income tax**
Income tax due from projects subject to this law is determined as a percentage of annual turnover:
1. 0.4% where annual turnover is less than EGP 500,000.
2. 0.5% where annual turnover is EGP 500,000 or more but less than EGP 2 million.
3. 0.75% where annual turnover is EGP 2 million or more but less than EGP 3 million.
4. 1% where annual turnover is EGP 3 million or more but less than EGP 10 million.
5. 1.5% where annual turnover is EGP 10 million or more and does not exceed EGP 20 million.

If the project's annual turnover exceeds EGP 20 million in any year during the five years from the application date, it may continue benefiting once and for no more than one year at the rate specified in item 5. If turnover exceeds the threshold again or repeats during that period, the project ceases to benefit from the law from the following year.

### Chapter Three — Tax Facilities

**Article 11 — Withholding/advance payment**
Projects subject to this law are not subject to the withholding or advance-payment-on-account systems provided in the Income Tax Law 91/2005.

**Article 12 — Returns and audit**
- A separate annual return form is prescribed for the project's commercial, industrial or professional activity, set by Finance Minister decision based on the Tax Authority Head's proposal, and submitted within the deadlines under Unified Tax Procedures Law.
- VAT returns are filed every three months on the form prepared for that purpose, during the month following the end of the relevant period, accompanied by payment.
- For payroll tax and similar taxes, projects are required only to submit the annual tax-settlement return under the Unified Tax Procedures Law.
- Returns of projects subject to this law are examined after five years from the date of the request to benefit, at the level of income tax and VAT.

**Article 13 — Simplified books and records**
Projects subject to the law are exempt from maintaining the registers, books and documents specified in Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020. They must instead comply with simplified systems for registers, books, documents and procedures issued by Finance Minister decision based on the Tax Authority Head's proposal.

### Chapter Four — Final Provisions

**Article 14 — Implementing decisions**
Finance Minister issues decisions necessary to implement the law within one month from its effective date.

**Article 15 — Amendments to MSME Law 152/2020**
Repeals Articles 85, 86, 87, 93, 94, 95, 96, 97, 98 and 99 of Law 152/2020 on Development of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises.

**Article 16 — Commencement**
Published in the Official Gazette and effective from the first day of the month following publication. Signed 12 February 2025.

# Collection checkpoint 28 — Anti-Money Laundering Law 80/2002 and updated AML/CFT rules (2026-11-17)

## 1. AML Law 80/2002 — official FRA updated Arabic text

- Official FRA updated-law page: https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قانون-رقم-80-لسنة-2002-بإصدار-قانون-مكافحة-غس/
- Official updated PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/قانون-مكافحة-غسل-الأموال-1.pdf
- Alternate official PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/قانون-1.pdf
- Search-indexed PDF text identifies Law 80/2002 and its amendment chain.
- Gazette base publication: 22 May 2002, issue 20 bis.
- FRA updated page identifies prior amendments:
  - Law 78/2003.
  - Law 181/2008.
  - Presidential Decree-Law 36/2014.
  - Law 17/2020.
  - Law 154/2022.
- The 2022-updated FRA version is a primary regulator consolidated-text locator, but compare all amendments to original Gazette pages before claiming a complete Gazette-consolidated text.

### Extracted Arabic definitions and enactment text
- Promulgation Article 1 applies the attached AML Law.
- Promulgation Article 2 directs issuance of the Executive Regulation within three months.
- Promulgation Article 3 states commencement on the day after publication.
- The updated Article 1 defines money/assets to include material and virtual assets, economic resources, national/foreign currency, securities, legal instruments, digital/electronic records, rights, proceeds and virtual assets with digital value usable for payment or investment.
- It defines predicate offence as a felony or misdemeanour under Egyptian law, whether committed domestically or abroad, where punishable in both countries.
- It defines proceeds as money/assets obtained directly or indirectly from a predicate offence.
- Financial institutions include banks, exchange companies, money-transfer providers and securities-sector entities; the complete definition list must be extracted from the PDF.

  - FRA Board Decision 100/2020 (updated 2024) on corporate governance for non-bank financial companies.
  - FRA Board Decision 53/2018 (updated 2024) on licensing and share-ownership controls.
  - AML Law 80/2002 (updated 2022).
  - Executive Regulation PM Decision 951/2003 (updated 2023).
  - AML/CFT due diligence procedures for customers of FRA-supervised financial institutions, issued by the AML/CFT Unit.

  - Laws 94/2015 (Counter-Terrorism), 8/2015 (terrorist entities/lists), 11/2017 and 22/2018 (freezing/inventory/management/disposal of funds of terrorist groups/terrorists).
- Collect each item as a separate instrument or procedure. FRA decision, AML Unit procedure, law, executive regulation and governance rule must not be merged into a single "AML law" document.

## 5. Updated September 2026 customer due diligence procedures

- FRA Circular 8/2026 (8 November 2026) confirms that the AML/CFT Unit adopted an updated September 2026 version of CDD procedures for customers of FRA-supervised financial institutions, replacing the previous February 2020 version.
- Circular 8/2026 requires institutions to:
  - comply with the updated procedures;
  - submit the updated board-approved procedures/internal-control manual with the 2026 annual AML/CFT activity report by 31 January 2027;
  - if unable to approve the updated version, explain why and submit the updated manual;
  - update customer onboarding forms and required information/documents for identifying and verifying customers and beneficial owners;
  - consider accessibility for customers with disabilities, illiterate customers and older people.
- Official Circular 8/2026 PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/كتاب-دوري-8.pdf
- The underlying September 2026 CDD procedures are a separate missing source. Do not substitute the circular for the actual procedure manual.

# Collection checkpoint 169 — Law 168/2025 Article 3 extension and Decision 480/2026 deadline ambiguity
**Date:** 18 November 2026

## Exact legal chain recovered

1. **Law 168/2025, Article 3:** Applications to regularize private State-property possession are to be submitted within six months beginning on the date the Executive Regulation comes into force. The President may extend that period for further equivalent periods, with a maximum aggregate extension of three years.
2. **PM Decision 200/2026:** Issued and published 18 January 2026; its Article 4 says it takes effect on the day after publication. The Executive Regulation therefore took effect **19 January 2026**.
3. **Initial six-month deadline discrepancy:**
   - A literal calculation from the regulation's effective date points to **19 July 2026** as the corresponding six-month date.
   - Multiple official Cairo Governorate district notices expressly announced **Saturday 18 July 2026** as the final day, including notices dated 20–28 June 2026.
   - Do not silently choose one. The notices are authoritative evidence of the administration's public position, but they do not amend the statute or regulation. A written Ministry/Presidency/State Council interpretation or an official corrigendum is still required to resolve the one-day discrepancy.
4. **Presidential Decision 480/2026:** Official DSpace metadata confirms Gazette issue 45 bis (ج), issued 10 November 2026, original bundle 178.37 KB, publisher Amiri Press. The operative Article 1 says: “تمد المدة المشار إليها بالفقرة الأولى من المادة (3) ... لمدة أخرى مماثلة”. Article 2 orders publication. Press reproductions confirm the same two-article text.
5. **Extension end date remains uncertified:** Decision 480 says “another equivalent period” but does not state the new period's start or a calendar expiry. Two constructions remain possible:
   - consecutive extension from expiry of the first six-month period: conditional end around **18/19 January 2027** (depending on the unresolved first-period day);
   - a new six-month period running from Decision 480's publication/effectiveness on 10 November 2026: conditional end around **10/11 May 2027**.
   The exact start and endpoint cannot be certified from the operative text alone without authoritative interpretation or implementing circular. Do not state either as a definitive deadline.

## Same-number instrument disambiguation

A different instrument also bears number 480/2026:
- **Housing Minister Decision 480/2026**, published Egyptian Proceedings issue 106, 14 May 2026, concerns deleting two properties from Cairo's architectural-heritage register under Law 144/2006. It is unrelated to Presidential Decision 480/2026 under Law 168/2025.
- The Housing Minister decision's accessible PDF is a two-page reproduction at https://eg-pp.com/Images/Index?T=1&folder=951&path=2026-5-14-2026-480.pdf. Article 1 deletes one property in El-Zeitoun and one in Maadi; Article 2 orders publication.
- Do not conflate the two Decision 480/2026 records or their subject matters.

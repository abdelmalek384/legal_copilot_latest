## Visible promulgation text from first inspected page

The scan visibly identifies:

- Law No. 25 of 1968, issuing the Law of Evidence in Civil and Commercial Matters.
- Gazette issue 22 dated 30 May 1968.
- Article 1: repeals Book 6 of the Civil Code (Articles 400–417), the first book of the Commercial Code, and the Evidence Law in civil/commercial matters issued by Law 77/1949, subject to the specific exception for provisions on commercial evidence in the Commercial Code.
- Article 2: the law is published in the Official Gazette and enters into force six months after publication.
- The attached Evidence Law begins with Book One, General Provisions, including rules on proof and judicial discretion.

## First visible substantive rules (visual transcription; must be checked against scan)

- Article 1: the creditor must prove the obligation; the debtor must prove discharge.
- Article 2: facts to be proved must be relevant to the action and admissible.
- Article 3: where the court determines the evidence procedure, it must set a time limit not exceeding three months, subject to extension where justified.
- The scan continues with rules on the judge's role, evidence procedures, documentary evidence and the evidentiary force of written records.

## 1. Law 95/1992 — scope and institutions
- Gazette issue 25 bis, 22 June 1992; effective 23 June 1992.
- Establishes regulatory framework for capital markets, securities issuance, listing, trading, brokers, investment funds, public offerings, takeovers, securities lending/borrowing, margin trading, custody, clearing and settlement.
- FRA is the current regulator following Law 10/2009 and transfer of the former Capital Market Authority's functions.
- Law applies alongside Companies Law 159/1981 where no special capital-market rule exists.
- Core chapters:
  1. Issuance of securities.
  2. Public offering and subscription.
  3. Listing and delisting.
  4. Trading and brokers.
  5. Investment funds.
  6. Securities companies and market intermediaries.
  7. Securities lending/borrowing and margin trading.
  8. Custody, clearing and settlement.
  9. Enforcement and penalties.
- Exact current article text must be taken from FRA's consolidated PDF and checked against each amending law.

# Environment Minister Decision 279/2025 — WMRA amendment, appeal and rice-straw fees

## 1. Primary-source capture

The official SIS-hosted Gazette PDF was opened and its text extracted. The issue contents list Environment Minister Decision 279/2025 at printed page 16. The decision itself occupies printed pages 16–17.

The decision is dated 24 September 2025 and cites Law 4/1994, Waste Management Law 202/2020 and its Executive Regulation, and Environment Minister Decision 113/2022. Its preamble records WMRA Board approval at meetings held on 18 August 2025 and 14 September 2025.

## 2. Article 1 — two new service charges

In addition to applicable VAT, WMRA charges:

| Service | Fee |
|---|---:|
| Studying an application to make any amendment to licences, approvals or permits issued by WMRA for integrated management activities involving hazardous and non-hazardous materials/waste | EGP 2,000 |
| Submitting an appeal against decisions arising from application of Waste Management Law 202/2020 | EGP 2,000 |

The first charge is for study of an application to amend an existing instrument; it is not described as a fee for the amendment itself or as an annual licence charge. The second is a fee for submitting an appeal; the text does not state that payment guarantees acceptance or success.

## 3. Article 2 — reduced rice-straw collection/transport licence fee

By way of exception to Article 1 of Environment Minister Decision 113/2022, the fee for issuing a licence to practise collection and transport of agricultural waste (rice straw) to collection sites is reduced to:

**EGP 1,000**, excluding applicable VAT.

The wording is specific to rice straw and collection sites. Do not generalize this reduced amount to all agricultural waste, all collection/transport activities, or other waste categories.

## 4. Article 3 — revenue destination

The proceeds of the amounts prescribed by Decision 279/2025 are credited to the Waste Management Regulatory Authority account. Collected VAT is remitted to the Egyptian Tax Authority.

## 5. Article 4 — commencement

The decision is published in Egyptian Proceedings and takes effect from its date of issuance. The instrument date is 24 September 2025; Gazette publication followed on 2 November 2025.

This express commencement clause must be preserved in retrieval. Do not assume the effective date is the day after publication, as was stated in Environment Minister Decision 120/2025.

## 6. Relationship to the WMRA fee framework

| Instrument | Captured role |
|---|---|
| Decision 113/2022 | Baseline WMRA fee schedule for integrated waste-management licences, hazardous-waste approvals and related services |
| Decision 120/2025 | Inserts Article 3 bis into Decision 113/2022 with five fee categories, including export and Basel-notification fees |
| Decision 150/2025 | Facility-level hazardous-material/waste approval fees and used-cooking-oil collection/transport/storage/export fees; currently captured only provisionally from secondary text pending Gazette page match |
| Decision 279/2025 | Adds amendment-application and appeal study charges; reduces rice-straw collection/transport licence fee to EGP 1,000 |
| Decision 346/2025 | Separate committee-approval decision; Gazette file remains restricted and operative content is not captured |

Decision 279/2025 expressly modifies the rice-straw licence fee by exception to Decision 113/2022. It does not purport in the captured text to repeal the entire 113/2022 schedule or Decision 120/2025. Do not infer that the two EGP 2,000 charges replace the fees under Decision 120/2025.

## 7. Retrieval controls

- Query terms for amendment service: تعديل الترخيص، تعديل الموافقة، تعديل التصريح، مقابل دراسة طلب تعديل.
- Query terms for appeal: تظلم، قرارات قانون المخلفات ٢٠٢ لسنة ٢٠٢٠، مقابل تقديم طلب تظلم.
- Query terms for rice straw: فش الأرز، قش الأرز، المخلفات الزراعية، مواقع التجميع، جمع ونقل.
- For an amendment request, retrieve Decision 279/2025 Article 1 alongside the underlying licence/approval and relevant original fee instrument.
- For an appeal, retrieve Decision 279/2025 Article 1 and the substantive decision being challenged; do not suggest that the fee itself determines admissibility or merits.
- For rice-straw collection/transport, retrieve Decision 279/2025 Article 2 and Decision 113/2022 together; preserve the specific rice-straw exception.

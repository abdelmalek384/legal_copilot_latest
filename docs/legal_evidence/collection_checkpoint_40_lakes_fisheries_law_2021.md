## 1. Law 146/2021 — scope and promulgation
- Applies to protection/development of lakes and fisheries, without prejudice to Natural Reserves Law 102/1983 and Water Resources/Irrigation legislation.
- Applies to lakes designated by Presidential decision, all water bodies and fish-farming lands designated by PM decision for protection/development of fisheries.
- Executive Regulation to be issued within six months; existing regulations continue insofar as consistent until issuance.
- Repeals Fishing/Aquatic Organisms and Fish Farms Law 124/1983, Presidential Decrees 336/1974 and 420/1978, Article 3 of Decree 70/2007 and Decree 190/1983 establishing the General Authority for Fishery Resources Development, plus conflicting provisions.
- 68 substantive articles in the current secondary transcription.

## 2. Key substantive rules inspected
- Establishes an economic public authority named the Lakes and Fisheries Protection and Development Agency, affiliated with the Prime Minister, with legal personality and Cairo headquarters.
- Agency objectives include protecting/developing lakes, lagoons, shores, buffers and fisheries/aquatic life; planning and supervising aquaculture and fishing.
- Agency has independent budget and statutory collection/enforcement powers; due amounts may enjoy general priority and administrative seizure.
- Prohibits draining/filling protected lakes, lagoons and ponds except for national purposes.
- Requires Agency licence for water-lifting equipment in lakes/lagoons/shores and certain connected inundated private lands.
- Requires licence for constructing islands, bridges, dams, enclosures or fish farms in lakes/lagoons/shores, subject to specified purposes and conditions.
- Prohibits encroachment on Agency-supervised land and unlicensed construction/activity within lake buffer zones.
- Fishing vessels must be numbered, categorized and approved; tracking devices required for licence issuance/renewal. Disabling/removing tracking equipment can cause licence suspension for three months, one year on repeat and permanent withdrawal on further repeat.
- Prohibits unlicensed fishing gear, undersized fish sale/possession, grinding/pressing fish without licence, unlicensed ornamental fish fishing, unlicensed introduction of foreign aquatic species, and unauthorized collection/transport/possession of fish fry, sea turtles and marine mammals.
- Prohibits toxic, radioactive, chemical, petroleum, oil, ship waste, laboratory/factory waste and untreated sewage discharge into lakes/territorial waters where harmful to aquatic resources.
- Fish farms/hatcheries require Agency licence and annual fee not exceeding EGP 2,000 per feddan or part; PM decision identifies aquaculture areas.
- Marine fish cages require licence and Armed Forces Operations Authority/other relevant approvals.
- Enforcement, penalties, inspection, environmental/health/technical standards and detailed licensing rules require full article extraction.

## 3. Executive Regulation 706/2023
- Promulgates detailed implementation rules and repeals the prior Executive Regulation under Agriculture Minister Decision 303/1987 for fishing/aquatic organisms/fish farms.
- Covers agency powers, lake boundaries, licences, fishing vessels, gear, closed seasons/areas, fish sizes, landing sites, aquaculture, hatcheries, cages, inspection and fees.
- Full regulation text must be extracted and compared to official Gazette; preserve technical schedules and licence forms.

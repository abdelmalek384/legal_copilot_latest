# Collection checkpoint 34 — Labour Law 14/2025 and 2025–2026 implementing instruments (2026-11-17)

## 1. Labour Law 14/2025 — official Amiri Press full text

- Official Amiri Press Arabic text:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=vPdoWGGYwoE%3D
- Gazette issue 18 supplement (b), 3 May 2025; commencement 1 September 2025 (as recorded in project source review).
- Ministry of Labour official index and publications:
  - Laws and legislation: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/القوانين-والتشريعات/
  - Law publication: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الإصدارات/قانون-العمل-رقم-14-لسنة-2025/
- The law replaces Labour Law 12/2003 and Law 180/2008, subject to the transitional provisions in its promulgation articles.
- It applies to employment relationships in the private sector and to foreign workers in Egypt as specified, while excluding specified categories such as State employees, domestic workers and others unless a special provision applies.
- The official Amiri Press page exposes machine-readable text across the Law, including provisions on wages, working time, leave, discipline, termination, labour courts, National Wages Council, inspection, occupational safety and health, collective bargaining and labour organizations.
- The law's 267 articles and commencement/transitional clauses need article-by-article extraction and Gazette comparison. The project's existing Labour Law 14/2025 Gazette-comparison packet and article inventory must be reused rather than regenerated.

## 2. Minister of Labour Decision 162/2026 — general rules for workplace regulations

- Secondary full text:
  https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/08/162-2026.html
- Secondary searchable explanation:
  https://laeha-misr.vercel.app/decree-162/
- Gazette: Al-Waqa'i issue 176 (supplement), 13 August 2026.
- Decision dated 28 July 2026; effective the day after publication according to the transcription.
- Contains 66 articles in six chapters.
- Applies to employers with 10 or more workers, requiring a workplace regulation tailored to the establishment's activity.
- Regulates drafting/submission/approval of internal work rules, consultation with the competent labour union, job announcements/selection, hiring, probation, employment contracts and employee files, performance evaluation, promotion, transfer, delegation, discipline, termination, grievance and periodic review.
- Article 19 prohibits internal regulations from reducing statutory worker rights; better rights/benefits under individual/collective contracts, internal rules or custom remain protected.
- Official Ministry of Labour announcement dated 5 September 2026 describes the rules as an important implementing decision under Law 14/2025:
  https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-يعلن-نص-القواعد-العامة-لائحة-تنظيم-العمل-بالقطاع-الخاص/
- Obtain the original Gazette PDF and visually verify the 66 articles; do not use the secondary transcription as final source-matched text.

## 3. PM Decision 3165/2026 — National Wages Council

- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=EreMgz%2FNoYY%3D
- Decision title: system of work of the National Wages Council and determination of its competences.
- It cites Labour Law 14/2025, Trade Union Organizations Law 213/2017, Social Insurance Law 148/2019 and earlier PM Decision 2659/2020.
- It allows the Council to establish specialist subcommittees, with balanced representation of government, employers' organizations and labour unions as appropriate.
- Extract full articles, meeting/quorum/decision procedures, wage-setting and minimum-wage review powers, and relation to Articles 102–106 of Labour Law 14/2025.

## 4. Labour Court and free legal assistance implementation

- Justice Minister Decision 4621/2025 designates seats for labour courts at primary courts.
- Justice Minister Decision 4693/2025 establishes and regulates Labour Legal Assistance Offices at each labour court and other locations where the court sits:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Jx8EYU41ZXI%3D
- Decision 4693/2025 cites Civil/Commercial Procedure Law 13/1968, Judicial Authority Law 46/1972, Civil Service Law 81/2016 and Labour Law 14/2025; it builds on earlier labour judicial-assistance offices.
- Justice Minister Decision 5614/2025 assigns venues for labour cases within some partial-court jurisdictions:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=7FYalJBNQ2s%3D
- It states effect from 1 October 2025 for the specified venues.
- These are procedural/administrative implementation instruments, not amendments to substantive Labour Law.

## 6. Temporary remote-work regime — PM Decision 982/2026 (historical status needs checking)

- PM Decision 982/2026:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=X+ynf8GR0jI%3D
- It applies a remote-work system to establishments/entities subject to Labour Law 14/2025.
- The decision's preamble cites Labour Law 14/2025, Child Law 12/1996, Disability Rights Law 10/2018 and Civil Society Law 149/2019.
- PM Decisions 1305/2026, 1572/2026, 2033/2026, 2377/2026 and 2590/2026 successively extended the system for May, June, July, August and September 2026.
- Decision 2590/2026 states continuation during September 2026:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=HAJdFlN7HCw%3D
- As of 17 November 2026, these located extensions do not establish a continuing October/November remote-work mandate. Search for a later extension before describing the temporary system as current.

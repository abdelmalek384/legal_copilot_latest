# Collection checkpoint 36 — Trade Union Organizations Law 213/2017 and labour representation (2026-11-17)

## 1. Trade Union Organizations and Protection of the Right to Organize Law 213/2017

- Ministry of Labour official laws index lists Law 213/2017 as a basic labour statute:
  https://www.labour.gov.eg/en/laws-and-legislations/
- It also lists a later amendment described in one page label as Law 142/2014; this is a metadata error. Ministry of Labour's own historical report correctly identifies **Law 142/2019** as amending Law 213/2017:
  https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/الجمهورية-الجديدة-وزارة-العمل-تصدر-تقرير-ا-بشأن-ملف-العمل-في-10-سنوات/
- Law 142/2019 reduced the minimum membership required to form a trade union organization and removed imprisonment penalties, according to the Ministry's summary.
- Ministry of Labour official index also exposes the Executive Regulation and implementing decisions.

## 2. Executive Regulation and model bylaws

- Ministerial Decision 35/2018 issues the Executive Regulation of Law 213/2017.
- Ministerial Decision 36/2018 provides indicative/model bylaws for labour trade union organizations.
- Official Ministry PDF for Decision 36/2018:
  https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/tyhngkrj/2018-36-لائحة-النظام-الاساسي-للمنظمات-النقابية.pdf
- Gazette issue 61 (supplement), 14 March 2018.
- Decision 36/2018 states that trade union organizations have the right to create their own bylaws and may use the attached models until they prepare their own.
- Ministerial Decision 42/2017 concerns the basic regulation of the Trade Union Law; Ministry index lists it.
- Obtain the official Decision 35/2018 full text and any amendments, including election/registration procedures, membership thresholds, financial rules, union leave, collective bargaining and dispute-resolution rules.

## 3. Election and union-organization implementation decisions

Ministry of Labour's official index lists:
- Decision 37/2018: dates for candidacy/elections for trade union organization boards.
- Decision 36/2018: model bylaws.
- Decision 35/2018: Executive Regulation.
- Decision 88/2018: establishment/management data to be provided by establishments to labour directorates for company board election processes.
- Decision 89/2018: candidacy rules for boards of company administrations.
- Decision 87/2018: worker representation on boards of public-sector/public-business entities.
- Decision 109/2018: election supervision (index labels it "unpublished"; verify whether it is a binding publication or internal instruction).
- Decision 50/2022: procedures for labour unions to participate in administrative-state negotiations and join collective agreements.
- Decision 227/2022: unified procedural guide for establishing labour trade union organizations.
- Decision 79/2023: issued under Law 213/2017 and Decision 35/2018, with union election-related provisions.
- Official Labour Ministry PDF for Decisions 79/2023 and related material:
  https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/ssnnpafx/قرار-رقم-80-لسنة-2023-بشأن-تنظيم-مزاولة-عمليات-التدريب-المهني-وتطوير-مستوياته.pdf
  The file includes Decision 79/2023 in its searchable text; verify whether the filename/record is a combined PDF or mislabelled attachment before using.

## 4. Employee representation on boards

- Law 18/2018 concerns representation of workers on boards of directors of entities affiliated with public-sector/public-business companies.
- The Ministry of Labour identifies it as a distinct statute designed to ensure worker participation in decisions affecting them.
- Retrieve Law 18/2018, implementing decisions and board-election rules. Do not conflate employee board representation with trade union organization rights under Law 213/2017.

## 5. 2026 legislative development — proposed amendments only

- Ministry of Labour news dated 19 October 2026 reports that the newly formed Supreme Council for Social Dialogue discussed proposed amendments to Law 213/2017:
  https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/في-ثاني-اجتماعاته-بعد-التشكيل-الجديد-وزير-العمل-يناقش-تعديلات-مقترحة-على-قانون-المنظمات-النقابية-العمالية/
- This is a legislative proposal/discussion, not proof of a bill passed or an enacted amendment. Keep it in a legislative-development register, not the operative law corpus.

## 6. Cross-links with Labour Law 14/2025

- Law 14/2025 references trade union organizations in provisions on workplace regulations, union consultation, collective bargaining, collective disputes and worker representation.
- PM Decision 3165/2026 on the National Wages Council requires balanced representation of government, employers and labour unions and cites Law 213/2017.
- Minister of Labour Decision 162/2026 on internal workplace regulations requires consultation with the competent trade union organization.
- Link each statutory/decision article to the specific Law 213/2017 provision and do not assume every employee is a union member.

## Schedule count reconciliation

The Gazette-layout page images show a continuous printed serial sequence from **1 through 435**, with the final entry numbered 435 on printed page 30. Article 1 of the 2023 predecessor Decision 111/2023 also expressly states that its attached list contains 435 occupations. The earlier repository description of a “434-occupation” schedule should therefore be corrected to **435 numbered schedule entries**. This does not yet establish that all 435 occupation names/codes are unique; duplicate code/name checks and official-original comparison remain outstanding.

## OCR and transcription cautions

- The PDF's searchable text layer is not authoritative for the occupation codes. Visual page images show that some extracted/searchable codes differ from the image, so any parser must preserve image-reviewed corrections separately from raw OCR.
- The LAW HUB HTML schedule is a useful discovery/transcription source but cannot be treated as identical to the Gazette page images; the five code differences above demonstrate the risk.
- The PDF is hosted on a third-party domain and bears a visible watermark. It is not an authenticated Amiri Press binary. A second third-party-hosted Gazette reproduction of Ministerial Decision 111/2023 (issue 85 supplement B, 11 April 2023) independently shows the same sampled codes for rows 3–6 and 53; see [historical-list note](decision_111_2023_historical_skill_measurement_list.md). This corroborates the page-image reading but does not authenticate either reproduction. No rights clearance for redistribution, embedding, or commercial reuse is established.
- This audit does not establish whether any listed occupation remains subject to the same licensing/measurement requirements after subsequent decisions, amendments, or administrative updates.

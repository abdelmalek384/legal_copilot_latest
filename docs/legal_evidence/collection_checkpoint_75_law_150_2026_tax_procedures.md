# Collection checkpoint 75 — Law 150/2026: temporary tax card and books requirement

## 1. Official source

- Law 150/2026 amends certain provisions of Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020.
- Official Ministry of Finance record:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-6a731e4263496600085dd859
- Official two-page PDF (Gazette issue 30 bis (A), 28 July 2026):

- PDF text extracted from the official source. Law is in force and commenced the day after publication.

## 2. Article 1 — replacement of Article 38(1) of Law 206/2020

The new Article 38(1) states, subject to Law 6/2025 tax incentives/facilitations for projects with annual turnover not exceeding EGP 20 million, and Companies Law 159/1981:
- Every taxpayer carrying on commercial, industrial, professional or craft activity must keep regular accounting books/registers prescribed by Commercial Code 17/1999.
- Records may be maintained manually or electronically.
- This provision expressly links the small-business incentive regime to the general bookkeeping obligation; do not infer that small-business tax simplification removes all commercial accounting obligations.

## 3. Article 2 — new Article 27 bis: temporary tax card

The new Article 27 bis allows the Egyptian Tax Authority, on taxpayer request, to issue a temporary tax card valid for **eight months** to complete establishment procedures and obtain the necessary activity licence.

- If tax liabilities become due during the temporary card's validity, taxpayer must pay them starting from the temporary card's expiry date.
- ETA Head issues the temporary tax-card form, including required data, validity date and electronic-system processing rules.
- The temporary tax card **cannot be used to issue electronic invoices or electronic receipts**.
- This is a temporary administrative/tax-registration facilitation, not a general exemption from tax registration or licensing.

## 4. Article 3 — commencement

- Law is published in the Official Gazette and commences the day after publication.
- Gazette issue 30 bis (A), 28 July 2026; commencement 29 July 2026.

## 5. Cross-law relationships

- Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020.
- Law 6/2025 on tax incentives/facilitations for projects with annual turnover not exceeding EGP 20 million.
- Commercial Code 17/1999, particularly commercial books and record retention.
- Companies Law 159/1981.
- VAT Law 67/2016, Income Tax Law 91/2005 and e-invoice/e-receipt rules.
- Investment Law 72/2017 and licensing requirements; temporary tax card does not itself grant a sector licence or other administrative approval.

## 6. Implementation tasks

5. Add a checklist requiring Commercial Code bookkeeping even for eligible small-business tax regimes.

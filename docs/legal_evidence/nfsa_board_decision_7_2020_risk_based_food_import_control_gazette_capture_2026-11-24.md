## 2. Article map

- **Preamble:** NFSA's sole food-import control remit; risk-based preventive control; Codex/CAC and WTO principles.
- **Article 1:** definitions, including food imports, production inputs, consignments, prohibited-import list, risk-based sampling/inspection, pre-arrival notification, approved conformity assessment bodies, clearance channels, final/temporary release, withdrawal and recall.
- **Article 2:** scope and exceptions (personal-use food, gifts/promotional samples, specified airline meals, immediate transit/reloading, Armed Forces provisions, laboratory samples and specified fish catches).
- **Article 3:** risk-based principles, consumer protection, support for operators, trade facilitation, non-discrimination, transparency and rapid clearance.
- **Article 4:** controls before arrival/departure from origin, at the customs point, and after final release.
- **Article 5:** prohibited import categories: listed prohibited food, non-compliant food, food harmful in origin, and unfit food.
- **Article 6:** NFSA importer licence prerequisite.
- **Article 7:** NFSA responsibilities for the risk framework, sampling rates, prohibited-food list, approved verification bodies, technical controls and importer information.
- **Article 8:** importer obligations and cost responsibility; right to complain to NFSA grievance committee.
- **Article 9:** risk-based sampling/inspection conditioned on compliant pre-arrival notification; notification window is not more than three months and not less than 72 hours before expected arrival.
- **Article 10:** four clearance channels — green (low risk), yellow (low-medium), orange (medium-high), red (high).
- **Article 11:** consignments without a compliant pre-arrival notice are assigned to the red channel.
- **Article 12:** one notice for qualifying same-type consignments at the same entry point/time; mixed consignments are risk-assessed by reference to the highest-risk item.
- **Article 13:** EGP 2,000 fee for examining a pre-arrival notification (historical text amount; current fee must be checked against later decisions).
- **Article 14:** minimum pre-arrival notification data: importer licence/contact data, tariff classification, goods/processing/packaging, containers, shipping and arrival dates, port, origin, competent authority recognition and conformity evidence.
- **Article 15:** notice updates must be made at least 72 hours before arrival.
- **Article 16:** risk methodology criteria include origin-country control system, product risk, processing level, intended vulnerable group, conformity evidence and importer compliance history.
- **Article 17:** release channels determine random/mandatory sampling and temporary/final release treatment.
- **Article 18:** post-release non-compliance permits hold/quarantine, market prohibition and withdrawal/recall; orange/red temporary-release consignments may be re-exported or destroyed when non-compliant.
- **Article 19:** transparency around risk methodology, sampling rates and changes.
- **Article 20:** periodic update of the risk system to reflect scientific evidence and international standards.
- **Article 21:** commencement after six months from publication; the Board may renew the period for a similar period.

## 3. Important caution: commencement discrepancy

The Arabic Article 21 says the decision takes effect after six months from publication, with the Board able to renew for a similar period. The USDA FAS report states 13 March 2021 as the planned start date, while FAOLEX's note describes six months after publication of the 13 August 2020 Gazette issue (which points to 13 February 2021 under a simple six-calendar-month calculation). This discrepancy must be resolved from the original Gazette and any Board renewal/implementation decision before using a commencement date operationally.

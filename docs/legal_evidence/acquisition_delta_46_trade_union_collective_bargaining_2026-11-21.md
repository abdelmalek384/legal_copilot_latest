## 1. Law 142/2019 — official Ministry-hosted Gazette PDF acquired

**Source:** Ministry of Labour-hosted Gazette PDF  
https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/e1pdvvco/تعديل-قانون-213-لسنه-2017-النقابات-العمالية-بموجب-القانون-142-لسنه-2014.pdf

- The document's own heading identifies **Law 142/2019**, Official Gazette issue 31 bis (b), 5 August 2019, pp. 3–5. The URL filename incorrectly says “142 لسنه 2014”; do not propagate that filename error into metadata.
- Article 1 replaces Law 213/2017 Articles 11, 12, 67, 68 and 76. Article 2 repeals Articles 69, 70, 72 and 75. The final publication clause makes it effective the day after publication (6 August 2019).
- Article 11 sets a minimum of 50 members for a workplace union committee and allows a professional committee at city/governorate level where the establishment cannot meet that threshold.
- Article 12 sets the general-union threshold at 10 union committees with at least 15,000 workers, and the federation threshold at 7 general unions with at least 150,000 workers.
- Articles 67, 68 and 76 replace prior penal provisions with monetary fines; Article 67 provides EGP50,000–200,000 for unauthorized use of union status/activities, Article 68 EGP5,000–20,000 for knowingly false union data, and Article 76 EGP50,000–100,000 for breach of the cross-referenced prohibition in Article54(4).
- Exact short Arabic anchors captured from the official PDF: Art.11 “بما لا يقل عن خمسين عاملاً منضماً لها”; Art.12 “عشر لجان نقابية” and “سبع نقابات عامة”.
- Source status: complete four-page official PDF searchable and text-extracted; full visual glyph-by-glyph review and consolidation into the base law remain pending.

## 2. Decision 36/2018 — official Gazette PDF and model-bylaw annex

**Source:** Ministry of Labour official PDF  
https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/tyhngkrj/2018-36-لائحة-النظام-الاساسي-للمنظمات-النقابية.pdf

- Gazette issue 61 (supplement), 14 March 2018; signed 13 March 2018; effective 15 March 2018.
- Article 1 says trade-union organisations have the right to draft their own basic bylaws and may use the attached models as guidance or apply them until their own bylaws are adopted.
- Operative Arabic excerpt: “للمنظمات النقابية العمالية الحق فى وضع أنظمتها الأساسية”.
- The attached model bylaws must be retained as a distinct annex/document component. They are not a mandatory uniform constitution for every union.
- Source status: official Ministry-hosted Gazette PDF located, searchable text returned; annex image and exact article/page match still require independent visual validation.

## 3. Decision 35/2018 — full Arabic transcription acquired, Gazette matching pending

**Sources:**
- Full Arabic transcription: https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12605
- Alternate PDF: https://manshurat.org/file/34494/download?token=K6bFlK_5
- ILO/NATLEX record identifies it as the implementing regulation to Law 213/2017: https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/r/natlex/fe/details?p3_isn=107361

The full transcription contains two issuing provisions and 48 numbered regulation articles. The article map in the transcription is:
- Arts.1–15: definitions, union rights, independence, organisation structure and general provisions.
- Arts.16–27: election supervision, candidate filing, required documents, challenges and election safeguards.
- Arts.28–40: full-time union officers, notification to employers, union tasks, leave, attendance records and remuneration.
- Arts.41–45: adjustment of existing organisations and proof of regularisation.
- Arts.46–48: judicial-police status, cooperation with inspectors and safeguards for union independence.

Short text anchors include the 15-day period for an election challenge (Art.25) and the employer's obligation to pay the full wage and other entitlements to a full-time union officer (Art.33). The transcription also contains older data-document requirements and transition rules that must not be applied without checking later amendments and actual present-day administrative practice.

**Source status:** complete secondary Arabic transcription acquired. It is not yet an official Gazette page-image match. The official Ministry index and the Gazette publication metadata support the instrument's identity/date, but not every transcribed character or annex.

## 4. Ministerial Decision 50/2022 — nine-section full English text and partial supersession

**Full translation:** ILO/NATLEX-hosted PDF, three pages  
https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/113009/EGY-113009%20%28EN%29.pdf

Issued 18 April 2022. It determines collective-bargaining levels, subjects, mechanisms and procedures. Its preamble expressly relies on Labour Law 12/2003, Decision 124/2003 and Law 213/2017. The nine sections cover: bargaining rights and representation; scope; five bargaining levels; negotiable subjects; bargaining parties/representation by establishment size; employer standstill during bargaining; written collective agreements and conciliation; Ministry promotion of bargaining; and publication/commencement.

**Important version-control issue:**
- Section 5's final paragraph authorizes the administrative authority, after a party refuses to bargain, to notify the union/employers' organisation to negotiate and sign on behalf of the refusing party.
- Labour Law 14/2025 Article 196 uses a different mechanism: the authority invites the relevant employers' organisation, union or worker representative to intervene and persuade the refusing party to reconsider. It does not authorize signing on behalf of that party.
- ILO supervisory material records the Government's statement that the final paragraph of Section 5 was revoked pursuant to Law 14/2025 and would be revisited in a replacement decision. Source: https://wwwex.ilo.org/dyn/f?p=1000:13101:0::NO:13101:P13101_COMMENT_ID:4062785
- No standalone formal repeal instrument or replacement ministerial decision was located in this pass. Do not label all of Decision 50/2022 repealed. Preserve its nine-section text historically; flag Section 5's final paragraph as superseded/non-operative subject to Egyptian counsel confirmation.
- Arabic Gazette text and its issue/page locator remain missing. The English NATLEX text is not a substitute for the Arabic legal source.

## 5. Cross-checks and additional findings

- NATLEX identifies Law 213/2017, Decision 35/2018, Decision 36/2018 and Law 142/2019 as the implementing/amending chain; it also records Decision 50/2022 as related text: https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/r/natlex/fe/details?p3_isn=107361
- The Ministry of Labour's own index page contains a metadata typo describing the 2019 amendment as Law 142/2014. The Ministry's official Gazette PDF and its own historical report identify it as Law 142/2019.
- The Ministry's October 2026 report about possible amendments to Law 213/2017 describes proposals discussed by the Social Dialogue Council, not an enacted amendment. No post-2019 enacted amendment to Law 213/2017 was identified in this pass.
- Law 18/2018 on worker representatives on boards is a related but distinct statute; do not conflate it with Law 213/2017 union formation and collective-bargaining rights.

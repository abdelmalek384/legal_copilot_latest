## New core capital-market/corporate governance acquisition — FRA Decision 195/2026
- Official four-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alamiria_2026_195.pdf
- Gazette issue 191 supplement, 1 September 2026, printed pages 2–5.
- Amends FRA Decision 11/2014 on listing and delisting rules of the Egyptian Exchange.
- Captured exact Arabic Articles 1–3, including the replacement of Article 38 and insertion of Articles 39 bis and 39 bis 1.
- Article 38: blackout for insiders, company officers and principal shareholders owning 20% or more during five business days before and the business day after publication of financial results/statements; requires an insider register, secure advance notice and Exchange publication of covered transactions.
- New Article 39 bis: FRA may exclude interested insiders/officers/20%+ principal shareholders from deliberation and voting on conflict matters, leaving voting to other shareholders; also applies to persons under investigation for crimes involving company funds.
- New Article 39 bis 1: written disclosure of conflicts before deliberation/voting, recorded in meeting minutes with name, interest and recusal steps.
- Effective 2 September 2026.
- Full record: [Decision 195/2026 Arabic text](agent3_fra_decision_195_2026_insider_trading_conflict_interest_listing_rules_arabic_text.md).

## Scope and legal linkage

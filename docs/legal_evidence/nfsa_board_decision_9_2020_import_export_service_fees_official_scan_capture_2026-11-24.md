## 2. Fee schedule transcribed from the three page images

### A. Administrative application/study fees

1. EGP 50 — request to inspect/examine or complete an incoming consignment.
2. EGP 100 — request concerning a returned export consignment imported under return.
3. EGP 300 — grievance request.
4. EGP 100 — request for an original/certified copy of a certificate of conformity.
5. EGP 500 — request for data on quantities imported for a specified food product.

### B. Service fees

1. EGP 1,500 — expedited grievance processing.
2. EGP 500 — inspection/examination outside the ordinary working hours (4 p.m. to 8 a.m.).
3. EGP 2,000 — second inspection of an imported food consignment rejected by the Authority where the grievance committee accepts the interested party's request for sorting/segregation and a second inspection.
4. EGP 500 — supervision of processing within the governorate containing the port; EGP 1,000 when processing is outside that governorate.
5. EGP 500 — supervision of destruction within the customs area or a sanitary landfill within the governorate; EGP 1,000 outside that governorate.
6. Authority vehicle use for inspection staff: EGP 500 up to 100 km; EGP 800 up to 200 km; EGP 1,500 up to 500 km, subject to the conditions in the text.
7. EGP 1,000 to extend the 14-day treatment period for an imported food consignment if the application is submitted within three days after the first period expires; EGP 3,000 if submitted later but before 28 days from notification of the grievance outcome.
8. EGP 3,000 to lift a ban imposed on companies that did not complete treatment within the required period, plus EGP 2,000 for an extension; the decision also restricts issuance of results for other incoming consignments while the extension request is not made.

### C. Sample coding, transport and laboratory fees

1. EGP 50 to code each food sample.
2. Transport of each sample to a laboratory: EGP 50 within the port; EGP 150 outside the port but within the same governorate; EGP 250 for another governorate up to 100 km, plus EGP 1 for each additional kilometre.
3. Laboratory examination fees are collected according to the applicable examination rules; an additional 10% of those fees is collected for the Authority as a laboratory-service charge.

### D. Commencement

- Article 2 states that the decision takes effect from the day following its publication in the Egyptian Chronicles.
- The Customs catalogue shows 24 August 2020 as the decision listing/issue date. The exact Gazette issue and effective date should be verified against the Gazette publication itself before deadline or historical liability analysis.

## 3. Crosswalk and currentness

- Decision 9/2020 concerns fees for NFSA port services; do not conflate it with Decision 6/2020's importer-licence fees or Decision 7/2020's EGP 2,000 pre-arrival-notice examination fee.
- Later NFSA fee instruments include Decision 4/2021 and Decision 11/2021 (amending it), plus later Board decisions. The Trade Portal identifies these in food-import procedures. These must be compared service-by-service before any current fee is shown.
- Decision 9/2020's rates are historical source text only; this record does not assert they remain payable in 2026.

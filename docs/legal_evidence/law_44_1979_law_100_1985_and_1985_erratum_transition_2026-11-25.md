# Personal Status transition, 1979–1985: Decree-Law 44/1979, Law 100/1985 and the 1985 erratum

## 1. Decree-Law 44/1979 — historical and constitutionally invalidated
- **Instrument:** Presidential Decree-Law 44/1979 amending certain personal-status laws.
- **Publication:** Official Gazette issue 25 supplement (A), 21 June 1979.
- **Commencement:** Article 7 states the day after publication: 22 June 1979.
- **Secondary full-text reproduction:** https://e-lawyersdirectory.com/blog/article/441979-b3689
- **LAWEG listing:** https://www.laweg.net/Default.aspx?Year=1979&action=LegsYears
- **Operative package:** added Articles 5 bis, 6 bis, 6 bis 2, 18 bis, 18 bis 2 and 23 bis to Law 25/1929; replaced Articles 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 16 and 20; also amended Article 1 of Law 25/1920 and introduced a matrimonial-home possession procedure.
- **Constitutional invalidation:** SCC case 28/2, judgment 4 May 1985, declared the entire Decree-Law unconstitutional because it was issued contrary to the constitutional procedure governing exceptional presidential legislation. Secondary sources report publication of the judgment in Official Gazette issue 20 on 16 May 1985.
- **Legal effect distinction:** Article 49 of Law 48/1979 generally makes a provision non-applicable from the day after publication of an unconstitutionality judgment (17 May 1985 here). Law 100/1985 Article 7 separately states that its own provisions take effect from the judgment-publication date (16 May 1985). Do not collapse these two dates or infer retroactive effect beyond the express statutory text.
- **Status:** historical only; do not include as current operative law. Original Gazette and official SCC judgment copies remain outstanding.

## 2. Law 100/1985 — replacement/re-enactment and commencement
- **Instrument:** Law 100/1985 amending certain personal-status laws.
- **Issuance:** 3 July 1985.
- **Publication:** Official Gazette issue 27 supplement, 4 July 1985.
- **Sources:** LAWEG full text: https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?NID=35976&action=viewactivepages&itemid=30080&type=6 ; Eastlaws final text: https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/04-07-1985/no-100?id=4619&sub=1714095&type=1
- **Operative package:** Article 1 adds Articles 5 bis, 11 bis, 11 bis 2, 18 bis, 18 bis 2, 18 bis 3 and 23 bis to Law 25/1929; Article 3 replaces Articles 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 16 and 20; Article 2 replaces Article 1 of Law 25/1920. Articles 4–7 address transitional transfer, repeal, implementation and commencement.
- **Commencement:** Article 7 provides that the law takes effect from publication of the SCC judgment invalidating Decree-Law 44/1979 (16 May 1985), except Article 23 bis, which takes effect the day after Law 100/1985's publication (5 July 1985). This is an express retroactive commencement and must be read with the judgment and transition clauses.
- **Text status:** LAWEG provides a full text reproduction; Eastlaws distinguishes original article text from final text after the later erratum. Neither is an original Gazette scan.

## 3. 1985 Gazette erratum
- **Instrument description:** Official Gazette corrigendum (استدراك تصحيح خطأ) to Law 100/1985 and other laws.
- **Publication:** Official Gazette issue 33, 15 August 1985.
- **Evidence:** Eastlaws links corrected final texts for Law 100/1985 Articles 1–3 to an erratum and retains the original versions. LAWEG marks multiple passages with a footnote stating they were corrected by the issue 33 erratum.
- **Gap:** the erratum itself has not been retrieved; its exact corrected words and complete scope are unknown. Do not infer that every correction is substantive. The legal classification could be typographical correction or a correction with substantive effect; it requires the Gazette page and lawyer review.

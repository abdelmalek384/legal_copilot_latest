## 1. Law 147/2021 — scope and repeal
- Article 1: attached law governs water resources and irrigation.
- Article 2: repeals Irrigation and Drainage Law 12/1984 and inconsistent provisions.
- Article 3: PM issues Executive Regulation within six months after presentation by Water Resources/Irrigation and Agriculture/Land Reclamation Ministers; existing regulations continue insofar as consistent until new regulation.
- Article 4: publication and commencement the day after Gazette publication.
- The law has 130 substantive articles in the updated transcription.

## 2. Substantive structure and topics
- Water resources and public water property.
- Protection of the Nile, canals, drains, waterways and shorelines.
- Irrigation and drainage works, structures, intakes, pumping stations and private works.
- Licences/approvals for works affecting public waterways, water use, abstraction and discharge.
- Groundwater, wells, springs and aquifers.
- Reuse of drainage water, treatment, water quality and pollution prevention.
- Irrigation associations, water-user organizations and cost-sharing.
- Navigation and use of waterways; protection of public water property.
- Encroachment, illegal abstraction, obstruction, damage to water structures and administrative enforcement.
- Offences, penalties, restoration and cost recovery.
- Exact article-level extraction and maps of protected areas, setback distances, licence requirements, fees and penalties remain to be completed.

## 3. Executive Regulation 81/2023
- PM Decision 81/2023 promulgates the attached Executive Regulation.
- Article 1 definitions include Ministry, Minister, Law, public/private water properties, waterways, irrigation/drainage systems, works, water users and technical terms.
- Regulation contains detailed procedures and technical controls for water-use licensing, waterway occupation, shoreline works, irrigation/drainage structures, groundwater, well drilling, drainage reuse, maintenance, inspection and administrative enforcement.
- Full text is available in the LawHub transcription and official Gazette PDF; compare article-by-article and extract technical tables/annexes.

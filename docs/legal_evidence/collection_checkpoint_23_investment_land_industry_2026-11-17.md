## 3. Law 141/2019 — amendment to Investment Law 72/2017

- Official MIFT direct Arabic text:
  https://www.mift.gov.eg/legislation/download/169
- The text identifies Law 141/2019 amending Investment Law 72/2017.
- It adds final paragraphs to Articles 12 and 48, a new item 14 to Article 74 and a new Article 91 bis.
- One exposed amendment allows expansions of existing investment projects to benefit from incentives under Articles 11 and 13 when additional assets increase production capacity, subject to PM rules.
- This is an official Ministry text locator; pin Gazette issue/date and compare all amendment provisions.

## 5. Investment land allocation — official MIFT text

- MIFT-hosted text of Chapter 5 of Investment Law:
  https://www.mift.gov.eg/legislation/download/229
- Article 71 governs disposition of private state/public-person land and real estate for investment, subject to the chapter's rules and project characteristics.
- Article 72 permits forms including sale, lease, lease-to-own, usufruct licence and land contribution as an in-kind share in specified circumstances.
- Link these provisions with State Private Property Law 168/2025 and Executive Regulation 200/2026, but do not assume the general investment-law land chapter overrides later special state-property rules.

## 6. Industrial establishment regularization and 2025 location controls

- Law 19/2023 on regularizing unlicensed industrial establishments is cited in later official Ministry of Industry decisions; obtain its full Gazette text and regularization window.
- Ministry of Industry Decision 16/2025:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=py03Z3WGjwc%3D
  - Restricts establishment, management, operation or expansion of industrial establishments outside industrial zones, subject to listed exceptions.
  - Also prohibits specified activities inside industrial zones, as listed in its annexes.
  - Existing licences outside industrial zones continue subject to ongoing compliance with industrial activity requirements.
  - IDA is tasked with issuing implementation rules and reviewing annex activities periodically.
- Industry Decision 378/2024 amends temporary operating permit Decision 149/2023 for unlicensed industrial facilities:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=aP7LuCLn2Bo%3D
- These are binding ministerial decisions affecting industrial location and licensing; capture annexes and effective dates, not just the main article.

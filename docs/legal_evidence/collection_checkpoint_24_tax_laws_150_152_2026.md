# Collection checkpoint 24 — Laws 150/2026 and 152/2026: unified tax procedures and tax dispute settlement

**Date:** 2026-11-17. Both official Ministry of Finance Gazette PDFs were text-extracted and inspected.

## 1. Law 150/2026 — amendment to Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020

- Ministry record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-6a731e4263496600085dd859
- Official Gazette PDF: https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/qanwn_rqm_150_lsnt_2026_tedyl_bed_ahkam_alijraeat_aldrybyt_almwhdt_alsadr_balqanwn_206_ls_2020_21f4d9efef.pdf
- Gazette issue 30 bis (A), 28 July 2026.
- Full searchable 2-page PDF text inspected (39 extracted lines).
- Article 1 replaces the first paragraph of Article 38 of Law 206/2020:
  - Without prejudice to SME tax incentives under Law 6/2025 for projects whose annual turnover does not exceed EGP 20 million, and subject to Companies Law 159/1981, every taxpayer carrying on commercial, industrial, professional or craft activity must maintain regular accounting books and records required under Commercial Code 17/1999, either manually or electronically.
- Article 2 adds Article 27 bis:
  - As an exception to Article 27, the Tax Authority may, upon taxpayer request, issue a temporary tax card for eight months to complete establishment and licensing procedures for the activity.
  - If tax dues become payable during the temporary card's validity, taxpayer must pay them starting from the card's expiry date.
  - Tax Authority Head issues the form and rules for data, validity and procedures, provided they do not conflict with the Authority's electronic systems.
  - Temporary tax card cannot be used to issue electronic invoices or receipts.
- Article 3: effective the day after Gazette publication.
- Data model: link Article 38 amendment and new Article 27 bis to Law 206/2020, Commercial Code 17/1999, Companies Law 159/1981 and SME Law 6/2025; record the 8-month temporary card period and e-invoice exclusion separately.

## 2. Law 152/2026 — renewal of tax dispute settlement regime

- Ministry record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-6a731e9163496600085dd85f
- Official Gazette PDF (1 page): https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/qanwn_rqm_152_lsnt_2026_tjdyd_aleml_bahkam_wijraeat_inhae_almnazeat_aldrybyt_wmd_sryanha_hta_31_dysmbr_2026_f92b0e754d.pdf
- Gazette issue 30 bis (A), 28 July 2026.
- Full searchable text inspected (23 extracted lines).
- Article 1 renews operation of Law 79/2016 provisions and procedures for ending tax disputes, as amended by Laws 14/2018 and 174/2018 and extended by Laws 16/2020, 173/2020, 153/2022 and 160/2024, through 31 December 2026.
- Existing committees formed under Law 79/2016 continue considering unresolved applications and also decide new applications submitted through 31 December 2026.
- Article 2: effective the day after Gazette publication.
- This is a temporary sunset/extension instrument, not a permanent general tax procedure rule. The corpus must model the original Law 79/2016, all extensions/amendments, the committee procedure and the 2026 sunset date.

## 3. Cross-checks and caveats

- Law 150/2026 is an amendment to Law 206/2020, not a replacement.
- Law 152/2026 extends Law 79/2016, not Law 206/2020.
- The 20m turnover threshold in Law 150/2026 is linked to Law 6/2025 SME incentives; preserve that cross-reference and do not treat it as a universal turnover threshold for every tax obligation.
- No lawyer review, rights clearance or full consolidation of the base tax laws is claimed.

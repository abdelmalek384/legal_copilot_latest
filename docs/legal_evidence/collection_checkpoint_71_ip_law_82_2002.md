## 1. Four-book structure
- Book I: patents, utility models, integrated-circuit layout designs and undisclosed information/trade secrets.
- Book II: trademarks, trade names, geographical indications and industrial designs.
- Book III: copyright and related rights.
- Book IV: plant varieties.
- Law 82/2002 repeals:
  - Law 57/1939 on trademarks/trade descriptions.
  - Law 132/1949 on patents/inventions/industrial designs (with transition for food/pharmaceutical chemical patents).
  - Law 354/1954 on copyright.
- The law also provides for public-health, nutrition, social/economic/technological development and anti-abuse safeguards.

## 2. Selected operative rules
- Patents require industrial applicability, novelty and inventive step; patentable subject matter and exclusions are governed by Book I.
- Patent term, application/examination, ownership, employee inventions, compulsory licences, government-use and infringement remedies require exact article-level extraction.
- Trademarks/industrial designs/geographical indications: registration, opposition, renewal, cancellation, use requirements, ownership and infringement.
- Copyright: original literary/artistic/scientific works, authors' moral/economic rights, related rights, limitations/exceptions, duration, collective management, enforcement and penalties.
- Plant varieties: breeder rights, registration, novelty/distinctness/uniformity/stability, denomination, term, exceptions and compulsory licensing.
- Law contains criminal penalties and civil remedies; do not conflate copyright, trademark and patent infringement standards.

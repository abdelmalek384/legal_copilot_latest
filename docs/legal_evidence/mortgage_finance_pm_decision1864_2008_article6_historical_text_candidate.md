# PM Decision 1864/2008 — Article 6 Historical Text Candidate

**Instrument:** PM Decision1864/2008 amending the Executive Regulation of Law148/2001.  
**Publication:** Official Gazette issue28 تابع (أ), 10 July 2008.  
**Text status:** The original decision is image-only in the available Egyptianlaws catalogue. The official FRA consolidated regulation identifies Article6 as amended by Decisions465/2005, 1864/2008 and 1/2015. The exact 1864/2008 replacement clause has not been isolated from the Gazette original. This file preserves the pre-2015 and current paragraph versions as a **candidate comparison**, not a certified transcription of Decision1864/2008.

## Earlier Article 6 priority paragraph in the 2016 secondary snapshot

وتكون الأولوية فى توفير التمويل للحصول على مسكن اقتصادى للمستثمر الأقل دخلا الذى لم يسبق له شراء وحدة سكنية.

Source: egylawsite historical regulation transcription (published 29 February 2016), which labels Article6 as amended by Decision465/2005 but does not identify Decision1864/2008 at paragraph level:

## Current Article 6 priority paragraph in the official FRA consolidated regulation

وتكون الأولوية في توفير الدعم للحصول على مسكن اقتصادي للمستثمر الأقل دخلاً الذي لم يسبق له الحصول على دعم نقدي ومن لا يملك وحدة سكنية.

The current consolidated Article6 footnote lists amendments by Decision1/2015, Decision1864/2008 and Decision465/2005:
https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/اللائحة-التنفيذية-للقانون-التمويل-العقاري.pdf

## Version-allocation analysis (provisional)

- Decision465/2005 replaced Article6 in the older text layer, with the EGP12,000 / EGP18,000 annual-income thresholds and a priority rule based on not previously purchasing a unit.
- Decision1/2015 replaced the first paragraph of Article6 with the current low-income criteria (no owned unit, no previous housing support, and an annual income threshold set by the Fund Board). Its text is captured separately in mortgage_finance_pm_decision1_2015_article6_replacement_text.md.
- The current second paragraph differs materially from the older 2005 text: it refers to no prior cash support and no owned unit. Since Decision1/2015's captured scope is the first paragraph, the second-paragraph change may be attributable to Decision1864/2008. However, without the original Decision1864/2008 text this allocation is **not verified**.

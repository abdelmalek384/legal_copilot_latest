# Collection checkpoint 79 — PM Decision 266/2026 reorganizes GOEIC and its import/export control powers

## 1. Official full text

- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Em3PNa2ZWXk%3D
- PM Decision 266/2026 amends Presidential Decision 1770/1971 establishing the General Organization for Export and Import Control (GOEIC).
- Issued 25 January 2026; effective the day after publication.
- The decision replaces Articles 2 and 4 of Presidential Decision 1770/1971 and repeals inconsistent provisions.

## 2. Article 1 — replacement of GOEIC Article 2

The revised Article 2 provides that GOEIC exercises, without prejudice to other governing laws/decisions, the following functions:

1. Competences under:
   - Import/Export Law 118/1975.
   - Commercial Agency and Mediation Law 120/1982.
   - Importers Register Law 121/1982.
   - Export Development Law 155/2002.
2. Competences under the Unified Standardization Law 2/1957 and its implementing decisions.
3. Technical inspection and control of imported/exported goods under the relevant laws and decisions.
4. Any additional functions assigned by law or Prime Minister/competent Minister decisions.

- This confirms GOEIC's central role across import/export control, importers registration, commercial agency and export development.
- It does not make GOEIC the only regulator for food, pharmaceuticals, customs, environment, animal/plant health or other specially regulated goods; the competent special authorities retain their statutory roles.

## 3. Article 2 — replacement of GOEIC Article 4 (Board composition)

The Board of Directors is chaired by the Minister of Investment and Foreign Trade (or delegate) and includes:
- Minister of Finance (or delegate).
- Minister of Industry (or delegate).
- Minister of Supply and Internal Trade (or delegate).
- Head of GOEIC.
- Head of the competent foreign-trade sector at the Ministry of Investment and Foreign Trade.
- A representative nominated by the General Federation of Chambers of Commerce.
- A representative nominated by the Federation of Egyptian Industries.
- One expert appointed by the competent foreign-trade Minister.
- Head of GOEIC Operations Sector.
- Head of GOEIC Branches Sector.
- Board Chair may invite experts to attend without voting rights.
- Board term: four years, renewable; PM issues the formation and financial treatment decision. Existing board continues until reconstituted.

## 4. Cross-law framework

- Import/Export Law 118/1975.
- Executive Regulation 770/2005.
- Commercial Agency and Mediation Law 120/1982.
- Importers Register Law 121/1982 and Executive Regulation 846/2017.
- Export Development Law 155/2002.
- Standardization Law 2/1957.
- Customs Law 207/2020 and Executive Regulation 430/2021.
- NFSA Law 1/2017 for food safety.
- EDA Law 151/2019 for medicines/medical devices.
- Consumer Protection Law 181/2018.
- Investment Law 72/2017 and Special Economic Zones Law 83/2002.

## 5. Operational implications for Legal Copilot

- Import/export answer retrieval should identify the correct authority by goods category and legal function:
  - GOEIC: general import/export controls, inspection, importers register and commercial agency.
  - Customs Authority: customs declaration, tariff, customs valuation, clearance, storage, transit and customs enforcement.
  - NFSA: food safety.
  - EDA: medicines and medical devices.
  - Plant/veterinary quarantine authorities: animal/plant health and quarantine.
  - CBE: foreign-currency settlement/payment rules where applicable.
- Do not answer "GOEIC approval required" without identifying the exact law, decision, product classification, tariff code, procedure and competent branch.
- Board composition is governance information; it does not itself create new importer licensing conditions.

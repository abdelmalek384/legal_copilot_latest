## Detailed official FRA summary — disclosure requirements
The official FRA account states that the prospectus/information memorandum must contain the minimum data required by the Capital Market Executive Regulation plus:
1. The fund's investment policy.
2. Target-investor eligibility conditions and verification of each investor's eligibility by subscription recipients or other marketing entities.
3. Risk limits acceptable to the investment manager and how they are managed, including at least:
   - Leverage limits.
   - Counterparty exposure limits, especially securities lending/borrowing and derivatives.
   - Liquidity limits.
   - Concentration limits.
   - Stress testing.
   - Stop-loss mechanisms.
   - Derivatives risk management.
   - Securities lending/borrowing risk management.
   - Other controls the investment manager considers necessary.
4. Performance-evaluation methodology, including risk-adjusted return measures and benchmark indicators.
5. Investment-manager obligations.
6. Any other information FRA requires.

## Detailed official FRA summary — investment-manager obligations
The official FRA account identifies the following duties:
- Have specialist experience in the fund's investment fields and the financial systems required to manage its strategies.
- Periodically assess counterparties' creditworthiness.
- Manage and monitor leverage and ensure that it does not exceed the limits stated in the prospectus/information memorandum.
- Perform periodic stress tests and scenario analysis to assess resilience under exceptional conditions.
- Verify the sufficiency of collateral connected with transactions.

## Additional implementation detail from secondary Q&A
Secondary coverage reports that:
- Existing investment funds may apply to change their nature and operate as hedge funds, subject to board approval and updating the prospectus/information memorandum.
- Eligible investments include listed shares/debt, derivatives, borrowed securities for short-selling, listed/open-ended fund units and other instruments approved by FRA.
- The fund's investment policy must set target investment fields, allocation structure, maximum/minimum asset limits, strategies and associated risks, use of derivatives/short-selling/margin, leverage limits and calculation/monitoring, borrowing limits, liquidity policy and subscription/redemption mechanics.
This is explanatory secondary reporting, not primary statutory text.

## Legal relationship
- Parent statute: Capital Market Law 95/1992.
- Executive Regulation: Decision 135/1993, especially Article 179 bis 2 (hedge-fund definition and eligible investment structure), inserted by PM Decision 2423/2024.
- Decision 154/2026 is a later FRA Board decision specifying operating, disclosure, investment-policy and manager-control requirements for hedge-fund activity. It should be linked as a subordinate rule under Article 179 bis 2.
- It is separate from the securities-lending framework in FRA Decision 155/2026; hedge-fund rules reference exposure to securities lending/borrowing and derivatives.

# Collection checkpoint 3 — public procurement, administrative and regulatory law (2026-11-17)

## Public procurement

### Law 182/2018 — Contracts concluded by public entities
- Ministry of Finance record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-5fe868cd7bfc600007cbed73
- Official-hosted Gazette PDF/text: https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/1948_8a91b8ae46.pdf
- Gazette issue 39 bis (d), 3 October 2018.
- Text is searchable from the official PDF source.
- Promulgation provision applies to public entities including state-budget entities, ministries and administrative units, local administration units, public service/economic authorities, certain special-purpose units and funds, subject to stated exceptions and special laws.
- Related instruments exposed by the Ministry:
  - Law 188/2020 amending Law 182/2018.
  - Minister of Finance Decision 692/2019 issuing the Executive Regulation.
  - Minister of Finance Decision 28/2020 amending the Executive Regulation.
- This is a linked law + amendment + executive regulation chain, not a standalone text.

## 1. Law 168/2025 application window — verified first period

The issuing provision of Law 168/2025, Article 4, states:

> يُنشر هذا القانون فى الجريدة الرسمية، ويعمل به بعد مرور ثلاثين يومًا من تاريخ نشره.

The law was published in Official Gazette issue 32 bis (و), 13 August 2025. PM Decision 200/2026 was published in Official Gazette issue 3 bis (أ), 18 January 2026; its issuing Article 4 states:

> يُنشر هذا القرار فى الجريدة الرسمية، ويعمل به من اليوم التالى لتاريخ نشره.

Accordingly, the Executive Regulation took effect on 19 January 2026. Law 168/2025, Article 3(1), provides:

> يكون التصرف المنصوص عليه فى المادة (2) من هذا القانون بالبيع أو بالإيجار أو بالإيجار المنتهى بالتملك أو بالترخيص بالانتفاع بناءً على طلب يقدم من واضع اليد إلى الجهة الإدارية المختصة، خلال ستة أشهر تبدأ من تاريخ العمل بأحكام اللائحة التنفيذية لهذا القانون.

The original six-month application period therefore ran from 19 January 2026, with the last day for filing confirmed as **18 July 2026**. This end date is expressly stated by the Cairo Governorate portal (notice dated 21 June 2026) and independently by the Ministry of Local Development official's statement reported on 1 November 2026, which says the law began operating on 18 January and the six-month period ended on 18 July 2026.

## 2. Presidential Decision 480/2026 — extension and endpoint qualification

Decision 480/2026 was issued on 10 November 2026 and published in Official Gazette issue 45 bis (ج). Article 1 reads:

> تمد المدة المشار إليها بالفقرة الأولى من المادة (3) من قانون بعض قواعد وإجراءات التصرف فى أملاك الدولة الخاصة الصادر بالقانون رقم 168 لسنة 2025، لمدة أخرى مماثلة.

Article 2 reads:

> ينشر هذا القرار في الجريدة الرسمية.

The decision therefore extends the six-month period by another six-month period. The first period's final day is established as 18 July 2026. **18 January 2027 is the resulting calculated endpoint if the extension is treated as the immediately successive six-month period.** The operative text of Decision 480 does not itself print that calendar date or expressly state a new commencement date. The repository must therefore label 18 January 2027 as a calculated endpoint pending an authoritative implementing circular or official agency confirmation; it must not quote it as an express date in the decision.

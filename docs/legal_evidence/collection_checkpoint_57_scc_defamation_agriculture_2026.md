# Collection checkpoint 57 — additional 2026 SCC precedents: defamation and agricultural possession jurisdiction

## 1. SCC case 244/23 Constitutional — defamation, insult and public-official criticism

- Official full judgment HTML:
  https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/18148_23_244_1_2.html
- Hearing/judgment date: 9 May 2026.
- Challenged provisions: Penal Code Law 58/1937 Articles 302, 303, 306 and 307, in conjunction with Article 185.
- The Court:
  - rejected the constitutional challenge to Article 185 (insult of public employee/representative/public-service person due to their duties) and Article 307 insofar as it doubles fines for publication;
  - held that earlier SCC judgments had already resolved challenges to specified parts of Articles 302, 303, 306 and 307, so that portion of the new case was inadmissible/res judicata;
  - explained that public-interest criticism of public officials is permissible under Article 302(2) only where it is in good faith, within the scope of the public function/service, and the critic proves the truth of each allegation;
  - reasoned that the publication-related fine under Article 307 for the relevant insult offence is doubled, with the cited Article 185 fine range becoming EGP 20,000–40,000 for publication.
- The judgment distinguishes constitutionally protected opinion/press freedom from insult/defamation, protects human dignity and privacy, and requires clear criminal provisions and individualized proportionate punishment.
- Link to Penal Code Article 185, Articles 302–307, Laws 93/1995 and 147/2006, Constitution Articles 51, 59, 65, 70–71, 92, 95–98 and prior SCC cases 60/22 (6 July 2024) and 16/24 (1 September 2025).
- Do not treat the judgment as amending the Penal Code; it is a binding constitutional precedent confirming/rejecting challenges within the scope stated.

## 2. SCC case 19/44 Conflict — agricultural possession-card disputes belong to administrative jurisdiction

- Official full judgment HTML:
  https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/17939_44_19_3_2.html
- Hearing/judgment date: 7 March 2026.
- Conflict between:
  - Cairo Administrative Court judgment 54381/63 (18 April 2018), ordering the Agriculture Directorate to correct agricultural holding records and issue an agricultural possession card; and
  - South Giza Primary Court judgment 136/2020 Civil Full Government (28 November 2020), ordering the same agricultural holding record/card in another person's name.
- The SCC found the two final judgments addressed the same subject and could not both be implemented.
- It held that decisions of agricultural holding-data appeal committees under Agriculture Law 53/1966 Articles 90–93 and Minister of Agriculture Decision 59/1985 are final administrative decisions.
- Disputes about registering/correcting agricultural holding data and issuing agricultural possession cards are administrative disputes within the exclusive jurisdiction of the State Council under Constitution Article 190.
- This is distinct from civil possession claims (recovery of possession, preventing interference, stopping new works), which remain within ordinary civil jurisdiction.
- Holding: the Administrative Court judgment is to be recognized and executed; the ordinary civil court judgment is not to be recognized.
- Link to Agriculture Law 53/1966, Ministerial Decision 59/1985, State Council Law 47/1972, Civil Code possession rules, Civil Procedure Law 13/1968 and Evidence Law 25/1968.
- This is a high-value jurisdictional precedent for agricultural land, possession cards and administrative records.

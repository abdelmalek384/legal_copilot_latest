## 1. Judicial Authority Law 46/1972

### Sources and version
- Gazette: issue 40, 5 October 1972.
- Full consolidated text, updated through 13 February 2023, hosted by Court of Cassation Information Centre:
  https://alhasswalawfirm.com/uploads/files/law-46-1972-1789185066466-210995563.pdf
- Full text pages:
  https://manshurat.org/node/63718
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=16481
  https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?ItemID=36189&NID=45774&Type=6&action=ViewActivePages
- Secondary transcription reports 2023 latest update; official Gazette matching required.

### Core structure
- Articles 1 onward establish ordinary courts: Court of Cassation, Courts of Appeal, Courts of First Instance and Partial Courts.
- Regulates Court of Cassation composition/circuits, technical office and legal principles, appellate and trial courts, judicial associations, judges' appointment/promotion/seniority, transfer, secondment, immunity/irremovability, judicial inspection, disciplinary proceedings, retirement, leave and judicial administration.
- Article 3 (as amended by Law 142/2006) Court of Cassation judgments issued by five judges.
- Article 4 regulates special panels of eleven judges for departure from prior legal principles, with required majority.
- Article 5 establishes Technical Office of Court of Cassation for extracting and classifying legal principles, publishing judgment collections and technical research.
- Article 38 states qualification requirements for judicial appointment, including nationality, civil capacity, age, law degree, reputation and no dishonourable conviction.
- Article 47 reserves minimum recruitment percentages for practising lawyers in certain judicial appointments.
- Article 63 restricts judges acting as arbitrators without Judicial Council consent, subject to family exception.
- Articles 67 onward protect judges from dismissal subject to statutory conditions.
- Articles 83–85 allocate judicial-service disputes and appeals to designated circuits.
- Full current text has 100+ articles; article-by-article extraction and current amendment graph remain outstanding.

## 2. Administrative Prosecution Law 117/1958

- Law 117/1958 reorganizes Administrative Prosecution and disciplinary trials.
- Official/secondary record:
  https://manshurat.org/node/7342
- Full text lead: Manshurat PDF attachment (178 KB) and related LawHub transcription.
- Gazette publication 24 August 1958; status amended.
- Administrative Prosecution investigates administrative/financial offences and may initiate disciplinary proceedings before competent disciplinary courts; its jurisdiction overlaps with, but is distinct from, criminal prosecution and internal administrative investigations.
- The law has been amended and is linked to State Council Law 47/1972, Judicial Authority Law 46/1972 and civil-service legislation.
- Need exact current article count, amendments, executive decisions and constitutional rulings.

## 3. State Lawsuits Authority Law 75/1963

- Law 75/1963 regulates State Lawsuits Authority (هيئة قضايا الدولة).
- Official/secondary record:
  https://manshurat.org/node/7277
- Gazette publication/commencement 12 August 1963; status amended.
- Authority represents the State and public legal persons in court, undertakes legal opinions/settlement and drafts public contracts within its statutory remit.
- 2014 Constitution Article 196 confirms the Authority's independent judicial status and core representation/settlement/contract functions.
- Official SIS overview:
  https://sis.gov.eg/ar/مصر/نظام-الحكم/السلطة-القضائية/هيئة-قضايا-الدولة/

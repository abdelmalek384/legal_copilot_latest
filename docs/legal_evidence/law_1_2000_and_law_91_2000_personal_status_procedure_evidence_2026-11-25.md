# Law 1/2000 and Law 91/2000 — Personal Status Litigation Procedures

## Law 1/2000 — base law
- **Instrument:** Law 1/2000 issuing the Law on Regulating Certain Litigation Procedures in Personal Status Matters.
- **Issuance/publication date:** 29 January 2000.
- **Gazette reference:** Official Gazette issue 4 bis, 29 January 2000, identified by LAWEG.
- **Sources:** MKSEgypt full Arabic text (CC BY 4.0): https://mksegypt.org/ar/laws/24131 ; LAWEG Gazette reproduction: https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?ItemID=34731&NID=43122&Type=6&action=ViewActivePages ; Alhasswa consolidated text: https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-1-2000
- **Text distinction:** MKSEgypt's base-law page displays issuing Articles 1–6 and operative Articles 1–79. A separate Alhasswa page describes an 86-article consolidated text through 5 September 2020. These are not equivalent snapshots; do not assume the 86-article consolidation is complete or correct without reconciling every amending instrument.
- **Commencement:** issuing Article 6 states the law takes effect after one month from the day following publication. The exact date calculation is not normalized here.
- **Status metadata:** MKSEgypt labels the law “ساري”; this is portal metadata, not a complete currentness review.

## Law 91/2000 — amendment
- **Instrument:** Law 91/2000 amending Law 1/2000.
- **Issuance date:** 18 May 2000.
- **Commencement:** Article 2 states the day after publication; with the source page's date of 18 May 2000, the provisional effective date is 19 May 2000.
- **Gazette issue:** not independently confirmed in this pass.
- **Source:** MKSEgypt full text, https://mksegypt.org/ar/laws/24155, under CC BY 4.0; LAWEG topic index, https://www.laweg.net/Default.aspx?FIID=240&action=LegsCates.
- **Operative change:** Article 1 inserts item 9 into Article 9(1) for imprisonment proceedings arising from non-compliance with maintenance judgments and adds Article 76 bis, which regulates coercive imprisonment (up to 30 days) for a debtor shown able to pay who fails to comply after court order, conditions for release, and the relationship with Penal Code Article 293.
- **Local text:** the complete two-article source transcription is preserved in [Law 91/2000 Arabic text capture](../text_captures/law_91_2000_personal_status_amendment_ar.md), with CC BY 4.0 attribution.

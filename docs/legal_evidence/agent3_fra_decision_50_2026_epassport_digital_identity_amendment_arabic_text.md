## Corroborated technical requirements (not a substitute for exact Gazette wording)
- Read the e-passport's electronic chip using NFC on the same mobile device held by the customer; OTP is part of the device-possession/verification flow.
- Match the Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) data against the data stored on the chip.
- Verify passport authenticity under ICAO mechanisms, including Passive Authentication and Active/Chip Authentication.
- Compare a live-captured face image with the image stored on the chip and apply liveness testing to reduce impersonation using static images or recorded video.
- The amendment exempts foreigners from the relevant clause 5(b) national-ID/mobile-number linkage requirement; other OTP/mobile verification requirements should not be assumed waived without reading the complete amended rule.

## Amendment mapping and legal effect
1. Decision 50/2026 inserts a new subparagraph (هـ) under item 3 of Annex 1 to Decision 140/2023.
2. It also adds a final paragraph to item 5 of Annex 1. The precise text of that paragraph is outstanding; secondary legal commentary describes it as an exception for foreigners to clause 5(b).
3. The rule concerns remote identity proofing of foreign persons using electronic passports for digital identity in non-bank financial activities. It does not create a general new electronic-signature regime and does not repeal Decision 140/2023.
4. Link this amendment to Electronic Signature Law 15/2004, its Executive Regulation and the fintech framework Law 5/2022, while keeping the FRA-specific identity-control layer distinct.

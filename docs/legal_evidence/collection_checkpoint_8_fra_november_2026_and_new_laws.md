## A. FRA Circular 8/2026 — updated customer due diligence procedures

- Official PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A-8.pdf
- Date: 8 November 2026.
- Subject: updating customer due diligence procedures for financial institutions supervised by FRA.
- Full searchable PDF text inspected.
- Verbatim operative text (Arabic):
  
  «في ضوء اعتماد مجلس أمناء وحدة مكافحة غسل الأموال وتمويل الإرهاب النسخة المحدثة من إجراءات العناية الواجبة بعملاء المؤسسات المالية الخاضعة لرقابة الهيئة لإصدار سبتمبر ٢٠٢٦ والتي تحل محل النسخة السابقة الصادرة في فبراير ٢٠٢٠.»
  
  «فإننا نؤكد على وجوب الالتزام بهذا التحديث وإرسال النسخة المحدثة المعتمدة من مجلس الإدارة مع التقرير الدوري السنوي عن نشاط المؤسسة في مجال المكافحة عن عام ٢٠٢٦ بحد أقصى ٣١/١/٢٠٢٧، وفي حال تعذر اعتماد النسخة المحدثة، ينبغي إرسال توضيح للأسباب التي أدت إلى هذا التعذر مع النسخة المحدثة من الدليل.»
  
  «وكذا نؤكد على أهمية الالتزام بتحديث النماذج المستخدمة من قبل العملاء لطلب الحصول على الخدمات والمنتجات ... بما يشمل البيانات والمعلومات والمستندات التي تتحصل عليها لأغراض التعرف على العميل والمستفيد الحقيقي والتحقق من هويتهما ...»
- Circular 8/2026 explicitly follows Circular 5/2026 and references updated September 2026 AML/CFT CDD procedures, replacing February 2020 version.
- Deadline specified: submit updated board-approved policies/procedures and internal-control manuals with 2026 annual AML/CFT activity report by 31 January 2027; where approval is not possible, explain why and submit the updated manual.
- It requires updating customer onboarding forms, including identification and verification information for customer and beneficial owner, and accessibility for customers with disabilities, illiterate customers and older people.
- Full text source lines/pages: PDF has 34 extracted lines; substantive text lines 4–19 in browser extraction. Preserve original PDF and referenced September 2026 CDD procedure separately; this circular is not the underlying procedure itself.

## B. FRA Board Decision 133/2026 — customer-data verification

- Official Gazette PDF: https://www.fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/alamiria_2026_133.pdf
- Gazette: Al-Waqa'i al-Misriyya issue 163 (supplement), 29 July 2026.
- Decision 133/2026 amends FRA Board Decision 186/2024 on verifying customer data.
- Full searchable PDF text inspected (50 extracted lines).
- Operative details:
  - Adds a second paragraph to Article 1 of Decision 186/2024.
  - Applies the first paragraph's customer-data verification rules to licensed consumer-finance companies, microfinance companies and licensed microfinance associations/institutions (categories A and B).
  - Requires sending an OTP to all customers on the queried number at contract execution or renewal and on use of financing, and retaining evidence of the OTP.
  - Applies to SMEs-finance licensees for their natural-person customers.
  - Gives affected companies/entities six months from entry into force to regularize their status.
  - Decision says it takes effect the day after Gazette publication.
- Textual source must be linked to Decision 186/2024 as an amendment, not stored as a replacement standalone rule. Compute calendar commencement only after confirming Gazette publication date and any general publication rules.

## Two new official capital-market acquisitions

### FRA Decision 155/2026 — securities borrowing for short selling
- Official 16-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alamiria_2026_155.pdf
- Gazette issue 191 supplement (أ), 1 September 2026, printed pages 5–20; adopted 5 August 2026.
- Captured all 19 operative articles from the official PDF.
- Regulates broker approval, capital/liquidity, internal controls, technology, client agreements, 50% minimum initial cash margin, 40% aggregate free-float lending cap, 2% per borrower/related persons, 140% margin call / 150% cure / 130% close-out thresholds, central depository duties, lender proceeds, corporate actions, direct contracts, data reporting and FRA intervention powers.

### FRA Chairperson Decision 3350/2026 — standard short-selling contract
- Official ten-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/alamiria_2026_3350_-R.pdf
- Gazette issue 239 supplement, 28 October 2026, printed pages 2–11; decision dated 19 October 2026.
- Captured both operative articles and structured all 14 attached model-contract clauses.
- Model governs brokerage-client agreement, KYC, collateral, margin calls, securities return/close-out, fees, confidentiality, notices, dispute resolution, one-year auto-renewal and five-year record retention.
- Effective 29 October 2026.
- Full record: [Decision 3350/2026 Arabic text](agent3_fra_decision_3350_2026_short_selling_model_contract_arabic_text.md).

## Legal relationship
- Decision 155/2026 establishes substantive short-selling/securities-borrowing controls; Decision 3350/2026 approves the standard contract required for client onboarding under those controls.
- These are distinct instruments: Board decision and Chairperson model-contract decision. Do not merge the template clauses into the statutory/regulatory article numbering of Decision 155.

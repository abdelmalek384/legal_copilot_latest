## 2. Suez Canal Authority — Law 30/1975 and amendments
### Law 30/1975
**Arabic:** قرار رئيس جمهورية مصر العربية بالقانون رقم 30 لسنة 1975 بنظام هيئة قناة السويس  
Gazette issue 24, 12 June 1975; issued 29 May 1975; effective on publication. The Suez Canal Authority's own official legal page reproduces all 17 articles in Arabic.  
Official authority source: https://www.suezcanal.gov.eg/Arabic/About/CanalTreatiesAndDecrees/Pages/ARepublicanDecreeLawNo.30of1975.aspx  
Manshurat catalogue: https://manshurat.org/node/30662

### Law 4/1998
Amends Law 30/1975:
- adds a second paragraph to Article 2 stating SCA reports to the Prime Minister;
- adds a second paragraph to Article 3 governing Cabinet approval/no-objection timing for board resolutions;
- deletes the phrase concerning expropriation for public benefit from Article 9.
Gazette issue 3 bis (أ), 19 January 1998; effective 20 January 1998. Full Arabic three-article text is on the official SCA page: https://www.suezcanal.gov.eg/Arabic/About/CanalTreatiesAndDecrees/Pages/LawNo4.aspx

### Law 34/2014
Replaces the first paragraph of Article 3 of Law 30/1975 concerning appointment of the SCA Board of Directors. Issued 6 May 2014; Gazette issue 18 bis (ب), 6 May 2014; effective 7 May 2014. Full Arabic two-article text is on the official SCA page: https://www.suezcanal.gov.eg/Arabic/About/CanalTreatiesAndDecrees/Pages/Law_No_34_2014.aspx

### Amendment lead: Law 11/1976
LAW HUB's consolidated text says Law 11/1976 deleted the second paragraph of Article 5 of Law 30/1975. I did not locate an authoritative source or the amendment text in this pass. Treat it as an unverified amendment lead, not a confirmed tranche instrument.

## 3. Dry and specialised ports — Law 1/1996 chain
### Law 1/1996
**Arabic:** قانون رقم 1 لسنة 1996 في شأن الموانئ التخصصية (later retitled for dry and specialised ports).  
Gazette issue 5, 1 February 1996; issued 24 January 1996; effective 2 February 1996 per Manshurat. The 8-article secondary transcription covers fishing, mining, petroleum and tourism ports, licensing/technical studies, Transport Ministry supervision, tariffs and implementation authority.
- Manshurat PDF/catalogue: https://manshurat.org/node/13859
- Full secondary Arabic text: https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-1-1996
- Consolidated text: https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11520
- Official Transport Sector service page confirms the current administrative workflow and identifies Decisions 81/1999 and 190/2021: https://www.mts.gov.eg/ar/الخدمات-المقدمة-من-خلال-قطاع-النقل-البحري/ترخيص-ميناء-تخصصى/

### Law 22/1998
Adds Article 4 bis to Law 1/1996 authorising public-utility concessions for public/specialised ports and specialised berths, subject to competition, supervision, maintenance, legal compliance, transfer at end of concession and Cabinet approval. Two issuing provisions identified in secondary text; publication date reported 25 April 1998.
- Secondary text: https://mksegypt.org/public/ar/laws/24083
- Consolidated annotation: https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11520
- Gazette issue number/page not captured.

### Law 10/2017
Retitles Law 1/1996 as the Law on Dry and Specialised Ports and replaces Articles 1, 2 and 4 bis. The amendments bring dry ports within scope, change establishment approvals to Prime Minister/Cabinet procedure, and change concession term to 15 years (extendable by statute up to 30 years).
- Gazette issue 13 bis, 31 March 2017; effective 1 April 2017 per Manshurat metadata.
- Manshurat catalogue/PDF: https://manshurat.org/node/24789
- Secondary text: https://lawyeregypt.net/قانون-رقم-10-لسنة-2017-بتعديل-بعض-أحكام-القانون-رقم-1-لسنة-1996-في-شأن-الموانئ-التخصصية/

### Executive regulation — Minister of Transport Decision 81/1999
The official Maritime Transport and Logistics Sector service page identifies Decision 81/1999 as the executive regulation of Law 1/1996. It is also cited in later ministerial decisions and official Ministry of Environment guidance.
- Official Transport Sector page: https://www.mts.gov.eg/ar/الخدمات-المقدمة-من-خلال-قطاع-النقل-البحري/ترخيص-ميناء-تخصصى/
- Official EEAA guide listing: https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/Uploads/Topics/Files/20221212130554989.pdf
- Full operative regulation not acquired in this pass.

### Minister of Transport Decision 190/2021
Amends Decision 81/1999; official Manshurat metadata gives publication 10 May 2021, and the official Transport Sector page describes the amendment as setting the fee for issuing port licences.
- Manshurat catalogue/PDF: https://manshurat.org/node/73644
- Secondary Gazette issue 106 text locator: https://egyptianlaws.com/?p=180733
- Full amendment text not transcribed.

## 4. Inland navigation — Law 10/1956, Law 57/1962 and Decision 282/1998
### Law 10/1956
**Arabic:** قانون رقم 10 لسنة 1956 في شأن الملاحة الداخلية  
Published in Egyptian Proceedings issue 9 bis (غير اعتيادي), 30 January 1956; issued 25 January 1956; effective after 30 days. Full 22-article text was read from a secondary legal blog. It governs licensing of internal-water vessels, vessel inspection, safety, licence validity, fees, ferries/regular passenger services, crew licences, offences, administrative detention and navigation restrictions.
- EastLaws metadata and text: https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/30-01-1956/no-10?id=2216&type=1
- Secondary full Arabic text with amendment notes: https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2013/03/10-1956.html
- Law 167/2022 later repeals Articles 4, 8, 9 and 10, and its issuing Article 3 changes the competent licensing authority in Article 3 of Law 10/1956. The rest of Law 10/1956 must be read with those express changes; do not mark the entire 1956 law repealed.

### Law 57/1962
Amends Law 10/1956, including Articles 2, 3, 5, 8, 9, 12 and 13; adds a paragraph to Article 14 and inserts Article 20 bis (priority lien for fees/royalties). Manshurat metadata reports issuance 31 January 1962 and publication 5 February 1962, Gazette issue 32.
- Manshurat catalogue/PDF: https://manshurat.org/node/33195
- Consolidated article notes in the secondary 1956 text above.
- Exact amendment law articles were not fully transcribed.

### Minister of Transport Decision 282/1998 — Navigation licences in internal waters
**Arabic:** قرار وزير النقل والمواصلات رقم 282 لسنة 1998 بشأن التراخيص الملاحية في المياه الداخلية  
Issued 17 December 1998; published in Egyptian Proceedings issue 68, 25 March 1999; effective 26 March 1999. The secondary text contains 73 articles and detailed rules for construction/import approvals, vessel technical safety, licence applications/renewals, crew qualifications, passenger and cargo limits, dangerous cargo, environmental protection, navigation hazards, administrative stoppage and penalties.
- Secondary full text: https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2021/03/282-1998.html
- Manshurat catalogue/PDF: https://manshurat.org/node/13937
- **Numbering anomaly:** Manshurat metadata shows document number 383, while the title and secondary text identify Minister Decision 282/1998. Do not treat “383” as the legal decision number without inspecting the PDF's first page.
- Full 73-article secondary text read; original Gazette pages not verified.

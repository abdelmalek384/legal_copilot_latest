## 1. Investment Law 72/2017 — scope and structure
- Applies to domestic and foreign investment regardless of size, under four systems: internal investment, investment zones, technological zones and free zones.
- Existing companies retain incentives/exemptions/guarantees for their original periods under transition provisions.
- Core topics:
  - Investment guarantees and protections.
  - Investor rights and non-discrimination.
  - Expropriation/withdrawal of licences and due process.
  - Investment incentives (general/special/additional).
  - Free zones and investment zones.
  - Investor service centres and one-stop-shop procedures.
  - Investment contracts, land allocation and licences.
  - Investor grievance committees and investment dispute settlement.
  - GAFI powers, statistics and reporting.
- Article 5: administrative authority may not cancel/suspend project licence or withdraw allocated land without prior notice of alleged violations, hearing investor's view and giving suitable cure period; GAFI opinion required within seven days; investor may grieve to committee under Article 83.
- Article 6: investor may establish, expand, develop, finance from abroad in foreign currency, own/manage/use/dispose of project and remit profits/liquidation proceeds abroad, subject to third-party rights and law.
- Article 9 promulgation: Executive Regulation by PM within 90 days; existing rules continue if consistent.
- Law 141/2019 adds final paragraphs to Articles 12 and 48, adds Article 74 item 14 and new Article 91 bis.

## 2. Executive Regulation PM Decision 2310/2017 — 140 articles
- 140 articles in secondary transcription; official consolidated PDF on MIFT/Invest in Egypt is current through latest amendments identified in that file.
- Promulgation Article 1 applies the Regulation without prejudice to State private-property Law 7/1991, Special Economic Zones Law 83/2002, Sinai Development Law 14/2012 and Industrial Licensing Law 15/2017.
- Promulgation Article 2 coordinates the Investment Law with Special Economic Zones Law 83/2002 and preserves special regimes.
- Promulgation Article 3 repeals the Executive Regulation of Investment Guarantees/Incentives Law 8/1997 (PM Decision 1820/2015), subject to transition.
- Regulation governs investor services, establishment, incorporation, licence coordination, general/special/additional incentives, investment zones, technological zones, free zones, project land allocation, committees, grievances and reporting.
- Article 42 requirements for projects benefiting from additional incentives include Egyptian joint-stock or LLC structure, proof of financial solvency and establishment after Investment Law commencement, subject to amendments.
- Article 126: annual statement of project investments, financial statements, employee numbers/nationalities/wages, capital/investment cost, headquarters/site, incentives, owners, commercial/tax numbers, community-development activities and approved/benefited purposes.
- Article 126 bis defines public/private entities for FDI statistical reporting; Article 126 bis A requires periodic data submissions through GAFI electronic system or other method.

## 3. Amendment and implementation graph
- Law 141/2019:
  - Adds final paragraphs to Articles 12 and 48.
  - Adds Article 74 item 14 empowering GAFI to collect information/data for FDI stock calculation, subject to national security, privacy and confidentiality.
  - Adds Article 91 bis penalty up to EGP 50,000 for actual manager of legal person where intentional/knowing failure to provide data contributes to offence.
- Law 160/2023: amendment to Investment Law 72/2017 (full Gazette text and affected article list still to be extracted).

## 4. Investment dispute mechanisms
- Law 72/2017 provides multiple routes:
  - Investor grievance committees against administrative decisions.
  - Investor Dispute Settlement Centre at GAFI (mediation).
  - Ministerial Committee for Investment Disputes.
  - Ministerial Committee for Settlement of Investment Contract Disputes.
  - Arbitration or courts, subject to contract and applicable law.

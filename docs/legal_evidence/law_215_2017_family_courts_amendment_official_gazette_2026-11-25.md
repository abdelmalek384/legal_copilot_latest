# Law 215/2017 — amendment to Family Courts Law 10/2004

## Instrument identity
- **Instrument:** Law 215/2017, amending certain provisions of Law 10/2004 on establishing Family Courts.
- **Arabic title:** قانون رقم ٢١٥ لسنة ٢٠١٧ بتعديل بعض أحكام القانون رقم ١٠ لسنة ٢٠٠٤ في شأن إنشاء محاكم الأسرة
- **Issuance date:** 24 December 2017.
- **Publication:** Official Gazette, issue 51 bis (A), 25 December 2017.
- **Commencement:** Article 2 states it takes effect on the day after publication: 26 December 2017.
- **Amended provision:** Law 10/2004, Article 11.
- **Source:** PDF hosted by the Egyptian State Information Service (SIS) human-rights studies portal:
  https://hrightsstudies.sis.gov.eg/media/1205/قانونplusرقمplus215plusلسنةplus2017plusبتعديلplusبعضplusأحكامplusقانونplusمحاكمplusالأسرةplusرقمplus10plusلسنةplus2004-1.pdf

## Text observed in the PDF text layer
The two-article PDF text layer states:
- **Article 1:** adds a final paragraph to Article 11 of Law 10/2004. If the case concerns proof of lineage, the social specialist must include in the report whether the child is subject to the alternative-families system of the Ministry of Social Solidarity.
- **Article 2:** orders publication in the Official Gazette and provides that the law takes effect on the day after publication.

The SIS-hosted PDF identifies the Gazette issue/date in its heading. A separate LAWEG listing also identifies Law 215/2017 and displays the operative addition. A law-firm reproduction and the SIS page agree on the amendment's subject and operative effect. The PDF page-image screenshot request returned HTTP 502 during this review, so the text layer has not been visually checked against the page image.

## Relationship to Law 10/2004
This is a discrete amendment to Article 11, not a consolidated re-publication of Law 10/2004. It must be represented as a separate instrument and as an Article 11 version event. Do not replace the base Gazette text in place. The Ministry of Finance portal currently labels Law 10/2004 **سارٍ**, but its own notice says its electronic texts are guidance and the Official Gazette is the sole legal reference. That portal label does not establish that all later amendments have been incorporated or that the full currentness chain is complete.

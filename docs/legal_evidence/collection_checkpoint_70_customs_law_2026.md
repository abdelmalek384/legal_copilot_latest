# Collection checkpoint 70 — Customs Law 207/2020, Executive Regulation and 2026 amendments

## 1. Law 207/2020 — official Egyptian Customs Authority full text

- Official Customs Authority Gazette PDF:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/55140478-3cd5-4ced-88dc-153f231804c5.pdf
- Alternate official Customs PDF:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace159396001.pdf
- Official Ministry of Finance record:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-678cc5c92ca3f70008dae5be
- Gazette issue 45 bis (d), 11 November 2020.
- Promulgation Article 1 preserves international agreements, customs exemptions under other laws, Special Economic Zones Law 83/2002 and Investment Law 72/2017.
- Article 5 repeals Customs Law 66/1963 and Customs Exemptions Law 186/1986, plus inconsistent provisions.
- Article 6 commences the Law the day after publication.
- The official Customs Authority PDF contains the Law and is machine-readable. Preserve the original bytes and hash.

## 2. Executive Regulation — Minister of Finance Decision 430/2021

- Customs Authority official legislation index:
  https://www.customs.gov.eg/Legislations/Manshorat?categoryId=5
- Decision 430/2021 issued 31 August 2021 and publishes the Executive Regulation of Law 207/2020.
- Customs Authority's official consolidated commentary/edition:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace474457860.pdf
- The Customs index lists a consolidated regulation "with all amendments" and records:
  - Ministerial Decision 475/2021.
  - Decision 597/2021.
  - Decisions 482/2022, 543/2022 and 642/2022.
  - Decisions 52/2023, 209/2023, 307/2023 and 347/2023.
  - Decisions 50/2024 and 54/2024.
  - Decision 328/2026 (published in Al-Waqa'i issue 132 supplement A, 21 June 2026).
- The consolidated October 2024 PDF is an official Customs Authority publication prepared by the Authority, but the 2026 Decision 328/2026 must be layered on top; do not call the October 2024 edition current after the 2026 amendment.

## 3. Decision 328/2026 — 2026 amendment to the Executive Regulation

- Official Customs Authority Procedure Notice 16/2026 identifies Minister of Finance Decision 328/2026 as amending Decision 430/2021.
- Official notice PDF:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace065890338.pdf
- Notice states Decision 328/2026 was published in Al-Waqa'i issue 132 supplement (A), 21 June 2026.
- Obtain the original Gazette issue and extract the exact amended articles/annexes; do not rely only on the procedural notice.

## 4. Customs Authority Procedure Notice 16/2026

- Official Customs Authority PDF:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace065890338.pdf
- It references Law 207/2020, Executive Regulation 430/2021 and prior Procedure Notices 1, 2 and 9/2026.
- It tells customs ports/offices to apply the new Decision 328/2026 amendments and related procedures.
- It is an operational customs instruction, not a statute or ministerial regulation. Keep it in the operational-guidance layer and record its effective date/scope.

## 5. Joint Procedure 11/2026 and Import Notice 18/2026 — scrap/waste shipments

- Official Customs Authority PDF:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace324063360.pdf
- Joint Notice: Procedure 11 and Import Notice 18/2026.
- References Customs Law 207/2020, Executive Regulation 430/2021, Import/Export Law 118/1975, Executive Regulation 770/2005, Investment Law 72/2017 and Joint Notice 16/2026.
- It regulates the release of specified ship-generated waste/scrap under Article 13 of the Import/Export Executive Regulation, with customs control and administrative expenses.
- This is a shipment/category-specific operational notice; preserve the covered goods, ports, procedures and effective date separately from the general Customs Law.

## 7. Key statutory topics to extract from Law 207/2020

- Customs territory, customs zones and customs offices.
- Customs value, tariff classification and origin.
- Import/export declarations, customs data and supporting documents.
- Advance rulings, pre-arrival processing and ACI (Advance Cargo Information).
- Customs warehouses, temporary storage, transit and transshipment.
- Temporary admission, drawback, inward/outward processing and re-export.
- Exemptions and special economic zones.
- Customs brokers and authorized economic operators.
- Inspection, search, seizure, post-clearance audit and customs guarantees.
- Abandoned goods, auctions, destruction and disposal.
- Customs offences, administrative settlements, penalties, confiscation and appeals.
- Customs refunds and repayment of overpaid duties.

- Investment Law 72/2017 and Special Economic Zones Law 83/2002.
- VAT Law 67/2016, Income Tax Law 91/2005 and Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020.
- CBE Law 194/2020, AML Law 80/2002 and payment/foreign-exchange controls.

- Maritime Commerce Law 8/1990, Ports Law 1/1996, Internal Navigation Law 10/1956 and Civil Aviation Law 28/1981.
- Labour Law 14/2025 for customs-broker/warehouse employees and port workers.

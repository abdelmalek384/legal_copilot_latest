## Attached financial-leasing model — Arabic clauses captured from page images

The form is headed «نموذج عقد تأجير تمويلي» and identifies the licensed lessor and lessee, their legal identities and registration particulars, the asset supplier/contractor, title to the asset, rental values, dates, purchase option and contract duration.

### Essential identification and asset fields

The form requires:
- Lessor company name, FRA financing-company register number, commercial register number, address and authorised signatory.
- Lessee name, activity, legal form, commercial register number, tax-card number, address and authorised signatory.
- Supplier/contractor name, lessor title/ownership basis, and price under the title document.
- Asset type (tangible/intangible/right of usufruct), any usufruct owner, asset description and purpose of use.
- Advance payment, periodic rental amount, aggregate rental value, due dates, contract commencement/expiry, purchase price/option and date for acquiring the asset.

### Model leasing agreement — selected verbatim operative clauses

**Asset and supplier responsibility (Article 2):**

«في حالة قيام المستأجر بالتفاوض مباشرة مع المورد أو المقاول بشأن مواصفات الأصل المؤجر فإنه يكون مسؤولاً عن خطئه في اختيار وتحديد مواصفات الأصل المؤجر.»

«ويكون المؤجر مسؤولاً عن أفعاله أو تصرفاته التي تؤدي إلى غلط في اختيار الأصل المؤجر أو إلى تمكين المورد أو المقاول أو الغير من التعرض للمستأجر على أي وجه في الانتفاع بالأصول المؤجرة.»

«ويتحمل المستأجر من تاريخ استلامه للأصل المؤجر، المسؤولية المدنية والجنائية عن الحوادث والأضرار التي يسببها الأصل المؤجر للغير، كما يتحمل المسؤولية عن الحوادث والأضرار التي تلحق بالأصل المؤجر الناجمة عن الاستخدام أثناء حيازته له، ولا يجوز الرجوع على المؤجر عن أي أضرار يحدثها الأصل المؤجر طوال مدة سريان العقد.»

**Purchase option (Article 3):**

«للمستأجر الحق في شراء الأصل المؤجر محل التعاقد كله أو بعضه وفقاً للشروط الآتية: ...»

«ولا تنتقل ملكية الأصل المؤجر إلى المستأجر إلا بعد قيامه بسداد كامل الثمن والقيم الإيجارية المتفق عليها، ويلتزم المؤجر بتحرير عقد بيع الأصل المؤجر إلى المستأجر.»

**Lessee obligations (Articles 4–5):**

The form requires the lessee to use, maintain and repair the asset consistently with its intended purpose and technical instructions; promptly notify the lessor of obstacles preventing use; pay rent and agreed charges on time; refrain from disposing of the asset or allowing third-party use without written consent; and return/hand over the asset in accordance with the agreement.

**Lessor obligations (Article 6):**

The lessor records the asset, contract number and registration date in the FRA contract register and must make the record visible; the lessor or its delegate has a right to inspect the asset periodically to verify its condition and use.

**Assignment and termination (Articles 7–8):**

The lessor may assign the financial-leasing contract to another lessor subject to notice to the lessee; the assignment does not prejudice the lessee's rights or contractual guarantees. The model lists termination events including failure to pay rent after notice and the specified cure period, death of a lessee/partner subject to continuation request, bankruptcy/insolvency or dissolution, and other contractually specified events. Termination is recorded in the contract register, and the model distinguishes cancellation of the register entry from third-party rights arising during the registration period.

**Disputes and notices (Articles 9–13):**

The model includes an economic-court jurisdiction clause for disputes arising from contract breach, with a separate arbitration clause if agreed; parties choose domiciles for notices; amendments require written form; and the contract is executed in multiple signed originals for registration and party retention.

## Attached factoring model — Arabic clauses captured from page images

The form is headed «عقد تخصيم» and identifies the licensed factor and seller, the seller's commercial register and tax details, the debtor/buyer, the underlying sale/service transactions and the financial rights assigned.

### Factoring model structure

- Parties: licensed factoring company (factor) and seller/provider of goods or services.
- Recitals: factor is licensed by FRA; seller owns current and/or future financial rights arising from commercial sale/service transactions.
- Article 1: recitals form part of the agreement.
- Article 2: factor purchases/receives the specified current and/or future financial rights and associated security rights, as set out in schedules.
- Article 3: transfer of rights and related guarantees follows Civil Code rules on assignment and the contractual notice mechanism.
- Article 4: seller warrants the existence, validity, maturity and commercial origin of the financial rights; the rights must arise from genuine commercial transactions and be transferable.
- Articles 5–6: term, advance amount, maximum funding, fees/commissions and other charges are agreed and entered in the form.
- Article 7: seller supplies complete supporting data and documents about the underlying transactions, debtors, guarantees and collection operations.
- Article 8: seller must not reassign, pledge or otherwise dispose of assigned rights or undertake inconsistent obligations without written consent.
- Article 9: recourse/repurchase arrangements are specified for defined cases, including debtor non-payment attributable to seller breach, invalidity or prior assignment; the form preserves the debtor's defenses and the factor's rights under the agreement.
- Article 10: the factor may transfer the factoring contract or assigned rights to eligible entities subject to the applicable law and notice/consent provisions.
- Article 11: disputes are referred to the competent economic courts, with an optional arbitration provision if expressly agreed.
- Articles 12–13: notices, amendments, counterparts and signatures.

**Important:** The factoring form is an attached model template, not a self-contained statement of the current substantive law of assignment. Apply Law 176/2018, Civil Code rules and subsequent FRA factoring decisions together; do not treat blank fields or optional clauses as enacted rules.

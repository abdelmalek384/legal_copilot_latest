- Gazette issue 40, 5 October 1972; replaces Law 55/1959.
- Ministry portal labels it in force and identifies Law 136/1984 as an amendment; the portal warns Gazette is authoritative.

### Structure and scope
- Establishes State Council as an independent judicial body with:
  1. Judicial section.
  2. Fatwa section.
  3. Legislation section.
- Judicial section includes Supreme Administrative Court, Administrative Court, Administrative Courts, Disciplinary Courts and State Commissioners Authority.
- Governs administrative disputes, annulment actions, compensation for administrative decisions, public-service disputes, disciplinary cases, administrative contracts and other statutory jurisdiction.
- Also regulates State Council Fatwa and Legislation departments, review of draft legislation, contracts and public-law opinions, membership, appointments, promotions, discipline and administration.

### Selected provisions
- Article 1: State Council is an independent judicial body.
- Article 2: consists of judicial, fatwa and legislation sections.
- Articles 3–5: judicial section/court organization and administrative court locations.
- Article 10 onward: Supreme Administrative Court and Administrative Court jurisdiction and appeal paths.
- Articles 58–67: fatwa/legislation sections, including review of draft laws/regulations and specified public contracts/obligations.
- Article 68: General Assembly of State Council.
- Article 69: annual report on legislative deficiencies, ambiguity and administrative abuse/excess of authority.
- Article 104 (as displayed in consolidated text): special jurisdiction over final administrative decisions concerning State Council members, compensation, salaries, pensions and related disputes.
- 141-article consolidated transcription contains annotations for amendments and constitutional invalidations; extract all 141 articles and linked judgments before legal use.

### Amendment and judicial invalidation graph

## 2. Supreme Constitutional Court Law 48/1979

### Sources
- Full text sources:
  https://manshurat.org/node/63719
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12211
  https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?ItemID=153&NID=53568&Type=14&action=ViewActivePages
- Gazette issue 36, 6 September 1979; effective 20 September 1979.
- 57 articles in current transcription; amendment history visible in sources.

### Structure and jurisdiction
- Establishes Supreme Constitutional Court as an independent judicial body headquartered in Cairo.
- Article 25 gives exclusive jurisdiction over:
  1. Judicial review of constitutionality of laws and regulations.
  2. Conflicts of jurisdiction between judicial bodies/tribunals.
  3. Conflicts between final judgments issued by different judicial bodies.
- Article 26 empowers the Court to interpret legislative texts where application conflict has importance requiring uniform interpretation.
- Article 27 allows incidental constitutional review of a statutory provision connected to a dispute before the Court.
- Article 27 bis added by Law 137/2021: review constitutionality of decisions of international organizations/bodies and foreign-court judgments requested to be enforced against the State.
- Articles 48–49: judgments and decisions final, binding on all State authorities and everyone; unconstitutionality judgment takes effect from day after publication unless Court specifies another date; tax provisions have direct effect only; criminal provision judgments can nullify convictions based on that provision.
- Article 50: Court exclusively decides disputes concerning execution of its judgments/decisions.

### Amendment graph
- Law 168/1998: amendment to Article 49 (effect of unconstitutionality judgments).
- Law 48/2011: replaces definition in Article 2 and related references from "member" to "Vice President of Court" as stated.
- Law 91/2015: repeals Article 44 bis 1.
- Law 78/2019: changes appointment provisions (Article 5).
- Law 137/2021: adds Article 27 bis.
- Verify all changes against Gazette and any later amendments.

## 3. Relationship and status
- State Council Law 47/1972 governs administrative judicial structure and jurisdiction; Supreme Constitutional Court Law 48/1979 governs constitutional review and jurisdiction conflicts.
- Both are distinct from Judicial Authority Law 46/1972, Administrative Prosecution Law 117/1958 and Criminal Procedure Law 174/2025.
- These laws are especially important to model judicial invalidation of statutory provisions, administrative decision review and public-law remedies.

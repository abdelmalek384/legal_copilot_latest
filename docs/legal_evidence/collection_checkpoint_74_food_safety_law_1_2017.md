## 1. Law 1/2017 — institutional design
- Establishes National Food Safety Authority (NFSA) as public legal person, service authority, headquartered in Cairo with branches in governorates, reporting to President of Republic.
- Article 2 gives NFSA exclusive responsibility for food-handling control previously assigned to ministries, public authorities, government departments and local administration under food-safety-related legislation.
- Transitional provisions: NFSA was to assume its functions within one year, extendable by another similar period by PM decision; until assumption, former authorities continued performing their legal functions and existing decisions remained effective if not inconsistent.
- Article 5: PM issues Executive Regulation and necessary decisions within six months.
- 25 substantive articles.
- Article 3 sets NFSA powers:
  - Mandatory food-safety rules aligned with international standards and national requirements.
  - Control/inspection of food handling and verification of standards at all stages.
  - Risk-based control and recall/rapid-alert systems.
  - Import/export control, food establishment licensing and certificates for locally produced food exports.
  - Emergency measures for unsafe imported/domestic food.
  - Laboratory/analysis, sampling, traceability and food information systems.
  - Coordination with relevant ministries and international bodies.
- Article 18 creates grievance committees chaired by a State Council vice-president; administrative decisions may be challenged within 15 days of notification/knowledge; committee decides within 15 days, or three days in urgent cases; judicial review before Administrative Court follows statutory conditions.
- Law also governs governance, board, staffing, finance, resources, inspections, violations and penalties.

## 2. Executive Regulation Decision 412/2019 — 30 articles, eight chapters
- PM Decision 412/2019 promulgates the attached Executive Regulation and repeals inconsistent provisions.
- Gazette issue 7 bis, 18 February 2019; effective day after publication.
- Chapter structure (eight chapters):
  1. General provisions/definitions.
  2. NFSA administration and governance.
  3. Obligations of food-establishment operators and food handlers.
  4. Food import/export and control.
  5. Inspection, sampling and laboratory analysis.
  6. Risk analysis, food incidents and recall.
  7. Grievances and administrative measures.
  8. Final/transitional provisions.
- Article 1 defines authority, competent authority, food, food handling, food establishment operator, food handlers, mandatory technical rules, risk assessment, traceability and related terms.
- Article 2 authorizes NFSA to issue mandatory technical rules covering food safety standards, product characteristics, production methods, packaging, labelling and information.
- Article 4 requires risk-analysis procedures to ensure high protection for human health and consumer safety.
- Inspection may occur without prior notice except where a review requires notification; frequency is risk-based and considers hazards, operator compliance history, confidence in private controls and other information.
- Regulation provides for licensing/registration of food establishments, import/export controls, certificates, traceability, sampling, analysis, recall, grievance and enforcement.

## Legal effect and relationship map

1. The decision temporarily suspends acceptance of new applications to establish or obtain an FRA licence for consumer-finance activity using fintech under the referenced non-bank-financial-activities fintech legislation.
2. The suspension period is one year and is renewable.
3. Applications submitted before the decision's commencement are expressly excluded from the suspension.
4. The decision does **not**, on the retrieved wording, cancel existing licences or prohibit already-licensed consumer-finance firms from continuing ordinary activity.
5. Link this instrument to Consumer Finance Law 18/2020 and the fintech framework under Law 5/2022; it is an administrative licensing measure, not an amendment to either statute.
6. Currentness check required: search for any later FRA Board decision renewing, lifting, replacing or extending the one-year suspension before representing the rule as operative.

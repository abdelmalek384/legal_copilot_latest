# Law 33/1992 — historical amendment to Personal Status Law 25/1929

## Instrument identity
- **Instrument:** Law 33/1992, amending Decree-Law 25/1929 on certain personal-status provisions and Armed Forces Pension and Insurance Law 90/1975.
- **Date:** 1 June 1992.
- **Gazette reference:** Official Gazette issue 22 bis (B), 1 June 1992, identified by a secondary consolidated annotation; primary issue not acquired.
- **Commencement:** Article 4 states the day after publication: 2 June 1992.
- **Source:** MKSEgypt full text under CC BY 4.0: https://mksegypt.org/public/ar/laws/23685 ; secondary consolidated annotation: https://www.diaalwkeel.com/qanwn-rqm-25-lsnh-1929 ; additional secondary text: https://lawyeregypt.net/قانون-بشأن-المفقودين-في-بعض-الحوادث/

## Operative changes observed
- Article 1 replaces Articles 21 and 22 of Decree-Law 25/1929. The historical Article 21 sets a four-year general period for a missing person presumed likely to have perished; it sets a one-year rule for specified shipwreck/aircraft loss or armed-forces operational disappearance, with a competent-authority decision naming persons treated as deceased. Article 22 states the resulting effects on the wife's waiting period, inheritance and other consequences.
- Article 2 applies the law to certain earlier missing-person cases where at least one year had elapsed by commencement.
- Article 3 changes Article 38 of Armed Forces Pension and Insurance Law 90/1975 to a one-year period from disappearance.
- Article 4 provides for publication and next-day commencement.

## Historical succession
Law 33/1992's replacement of Articles 21 and 22 is a historical version. Law 140/2017 later replaced Article 21's second and third paragraphs and Article 22. The 2017 replacement should not be mistaken for the full repeal of every historical rule or every transitional effect in Law 33/1992; exact succession and residual effect require lawyer review.

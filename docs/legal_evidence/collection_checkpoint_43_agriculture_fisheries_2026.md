# Collection checkpoint 43 — agriculture, fisheries, lakes and aquaculture legislation (2026-11-17)

## 1. Agriculture Law 53/1966 — official Ministry of Agriculture full Arabic text

- Official Ministry PDF:
  https://moa.gov.eg/media/fill1ubq/egy153081.pdf
- Ministry of Agriculture official legislation catalogue:
  https://moa.gov.eg/ar/publications?type=Laws+and+regulations
- The official Ministry PDF is machine-searchable and begins with the promulgation of Law 53/1966 issuing the Agriculture Law.
- The Law repeals numerous earlier agricultural instruments, including laws on animal cruelty, cattle slaughter, cotton bollworm control, protection of useful birds, cotton seed control and animal export, as specified in its promulgation provisions.
- The Ministry's dedicated agricultural legislation search index currently reports 127 records:
  https://moa.gov.eg/agricultural-legislation/
- The 127-record index exposes ministerial decisions by year, decision number, publication date, decision type and subject area; examples include construction/executive regulations, plant variety registration, slaughterhouses, veterinary quarantine and other agricultural matters.
- This is a major official regulatory source. It should be harvested as individual decisions and linked to enabling articles of Law 53/1966, not bulk-copied as a single undifferentiated file.

## 2. Fisheries and Lakes Protection and Development Law 146/2021 — official Gazette PDF

- Official Ministry of Agriculture Gazette PDF:
  https://moa.gov.eg/media/zciejl0p/530289083-العدد-39-مكرر-أ-مؤمن.pdf
- Gazette issue 39 bis (A), 2 October 2021.
- Search-indexed text identifies Law 146/2021 issuing the Law on Protection and Development of Lakes and Fisheries Resources.
- Promulgation Article 1 applies the law to lakes specified by Presidential Decision, all water surfaces, and aquaculture lands specified by PM decision, without prejudice to Nature Reserves Law 102/1983 and water-resources/irrigation legislation.
- Promulgation Article 2 requires the Executive Regulation within six months; existing regulations/decisions continue insofar as they do not conflict until new rules are issued.
- Promulgation Article 3 repeals Fishing, Aquatic Life and Fish Farm Regulation Law 124/1983 and specified older authorities/decisions.
- This is a primary-source Gazette PDF with searchable Arabic text. Extract all articles, lake designations, fishing rights, aquaculture permits, environmental conditions, penalties and authority powers.

## 3. Executive Regulation — PM Decision 706/2023

- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Wj9D+7y4ExU%3D
- Decision 706/2023 issues the Executive Regulation of Law 146/2021.
- The preamble links the law to Internal Navigation 10/1956, Cooperatives 317/1956, Public Authorities 61/1963, Agriculture 53/1966, Local Administration 43/1979, Nile Protection 48/1982, Nature Reserves 102/1983, Water Cooperatives 123/1983, Ship Safety 232/1989, Maritime Commerce 8/1990, Environment 4/1994, Dry/Specialized Ports 1/1996, Public Contracts 182/2018, Non-Cash Payments 18/2019, Waste 202/2020, Water Resources 147/2021, Public Finance 6/2022 and River Transport 167/2022.
- The Regulation is essential for licensing, fishing activity, aquaculture, lake management, navigation, shoreline use, inspection and enforcement.
- Obtain full text and all schedules/annexes; map each regulation provision to the enabling law article.

## 4. Lake Nasser licensing and inter-agency decisions

- PM Decision 990/2023 establishes a permanent coordination committee for licensing activities in Lake Nasser, its shores, benefits, depressions and reservoir areas between the High Dam and Aswan Reservoir:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=xyurarNac44%3D
- It gives the committee exclusive review of permits for works/occupations in the defined area under Water Resources Law 147/2021 and Executive Regulation 81/2023; recommendations become binding after ministerial approval.
- PM Decision 1797/2025 adds a representative of the Future of Egypt Sustainable Development Agency to the committee:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=q4+mibDh+FM%3D
- PM Decision 989/2023 establishes a Higher Committee for permits for works/occupations on the Nile and its branches/banks downstream of the High Dam, including settlement applications for specified pre-147/2021 unlicensed works:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=X3mewbWhj%2Fk%3D
- These decisions create geographically scoped licensing authorities and must be represented as spatial rules, not generic national permissions.

## 5. Lake and fisheries land/property decisions

- Presidential Decision 227/2023 removes public-utility status from specified areas near Alexandria Port that were under the supervision of the Lake Protection and Development Authority and reclassifies them as State private property for Alexandria Port Authority logistics use:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=lHAn3kwNMbo%3D
- The decision includes exact areas in feddans/square metres and attached maps/coordinate tables.
- This is an example of a property-status change affecting a water/fisheries authority; the annexed maps and coordinates are essential to its legal effect.

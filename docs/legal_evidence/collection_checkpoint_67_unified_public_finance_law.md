# Collection checkpoint 67 — Unified Public Finance Law 6/2022 and amendment 18/2024

## 1. Law 6/2022 — official Ministry of Finance record

- Official Ministry of Finance record:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-63cd142f79394f000815481a
- Law 6/2022 issues the Unified Public Finance Law.
- The Ministry labels the law in force and warns that its electronic text is guidance; the Official Gazette is the authoritative legal reference.
- Related record:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-13
- The Law consolidates and replaces the previous public-budget and government-accounting frameworks, including Law 53/1973 on the State General Budget and Law 127/1981 on Government Accounting, subject to transitional provisions.
- Its policy is to modernize public financial management, introduce programme/performance budgeting, integrate budget execution/accounting and improve transparency, accountability and fiscal discipline.

## 2. Law 18/2024 — major amendment introducing General Government framework

- Official Ministry of Finance index lists Law 18/2024 as amending Law 6/2022:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/laws/law
- Official Ministry of Finance summary:
  https://assets.mof.gov.eg/files/dc8cfaa0-c7a6-11ef-9bbb-8bcad7a539db.pdf
- It introduces/defines "General Government" and expands the fiscal reporting/consolidation scope to include 59 economic public authorities.
- It establishes a ceiling on the General Government debt ratio/related fiscal indicators, with exceptions requiring approval by the President, Cabinet and House of Representatives as stated in the law.
- It requires full implementation of programme and performance budgeting within no more than six years.
- The Law 18/2024 amendments must be integrated into the 2022 base text, not stored as a standalone policy note.

## 4. Programme and performance budgeting

- Law 6/2022 and Law 18/2024 require transition from traditional line-item budget to programme/performance budgeting.
- The Ministry's official financial material describes the programme budget as a tool for tying allocations to programmes, projects, activities, outputs and performance indicators.
- A transition period is provided; full implementation is to occur within the statutory maximum of six years under Law 18/2024.
- The budget continues to use chapters/items while programme/performance budgeting is progressively implemented.
- Store this as a staged implementation obligation, not as proof that all entities have already fully migrated.

## 5. Current 2026 spending restrictions — PM Decision 3530/2026

- PM Decision 3530/2026 is recorded in checkpoint 50:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=TXzT8vO0lfQ%3D
- It imposes temporary three-month expenditure-rationalization measures, with some restrictions extending through 30 June 2027.
- It cites Law 6/2022, Budget Law 77/2026 and Development Plan Law 78/2026.
- It is a temporary executive measure under the public-finance framework, not a permanent amendment to Law 6/2022.

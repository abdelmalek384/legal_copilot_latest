# Collection checkpoint 48 — Social Security Regulation 522/2026 and Social Insurance Law 148/2019 (2026-11-17)

## 1. Social Security Law 12/2025 — Executive Regulation Decision 522/2026

- Law 12/2025 and its Executive Regulation are separate instruments.
- Ministry of Social Solidarity official announcement:
  https://www.moss.gov.eg/المركز-الإعلامي/الأخبار/وزيرة-التضامن-الاجتماعي-تصدر-قرار-ا-بإصدار-اللائحة-التنفيذية-لقانون-الضمان-الاجتماعي-الصادر-بالقانون-رقم-12-لسنة-2025/
- The Ministry confirms in July 2026 that it issued the Executive Regulation and that it was published in Al-Waqa'i.
- Decision number/publication metadata from secondary legal transcriptions:
  - Minister of Social Solidarity Decision 522/2026.
  - Gazette issue 138 supplement (b), 28 June 2026.
  - Signed 21 June 2026; effective the day after publication.
  - Full 57-article Arabic transcription:
    https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/decree-522-2026
  - Secondary transcription also available:
    https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/06/522-2026-12-2025.html
- Decision 522/2026 expressly repeals the Executive Regulation of the former Social Security Law 137/2010, issued by Ministerial Decision 451/2010, and repeals inconsistent provisions.
- The Regulation has five books/chapters:
  1. Definitions and general provisions.
  2. Conditional cash support (Takaful), unconditional cash support (Karama), application, review, smart cards and lapse of support.
  3. Governance of eligibility, review, support committees, grievance committees and appeals.
  4. Exceptional assistance, disasters/calamities, equal opportunities and employment/means-of-livelihood support.
  5. Investment of the Takaful and Karama Fund.
- This is a major implementation update: earlier 2025 ministry workshops were drafting activity only; by June/July 2026 the Executive Regulation had been issued.
- Obtain the original Official Gazette issue 138 supplement (b), compare all 57 articles, and retain the official PDF/hash before source-matched status.

## 2. Extracted provisions from Decision 522/2026 secondary text — validation required

- Article 1 definitions include field social research, electronic system and electronic form, disaster/calamity, support recipient and related terms.
- Article 3 defines poverty for support eligibility using a poverty map and a proxy means-testing formula developed with CAPMAS.
- Article 4 permits notifications by registered mail, SMS to registered mobile numbers, electronic system messages or official field visits.
- Articles 19 onward govern verification of eligibility through data exchange with relevant entities, including income, social insurance/pensions, financial inclusion, economic empowerment and lending databases.
- Articles 26–33 establish ministry/directorate grievance committees and grievance procedures.
- Article 33 sets a fee for grievance applications by percentage of requested support: 1% for rejection of an initial support application, 1.5% for deductions and 2% for suspension.
- Articles 34 onward address refusal of suitable work/livelihood opportunities without acceptable reason, including notification, records, interviews and reporting.
- The secondary text is long and may contain transcription/OCR errors. Do not operationalize fees, eligibility or deadlines without Gazette page verification and lawyer review.

## 3. Social Insurance and Pensions Law 148/2019 — Executive Regulation 2437/2021

- Law 148/2019 is a separate social insurance/pensions statute, not the same as Social Security Law 12/2025.
- Executive Regulation: PM Decision 2437/2021.
- The preamble to National Social Insurance Authority Decision 2900/2026 expressly identifies PM Decision 2437/2021 as the Executive Regulation of Law 148/2019.
- Ministry of Social Solidarity official catalogue lists Law 148/2019:
  https://www.moss.gov.eg/الإصدارات/القوانين-والتشريعات/
- Obtain the full Law 148/2019, Decision 2437/2021 and all amendments/ministerial decisions, contribution tables, insured-wage ceilings, pensions, disability, work injury, unemployment, survivor rights and actuarial rules.

## 4. Alternative private social insurance systems — Law 64/1980 and Decision 52/1985

- Law 64/1980 regulates alternative private social insurance systems.
- Executive Regulation: Minister of Insurance/Social Affairs Decision 52/1985.
- These systems are distinct from the statutory national social insurance system under Law 148/2019; they operate subject to registration/approval and regulatory oversight.
- National Social Insurance Authority Decision 2900/2026:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=wVBiB4iWnPw%3D
- Dated 6 May 2026; amends Articles 52, 54 and 55 of the alternative private social insurance system rules for employees of Suez Canal Bank.
- Article 52 revises funeral expenses to three months' pension.
- Article 54 revises end-of-service lump-sum benefits for old age, total disability, death, permanent partial disability and missing insured persons, with minimum benefits in specified cases.
- Article 55 revises resignation benefit rates based on service length, ranging from 150% to 600% of monthly contribution wage per year of service.
- This is a scheme-specific decision, not a general amendment to Law 148/2019.

## 5. Universal Health Insurance and "non-capable" eligibility cross-link

- PM Decision 4586/2023 identifies categories of persons unable to bear the costs of Universal Health Insurance under Law 2/2018, including beneficiaries of Takaful/Karama and social security, unemployed persons without unemployment benefit, institutionalized persons, persons with disabilities unable to earn, disaster-affected groups and low-income households.
- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=IFBtS0Zru+8%3D
- It repeals PM Decision 1948/2019 and establishes data-sharing and review mechanisms among the Ministry of Social Solidarity, National Social Insurance Authority, Ministry of Finance and Universal Health Insurance Authority.
- Link Law 12/2025, Decision 522/2026, Law 148/2019 and Law 2/2018, but do not conflate their eligibility and contribution regimes.

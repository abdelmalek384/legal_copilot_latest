# Checkpoint 114 — Law 114/1946: official Ministry of Justice text, consolidated Court of Cassation text and version discrepancies

## 1. New official government-hosted text source

**Ministry of Justice / Real Estate Publicity and Notarization Authority PDF:**
https://www.investinegypt.gov.eg/PublishingImages/Lists/LawsAndRegulations/AllItems/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%20114%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%201946%20%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%89.pdf

- The PDF is hosted on the official Invest in Egypt portal and identifies the Ministry of Justice and the Real Estate Publicity and Notarization Authority.
- Web text extraction exposes 317 lines and the full statutory structure through Article 61, including articles later repealed, amendments incorporated in an older consolidated text, and some original wording.
- This is an official government-hosted full-text reproduction, **not a verified image of the original Official Gazette issue 85 dated 24 August 1946**.
- It is not the latest consolidated text: it omits the 2022 Law 9/2022 insertions/replacements and reflects older versions of several provisions. It must not be used as the current text without the amendment layer.

## 2. Official Court of Cassation source and consolidated version

A PDF headed “محكمة النقض — مركز المعلومات” reproduces Law 114/1946 and labels it “وفقاً لآخر تعديل صادر في 6 مارس عام 2022”:
https://alhasswalawfirm.com/uploads/files/law-114-1946-1789186916244-431326360.pdf

- It identifies the original publication as الوقائع المصرية issue 85, 24 August 1946.
- The PDF includes Article 10 bis, Article 22 bis, Article 23 bis, Article 27 bis, Article 35 bis and Article 36 bis and the 2022 amendment annotations.
- It is hosted on a private law-firm domain but bears the Court of Cassation Information Center header; therefore record it as a judicially attributed reproduction with external hosting, not as a direct official Court of Cassation domain or original Gazette scan.
- Its 2022 version states that Law 9/2022 (Official Gazette issue 9 bis (A), 6 March 2022) replaced Articles 9 (specified paragraphs), 21, 22, 23 bis, 28, 33, 35, 36 bis, 48, 49, 50 and 57; repealed Articles 9(5), 24, 26, 29 and 34; and inserted Articles 10 bis and 22 bis.
- It also records the 2020 insertion of Article 19 bis (river accretion compensation) and the 2021 postponement Law 5/2021 affecting Law 186/2020.

## 3. Amendment and publication chronology confirmed or cross-referenced

- Base Law 114/1946: issued 11 August 1946; published in Egyptian Proceedings issue 85, 24 August 1946; commencement 1 January 1947 (the exact promulgation/commencement clause should be checked against the original Gazette page).
- Corrective Royal Decree, 29 September 1947: correction to Article 5 and other text references.
- Law 69/1950: amended Article 2 (later affected by subsequent repeal).
- Law 181/1957: repealed a provision added in 1956.
- Law 82/1957: inserted Article 12 bis.
- Law 25/1976: major amendment, published Official Gazette issue 12, 23 March 1976; replaced Articles 6, 8 and 17; added/changed Articles 9, 10, 16, 23, 23 bis, 27 bis, 36 bis, 53 bis and other provisions; amended the registration procedure.
- Law 223/1996: amendment to Article 21, Official Gazette issue 27 bis, 14 July 1996.
- Law 186/2020: inserted Article 19 bis; its commencement was postponed by Law 5/2021.
- Law 9/2022: major amendments as mapped above; Official Gazette issue 9 bis (A), 6 March 2022. The original Gazette issue PDF is available in IDSC DSpace metadata but the original bundle is request-only.

The complete amendment chain remains open: the exact text and publication of every intermediate amendment and correction (including Law 5/1964 and older registration reforms) must be mapped and compared against Gazette issue scans.

## 4. Reconciliation cautions

- The Ministry of Justice PDF is a government-hosted statutory reproduction, but not the original Gazette scan. It contains a mixture of base and earlier amended wording; do not assume it is a single coherent historical version.
- The Court of Cassation Information Center PDF reflects the 2022 amendment but is hosted outside the official court domain. It is not an original Gazette scan either.
- The Law 9/2022 original Official Gazette issue 9 bis (A), 6 March 2022, has official DSpace metadata and a 194.71 KB original bundle; access is request-only. An Al-Ahram Gate Gazette scan reproduction was already recorded in checkpoint 85.
- No new statutory instrument is counted here. This is a source upgrade and provenance reconciliation for existing acquisition record EG-0144.
- No original 1946 Gazette PDF was acquired in this step. Do not change that status to “obtained”.

## Scope and cross-links
- Applies to appellate chambers hearing family appeals originating from the courts in Quesna, Berket El-Sabaa and El-Bagour centres.
- Changes the hearing venue from the Shebin El-Kom Court of Appeal mission to the Benha Court of Appeal mission, Benha Courts Complex, Abdel Salam Aref Street, El-Mansheya district, Qalyubia.
- The same Article 1 also covers civil and specialized labour appellate chambers; the family-court component is explicitly within scope.
- The preamble cites Family Courts Law 10/2004, Personal Status Procedure Law 1/2000, and Justice Minister Decision 4844/2004 on family prosecution organization.
- This is a venue/allocation instrument, not an amendment to substantive personal-status rules.

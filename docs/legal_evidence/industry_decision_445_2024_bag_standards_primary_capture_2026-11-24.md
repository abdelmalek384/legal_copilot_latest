## 1. Primary Gazette capture and exact legal scope

The SIS-hosted Official Proceedings PDF for issue 217 contains Decision 445/2024 at printed pages 21–24. The decision's first article requires producers and importers of the engineering and chemical goods listed in its schedule to produce in accordance with the Egyptian standards listed there.

The Gazette table identifies four bag-related standards:

## 3. Publication and transition dates

- Decision date shown in the Gazette: 7 September 2024.
- Publication: Egyptian Proceedings issue 217 dated 2 October 2024.
- Article 4: effective from the day after publication.
- Article 2: one-year compliance adjustment period for item 11 (Standard 8917); six months for the remaining listed items.
- The text does not provide a separate formula for counting the compliance periods. Do not turn these dates into a definitive current enforcement position without checking subsequent amendments, extensions, or administrative directions.

## 5. Relationship with Decisions 559/2022 and 662/2025

1. **Decision 559/2022 / Standard 3040:** the separate mandatory standard for single-use plastic shopping bags. It is not one of the four bag-related standards in Decision 445/2024's table.
2. **Decision 445/2024:** adds/mandates standards for woven plastic shopping bags (8918), reusable non-woven plastic shopping bags (8919), paper shopping bags (8925), and compostable carrying bags (8917).
3. **Decision 662/2025:** its Article 1 applies EPR to plastic shopping bags produced/imported under Standard 3040. Its defined scope should not automatically be extended to Standards 8917, 8918, 8919 or 8925 merely because they concern other types of bags.
4. The fact that Decision 445/2024 and Decision 559/2022 both concern bags does not make their technical standards interchangeable. The material, reuse characteristics, purpose and standard number must remain explicit in retrieval.
5. No repeal or amendment of Decision 559/2022 or Standard 3040 is established by Decision 445/2024's captured provisions.

## 2. Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020 — fuller prior extraction record

- Official Gazette PDF: https://escc.gov.eg/assets/docs/law-151-2020.pdf
- Gazette issue 28 bis (e), 15 July 2020.
- Earlier project checkpoint collection_checkpoint_13_escc_data_cyber_civil_service.md records 723 extracted lines and a more complete coverage note.
- Applies to electronic processing, wholly or partly, of natural persons' personal data by a holder, controller or processor.
- Exclusions include specified personal-use data, official/statistical/legal processing, certain journalistic processing, judicial records/investigations/cases, national-security data and certain CBE-supervised entities, subject to the Law's exceptions.
- Commencement is three months after the day following publication.
- Covered entities' one-year adjustment period runs from issuance of the Executive Regulation.
- Executive Regulation Decision 816/2025 remains the relevant later implementing instrument; official Gazette bytes should still be obtained and article-by-article matched. Official MCIT news of 4 November 2026 confirms the Regulation is being applied.

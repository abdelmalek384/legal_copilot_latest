# Collection checkpoint 7 — criminal procedure, evidence, public finance and financial regulation (2026-11-17)

## 1. Criminal Procedure Law 174/2025 — official Ministry of Justice Gazette PDF

- Direct official document endpoint:
  https://moj.gov.eg/backend/api/documents/download/20F3150C-29F1-487E-A0F0-FCCE10CB207B
- Gazette: issue 45 bis (d), 12 November 2025.
- Official search-indexed Arabic text is available, including the promulgation provisions.
- Key transitional/repeal clauses extracted:
  - Article 1: the new law and attached code govern criminal procedure, without prejudice to procedural provisions in other laws.
  - Article 2: appeals in absentia judgments in felony matters issued before commencement continue under the prior procedures.
  - Article 3: felony appeal provisions apply only to cases not decided by felony courts as of the effective date of Law 1/2024 amending Criminal Procedure Law.
  - Article 4: repeals Criminal Procedure Law 150/1950 and Law 140/2014 on extradition of accused persons and transfer of sentenced persons, and repeals inconsistent provisions.
  - Article 5: Minister of Justice issues implementation decisions; until issued, existing decisions continue insofar as they do not conflict with the new law.
- This is a major primary-source acquisition route. Store the full PDF, exact Gazette pages, SHA-256 and a clause-level transitional analysis before relying on it. Do not infer commencement from publication alone without reading the final commencement clause and any subsequent instrument.

## 4. FRA Law 10/2009 — Non-Banking Financial Markets and Instruments Regulatory Authority

- FRA updated consolidated PDF:
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/10_2009-%D9%88%D9%81%D9%82%D8%A7-%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84-1.pdf
- FRA general regulatory framework index:
  https://fra.gov.eg/en/%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A6%D8%A9/
- FRA record labels this as Law 10/2009, updated according to the last amendment (the portal page says updated in 2019).
- Search-indexed opening provisions state that the FRA is a public legal person and supervises non-banking financial markets/instruments, including capital markets, futures exchanges, insurance, real estate finance, financial leasing, factoring and securitization.
- The FRA regulatory index also lists:
  - Law 5/2022 regulating/developing use of financial technology in non-banking financial activities.
  - Law 176/2018 regulating financial leasing and factoring.
  - Presidential Decree 192/2009 issuing the FRA statute.
  - PM Decision 2978/2022 (updated 2025) concerning FRA board formation.
- These are official regulator source routes. Retrieve each text, Gazette details and amendments individually; do not assume the consolidated 2019 copy includes later legislation.

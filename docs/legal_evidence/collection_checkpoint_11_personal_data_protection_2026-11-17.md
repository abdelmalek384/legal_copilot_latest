# Collection checkpoint 11 — Personal Data Protection Law and Executive Regulations (2026-11-17)

## 1. Law 151/2020 — official Gazette PDF located

- Official PDF hosted by Egypt's Executive Security / government domain:
  https://escc.gov.eg/assets/docs/law-151-2020.pdf
- Search-indexed PDF metadata: Official Gazette issue 28 bis (هـ), 15 July 2020.
- Search-indexed opening provisions:
  - Promulgation Article 1: the law and attached statute apply to personal data processed wholly or partly electronically by any holder, controller or processor, concerning natural persons.
  - Promulgation Article 2 sets extraterritorial application for specified data-related crimes involving Egyptians and non-Egyptian residents in Egypt, and certain conduct abroad involving their data.
  - Promulgation Article 3 lists exceptions including personal data held by natural persons for personal use, official statistical processing or statutory processing, and certain exclusively journalistic processing subject to conditions.
- This is an actual official-hosted PDF locator and searchable Arabic text, not just a secondary transcription.

Do not state a final legal deadline without checking the exact Gazette publication wording and applicable time-computation rule.

## 3. Official 2026 confirmation that the Executive Regulation exists and is being applied

- MCIT official news dated 4 November 2026:
  https://mcit.gov.eg/ar/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/121612
- The Ministry reports that the Personal Data Protection Center participated in a forum discussing institutional readiness to implement Law 151/2020 in accordance with its Executive Regulation, which defines compliance requirements/procedures under the Center's supervision.
- The Ministry page links the Personal Data Protection Center: https://pdpc.gov.eg/
- This confirms the Executive Regulation is no longer merely a pending draft. Earlier MCIT material that described the regulation as "under approval" is outdated relative to the November 2026 statement.

## 4. Cross-source status conflict resolved

- Earlier 2023–2024 government strategy material stated that the Executive Regulation was still being approved at that time.
- Later 2025 legal-source records identify Decision 816/2025 and its Gazette publication.
- MCIT's 4 November 2026 official news explicitly refers to the Executive Regulation as setting compliance requirements.
- Therefore, the correct current research status is: **Executive Regulation 816/2025 issued and effective according to publication metadata; obtain and Gazette-match the official Arabic text.**
- Do not use the old 2023/2024 "pending approval" description as current status.

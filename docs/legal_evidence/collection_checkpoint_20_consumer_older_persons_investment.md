## 1. Consumer Protection Law 181/2018 — 2024 amendment

- Base law Arabic/English record: WIPO Lex Arabic https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/ar/legislation/details/19866 ; English https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/19866
- WIPO record states Law 181/2018 was issued/published 13 September 2018, entered into force 14 December 2018, repealed Law 67/2006, and is implemented by PM Decision 822/2019 (Executive Regulation).
- Ministry of Finance official record for Law 20/2024: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-6643253a8897b900092f6319
- Gazette issue 14 (bis), 5 April 2024.
- Ministry portal labels Law 20/2024 in force and says it amends Article 71 of Law 181/2018.
- The Gazette PDF is discoverable in the Ministry's link but timed out during retrieval. Public Gazette catalogue record also exists at:
  https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/handle/123456789/58298
  Official PDF file named OG14_20240405_3178.pdf (174.8 KB).
- Secondary full Arabic text lead: https://alberonsy.com/قانون-رقم-۲۰-لسنة-۲۰۲٤-المنشور-بالجريدة/
- Article 71 replacement text in the secondary transcription:
  - Violation of Article 8 (withholding strategic goods from circulation) is punishable by imprisonment of at least one year and a fine of at least EGP 150,000 and not more than EGP 3m or the equivalent value of the goods, whichever is greater.
  - Recidivism: imprisonment from two to five years and doubling the minimum and maximum fine limits.
  - Seizure/confiscation of goods; closure of the place for up to six months; publication of judgment in two widely circulated daily newspapers at convict's expense; possible cancellation of licence.
  - Effective the day after publication.
- This summary must be checked against the actual Gazette PDF before use; do not replace Law 181/2018 with the 2024 amendment.

## 2. Older Persons' Rights Law 19/2024

- Official Gazette catalogue record: https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/handle/123456789/57741
- Gazette issue 14 supplement (d), 4 April 2024.
- Official file listed: OG14_20240404_3001.pdf (301.3 KB), accessible in principle through the SIS DSpace record. Direct retrieval timed out/was inaccessible in this session.
- Law 19/2024 promulgates the Older Persons' Rights Law. Secondary summary identifies 48 substantive articles plus 3 promulgation articles, provisions on eligibility for care, establishment of an Older Persons' Care Fund, social care and institutional safeguards.
- A 2024 Council for Human Rights report also references Law 19/2024 as establishing rights for older persons across social, political, health, economic, cultural and recreational domains:
  https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/xmlui/bitstream/handle/123456789/75773/NCHR202407_1030.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1
- Need full Gazette PDF, Executive Regulation and Ministry of Social Solidarity decisions. No complete article text is claimed.

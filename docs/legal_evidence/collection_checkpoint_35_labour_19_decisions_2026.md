# Collection checkpoint 35 — Labour Law 14/2025: 19 ministerial implementation decisions

## 1. Legal architecture — no single consolidated Executive Regulation

- Labour Law 14/2025 promulgation Article 11 requires the competent Minister of Labour to issue implementing decisions within 90 days after the Law commences.
- Until a new decision is issued on a subject, earlier decisions under Labour Law 12/2003 continue insofar as they do not conflict with the new Law.
- Therefore, the practical implementation layer is a set of separate ministerial decisions, not one omnibus Executive Regulation.

## 3. Specific source-backed detail for Decision 162/2026

- Gazette issue 176 (supplement), 13 August 2026.
- Decision 162/2026 has 66 articles and six chapters according to multiple secondary transcriptions.
- It implements Article 137 of Law 14/2025 and applies to establishments with 10 or more workers.
- It governs required content of internal work regulations and the approval process with the Labour Directorate and competent trade union.
- Key themes include hiring, training, contracts, worker files, performance, promotion, transfers, wages, working hours, overtime, leave, anti-harassment/anti-bullying, equality, discipline, termination and grievance.
- Article 19 prohibits clauses that reduce statutory or acquired worker rights.

## 4. New Decision 3165/2026 — National Wages Council

- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=EreMgz%2FNoYY%3D
- Dated 12 October 2026; effective the day after publication.
- Repeals PM Decision 2659/2020.
- Gives the National Wages Council authority to:
  - set the national minimum wage across sectors;
  - review it at least annually or when needed, considering living costs, inflation and economic/social conditions;
  - set the minimum annual periodic allowance at no less than 3% of insured wage, subject to the Labour Law;
  - decide employers' requests for reductions/exemptions from periodic allowance due to exceptional economic circumstances within 30 days.
- The Decision also regulates Council subcommittees, balanced tripartite representation and technical secretariat.
- This is a current 2026 primary-source instrument and should be linked to Labour Law Articles 102–106, not merely added as a generic labour decision.

## 5. Remote work and new work patterns — distinguish two separate regimes

- Law 14/2025 Article 100 anticipates ministerial decisions regulating new work patterns such as remote, flexible and platform work.
- The 28 September 2026 secondary digest reports that a permanent ministerial decision regulating those new work patterns had not yet been issued by that date.
- Separately, PM Decision 982/2026 created a temporary remote-work regime, extended monthly through September 2026 by Decisions 1305, 1572, 2033, 2377 and 2590/2026.
- The last located extension ends September 2026; no October/November extension has been identified in the current search. Do not present that temporary order as currently active without a later Gazette extension.
- An employer's internal remote-work arrangements may still need to be addressed in the workplace regulation under Decision 162/2026, subject to the Law and current binding rules.

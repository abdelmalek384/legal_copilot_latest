# Administrative-court and Supreme Constitutional Court judgments — Arabic acquisition, 2026-11-23

## 1. Supreme Constitutional Court — Case47/44, constitutional, 8 April 2023
**Official full Arabic judgment:** https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/13349_44_47_1_2.html

The case arose from referral by the Supreme Administrative Court (Second Circuit) in appeal74219/66 concerning the constitutionality of Article40 bis-1 of Law117/1958, added by Law12/1989. The challenged provision vested certain claims by Administrative Prosecution members exclusively in a Supreme Administrative Court circuit, leaving them only one level of litigation.

**Operative Arabic holding (verbatim):**
«فلهذه الأسباب حكمت المحكمة بعدم دستورية نص المادة (40 مكررًا-1) من قرار رئيس الجمهورية بالقانون رقم 117 لسنة 1958 بإعادة تنظيم النيابة الإدارية والمحاكمات التأديبية، المضافة بالقانون رقم 12 لسنة 1989، فيما تضمنه من قصر الاختصاص بالفصل في طلبات إلغاء القرارات الإدارية النهائية المتعلقة بأي شأن من شئون أعضاء النيابة الإدارية وطلبات التعويض عنها، والمنازعات الخاصة بالمرتبات والمعاشات والمكافآت المستحقة لهم أو لورثتهم، على درجة واحدة.»

**Important scope:** The holding invalidates the one-instance restriction for the listed classes of claims. It does not invalidate all of Article40 bis-1 or the whole Law117/1958. The judgment relies on Articles4,9,53,92,94,97,98,185,186 and197 of the Constitution. The SCC official page is full HTML. Check Official Gazette publication and later legislative response before treating downstream procedural rules as settled.

## 2. Supreme Administrative Court — Unification of Principles, Appeal10639/54, 5 May 2018
**Secondary full Arabic judgment:** https://www.elmodawanaeg.com/%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A6-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B7%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-10639-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-54-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B6%D8%A7%D8%A6/

**Extracted principle:** A positive administrative course in handling a grievance can extend the period for bringing an annulment action, but only where the administration's conduct is clear, serious and indicates an intention to grant the grievance, wholly or partly. Mere investigation, gathering documents or routine review of a grievance is not enough. The court links the exception to Article24 of Law47/1972.

**Arabic formulation from the published summary:**
«أن يكون مسلك الجهة الإدارية واضحا في سبيل إجابة المتظلم إلى تظلمه... وأن تنبئ ظروف الحال عن اتجاه نية الإدارة لاتخاذ بعض الإجراءات نحو الاستجابة لطلبات المتظلم... وأن يتسم المسلك الإيجابي لجهة الإدارة بالجدية والوضوح.»

The full secondary reproduction contains the judgment text and procedural history. Official court copy not yet located; source-specific reuse permission for the judgment reproduction not confirmed.

## 3. Supreme Administrative Court — Appeal2417/45, 13 December 2003
**Secondary full Arabic judgment:** https://lawhub.info/eg-j/?p=49879

The judgment concerns a challenge to a land-allocation decision for a secondary agricultural school, with the action initially dismissed as late. The text addresses Article24 of Law47/1972, the 60-day annulment period, the effect of grievance, and the date of knowledge of an individual administrative decision. This is a secondary full-text reproduction; official judgment scan and source-specific reuse rights remain unverified.

## Acquisition and legal-use controls
- SCC Case47/44/2023 is an official court-hosted full judgment, not a secondary summary.

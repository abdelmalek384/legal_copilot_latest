## 1. Law 213/2017 — scope and basic rights
- Promulgates Trade Union Organisations and Protection of the Right to Organise Law; repeals Trade Union Law 35/1976 and conflicting provisions.
- Protects workers' right to form, join or withdraw from trade union organisations without discrimination, subject to statutory rules.
- Prohibits forming unions on religious, ideological, partisan, racial or political basis.
- Establishes three levels: workplace/professional union committee, general trade union and trade union federation.
- Provides legal personality upon deposit of founding documents with competent administrative authority; bylaws must be published in Al-Waqa'i.
- Defines union purposes including protecting legitimate members' rights, collective interests, improving working conditions, social/cultural/sport/health activities, education and vocational development.
- Union workplace committees participate in resolving individual/collective disputes and negotiating/concluding collective agreements at establishment level.
- Regulates general assemblies, boards, elections, membership, financial management, union funds, exemptions/benefits, inspection and penalties.

## 2. Law 142/2019 amendment
- Gazette issue 31 bis (b), 5 August 2019.
- Amends Articles 11, 12, 67, 68 and 76 and repeals Articles 69, 70, 72 and 75.
- Updated text in the legal-library source states:
  - General trade union can be formed by at least 10 union committees with at least 15,000 workers (previously 15 committees and 20,000 workers).
  - Trade union federation can be formed by at least 7 general trade unions with at least 150,000 workers (previously 10 unions and 200,000 workers).
- Other amended articles concern elections, legal status/representation and penalties; transcribe exact amended text from Gazette scan before ingestion.

## 3. Executive Regulation Decision 35/2018
- Issued by Minister of Manpower, Decision 35/2018, dated 13 March 2018; published 14 March 2018; effective 15 March 2018.
- Contains detailed procedures and definitions for:
  - Union education/training courses and union work missions.
  - Non-regular/seasonal/domestic workers.
  - Founding, deposit and registration of union organisations.
  - Membership and membership records.
  - General assembly, board and elections.
  - Union financial records, resources, expenditure and audits.
  - Union federation structures, collective bargaining and dispute handling.
- Full regulation PDF is searchable; full article-by-article extraction and comparison with 2019 amendment remains outstanding.

## 4. Related ministerial decisions
- Ministerial Decision 36/2018: model bylaws for union organisations; unions may adopt the models until they issue their own bylaws.
- Ministry of Labour official index lists Decision 37/2018 on election dates/procedures, Decision 35/2018 executive regulation and Decision 36/2018 model bylaws.

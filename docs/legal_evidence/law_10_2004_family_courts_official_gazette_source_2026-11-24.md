# Law 10/2004 — Family Courts Law: official Gazette source located

## Instrument identity

- **Instrument:** Law 10/2004, بإصدار قانون إنشاء محاكم الأسرة
- **Issuing date:** 17 March 2004 (26 Muharram 1425 AH)
- **Publication:** Official Gazette, issue 12 supplement (A), 18 March 2004
- **Commencement:** 1 October 2004, expressly stated in Article 5 of the issuing provisions
- **Instrument content:** Five issuing provisions followed by the 15-article Family Courts Law
- **Domain:** Personal status / family-court jurisdiction and procedure
- **Source URL:** https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/1100_a5e6ce8477.pdf
- **Source host:** Egyptian Ministry of Finance official domain; PDF title/masthead identifies the Official Gazette issue
- **Source inspection:** PDF text extraction and page images inspected. The extracted PDF labels the Gazette pages 3–9; the last inspected page contains Articles 13–15. The PDF is an official-government-hosted Gazette reproduction, but the original Amiri Press binary/hash and source rights terms were not independently confirmed.

The PDF exposes the complete issuing provisions (Arts 1–5) and the attached Family Courts Law (Arts 1–15). The following page locators are based on the page labels visible in the PDF/text extraction:

The PDF includes, among other provisions:
- Article 1: establishment of a Family Court within each summary court's territorial jurisdiction and specialised appellate circuits.
- Article 2: three-judge composition and appointment of social/psychological experts in specified proceedings.
- Article 3: exclusive Family Court jurisdiction over personal-status matters allocated to summary and primary courts under Law 1/2000, with related exceptions and references.
- Articles 5–9: Family Dispute Settlement Offices, referral/settlement process, and admissibility requirements.
- Articles 10–12: separate hearing facilities, best interests of the child, expert attendance, and family-file/local-jurisdiction rules.
- Articles 13–15: procedural rules, appealability limits, and enforcement administration.

These bullets are orientation aids, not substitutes for the Arabic statutory wording. The source PDF is the controlling text for this record.

## Later amendment located — Law 215/2017

The Ministry of Finance's Law 10/2004 portal page currently labels the instrument **سارٍ** and links the same Official Gazette PDF. The page also expressly cautions that its electronic texts are for guidance and that the sole legal reference is the Official Gazette. This status label is therefore portal metadata, not a consolidated-currentness certification.

A distinct government-hosted PDF reproduction of **Law 215/2017** has now been located on the State Information Service (SIS) human-rights studies portal. Its text layer identifies Official Gazette issue 51 bis (A), 25 December 2017, and contains two articles. Article 1 adds a final paragraph to Article 11 of Law 10/2004: where the case concerns proof of lineage, the social specialist's report must state whether the child is subject to the Ministry of Social Solidarity's alternative-families system. Article 2 provides for publication and commencement on the day after publication (26 December 2017). The PDF text layer was inspected, but the page-image request failed; this amendment is not yet source-matched or locally captured.

## 4. Financial Leasing and Factoring Law 176/2018 — full Arabic text indexed

- Official FRA PDF:
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%B7%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%85%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%84%D9%8A-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%85.pdf
- FRA record: https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قانون-رقم-176-لسنة-2018/
- Law promulgated 14 August 2018; the indexed promulgation text states commencement on the day after Gazette publication.
- Promulgation clauses:
  - Article 1 applies the attached law to financial leasing and factoring, repeals Law 95/1995 on financial leasing and PM Decision 1446/2003 on factoring, and repeals inconsistent provisions.
  - Article 2 gives Economic Courts jurisdiction over disputes and cases arising from the law (except State Council matters), including execution disputes and criminal cases, and applies specified general laws where the special law is silent.
  - Article 3 requires existing companies to regularize their status within six months, subject to possible extensions by FRA Board decision; noncompliance can lead to licence cancellation.
  - Article 4 provides commencement the day after publication.
- Article 1 definitions include financial leasing as a financing activity granting temporary possession/use of an asset for a defined term against lease payments, with a purchase option; factoring is purchase of current/future financial rights arising from sales and services.
- This is an actual official-regulator PDF text locator. Need file bytes/checksum, Gazette-page comparison, implementing FRA decisions and currentness review.

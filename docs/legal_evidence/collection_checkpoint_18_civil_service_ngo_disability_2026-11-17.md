## 2. Civil Status Law 143/1994

- Base Law 143/1994: Official Gazette issue 23 (supplement), 9 June 1994; effective 10 June 1994 according to secondary legal-source metadata.
- Secondary full-text record:
  https://www.mksegypt.org/ar/laws/23813
- Search-indexed provisions:
  - Article 1 assigns implementation to the Civil Status Authority at the Ministry of Interior and empowers the Minister of Interior to issue decisions establishing information centres, automated identity-card/document issuance stations and civil registry offices.
  - Article 2 establishes a national citizen database with a unique national number from birth throughout life, not reused after death, and requires public bodies to deal with citizens using that number.
- Secondary amendment-aware records note Law 15/2018 changed the organizational designation from "Civil Status Authority" to "Civil Status Sector":
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11399
- Retrieve official Gazette PDF, full text, Executive Regulation, Ministry of Interior decisions, amendments and data-protection interfaces. This law is a key source for civil records, births, marriages, divorces, deaths, national IDs and corrections.

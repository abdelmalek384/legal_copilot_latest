## 4. Supreme Constitutional Court — Law 142/1964 Article 21, 23 and 24

### Case 10/35 (constitutional), judgment 2 June 2018

- Court: Supreme Constitutional Court.
- Case: الدعوى رقم 10 لسنة 35 قضائية "دستورية".
- Judgment date: 2 June 2018.
- Operative result, confirmed in a later official SCC judgment:
  1. Articles 21 and 24 of Law 142/1964 were held unconstitutional.
  2. Article 23 of Law 142/1964 fell consequentially.
  3. Justice Minister Decision 553/1976, regulating proceedings before the Article 21 cadastral-section committee, also fell.
- The judgment invalidated the statutory framework assigning adjudicative jurisdiction to committees in each cadastral section, composed of a court president and two ministry/department employees, and the statutory appellate structure for those committees.
- Direct later SCC-hosted judgment explaining and reproducing the operative ruling:
  https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/12158_41_14_3_2.html
- At lines 65–67 of that official judgment, the Court states the precise invalidity and explains the retroactive consequence for committee proceedings that had not reached a final settled position before the constitutional ruling.
- Secondary Gazette publication metadata for the original judgment: Official Gazette issue 22 bis (ط), 6 June 2018, page 16. The original Gazette scan and a direct SCC judgment URL for Case 10/35 itself were not obtained in this pass.
- Do not describe the later SCC case 14/41 (تنازع), decided 16 January 2022, as the 2018 constitutional case itself; it is a later official judgment explaining the effect of Case 10/35.

### Article 37 — Case 42/17 (constitutional), judgment 6 June 1998

- Case: الدعوى رقم 42 لسنة 17 قضائية "دستورية".
- Judgment date: 6 June 1998.
- Subject: Article 37(2) of Law 142/1964, prohibiting acquisitive prescription contrary to the land-register entry, and Article 38.
- Full searchable judgment transcription from secondary legal repository:
  https://mksegypt.org/ar/judgments-of-the-supreme-constitutional-court/15553
- Gazette publication metadata: Official Gazette issue 35 supplement, 18 June 1998, as cross-referenced by later Cassation material and secondary judgment repositories.
- Operative holding (Arabic):
  > حكمت المحكمة بعدم دستورية ما نصت عليه المادة (37) من قانون السجل العينى الصادر بالقانون رقم 142 لسنة 1964 بنظام السجل العينى من حظر التملك بالتقادم على خلاف ما هو ثابت بالسجل، وبسقوط نص المادة (38) من هذا القانون.
- Legal effect: the statutory prohibition on acquisitive prescription contrary to the register entry was invalidated; Article 38 fell with it. This is not a general repeal of the register's evidentiary function in Article 37(1). Apply Civil Code acquisitive-prescription requirements and the exact scope of the judgment.
- Official SCC judgment URL and original Gazette PDF not found in this pass. A later SCC judgment references this case and the 18 June 1998 publication:
  https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/14996_32_39_1_2.html
- Another later Supreme Court/Cassation source confirms the consequence for acquisitive prescription: https://sadanykhalifa.com/ar/section-data/أحكام%20الدوائر%20المدنية%20بمحكمة%20النقض/28

### Case 39/32 (constitutional), judgment 3 August 2024 — Article 30

- Official SCC-hosted judgment:
  https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/14996_32_39_1_2.html
- Case: الدعوى رقم 39 لسنة 32 قضائية "دستورية".
- Judgment date: 3 August 2024.
- Challenge: Article 30 of Law 142/1964, as substituted by Law 83/2006, requiring registration of inheritance rights in real rights in land-register areas and restricting registration of dispositions by heirs before registration of the inheritance.
- Holding: الدعوى was rejected as inadmissible for lack of the claimant's direct personal interest because he was a transferee, not an heir. The Court did not invalidate Article 30. It explained that the duty to register inheritance and pay any applicable late registration fee rests on the heir, and identified routes available to a transferee.
- The Court reproduces the post-Law 83/2006 text of Article 30 in the judgment. The judgment should be stored as an interpretation/holding, not an amendment or repeal.
- Gazette publication date/issue for this judgment was not established in the retrieved official page.

## 5. Supreme Constitutional Court — rent statutes

### Case 24/20 (constitutional), judgment 9 November 2024 — Law 136/1981 Articles 1 and 2

- Direct official SCC-hosted judgment:
  https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/SCC/faces/Rules_Html/15196_20_24_1_2.html
- Case: الدعوى رقم 24 لسنة 20 قضائية "دستورية".
- Judgment date: 9 November 2024.
- Held unconstitutional: the first paragraph of each of Articles 1 and 2 of Law 136/1981, but only insofar as those paragraphs fixed the annual rent of residential premises licensed after the law came into force by reference to the historical valuation formula and thereby froze the annual rent.
- The Court expressly deferred the effect date to the day following the end of the current ordinary legislative session of the House of Representatives.
- The operative Arabic text is reproduced at the end of the official SCC page. The judgment does not invalidate all of Articles 1 and 2 or every provision of Law 136/1981.
- Secondary source identifies publication in the Official Gazette on 10 November 2024. The direct SCC judgment page itself does not show the Gazette issue number in the extracted content; original Gazette scan and exact issue/page remain outstanding.
- This is a separate judgment from Law 164/2025 and must remain a distinct judicial authority record. Preserve the deferred effect date and subsequent legislative response as a linked temporal relationship, not as a retroactive repeal.

## 6. Important corrections to prior source interpretation

1. Law 142/1964 Article 21/23/24: the 2018 invalidation and consequential fall of Decision 553/1976 are confirmed by an official SCC-hosted judgment in Case 14/41 (تنازع), 16 January 2022. The legal consequence is not merely a prospective amendment.
2. Law 142/1964 Article 37: Case 42/17 invalidated the prohibition on acquisitive prescription contrary to the register and Article 38 fell. Article 37(1)'s evidentiary force was not itself invalidated by that operative wording.
3. Law 142/1964 Article 30: the 2024 SCC judgment rejected a challenge for lack of direct personal interest; it did not invalidate Article 30.
4. Law 136/1981 Articles 1 and 2: the 2024 judgment invalidates the historical rent-freeze formula within the specified scope, but expressly defers its effect. Do not apply it as if every provision became void on 9 November 2024.
5. Law 136/1981 Article 18(b): the 1999 judgment invalidated only the phrase requiring execution in the tenant's presence.
6. Law 49/1977 Article 29(1): the 2002 challenge was rejected; distinguish it from Article 29(3) and later amendment/judgment history.

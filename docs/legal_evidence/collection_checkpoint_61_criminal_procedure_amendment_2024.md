# Collection checkpoint 61 — Criminal Procedure Law 1/2024: full 33-article amendment text

## 1. Official Gazette source and version

- Law 1/2024 amends Criminal Procedure Law 150/1950.
- Official Gazette: issue 2 bis, 16 January 2024.
- Nine-page Gazette scan:
  https://alhasswalawfirm.com/uploads/files/law-1-2024-1789187159544-488956923.pdf
- Full 33-article transcription:
  https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-1-2024
- The transcription states it is copied from the Court of Cassation legislation portal; the attached nine-page scan is a Gazette image copy. The Court of Cassation portal's general terms remain a separate restriction on commercial reuse/bulk extraction.
- Law commenced the day after publication.
- This law changed the felony-court structure and created a two-tier felony trial/appeal system within Law 150/1950. It remains relevant to proceedings under the current procedural law before 1 October 2027 and to the transitional clause in Law 174/2025.

## 2. Article-by-article text map

### Article 1 — terminology and court names
- Replaces references across multiple provisions of Law 150/1950:
  - "First Instance Felony Court" with "Felony Court";
  - "Felony Court at both levels" with "Felony Court";
  - "Appeal Felony Court" and "Criminal Circuit of Court of Cassation" with the specified criminal circuit terminology;
  - changes the wording of Article 151(2) on urgent decisions outside court terms.
- The precise amended article list is in the Gazette PDF; preserve the exact list from the original.

### Article 2 — replacement of Book II, Chapter III, Chapters 1 and 2
- Replaces Articles 366–383 and creates:
  - felony-court formation and sitting schedules;
  - first-instance and appellate felony courts;
  - court composition (three judges);
  - allocation of judges and court circuits;
  - notice of hearings (normally ten full days);
  - lawyer attendance and appointment rules;
  - defence counsel fee awards;
  - eligibility of lawyers to plead before first-instance/appellate felony courts;
  - detention/bail and procedural rules;
  - death-penalty unanimity and Mufti consultation;
  - cassation/retrial routes;
  - jurisdictional referral when a felony is found to be a misdemeanour.

### Article 3 — new appellate felony procedure Articles 419 bis to 419 bis 9
- Adds a new chapter for appeals in felony cases.
- Article 419 bis: prosecution and accused may appeal in-person felony judgments from first-instance felony courts.
- Article 419 bis 1: civil claims may be appealed by civil claimant/responsible party/accused where the amount exceeds the final jurisdictional threshold of the primary court.
- Article 419 bis 2: prosecution may appeal absentia felony judgments.
- Article 419 bis 3: misdemeanour appeal rules apply unless the law provides otherwise.
- Article 419 bis 4: appeal is filed with the clerk of the court that issued judgment within 40 days; the Public Prosecution appeal is signed by at least an Advocate General; the Attorney General may appeal within 60 days.
- Article 419 bis 5: case file is sent to the Court of Appeal after expiry of the judgment-reasons filing period; the President sets hearing and orders notification.
- Article 419 bis 6: appellate judges receive file copies before hearing.
- Article 419 bis 7: parties are heard; the accused speaks last.
- Article 419 bis 8: if a final death sentence is not appealed within the period, prosecution follows Article 46 of Law 57/1959 on Cassation.
- Article 419 bis 9: appeal does not automatically stay execution unless appellate felony court orders stay or judgment is death; if accused/counsel fails to attend without excuse, court appoints defence counsel and decides the appeal.

### Article 4 — transitional application
- The Law's felony-appeal provisions apply only to cases not decided by felony courts as of its commencement date.
- This transitional rule is carried into Law 174/2025 promulgation Article 3, which ties application to the date Law 1/2024 commenced.

### Article 5 — publication and commencement
- Publication in the Official Gazette; commencement the day after publication (17 January 2024).

## 3. Key verbatim Arabic text — Article 419 bis 4

> يحصل الاستئناف بتقرير فى قلم كتاب المحكمة التى أصدرت الحكم، وذلك خلال أربعين يوماً من تاريخ صدور الحكم.
>
> فإذا كان الاستئناف مرفوعاً من هيئة قضايا الدولة يجب أن يكون التقرير موقعاً من مستشار بها على الأقل.
>
> وإذا كان مرفوعاً من النيابة العامة فيجب أن يكون التقرير موقعاً من محام عام على الأقل.
>
> وللنائب العام أن يستأنف الحكم خلال ستين يوماً من تاريخ صدوره، وله أن يقرر بالاستئناف فى قلم كتاب المحكمة المختصة بنظر الاستئناف.

## 4. Key verbatim Arabic text — Article 419 bis 9

> لا يترتب على استئناف الحكم الصادر من محكمة جنايات أول درجة وقف تنفيذ الحكم، إلا إذا رأت محكمة الجنايات المستأنفة وقف التنفيذ، أو كان الحكم صادراً بالإعدام.
>
> وإذا تخلف المحكوم عليه أو وكيله بغير عذر عن الحضور فى الجلسة المحددة لنظر استئنافه أو فى أى جلسة تالية تندب له المحكمة محامياً للدفاع عنه وتفصل فى الاستئناف.

## 5. Version control with Law 174/2025 and Law 160/2026

- Law 174/2025 promulgation Article 3 preserves the Law 1/2024 transition: felony appeal rules apply only to cases not decided by felony courts as of Law 1/2024's commencement.
- Law 174/2025 promulgation Article 6 originally set commencement on 1 October following publication (1 October 2026).
- Law 160/2026 amended Article 6 so Law 174/2025 commences on **1 October 2027**.
- Therefore:
  - Law 150/1950 as amended by Law 1/2024 remains the operative general Criminal Procedure Law through 30 September 2027.
  - Law 174/2025 as amended by Law 160/2026 is future-effective from 1 October 2027.
  - Law 1/2024 transitional felony-appeal provisions must be applied by case status at its commencement and the transition in Law 174/2025.
- Build retrieval tests to select the law based on procedural event date and case status, not merely the law's publication date.

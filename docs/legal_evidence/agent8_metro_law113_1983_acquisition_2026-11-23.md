## 1. Base law — Law 113/1983
**Arabic title:** قانون رقم 113 لسنة 1983 بإنشاء الهيئة القومية للأنفاق  
**Official Gazette metadata:** issue 32, 11 August 1983; issuance date 1 August 1983; commencement 12 August 1983 (EastLaws bibliographic page).  
**Sources:**
- Manshurat catalogue/PDF locator: https://manshurat.org/node/13920
- EastLaws metadata: https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/11-08-1983/no-113?id=4291&type=1
- Secondary 21-article transcription: https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-113-1983
- Secondary consolidation: https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12383

The Authority's mandate was expanded over time beyond metro construction to metro and electrically propelled rail transport projects. The secondary text identifies 21 articles and includes amendment annotations for Laws 33/2018, 180/2020 and 83/2025.

**Source anomaly:** the Al-Hasswa page displays an inconsistent Gazette date/year in its heading (issue 32, 11 August 1982), while EastLaws metadata says Law 113/1983, issued 1 August 1983, published 11 August 1983 and effective 12 August 1983. Do not silently normalize the discrepancy. Use the EastLaws date as a metadata lead only until the original Gazette page is visually checked. EastLaws' page also contains an apparent unrelated Law 4/2025 ship-registration amendment footnote attached to Articles 1–2; treat that as a database contamination error, not a real amendment to Law 113/1983.

## 2. Amendment chain

### Law 33/2018
- Amends Articles 2 and 3 of Law 113/1983.
- Gazette issue 20 bis, 19 May 2018; published/issued 19 May 2018; effective 20 May 2018 per Manshurat metadata.
- Manshurat catalogue/PDF: https://manshurat.org/node/25915
- Secondary full text: https://lawyeregypt.net/قانون-رقم-33-لسنة-2018-بتعديل-بعض-أحكام-القانون-رقم-113-لسنة-1983-بإنشاء-الهيئة-القومية-للأنفاق/
- The replacement Article 2 concerns construction, design and implementation of metro lines and electrically propelled rail transport systems; the replacement Article 3 is also part of the amendment but requires full article-level extraction.

### Law 180/2020
- Replaces Article 1 of Law 113/1983, describing the National Authority for Tunnels as an economic public authority headquartered in Cairo and affiliated to the minister responsible for transport.
- Gazette issue 36 bis, 5 September 2020; effective 6 September 2020.
- Gazette-style scan reproduction: https://manshurat.org/file/79274/download?token=YIajN6I_
- Secondary consolidation: https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12383

### Law 83/2025
- Law No. 83/2025 amends Law 113/1983.
- Gazette issue 21 bis (ب), 27 May 2025; effective 28 May 2025 (EastLaws metadata).
- Official Ministry of Finance legislation listing: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-685d78ea3b904a000878ac7c
- EastLaws metadata and issuing text: https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/27-05-2025/no-83?id=2167303&type=1
- Official SIS announcement: https://sis.gov.eg/ar/الرئاسة/شئون-داخلية/القرارات-الرئاسية/الرئيس-السيسي-يوقع-تعديل-بعض-أحكام-قانون-إنشاء-الهيئة-القومية-للأنفاق/
- Article 1 substitutes the phrase “الوزير المختص بشئون النقل” with “وزير النقل” wherever it appears in Law 113/1983.
- Article 2 inserts two new provisions numbered Article 4 bis and Article 4 bis 1.
- Article 3 provides publication and commencement on the day after Gazette publication.
- The SIS announcement describes safeguards classifying metro lines, electrically propelled rail systems, rights-of-way and operating assets as public funds/public utilities, with restrictions on disposal/attachment/acquisitive prescription. The exact full inserted article texts still need Gazette transcription.

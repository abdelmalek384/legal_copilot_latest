## Verbatim text already preserved from earlier checkpoint
- Promulgation Article 1 repeals Book 6 of the Civil Code (Articles 400–417), the first book of the Commercial Code and the Evidence Law 77/1949, subject to the specific commercial-evidence exception.
- Promulgation Article 2 provides commencement six months after publication.
- Substantive Articles 1–3 (prior visual transcription):
  - creditor proves the obligation; debtor proves discharge;
  - facts to be proved must be relevant and admissible;
  - where the court determines evidence procedures, it sets a period not exceeding three months, subject to extension where justified.

## Important consolidation relationships
- Civil Code 131/1948: Law 25/1968 repealed former Civil Code Book 6 evidence provisions (Articles 400–417).
- Commercial Code: Law 25/1968 repealed the first book of the former Commercial Code, subject to the stated exception for commercial evidence.
- Civil and Commercial Procedure Law 13/1968: procedural interaction must be mapped separately; the Evidence Law governs proof rules while the Procedure Law governs procedural mechanisms.
- Electronic evidence and digital proof must be cross-linked to Cybercrime Law 175/2018, its Executive Regulation PM Decision 635/2020, E-Signature Law 15/2004 and Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020, as applicable.
- Do not use an old unamended Evidence Law scan as current without checking subsequent amendments, especially any 2024/2025 amendments and special evidence provisions.

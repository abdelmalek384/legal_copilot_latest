## 2. Law 157/2025 — amendment to VAT Law 67/2016

- Official ETA Gazette PDF:
  https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2025-07/vat-law_no_.157.of_.2025.pdf
- Gazette issue 29 (supplement), 17 July 2025.
- Law 157/2025 replaces line item 1/B/3 in the schedule of goods/services attached to VAT Law 67/2016, changing excise/VAT treatment for cigarettes.
- The extracted schedule applies an ad valorem rate of 50% of final consumer sale price plus fixed amounts by cigarette price band (the searchable text exposes 500 piastres and 750 piastres for the first two bands, with further bands in the original table).
- This is a schedule amendment; do not rewrite the entire VAT Law or treat the fixed amounts as the standard VAT rate.

## 3. Law 149/2026 — major VAT amendment

- Official ETA PDF:
  https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.149.of_.2026.pdf
- Gazette issue 30 bis (A), 28 July 2026.
- Law 149/2026 amends VAT Law 67/2016:
  - Article 3: standard rate stated as 13%, rising to 14% from FY 2017/2018; medical machinery/equipment used to produce goods/provide services addressed at 5%.
  - Article 5: goods/services in transit may be treated under the specified customs-control condition.
  - Adds Article 28 bis: suspends VAT on specified medical machinery/equipment imported by local factories/production units for industrial use, subject to conditions and time limits.
  - Amends Article 30 and the exempt-goods/services schedule.
- It is an amending law, not a consolidated VAT Code. Reconcile with Law 157/2025, Executive Regulation 66/2017 and all later amendments.

## 5. Digital services supplied by non-residents

- Minister of Finance Decision 160/2022:
  https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2023-03/law-160-2023-wezary.pdf
- Issues the first guidance document on VAT for digital services and other remotely supplied services by non-resident persons.
- The decision cites VAT Law 67/2016 and Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020.
- The guidance covers registration/collection for remote digital services, but is not a statutory amendment. Store the guidance and decision separately from the Law and Executive Regulation.
- Link to cross-border services, place of supply, non-resident suppliers, digital platforms, invoicing, registration and tax collection.

## 6. Consumer finance interest — ETA Instruction 1/2025

- ETA announcement and official instruction:
  https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2025-12/instructions.no_.1.of_.2025.pdf
- ETA states that where a licensed consumer-finance company separately identifies consumer-finance interest from the taxable value of goods/services, the interest component is VAT-exempt.
- This is an administrative instruction/interpretation under VAT Law 67/2016 and Executive Regulation, not a statutory amendment.
- It should be linked to Consumer Finance Law 18/2020 and FRA licensing rules; verify the instruction's precise wording and scope before applying.

## 7. ETA operational guidance and registration

- ETA states that VAT-taxable businesses must register the head office and each branch separately, display the registration certificates and obtain a branch certificate for each branch:
  https://portal.eta.gov.eg/en/node/770
- ETA FAQ currently states the compulsory registration threshold is EGP 500,000; goods/services listed in the exemption schedule are exempt only as expressly provided, and table-goods/importers/exporters have special rules:
  https://portal.eta.gov.eg/ar/alasylt-alshayt
- These are official administrative guidance/FAQ, not substitutes for the statutory registration provisions and Executive Regulation. Recheck currentness and applicable sector exceptions.

## 8. VAT refunds and procedure

- ETA guidance says VAT refund requests are checked for completeness within five working days; the statutory refund clock under Article 30 of VAT Law and Article 53 of Unified Tax Procedures Law begins when the required documents are complete.
- If documents are missing, ETA notifies the claimant to complete them within up to ten working days; the legal period is then calculated after completion:
  https://www.eta.gov.eg/ar/news/rd-aldrybt-ly-alqymt-almdaft
- This is administrative guidance explaining the interaction of VAT Law Article 30 and Unified Tax Procedures Law Article 53; preserve the statutory text and current procedures separately.

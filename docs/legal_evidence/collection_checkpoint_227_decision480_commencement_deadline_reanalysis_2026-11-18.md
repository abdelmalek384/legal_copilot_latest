## 2. Original period

- PM Decision 200/2026 was published on 18 January 2026. Its Article 4 says it comes into force on the day following publication: **19 January 2026**.
- Article 3(1) of Law 168/2025 measures the initial six-month filing period from the date the Executive Regulation comes into force.
- The official Cairo Governorate notice dated June 2026 expressly identified **18 July 2026** as the final filing day.
- There is a one-day calculation discrepancy: Civil and Commercial Procedure Law Article 15 excludes the triggering day and measures month/year periods by the solar calendar. Applying that general rule to 19 January may produce 19 July 2026. The official municipal notice says 18 July. Preserve both source facts; do not silently normalize the discrepancy.

## 3. Decision 480/2026 text and commencement

The Gazette page image reproduced by Al-Masry Al-Youm shows:
- Article 1: the period referred to in the first paragraph of Article 3 of Law 168/2025 is extended “for another equivalent period”.
- Article 2: the decision is to be published in the Official Gazette.
- The decision is signed at the Presidency on **10 November 2026** and the DSpace Amiri Press catalogue confirms publication in issue 45 bis (ج) on that date.

The published decision does **not** state:
- a separate commencement date;
- whether the new period runs from the previous expiry or from publication/effectiveness;
- a calendar end date;
- whether applications lodged in the 20 July–10 November 2026 interval are validated.

No post-Decision-480 official circular or administrative notice giving a precise final calendar date was located in this pass. The Cabinet's 10 November meeting page does not itself state a filing deadline.

## 4. Competing calculations

### A. Consecutive-extension construction
If “another equivalent period” is appended to the original official deadline of 18 July 2026, the second six-month period would run **19 July 2026–18 January 2027**, producing a final date of **18 January 2027**. This is the previous working calculation, not an express date in Decision 480.

If the Article 15 calculation is applied to the 19 July trigger date instead, the corresponding six-month endpoint could be 19 January 2027. This is part of the existing one-day discrepancy and is not adopted as a certified date.

### B. New-period-from-publication construction
If Decision 480 is treated as commencing upon publication on 10 November 2026 and the new six-month period begins the following day under the Article 15 counting rule, the period would run **11 November 2026–10 May 2027**, producing a provisional final date of **10 May 2027**.

This is a reasoned operational construction, not an express date. Article 15 is a general procedural time-computation rule; the precise application of that rule to this administrative filing period, and the commencement effect of Article 2, have not been authoritatively confirmed for this decision.

## 5. Current research conclusion

- **No precise final application deadline is expressly established by Decision 480/2026 itself.**
- **10 May 2027** is the leading publication-based working calculation if the new six-month period starts after publication.
- **18 January 2027** remains the alternative consecutive-extension calculation if the period is appended to the original 18 July deadline.
- The commencement date of Decision 480 is not stated in its text; publication is confirmed as 10 November 2026.
- The 20 July–10 November 2026 gap and treatment of applications in that interval remain unresolved.
- Do not describe either 10 May or 18 January as an officially confirmed deadline. Obtain a written Ministry of Local Development / competent authority circular or formal legal interpretation before advising applicants to rely on either date.

# Collection checkpoint 17 — family courts and civil procedure amendment texts

**Date:** 2026-11-17. Official Gazette PDFs hosted by the Ministry of Finance. These are primary texts, not consolidated current-law statements.

## 1. Law 10/2004 — establishing Family Courts

- Official Gazette PDF: https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/1100_a5e6ce8477.pdf
- Gazette: issue 12 supplement (A), 18 March 2004.
- Searchable text inspected (129 extracted lines).
- Promulgation provisions:
  - Article 1 applies the attached Family Courts Law and repeals conflicting provisions.
  - Article 2 transfers pending cases that fall within Family Court jurisdiction to the Family Courts, without fees, in their existing state; exceptions include cases already adjudicated or reserved for judgment. Appeal rules for judgments in transferred cases remain governed by the prior applicable rules.
  - Article 3 leaves appeals already pending before Court of Cassation, Courts of Appeal and Courts of First Instance to those courts.
  - Article 4 authorizes the Minister of Justice to issue implementation decisions within three months.
  - Article 5 commencement: 1 October 2004.
- Attached Law provisions visible in the PDF:
  - Article 1 creates a Family Court within each partial-court jurisdiction and specialized appellate circuits within each Court of Appeal's jurisdiction.
  - Article 2: Family Court is formed of three judges; certain matters require two experts, one social and one psychological, with at least one woman.
  - Article 3: Family Courts have exclusive jurisdiction over personal-status matters allocated to partial and first-instance courts under Law 1/2000.
  - Article 4 creates a specialized Family Prosecution.
  - Article 5 establishes one or more Family Dispute Settlement Offices within each partial-court jurisdiction, under Ministry of Justice.
  - Articles 6–9 require pre-filing referral to the competent settlement office for matters capable of reconciliation, subject to exclusions for non-reconcilable, urgent, execution and provisional matters. Settlement is free and ordinarily must finish within 15 days; settlement records have enforceable-instrument force.
  - Article 10 directs courts to be guided by the best interests of the child.
  - Article 11 requires expert attendance in specified divorce, dissolution, custody, residence, visitation, lineage and obedience disputes.
  - Article 12 centralizes related subsequent family disputes before the court that first received a case between spouses.
  - Article 13 applies this law and Law 1/2000, with Civil/Commercial Procedure, Evidence and Civil Code inheritance-estate rules filling gaps.
  - Article 14 states appellate-circuit judgments/decisions are not subject to cassation, without prejudice to Article 250 of the Civil/Commercial Procedure Law.
  - Article 15 creates a dedicated enforcement administration in each Family Court.
- This is a full searchable official Gazette text source; article-level currentness and later amendments still need review.

## 2. Law 11/2004 — Family Insurance System Fund

- Official Gazette PDF: https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/1101_ace9fd8bab.pdf
- Gazette: issue 12 supplement (A), 18 March 2004.
- Search-indexed opening text:
  - Establishes a non-profit Family Insurance System Fund as a public legal person with its own budget, headquartered in Cairo and affiliated with Nasser Social Bank.
  - Article 2 requires family participation in the insurance system under Article 71 of Law 1/2000 through fees of EGP 50 per marriage, EGP 50 per divorce/reconciliation event, and EGP 20 per birth, payable by the persons specified in the text.
  - Collection/remittance procedures are to be set by Minister of Justice in agreement with the competent minister.
- Full text and amendments remain to be extracted.

## 3. Law 157/2024 — amendment to Civil and Commercial Procedure Law 13/1968

- Official Ministry record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-66fba04be1ceb700085b001b
- Official Gazette PDF: https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/qanwn_rqm_157_lsnt_2024_0aca1ef996.pdf
- Gazette: issue 27 (d), 10 July 2024.
- Full 4-article text inspected (26 extracted lines).
- Article 1 replaces monetary thresholds wherever they appear in Articles 41, 42 paragraph 1, 43, 47 paragraph 1, 248 and 480:
  - EGP 200,000 becomes EGP 100,000.
  - EGP 30,000 becomes EGP 15,000.
  - EGP 500,000 becomes EGP 250,000.
- Article 2: cases filed before commencement continue before the courts where pending until a final judgment, under procedures and appeal routes in force at filing.
- Article 3 repeals inconsistent provisions.
- Article 4: effective from 1 October following publication (1 October 2024).
- This amendment must be linked to Law 13/1968 and the exact affected articles. Do not treat it as the complete Code of Civil and Commercial Procedure.

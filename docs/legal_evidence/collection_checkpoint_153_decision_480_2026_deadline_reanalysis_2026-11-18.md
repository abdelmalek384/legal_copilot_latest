## 2. Recalculation of the original six-month period

Law 168/2025 Article 3 gives six months from the date the Executive Regulation comes into force. Decision 200/2026 commenced on 19 January 2026.

Civil Procedure Law 13/1968 Article 15 provides that the day of the event triggering a period is not counted and the period expires at the end of its final day. A literal application to a 19 January commencement gives a provisional end date of **19 July 2026**.

However, the official Cairo Governorate notice states **18 July 2026**. The discrepancy must be preserved, not silently harmonized.

A possible, but not yet confirmed, explanation is that:
- the administrative notice counted 19 January as the first day, producing 18 July;
- 18 July 2026 was a Saturday, and Article 18's next-working-day rule could potentially move a deadline falling on a non-working day to Sunday 19 July;
- alternatively, Article 15 may apply as a general computation rule to this administrative filing period, making 19 July the correct end date from the outset.

Whether Article 18's holiday-extension rule applies to this particular substantive administrative filing period, and whether the online platform remained open on Saturday 18 July, require authoritative confirmation. The Cairo Governorate page does not mention a weekend adjustment. No final initial deadline is certified.

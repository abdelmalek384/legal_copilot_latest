## Attached standard contract — 14 clauses

1. **التمهيد وحكمه:** party identification; broker licence/approval; borrower identity and KYC; preamble incorporated into contract; Capital Market Law 95/1992, Executive Regulation and FRA rules prevail where inconsistent; future legislative amendments automatically apply and broker must notify borrower.
2. **نطاق العقد:** borrower may issue orders to borrow securities for short selling from central lending inventory through the broker; borrowed securities must be returned by the end of the lending period.
3. **حق الإدارة:** borrower authorizes broker to manage/invest the cash collateral margin if agreed, and acknowledges FRA/Exchange access to relevant securities accounts; collateral cannot be reclaimed during the loan except in contract-defined cases.
4. **التزامات الشركة:** due-care standard; retain contract and KYC documents for at least five years after termination or until final judgment in a dispute; deliver and annually update a detailed short-selling guide; segregate cash margin in a dedicated account or earmark non-cash collateral; no short-sale execution before collateral is posted/blocked; daily mark-to-market; notify borrower of margin deficiency and allow two business days to restore; suspend new borrowing until restored; force-return/close positions if required; return excess collateral within two days after request; disclose fees/commissions and calculation basis; buy securities to return them if borrower fails to do so; close positions in defined events; permit transfer of open positions and collateral to another approved broker within five business days after licence suspension/cancellation; provide periodic account statements and allow objections within 15 days.
5. **حقوق الشركة:** accept eligible securities/collateral under FRA/Exchange criteria; receive agreed share of investment return on cash collateral; receive agreed fees/commissions/expenses and amend future charges only under the contract's express-consent and termination safeguards.
6. **التزامات العميل المقترض:** open account; sign KYC and trading documents; provide accurate/current information; deposit and replenish collateral; return borrowed securities by maturity through the central system; pay fees and charges.
7. **حقوق العميل المقترض:** place buy/sell orders; close open position partially or fully before maturity by returning equivalent securities or instructing broker to repurchase; receive agreed return on cash collateral; receive any agreed investment return on sale proceeds where market value rises after short sale.
8. **أحكام عامة:** borrower declares beneficial ownership and lawful source of funds and completes AML/KYC; acknowledges understanding the product and its risks; lending term may be extended for securities under trading suspension where return purchase is not possible on scheduled day.
9. **مدة العقد:** one year from signature, automatically renewable for similar periods unless a party gives 30 days' notice; termination only after settlement of the account.
10. **سرية البيانات:** broker must protect customer data and transaction information; disclosure is allowed with prior written consent or where required by FRA, court order or applicable law.
11. **المراسلات:** contract addresses are elected domiciles; changes must be notified within five days, otherwise notices sent to the old address are effective.
12. **طرق الاتصال والمخاطبات:** post, telephone, email or another method agreed by the parties subject to FRA approval.
13. **تسوية المنازعات:** Economic Courts have jurisdiction; where parties agree to arbitration, disputes are resolved under Egyptian law and the rules of the Egyptian Center for Voluntary Arbitration and Settlement of Non-Banking Financial Disputes, seated in Cairo and conducted in Arabic.
14. **نسخ العقد:** two signed copies, one for each party.

## Legal effect and implementation
- Decision 3350/2026 makes the attached standard model contract the operative template for the agreement between an FRA-approved brokerage firm and clients using securities borrowing for short selling.
- It operationalizes Decision 155/2026's requirement to conclude a borrower contract and provide a detailed explanation of the product's mechanism, benefits, risks and procedures.
- Contract covers collateral margin, margin calls, close-out, return of borrowed securities, confidentiality, notices, dispute resolution and record retention.
- It requires retention of contract and identity-verification documents for at least five years after contract end, or until a final judgment if a dispute is pending.
- The model provides a one-year automatically renewable term, with 30 days' non-renewal notice.
- Effective 29 October 2026.

## Legal classification and relationships
- Decision 3350/2026 is a Chairperson decision approving a standard contract template, not a statute or Board decision.

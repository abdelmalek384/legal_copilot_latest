## 4. Decision 252/2026 — newly expanded training-rule leads and Arabic definitions

**Title:** قرار وزير العمل رقم 252 لسنة 2026 بشأن قواعد منح تراخيص ونظم التدريب في مجال السلامة والصحة المهنية وتأمين بيئة العمل.

- Gazette issue 225 supplement (A), 12 October 2026.
- Secondary article with Article 1 and definitions: https://www.almasdar.com/print.aspx?id=175156
- Further search-indexed account of operational requirements: https://eg.linkedin.com/in/ehab-abdelatty-alian

### Short Arabic text captured from Article 1 definitions

- «البرامج التدريبية: البرامج الأساسية، والمتقدمة، والنوعية، والتخصصية»
- «جهة التدريب: كل شخص اعتباري يرخص له بمزاولة نشاط التدريب»

The secondary article defines the training programmes as basic, advanced, specific and specialist; identifies the competent Ministry/authority, the central OSH administration, the licensing committee and the programmes/curricula committee; and defines a training provider and an accredited trainer.

### Additional requirements surfaced by a Ministry-linked professional social account — unverified secondary lead

The LinkedIn post attributes the following to Decision 252/2026:
- training activity, including remote/electronic training, requires prior Ministry licensing;
- existing providers receive a six-month regularisation period;
- possible licensees include colleges/institutes, associations and civil-society organisations, and specialist companies;
- in-house employer training may be exempt where it is limited to the employer's own workers, programmes are approved, and no fee is charged;
- training premises: independent location, at least 90 m² net total area, with a training room of at least 30 m² and at least 1.5 m² per trainee;
- trainer: relevant higher qualification, at least eight years' OSH experience, approved trainer-of-trainers course, and a clean criminal record;
- trainer licence fee reportedly EGP 5,000 and three-year term;
- basic training reportedly 80 hours for specialist/supervisor/technician streams, 40 hours for committee members, and 16 hours for employers.

**Reliability:** LinkedIn professional post, not the Gazette text. None of these detailed dimensions, fees, experience thresholds, exemptions, hours or transition periods is confirmed against the signed instrument. Treat as a targeted checklist for acquisition, not a legal rule or a verified extract.

## 3. Constitutional judgments — separate legal-effect layer

### SCC Case 42/17

- Judgment date: 18 June 1998 (case text).
- Gazette publication: issue **25 supplement**, 18 June 1998, corroborated by Egyptian State Council General Assembly Opinion 512 (file 58/1/595, session 10 March 2021):
  https://www.elmodawanaeg.com/الفتوى-رقم-512-،-ملف-رقم-58-1-595-،-بتاريخ-جلسة-2021-3-10/
- Exact operative effect from the judgment text: invalidated **Article 37(2)**, the restriction concerning acquisitive prescription contrary to the register; Article 37(1) remains. Article 38 fell consequentially.
- Original SCC judgment and original Gazette scan remain outstanding. The consolidated Law 142 footnote at the Alhasswa page overgeneralizes this as invalidation of “Article 37”; preserve the more precise Article 37(2) scope from the judgment text.

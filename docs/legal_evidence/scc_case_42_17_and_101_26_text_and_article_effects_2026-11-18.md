## Case 42/17 — Law 142/1964, Real-Estate Register Law

- **Judgment date:** 6 June 1998.
- **Gazette publication:** Official Gazette issue 35 تابع, 18 June 1998 (publication metadata reported in later case-law sources; original Gazette page still not acquired).
- **Full searchable text:** https://mksegypt.org/ar/judgments-of-the-supreme-constitutional-court/15553
- **Later official SCC reference:** https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/12158_41_14_3_2.html
- The MKSEgypt page footer identifies a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. This is a secondary full-text transcription, not the original SCC or Gazette binary.

### Operative order — Arabic
حكمت المحكمة بعدم دستورية ما نصت عليه المادة (37) من قانون السجل العينى الصادر بالقرار بقانون رقم 142 لسنة 1964 بنظام السجل العينى من حظر التملك بالتقادم على خلاف ما هو ثابت بالسجل، وبسقوط نص المادة (38) من هذا القانون، وألزمت الحكومة المصروفات ومبلغ مائة جنيه مقابل أتعاب المحاماة.

### Article effect
- Invalidated only the prohibition on acquisition by prescription contrary to the register, located in Article 37(2).
- Article 38 fell consequentially because it was an exception to the invalidated prohibition.
- Article 37(1), which gives the register evidentiary force, was not declared unconstitutional.
- Do not mark the whole of Article 37 as invalid.

## Case 101/26 — Law 143/1981, Desert Lands Law

- **Judgment date:** 1 February 2009.
- **Gazette publication:** Official Gazette issue 7 bis (أ), 15 February 2009, as recorded by the official SCC page.
- **Official full judgment:** https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/3204_26_101_1_2.html

### Operative order — Arabic
حكمت المحكمة بعدم دستورية نص الفقرة الأولى من المادة (22) من القانون رقم 143 لسنة 1981 فى شأن الأراضى الصحراوية.

### Article effect
- Invalidated Article 22(1), which had assigned ordinary courts exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising from application of Law 143/1981.
- The judgment's reasoning treats the second and third paragraphs as inseparably connected to the first paragraph and states that they fall to that extent.
- Do not describe the whole of Article 22 as invalid without preserving the judgment's limited jurisdictional scope.

## Amendment effect / integration notes

- Decision 100/2026 substitutes only Article 6 item 1 and Article 7 item 2 of Decision 44/2026 according to the retrieved text.
- Regional supervision: one regional area per five financing branches; each regional area must have a risk officer and a credit officer; each officer may supervise up to four regional areas.
- Branch eligibility: judicial judgments, criminal-prosecution requests and administrative measures are addressed by requiring execution/settlement/removal and a three-month period after removal; where the underlying reason is not removable, FRA assesses a period between three months and three years based on seriousness.
- Apply this as an amendment overlay to Decision 44/2026, not as a free-standing replacement of its other 15 articles.
- FRA's June 25, 2026 announcement paraphrases the amended rules; it is supporting official guidance, not a substitute for Gazette wording.

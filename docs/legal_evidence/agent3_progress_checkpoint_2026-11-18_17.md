## Two new official Gazette acquisitions — adjacent consumer-finance regulatory instruments

### FRA Decision 2863/2026 — digital verification and behavioural scoring
- Official two-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alamiria_2026_2863_-R.pdf
- Gazette issue 210 supplement (أ), 23 September 2026, printed pages 3–4; decision dated 9 September 2026.
- Captured exact Arabic preamble and Articles 1–3.
- Requires consumer-finance companies, before granting or renewing financing, to digitally verify customer data and obtain alternative-data behavioural scoring through the credit information company, and to consider the results in credit decisions.
- General effective date 24 September 2026; behavioural-scoring component deferred to 1 April 2027 subject to system readiness.
- Full record: [Decision 2863/2026](agent3_fra_decision_2863_2026_consumer_finance_digital_verification_behavioral_scoring_arabic_text.md).

### FRA Decision 2864/2026 — consumer-finance database integration with FRA
- Official two-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/alamiria_2026_2864_-R.pdf
- Gazette issue 210 supplement (أ), 23 September 2026, printed pages 5–6; decision dated 9 September 2026.
- Captured exact Arabic preamble and Articles 1–3.
- Requires licensed consumer-finance companies to integrate their databases with FRA and provide customer data immediately upon financing approval, real-time purchase/service transaction data, financed-goods classifications, repayment-performance classifications, company solvency indicators and other requested data.
- FRA must issue implementation controls within six months; effective 24 September 2026.
- Full record: [Decision 2864/2026](agent3_fra_decision_2864_2026_consumer_finance_data_integration_arabic_text.md).

## Scope qualification
- Decisions 2863 and 2864 apply to consumer-finance companies, not directly to financial leasing/factoring companies under Law 176/2018. They are retained in the broad Agent 3 cross-sector regulatory collection because they connect to Consumer Finance Law 18/2020, Fintech Law 5/2022, electronic verification, credit information and customer-data governance.
- Do not count these as amendments to Consumer Protection Law 181/2018 or Financial Leasing and Factoring Law 176/2018.

## B. National Social Insurance Authority Decision 761/2025 — employer objection committees

- **Arabic title as machine-extracted from the Gazette heading:** قرار رئيس الهيئة رقم ٧٦١ لسنة ٢٠٢٥ بشأن تشكيل وإجراءات عمل لجان بحث اعتراضات أصحاب الأعمال.
- **Decision date:** 12 November 2025.
- **Publication:** Egyptian Gazette (الوقائع المصرية), issue 6, 8 January 2026, printed pages 23–28.
- **Primary source:** State Information Service-hosted Gazette PDF: https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/118102/WQ6_20260108_9841.pdf?sequence=2&isAllowed=y
- **Text status:** full 10-article Arabic text is machine-extracted from the Gazette PDF (PDF lines/pages in web extraction around printed pp.23–28). This is an official-government-hosted Gazette copy, but the instrument-number discrepancy below must be resolved against the page image.

### Article-level capture

1. **Art. 1:** establishes a committee in each Authority region to examine employer objections to insurance contributions calculated from Authority investigations under Article 120 of Law 148/2019. Membership includes the regional director, quality-control, inspection, revenue, information, legal and technical representatives; a technical secretariat of no more than five; meetings at the region or relevant office; non-voting assistance capped at 50% of committee membership.
2. **Art. 2:** defines jurisdiction over employer objections and requires the committee to meet at least weekly.
3. **Art. 3:** employer or legal representative submits the objection to the relevant insurance region/office within 30 days of receiving the contribution assessment notice, using the form in the insurance-services guide; filing fee EGP 100; supporting documents required; objection does not suspend payment of undisputed obligations.
4. **Art. 4:** assigns the technical secretariat intake, fee/document checks, technical study, registers, file preparation, hearing notices (15 days before first hearing; 7 days before second), minutes, committee decision, internal notification and notice to the employer within three days.
5. **Art. 5:** quorum is chair plus at least three members; majority vote, chair has casting vote; decision within 30 days; employer may attend; committee may decide after second non-attendance.
6. **Art. 6:** technical report and recalculation if the employer's objection is substantiated.
7. **Art. 7:** after rejection, employer may refer the dispute to the Article 148 dispute-review committee within 30 days of receiving the rejection notice.
8. **Art. 8:** quarterly statistical reporting to the strategic-planning/customer-service sector.
9. **Art. 9:** committee remuneration under PM Decision 511/2014 as amended by PM Decision 549/2014.
10. **Art. 10:** publication in Egyptian Gazette; effective from date of issuance; repeals conflicting provisions.

### Numbering discrepancy — do not silently normalize

The accessible PDF text extraction renders the heading on printed page 23 as **٧٦١ لسنة ٢٠٢٥**. However:
- the Gazette issue contents page (PDF extraction, printed page 1) lists the Authority decisions as **٧٦٢١ و٧٦٢٣ لسنة ٢٠٢٣** (likely OCR or source-heading errors; year also conflicts with the body);
- the second decision's body (below) refers back to **٧٦٢١ لسنة ٢٠٢٥**;
- a secondary news report and EastLaws record use **٧٦٢١ لسنة ٢٠٢٥**;
- the body of the first decision itself is dated 12 November 2025 and signed by Authority Chair Major General Gamal Awad Mahmoud.

**Working identification:** treat this as the employer-objection committee instrument associated with 12 November 2025 and Gazette issue 6/2026, but retain number as **761/2025 (heading OCR) / 7621/2025 (cross-reference and secondary index; unresolved)**. Do not use a normalized number in authoritative retrieval until the Gazette page image is visually checked.

## C. National Social Insurance Authority Decision 7223/2025 — dispute-review committees

- **Arabic title as machine-extracted from the Gazette heading:** قرار رئيس الهيئة رقم ٧٢٢٣ لسنة ٢٠٢٥ بشأن تشكيل وإجراءات عمل لجان فحص المنازعات.
- **Decision date:** 12 November 2025.
- **Publication:** same Egyptian Gazette issue 6, 8 January 2026, printed pages 29–34.
- **Primary source:** same State Information Service-hosted Gazette PDF: https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/118102/WQ6_20260108_9841.pdf?sequence=2&isAllowed=y
- **Text status:** full 10-article Arabic text machine-extracted from the Gazette PDF.

### Article-level capture

1. **Art. 1:** forms a dispute-review committee in each Authority region, chaired by the regional legal director and comprising legal, operations-centre, quality-control and subject-matter technical members. Members must not overlap with the committee that issued the contested employer-objection decision. A technical secretariat of no more than five is established.
2. **Art. 2:** committee reviews disputes by employers, insured persons, pensioners, beneficiaries and others under Law 148/2019 for amicable resolution before court; it also hears employer disputes against the objection committee decision. It meets at least weekly.
3. **Art. 3:** filing by prescribed insurance-services form at region/office, with supporting papers and receipt or registered post; employer challenge to the objection committee must be filed within 30 days of rejection; filing does not suspend undisputed payment obligations.
4. **Art. 4:** secretariat receives/registers disputes, checks completeness, prepares technical memo, gives hearing notice 15 days before first hearing and 7 days before second, records minutes and informs the interested party within three days of decision.
5. **Art. 5:** quorum is chair plus at least three members; majority vote with chair's casting vote; decision within 30 days; second non-attendance does not prevent decision.
6. **Art. 6:** no court action may be filed before 30 days from the committee application; for Article 120 disputes, if no court challenge is brought within 30 days after the committee decision, the assessment becomes final.
7. **Art. 7:** technical reports use separate forms for ordinary insurance disputes and employer challenges to the objection committee.
8. **Art. 8:** quarterly statistical reporting.
9. **Art. 9:** committee remuneration under PM Decisions 511/2014 and 549/2014.
10. **Art. 10:** publication; effective from date of issuance; conflicting provisions repealed.

### Numbering discrepancy

The body heading is extracted as **٧٢٢٣ لسنة ٢٠٢٥**, while the issue contents page lists **٧٦٢٣ لسنة ٢٠٢٣**. The body cites the employer-objection decision as **٧٦٢١ لسنة ٢٠٢٥**, while that earlier decision's own heading is extracted as **٧٦١ لسنة ٢٠٢٥**. Retain these variants verbatim in source metadata and visually verify printed pages 23 and 29 before canonicalizing numbers or years.

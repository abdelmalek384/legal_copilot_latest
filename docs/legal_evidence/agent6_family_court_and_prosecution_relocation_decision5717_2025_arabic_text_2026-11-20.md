## Legal/data notes
- The instrument relocates both the family court/settlement office and the related family prosecution, not just the court. Article 2 expressly identifies the prosecution as التابعة لنيابة جنوب القاهرة الكلية لشئون الأسرة.
- Decision 5717 cites Decision 4844/2004 as the family-prosecution organizing decision. It does not amend the substantive jurisdiction of family courts or create a new prosecution.
- Article 4 says the decision is effective 1 October 2025, while publication is 1 November 2025. Preserve both dates as printed; this is a retroactive-looking commencement/publication sequence requiring lawyer review. Do not normalize or infer an alternative effective date.
- A news item dated 29 September 2025 described the relocation before Gazette publication and mentioned the prosecution move; the Gazette text itself is the controlling acquisition source here.
- The Gazette OCR uses «زينهم» in its transcription; news sources alternatively spell the former complex «زنانيري». Preserve the Gazette transcription pending page-image verification.

# Collection checkpoint 39 — sports, youth, media, tourism companies and Hajj/Umrah regulation (2026-11-17)

## 1. Sports Law 71/2017

- Law 71/2017 issues the Sports Law.
- Official Amiri Press enforcement instrument:
  - Justice Minister Decision 2558/2024 grants named Ministry of Youth and Sports personnel judicial-officer status for offences under Sports Law 71/2017 and Youth Bodies Law 218/2017:
    https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=YRmuKOh5ZTE%3D
  - The decision states the authorization runs through 13 May 2026. As of 17 November 2026 it is expired unless renewed; do not present it as current.
- PM Decision 2850/2022 on electricity conservation cites Sports Law 71/2017 and Youth Bodies Law 218/2017; this is cross-reference evidence, not an amendment.
- Obtain Law 71/2017, Executive Regulation, sports federation/club rules, licensing, governance, dispute-resolution and anti-doping decisions.

## 2. Youth Bodies Law 218/2017

- Law 218/2017 regulates youth bodies.
- Official Ministry of Youth and Sports implementing rules include:
  - Decision 90/2021 issuing the unified financial regulation for youth centres.
  - Decision 210/2022 issuing the basic regulation for Youth Development Centres.
  - Decision 10/2022 amending Article 18 of the unified financial regulation to exempt specified families of martyrs/victims/missing/injured persons from subscription and annual renewal charges.
- Decision 10/2022 official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=traQ9SwZv0E%3D
- PM Decision 4574/2023 approves converting Sixth District Youth Centre in 6 October City into a Youth Development Centre under Law 218/2017 and Decision 210/2022:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=4DGfeg3lAyU%3D
- Obtain Law 218/2017, Executive Regulation, Decision 90/2021, Decision 210/2022 and amendments, centre financial/administrative rules, elections, membership and oversight.

## 3. Press and Media Regulation Law 180/2018

- Law 180/2018 issues the Law Regulating the Press, Media and Supreme Council for Media Regulation.
- Presidential Decision 518/2024 forms the Supreme Council for Media Regulation for a four-year term and lists its members:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Dlco+fY0SVo%3D
- The Council includes representatives/experts from State Council, Competition Authority, NTRA, journalists, media and Supreme Council of Universities.
- Obtain Law 180/2018, Executive Regulation, Supreme Council regulations, licensing, media ownership, digital publication, content standards, sanctions, press syndicate and journalists' rights rules.
- Distinguish constitutional press freedoms, binding statute, Council regulations and individual licensing/sanction decisions.

## 4. Tourism companies Law 38/1977

- Law 38/1977 regulates tourism companies.
- Justice Minister Decision 5106/2025 grants named Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities personnel judicial-officer status for offences under:
  - Law 38/1977 regulating tourism companies.
  - Law 72/2021 establishing the Egyptian Umrah Portal and regulating tourism companies' Umrah trips.
  - Law 84/2022 issuing the Hajj Regulation Law and establishing the Unified Egyptian Hajj Portal.
- Decision 5106/2025 validity runs through 31 August 2027:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=5iad0CIHxzI%3D
- Obtain Law 38/1977, its Executive Regulation and amendments; map tourism-company licensing, guarantees, management, trip organization, violations and judicial enforcement.

## 5. Umrah Portal Law 72/2021 and Hajj Law 84/2022

- Law 72/2021 establishes the Egyptian Umrah Portal and regulates tourism companies' implementation of Umrah trips.
- Law 84/2022 regulates Hajj and establishes the Unified Egyptian Hajj Portal.
- Official Amiri Press search result for Law 84/2022 exposes substantive provisions including:
  - Definitions of Hajj types, tourism companies, seasonal labour, portal identification codes and transport companies.
  - Electronic linkage obligations for carriers.
  - Passport verification and seasonal work permits at departure ports.
  - Penalties of EGP 1m–3m for unauthorized Hajj trip implementation, doubled on recidivism.
  - Penalties of EGP 1m–5m for violation of Article 18 electronic-linkage obligations, doubled on recidivism.
  - Administrative suspension/cancellation of tourism-company licences for specified violations.
- Full Law 84/2022 text locator:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=YxPuPK9pLac%3D
- Decision 5106/2025 provides judicial-officer enforcement authority through 31 August 2027.
- Obtain both statutes, Executive Regulations, portal rules, seasonal-worker rules, data exchange requirements, carrier obligations, annual Hajj/Umrah controls and fees.

## 6. Tourism sector governance

- Presidential Decision 169/2022 reorganizes the Supreme Council for Tourism:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=iM9U8+su6S4%3D
- The Council approves general tourism policy/strategy, monitors sector developments and approves tourism-area development plans/investment projects; its approved decisions bind ministries/governorates and administrative authorities.
- PM Decision 2236/2024 adds the Minister of Housing to the Ministerial Tourism Committee:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Jx2FE0PTpUA%3D
- Link these governance instruments to Law 8/2022, Law 38/1977, Law 72/2021, Law 84/2022, Sinai Law 14/2012, Investment Law 72/2017 and tourism-area development.

# Collection checkpoint 10 — FRA non-bank finance and fintech regulatory decisions (2026-11-17)

## 1. FRA Board Decision 87/2026 — warning lists, negative lists and administrative measures

- Official FRA regulation page: https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قرار-مجلس-إدارة-الهيئة-رقم-87-لسنة-2026/
- Official Gazette PDF (3 pages): https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/قرار-رقم-87_unlocked-1.pdf
- Gazette: Al-Waqa'i al-Misriyya issue 111 (supplement), 20 May 2026.
- Full searchable PDF text inspected.
- Legal basis cites Laws 146/1988, 95/1992, 93/2000, 148/2001, 10/2009, 141/2014, 176/2018, 18/2020, 5/2022 and 155/2024.
- Article 1 establishes three lists: (1) warning list for unlicensed regulated non-bank financial activity; (2) negative list for persons with final judicial judgments for violating non-bank financial laws; (3) administrative-measures list for persons whose licences are cancelled or registration struck off.
- Article 2: listing is based on an FRA study memorandum and committee review. Warning-list persons are notified and heard before decision; urgent cases threatening customer interests/market stability may be temporarily listed, with notice/hearing within one week.
- Lists are published on FRA website or a dedicated site.
- Article 3: grievance to FRA Chair within 60 days. A committee chaired by one of the Chair's deputies and including two advisers with judicial expertise handles grievances; it may request information and must decide within 30 days after a complete submission.
- Article 4: periodic review and removal when grounds cease; removal may follow a reasoned request showing grounds have ceased, judgment executed, lapsed or settled as applicable.
- Article 5: Gazette/FRA publication and commencement the day after publication.
- Preserve notice, hearing, grievance and deadline safeguards as structured legal rules.

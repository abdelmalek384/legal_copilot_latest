# Law 25/1929 — amendment-chain leads: Laws 4/2005 and 140/2017

## Scope
This note records two amendment instruments identified in the supplied Research Agent 6 material and checked against publicly accessible legal portals. It does **not** claim a complete amendment chain or a consolidated current text of Decree-Law 25/1929.

## Law 4/2005 — Article 20(1)
- **Instrument:** Law 4/2005 amending Article 20(1) of Decree-Law 25/1929.
- **Gazette reference shown by LAWEG:** Official Gazette issue 9 bis, 8 March 2005.
- **Issuance date in text:** 7 March 2005.
- **Commencement:** Article 2 states the day after publication: 9 March 2005.
- **Text observed:** Article 1 replaces the first paragraph of Article 20. The text states that women's custody entitlement ends when the child reaches 15; the judge may choose continuation with the custodian without custody remuneration until the boy reaches majority and the girl marries.
- **Source:** LAWEG page headed with the Gazette issue/date and displaying the two-article amendment: https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?NID=18518&action=viewactivepages&itemid=18468&type=6
- **Evidence limitation:** secondary legal portal reproduction; no original Gazette PDF or page image acquired in this pass.

## Law 140/2017 — Articles 21(2)-(3) and 22
- **Instrument:** Law 140/2017 amending Decree-Law 25/1929.
- **Gazette reference reported by secondary sources:** Official Gazette issue 29 bis, 22 July 2017.
- **Issuance/publication date:** 22 July 2017.
- **Commencement:** Article 2 states the day after publication: 23 July 2017.
- **Text observed in secondary full-text reproduction:** Article 1 replaces the second and third paragraphs of Article 21 and Article 22. It addresses specified presumptions of death for missing persons (including shipwreck/aircraft loss and certain military/police operational disappearances), the competent authority's declaration, and resulting marital/inheritance consequences.
- **Sources:** Lawyer Egypt full-text reproduction: https://lawyeregypt.net/قانون-رقم-140-لسنة-2017-بتعديل-بعض-أحكام-المرسوم-بقانون-رقم-25-لسنة-1929-الخاص-ببعض-أحكام-الأحوال-الشخصية/ ; LAWEG index listing: https://www.laweg.net/Default.aspx?FIID=240&action=LegsCates
- **Evidence limitation:** secondary reproductions only; no original Gazette PDF or page image acquired in this pass.

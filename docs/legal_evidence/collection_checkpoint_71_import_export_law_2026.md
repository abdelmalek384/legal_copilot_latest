# Collection checkpoint 71 — Import/Export Law 118/1975 and Executive Regulation 770/2005

## 1. Official source register

- Egyptian Customs Authority official legislation index:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Legislations/Documents
- Customs Authority says it published a PDF handbook of Import/Export Law 118/1975 in June 2026:
  - Joint Import/Export Notice 16/2026, dated 1 June 2026, announces the PDF handbook on the official Customs website.
- Customs Authority's export notices index:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Legislations/Documents
- Executive Regulation: Minister of Trade and Industry Decision 770/2005 issuing the rules implementing Law 118/1975 and procedures for inspection/control of imported and exported goods.
- Law 118/1975 and Regulation 770/2005 are distinct from Customs Law 207/2020 and Regulation 430/2021. Customs Law governs customs clearance/tax/inspection; Import/Export Law governs trade controls, licensing, product restrictions and inspection of imported/exported goods.

## 2. 2025 amendments to Executive Regulation 770/2005

### Minister of Investment and Foreign Trade Decision 228/2025
- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=AAzJR1nSg9M%3D
- Published in Al-Waqa'i issue 132 supplement (A), 17 June 2025.
- Amends provisions of the Executive Regulation 770/2005; obtain full decision and all annexes.
- Customs Import Notice 15/2025 explains implementation of Decision 228/2025:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace224682377.pdf
- This is a ministerial amendment to the Regulation, not an amendment to Law 118/1975.

### Minister of Investment and Foreign Trade Decision 273/2025
- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=AAzJR1nSg9M%3D
- Adds specified tariff headings to the list attached to Decision 235/2013, requiring full value of certain exported goods to be paid in convertible foreign currency through a CBE-approved bank, by full documentary credit, full transfer before shipment or approved guaranteed payment method.
- Exporter must provide bank certificate to the competent customs office before shipment.
- Verify the separate decision page/annex and avoid conflating Decision 273/2025 with Decision 228/2025; they are distinct instruments.

### Minister of Investment and Foreign Trade Decision 395/2025
- Customs Import Notice 30/2025 identifies Decision 395/2025, issued 9 September 2025 and published in Al-Waqa'i issue 228, 12 October 2025:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace064094820.pdf
- Adds serial 4 bis to Appendix 8 (qualitative import-control goods and inspection fees) of Executive Regulation 770/2005.
- This changes the controlled-goods list; retrieve the original Gazette decision and appendix.

## 3. 2026 amendment — new Article 41 bis in Regulation 770/2005

- Customs Authority export notices index records a 9 February 2026 notice that a new Article 41 bis was added to the Executive Regulation 770/2005:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Legislations/Documents
- The 2026 notice says the new provision is added to the import/export implementing regulation; the original Ministerial Decision number and full article text must be obtained.
- It is a regulatory amendment and should be stored with its own decision number, Gazette issue, effective date and affected import/export procedures.

## 4. 2026 import/export control and operational notices

The Customs Authority index lists the following recent notices:
- Export Notice 13/2026 (5 January 2026): restricts export of white sand raw material in shipments to production projects in free zones, within quantities approved by GAFI.
- Export Notice 61/2026 (9 February 2026): notice concerning new Article 41 bis in Regulation 770/2005.
- Export Notice 109/2026 (9 March 2026): extends Ministerial Decision 88/2023 through 30 April 2026; historical/time-limited.
- Import Notice 283/2026 (24 August 2026): adds a second paragraph to the special conditions for goods at serial 14 of Appendix 3 of Regulation 770/2005.
- Import Notice 316/2026 (7 September 2026): rules/controls for personal-use imports.
- Import Notice 327/2026 (12 September 2026): customs offices may not proceed with waiver/transfer of shipments referred to GOEIC for inspection without consulting the competent branch.
- Customs instructions must be classified as operational notices, not laws; record their affected tariff codes, goods, ports, approval authority and expiry.

## 5. 2026 current customs cross-reference

- Customs Law 207/2020 and Executive Regulation 430/2021 (checkpoint 70).
- Import/Export Law 118/1975 and Executive Regulation 770/2005.
- Investment Law 72/2017 and GAFI approval.
- CBE Law 194/2020 and foreign-currency payment requirements.
- VAT Law 67/2016 and customs import VAT.
- NFSA Law 1/2017 and food import/export controls.
- EDA Law 151/2019 and pharmaceutical/medical-device controls.
- Plant quarantine, veterinary controls, consumer product quality and environment/waste rules.

## 6. Other important statutory relationships

- Importers Register Law 121/1982 and Executive Regulation.
- Exporters Register rules and GOEIC procedures.
- Law 118/1975 is distinct from Customs Law 207/2020; do not assume every import restriction is a customs tariff or customs offence.
- Free zones and special economic zones are subject to Investment Law 72/2017 and Special Economic Zones Law 83/2002.
- Export proceeds and payment conditions may be linked to CBE regulations and Ministerial Decisions 235/2013, 273/2025 and later amendments.

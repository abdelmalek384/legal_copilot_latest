## Two new official leasing/factoring amendment acquisitions

### FRA Decision 82/2019 — sale-and-leaseback controls
- Official FRA-hosted PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/fra_live_data/pdfs/UG47647_UG47648.pdf
- Decision dated 16 July 2019; amends Decision 7/2019 by inserting Article 4 bis.
- Captured exact Arabic Article 1 and Article 2. Four sale-and-leaseback finance types are addressed: operating expenses; special/high-tech assets paid directly to supplier; construction/extension/finishing disbursed by progress tranches; and debt-swap finance requiring detailed creditor/source/cash-flow evidence.
- Full record: [Decision 82/2019 Arabic text](agent3_fra_decision_82_2019_sale_leaseback_controls_arabic_text.md).

### FRA Decision 38/2020 — Central Bank Law supervisory enforcement bridge
- Official two-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/alamiria_2020_38.pdf
- Gazette issue 82 supplement, 7 April 2020, printed pages 19–20; decision dated 2 March 2020.
- Captured exact Arabic Articles 1–3. Inserts Article 14 bis into real-estate finance Decision 111/2015 and Article 13 bis into leasing/factoring Decision 7/2019.
- Allows FRA to apply sector-law measures when a licensed real-estate finance/leasing/factoring company violates Central Bank and Banking System Law provisions relevant to its activity, where the violation is established by FRA or CBE.
- Full record: [Decision 38/2020 Arabic text](agent3_fra_decision_38_2020_central_bank_law_enforcement_leasing_factoring_arabic_text.md).

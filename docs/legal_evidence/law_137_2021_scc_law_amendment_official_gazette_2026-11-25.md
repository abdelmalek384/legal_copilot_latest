## Operative changes observed in secondary text
Article 1 adds two new provisions to Law 48/1979:
- **Article 27 bis:** the Supreme Constitutional Court exercises constitutional review over decisions of international organisations and entities and foreign court judgments requested to be enforced against the State.
- **Article 33 bis:** the Prime Minister may request a ruling that the decisions/judgments described in Article 27 bis, or obligations arising from them, are not to be recognised. The provision requires joinder of interested parties, a notified copy of the decision/judgment and a certified translation, identification of the constitutional provision allegedly violated and the grounds, and directs the Court to decide urgently.

Article 2 provides for publication and commencement on the day after publication. The Gazette issue date and commencement are also reflected in Eastlaws metadata.

## Relationship to Law 48/1979
This is a discrete insertion of Articles 27 bis and 33 bis, not a consolidated re-publication of Law 48/1979. Provisional article-version events EV-0355 and EV-0356 have been recorded against the base instrument EG-0547. They remain pending Gazette-page visual comparison and lawyer review.

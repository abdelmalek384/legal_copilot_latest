### FRA Decision 140/2026 — auditor independence
- Official two-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/alamiria_2026_140.pdf
- Gazette issue 163 supplement (أ), 29 July 2026, printed pages 2–3; adopted 8 July 2026.
- Amends Decision 58/2018 Article 7 bis 2(3): one auditor may not audit more than five investment funds simultaneously without prior FRA approval and compliance with FRA standards, including conflict-of-interest safeguards in the prospectus/information memorandum.
- Effective 30 July 2026.
- Full record: [Decision 140/2026 Arabic text](agent3_fra_decision_140_2026_investment_fund_auditor_limit_arabic_text.md).

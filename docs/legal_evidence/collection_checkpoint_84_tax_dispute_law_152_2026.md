## 2. Actual Article 1 — renewal of tax-dispute settlement procedures

Law 152/2026 renews the operation of Law 79/2016 on ending tax disputes, as amended by:
- Law 14/2018.
- Law 174/2018.
- Law 16/2020.
- Law 173/2020.
- Law 153/2022.
- Law 160/2024.

The renewal is through **31 December 2026**.

- Existing committees formed under Law 79/2016 continue to decide applications already filed and not yet determined.
- The committees also receive and decide new applications submitted through 31 December 2026.

## 4. Practical scope

- This is a time-limited alternative dispute-resolution/settlement route for tax disputes covered by Law 79/2016 and its amendments.
- It does not automatically settle every tax dispute or waive all tax/interest/penalty liabilities.
- Eligibility, tax periods, application requirements, settlement amounts, waivers and exclusions are governed by Law 79/2016 as amended and its implementing decisions.
- The filing deadline for new applications is 31 December 2026 unless later legislation extends it.
- The existing settlement committees remain competent for pending applications.
- ETA index lists Minister of Finance Decision 450/2026 concerning applications for settlement of tax/customs disputes under Law 152/2026; obtain the full decision and application forms.

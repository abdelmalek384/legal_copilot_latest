Existing repo already contains Decision 162 candidate/transcription files; this entry is a new source acquisition pointer and should not replace or duplicate those existing files.

The secondary transcription identifies a 66-article instrument, including:
- Art. 2: establishments employing ten or more workers must prepare internal work rules.
- Art. 7–10: submission, union consultation and review/approval procedures.
- Art. 11: scope across establishment/branches/sites and employment types.
- Art. 46: worker duties, investigation safeguards and deadlines.
- Art. 61–63: separation of termination grounds, resignation rules and union-activity protections.
- Art. 64–65: periodic review and amendment process for internal rules.
- Art. 66: publication and commencement.

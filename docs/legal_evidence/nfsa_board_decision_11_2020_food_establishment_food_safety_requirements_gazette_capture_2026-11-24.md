## 2. Scope and article/annex map

The searchable text includes:
- definitions and scope for applying food-safety requirements in food establishments;
- a preventive approach incorporating prerequisite programmes (PRPs), HACCP, traceability, withdrawal and recall;
- food-trading licence and establishment-operator obligations;
- the annex of basic food-safety requirements, including establishment hygiene and maintenance, cleaning/disinfection, pest control, personnel hygiene/training, raw materials and supplier controls, food handling and processing, temperature controls, storage, packaging materials, potable water, records, transport, product withdrawal/recall and labelling/information.

The extracted text reaches the annex's later provisions on records, transport, recall/withdrawal, product information and consumer education. Article numbering in the PDF's RTL text layer can appear visually reversed in extracted text; verify provision numbers against page images before citing an individual numbered clause. The main decision states that the annex is inseparable; do not detach or omit it when indexing the instrument.

## 3. Crosswalk to the parent legal framework

- Law 1/2017 establishes the NFSA and its food-safety rulemaking, licensing, inspection and recall functions.
- PM Decision 412/2019 Executive Regulation supplies the general regulatory framework, including Articles 12–15 on licensing, operator duties and imported/exported food controls.
- NFSA Board Decision 11/2020 is a sector-operational instrument for food establishments and should be indexed separately from the parent law and executive regulation.
- Its preamble references other NFSA Board decisions (including 6/2020 and 7/2020). Acquire those texts separately before assuming the full import-control or establishment rule set is contained here.

# Collection checkpoint 6 — commercial, family, criminal and procedure discovery (2026-11-17)

## Newly located official company-law text and amendment graph

### Companies Law 159/1981
- Ministry of Investment and Foreign Trade's official detail record states the law is in force and identifies the current displayed text as last amended 15 September 2020.
- Official direct text/download endpoint: https://www.mift.gov.eg/legislation/download/231
- Detail and amendment register: https://www.mift.gov.eg/legislation/details/252
- Amendments shown: 212/1994, 3/1998, 159/1998, 94/2005, 68/2009, 17/2015, 4/2018.
- Executive Regulation Decision 96/1982; portal displays 21 regulatory attachments, including Decision 2928/2022 and multiple 2018/2019/2010/2009/2008/2005/2004/1998/1997/1995/1994/1991/1987 decisions.
- This is a major official full-text source. Its direct endpoint is indexed as Arabic text. It is not yet byte-archived or page-verified in this workflow.

## Family law — secondary discovery leads
- Personal Status Law 25/1929: https://manshurat.org/node/12369
- Litigation Procedures in Personal Status Matters Law 1/2000: https://manshurat.org/node/27318
- Law 91/2000 amendment to Law 1/2000: https://manshurat.org/node/12574
- These are marked amended by the secondary repository. Current law requires careful reconciliation of amendments, Supreme Constitutional Court judgments and later legislation. Do not treat any single PDF as a complete current personal-status code.

## Civil and Commercial Procedure — amendment lead
- Civil and Commercial Procedure Law 13/1968 full text is available on non-government repositories, but an official government full text has not been pinned in this checkpoint.
- Ministry of Finance record: Law 157/2024 amended Law 13/1968, Gazette issue 27 bis (d), 10 July 2024:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-66fba04be1ceb700085b001b
- A consolidated procedural corpus must include Law 157/2024 and all other applicable amendments, plus special procedural statutes.

  https://www.mift.gov.eg/legislation/details/252

## Data handling
- Keep official and secondary texts in separate trust tiers.
- Never present a secondary transcription as an official Gazette copy.
- Capture the exact version date, amendment list, Gazette issue, effective date, source URL, retrieval time, original file checksum and extraction details for each document.
- Lawyer approval and rights clearance remain separate gates.

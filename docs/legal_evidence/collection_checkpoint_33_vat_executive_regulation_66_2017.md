# Collection checkpoint 33 — VAT Executive Regulation 66/2017, 92 articles

## Source and version
- Finance Minister Decision 66/2017 issuing the Executive Regulation of VAT Law 67/2016.
- Official publication: Al-Waqa'i al-Misriyya issue 55 (supplement A), 7 March 2017.
- Full 92-article Arabic transcription accessible at:
  https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/decree-66-2017
- Transcription states it is copied verbatim from Official Gazette/Court of Cassation legislation portal and updated through 23 October 2025; 1,395 indexed lines.
- Official ETA VAT legislation index: https://www.eta.gov.eg/ar/content/qwanyn-aldrybt-ly-alqymt-almdaft
- Official ETA base VAT law and regulation are separately indexed. This transcription is a secondary reproduction; compare each amendment with original Gazette decision.

## Promulgation and transition
- Article 1 applies attached Executive Regulation to VAT Law 67/2016.
- Article 2 repeals Executive Regulation 749/2001 under the old General Sales Tax Law, with temporary transition for pending appeals and specified matters.
- Articles 3–7 set transition for existing sales-tax registrants, registration, final returns, inventory, input credits, status regularization and timing of substantive/procedural rules.
- Article 8: published in Al-Waqa'i and effective day after publication.

## Selected article-level provisions inspected

### Definitions and scope
- Article 1 adopts definitions in VAT Law and defines competent office, indirect inputs, imported/exported services and electronic distribution platform (website/portal/e-store/online marketplace connecting supplier/service provider and customer).
- Article 2: inheritance/legacy transfers and self-produced goods not intended for sale/trade are not taxable sales.
- Article 3: excluded from taxable services include employee work for employer under employment contract, partners' work under partnership agreement, specified representative offices' cost-recovery services, government public services, intra-group loans and securities trading.
- Article 4: standard VAT rate 13% until 30 June 2017 and 14% from 1 July 2017; 5% rate for qualifying machinery/equipment used exclusively for goods/services production, excluding buses/passenger cars; production lines included, parts/spare parts taxed at general rate.
- Article 5: zero-rating of exported goods/services subject to customs export proof, invoice/contract, service details and evidence of foreign payment or permitted alternative settlement.
- Article 6 identifies persons required to collect, report and remit VAT: producers, importers, service providers, distribution agents, exporters, traders (with table-tax-only exception), representatives of non-residents pending simplified registration, and non-resident simplified suppliers.
- Article 7 determines tax due on local sales/services, imports at customs release and imported services used in Egypt; electronic distribution platform and digital-location rules determine whether non-resident service is supplied in Egypt.
- Articles 7 bis and 7 bis 1 set platform liability exceptions where provider is contractually responsible and conditions for platform reliance on supplier data.

### Non-taxable transfers and free zones
- Article 8: no VAT on transit goods under customs control, movement between production stages/lines, transfer from production/storage to own distribution outlets, or stages of service within same unit, subject to rules.
- Article 9: zero-rate for specified goods/services imported or bought by free-zone/industrial/economic-zone projects for licensed activity, subject to documentation; domestic consumption inside such zones triggers tax on customs release.
- Article 10: cessation/transfer of taxable activity requires notice within 30 days; tax/table tax due on goods/services held at disposition unless successor is registered.

### Valuation and related topics
- Article 11: taxable value is amounts actually paid/payable for goods/services in normal independent transactions; free/undervalued supplies use market conditions; customary trade discounts accepted if reflected on invoice and not conditional.
- The remaining regulation addresses valuation, discounts, imports, tax credits, invoices, records, returns, refund, registration, special sectors, services and implementation.
- Further complete article-by-article extraction is required; this checkpoint records only provisions directly inspected.

## Amendment graph identified in the transcription
- Decision 24/2023: amends the VAT Executive Regulation (ETA index).
- Decision 417/2025 and Decision 418/2025: listed on ETA VAT legislation index; obtain their exact Gazette texts and affected articles.
- Law 149/2026 amends VAT Law 67/2016, including Article 28 bis and schedule; its corrigendum dated 8 August 2026 must be applied.
- The 2026 amendment's implementation may require changes to this Executive Regulation; do not assume a 2025 consolidated copy includes later 2026 changes.
- The 2017 regulation also references other laws: Customs, Commercial Code, Civil Code, Income Tax, FRA Law 10/2009, Companies Law 159/1981, Electronic Signature Law 15/2004 and old tendering Law 89/1998.

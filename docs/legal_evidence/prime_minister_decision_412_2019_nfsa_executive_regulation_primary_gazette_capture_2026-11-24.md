# Prime Minister Decision 412/2019 — NFSA Executive Regulation: primary Gazette capture

## 2. Article map

- **Issuing Article 1:** puts the attached Executive Regulation into effect and repeals inconsistent provisions.
- **Issuing Article 2:** publication and commencement the following day.
- **Regulation Article 1:** definitions, including NFSA, competent authority, mandatory technical rules, hazard/risk, food establishment, food-business operator, food-trading licence and inspection concepts.
- **Articles 2–3:** mandatory technical rules and internal administrative/financial rules.
- **Articles 4–7:** risk analysis, food traceability, labelling/non-deception and scientific data collection.
- **Articles 8–11:** inspection system, risk-based frequency, coordination on mandatory technical rules and training of inspection personnel.
- **Articles 12–14:** food-trading licensing, operator obligations, hygiene/HACCP-related duties, special/genetically modified/irradiated foods.
- **Articles 15–17:** exclusive NFSA documentary/physical control of imported and exported food, sampling/testing and export fitness certificates.
- **Article 18:** contracting with accredited government laboratories, with private Egyptian or foreign laboratories where required government capacity is unavailable.
- **Article 19:** emergency plans and temporary risk-management measures.
- **Articles 20–24:** grievance committees, technical secretariat, filing requirements, 15-day grievance window and decision period (three days for designated urgent cases), committee powers and judicial review sequence.
- **Articles 25–30:** judicial-officer status, designation, powers, identification and inspection authority.

## 3. Important statutory interfaces

1. **Licensing and operational obligations:** Articles 12–14 establish the regulation-level framework, but do not substitute for current NFSA Board rules, sector-specific licensing decisions, or updated technical standards.
2. **Imports and exports:** Article 15 assigns NFSA the documentary and physical inspection and sampling/testing role for imported and exported food, with Customs to act on NFSA determinations on food safety. Read together with Article 16 sampling/testing rules and Article 17 export fitness certificates.
3. **Rejected consignments:** Article 12(4) gives the NFSA Chair or delegate powers, at the specified levels, to order destruction, licence suspension, production stoppage, closure and re-export where food is harmful or unfit. This is relevant to the September 2026 EEAA report on rejected consignments, but that agency report does not replace the governing instrument or shipment-specific decision.
4. **Grievances:** Articles 20–24 implement the parent Law's Article 18 grievance framework. Do not calculate a deadline without confirming the date of notification/knowledge, the exact decision, and any applicable urgent-case designation.
5. **Enforcement:** Articles 25–30 detail judicial-officer appointment, scope and inspection; do not treat every employee as a judicial officer without the required designation.

## Selected actual provisions inspected

### Articles 1–4: taxpayer and five-year valuation
- Article 1 definitions: Real Estate Tax Law, tax, Finance Minister, Real Estate Tax Authority, competent governor.
- Article 2: liable person is natural/legal person with ownership, usufruct or exploitation right; proof may be contracts, court judgments, real-estate register documents or any other document, whether registered or not.
- Article 3: five-year rental-value reassessment process begins at least one year and no more than three years before expiry of previous five-year valuation; committees formed and announced; assessments published and notified; effective from 1 July after completion.
- Five-year reassessment may not increase rental value by more than 30% for residential property or 45% for non-residential property compared with previous five-year assessment.
- Article 4: Real Estate Tax Authority must respect local-government/new-urban-community jurisdiction boundaries in inventory and valuation.

### Articles 5–8: inventory and classification
- Article 5: all buildings are inventoried every five years, with annual updates for new buildings, additions, material modifications, buildings whose exemption ended, exploited vacant land, and units whose old statutory rent relationship ended under Laws 49/1977 and 136/1981.
- Article 6: buildings on agricultural land may be taxed subject to licensing, final acquittal/expiry of criminal case, or passage of three years plus connection to basic utilities without violation report; agricultural land tax may be removed for the built-up plot when building tax begins.
- Article 7: rental value assessed based on geographic location, street, proximity to beaches/parks, construction quality/materials, utilities, services, roads and transport, actual use and declarations.
- Article 8 is marked repealed in the 2020 consolidated text.

### Article 9: annual tax calculation
- Annual tax for residential and non-residential property is 10% of assessed annual rental value after deductions:
  - Residential: deduct 30% of annual rental value for all expenses/maintenance, then statutory exemption threshold.
  - Non-residential: deduct 32% for expenses/maintenance, then statutory exemption threshold.
- The base-law exemption thresholds and any later changes must be checked against current Law 196/2008.

### Articles 10–12: valuation committees and returns
- Article 10: committees formed by Finance Minister/delegate, including Real Estate Tax Authority, governorate representative and taxpayer representative; specialized properties (industrial, tourist, petroleum, airports, ports, mines/quarries) require expert representatives nominated by relevant unions/minister.
- Article 11: committee quorum requires full formation; decisions by majority; committees inventory and value properties within assigned area.
- Article 12: taxpayer files annual/five-year property declaration in second half of year before five-year inventory, or by end of December each year for annual inventory; required data include taxpayer identity/capacity, property address, floors/units/areas/values, occupants/rents/use, national ID, acquisition documents and correspondence address.

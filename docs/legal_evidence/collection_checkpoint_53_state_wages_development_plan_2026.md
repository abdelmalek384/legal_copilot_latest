# Collection checkpoint 53 — 2026 public-sector wage increases, minimum wage and development plan

## 1. Law 75/2026 — annual raises and special grants for State employees

- Official Amiri Press full text:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=plow6SJe%2F6I%3D
- Law title: sets periodic allowance for Civil Service Law employees, special allowance for non-Civil-Service employees, increases additional incentive and grants special benefit to public-sector/public-business-sector company employees.
- Issued 1 July 2026; applies from 1 July 2026.
- Article 1: employees covered by Civil Service Law 81/2016 receive a periodic allowance of 12% of job wage as at 30 June 2026, minimum EGP 150/month; it becomes part of job wage from 1 July 2026.
- Article 2: employees not covered by Civil Service Law receive a special allowance of 15% of basic wage as at 30 June 2026 (or appointment date for later hires), minimum EGP 150/month, subject to the specified exception/offset for entities already granting an annual allowance at 12% or more.
- Article 3: additional monthly incentive increases by EGP 750 for Civil Service and non-Civil-Service employees, with the law's pay classification rules.
- Article 5: public-sector and public-business-sector companies grant a monthly benefit equal to the difference between their annual periodic allowance and the State non-Civil-Service special allowance, capped as stated; it is not added to basic wage.
- Article 5 also sets a minimum total monthly income of EGP 8,000 for workers in those companies after the increase, with implementing rules by competent ministers.
- Article 6 regulates non-accumulation with pension increases and how to calculate the difference.
- Article 7 authorizes the Minister of Finance and relevant ministers to issue implementing decisions.
- This law is an annual 2026 compensation instrument; do not assume the same rates automatically apply in later fiscal years.

## 2. PM Decision 2170/2026 — minimum wage for State employees

- Official Amiri Press full text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=7R2Fh+fuJCg%3D
- Amends PM Decision 1627/2019 and prior minimum-wage amendments.
- Effective 1 July 2026.
- Monthly minimum total wage amounts:
  - Excellent grade: EGP 14,500.
  - High grade: EGP 12,250.
  - General Manager: EGP 11,250.
  - First grade: EGP 9,500.
  - Second grade: EGP 9,000.
  - Third grade: EGP 8,750.
  - Fourth grade: EGP 8,500.
  - Fifth grade: EGP 8,250.
  - Sixth grade: EGP 8,000.
- Article 2 replaces Article 3 of Decision 1627/2019 and governs the supplementary incentive, including how general/collective bonuses and allowances are counted and which allowances are excluded.
- This is the State/public-authority minimum wage, not the private-sector minimum wage under Labour Law 14/2025 and National Wages Council Decision 3165/2026.

## 3. Law 78/2026 — State Economic and Social Development Plan FY 2026/2027

- Law 78/2026 approves the Economic and Social Development Plan for FY 2026/2027.
- It is cited in PM Decision 3530/2026 on public expenditure rationalization and is listed in the official Ministry of Finance 2026 legislation index.
- The full plan law and annexed tables should be obtained from the Ministry of Planning/Official Gazette and extracted by sector, programme, target, investment and geographic allocation.
- It is an annual planning instrument, not a permanent substantive law; link to State Budget Law 77/2026 and Unified Public Finance Law 6/2022.

## 4. Distinguish three different wage regimes

1. **State employees/public authorities:** Law 75/2026 and PM Decision 2170/2026; Civil Service Law 81/2016 and entity-specific employment statutes.
2. **Private-sector workers:** Labour Law 14/2025, National Wages Council Decision 3165/2026, and current private-sector minimum wage/periodic allowance decisions.
3. **Public-sector/public-business-sector company employees:** Law 75/2026 Article 5 and applicable company employment rules under Public Business Sector Law 203/1991, Companies Law 159/1981 and entity-specific regulations.

Do not apply public-sector minimum wage figures to private-sector employees, or vice versa.

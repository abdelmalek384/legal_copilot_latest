## Confirmed structure and topic map from the extracted PDF

The document is arranged in five chapters and reaches closing provisions on printed page 56. The visible extracted text identifies these subject clusters:

1. **Definitions and general rules:** definitions include the ministry, competent authority, competent administration, domestic/overseas recruitment administration, private recruitment agencies, electronic recruitment agencies, unemployed-person employment offices, licence, headquarters, branch, labour-supply contractor, agency representative and follow-up register.
2. **Recruitment through authorised bodies and private agencies:** identifies permitted public and private routes for domestic and overseas recruitment, licensing, company form, capital/ownership requirements, application documents, guarantees and ongoing obligations.
3. **Domestic and overseas recruitment operations:** regulates agency dealings with employers and workers, contracts, recruitment requests, advertisements, worker information, fees, wages, travel and worker records.
4. **Electronic recruitment:** requires a licence for electronic recruitment operations and imposes user-facing information, privacy, security, backup, incident-notification and user-data correction/deletion requirements.
5. **Pre-departure orientation and complaints:** requires pre-departure orientation for workers travelling abroad, and creates a specialist unit for receiving and investigating affected workers' complaints.

This topic map is not a substitute for an article-by-article transcription. The PDF appears to contain provisions numbered into the 50s; the exact final article count and all intervening article boundaries must be confirmed visually before corpus ingestion.

### Article 2 — permitted recruitment channels (printed page 43)

**المادة (2)**

Opening text:

«مع عدم الإخلال بالاتفاقيات الدولية المتعلقة بالتشغيل، تكون مزاولة عمليات التشغيل بالداخل أو الخارج عن طريق الإدارات المختصة بالوزارة، أو الجهات الآتية:»

The visible list includes:
1. Ministries and public authorities for their own workers.
2. Egyptian public-sector, public-business-sector and private-sector companies for contracts with foreign entities within their work and activity.
3. Licensed private recruitment agencies in specified company forms.
4. Electronic recruitment agencies licensed by the Ministry.

### Article 10 — capital and ownership conditions (printed page 44)

The extracted passage states that for a company wishing to operate domestically, issued or paid-up capital (as applicable) must not be less than **EGP 250,000**, and the company must be wholly Egyptian-owned. For overseas recruitment, or combined domestic and overseas recruitment, capital must not be less than **EGP 500,000**, with an absolute majority of founders and board members being Egyptians who collectively own at least **51%** of company capital.

**Important:** these figures are high-impact licensing conditions. They must be checked against the printed page image and reconciled with Labour Law 14/2025 Article 41 before legal use.

### Article 11 — licensing application documents (printed pages 44–45)

The application is made to the competent administration on the prescribed form. The visible list includes:
- official copy of the company's incorporation contract and articles;
- official statement of founders, board members and relevant managers and their nationalities;
- criminal records for those persons;
- tax card;
- official commercial-register extract;
- six national-ID photo copies for board members and relevant managers;
- fixed-date lease or registered ownership deed for the company's operating premises;
- a power of attorney (the extracted page continues beyond this excerpt).

This is a partial list only; do not use it as a complete application checklist.

### Article 14 — financial guarantee (printed page 45)

The extracted passage states that a licence is not issued, if approved, until the company provides a guarantee of at least **EGP 1,000,000**, either cash or an unconditional, irrevocable bank guarantee issued by a bank registered with the Central Bank, in the company's name and for the Ministry's benefit. It must remain valid throughout the licence period; where deductions are made for fines, compensation or amounts improperly collected, the company must replenish it within 15 days after notice by registered letter with acknowledgment of receipt.

**Important:** verify the guarantee amount, form and replenishment period from the page image and Law 14/2025 Article 41.

### Article 12 — licensed agency duties (printed page 44)

The visible text requires the licensed company to:
- comply with Ministry instructions and circulars concerning recruitment of Egyptians domestically and abroad;
- monitor changes in wage policy and working conditions and include available information in periodic reports to the competent administration;
- conclude contracts with employers, authenticated by competent authorities, and provide the operating administration with copies, including worker numbers, occupations, wages and financial relations.

The article continues beyond the captured lines; this is not the complete list.

### Article 13 — travel costs and retention of records (printed page 45)

Extracted text:

«يلتزم صاحب العمل بنقل العامل وتحمل نفقات سفره في حالة التعاقد معه للعمل بالخارج، من محل التعاقد إلى مقر العمل والعكس، سواء في أول مرة أو في الإجازات أو عند نهاية الخدمة»

The following line requires the company to retain files of workers recruited abroad for at least one full year from the end of the contract.

### Article 29 — job-advertisement information (printed page 49)

The extracted text begins:

«يجب أن يتوفر في الإعلان الذي ينشر عن الوظائف بالداخل أو الخارج الشروط الآتية:»

The visible required information includes the licensed recruitment agency's name, licence number and address; the employer/company/establishment seeking the advertised positions (subject to a stated confidentiality exception); the job title and number of positions; and job requirements and specifications, including the type, nature and location of work, required qualifications/documents and contract duration.

### Article 34 — electronic recruitment licensing (printed page 52)

Extracted text:

«لا يجوز ممارسة عمليات إلحاق المصريين بالداخل والخارج إلكترونيًا، من خلال مواقع أو صفحات أو منصات إلكترونية، إلا بعد الحصول على ترخيص بذلك من الإدارة المختصة»

The text then states an exception for the private recruitment agencies described in Article 2(3), and applies Chapter Two provisions to electronic recruitment operations where no specific rule in that chapter conflicts with their nature.

### Article 46 — electronic platform security and privacy (printed page 54)

The extracted passage, subject to page-image QA, requires licensed companies to protect electronic sites/pages/platforms, including:
1. strict protection of users' or customers' data, including encryption and secure storage;
2. compliance with information-security frameworks and international standards;
3. periodic backup copies to enable restoration after a breach or failure;
4. retention of system-activity logs for at least five years;
5. notifying the competent administration within five working days of an incident affecting information security;
6. a clear, regularly updated privacy policy and prevention of unauthorised access to data;
7. a mechanism for users to request correction or deletion of their data.

These are obligations in the reproduced instrument text; legal scope, interaction with Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020 and Cybercrime Law 175/2018, and enforceability require counsel review.

### Article 48 — transition for existing electronic agencies (printed page 54)

The extracted text requires companies already conducting electronic recruitment before the Law and Decision took effect to regularise their status within no more than one year from the Decision's commencement.

### Article 49 — pre-departure orientation (printed page 55)

The extracted text requires all entities conducting recruitment operations, including Ministry administrations, to enrol workers nominated to travel in pre-departure orientation programmes implemented by the Ministry's «وحدة توجيه قبل المغادرة». The visible minimum topics include the host country and its labour legislation, workers' rights and duties, skills required for the nominated occupations, recruitment pathways, travel/return procedures, and how to reach labour representation offices abroad or the competent consulate. The programme is to be delivered in Arabic or a language understood by a worker who does not speak Arabic.

### Article 50 — complaints unit (printed page 55)

The extracted text provides for a specialist unit within the competent Ministry administration, including legal and technical members, to receive and investigate complaints of affected workers. Its powers include collecting evidence, hearing witnesses and seeking assistance from Ministry administrations such as inspection, legal affairs and foreign relations. It prepares a report of findings, proposed remedies and proposed sanctions where appropriate.

### Closing provisions — repeals and commencement (printed page 56)

The extracted final provisions state that prior decisions/texts inconsistent with the Decision are repealed, and that the Decision is published in Egyptian Proceedings and takes effect the day after publication. The PDF OCR gives the date of issue as 4/12/2025, but the date's digit order and the final article number must be visually confirmed.

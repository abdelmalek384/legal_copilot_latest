## Promulgation provisions visually inspected

- Article 1 applies the attached law establishing:
  1. Egyptian Authority for Unified Procurement, Medical Supply and Management of Medical Technology.
  2. Egyptian Drug Authority.
  Conflicting provisions are repealed.
- Article 2 transfers to the Egyptian Drug Authority the relevant competencies, assets and obligations of:
  - National Organization for Drug Control and Research (established by Presidential Decree 382/1976).
  - National Organization for Research and Control of Biologicals (Presidential Decree 398/1995).
  - Other entities/administrative bodies responsible for control of medical preparations and supplies, within the scope specified by the law.
  - It also transfers the relevant health ministry competencies concerning regulation, registration, inspection and control of pharmaceutical practice under Law 127/1955.
- Article 3 transfers certain employees and personnel from ministries, public authorities, local administration and other specified entities to the new bodies, with rights/financial entitlements preserved as stated.
- Article 4 requires the Prime Minister to issue the Executive Regulation within six months after publication in the Official Gazette.
- Article 5 replaces the final paragraph of Article 23 of Universal Health Insurance Law 2/2018: the Egyptian Authority for Unified Procurement, Medical Supply and Management of Medical Technology is responsible for providing medical supplies necessary for healthcare services, in coordination with the Universal Health Insurance Authority and under Law 127/1955 regulating the pharmacy profession.
- Article 6: publication and commencement six months after publication.

## Attached Unified Procurement Authority law — opening provisions

- The attached law begins on Gazette page 6.
- Article 1 definitions include medical technology, medical supplies, medical preparations, procurement, unified procurement, strategic stock, medical technology management and other institutional terms.
- Article 2 establishes the Authority as a public legal person reporting to the Prime Minister, headquartered in Cairo, with branches/offices as needed.
- Article 3 sets out the Authority's purposes, including unified procurement and supply-chain management for public entities and the medical-technology management function.
- Page 7 begins further definitions and functions. Full article extraction remains outstanding.

## Data model and cross-law relationships

- Link Law 151/2019 to Universal Health Insurance Law 2/2018 (Article 5 amendment), Pharmacy Practice Law 127/1955, the transferred predecessor bodies' founding decrees, and subsequent procurement/medical-supply regulations.
- Distinguish the Unified Procurement Authority from the Egyptian Drug Authority: separate legal persons/competencies may be set by the attached statute and its companion statute.
- Link public procurement Law 182/2018 only where the statutes and implementing rules say it applies; do not assume that all procurement by the new authority is governed identically to every public entity.

## 2. Actual Article 1 — two different contribution rates

### A. Wholly State/public-legal-person-owned companies — 5%

- Boards of companies whose capital is wholly owned by the State or public legal persons must set aside **5% of distributable net profits**.
- Applies regardless of the legal system under which the company operates, except companies established in implementation of international agreements.
- Calculation:
  1. cover accumulated losses first;
  2. before setting aside any reserves;
  3. set aside 5% of the remaining distributable net profits.
- Amount is treated as tax revenue and remitted to the State Treasury within four months after financial-year close.

### B. Majority State/public-legal-person participation — 4%

- For companies in which the State or public legal persons own **more than 50% of capital**, the board must collect/remit **4% of distributable net profits**.
- The amount may not exceed the State/public legal person's share in those profits.
- Calculation:
  1. after covering accumulated losses;
  2. before setting aside any reserves;
  3. deduct the contribution in full from the State/public legal person's share of net profits.
- Amount is treated as tax revenue and remitted to the State Treasury within four months after financial-year close.

### C. Temporary exemption

- For economic or social necessities required by the public interest, Cabinet may exempt specified companies from the Law for a temporary period by decision based on the Finance Minister's submission.
- The Law does not automatically exempt companies because they operate in a strategic sector or make losses; the Cabinet decision and exact scope are required.

## 4. Legal scope and calculation controls

- This Law applies to:
  - wholly State-owned companies;
  - wholly public-legal-person-owned companies;
  - companies in which State/public legal-person ownership exceeds 50%.
- It does not automatically apply to a company with a 50% or lower State/public shareholding unless another rule applies.
- The majority-participation limb is capped by the actual State/public share of distributable net profits.
- The 5% and 4% rates are not additive for the same company; classify the ownership structure and apply the appropriate limb.
- The contribution is calculated after accumulated losses and before reserves.
- The four-month remittance deadline runs from financial-year closing.
- A Cabinet exemption must be verified for the relevant company and period.

## 2. Article map

- **Article 1 — definitions:** NFSA, food imports, approved verification bodies, competent exporting-country authority, food safety/quality requirements, audit, conformity certification, hazard/risk analysis, traceability, withdrawal/recall and recognition memorandum.
- **Article 2:** NFSA conducts an equivalence assessment of the exporting country's competent food-control authority, in whole or in part, including controls that verify exports destined for Egypt.
- **Article 3:** NFSA and the exporting-country authority agree the scope, components and assessment methodology; the evaluation may be limited to particular foods or agricultural food products destined for Egypt.
- **Article 4:** parties may establish an evaluation framework, procedures, future steps and timetable, taking account of international best practices and Codex/FAO/WHO guidance.
- **Article 5:** NFSA may use interim measures for rapid assessment of an exporting authority's control system and update initial findings after detailed evaluation.
- **Article 6:** recognition memorandum contents include recognised control scope/products/geography, duration, evaluation procedures and information-exchange obligations, including electronic conformity/health certificates.
- **Article 7:** a recognised exporting-country competent authority becomes an approved verification body under the Decision 7/2020 risk-based import system; NFSA may accept its conformity certificates and must publish/update the list of recognised authorities.
- **Article 8:** NFSA may update its assessment approach and must notify trading partners and relevant stakeholders of changes.
- **Article 9:** NFSA may suspend/cancel a recognition memorandum after consultation; the decision requires notice and gives a two-month period before cancellation.
- **Article 10:** in exceptional circumstances, NFSA may temporarily suspend acceptance of conformity/health certificates issued by an exporting-country authority for a specified period and notify food-business operators.
- **Article 11:** the Board may amend or add annexes.
- **Article 12:** publication and commencement the day after publication; conflicting provisions are repealed.

## 3. Annex 1 — evaluation criteria

The annex sets criteria and illustrative performance indicators for evaluating an exporting country's food-control authority, including:

1. Availability and scientific basis of food-safety/quality standards and their relationship to Codex/international standards.
2. Preventive food-control rules, prerequisite programmes, HACCP and traceability.
3. Inspection capacity and risk-based enforcement.
4. Food incidents, outbreak response, withdrawal and recall effectiveness.
5. Organisational structure, clear roles and inter-agency cooperation.
6. Accredited laboratory infrastructure, analytical methods and competence.
7. Participation in international standard-setting and use of Codex standards.
8. Trained specialist workforce and scientific capability.
9. Continuous learning, training and improvement.

The annex describes evidence examples such as legal instruments, protocols, inspection reports, laboratory accreditation records, risk studies, recall reports, workforce plans and training records. These are evaluation indicators, not automatic conditions that can be inferred without a formal assessment.

## 4. Crosswalk and operational cautions

- Decision 10/2021 is the legal framework for NFSA recognition of exporting-country competent authorities and acceptance of certificates issued by recognised authorities.
- Decision 7/2020 supplies the risk-based import system into which recognised authorities are integrated.
- Decision 270/2024 expressly cites Decision 10/2021 as part of its import-control framework.
- A recognised competent authority is not the same as any private inspection/conformity company; the decision treats these as distinct approved-verification routes.
- The existence of the decision does not prove that a particular country, authority, product, facility or certificate is currently recognised. The current NFSA recognition list and memorandum scope must be checked.

# Collection checkpoint 12 — 2025–2026 digital regulation updates (2026-11-17)

## 1. Personal Data Protection — current regulatory status

### Law 151/2020
- Official Gazette PDF locator: https://escc.gov.eg/assets/docs/law-151-2020.pdf
- Gazette issue 28 bis (هـ), 15 July 2020.
- Search-indexed full promulgation text and opening provisions are visible.

### Executive Regulation Decision 816/2025
- Decision 816/2025 issues the Executive Regulation of Law 151/2020.
- Gazette publication metadata found: issue 244 supplement (A), 1 November 2025; commencement 2 November 2025.
- Secondary full-text records:
  - https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/tech-minister-decision/01-11-2025/no-816?id=5008447&type=1
  - https://masaar.net/ar/egypt_laws/اللائحة-التنفيذية-لقانون-حماية-البيانات-الشخصية/
- Official MCIT news on 4 November 2026 confirms the PDPC was discussing implementation of Law 151/2020 in accordance with its Executive Regulation and that the Regulation sets compliance requirements/procedures:
  https://mcit.gov.eg/ar/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/121612
- This supersedes old 2023/2024 government strategy material that said the Executive Regulation was still pending approval.
- The one-year adjustment period is commonly calculated from the Regulation's issuance/publication and appears to have elapsed around 1 November 2026. Do not turn this into a legal conclusion without checking the exact wording of Article 6, the Gazette publication date and any subsequent extensions/PDPC transitional guidance.

## 2. Electronic Signature Law 15/2004 — 2020 amendment to Executive Regulation

- ITIDA official announcement states that the Executive Regulation of Law 15/2004 was amended by Minister of Communications Decision 361/2020.
- Official ITIDA source: https://itida.gov.eg/Arabic/PressReleases/Pages/ITIDA-Issues-New-E-signature-Licenses-to-Accelerate-Digital-Transformation-in-Egypt.aspx
- Gazette/Executive Regulation amendment text locator:
  https://itida.gov.eg/English/Documents/3.pdf
- The 23 April 2020 Gazette text contains technical security requirements for the signature-creation system, including unique and confidential creation data, anti-forgery/manipulation controls, integrity of the signed document, signer awareness of document content and tamper detection.
- Link Decision 361/2020 to the original Executive Regulation and Law 15/2004; capture the full Arabic text and exact Gazette title/issue before consolidating.

## 4. Financial Technology Law 5/2022 — current FRA regulatory decision index

- Official FRA fintech legislation index:
  https://fra.gov.eg/تشريعات-تكنولوجيا-المالية/
- FRA Law 5/2022 page:
  https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قانون-رقم-5-لسنة-2022-2/
- The index currently exposes important later regulatory decisions, including:
  - FRA Chair Decision 2872/2026: registration in the registered email service (Bareedi).
  - FRA Board Decision 87/2026: warning/negative lists and administrative measures.
  - FRA Board Decision 43/2026: suspension of accepting establishment/licensing applications for consumer-finance activity under the fintech-law route.
  - FRA Board Decision 40/2026: investor identification number controls.
  - FRA Board Decision 35/2026: mandatory insurance covering liability risks of boards and executive directors of licensed non-bank financial companies.
  - FRA Board Decision 186/2024, updated 2026: obligation to inquire into customer-data accuracy.
  - FRA Board Decision 140/2023, updated 2025: digital identity, digital contracts, digital registry, fintech use and compliance requirements.
  - FRA Board Decision 57/2024: robo-advisor investment rules.
- These are decision-index leads. Each must be captured as its own instrument, with exact Arabic text, issue/publication, effective date, amendments, affected activities and repeal/status flags.

## 5. Consumer Finance Law 18/2020 — 2026 regulatory guide

- Official FRA updated law page: https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قانون-رقم-18-لسنه-2020-باصدار-قانون-تنظيم-نشا/
- Official Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/UG50279UG50280.pdf
- FRA reported on 5 September 2026 that it issued a comprehensive guide consolidating licensing, governance, internal controls, solvency, financial statements, model contracts and branch registration rules under Law 18/2020 and its implementing decisions/circulars:
  https://fra.gov.eg/fra_news/الرقابة-المالية-تصدر-أول-دليل-شامل-لق/
- Treat the guide as regulator guidance, not a statute or executive regulation. Preserve its date/version and link each cited binding decision separately.

## 6. Research implications

- Digital legal corpus now needs three distinct layers: (a) statutes and executive regulations; (b) binding regulator decisions/circulars; (c) explanatory guides, FAQs and policy statements.
- The project should not rely on 2023–2024 strategy reports for current implementation status where later 2025–2026 legal/regulator sources exist.
- For Legal Copilot, Law 151/2020 and Decision 816/2025 are immediate privacy compliance priorities; Law 175/2018 and its executive regulation are relevant to cybersecurity and incident handling; Law 15/2004 and Decision 361/2020 are relevant to electronic execution and evidentiary provenance.

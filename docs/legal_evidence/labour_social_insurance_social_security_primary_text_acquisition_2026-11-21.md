## 1. New acquisition results

### 1.1 Law 148/2019 — complete Official Gazette text located

- Instrument: قانون رقم ١٤٨ لسنة ٢٠١٩ بإصدار قانون التأمينات الاجتماعية والمعاشات.
- Gazette: الجريدة الرسمية، العدد ٣٣ مكرر (أ)، ١٩ أغسطس ٢٠١٩.
- Primary PDF: https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/2076_aad99154bd.pdf
- Text extraction: 4,470 lines; statute and promulgation provisions are text-searchable. The first operative provisions appear at Gazette printed pp. 2–4; Article 19 begins around printed p. 22; work-injury provisions Articles 47–50 are around printed pp. 40–42; unemployment benefits Articles 88–91 are around printed pp. 56–57. Confirm exact page for each article from the PDF page image before final source matching.
- Secondary official catalogue: Ministry of Finance labels Law 148/2019 “in force” and lists PM Decisions 2165/2019 and 1218/2020 under Article 111: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-5fe868b37bfc600007cbec80
- Important: Law 148/2019 is not the Social Security Law 12/2025. Keep contributory insurance/pensions and non-contributory social assistance as separate instruments, indexes and entitlement tracks.

**Exact Arabic source excerpt — Article 19 (primary Gazette PDF; short excerpt):**

> الحصة التي يلتزم بها صاحب العمل بواقع (١٣٪) من أجور المؤمن عليهم العاملين لديه شهرياً.

The same Article 19 provides a 9% insured-person share for the covered employee category. Do not hardcode a current contribution calculation without checking the full Article 19 category structure, later amendments and current insured-wage base.

**Exact Arabic source excerpt — Article 49 (primary Gazette PDF; short excerpt):**

> تؤدي الجهة المختصة بصرف تعويض الأجر ... تعويضاً عن أجره يعادل كامل أجره المسدد عنه الاشتراك.

Article 49 governs wage compensation where a work injury prevents the insured person from performing work. Adjacent Articles 47–50 address medical care, wage compensation and transport. Cross-map to Labour Law OSH reporting and the 2026 OSH decisions, but do not conflate employer liability, insurance benefits and civil compensation.

### 1.2 Executive Regulations of Law 148/2019 — complete Gazette text located

- Instrument: قرار رئيس مجلس الوزراء رقم ٢٤٣٧ لسنة ٢٠٢١ بإصدار اللائحة التنفيذية لقانون التأمينات الاجتماعية والمعاشات.
- Gazette heading in the AUN-hosted Gazette copy: العدد ٣٨ مكرر (ب)، ٢٨ سبتمبر ٢٠٢١.
- Primary-source access (official regulator / FRA-hosted PDF): https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/لائحة-التنفيذية148-2019.pdf
- Additional official-hosted copy on Ministry of Labour domain: https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/fy4heqld/اللائحة-التنفيذية-لقانون-التأمينات-والمعاشات-رقم-148-لسنه-2019.pdf
- Alternate institutional copy: https://b.aun.edu.eg/administrations/sites/default/files/pdf/fgaa/law104.pdf
- Extraction: FRA PDF exposes 10,980 text lines and full regulation body/annex references. This is a substantial full-text acquisition, not merely a catalogue reference.
- Metadata QA: one FRA PDF rendering contains inconsistent internal Gazette header strings (the first header identifies issue 38 bis (B), while later extracted running headers show issue 36 bis (B) and a 21 September date). The AUN-hosted PDF and Ministry-hosted PDF identify issue 38 bis (B), 28 September 2021. Use issue 38 bis (B), 28 September 2021 provisionally, but visually inspect the original cover and relevant article pages and record the conflicting running-header text. Do not silently normalize the inconsistent PDF metadata.

**Exact Arabic source excerpt — Executive Regulation Article 17 (official FRA-hosted PDF; short excerpt):**

> يلتزم صاحب العمل أن يتقدم إلى الهيئة خلال أسبوعين من تاريخ بدء النشاط بطلب الاشتراك

Article 17 also requires supporting documents and prescribed forms. Article 18 requires annual employee/wage updates and monthly electronic data. Article 19 addresses reporting employee commencement and related changes. Use the full official PDF to create the complete article-to-page index and annex/form inventory.

**High-value coverage found in the full regulation:**
- Articles 1–8: definitions, covered categories (employees, employers, Egyptians abroad, irregular workers) and insured branches (old age/disability/death, work injury, sickness, unemployment).
- Articles 9–19: insured-person file, employer registration, commencement notifications and employee/wage data.
- Article 16/17 employer registration procedures and two-week commencement window.
- Article 145 is expressly referenced in the definition of work injury for injury caused by exertion/fatigue; extract and map its conditions in the full regulation.
- The regulation contains detailed tables, forms and schedules. Article-only text extraction is not sufficient; annexes and forms must be indexed as separate source objects.

### 1.3 New amendment: Law 11/2026 amending Article 111 of Law 148/2019

This is a material post-2024 amendment that must be present in the consolidated Law 148 record.

- Instrument: قانون رقم ١١ لسنة ٢٠٢٦ بتعديل بعض أحكام قانون التأمينات الاجتماعية والمعاشات الصادر بالقانون رقم ١٤٨ لسنة ٢٠١٩.
- Gazette: الجريدة الرسمية، العدد ٢٣ تابع، ٤ يونيو ٢٠٢٦; Article 2 states it takes effect the day after publication (5 June 2026).
- Official Amiri Press indexed text: https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=s6iZUpH%2FRi4%3D (search-indexed text available; direct open intermittently returned 502/timeout).
- Official SIS report reproducing the full operative text: https://sis.gov.eg/ar/الرئاسة/شئون-داخلية/القرارات-الرئاسية/الرئيس-السيسي-يصدق-على-تعديل-بعض-أحكام-قانون-التأمينات-الاجتماعية-والمعاشات/
- Official Gazette repository record: https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/93ae6816-fc86-4f2a-b91c-38fa25f2ce15 (issue 23 supplement, Amiri Press, 4 June 2026).
- The Arabic Gazette PDF is listed in the repository as “العدد 23 تابع - مؤمن.pdf”, 215.47 KB. Acquire the original PDF and compare every line/amount with the Amiri Press/SIS text before upgrading.
- Secondary complete transcription and article text: https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/06/11-2026-148-2019.html
- DSpace law record (Arabic and English PDF listed): https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/05a0bd87-fdb3-49a3-b692-fe4ed41bf966

**Exact Arabic amendment excerpt — Article 1 (Amiri Press/SIS text; short excerpt):**

> يُستبدل بنص المادة (۱۱۱) من قانون التأمينات الاجتماعية والمعاشات ... النص الآتي

The replacement Article 111 sets the annual Treasury instalment at EGP 238.55 billion for FY 2025/2026, a compound 6.4% increase from 1 July 2026, an additional 0.2 percentage-point annual step from 1 July 2027 until 7% from 1 July 2029, and an additional EGP 1 billion annually for five years from 1 July 2026. It specifies an overall 50-year payment period and enumerates the liabilities/financial items borne by the Social Insurance Fund. Article 111 also excludes specified later exceptional pensions and new benefits from the annual instalment. Preserve all numbered items and the distinction between the annual instalment and the Fund’s assumption of listed liabilities.

### 1.6 Current insured-wage and pension thresholds for 2026

- NOSI official announcement dated 30 November 2025: https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2025-11-30.aspx
- Effective date: 1 January 2026.
- Minimum insured wage: EGP 2,700 (previously EGP 2,300).
- Maximum insured wage: EGP 16,700 (previously EGP 14,500).
- Minimum new pension: EGP 1,755 (previously EGP 1,495).
- Maximum new pension: EGP 13,360 (previously EGP 11,600).
- The NOSI announcement says employers must insure the actual wage; the minimum/maximum insured wage is particularly relevant to categories where actual wage is difficult to determine. Store these as dated annual parameters, not static legal text. No later 2026 replacement parameter was located in this pass.

### 1.7 Social Security Law 12/2025 — original Gazette PDF acquired

- Instrument: قانون رقم ١٢ لسنة ٢٠٢٥ بإصدار قانون الضمان الاجتماعي.
- Gazette: الجريدة الرسمية، العدد ١٤ تابع، ٣ أبريل ٢٠٢٥.
- Primary PDF hosted by Ministry of Finance: https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/qanwn_aldman_alajtmaey_2025_12_22544853c7.pdf
- Official Amiri Press indexed text: https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=JSYCcY13vvQ%3D
- PDF extraction: 484 text lines; the complete promulgation provisions and 43 numbered law articles are visible, with some OCR/article-number duplication anomalies that require image QA.
- Gazette printed pages: issuance provisions pp. 2–3; attached Law begins p. 4 and continues through p. 24.
- Article 4 of the promulgating provisions repeals Law 137/2010 and Child Law Article 49, but preserves existing beneficiaries’ benefits while they regularize under the new law and its executive regulation within one year, extendable once for a similar period by Cabinet decision.
- Article 5 requires the executive regulation within six months; until then, non-conflicting existing regulations/decisions continue.
- Article 6 provides commencement on the day after three months have elapsed from publication.

**Exact Arabic source excerpt — Article 2 (primary Gazette PDF; short excerpt):**

> يكون لكل مواطن تحت خط الفقر القومي، ولا يتمتع بنظام التأمين الاجتماعي، الحق في التقدم للحصول على الدعم النقدي

This law concerns non-contributory social assistance (Takaful/Karama and related supports); it must not be merged with Law 148/2019 contributory insurance and pensions.

**Exact Arabic excerpt — Regulation Article 33 (secondary transcription; short excerpt):**

> تُحدد فئات الرسم المقررة للتظلم على النحو الآتي

Article 33 specifies appeal/grievance fee bands of 1%, 1.5% and 2% depending on the type of support decision challenged. This is a substantive implementation detail; verify against Gazette PDF before production use.

**Exact Arabic excerpt — Regulation Article 49 (secondary transcription; short excerpt):**

Articles 47–53 establish training, employment placement, productive assets, microenterprise loans and collective productive projects for beneficiaries capable of work. This creates an operational link to the Ministry of Labour, but it is not an amendment to Labour Law 14/2025.

## 2. Additional labour decision source upgrade — Decision 289/2025

- Instrument: قرار وزير العمل رقم ٢٨٩ لسنة ٢٠٢٥ بشأن تحديد ساعات العمل في المنشآت الصناعية.
- Signed date shown in Ministry PDF: 27 December 2025.
- Official SIS-hosted Ministry PDF: https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/117208/MOM20251227_0415.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=4
- SIS page reproduces the full two-article decision text; the page itself is a Ministry of Labour publication, not the Gazette issue scan. Existing harvest identifies Gazette issue 295 supplement (D), 30 December 2025, as publication lead. Obtain the actual Gazette page to match it.
- Article 1 sets the industrial-establishment baseline at eight actual hours/day or 48/week, excluding meal/rest periods, while preserving better terms in individual/collective contracts or workplace regulations; unusual/exceptional overtime is subject to Labour Law Article 121.
- Article 2 states publication in Al-Waqa’i and commencement the day after publication.

**Exact Arabic excerpt — Article 1 (official Ministry PDF; short excerpt):**

> لا يجوز تشغيل العامل فعليًا في المنشآت الصناعية أكثر من ثماني ساعات في اليوم، أو ثمان وأربعين ساعة في الأسبوع

This is a source upgrade to an instrument already indexed in the November 17 labour harvest; do not create a duplicate Decision 289 record. Attach this official Ministry text source to the existing record.

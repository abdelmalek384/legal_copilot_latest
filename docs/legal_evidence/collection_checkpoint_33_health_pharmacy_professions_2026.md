# Collection checkpoint 33 — pharmacy, medical facilities, blood services and health professions (2026-11-17)

## 1. Pharmacy Practice Law 127/1955 — 2025 amendment

- Base Law 127/1955 regulates the practice of the pharmacy profession.
- Amendment Law 90/2025:
  - Official Gazette repository record: https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/7b499646-9853-4e01-a7c9-9af520427f72
  - Gazette issue 25 bis (هـ), 24 June 2025.
  - Secondary full text: https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=14336
  - Secondary text and Gazette details: https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/24-06-2025/no-90?id=2169442&type=1
- Law 90/2025 replaces Articles 2, 3, 5, first paragraph of 19 and Article 37; repeals Articles 4, 24 and 87.
- The amended Article 2 requires a pharmacy bachelor's degree or equivalent, plus one year of compulsory training in an approved university hospital, hospital, pharmacy institution or training unit under specified academic/health supervision.
- The amendment includes training and licensing/registration provisions and connects licensing to the Egyptian Health Council Law 12/2022.
- Do not use the original 1955 text alone for registration, training, pharmacy ownership/management or dispensing rules.
- Obtain official Gazette bytes and full text for Law 90/2025, then update the affected article versions and repeal graph.

## 2. Private Medical Facilities Law 51/1981

- Ministry of Health and Population official investment guide lists Law 51/1981 as the governing statute for private medical facilities, amended by Law 153/2004.
- Official Ministry PDF guide:
  https://www.mohp.gov.eg/UserFiles/LibraryFiles/709026.pdf
- Executive Regulation: Minister of Health Decision 216/1982.
- The guide also lists Ministerial Decision 439/2024 reconstituting the permanent committee for applications to establish medical facilities and defining its competences.
- Obtain base Law 51/1981, amendment Law 153/2004, Executive Regulation 216/1982, later amendments, facility licensing conditions, inspection and closure decisions.

## 3. Medical and allied-health professional statutes

The official Ministry of Health guide lists the following foundational statutes:
- Medical Practice Law 415/1954.
- Dentistry Practice Law 537/1954.
- Physiotherapy Practice Law 3/1985 and Executive Regulation Minister of Health Decision 150/1986.
- Pharmacy Practice Law 127/1955, as amended by Law 90/2025.
- Law 367/1954 regulating professions of medical chemistry, bacteriology and biology and medical diagnostic/research laboratories and biological-product laboratories.
- Obtain original Gazette texts, amendments, professional registration rules, syndicate requirements, licensing/discipline decisions and current Egyptian Health Council requirements.

## 4. Blood and plasma services — Law 8/2021

- The Ministry of Health guide identifies Law 8/2021 regulating blood operations and plasma centres.
- It is a separate special law and must be linked to the private medical facilities framework, medical professions, medicines/pharmaceutical regulation, health insurance and public health.
- Obtain Law 8/2021 official Gazette text, Executive Regulation and Ministry of Health/Health Authority decisions governing blood banks, plasma centres, donor eligibility, collection, testing, processing, storage, transport, quality and licensing.

## 5. Mental Health Care Law 71/2009 and amendment 210/2020

- Retrieve the full base law, amendment and Executive Regulation, plus National Council for Mental Health decisions and patient-rights/consent/compulsory-admission rules.
- Keep clinical standards and Ministry guidance separate from statutory provisions.

## 6. Health insurance and Universal Health Insurance

- Universal Health Insurance Law 2/2018 is already identified in checkpoint 4.
- PM Decision 725/2025 exempts the General Authority for Healthcare from PM Decision 1804/2022 concerning extensions of secondments and unpaid overseas leave:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=ah%2F3uqNCsEM%3D
- The decision cites Universal Health Insurance Law 2/2018 and Civil Service Law 81/2016; it is an administrative employment exception, not a substantive amendment to the health-insurance law.
- Continue collecting the Law 2/2018 Executive Regulation, phased implementation decisions, contributions/financing schedules, provider accreditation, claims and complaints rules, and 2025–2026 amendments.

## 7. Additional Egyptian health regulator sources

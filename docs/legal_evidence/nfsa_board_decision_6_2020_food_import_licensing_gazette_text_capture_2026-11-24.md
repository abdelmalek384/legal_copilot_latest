# NFSA Board Decision 6/2020 — food-import licensing rules

**Acquisition date:** 24 November 2026  
**Instrument:** NFSA Board Decision No. 6 of 2020 on rules regulating the licensing/registration of food imports  
**Text date shown in source:** 2020; board approval 25 June 2020

## 2. Article map and searchable topics

- **Article 1:** definitions for NFSA, food, food-contact material, food handling, food establishments/operators, food imports and importers.
- **Article 2:** purpose and scope — importer licensing for food imported for commercial use/placement on the Egyptian market.
- **Article 3:** prohibits import activity without an NFSA importer licence; existing importers must apply within the transitional period stated in the text; registered food manufacturers receive the specified treatment for inputs imported for their own production/service activity.
- **Article 4:** licence application and documentary requirements, including tax and commercial registration, company/legal documents, activities, Egyptian establishment address, contact details, customs number and importer history.
- **Article 5:** importer obligations concerning affiliated establishments, changes/closures, food-chain records, quality management, storage/transport/distribution, foreign-supplier preventive controls, traceability and recall.
- **Article 6:** NFSA document review and licensing workflow, including a 30-working-day review period, register publication, reasoned refusal and corrective-action route.
- **Article 7:** annual licence fees and service fees. The reproduced Arabic table states EGP 2,000 for importer licensing and EGP 5,000 for NFSA-registered food-manufacturing establishments; additional service amounts include EGP 1,000 for data amendment, EGP 500 for replacement copy and EGP 200 for a licence copy. These are historical decision text amounts, not confirmed current fees.
- **Article 8:** suspension/revocation grounds and grievance route.
- **Article 9:** authority to amend licence conditions following Board review and issue updates.
- **Article 10:** confidentiality of pricing, invoice and customer data.
- **Article 11:** commencement on the day after publication.

## 3. Crosswalk

- Read with Law 1/2017 and PM Decision 412/2019.
- Decision 6/2020 is the importer licensing instrument; it should not be conflated with Decision 7/2020, which establishes the risk-based import control system.
- Decision 7/2020, Decision 8/2020, Decision 9/2020, later fee decisions and the storage-facility Decision 4/2022 are related instruments and require separate version/currentness treatment.
- The Trade Portal lists Decision 6/2020 among the legal bases for food-import procedures, but this does not establish that its original fee schedule or transitional deadlines remain current.

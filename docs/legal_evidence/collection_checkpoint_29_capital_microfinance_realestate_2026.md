# Collection checkpoint 29 — capital markets, microfinance and real-estate finance statutes (2026-11-17)

## 1. Capital Market Law 95/1992 — official FRA updated text

- Official FRA updated-law page: https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قانون-سوق-رأس-المال-رقم-95-لسنة-1992/
- Official updated Arabic PDF/Word source:
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/قانون-سوق-المال-بصيغة-WORD-4.pdf
- FRA page identifies the version as updated in 2022.
- The PDF search text identifies Law 95/1992 dated 25 June 1992.
- Promulgation Article 1 applies the attached law to regulation of the capital market and applies Companies Law 159/1981 where the Capital Market Law has no special provision.
- Promulgation Article 2 assigns references to the Authority/administrative authority in the law and its Executive Regulation to the Financial Regulatory Authority, and references to the Chair/Minister to the relevant FRA Chair/competent minister.
- FRA lists amendment history:
  - Law 10/1995.
  - Law 89/1996.
  - Law 158/1998.
  - Law 13/2004.
  - Law 143/2004.
  - Law 123/2008.
  - Law 27/2014.
  - Law 17/2018.
  - Law 13/2022.
- The updated text must be reconciled against each amendment Gazette and any subsequent 2023–2026 amendments before claiming currentness.

## 3. Microfinance Law 141/2014 — official FRA updated text

- Official FRA updated page: https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قانون-رقم-141-لسنة-2014/
- Official PDF:
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/قانون-رقم-141-لسنة-2014-بشأن-تنظيم-نشاط-التمويل-متناهي-الصغر.pdf
- FRA labels updated version as 2022.
- Amendment history:
  - Law 201/2020.
  - Law 155/2022.
- The updated Article 1 states that the law regulates financing of micro, small and medium enterprises and that the activity is treated as a non-bank financial instrument under FRA Law 10/2009.
- It preserves the relationship with the Central Bank and Banking Sector Law and MSME Development Law 152/2020.
- The original 2014 microfinance-only version and the amended 2020/2022 MSME financing version must be kept as separate historical versions, not mixed.
- FRA official explanatory page:
  https://fra.gov.eg/ufaq/الاطار-القانونى-لتمويل-المشروعات-المتوسطة-والصغيرة-ومتناهية-الصغر/
  identifies Law 141/2014 and amendment Law 201/2020 as the legal framework for financing MSMEs in the non-bank financial sector.

## 4. Real Estate Finance Law 148/2001 and Executive Regulation

- Executive Regulation: PM Decision 1/2001.
- Official FRA-hosted PDF:
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/اللائحة-التنفيذية-للقانون-التمويل-العقاري.pdf
- Searchable Article 1 provides that real-estate finance may cover residential and administrative units, service facilities and commercial premises, including purchase, construction, restoration or improvement of real property and lease-to-own structures.
- Obtain base Law 148/2001, amendments and all subsequent regulatory decisions; do not treat the Executive Regulation alone as the complete law.
- Link with Civil Code, real-estate registration, mortgage/security, consumer finance, banking, property tax and building/land legislation.

# FRA Board Decision 213/2026 — extension of traditional-channel consumer-finance and microfinance licensing freeze

**Instrument:** قرار مجلس إدارة الهيئة العامة للرقابة المالية رقم (213) لسنة 2026.  
**Subject:** تمديد وقف قبول طلبات التأسيس والموافقة المبدئية على التأسيس والترخيص بمزاولة نشاطي التمويل الاستهلاكي وتمويل المشروعات متناهية الصغر، وكذلك طلبات الجمعيات والمؤسسات الأهلية لمزاولة تمويل المشروعات متناهية الصغر.

## Operative effect confirmed by FRA

- Extends the freeze on new incorporation applications, preliminary incorporation approvals and operating-license applications for companies in **consumer finance** and **microfinance** through **11 October 2027**.
- Extends the freeze on applications by **NGOs and civil foundations** seeking to conduct microfinance through the same date.
- Continues the traditional-channel suspension first introduced by FRA Board Decision 184/2024.
- This is separate from Decision 43/2026, which concerns fintech-based consumer-finance licensing applications and remains in force through 25 February 2027, subject to renewal.

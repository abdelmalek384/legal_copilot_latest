## 1. Promulgation provisions (lines 13–28)

> المادة الأولى: يعمل بأحكام القانون المرافق في شأن الموارد المائية والري.
>
> المادة الثانية: يلغى القانون رقم 12 لسنة 1984 في شأن الري والصرف، كما يلغى كل حكم يخالف أحكام هذا القانون.
>
> المادة الثالثة: يصدر رئيس مجلس الوزراء، بعد العرض من وزير الموارد المائية والري والزراعة واستصلاح الأراضي، اللائحة التنفيذية لهذا القانون المرافق خلال ستة أشهر من تاريخ العمل بهذا القانون، وإلى أن تصدر هذه اللائحة يستمر العمل باللوائح والقرارات القائمة في تاريخ العمل بهذا القانون والقانون المرافق وبما لا يتعارض مع أحكامه.
>
> المادة الرابعة: ينشر هذا القانون في الجريدة الرسمية، ويعمل به من اليوم التالي لتاريخ نشره.

## 2. Article 1 — definitions (lines 44–53 and 95–180)

The extracted definition list begins with:
- الوزارة: ministry responsible for water resources and irrigation.
- الوزير: competent minister.
- الإدارة العامة المختصة: competent General Directorate connected with water resources and facilities.
- المدير العام المختص: head of the competent General Directorate.
- المهندس المختص: engineer granted judicial-officer status for implementing the Law within the scope of competence.
- رابطة مستخدمي المياه: entity grouping water users and beneficiaries of public/private irrigation/drainage networks and systems, groundwater wells, dams and reservoirs.

Further definitions include:
- Nile River course, maintenance/embankment lines, cross-section, water-sector area, prohibited area, restricted area, Nile islands, Nile alluvium lands, river embankments, shoreline, coastal prohibition line, flash-flood channels, natural valleys and protection works.
- The statutory zones include specified distances/contour lines around the Nile, Lake Nasser, Toshka spillway/depressions and major barrages. Exact distances and elevations must be captured from the original page and stored as structured spatial rules, not inferred from the text summary.

## 3. Articles 5–12 — public water-resource property and licensed works (lines 227–266)

### Article 5
> تختص الوزارة دون غيرها في إطار أحكام هذا القانون بالإشراف على جميع الأعمال أو غيرها من الأنشطة التي تقوم بها الجهات المعنية على الأملاك العامة ذات الصلة بالموارد المائية والري، ومع ذلك يجوز للوزارة أن تعهد بالإشراف على أي جزء من هذه الأملاك إلى إحدى الوزارات أو المصالح العامة أو جهات الإدارة المحلية أو الهيئات العامة أو الوحدات التابعة لها.

### Article 6
- State liability is limited for damage to lands/structures within public water-resource property where damage results from emergency water-level changes, irrigation/drainage balancing works, sea-level rise or storms, provided the Ministry took necessary preventive measures; Civil Code liability rules remain relevant.

### Article 7
> لا يجوز زراعة الأراضي المملوكة للدولة والواقعة داخل الأملاك العامة ذات الصلة بالموارد المائية والري أو استخدامها لأي غرض إلا بترخيص من الإدارة العامة المختصة، وطبقًا للشروط التي تحددها اللائحة التنفيذية لهذا القانون.

### Article 8
- Cutting/removing trees or palms planted on public water-resource property requires a licence from the competent General Directorate.

### Article 9
> لا يجوز إجراء أي عمل خاص داخل حدود الأملاك العامة ذات الصلة بالموارد المائية والري أو إحداث تعديل أو ترميم فيها إلا بترخيص من الوزارة لمدة لا تزيد على عشر سنوات قابلة للتجديد بعد أداء رسم لا يجاوز مقداره خمسمائة ألف جنيه...

- Unlicensed works/structures are subject to administrative removal.

### Article 10
- Licence may require works to become public property at expiry without compensation.
- If the licence is not renewed and the Ministry does not absorb the work into public property, the licensee must remove it and restore the public property; otherwise the Ministry may do so at the licensee's expense.

### Article 11
- A licence for irrigation/drainage works may require the licensee to allow other owners/holders/tenants to benefit after payment of their proportionate share of construction costs.
- The licence records the area benefiting; benefit continues despite changes in ownership/possession/tenancy.

### Article 12
- Licensee must maintain the licensed works, carry out required repairs/modifications, permit inspection at any time and obtain prior Ministry approval for changes.
- Multiple licensees are jointly liable for contractual obligations.

## 4. Articles 51–57 — water councils and irrigation/drainage modernization (lines 473–514)

### Article 51
- PM decision may create national, regional and local councils with ministry, water-user association and relevant-agency representation to coordinate/consult on management and use of water resources.

### Article 52
- The Ministry's Permanent Undersecretary may order conversion of private irrigation channels to developed channels based on technical field and social studies; the decision binds all beneficiaries, and actual costs are recovered under Article 57.

### Article 53
- The Irrigation and Watercourse Development and Maintenance Fund succeeds the former fund established under Law 213/1994, assuming its rights/assets.
- Its purposes include financing development projects, raising awareness, research and supporting water-user associations.
- Fund resources include State budget allocations, loans, grants, beneficiary instalments and investment returns; its money is public money.

### Article 54
- No connection/tapping into developed irrigation channels or modification of their components without competent Directorate approval.
- The Executive Regulation governs operation, management and farmer use of modern irrigation systems in old agricultural lands.

### Article 55
- Ministry prepares plans to improve irrigation/drainage systems and may take expropriation steps for land needed for open/covered irrigation/drainage networks under Expropriation Law 10/1990.

### Article 56
- Ministry constructs/replaces/renews covered or open field drainage networks; costs are distributed across lands in the drainage unit by area share.
- The competent Directorate bears periodic maintenance costs for covered drains; other costs are borne by the land holder/farmer.

### Article 57
- Ministry prepares actual-cost statements for construction/replacement/renewal of covered drains, open field drains and developed irrigation channels, including compensation, plus 10% administrative expenses.
- Landowners bear the relevant per-feddan cost.

## 5. Articles 68–70 — groundwater wells (lines 597–620)

### Article 68
- A higher committee reviews applications for groundwater well licences and legalization of some wells; PM decision establishes its composition, work and powers, and recommendations are submitted to the Minister for approval.
- Ministry studies groundwater sources, develops use policy and monitors quantity/quality.

### Article 69
> يصدر الترخيص بحفر البئر الجوفية وتحديد مواصفاتها وإحلال غيرها بدلاً منها بقرار من الإدارة العامة المختصة بناءً على توصية من اللجنة العليا للتراخيص، على أن تكون مدة الترخيص خمس سنوات قابلة للتجديد...

- The extracted text states a licence period of five years, renewable, with a statutory nominal fee cap; the Executive Regulation determines conditions and fee categories.
- Certain wells used for supplementary irrigation in old Valley/Delta lands are exempt from the fee as stated.

### Article 70
> يحظر حفر أية آبار للمياه الجوفية داخل أراضي الجمهورية إلا بترخيص من الوزارة وطبقًا للشروط التي تحددها.
>
> ويحظر على القائمين بحفر الآبار الجوفية من المقاولين والشركات والأفراد التعاقد على حفر أية آبار ما لم تكن حاصلة على ترخيص بذلك من الوزارة...

- Article 70 prohibits unlicensed groundwater drilling and restricts contractors/companies/individuals from contracting to drill unless licensed by the Ministry, with coordination to Desert Lands Law 143/1981.
- Article 107 was replaced by Law 167/2025 for penalties concerning the first and second paragraphs of Article 70; see checkpoint 21.

## 6. Articles 96–109 — flood protection and penalties (lines 746–800)

- Article 96 requires each governorate, coordinating with other competent authorities, to plan/protect lives, infrastructure and public/private facilities from rain and flash-flood risks, and submit technical studies/protection plans to the Ministry for approval.
- Article 97 authorizes the competent General Director to order removal of unlicensed works or suspension of activity affecting flood channels/protection works/drainage of rain and floodwater.
- Article 98 introduces the penalty chapter, without prejudice to restoration at the violator's expense or any harsher penalty in another law.
- Articles 99–106 establish fines and imprisonment/fine combinations for specified violations; amounts and cross-references must be checked against the original PDF.
- Original Article 107 in the 2021 PDF imposed a fine for violations of specified paragraphs of Articles 32 and 70 and Articles 76, 78, 90 and 94. This text was replaced by Law 167/2025; do not use the original penalty text as current.
- Articles 108–109 provide additional fines/imprisonment for specified violations, including Article 7 and Article 59.

# Collection checkpoint 24 — migration, cybercrime enforcement and AML procedural instruments (2026-11-17)

## 1. Illegal Migration and Smuggling of Migrants Law 82/2016 — 2026 implementation update

- Law 82/2016 regulates combating illegal migration and smuggling of migrants.
- A new PM Decision 2528/2026 amends PM Decision 369/2023 regulating the Fund for Combating Illegal Migration and Protecting Migrants and Witnesses:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=iTpXFlSwW+U%3D
- Decision 2528/2026 replaces the text of first paragraph of Article 5(First) and first paragraph of Article 7 of Decision 369/2023.
- The preamble cites Law 82/2016 and Presidential Decision 75/2026 amending the government formation.
- This is an up-to-date implementation/administrative instrument. Obtain the full Law 82/2016, PM Decision 369/2023 and amendment 2528/2026, preserving the Fund's statutory remit and the changes to its composition/administration.
- Do not infer the content of replaced provisions from the preamble; capture the full text and compare the old/new wording.

## 2. Cybercrime Law 175/2018 — judicial-officer authorizations

- Justice Minister Decision 8256/2022:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Lqype2ZkKCM%3D
- The decision grants named Ministry of Communications and Information Technology personnel judicial-officer status for offences under:
  - Telecommunications Regulation Law 10/2003.
  - Cybercrime Law 175/2018.
- It distinguishes named personnel and roles for telecom violations from those for cybercrime offences, including personnel in cybersecurity incident response, digital forensics, malware analysis, penetration testing and network security.
- It renews specified judicial-officer status and is linked to earlier Decisions 2888/2022 and others.
- This is a personnel/competence instrument, not a substantive amendment to Law 175/2018. Capture validity, scope, named roles and any superseding decisions before relying on it.

## 3. Tax, customs and AML prosecution structure

- Justice Minister Decision 6261/2022:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=n4Ml1NPJf24%3D
- Renames the "Tax Evasion Prosecution" to the "Tax and Customs Affairs Prosecution", with the new title replacing the old title wherever it appears.
- Its legal basis cross-references:
  - Stamp Tax Law 111/1980.
  - Law 80/2002 on Anti-Money Laundering.
  - Income Tax Law 91/2005.
  - Economic Courts Law 120/2008.
  - Real Estate Tax Law 196/2008.
  - VAT Law 67/2016.
  - Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020.
  - Customs Law 207/2020.
- This decision is a judicial-organization/competence record and does not amend the cited substantive laws.

## 4. Anti-Money Laundering Law 80/2002 — acquisition lead

- Law 80/2002 is cited by Justice Decision 6261/2022 and is a core source for money laundering offences, reporting entities, suspicious transaction reporting and competent authorities.
- Secondary PDF lead previously located:
  https://manshurat.org/node/13878
- Official primary-source collection remains open: obtain original Gazette Law 80/2002, amendments (including later changes), Executive Regulation, AML Unit decisions, CBE/FRA regulatory rules, sanctions and current reporting forms.
- Keep criminal offences and penalties separate from regulator AML/CFT compliance rules and administrative circulars.

## 5. Human trafficking — acquisition lead

- Anti-Human Trafficking Law 64/2010 is cited by Ministry of Social Solidarity as a statute under which specified Ministry staff have judicial-officer status.
- It is a distinct statute from Illegal Migration and Smuggling of Migrants Law 82/2016.
- Obtain Law 64/2010 original Gazette, Executive Regulation, national referral mechanisms, victim protection/assistance decisions, National Coordinating Committee instruments and relevant international treaties.

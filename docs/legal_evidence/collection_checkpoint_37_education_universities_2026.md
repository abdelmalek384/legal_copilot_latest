# Collection checkpoint 37 — Education Law, teacher regulation and university legislation (2026-11-17)

## 1. Education Law 139/1981 — official Ministry of Education full text

- Official Ministry of Education PDF:
  https://moe.gov.eg/media/l3hnjmv4/educationactno139of1981.pdf
- The Ministry-hosted PDF exposes the full Arabic text of Law 139/1981 issuing the Education Law.
- Promulgation Article 1 applies the attached Education Law.
- Article 2 authorizes the Minister of Education to issue decisions implementing the law and transitional rules for curricula, study systems and examinations.
- Promulgation Article 3 repeals Laws 68/1968 (general education), 16/1969 (private education) and 75/1970 (technical education), plus inconsistent provisions.
- Signed 9 August 1981; commencement the day after publication.
- The full base text must be extracted and compared against all amendments; this is not a current consolidated version.

## 2. Teacher professional status — Law 155/2007, Law 93/2012 and regulations

- Law 155/2007 amends Education Law 139/1981 by adding Book/Chapter Seven on teaching personnel:
  https://moe.gov.eg/media/vwfdwsqq/155q.pdf
- It applies to teachers, technical supervisors, school administration and specified social/psychological/technology/media/library specialists.
- Executive Regulation of the seventh chapter: PM Decision 428/2013, Gazette issue 97 (supplement), 27 April 2013:
  https://moe.gov.eg/media/lzahpwbn/5.pdf
- Decision 428/2013 repeals the earlier PM Decision 2840/2007 regulation for the seventh chapter.
- PM Decision 2297/2022 amends Decision 428/2013:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=DQdKoo+SwXU%3D
- It replaces several provisions (Articles 4, 6, 10 and 12) and updates references to the Government Jobs Portal, appointment/contracting and teacher/specialist recruitment processes.
- Law 93/2012 is an additional amendment to the teacher chapter; obtain the Gazette original and reconcile it with Law 155/2007 and Decision 428/2013.
- PM Decision 1764/2020 is also listed as an amendment to the seventh-chapter Executive Regulation and is cited in Decision 2297/2022.

## 3. Law 18/2023 — salary/benefit and teacher incentive amendments

- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=VkYoIytkCEE%3D
- Law 18/2023 includes several social/wage provisions and amends:
  - Law 415/1954 on medical practice.
  - Law 14/2014 on members of medical professions in Ministry of Health entities.
  - Social Insurance and Pensions Law 148/2019.
  - Law 212/2020 amending Education Law 139/1981 and Law 103/1961 on Al-Azhar.
- It replaces Article 2 of Law 212/2020 to revise additional monthly performance incentives for teachers and related categories.
- The Law also provides a quality incentive for university faculty and equivalent research-centre staff.
- This is a multi-statute amending law. Its provisions must be linked to each affected base statute, not stored as a standalone "education amendment" only.

## 4. Universities Organization Law 49/1972 and Executive Regulation

- Base law: Law 49/1972 on the Organization of Universities.
- Executive Regulation: Presidential Decision 809/1975.
- Official Amiri Press examples confirm continuing application and amendment:
  - PM Decision 699/2022 adds the Faculty of Mass Communication to Ain Shams University and amends the relevant executive-regulation provision:
    https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=gj9R7bXAgKg%3D
  - PM Decision 1952/2022 adjusts tuition fees for international students in specified universities:
    https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=bjwNTPW9zys%3D
- Supreme Council of Universities official guidance says internal academic regulations and amendments require approval by the university council, Supreme Council of Universities and Minister of Higher Education; students ordinarily remain subject to the regulation in force when they enrolled, subject to specified transition options:
  https://scu.eg/faqs/هل-يتوجب-اعتماد-المجلس-الأعلى-للجامعات/
- Obtain full Law 49/1972, Decision 809/1975 and all amendments, plus each university-specific creation/organization decree and approved internal regulation.

## 5. Private and National Universities Law 12/2009

- Law 12/2009 regulates private and national universities.
- Executive Regulation: Presidential Decision 302/2010.
- Presidential Decision 566/2024 amends the Executive Regulation's definition of the competent Council to refer to the Council of Private or National Universities as appropriate:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=+bJzh3QkJao%3D
- Presidential Decision 564/2024 establishes Obour University for Science and Technology and cites Law 49/1972 and Law 12/2009:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Ffv9zGObvg0%3D
- Obtain Law 12/2009, Decision 302/2010, Decision 566/2024 and later amendments/creation decisions.

## 6. Al-Azhar education and higher education

- Law 103/1961 reorganizes Al-Azhar and its bodies.
- Law 212/2020 amends aspects of Law 103/1961 and Education Law 139/1981 and establishes the social-care fund for teachers in general education, technical education and Al-Azhar.
- Law 18/2023 further amends Law 212/2020.
- Obtain the full Law 103/1961, Law 212/2020, Law 18/2023 and all executive regulations/Al-Azhar-specific decisions; keep Al-Azhar institutions distinct from general universities.

# FRA Decision 137/2025 — annexes on operational-risk measurement and internal loss data (Arabic text supplement)

**Primary source:** official 67-page Gazette PDF, الوقائع المصرية العدد 173 تابع، 7 August 2025: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/alamiria_2025_137.pdf  
This supplement covers Gazette pages 45–67, including Annexes 1–4 and the two operational-loss tables. It is based on the official PDF text layer; some table layout has been normalized.

## ملحق رقم (1) — تعريفات (Gazette pp. 45–47)

**مخاطر التشغيل:** «هي الخسائر التي يمكن أن تتعرض لها الشركة نتيجة الخلل في نظام الرقابة الداخلية أو نظام التشغيل الداخلي لديها، أو إخفاق وفشل العاملين والموارد البشرية في أداء مهامهم بكفاءة، أو أعطال في أنظمة التشغيل الإلكترونية، أو نتيجة عوامل وأحداث خارجية، ويتضمن ذلك عمليات التزوير والغش، ويشمل المخاطر القانونية ويستثنى من ذلك مخاطر السمعة.»

**المخاطر القانونية:** «هي الخسائر الناجمة عن الغرامات والعقوبات والجزاءات المطبقة على الشركة في حالة إخفاقها في التزاماتها التعاقدية والقانونية أو عدم التزامها بالتعليمات الرقابية أو نتيجة تطبيقها بشكل مخالف لنصوص العقد أو لتكون تلك النصوص لا تعكس الحقوق والالتزامات التعاقدية للبنك و/أو الطرف المقابل بشكل واضح وسليم.»

**مؤشر الأعمال (BI):** «عبارة عن مؤشر بسيط لقياس مخاطر التشغيل، يتم حسابه من واقع بيانات القوائم المالية للشركات (قائمة الدخل)، ويتكون من ثلاثة عناصر هي صافي العوائد وإيرادات التأجير التمويلي والأرباح الموزعة (ILDC)، ومكون الخدمات (SC)، والمكون المالي (FC).»

**مكون مؤشر الأعمال المرجح (BIC):** «هو ناتج ضرب مؤشر الأعمال (BI) في المعامل الرقابي (α) والذي يحدد وفقاً لحجم نشاط الشركة الذي يعكسه مؤشر الأعمال.»

**مكون الخسائر (LC):** «هو متوسط صافي قيمة الخسائر الناتجة عن مخاطر التشغيل خلال عشر سنوات كحد أدنى مضروباً في 15 (والذي يمثل معامل ثابت محدد من قبل لجنة بازل).»

**مضاعف الخسائر الداخلية (ILM):** عامل قياس يستند إلى العلاقة النسبية بين متوسط صافي الخسائر التاريخية المحققة للشركة ومكون مؤشر الأعمال المرجح، ويعتمد على مراقبة خسائر التشغيل التاريخية للشركة لفترة محددة.

**التسوية:** تفاوض جهة التمويل مع العميل وإبرام اتفاق جديد يتضمن التنازل عن جزء من المستحقات أو تخفيض سعر العائد/العمولات، ويعتد بتاريخ تفعيل اتفاق التسوية؛ وتتم بعد دراسة خاصة توضح الظروف الخارجة عن إرادة العميل التي أثرت على قدرته على الوفاء، مع تحديد حجم الائتمان محل التسوية وعدد مرات التسوية. ويسري ذلك على حالات التسوية الفورية.

**الجدولة:** تفاوض جهة التمويل مع العميل وتوقيع اتفاق جديد لتعديل شروط التمويل دون التنازل عن أي من مستحقات جهة التمويل؛ ويعتد بتاريخ تفعيل اتفاق الجدولة، بعد دراسة خاصة لطبيعة الظروف الخارجة عن إرادة العميل التي أثرت على قدرته على الوفاء، مع تحديد نوع الائتمان وحجمه وعدد مرات الجدولة.

**العميل الواحد:** العميل الحاصل على تمويل من شركة التمويل وأطرافه المرتبطة، بما يشمل الأشخاص المرتبطين باتفاق للاستحواذ أو السيطرة الفعلية على شركة، والأشخاص الطبيعيين وأزواجهم وأقاربهم حتى الدرجة الثانية، والأشخاص الاعتبارية الخاضعة لذات السيطرة الفعلية، والأشخاص الذين يتفقون على ممارسة حقوقهم في الجمعية العامة أو مجلس الإدارة، والشركات القابضة والتابعة والشقيقة بحسب الأحوال. وتشمل السيطرة الفعلية القدرة على تعيين أغلبية أعضاء مجلس الإدارة أو التحكم في قراراته أو قرارات الجمعية العامة.

**احتمالية التعثر (PD):** «احتمال تعثر المقترض عن سداد القرض خلال فترة زمنية معينة.»

**الخسارة في حالة التعثر (LGD):** معدل الخسارة في المكشوف بعد استبعاد معدل الاسترداد المتوقع؛ ومعدل الاسترداد هو القيمة الحالية لما يمكن استرداده من ضمانات أو تدفقات نقدية مقسوماً على القيمة عند التعثر.

## ملحق رقم (2) — تعريفات مكونات مؤشر الأعمال وفقاً لبازل 3 (Gazette pp. 48–51)

The Annex defines the line items to be included in each component of the Business Indicator.

### Interest, lease/rent and dividend component (ILDC)

Includes:
- interest income from loans, financial assets available for sale, assets held to maturity and assets held for trading;
- finance-lease and operating-lease income, including gains on leased assets;
- income from derivatives and hedging accounts;
- other interest income;
- interest expenses on financial liabilities and other interest expenses, including deposits, issued debt securities and derivatives;
- losses, depreciation and impairment on leased assets;
- dividends from investments in shares, non-consolidated investment funds, associates/subsidiaries and joint ventures.

### Services component (SC)

Includes income from consulting and services and income received as an external financial-services provider, including fees and commissions arising from:
- securities issuance/underwriting and ownership transfer;
- executing buy/sell orders on behalf of clients;
- clearing and settlement;
- asset management and custody services;
- payment services;
- securitization services;
- financing services and foreign-exchange operations.

Includes expenses paid for consulting/services and external financial-service suppliers, including fees and commissions for clearing, settlement, custody and securitization. Excludes costs of non-financial services such as logistics, IT and human resources. Other operating income/expenses of similar nature are included, including investment-property rent, gains/losses on disposal of non-current assets held for sale, operational fines/settlements, asset-replacement costs and provisions/reserves for operational-loss events.

### Financial component (FC)

Includes net profit/loss from assets and liabilities held for trading (derivatives, debt securities, equities, loans/advances, other assets/liabilities), net profit/loss from hedging operations, and net foreign-exchange differences. Separate treatment is provided for assets/liabilities measured at fair value through profit and loss, available-for-sale assets and assets/liabilities held to maturity.

## ملحق رقم (3) — تعريفات مكونات مؤشر الأعمال للأنشطة المالية غير المصرفية (Gazette pp. 52–58)

The annex gives activity-specific BI line items:

### Mortgage finance
- Interest/return income: individual mortgage finance returns, purchased mortgage portfolios, late-payment charges, early-settlement commissions.
- Interest expenses: financing interest.
- Interest-earning assets: cash and bank balances, investments in affiliates, Treasury bills, real-estate investments, financial investments, individual mortgage customers and portfolio customers.
- Service component: valuation income, gains on assets held for sale, other operating income; expenses include general/administrative costs, appraiser fees, mortgage broker commissions, credit inquiry fees and FRA development fees.
- Financial component: net income on financial assets held for trading (bank interest, Treasury bill yields, money-market fund returns, fair-value gains and securitization returns) and held-to-maturity financial assets.

### Financial leasing and factoring
- Interest income: finance-lease contract returns, administrative charges and early-settlement commissions.
- Interest expenses: financing expenses.
- Interest-earning assets: investments in subsidiaries/associates, Treasury bills, financial assets, investment property, finance-lease and factoring receivables, and assets held for sale.
- Service component includes other operating income, service/collection income, administrative service fees, interest income, foreign-currency translation differences; expenses include selling/distribution, administrative/general expenses, depreciation, foreign-currency translation differences and other operating expenses.
- Financial component includes net income on trading and held-to-maturity assets, including Treasury bills, fair-value sale gains, discounting of receivable portfolios, securitization and sukuk returns.

### Consumer finance
- Interest income: financing interest, activity revenue, merchant discounts and durable-goods sales revenue.
- Interest expenses: cost of purchasing durable goods and financing expenses.
- Interest-earning assets: cash/bank balances, financial investments, equity-method investments, subsidiaries/associates, financial-rights portfolios, consumer-finance customers, Treasury bills and real-estate investments.
- Service component includes operating/service income, collection/administrative income and related operating costs.
- Financial component includes net income on trading and held-to-maturity assets.

### MSME and microfinance
- Interest income: financing returns and activity revenue.
- Interest expenses: financing costs, interest, administrative costs, early-repayment fees and late-payment penalties.
- Interest-earning assets: investments in affiliates, customers, Treasury bills, cash, bank deposits and financial investments.
- Service component includes sales/marketing, administrative expenses, claims provisions, lease obligations, expected credit losses, financial-guarantee provisions, depreciation, board allowances, right-of-use lease costs, customer mutual contributions and net finance-risk provisions.
- Other operating income includes securitization gains, other operating income, recoveries from previously written-off debts, reversals of customer impairment and reversals of expected credit losses.
- Financial component includes Treasury-bill yields, financing income, interest income, fair-value investment-sale gains and securitization returns.

## ملحق رقم (4) — standards for determining internal-loss data for operational risk (Gazette pp. 59–63)

**General standards:**
1. Document procedures for collecting historical loss data; verify procedures before using loss data in the operational-risk capital methodology; independently review the process regularly through internal audit and/or the external auditor.
2. Classify data using the Level 1 supervisory categories and maintain a loss-data collection matrix; report data periodically to FRA and document the classification standards.
3. Ensure completeness and accuracy across all company activities, systems and branches; have internal audit independently review the data.
4. Record operational-loss events of EGP 50,000 or more for the historical loss database used in calculating operational-risk capital.
5. Operational losses related to market risk are included in the operational-loss database; losses related to credit risk that are already captured in risk-weighted assets are not double-counted in the operational-loss database.
6. Include direct loss, recoveries and costs related to the event; distinguish between gross loss, insurance recoveries, other recoveries and net loss.

**Level 1 event categories in the standard:**
1. Internal fraud.
2. External fraud.
3. Employment practices and workplace safety.
4. Improper practices affecting customer rights.
5. Damage to physical assets.
6. Business disruption and system failures.
7. Deficiencies in management and execution of processes.

The annex further classifies each category into Level 2/Level 3 examples and provides a collection matrix (Table 1) with event counts, total activities, largest loss, gross loss, insurance recoveries, other recoveries and net loss.

**Loss categories and treatment:**
- Recoveries may include amounts recovered from insurers, perpetrators of fraud and erroneous transfers.
- Certain potential operational-loss events may be temporarily recorded in suspense accounts or provisions before their financial effect is reflected in profit and loss; material pending losses must be included in the loss database for a period proportionate to their amount and duration.
- Time-lagged losses are adverse economic impacts recorded in financial statements in a later period due to an operational-risk event; material time-lagged losses should be recorded in the loss database where they span more than one financial period or create legal risk.
- Losses arising from mergers/acquisitions are separately classified.
- Items excluded from gross operational-loss calculations include ordinary maintenance contracts for buildings/equipment; internal/external business-restoration expenses and risk-methodology development costs; insurance premiums; and losses relating to disposed subsidiaries where FRA is satisfied they are not connected with current business activities.

# Collection checkpoint 55 — Military Technical College amendment and Kayan University Law 161/2026

## 1. Law 156/2026 — amendment to Military Technical College Law 93/1975

- Secondary full-text transcription:
  https://lawyeregypt.net/قانون-رقم-156-لسنة-2026-بتعديل-بعض-احكام-القانون-رقم-93-لسنة-1975/
- Publication date: 18 August 2026.
- Amends Law 93/1975 on the basic statute of the Military Technical College.
- Article 1 replaces Articles 4, 10, 16, 18 and 19.
- New Article 4: admission is for students holding the General Secondary Certificate or equivalent; internal regulations set study duration and required specializations.
- New Article 10: applicant must be Egyptian, with both parents Egyptian other than by naturalization, and hold General Secondary Certificate/equivalent in accordance with Supreme Council of Universities engineering-admission decisions and College Council conditions.
- New Article 16: dismissal grounds include final conviction for an offence involving dishonour, loss of an admission condition, or academic failure under internal rules; reasons accepted by the College Council are excluded from counted failures.
- New Article 18: resignation requires College Council approval; student and guardian are jointly liable for actual State expenses during enrolment. Similar liability applies to specified dismissal cases, with exceptions for public-interest dismissal, medical unfitness, military unsuitability and non-deliberate failure.
- New Article 19: graduates receive a Bachelor of Engineering degree by Defence Minister decision after meeting internal requirements, with rights/benefits of engineering graduates including Engineers Syndicate registration, and a certificate of military studies.
- Article 2: commencement the day after publication.
- Official Court of Cassation legislation index lists Law 156/2026:
  https://cc.gov.eg/
- The complete original Gazette PDF and hash remain to be acquired. Secondary transcription should be Gazette-matched.

## 2. Law 161/2026 — establishment of Kayan University for Applied Sciences under the Armed Forces

- Secondary full-text transcription:
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=20129
- Official Parliament announcement (12 October 2026) confirms final parliamentary approval after the President objected to Article 25 and the House deleted that article:
  https://parliament.gov.eg/News_Show.aspx?frm=5891
- Gazette: issue 43 bis, 25 October 2026; effective 26 October 2026 according to the transcription.
- Official Court of Cassation legal index lists Law 161/2026 twice, including an updated version:
  https://cc.gov.eg/
- Law 161/2026 creates Kayan University for Applied Sciences as a public body with legal personality, affiliated with the Minister of Defence and headquartered in Cairo.
- It establishes military and civilian streams, with branches possible by Presidential Decision.
- Objectives include undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, research, specialist training and applied sciences.
- The law transfers the Armed Forces Medical College created by Law 74/2013 to Kayan University, including assets, rights, obligations, staff and faculty members; Law 74/2013 is repealed.
- Existing students retain their legal status under the prior Law 74/2013 and its implementing regulations.
- The statute establishes governance through a Supreme University Council chaired by the Defence Minister, a University Council and a University President; it also regulates admission, internal bylaws, faculty, student discipline, degrees and university funding.
- Article 22 requires academic content and regulations to be approved by the Supreme Council of Universities.
- Article 26 applies military-student discipline rules to the military stream under Law 149/2022 establishing the Egyptian Military Academy.
- Article 28 establishes a separate disciplinary board for civilian-stream students, with a judicial member.
- Article 34 assigns administrative disputes involving military-stream students to military judicial committees, not civilian-stream students.
- Article 35 addresses compulsory clinical training for health-science graduates in military hospitals, with possible placements in civilian hospitals/health institutions subject to approval.
- Article 37 provides civilian and military graduates with degrees equivalent to those granted by Egyptian universities in the relevant specializations and rights including syndicate membership, subject to the statute.
- The parliamentary record confirms the originally proposed Article 25 was deleted after presidential objection on equality/equal-opportunity grounds; do not ingest the deleted draft text as enacted law.
- **Potential source typo:** the secondary transcription's promulgation Article 1 appears to cite University Organization Law 49/1973, while the general University Organization Law is Law 49/1972. Verify the exact enacted Gazette text before reproducing this cross-reference.

## 3. Cross-law relationships

- Kayan University Law 161/2026 ↔ University Organization Law 49/1972, Labour Law 14/2025, Armed Forces Academy Law 149/2022, Medical College Law 74/2013 (repealed), medical-profession statutes, Egyptian Health Council Law 12/2022 and relevant professional syndicate laws.
- Military Technical College Law 93/1975 as amended by Law 156/2026 ↔ university admission standards, military service rules, Engineers Syndicate Law 66/1974 and applicable military student regulations.
- These are special military-affiliated higher-education institutions; do not assume the ordinary university framework applies where the special law provides otherwise.

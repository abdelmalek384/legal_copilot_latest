# Collection checkpoint 17 — elder-care statute, disability regulation amendment and consumer-law amendment (2026-11-17)

## 1. Older Persons Care Law 19/2024 — Gazette issue and public PDF located

- Law: 19/2024 issuing the Older Persons Care and Rights Law.
- Gazette: Official Gazette issue 14 supplement (d), 4 April 2024.
- Government SIS digital repository record:
  https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/handle/123456789/57741
- PDF file listed on the SIS record: `OG14_20240404_3001.pdf` (301.3 KB).
- Another PDF copy:
  https://elsayyad.net/UploadFiles/LegislationDetails/2024-019.pdf
- Secondary full Arabic text:
  https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-19-2024
- Secondary text reports 51 substantive articles, but the SIS repository record is the preferred Gazette issue locator.
- Promulgation Article 1 applies to Egyptians aged 65 or older, and to lawfully resident foreigners at the retirement age specified by their nationality's law subject to reciprocity (with a possible Presidential exception).
- Promulgation Article 2 directs issuance of the Executive Regulation within six months of commencement and requires relevant entities to regularize within a period not exceeding six months from issuance of the Regulation.
- Promulgation Article 3 provides commencement on the day after publication.
- Obtain the SIS original PDF bytes, hash, page map and compare against secondary text. The implementing regulation and subsequent decisions remain to be located.

## 3. Consumer Protection Law 181/2018 and amendment Law 20/2024

### Base Law 181/2018
- Gazette: issue 37 (supplement), 13 September 2018.
- Official-source text PDF surfaced through WIPO Lex:
  https://wipolex-res.wipo.int/edocs/lexdocs/laws/ar/eg/eg065ar.pdf
- Another searchable text:
  https://serviceapi.egyptianlaws.com/Images/Index?T=1&folder=3&path=2018-9-13-2018-181.pdf
- WIPO Lex identifies Executive Regulation PM Decision 822/2019 and repeal of Consumer Protection Law 67/2006:
  https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/ar/legislation/details/19866
- The base law's promulgation text assigns civil/commercial disputes to Economic Courts and administrative disputes to the Administrative Court; it repeals Law 67/2006.

### Amendment Law 20/2024
- Ministry of Finance official record:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-6643253a8897b900092f6319
- Gazette issue 14 bis, 5 April 2024.
- SIS government repository record and PDF:
  https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/xmlui/handle/123456789/58298
- The amendment replaces Article 71 of Law 181/2018. The indexed summary states that violation of Article 8 is punishable by imprisonment for at least one year and a fine of at least EGP 150,000 and up to EGP 3 million or the equivalent value of the goods subject of the crime, whichever is greater; recidivism increases imprisonment and doubles minimum/maximum fine thresholds, with seizure/confiscation and possible closure/licence cancellation.
- Do not rely on this summary for a prosecution or penalty computation until the complete Arabic Article 71 replacement is checked against the Gazette PDF.
- The amendment must be linked to Law 181/2018 and Executive Regulation Decision 822/2019.

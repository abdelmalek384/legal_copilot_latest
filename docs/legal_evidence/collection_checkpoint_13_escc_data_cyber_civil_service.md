# Collection checkpoint 13 — official Gazette texts: personal data, cybercrime and civil service

**Date:** 2026-11-17. Primary-source copies hosted by ESCC and official government sites. Currentness/amendment verification remains outstanding.

## 1. Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020
- Official Gazette PDF: https://escc.gov.eg/assets/docs/law-151-2020.pdf
- Gazette issue 28 bis (e), 15 July 2020; searchable PDF text inspected (723 extracted lines).
- Applies to electronic processing, wholly or partly, of natural persons' personal data by a holder, controller or processor.
- Extraterritorial application covers Egyptian perpetrators inside/outside Egypt, non-Egyptian residents in Egypt, and certain non-Egyptians abroad where conduct is criminal where committed and data concerns Egyptians or foreign residents in Egypt.
- Exclusions include personal-use data held by natural persons, official statistical/legal processing, certain exclusively journalistic processing subject to conditions, judicial records/investigations/cases, national-security data, and data at CBE and supervised entities (with stated exceptions for money-transfer and exchange companies).
- The competent minister was required to issue the Executive Regulation within six months; economic courts have jurisdiction over offences; covered entities must regularize within one year from issuance of the Executive Regulation.
- Commencement: after three months from the day following publication.
- Article 1 definitions include personal data, processing, sensitive data, data subject, holder, controller, processor, availability, data security, breach, cross-border data movement, electronic marketing and national-security entities. Sensitive data includes health/genetic/biometric/financial/religious/political/security data; children's data is always sensitive.
- Executive Regulation Decision 816/2025 is recorded in prior project research; law 151/2020 alone is not sufficient for current compliance analysis. Reconcile amendments, implementing rules, licences/permits, Centre decisions and Gazette corrections.

## 2. Cybercrime / Information Technology Crimes Law 175/2018
- Official Gazette PDF: https://escc.gov.eg/assets/docs/law-175-2018.pdf
- Gazette issue 32 bis (c), 14 August 2018; searchable full PDF text inspected (416 extracted lines).
- Article 1 definitions include NTRA, competent ICT minister, electronic data/information, personal/government data, electronic processing, IT, service provider, user, software, information system/network, website, website manager, account, email, interception, hacking, content, digital evidence, expertise, traffic data, computer, electronic media, national security and national-security entities.
- Digital evidence is electronic information with evidentiary value stored, transmitted, extracted or obtained from computers/networks and capable of collection/analysis through specialized technological devices/programs/applications.
- Extract all 45 provisions and map amendments, criminal procedure, communications law and NTRA/CBE rules. Article 1 is not full-text completion.

## 3. Executive Regulation of Law 175/2018 — PM Decision 635/2020
- Official Gazette PDF: https://escc.gov.eg/assets/docs/law-175-2018-regulations.pdf
- Searchable PDF text inspected (201 extracted lines). Decision makes the attached regulation applicable and says it takes effect the day after Gazette publication.
- Definitions include encryption, encryption key, critical information infrastructure, industrial control systems and vulnerabilities.
- Critical infrastructure examples include electricity, gas, petroleum, telecom, finance/banks, industry, transport/aviation, education/research, broadcasting, water/sewage, health, government and emergency services.
- Article 2 requires service providers to use AES-128 or stronger encryption (or equivalent), malware protection, secure protocols such as HTTPS, access permissions, asset/software/database inventories, password controls, documented installation/operations, SLAs and allocation of responsibilities, periodic updates and pre-update testing, annual security/penetration testing, and firewalls/NGFW/UTM.
- Remaining articles and annexes require extraction and reconciliation with later technical standards and NTRA/CERT decisions.

## 4. Civil Service Law 81/2016
- Official Gazette PDF hosted on official government site: https://www.esa.gov.eg/files/laws/10.pdf
- Gazette issue 43 bis (A), 1 November 2016; searchable full PDF text inspected (962 extracted lines).
- Applies to ministries, departments, government agencies, local administration units and public authorities unless establishment law/decision provides otherwise.
- Repeals Civilian State Employees Law 47/1978 and conflicting provisions.
- Executive Regulation was to be issued by Prime Minister after Civil Service Council opinion; pending it, existing regulations/decisions continue insofar as consistent.
- Article 1: civil posts are a citizen right based on competence/merit and public-service duty; prohibits discrimination based on religion, sex or other grounds.
- Article 2 defines competent authority, unit, leadership/supervisory posts, employee, functional/complementary/full wage, budget year, competent minister and CAOA.
- Article 3 establishes Civil Service Council with advisory functions on civil-service development, draft legislation, performance assessment, training, ethics and budget proposals.
- CAOA index lists Executive Regulation PM Decision 1216/2017 and related decisions. Harvest implementation rules and all amendments, wage/allowance laws and Constitutional Court judgments.

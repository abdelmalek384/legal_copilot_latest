## 3. Law 165/2025 — amendment to Law 4/1996 (not a direct amendment to Civil Code 131/1948)

- Official Amiri Press Arabic text:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=PQXvLW52gd8%3D
- Official Gazette PDF (issue 31 bis, 4 August 2025):
  https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/xmlui/bitstream/handle/123456789/105149/OG31_20250804_2885.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2
- Law 165/2025 amends Law 4/1996 on the application of Civil Code rules to premises never previously rented and premises whose leases have ended/expire without a person having a right to remain.
- It adds Article 2 bis to Law 4/1996.
- New Article 2 bis requires the tenant or universal/special successor to vacate and return the leased premises at the end of the term stated in the lease.
- If the tenant refuses, the landlord/lessor may request an order from the judge of urgent matters in the court where the property is located to evict the refusing occupant, without prejudice to a damages claim where appropriate.
- The tenant/successor may still bring an ordinary substantive claim before the competent court, but filing that claim does not stay the urgent-matters eviction order.
- Law 165/2025 was issued 4 August 2025 and commences the day after publication.
- This is a direct amendment to Law 4/1996, not a replacement or amendment of Civil Code Law 131/1948. Preserve that legal relationship precisely.

### Extracted verbatim Arabic — new Article 2 bis
> (المادة الثانية مكررًا) : يلتزم المستأجر أو خلفه العام أو الخاص، بحسب الأحوال ، بإخلاء المكان المؤجر ورده إلى المالك أو المؤجر ، بحسب الأحوال ، فى نهاية المدة المبينة في عقد الإيجار ، وفى حال الامتناع عن ذلك يكون للمالك أو المؤجر أن يطلب من قاضي الأمور الوقتية بالمحكمة الكائن في دائرتها العقار إصدار أمر بطرد الممتنع عن الإخلاء دون الإخلال بالحق في التعويض إن كان له مقتض .
>
> ومع عدم الإخلال بحكم الفقرة الأولى من هذه المادة يحق للمستأجر أو خلفه العام أو الخاص ، بحسب الأحوال ، رفع دعوى موضوعية أمام المحكمة المختصة وفقًا للإجراءات المعتادة على ألا يترتب على رفع الدعوى الموضوعية وقف أمر قاضي الأمور الوقتية المشار إليه.

## 4. Related lease-law framework

- Law 4/1996 remains the base instrument for premises never previously rented and leases that have ended without a right to remain.
- Law 165/2025 inserts Article 2 bis into Law 4/1996.
- Law 164/2025 separately addresses specified residential and non-residential leases governed by Laws 49/1977 and 136/1981, with a deferred repeal mechanism after seven years.
- These are different legal tracks. Do not apply Law 165/2025's urgent eviction mechanism indiscriminately to every old-rent tenancy covered by Law 164/2025.
- Keep Law 4/1996, Law 165/2025, Law 164/2025, Laws 49/1977, 136/1981 and 6/1997 linked but distinct, with scope and transitional rules explicitly represented.

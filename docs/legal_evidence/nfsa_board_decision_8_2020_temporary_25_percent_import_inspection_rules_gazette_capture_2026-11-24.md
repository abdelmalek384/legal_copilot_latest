# NFSA Board Decision 8/2020 — temporary 25% inspection rates for specified imported food

**Acquisition date:** 24 November 2026  
**Instrument:** NFSA Board Decision No. 8 of 2020  
**Decision date:** 26 July 2020

## 2. Article map (page-image inspected)

- **Article 1:** for NFSA-approved food establishments on the Authority's white list, inspection may be limited to 25% of imported raw-material consignments that are factory production inputs covered by factory needs cards or industrial inputs covered by investment cards.
- **Article 2:** 25% inspection applies to finished food products covered by NFSA Board Decision 2/2020 where the consignment is accompanied by a pre-shipment inspection certificate from an NFSA-registered inspection/conformity company.
- **Article 3:** NFSA Board Chair issues implementation controls and may change the inspection percentage to reflect changing circumstances.
- **Article 4:** effective the day after publication and remains in force until **31 October 2020**. The signed decision page shows issuance on 26 July 2020.

## 3. Version and currentness crosswalk

- Decision 8/2020 is expressly time-limited; it must not be served as a current inspection-rate rule.
- Decision 14/2020, dated 4 November 2020 and published 15 November 2020 in issue 257 supplement (A), refers to Decision 8/2020 and provides a later 25% framework, including a 10% rate for qualifying raw-material consignments accompanied by an NFSA-registered conformity certificate, and an expiry date of 15 January 2021 in its Article 4 text. That is a secondary full-text lead and requires its own evidence record/page check.
- Later Decision 269/2022 and Decision 270/2024 refer to reduced sampling / fast-release arrangements for specified white-list manufacturing facilities. These later instruments require separate currentness and scope analysis.
- Decision 7/2020 supplies the general risk-based import-control framework; Decision 8/2020 is a temporary rate adjustment, not a replacement for the whole system.

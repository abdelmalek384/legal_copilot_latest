## 1. Law 149/2026 amends VAT Law 67/2016

### Article 1 — replacement provisions

**Article 3, first paragraph — standard and medical-equipment rates**
- General VAT rate on goods/services: 13%, and 14% from fiscal year 2017/2018 (as stated in the law's text).
- One percentage point of the tax is allocated to social-justice programmes.
- Medical machinery/equipment used to produce a good or provide a service is subject to 5%, excluding buses and passenger cars.
- Other devices used for medical purposes are treated as medical equipment for this exception.

**Article 5, fourth paragraph — transit**
- No VAT is due on transit goods and services performed on them, provided transport occurs under Customs Authority supervision according to Customs Law rules.

**Article 28 bis — suspension for medical machinery/equipment used in local industrial production**
- Payment of tax due on medical machinery/equipment imported from abroad or purchased from the local market for local factories/production units is suspended for one year from customs release date or local purchase, as applicable.
- Tax Authority may extend for justified reasons accepted by it, for further periods, provided total extension does not exceed three years.
- If Authority establishes that machinery/equipment was used in industrial production during the period, it is exempt from the suspended tax.
- The industrial product may not dispose of the exempt machinery/equipment for purposes other than those for which exemption was granted during the following five years, unless it notifies the Authority and pays the tax due based on its condition/value and tax rate in force at payment.
- If the one-year/extended period expires without use in industrial production, tax and additional tax become due on the machinery/equipment from customs release or local purchase date, as applicable, through payment date.
- Devices used for medical purposes are treated as medical equipment for this article.
- Executive Regulation sets detailed rules and procedures.

**Article 30, first paragraph, item 3 — input credit/refund**
- Creditable balance outstanding for more than four consecutive tax periods may be refunded.
- For projects subject to Law 6/2025 with annual turnover not exceeding EGP 20 million, refund may be made after three months.

### Article 2 — replacement entries in exempt-goods/services schedule
Replaces schedule items 19, 20, 28, 32, 35 and 36:
- Item 19: bottled cooking gas (butane).
- Item 20: natural materials in their natural state, including quarry and mine products, excluding crude petroleum and natural gas.
- Item 28: sale/lease of vacant land and agricultural land/buildings/units; sale/lease of buildings and non-residential units, excluding rental of buildings/units that are independent premises for management of religious, charitable, social, educational and health activities and other activities designated by ministerial decision for social/public-interest reasons.
- Item 32: prosthetic limbs, their separate parts/components, artificial body organs, hearing aids and parts, devices worn/carried/implanted to compensate deficiency/disability, their production inputs, dialysis machines/parts/filters/supplies, incubators, blood and derivatives, blood-collection bags, vaccines/sera and family-planning devices.
- Item 35: banking and financial services provided by the Postal Savings Fund of the National Postal Authority.
- Item 36: non-bank financial services subject to FRA or CBE supervision.

### Article 3 — new schedule item
- Adds item 3 to second paragraph of Article 22: sales of machinery/equipment covered by Article 28 bis.
- Adds item 17 to Schedule "A" (goods/services subject to table tax): natural gas, measured per 1,000 cubic feet, at EGP 20.

### Article 4 — deleted schedule items
- Deletes serial items 8 and 10 from Part One of the table of goods/services annexed to VAT Law 67/2016.

### Article 5 — commencement
- Effective the day after publication in the Official Gazette.

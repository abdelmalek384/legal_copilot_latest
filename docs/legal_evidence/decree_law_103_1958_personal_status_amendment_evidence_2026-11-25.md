# Decree-Law 103/1958 — amendment to Personal Status Law 25/1929

## Instrument identity
- **Instrument:** Presidential Decree-Law 103/1958 amending Articles 21 and 22 of Decree-Law 25/1929.
- **Publication:** Official Gazette issue 21, 31 July 1958.
- **Commencement:** Article 2 states the law takes effect in the Egyptian region from the date of publication (31 July 1958).
- **Source:** LAWEG two-article text reproduction: https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?NID=29049&action=viewactivepages&itemid=25469&type=6 ; secondary consolidated annotation: https://www.diaalwkeel.com/qanwn-rqm-25-lsnh-1929

## Operative changes observed
Article 1 replaces Article 21 first paragraph and Article 22 of Law 25/1929:
- The missing person presumed likely to have perished is treated as deceased after four years from disappearance.
- For members of the armed forces missing during war operations, the Minister of War issues a decision treating them as deceased after four years; the decision stands in place of the judgment.
- Article 22 provides for the wife's waiting period and distribution of the estate after the judgment/decision.

## Historical succession
- Law 33/1992 later replaces both Articles 21 and 22, introducing special treatment for specified shipwreck/aircraft and armed-forces disappearance cases.
- Law 2/2006 later replaces Article 21(2).
- Law 140/2017 later replaces Article 21(2)-(3) and Article 22.
These are distinct historical versions; the 1958 text must not be treated as current.

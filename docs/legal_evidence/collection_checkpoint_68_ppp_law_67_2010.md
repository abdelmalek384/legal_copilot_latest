## 1. Law 67/2010 — scope and core structure
- Applies to public-private partnership contracts and related advisory contracts concluded by administrative entities for infrastructure, public services and public utilities.
- It disapplies specified old public-utility concession statutes and old Tenders/Auctions Law 89/1998 to PPP contracts, subject to the law.
- The law allows public entities to contract with a project company to finance, build, equip, develop, maintain and operate infrastructure/public utility projects and provide services.
- Contract duration normally 5–30 years from completion of construction/equipment or development; total contract value at least EGP 100 million. Cabinet may approve longer than 30 years for a compelling public interest.
- Project company cannot start receiving payments for outputs/services until contracting authority issues quality acceptance certificate.
- Chapters cover definitions, PPP project scope, central PPP unit, Supreme Committee for PPP Affairs, project preparation, tendering, evaluation, project company, contract terms, financing, monitoring, dispute settlement and termination.
- The statute distinguishes operation (providing service to public entity) from exploitation (selling product/providing service to a party designated by administrative entity).
- Article 35: Egyptian law governs PPP contract; contrary choice is void; arbitration or other non-judicial dispute resolution may be agreed with Supreme Committee approval.
- Article 36 restricts dissolution, legal-form change, capital reduction and share transfers in project company; share pledges only for project financing/refinancing.
- Article 37 contract changes require agreement of parties; where another administrative entity has financial obligations, its prior written consent is required.

## 2. Law 153/2021 — major amendment
- Replaces provisions of Articles 3(2), 4, 14(3), 15(1)(b), 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 23, 26, 28(1), 32(1)(c), 32 final paragraph and 39.
- Changes project exploitation approval, committee composition, PPP unit/committee functions, project preparation, tendering/award and contract-management provisions.
- The amendment enables a more structured central PPP project pipeline and adjusts committee roles and procedures.
- Exact replacement wording must be extracted from the official Gazette PDF, including all amended article texts.

## 3. Executive Regulation PM Decision 238/2011
- Implements PPP Law 67/2010 and details:
  - Annual project planning and feasibility.
  - Technical, economic, financial, legal, social and environmental studies.
  - Central PPP Unit and administrative-entity PPP units.
  - Project preparation and approval by Supreme Committee.
  - Tender documents, prequalification, public tender, two-envelope process and evaluation.
  - Project company formation and ownership/transfer restrictions.
  - PPP contract minimum terms, payment, quality, risk allocation, insurance and guarantees.
  - Monitoring, performance deductions, variations, compensation, termination and dispute resolution.

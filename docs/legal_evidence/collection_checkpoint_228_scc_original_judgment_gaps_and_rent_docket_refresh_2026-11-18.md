## 1. Four priority historical judgments — source status

### Case 10/35 — Law 142/1964

- Judgment date: 2 June 2018.
- Gazette publication: Official Gazette issue 22 bis (ط), 6 June 2018, printed pages 16–25.
- Source: Manshurat scan record https://manshurat.org/node/65064; PDF https://manshurat.org/file/75813/download?token=E2jG1O_x.
- The ten-page scan reproduces the Gazette pages and was visually inspected page by page. The masthead and judgment caption are visible.
- Holding: Articles 21 and 24 of Law 142/1964 unconstitutional; Article 23 and Minister of Justice Decision 553/1976 fell consequentially.
- **Acquisition status:** a Gazette-page scan copy is available from a secondary host; not an Amiri Press-hosted original binary, not certified, and no official SCC signed PDF acquired. The official SCC website contains later references to the holding, but not the original judgment in a directly retrievable PDF.

### Case 42/17 — Law 142/1964

- Judgment date: 6 June 1998.
- Full searchable Arabic text: MKSEgypt https://mksegypt.org/ar/judgments-of-the-supreme-constitutional-court/15553.
- Official SCC later judgment corroborating the operative order: https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/12158_41_14_3_2.html.
- Holding: Article 37(2)'s prohibition on acquiring registered land by prescription contrary to the register was unconstitutional; Article 38 fell consequentially. Article 37(1)'s evidentiary force remains.
- Gazette publication date reported as 18 June 1998, but issue metadata is inconsistent: later Court of Cassation digest cites issue 35 تابع; CRCICA catalogue lists issue 25 تابع (ب), same date. Preserve both.
- **Acquisition status:** complete secondary text and later official SCC corroboration; original signed judgment and original Gazette scan not acquired. The original printed page is required to resolve the issue number.

### Case 101/26 — Law 143/1981

- Judgment date: 1 February 2009.
- Full official SCC judgment HTML: https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/3204_26_101_1_2.html.
- Gazette publication: Official Gazette issue 7 bis (أ), 15 February 2009, recorded on the official SCC page.
- Holding: Article 22(1) unconstitutional insofar as it gave ordinary courts exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising under Law 143/1981; connected paragraphs fall only to the extent inseparably linked to that jurisdictional rule.
- **Acquisition status:** full official SCC HTML text available; original printed Gazette page/image and a hash-verified PDF remain outstanding.

### Case 11/23 — Law 136/1981, non-residential leases to legal persons

- Judgment date: 5 May 2018.
- Gazette publication: Official Gazette issue 19 bis (ب), 13 May 2018.
- Full searchable reproduction of the judgment: Laweg https://www.laweg.net/Default.aspx?ID=5943&action=DisplayNews. This page reproduces the operative order and extended reasoning, not the Amiri Press signed binary.
- Official SCC subject page: https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/SCC/?a=cp3&id=802; it requires JavaScript and does not expose the full judgment in the current text-accessible session.
- Later Manshurat PDF previously associated with this case is **not** the original Case 11/23 judgment; it is Case 32/43/2023 and quotes the Case 11/23 operative order. Do not mislabel it.
- Holding: the opening of Article 18(1) of Law 136/1981 unconstitutional insofar as the prohibition on eviction after expiry of the agreed term extended to premises leased to legal persons for non-residential use. The Court deferred effect until the day after the end of the ordinary annual legislative session following publication.
- **Acquisition status:** complete secondary full-text reproduction and official SCC subject-page confirmation; original signed judgment and Gazette issue 19 bis (ب) scan not acquired.

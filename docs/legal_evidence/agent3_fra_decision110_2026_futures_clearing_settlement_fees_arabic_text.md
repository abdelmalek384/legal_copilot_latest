### المادة الأولى

> يكون الحد الأقصى لمقابل الخدمات التي تقدمها شركة المقاصة والتسوية للعقود الآجلة على النحو الآتي:

| م | الخدمة | المقابل المستحق |
|---:|---|---|

| ٣ | فتح حساب للعميل | مجاناً |

## Operational notes

- Article 1 sets maximum charges for 12 categories of clearing/settlement services. The stated rates include EGP 20,000 one-time membership; EGP 10,000 annual settlement-member subscription; free client-account opening, cash deposits and withdrawals; 1/10,000 transaction-value settlement fee capped at EGP 5,000; 1/1,000 agency execution and member-position transfer fees capped at EGP 5,000; 1/10,000 final settlement of open positions capped at EGP 5,000; EGP 100 per statement; EGP 5,000 annual additional user; and EGP 50,000 annual automated integration.
- Article 2 temporarily waives the fees in items 7–12 for one year from the decision's effective date. It does not waive the fees in items 1–6 except where Article 1 itself sets them at zero.
- Article 3 requires publication in the Official Gazette, on the FRA website and the futures clearing/settlement company's website; commencement is the day after Gazette publication.
- Derived commencement date: 1 July 2026, based on Gazette publication dated 30 June 2026. The text's day-after formula remains controlling.
- FRA's 6 July 2026 public announcement confirms the policy purpose and summarizes the temporary exemptions; use the Gazette text above for operative wording and exact rates.

## 1. Law 15/2004 — scope and legal effect
- Regulates electronic signature and establishes Information Technology Industry Development Authority (ITIDA).
- Defines electronic writing, electronic document, electronic signature, electronic medium, signer, e-certification, and ITIDA.
- Electronic signature must be unique, identify signer and distinguish them from others.
- Article 2 establishes ITIDA as public authority with legal personality, attached to competent minister, headquartered in Giza with possible branches.
- Article 3 objectives include developing ICT industry, encouraging investment, supporting SMEs, regulating e-signature services and e-transactions.
- Article 4 authorizes ITIDA to license/renew e-signature service providers and other e-transaction/IT activities.
- Article 14 (core evidentiary rule) gives electronic writing and signatures the same legal effect as paper writing/signature where technical requirements are satisfied, including:
  1. Signature uniquely identifies signer.
  2. Signer has exclusive control over creation data at signing.
  3. Data is linked to electronic document so later modification is detectable.
  4. ITIDA technical rules and licensing conditions are met.
- Law also governs e-certification providers, licence conditions, technical/financial guarantees, audits, suspension/cancellation and penalties.

## 2. Executive Regulation Decision 109/2005 and Decision 361/2020
- Decision 109/2005 originally implemented Law 15/2004.
- Decision 361/2020 reissued/updated the Executive Regulation, published 23 April 2020, and is the operative regulation version replacing the old 2005 regime as specified.
- 2020 regulation covers:
  - Electronic signature creation data and secure devices.
  - Electronic certification service providers.
  - Electronic certificates and certificate revocation/status.
  - Electronic seals, timestamps, secure electronic delivery and related trust services.
  - Technical standards, security controls, audit, inspection and records.
  - Licence applications, conditions, capital/financial guarantees, personnel, facilities and technical infrastructure.
  - User identity verification and certificate issuance/renewal/suspension/revocation.
  - Digital evidence, integrity, attribution and validation.

## 3. Ministerial Decision 467/2024
- Decision 467/2024 amends the Executive Regulation under Law 15/2004.
- Gazette issue 141 supplement, 2 July 2024; effective 3 July 2024.
- It introduces/updates provisions on trust services and electronic transactions, including service categories, licensing/authorization, security requirements, certificate and timestamp rules.

## 1. Law 147/2026 — reorganization of Future of Egypt Authority

- Official Amiri Press full text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=oHXoEUHb8y0%3D
- Law title: Law 147/2026 reorganizing the Future of Egypt Authority for Sustainable Development.
- Issued 26 July 2026; Gazette issue/publication metadata must be pinned from the original Official Gazette.
- The Law reorganizes the Authority as a national body of a special nature affiliated with the President of the Republic, with independent legal personality and technical/financial/administrative autonomy.
- It establishes:
  - sustainable development zones;
  - a sovereign fund (Future of Egypt Sovereign Wealth Fund, "Nile Pyramids");
  - a service fund ("Daem");
  - a special internal financial framework and related-party transactions regime;
  - authority to manage/receive assets, companies, land and state-owned shares under specified Presidential decisions.
- Articles 26–30 regulate creation and operation of sustainable development zones, including presidential establishment decisions, parliamentary review, land/assets transfer, internal zone systems and registration/licensing of companies operating in the Authority's defined activity/geographic scope.
- Articles 43–46 cross-reference water/environment/industrial licensing/investment/Sinai/economic-zone rules and create a one-stop service centre for land/facility licences and approvals, subject to exceptions and security/constitutional competencies.
- Articles 59–64 govern the sovereign and service funds.
- Articles 76–77 regulate administrative financial penalties and grievance committees.
- Articles 86–92 address transfer of state assets/shares, related-party transactions, standing to challenge contracts/land allocations and data/information provision by State entities.
- Article 90 restricts standing to challenge contracts/decisions/land allocations involving the Authority or its funds to contracting parties, subject to the stated criminal-conviction exception. This is a major procedural/administrative rule requiring close constitutional and judicial review.
- Law 147/2026 is a major special-regime statute. Link it to Law 177/2018 (Egypt Sovereign Fund), Public Business Sector Law 203/1991, Companies Law 159/1981, Public Finance Law 6/2022, Investment Law 72/2017, Industrial Licensing 15/2017, Water Law 147/2021, Environment Law 4/1994, Sinai Law 14/2012 and State Property Law 168/2025.

## 2. Law 154/2026 — 5% of distributable net profits of fully State-owned companies

- Ministry of Finance official record:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-6a731fef63496600085dd865
- Official record labels the Law active and describes it as requiring companies wholly owned by the State or public legal persons to remit a percentage of net profits to the State Treasury.
- Official Gazette PDF/source is linked from the Ministry record; publication date/issue and complete text should be captured.
- Extracted Article 1 from the official-source text:
  - Boards of companies whose capital is wholly owned by the State or public legal persons, regardless of legal form, must set aside **5% of distributable net profits**.
  - This is after covering accumulated losses and before setting aside any reserves.
  - The 5% is treated as tax revenue and remitted to the State Treasury within four months after closing the financial year.
  - Companies established under international agreements are excluded.
- This is a new fiscal obligation affecting wholly State-owned companies, not all companies in which the State owns a minority stake.
- Link to Companies Law 159/1981, Public Business Sector Law 203/1991, Public Finance Law 6/2022 and any company-specific statute; verify treatment of subsidiaries and public authorities under the exact Law text.

## 3. Law 153/2026 — Stamp Tax amendment for listed securities transactions

- Ministry of Finance official record:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-6a73201f63496600085dd868
- Official Gazette repository record:
  https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/7afc9fe7-cd63-4578-9e23-59977080b5db
- Gazette metadata: 28 July 2026 (confirm exact issue/date from Gazette PDF).
- Amends Stamp Tax Law 111/1980:
  - Replaces Article 83 bis.
  - Stamp tax applies to total sale transactions of listed securities on the Egyptian Exchange, except listed investment-fund certificates, without deducting costs.
  - Both buyer and seller bear the tax.
  - Ordinary listed-security trades: 0.5 per thousand on buyer and 0.5 per thousand on seller.
  - Same-day buy/sell transactions: 0.25 per thousand on buyer and 0.25 per thousand on seller.
  - The tax does not apply to transactions executed by licensed market makers under Capital Market Law 95/1992.
  - Settlement entity withholds the tax and remits it to the competent tax office within five days from the beginning of the month after the transaction month; it is jointly liable with buyer/seller for tax and delay charges.
  - Article 2 repeals Article 5 of Law 199/2020.
- Link to Stamp Tax Law 111/1980, Capital Market Law 95/1992, Executive Regulation and EGX settlement rules.

- Full text:
  https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/14-08-2018/no-176?id=1914546&type=1
  https://manshurat.org/node/31486
- Gazette issue 32 bis (c), 14 August 2018; effective 15 August 2018.

### Core provisions
- Regulates financial leasing and factoring as non-bank financial activities under FRA supervision.
- Repeals Financial Leasing Law 95/1995 and PM Decision 1446/2003 on factoring.
- Economic Courts have jurisdiction over disputes and criminal offences under the law, except disputes within State Council jurisdiction.
- Existing companies had six months to regularize, extendable by FRA Board decision.
- Article 1 defines financial leasing as financing that gives lessee temporary possession/use of an asset for a defined period against lease payments, with option to purchase all/part at specified time/price.
- Factoring means purchase of current/future financial rights arising from sales of goods or provision of services.
- Article 2: activity must be conducted under law and FRA Board controls/standards.
- Article 3: FRA has exclusive licensing authority; microfinance companies/associations may be licensed for micro-leasing under statutory controls.
- Article 4 excludes operating leases without purchase option, natural-resource exploration/exploitation leases, and leases not relating to assets needed for productive/service/goods activity.
- Article 5 requires FRA registry for financial leasing contracts and related sale contracts/amendments; registration details include asset, parties, capacity and term.
- Further provisions regulate lease contracts, factoring assignment of receivables, notice to debtors, registration, rights/obligations, recourse, insolvency, licensing, capital, governance, disclosure, records and penalties.

## 2. Consumer Finance Law 18/2020

### Sources
- FRA updated law PDF (2022 version):
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/قانون-نشاط-التمويل-الاستهلاكي-3.pdf
- Official Gazette PDF:
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/UG50279UG50280.pdf
- FRA law page:
  https://fra.gov.eg/regulations/قانون-رقم-18-لسنه-2020-باصدار-قانون-تنظيم-نشاط/
- Ministry of Finance record:
  https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation
- Full text:
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12752
  https://www.eglf.org/قانون-18-لسنة-2020/
- Gazette issue 11 bis (k), 17 March 2020; effective 18 March 2020.
- Amendment Law 158/2022 appears in FRA page's previous-legislation list; obtain Gazette PDF and map exact affected provisions.

### Core provisions
- Article 1 defines consumer finance as financing intended to purchase goods/services for consumer purposes on a regular basis, including commercial payment cards or CBE-approved payment methods.
- Promulgation Article 3 excludes consumer financing activities conducted by banks supervised by CBE, even if financing consumer purchases.
- Article 3 prohibits licensed consumer-finance companies/providers from accepting deposits.
- Article 4 allows registration of security rights over movables securing consumer finance in Movable Collateral Register under Law 115/2015.
- Article 5 requires strict confidentiality of customer information, subject to statutory exceptions.
- Article 6 requires FRA marketing/advertising controls, especially disclosure of how interest/return is calculated.
- Article 12 requires companies to comply with FRA Board rules on governance, capital adequacy, financing ratios based on customer repayment capacity, impairment/provisions, branches and AML/CFT.
- Article 16 imposes parallel solvency, repayment-capacity, impairment/provision and AML/CFT controls on other licensed consumer-finance providers.
- Article 17 establishes Egyptian Federation of Consumer Finance Entities with separate legal personality under FRA supervision.
- Further chapters regulate consumer protection, disclosure, complaints, inspections, records, enforcement and penalties.

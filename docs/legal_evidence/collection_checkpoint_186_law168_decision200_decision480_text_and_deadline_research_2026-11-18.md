## 2. Commencement and filing deadline — improved legal analysis, still not finally certified

### Commencement

Decision 200/2026 was published 18 January 2026. Its issuing Article 4 says it operates from the day after publication. Commencement of the Executive Regulation is therefore **19 January 2026**. The EastLaws catalogue independently labels the effective date as 19 January 2026.

### Initial six-month period

Law 168/2025 Article 3(1) starts the six-month filing period from the date the Executive Regulation comes into force. The relevant trigger is therefore 19 January 2026.

Evidence of the competing deadline:
- Cairo Governorate's official regularization page, published 21 June 2026, repeatedly says the last day is **18 July 2026**.
- The State Land Recovery Committee coordinator, in a Shorouk interview published 21 August 2026, described the initial period as having expired on **18 July 2026** and said a further six-month extension had been decided, with the presidential decree still being prepared.
- Civil Code Article 3 requires Gregorian calendar calculation.
- Civil and Commercial Procedure Law Article 15 provides that, for periods in days, months or years, the day of the triggering event is not counted; the period expires at the end of its last day. It requires Gregorian calculation for months/years unless otherwise specified.
- A Court of Cassation principle reported by the Egyptian Bar Association says this general rule is followed in calculating periods in other branches of law and that a period in months runs from the end of the triggering day to the end of the corresponding day in the final month.

Applying the procedural calculation rule to a 19 January trigger points to **19 July 2026**, not 18 July. The official Cairo Governorate notice and the committee coordinator's statement both say 18 July. The one-day discrepancy remains unresolved; the government notice may be the operational date but does not reconcile the statutory calculation.

### Effect of Presidential Decision 480/2026

The decision's Article 1 says the period in the first paragraph of Article 3 is extended "for another similar period". Article 2 only directs publication. The two articles do not name a new calendar start or end date.

There are therefore two legally distinct interpretations requiring an authoritative instrument/circular:
1. **Extension appended to the original period:** six further months after the initial period. Using the official operational date 18 July gives **18 January 2027**; applying the general period-calculation rule to the 19 January trigger gives **19 January 2027**.
2. **New period running from publication/effectiveness of Decision 480:** publication is 10 November 2026; if the additional six months runs from that event, a candidate endpoint is **10 or 11 May 2027**, depending on the applicable commencement/counting rule.

The August committee-coordinator interview strongly supports the first interpretation as the intended administrative extension (six months after 18 July), but it predates the actual Gazette publication of Decision 480 on 10 November and is not a substitute for the signed decision or an authoritative implementing circular. No official post-publication notice specifying a final calendar date was found in this pass.

**Research conclusion:** do not certify a final deadline yet. The most defensible operational planning date from government messaging is 18 January 2027, with 19 January 2027 the alternative under the general corresponding-date calculation rule. The May 2027 candidate is conditional on measuring the new period from Decision 480's publication/effectiveness. Obtain a written clarification from the competent authority or State Land Recovery Committee before treating any date as definitive. For risk control, a filer should not rely on the later candidate without written confirmation.

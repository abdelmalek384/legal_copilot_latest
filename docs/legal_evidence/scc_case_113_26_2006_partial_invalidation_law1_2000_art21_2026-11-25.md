# Supreme Constitutional Court judgment — Case 113/26 (constitutional), 15 January 2006

## Source and identity
- **Court:** Supreme Constitutional Court of Egypt.
- **Case:** No. 113 of Judicial Year 26, Constitutional.
- **Judgment date:** 15 January 2006.
- **Challenged provision:** Article 21 of Law 1/2000 (Personal Status Litigation Procedures Law).
- **Full judgment reproduction:** LAW HUB, https://lawhub.info/eg-j/?p=24404 (page identifies the judgment and reproduces its full text and final disposition).
- **Corroborating sources:** https://mshrf.com/عدم-دستورية-قصر-الاعتداد-فى-إثبات-الطلاق-عند-الإنكار-على-الإشهادوالتوثيق/ ; https://kenanaonline.com/users/basune1/posts/919926
- **Publication:** secondary sources report Official Gazette issue 24 bis, 24 January 2006. Original Gazette page and official SCC page were not located in this pass.

## Exact scope of invalidation
The Court declared Article 21 unconstitutional **only insofar as it restricts recognition of proof of divorce upon denial to witnessing and documentation**, excluding other legally admissible methods of proof.

The judgment's reasoning section expressly confines the case to this part of Article 21. The rest of the article's provisions — notarial warnings, reconciliation, recording the steps, attendance/notification and related procedures — were outside the scope of the constitutional challenge and were not invalidated by this judgment.

Do not delete or mark the whole of Article 21 as unconstitutional. Preserve the surviving text and annotate only the restricted proof clause.

## Effective date
Under Article 49 of Law 48/1979, constitutional judgments ordinarily make the invalidated text non-applicable from the day after publication unless the judgment specifies another date. With secondary publication metadata of 24 January 2006, EV-0369 provisionally records 25 January 2006. The original Gazette and any express date in the judgment must be checked.

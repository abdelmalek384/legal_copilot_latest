# Collection checkpoint 13 — cybercrime and consumer-protection implementing instruments (2026-11-17)

## 1. Cybercrime Law 175/2018 — Arabic full-text transcription located

- Full Arabic transcription and PDF/Word download links on secondary legal archive:
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11428
- Law title: قانون مكافحة جرائم تقنية المعلومات رقم 175 لسنة 2018.
- The record links Executive Regulation Decision 1699/2020.
- This is not yet the official Gazette file. Use it as a transcription aid only and compare against the original Gazette.

### Important provisions exposed in the indexed text (transcription, not primary-source verification)
- Article 2 requires service providers to retain and store specified system/service data for 180 consecutive days, including user-identification data, certain content under provider control, connection-traffic data, terminal-device data and other data specified by NTRA Board decision.
- Article 6 allows a reasoned investigative order for specified seizure, collection, preservation, tracking, search and delivery of stored service/user/traffic data, with defined time limits and judicial challenge procedures.
- Articles 7–8 concern judicially reviewable website blocking in specified national-security/economic-risk cases.
- Article 11 gives qualifying digital evidence evidentiary value equivalent to material criminal evidence when the technical conditions in the Executive Regulation are met.
- Article 44 provides for an Executive Regulation; the secondary archive identifies Decision 1699/2020.
- The law contains offences involving unauthorized access, interception, data/system damage, misuse of payment-card data, and other conduct. Do not use a secondary transcription for charging decisions or penalty calculations without Gazette verification and amendment review.

## 3. Consumer Protection Law 181/2018 and Executive Regulation 822/2019

- Consumer Protection Agency official legislation index lists Law 181/2018:
  https://cpa.gov.eg/ar-eg/تشريعات
- CPA official publication (25 September 2021) expressly identifies Executive Regulation issued by Prime Minister Decision 822/2019:
  https://cpa.gov.eg/ar-eg/الجهاز-في-الصحف/ArtMID/481/ArticleID/6615
- The CPA publication states that the Regulation requires complaints affecting consumer health/safety to be brought to the Chair/Executive Director, and that consumers/consumer associations may complain to the Agency without a fee.
- This is an official regulator confirmation of Decision 822/2019, but not the full Regulation text. Obtain the Gazette copy and compare any amendments.

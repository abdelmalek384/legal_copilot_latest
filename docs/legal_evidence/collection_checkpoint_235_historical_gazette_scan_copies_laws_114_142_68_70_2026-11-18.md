## 2. What this does and does not establish

- **Established:** a PDF scan copy of each identified original Gazette/Egyptian Proceedings issue is publicly accessible through Manshurat; the issue number/date and page count are recorded above.
- **Not established:** that the hosted PDF bytes are the original Amiri Press digital export, that they are certified copies, or that the host's copy is byte-identical to the official publisher's archive.
- **Not completed:** local binary acquisition, SHA-256 hashing, page-by-page comparison with official Amiri Press files, or a certificate of authenticity.
- **Rights:** public access is not a licence. All four permission records keep collection and commercial reuse permissions unknown. No bulk extraction, commercial display, republication or redistribution permission is claimed.

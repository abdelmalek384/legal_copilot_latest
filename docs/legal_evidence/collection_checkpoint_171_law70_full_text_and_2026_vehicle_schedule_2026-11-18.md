# Collection checkpoint 171 — Law 70/1964 complete searchable text and 2026 vehicle schedule
**Date:** 18 November 2026

## New text acquisition

### Law 70/1964 — Notarization and Registration Fees
- Recovered full searchable Arabic text for 44 numbered provisions and Schedules A, B and C from the Al-Hasswa legal library, which states it reproduces the Court of Cassation portal and is updated through 13 June 2021.
- Original scan metadata from Manshurat confirms Gazette issue67, 22 March 1964, issued 21 March 1964 and effective 22 March 1964.
- Captured article text/structured summaries for Articles1–39, inserted Articles24 bis and34 bis/34 bis1/34 bis2/34 bis3, and the three schedules.
- Schedule A covers registrable instruments (real rights, mortgages/privileges, partition, leases and related acts); Schedule B covers non-registrable instruments (movables, companies, construction contracts, debts, guarantees, leases, etc.); Schedule C covers civil-status declarations and related powers of attorney.
- Article2's list numbering is malformed in the secondary text. Schedule rows have not yet been normalized or compared with the Gazette scan; no fee should be operationalized solely from this transcription.

### Amendment chain recovered from footnotes
- Law94/1980: changes Articles21/22 valuation and fee schedule bands.
- Law6/1991: replaces Articles21,25,26,27; adds provisions to Article19; inserts 24 bis,34 bis and34 bis1; repeals Article20; updates Article32 thresholds.
- Law224/1996: replaces first paragraph of Article24 bis and Article34 bis.
- Law225/1996: repeals Article21(8).
- Law9/2003: adds Article34 bis2.
- SCC Case2/24: invalidates Article30 (Gazette issue52 bis, 31 Dec 2003).
- Law3/2004: adds Article34 bis3.
- Law83/2006: replaces Article19.
- SCC Case10/16: invalidates Article21(9) (Gazette issue21 bis (أ), 24 May 2014).
- Law163/2019: adds a Schedule B item.
- Law67/2021: changes the penultimate Schedule B item to refer to construction companies.

### Decision 7657/2025 — 2026 vehicle valuation schedule
- Amiri Press searchable text confirms three issuing articles and that the attached vehicle values under Article21(II) apply from 1 January through 31 December 2026. Decision signed 25 November 2025; published in Egyptian Proceedings issue16, 20 January 2026; Article3 states effect from 1 January 2026.
- It supersedes Decision278/2025, which applied through 31 December 2025.
- Attached vehicle schedule values remain to be extracted and visually checked; Decision7657 is an annual valuation instrument, not a statutory amendment.

- Official regulator/public portal hosting does not itself grant commercial republication or bulk indexing rights.
- Keep primary official PDFs, Gazette reproductions, secondary transcriptions, press releases and guidance in separate provenance classes.
- Preserve source URL, publisher, Gazette issue/date/page, retrieval timestamp, file SHA-256, exact Arabic text and amendment event.
- Mark text 'source-matched, not lawyer-reviewed' only after comparison to an authoritative text source.
- No 'verified' label until a qualified Egyptian lawyer signs off.

### Operational/legal significance (research note, not legal advice)

- Factoring companies must query invoices proposed for factoring through the FRA-designated electronic system during credit assessment and before financing.
- They must ensure that the same receivable has not already been factored and keep the invoices renewed/registered on the system for the financing-contract term.
- They must pay the applicable system-service fee under FRA rules.
- The factoring contract with the seller must include evidence that the security right arising from the financing has been registered in the movable-collateral register under Law 115/2015.
- The exact Arabic uses "عقد التخصيص" in the text extracted on page 11; preserve the Gazette wording exactly and do not silently normalize it to "التخصيم". A visual check of the printed page is required before treating this potentially unusual word as definitively printed rather than OCR/extraction error.

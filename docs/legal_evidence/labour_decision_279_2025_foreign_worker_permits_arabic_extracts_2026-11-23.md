## Selected Arabic text extracts

### Article 1 — definitions (printed page 32)

**مادة (1)**

«في تطبيق أحكام هذا القرار يقصد بالكلمات والعبارات التالية المعنى المبين قرين كل منها:»

- «الوزارة: الوزارة المعنية بشئون العمل.»
- «السلطة المختصة: وزير العمل.»
- «الإدارة المختصة: الإدارة العامة لتراخيص وتنظيم عمل الأجانب.»
- «القانون: قانون العمل الصادر بالقانون رقم 14 لسنة 2025.»
- «العمل: كل عمل تابع أو حر أو لحساب النفس أو العمل في أي مهنة أو صنعة أو حرفة بما في ذلك العمل في الخدمة المنزلية.»

### Article 2 — scope and reciprocity (printed page 32)

**مادة (2)**

Extracted text:

«يخضع عمل الأجانب في جميع منشآت القطاع الخاص، ووحدات القطاع العام، وقطاع الأعمال العام، والهيئات العامة والإدارة المحلية، والجهاز الإداري للدولة للأحكام الواردة في القانون وهذا القرار، وذلك مع مراعاة شرط المعاملة بالمثل، ويجوز الإعفاء من هذا الشرط بناء على عرض الإدارة المختصة.»

### Article 3 — work-permit requirement (printed page 32)

**مادة (3)**

Extracted text:

«لا يجوز للأجنبي أن يزاول عملاً إلا بعد الحصول على ترخيص بذلك من الوزارة المختصة، أو من مديريات العمل التابعة لها، أو المكاتب المنشأة من الوزارة لهذا الغرض في الجهات الأخرى، مثل الهيئة العامة للاستثمار والهيئة العامة للبترول وغيرها.»

The reproduced text further requires the foreigner to be authorised to enter and reside in Egypt for the purpose of work, and prohibits employers from employing foreign workers before the permit is obtained.

### Article 4 — permit exemptions (printed pages 32–33)

**مادة (4)**

The extracted list includes:
1. Persons exempt under an express provision of an international agreement to which Egypt is party, within the agreement's scope.
2. Administrative employees working in embassies and consulates of Arab and foreign states and organisations/agencies affiliated with regional and international organisations in Egypt, arriving under decisions of their home authorities.
3. Foreign correspondents working in Egypt.
4. Foreign clergy practising without remuneration.
5. Foreign workers on Egyptian vessels on the high seas outside territorial waters.
6. Foreign workers with the Commonwealth War Graves Commission.
7. Members and experts of institutes, centres and missions working on ancient Egyptian architectural and archaeological research.
8. Foreign investors holding investor residence for their activity in Egypt under the governing law.

The list is reproduced from the secondary PDF; verify the exact category wording and any later exemptions against the Gazette image and bilateral instruments.

### Article 5 — notice for exempt foreign workers (printed page 33)

**مادة (5)**

Extracted text requires anyone employing a foreigner exempt from the work-permit condition to notify the Labour Directorate in whose jurisdiction the establishment is located within seven days of the foreigner's starting work and again when the service ends.

### Article 7 — professional experience and national-labour conditions (printed page 33)

**مادة (7)**

Visible conditions include:
- foreigner's experience must be appropriate to the occupation and not less than three years, duly certified by competent authorities;
- where the occupation legally requires a special licence, the foreigner must hold it;
- the foreigner must not displace national labour;
- the country's economic need and the establishment's actual need are relevant;
- establishments permitted to use foreign experts/technicians must appoint two Egyptian assistants whose qualifications fit the foreigner's experience, train them in the work and prepare periodic progress reports. The competent authority may waive some or all conditions on a reasoned recommendation supported by documents.

### Article 8 — prohibited occupations (printed page 33)

**مادة (8)**

The visible text prohibits foreigners from working in:
- «الإرشاد السياحي»
- «أعمال التصدير والتخليص الجمركي» — with an express exception for Palestinians in the extracted text.

This narrow list should not be expanded by inference.

### Article 9 — maximum foreign-worker percentage (printed page 33)

**مادة (9)**

The extracted text states:

«لا يجوز أن يزيد عدد العاملين الأجانب في أى منشأة ولو تعددت فروعها عن (10٪) من مجموع عدد العاملين المصريين بها.»

The article then lists exceptions. Do not implement the 10% ceiling without checking the full exception list, denominator wording and any sector-specific rules on the printed page.

### Articles 10–11 — exception committee (printed page 33)

The extracted text establishes a committee chaired by the Director General of the General Administration for Licensing and Regulating Foreigners' Work, with representatives from legal affairs, domestic employment, labour inspection and the foreigners' work administration. It decides applications for exemption from the foreign-worker percentage ceiling, and may invite relevant bodies and experts without voting rights.

### Article 13 — foreign-worker register and recurring reporting (printed pages 42–43)

**مادة (13)**

The extracted text requires establishments employing foreign workers to keep a register containing, among other things:
- foreign worker's name in Arabic and English, surname, date of birth, nationality and passport number;
- occupation, qualifications, type of work and wage;
- date of entry into Egypt, work-permit number, issuance and expiry dates;
- names of Egyptian assistants assigned to train alongside a foreign expert.

It also requires the establishment to provide the competent Ministry administration with a detailed foreign-worker statement, including numbers and their proportion to Egyptian labour. In the first week of January and July each year, it must provide a list of foreign workers, nationalities, occupations, permit numbers/dates/expiry, assistants, and a statement of Egyptian workers and insured-worker documentation.

### Article 32 — regularisation fee for irregular foreign employment (printed page 40)

**مادة (32)**

The extracted text permits a foreigner who worked in Egypt without the required permit to apply to the competent Ministry to regularise their status. If approved, the foreigner pays a fee described as an exemption from the recruitment condition of **EGP 15,000**. The competent authority may exempt or reduce the fee in certain cases on a reasoned submission from the competent administration.

**High-impact amount:** verify against the Gazette image and current amendments before any use.

### Article 34 — renewal procedure (printed page 40)

The extracted text requires the foreign worker to submit a renewal application at least two months before the permit expires, using the prescribed form to the competent Directorate or Ministry office. For renewal from the fourth year onward, the request is submitted to the Ministry's competent administration with reasons for renewal.

### Article 35 — unexcused absence reporting (printed page 40)

**مادة (35)**

The employer must notify the competent Labour Directorate and relevant authorities if a foreign worker is absent from work for 15 consecutive days without lawful justification, within seven actual working days after the 15-day period ends.

### Article 36 — notice to worker following five days' absence (printed page 40)

The extracted text requires the employer to notify the foreign worker at the fixed address in the service file after five consecutive working days of absence without excuse. The remaining text should be checked visually before operational use.

### Article 37 — foreign-worker data system (printed pages 42–43)

**مادة (37)**

The competent Ministry administration must create foreign-worker data records. The visible fields include the foreigner's Arabic/English name, date of birth, nationality and passport number; establishment name; occupation and qualifications; work type and wage; date of entry; work-permit number and issue/expiry dates; Egyptian assistants assigned to acquire expertise; and number of insured Egyptian workers.

### Article 38 — foreign-worker rights and duties (printed page 43)

**مادة (38)**

Extracted text:

«تسرى على العمالة الأجنبية عقود العمل الفردية واتفاقيات العمل الجماعية، وفيما لم يرد بشأنه نص خاص فيهما، تسرى عليهم كافة أحكام قانون العمل.»

### Article 39 — incentives and regularisation support (printed page 43)

The extracted text assigns the competent Ministry the task of announcing incentive initiatives to help move foreign employment from the informal to the formal sector without imposing financial or procedural penalties, including reducing or waiving fees or the work-permit requirement.

### Article 40 — closing provision (printed page 44)

The extracted text states that the Decision is published in Egyptian Proceedings and takes effect on the day following publication.

## Critical legal and product-review flags

1. **Percentage denominator:** Article 9's reproduced text says 10% of the total number of Egyptian workers, not necessarily 10% of total workforce. Preserve the exact denominator and confirm exceptions before implementing a compliance calculation.
2. **Exceptions:** Articles 9–11 and Article 19 contain exception procedures/categories; the whole list and current status must be reviewed before building automated eligibility decisions.
3. **Reciprocity:** Article 2 makes reciprocity a condition across a broad set of public/private establishments, with possible waiver. This requires bilateral-treaty review; do not infer a country's status from nationality alone.
4. **Fees:** The PDF includes a multi-year permit fee schedule and nationality/reciprocity exemptions. Only part of the fee table was visible in the extracted snippet; do not copy or calculate amounts until the full printed table is visually checked.
5. **Regularisation:** Article 32's EGP 15,000 fee and waiver discretion are high-impact and require currentness verification.
6. **Personal data:** Articles 13 and 37 require detailed foreign-worker personal and employment data, including passport and nationality information. Any Legal Copilot workflow must separately review lawful basis, minimisation, retention, access control and security under Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020.
7. **Employer conduct:** Article 36 onward contains worker-protection rules; the visible Article 36/related provisions prohibit passport retention, restriction of movement, wage deductions and nationality/race discrimination. Verify exact article numbering and complete text from page images.
8. **Currentness:** Decision 279 repeals prior conflicting decisions/texts in its Article 39 as extracted. Identify the exact superseded foreign-work-permit rules and later amendments/circulars before asserting the full old regime is repealed.

### Exact Arabic text excerpt (Article 2; secondary Gazette facsimile)

> نسبة (٠,٢٥٪) من الحد الأدنى للأجر التأميني ... بحد أدنى عشرة جنيهات، وبحد أقصى ثلاثون جنيهاً عن كل عامل

This is the operative contribution formula, not a generic 1% levy. The amount is calculated against the minimum insured wage; the statutory trigger is 30 or more workers and the per-worker annual floor/ceiling is EGP 10/30. Store this as a dated rule and avoid calculating an amount without the applicable minimum insured wage and worker count.

### Material transition / supersession issue

- Labour Law 14/2025 issuance Article 2 continues the Training and Qualification Fund created under the former Labour Law 12/2003.
- Labour Law 14/2025 Article 20 sets the Fund's statutory functions and delegates the board's formation, other powers, working system, financial treatment, branches, basic regulations and collection/accounting rules to a PM decision.
- PM Decision 3356/2026 now supplies the current governance and operational framework and expressly repeals PM Decision 1543/2003. The 0.25% contribution formula itself is prescribed by Labour Law Article 21; Decision 3356 Article 2 mirrors that statutory resource rule, while Article 3 delegates only the full-exemption rules to a separate Minister of Labour decision.
- PM Decision 3356 Article 3 leaves a further ministerial decision outstanding: rules/conditions for full exemption from the 0.25% contribution where establishments train their own workers.
- Do not assume that the former 1% training-fund contribution under Law 12/2003 remains collectible after the new statutory transition and PM Decision 3356/2026. Reconcile issuance Article 2, Labour Law Article 20, Decision 3356 Article 2, the old 1% litigation savings and any later ministerial exemption/collection rules before encoding liabilities.

## 3. Additional Labour Law 14/2025 currentness update — PM Decision 982/2026 remote work

The Cabinet meeting dated 30 September 2026 approved a *draft decision* to suspend PM Decision 982/2026, which had required Sunday remote work during specified monthly periods. Official Cabinet record: https://www.cabinet.gov.eg/Meeting/Details/11910 (date and draft-approval item 1).

The Cabinet approval is not the final signed/published suspension decision. No final decision number, Gazette issue/date/page or commencement clause for the suspension instrument was located as of 21 November 2026. PM Decisions 1305, 1572, 2033, 2377 and 2590/2026 were month-specific extensions through September; they do not create an open-ended extension. Keep Decision 982 historically versioned and its operative status unresolved pending the signed instrument.

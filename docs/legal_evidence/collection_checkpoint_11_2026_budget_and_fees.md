- Article 11: from 1 July 2026, 15% of monthly revenues of special funds/accounts and special-purpose units and self-financing public service authorities with carried-forward surpluses is allocated to the State Treasury, subject to exceptions (including certain research/educational projects, grants/donations/international-agreement-funded projects, university hospitals, social housing, health-service accounts and staff welfare/insurance funds). Higher percentages in approved regulations remain relevant.
- Article 12: one-time transfer of balances in certain special funds/accounts as at 30 June 2026, on a tiered basis: 5% of balances from EGP 5m up to EGP 7.5m; 10% of balances above EGP 7.5m up to EGP 15m; 15% of balances above EGP 15m. Multiple categories are excluded, including research-project funds funded by grants/donations/international agreements, university hospitals/research centres/health directorates, social housing projects, employee welfare funds and private insurance funds.
- Article 12 also provides for a one-time 10% transfer of specified carried-forward surpluses/investment balances of public service/economic/national authorities, subject to its text and exceptions.
- Article 17: effective from 1 July 2026.

## 3. Relationships and caution

- Law 77/2026 is an annual appropriations/budget instrument, not a permanent general tax or finance code.
- Link it to Unified Public Finance Law 6/2022 and its amendment Law 18/2024, plus Executive Regulation Decision 73/2024 and amendment Decision 94/2025.
- Preserve all exclusions, percentage bases, reference dates, payment deadlines and authority-specific exceptions as conditions; a simplified "15% sweep" statement without exclusions would be misleading.
- Ministry portal warns its electronic texts are guidance and Official Gazette is the legal reference.
- Lawyer review and commercial reuse rights remain separate gates.

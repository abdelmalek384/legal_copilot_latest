## 1. Why this crosswalk matters

The Executive Regulation contains an express transitional rule for the pre-existing hazardous-materials and hazardous-waste lists. Decision 151/2025 later adopted a unified list and states that the list applies from its Gazette publication date. This establishes a clear statutory transition milestone for research and retrieval, but does not justify silently deleting every older sectoral list or assuming that all separate technical requirements in those lists were repealed.

## 2. Primary Arabic text captured

The Amiri Press searchable text for PM Decision 722/2022 returns the following relevant provisions.

**مادة (٤٧)**  
تختص اللجنة المشار إليها فى المادة السابقة بوضع وإصدار ومراجعة القوائم الموحدة للمواد والمخلفات الخطرة، ووضع ضوابط واشتراطات التداول والإدارة المتكاملة للمواد والمخلفات الخطرة وتحديد أسلوب الحد من تولدها.  
كما تختص بتحديد الوزارة أو الجهة المعنية المسئولة عن إصدار تراخيص التداول والإدارة المتكاملة للمواد والمخلفات الخطرة.  
ويصدر كل وزير أو مسئول عن الوزارات والجهات المبينة فى المادة السابقة، كل فى نطاق اختصاصه، قرارًا يحدد فيه الجهة المسئولة عن هذا الشأن داخل هذه الوزارات أو الجهات.  
ويقوم الجهاز بالتنسيق مع تلك الجهات فى وضع وتنظيم آليات وإجراءات مراقبة تداول المواد والمخلفات الخطرة، واتخاذ ما يلزم من تدابير فى هذا الشأن، كما يتولى تسجيل الشركات التى تمارس أى نشاط من أنشطة الإدارة المتكاملة للمخلفات الخطرة.

**مادة (٥٦)**  
يستمر العمل بقوائم المواد والمخلفات الخطرة الصادرة عن الجهات المعنية لحين إصدار اللجنة الفنية المنصوص عليها فى المادة (٥٣) من القانون للقوائم الموحدة للمواد والمخلفات الخطرة.

## 3. Crosswalk with Decision 151/2025

Decision 151/2025 is recorded as issued 12 June 2025 and published in Egyptian Proceedings issue 163 (supplement), 27 July 2025. Its captured operative clauses state:

**(المادة الأولى)**  
تعتمد القائمة الموحدة للمواد والمخلفات الخطرة المرفقة بهذا القرار.

**(المادة الثانية)**  
تنشر بالوقائع المصرية، ويعمل بهذه القائمة اعتبارًا من تاريخ نشرها بالوقائع المصرية.

The SIS catalogue identifies the issue and a 92.21 MB PDF attachment. The annex itself remains unacquired in the current repository note.

### Limited legal inference

- Article 56 of the 2022 Executive Regulation made continued use of pre-existing authority-issued lists transitional, ending when the statutory technical committee issued the unified lists.
- Decision 151/2025 is the located instrument expressly adopting the unified list, and its Article 2 fixes application from Gazette publication.
- Accordingly, 27 July 2025 is the evidenced transition milestone for the Article 56 interim arrangement, subject to confirmation against the Gazette page images and the complete annex.
- This is not an express repeal clause for every earlier ministerial/sectoral instrument. Do not assert that all old lists, technical conditions, licensing allocations or independent legal requirements were automatically repealed in full.
- Until the annex is acquired, do not make article-level or material-code-level assertions about which prior entries were included, changed, omitted or reclassified.

## 4. Operational impact for the legal corpus

1. Preserve Decision 151/2025 as the canonical instrument adopting the unified list; do not use the older sector-specific lists as an unqualified current consolidated list.
2. Keep each old list as a historical/source record with its issuing authority, date, subject, and independent legal basis where known.
3. Mark old-list status as **transitional endpoint reached / item-level supersession unresolved**, not **repealed** unless a separate express repeal or amendment is located.
4. Do not ingest the older lists as current authoritative classification without comparing them against the Decision 151 annex and subsequent instruments.
5. Decision 121/2025 export controls refer to the unified lists and are related, but their own operative requirements must be preserved independently.
6. The later committee reconstitution reports in January 2026 do not, by themselves, amend Decision 151 or prove that the list was revised.

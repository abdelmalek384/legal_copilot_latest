## 4. Decision 480/2026 commencement and filing deadline

- PM Decision 200/2026 was published 18 January 2026 and expressly takes effect the following day, 19 January 2026.
- Law 168/2025 Article 3(1) measures the initial six-month period from commencement of the Executive Regulation. Cairo Governorate stated 18 July 2026 as the last filing day; CPC Article 15 may produce a one-day calculation difference.
- Decision 480/2026 was published 10 November 2026. Article 1 extends the period for “another equivalent period”; Article 2 only directs publication. It does not specify commencement, a new start date, a final calendar date, or treatment of applications lodged during the 20 July–10 November 2026 gap.
- **10 May 2027** is the leading publication-based working calculation if the new six-month period begins after publication on 10 November (11 November 2026–10 May 2027 under Article 15-style counting).
- **18 January 2027** is the alternative consecutive-extension calculation if the new period is appended to the original official 18 July 2026 deadline (19 July 2026–18 January 2027).
- Neither date is expressly printed in Decision 480 or officially confirmed. Obtain a written competent-authority circular or interpretation before treating either date as the final deadline. The commencement date itself is not stated in the decision.

See [checkpoint 227](collection_checkpoint_227_decision480_commencement_deadline_reanalysis_2026-11-18.md) and the updated EG-0175 acquisition record.

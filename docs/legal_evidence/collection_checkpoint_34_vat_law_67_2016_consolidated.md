## Structure and core provisions

### Promulgation
- Article 1: attached law governs VAT.
- Article 2: repeals General Sales Tax Law 11/1991 and conflicting rules; transition of pending appeals to new committees after three months.
- Articles 3–9: replace sales-tax authority name, preserve registration in specified cases, final returns/records and input-tax balances, status regularization and exemption for treaty-based exemptions; Executive Regulation to issue within 30 days; commencement day after publication.

### Articles 1–11: definitions, charge, rate and valuation
- Article 1 defines taxable person, registered person, related persons, service supplier, importer, resident, permanent establishment, VAT, additional tax, input tax, table tax, goods/services, sale, invoice, tax period, simplified non-resident supplier registration, reverse charge, personal consumption and private use.
- Article 2 imposes VAT on goods/services (including table goods/services), domestic and imported, through all stages unless specifically exempt.
- Article 3 general rate: 13% in FY 2016/2017 and 14% from FY 2017/2018; medical equipment/machinery used to produce goods/services is 5%, excluding buses/passenger cars; exports are zero-rated subject to conditions.
- Article 4 liable persons collect, return and remit tax.
- Article 5 tax arises on sale/service performance; imports taxed at customs release and later domestic circulation; imported services taxed when supplied to recipients in Egypt; transit goods/services excluded under customs supervision; use for personal consumption/private use treated as sale; non-resident simplified supplier may collect import VAT.
- Articles 6–7: free-zone/industrial/economic-zone zero-rating for licensed activities; domestic consumption in zones/imports into local market taxable.
- Article 8: tax on goods held at cessation/liquidation unless successor is registered.
- Article 9: smuggled/non-compliant sales taxed at rates in force at offence or discovery.
- Article 10: taxable value is consideration paid/payable plus incidental amounts; related-party price cannot be below arm's-length market; barter uses market value; private use uses total cost; personal consumption uses market value; instalment interest above CBE credit/discount rate included; import valuation uses customs base plus customs/other duties; separate special rules for free zones, precious metals and table goods/services.
- Article 11: VAT added to prices, including administered/controlled prices; existing contracts adjusted for VAT burden/rate changes.

### Articles 12–21: invoicing and registration
- Articles 12–13 and 19–20 shown as repealed by Unified Tax Procedures Law 206/2020.
- Articles 14–15 cover ETA estimate where no return filed and limits on additional tax after three years in specified amended-return context.
- Article 16: registration threshold originally EGP 500,000 over prior 12 months; importers/exporters/distribution agents and specified producers/importers must register regardless of turnover.
- Article 17: simplified registration for non-resident supplier providing taxable goods/services to non-registered customers in Egypt; reverse-charge registration for legal persons with imported-service obligations; phase-in for services then goods.
- Article 18 permits voluntary registration below threshold.
- Article 21: ETA may cancel registration under Executive Regulation conditions.

### Articles 22–30: input credit, exemptions, refund and capital assets
- Article 22 allows input tax credit for tax previously paid/charged on returns and inputs, subject to regulations; no credit for table tax (unless specific exception), input tax included in cost, exempt goods/services and simplified non-resident supplier cases.
- Article 23 diplomatic/consular and other treaty-based exemptions, subject to reciprocity and limits.
- Article 24 additional exemptions under law.
- Article 28 bis added by Law 3/2022 and replaced by Law 149/2026: suspension of VAT on medical machinery/equipment for local factories/production units for one year, extendable up to aggregate three years; tax exempted if used in industrial production; disposition restriction for five years; tax and additional tax due retroactively if not used within time; detailed conditions in Executive Regulation.
- Article 30 governs refund/credit balance; 2026 amendment allows refund after more than four consecutive tax periods and after three months for eligible Law 6/2025 projects up to EGP 20m turnover.
- Articles 27, 28, 30 and 31 were amended by Law 3/2022; exact version history needed.

### Articles 31–50 and 67 bis: procedural and enforcement provisions
- Article 31 as amended by Law 3/2022 covers refund/credit and related rules.
- Article 32 contains reverse-charge rules and imported services.
- Article 50 and 67 bis are added by Law 3/2022; later procedural rules in Law 206/2020 supersede/repeal various older return/appeal provisions.
- Some provisions in VAT Law were repealed by Law 206/2020; do not rely on historical original numbering without current amendment graph.

## Schedules (high priority for exact structured ingestion)
- Table of goods/services subject to table tax contains rates/amounts for oils, processed food, fertilizers, pesticides, construction/contracting, inter-governorate transport, professional/investment services, media/films, goodwill/customer connections, crude oil, natural gas (EGP 20 per 1,000 cubic feet from Law 149/2026), beverages, cosmetics, appliances, vehicles, mobile telecom.
- Exempt-goods/services schedule includes dairy, infant foods, eggs, tea/sugar/coffee, flour/bread/pasta, animals, meat, fish, agricultural produce, food products, utilities, postage, land/buildings/rent, money, civil aircraft, medical devices, prosthetics, dialysis equipment, vaccines/blood, banking, currency exchange, postal financial services, non-bank financial services, insurance/reinsurance, education/research, health, passenger transport, places of worship, libraries/museums, arts/publishing, agriculture, union/sports subscriptions, burial services, assistive devices, medicines, international education, charitable health advertising and Suez Canal services.
- Law 149/2026 amended exemption items 19, 20, 28, 32, 35, 36; added natural gas table item 17; removed table items 8 and 10.
- Law 157/2025 amended table entries for oils, alcoholic beverages, construction/contracting and exempt natural materials/advertising, introduced annual table-tax increases for specified alcohol categories (15% annually for three years then 12%), and other schedule changes.
- Law 3/2022 amended numerous table and exemption schedule items.
- Tables must be stored as structured rows with effective dates, unit, rate/amount, scope and source page; do not flatten into article prose.

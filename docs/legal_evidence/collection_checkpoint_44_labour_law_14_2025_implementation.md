## 1. Law 14/2025 — promulgation and commencement
- Applies to employment relationships, including foreign workers in Egypt where contracts/collective agreements do not provide otherwise.
- Excludes State administrative employees and domestic workers unless special provisions apply.
- Continues the Training and Qualification Fund established under repealed Law 12/2003 with legal personality and functions under the new law.
- Pending lawsuits about the former 1% Training Fund contribution are extinguished unless the establishment elects to continue within six months; no refund of amounts already paid.
- Minister responsible for labour must issue implementing decisions within 90 days of commencement; until then, existing decisions continue insofar as not inconsistent.
- Minister of Justice issues decisions for specialized labour courts.
- Repeals Labour Law 12/2003, Law 125/2010 on workers' privileged debt rank and conflicting provisions.
- Published 3 May 2025; effective from first day of month following 90 days after publication: 1 September 2025. Specialized labour courts commence 1 October 2025.

## 3. Important implementation design: no single omnibus Executive Regulation
- Article 11 of promulgation directs the Labour Minister to issue implementing decisions within 90 days; it preserves prior decisions until replaced where not inconsistent.
- Therefore implementation corpus must be a collection of ministerial/PM/Justice decisions, not a single guessed "Executive Regulation".
- The Ministry's official website maintains a laws/decisions catalogue and older implementing instruments:
  https://www.labour.gov.eg/en/laws-and-legislations/

## 4. Specific implementing decisions and circulars identified
- Ministerial Decision 187/2025: controls/mechanisms for approval of employee resignations.
- Ministerial Decision 260/2025: identifies competent administrative authority for implementing Labour Law 14/2025.
- Ministerial Decision 294/2025: identifies full-pay public holidays for workers; Article 129 of Law 14/2025 is statutory basis.
- Labour Ministry general rules for internal work regulations in private-sector establishments with 10 or more workers, announced 5 September 2026; retrieve the full decision/text and legal number.
- Labour Ministry Circular 22/2026 (1 October 2026): implements private-sector paid leave on 8 October 2026 in lieu of 6 October; relies on Article 129, Decision 294/2025 and PM Decision 2898/2026. Circular is a date-specific administrative instruction, not a permanent amendment.
- Further decision leads from the secondary tracker include decisions on working-time, employment records, vocational training, labour inspection, foreign worker permits, occupational safety, wage protection, collective bargaining, labour courts and new work patterns; retrieve each separately.

## 5. Source hierarchy and currentness controls
- Statute Law 14/2025 is the primary source; ministerial decisions are delegated implementation; circulars are administrative instructions; Ministry FAQs and press releases are explanatory only.
- Do not call a set of ministerial decisions "the Executive Regulation" unless the legislature/minister has formally issued a single instrument under that title.
- Link each decision to exact enabling article, commencement, repeal/replacement of old Law 12/2003 decisions and any transitional period.
- Maintain a decision matrix with topic, article authority, decision number/date, Gazette issue/page, operative scope, replaced prior decision and currentness.

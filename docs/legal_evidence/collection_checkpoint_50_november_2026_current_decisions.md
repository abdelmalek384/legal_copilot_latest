# Collection checkpoint 50 — November 2026 current government decisions and deadline extensions

**Cut-off:** 17 November 2026. This checkpoint records newly published, time-sensitive instruments from the Official Gazette/Amiri Press. Each must be stored with its publication date, commencement and expiry/deadline metadata.

## 1. PM Decision 3530/2026 — public expenditure rationalization for three months

- Official Amiri Press full text:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=TXzT8vO0lfQ%3D
- Issued 9 November 2026; effective the day after publication; applies for three months unless extended by a later decision.
- Applies to all entities within the State General Budget and economic public authorities.
- Legal basis includes Public Authorities Law 61/1963, Unified Public Finance Law 6/2022 (as amended by Law 18/2024), State Budget Law 77/2026 for FY 2026/2027 and State Development Plan Law 78/2026.
- Key controls:
  - defer non-essential expenditure;
  - foreign-currency spending requires Ministry of Finance authorization after coordination with CBE/competent authorities;
  - no spending decision increasing budget expenditure without prior Ministry of Finance coordination and Cabinet approval;
  - overseas travel restricted to absolute necessity, subject to Prime Minister/competent approval or host-funded travel;
  - electricity use reduced by 20% against the same period in FY 2025/2026;
  - government vehicle fuel use reduced by at least 30%;
  - delay/slow fuel-intensive national projects for at least three months, subject to assessment and exceptions;
  - training, advertising, publicity, ceremonies, conferences, certain travel and other specified purchases are prohibited/restricted;
  - at least 20% of remaining Chapter II goods/services appropriations are frozen;
  - new investment projects funded from the Treasury are deferred; new contracts are prohibited through 30 June 2027 unless already contracted or within listed exceptions;
  - purchase of passenger cars is prohibited through 30 June 2027;
  - exceptions include Presidential/Prime Ministerial assignments, expropriation compensation, prior-year obligations, Hayah Karima, health, education, interior, water/wastewater and national-security/water/food/cyber/energy security projects.
- This is a temporary executive spending-control decision, not a permanent amendment to Public Finance Law 6/2022 or Public Contracts Law 182/2018.
- Store each spending restriction as a dated rule with the applicable entity, expenditure chapter, approval authority, exceptions and expiry.

## 2. PM Decision 3531/2026 — transfer of two maritime companies to Suez Canal Authority

- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=z%2FPeUHV0KtU%3D
- Issued 9 November 2026; effective the day after publication.
- Transfers all shares in:
  - Canal Mooring and Lights Company (شركة القناة لرباط وأنوار السفن).
  - Port Said Engineering and Marine Construction Company (الشركة البورسعيدية للأعمال الهندسية والإنشاءات البحرية).
  from the Holding Company for Marine Industries, Services and Investment to the Suez Canal Authority.
- It states that both companies become subject to Companies Law 159/1981.
- Legal basis cites Suez Canal Authority Law 30/1975, Companies Law 159/1981, Public Business Sector Law 203/1991, Labour Law 14/2025 and PM Decisions 2514/2023 and 2515/2023.
- This is a share-ownership transfer and corporate-regime change, not an amendment to the Suez Canal Authority Law or Companies Law.
- Link to company registers, employment transition, contracts, assets, licences and corporate-governance rules.

## 3. PM Decision 2807/2026 — extension for foreigners regularizing unlawful residence

- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=5ASSvwFHFpQ%3D
- Issued 14 September 2026.
- Extends by one additional year the period for foreigners residing in Egypt unlawfully to regularize their status under Article 2 of PM Decision 3326/2023.
- The period had previously been extended by PM Decisions 4313/2023, 1050/2024, 3004/2024 and 3175/2025.
- Legal basis cites Foreigners' Entry and Residence Law 89/1960.
- Do not infer the exact final calendar deadline without reading the original Decision 3326/2023 and each extension; store the one-year extension as a relative extension from the prior deadline.
- This is a temporary administrative regularization measure, not a permanent amendment to Law 89/1960.

## 4. PM Decision 89/2026 — extension of alternative-housing applications under old-rent reform

- Official Amiri Press full text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=fnOXX0a9GSE%3D
- Issued 1 November 2026.
- Extends the application period in item 1 of Article 3 of the rules attached to PM Decision 53/2025 until **12 January 2027**.
- Decision 53/2025 establishes procedures and priorities for receiving applications for State-available residential/non-residential units under Article 8 of Law 164/2025 (old-rent reform).
- Earlier extensions:
  - PM Decision 4/2026 extended by three months to 12 April 2026.
  - PM Decision 24/2026 extended the period by a further three months.
  - PM Decision 59/2026 extended it until 12 October 2026.
  - PM Decision 89/2026 now extends it until 12 January 2027.
- Decision 53/2025 provides for an online platform and central administration to receive/assess tenant applications, rank applicants by priority and allocate State-available units.
- Priority criteria include income categories, original tenants/eligible successors and other factors set in the attached rules.
- This is a current application deadline under a transitional housing-allocation mechanism; it does not extend the seven-year lease termination period in Law 164/2025.

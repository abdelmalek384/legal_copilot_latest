# Collection checkpoint 31 — Public Shops Law, licensing rules and food-safety cross-links (2026-11-17)

## 1. Public Shops Law 154/2019

- Law 154/2019 issuing the Public Shops Law.
- Gazette: issue 39 bis (b), 1 October 2019.
- Secondary PDF copy of Gazette text:
  https://elmahrousanews.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/قانون-رقم-154-لسنة-2019-بإصدار-قانون-المحال-العامة.pdf
- Secondary full-text record:
  https://manshurat.org/node/64981
- Promulgation Article 1 applies to public shops, excluding those owned/managed by the Armed Forces, and preserves special laws for special economic zones, Sinai development, investment and sector-specific licensing.
- Promulgation Article 2 preserves existing licences subject to regularization under the Executive Regulation.
- Promulgation Article 3 requires previously unlicensed operating shops to apply for licence/notification within the statutory period triggered by publication of general/special requirements.
- This is a secondary-hosted Gazette copy; obtain original Gazette bytes from official Amiri Press or government portal and hash them.

## 2. Executive Regulation — PM Decision 590/2020

- Decision 590/2020 dated 4 March 2020; Gazette issue 9 bis (d), 4 March 2020.
- Cabinet official announcement:
  https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/344
- Secondary full-text record:
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12742
- Decision 590/2020 issues the Executive Regulation of Law 154/2019.
- The Regulation establishes the licensing centre process, non-time-limited licences subject to exceptions, temporary permits for seasonal/event shops, inspection and licensing fees, amendment of activity/place, closure/cancellation, judicial inspection and settlement procedures.

## 3. PM Decision 10/2025 — amendment to Executive Regulation 590/2020

- Secondary full-text source states PM Decision 10/2025 replaced the original two-year regularization period in Articles 2 and 11 of Decision 590/2020 with four years:
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12742
- The updated Regulation transcription:

- Obtain the original Gazette text of Decision 10/2025 and calculate the actual deadline based on the date the licensing committee published the general/special requirements, not the date of the Regulation alone.

## 5. National Food Safety Authority Law 1/2017

- Law 1/2017 establishes the National Food Safety Authority.
- It is cited by the official GAFI licensing guide and PM Decision 705/2023 (Tourism and Hotel Establishments Executive Regulation).
- A 2026 PM Decision 268/2026 cites Law 1/2017 and exempts the Authority from PM Decision 1804/2022 on extension of secondments/special unpaid leave for overseas work:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=RwevtmoqDN0%3D
- Obtain Law 1/2017 original Gazette text, Executive Regulation, NFSA board/technical decisions, food handling/licensing standards, import/export requirements and amendments.
- Food-safety obligations must be distinguished from public-shop premises licensing and from tourism/hotel licensing.

## 6. Time-limited 2026 opening-hours decision — historical only

- PM Decision 909/2026:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=0j8cX4kuyC0%3D
- Issued 27 March 2026 and applied for one month from Saturday 28 March 2026.
- It required general public shops, malls, restaurants, cafes and bazaars to close at 9 PM daily, with 10 PM closing Thursdays/Fridays/official holidays, subject to listed exceptions and geographical exclusions (South Sinai, Luxor, Aswan, Hurghada, Marsa Alam and Nile-side venues in Cairo/Giza).
- It is a time-limited one-month measure and should not be presented as current opening-hour law in November 2026. It is retained as historical legislation/event evidence only.

# Collection checkpoint 4 — health, environment, IP, competition and education (2026-11-17)

## Official Ministry of Finance source

### Law 2/2018 — Universal Health Insurance System
- Ministry of Finance record: https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-67862fecb619900008a3cfa9
- Record identifies Law 2/2018 dated 11 January 2018 and labels it in force.
- Ministry notice: online texts are guidance; the Official Gazette remains the legal reference.
- Need to obtain the full linked PDF, implementing regulations, phased governorate decisions, financing/contribution decisions, and subsequent amendments. Do not confuse this law with Social Insurance Law 148/2019 or Social Security Law 12/2025.

### Intellectual property

### Competition
- Competition Protection and Anti-Monopoly Practices Law 3/2005: https://manshurat.org/node/360
- Executive Regulation, Prime Minister Decision 1316/2005: https://manshurat.org/node/456
- Relationship index: https://manshurat.org/node/360/relations
- Displayed amendment leads include Laws 190/2008, 193/2008, 56/2014, 15/2019 and 175/2022 (economic concentration).
- The 2022 economic-concentration amendment is especially important for merger-control workflows; verify current thresholds and implementing rules from primary texts.

### Education

- Executive regulation relationship index lists multiple 2013 decisions (including 23, 52, 83, 126 and 146/2013); each needs an individual source/version record.

### Telecommunications
- Telecommunications Regulation Law 10/2003: https://manshurat.org/node/13787
- Secondary-hosted PDF lead only. Need official NTRA and Gazette source locators, amendments, executive regulation and relevant licensing/numbering/privacy decisions.

### Banking
- Central Bank and Banking Sector Law 194/2020: https://manshurat.org/node/68148

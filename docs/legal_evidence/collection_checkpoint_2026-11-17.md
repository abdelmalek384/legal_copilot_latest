### Law 149/2026 — VAT amendment (official PDF text inspected)
- Official PDF: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.149.of_.2026.pdf
- PDF metadata: 4 pages; Gazette issue 30 (bis A), 28 July 2026.
- Text inspected includes amendments to VAT Law 67/2016:
  - Article 3: standard VAT rate stated as 13%, and 14% from fiscal year 2017/2018; medical machinery/equipment used to produce a good or provide a service addressed at 5%.
  - Article 5: treatment of goods/services in transit, subject to customs-control condition.
  - New Article 28 bis: suspension of VAT on specified medical machinery/equipment for local factories/production units for industrial use, subject to conditions and time limits.
  - Article 30 and exempt-goods/services schedule amendments.
- This is an amending instrument, not a consolidated VAT Code. Do not replace the base law with this text or infer all later amendments are absent.

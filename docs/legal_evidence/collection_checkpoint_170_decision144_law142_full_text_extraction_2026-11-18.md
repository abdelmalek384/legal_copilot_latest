# Collection checkpoint 170 — Decision 144/2009 and Law 142/1964 full searchable text upgrades
**Date:** 18 November 2026

## New extraction work

### Decision 144/2009 — Executive Regulation of Law 119/2008
- Opened and reviewed the LAW HUB searchable page (2,786 source lines), corroborated against EastLaws, Manshurat and the government-hosted NIB PDF.
- Confirmed four issuing provisions, preliminary definitions, and **185 base regulation articles**.
- Captured issuing Articles 1–4, preliminary Article 1 definitions, Articles 1–10 (selected), Article24, Articles104,106–107,112,112 bis,112 bis 1,119–120,124–126 and182–185 in the new working file.
- Substantive examples include building-permit documents (Art112), construction start/permit validity (Art119), stop-work and inspection (Art120), categories of work and multi-use project thresholds (Arts124–126), occupants' association grievance period (Art182), repair/strengthening/demolition implementation (Arts183–185).
- The page exposes pre-amendment wording and annotations. Article104 is marked amended by Decision109/2013; Article112 bis 1 contains the earlier Decision410/2021 wording and Decision943/2024 replacement. This is valuable for versioning but is not a clean current consolidated text.
- Major amendment references confirmed: 200/2010, 109/2013, 67/2014, 774/2014, 410/2021 and 943/2024. Decision188/2010 is a fee decision, not a text amendment; Decision200/2009 is the model occupants' association system, not a general amendment.

### Law 142/1964 — Real-Estate Register Law
- Located full searchable fallback text of all **66 substantive articles** and issuing provisions at LAW HUB, cross-listed with EastLaws and Manshurat original scan catalogue.
- Confirmed Articles63–66 text. Article63 requires monthly notifications of building/demolition permits and new property-tax assessments; Article64 sets a 20-day authority response obligation; Article65 contains the offence provision and official corrigendum note; Article66 sets penalties for Articles63–64 violations.
- The page's Article65 corrigendum is noted as Official Gazette issue77, 2 April 1964.
- Full text recovery does not replace the remaining amendment and constitutional judgment redline.

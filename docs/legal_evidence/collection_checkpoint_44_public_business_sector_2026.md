# Collection checkpoint 44 — public business sector, public authorities and public-sector employment (2026-11-17)

## 1. Public Business Sector Companies Law 203/1991 — official FRA full text

- Official FRA PDF:
  https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/قانون-شركات-قطاع-الاعمال-العام.pdf
- Secondary consolidated text with Law 185/2020 amendments:
  https://manshurat.org/node/71081
- Base Gazette: issue 24 bis, 19 June 1991.
- Law 203/1991 applies to holding companies and their subsidiaries in the public business sector, both taking the joint-stock-company form.
- It provides that Companies Law 159/1981 applies where the Public Business Sector Law has no special provision and where consistent with it.
- It states that Law 97/1983 on Public Sector Authorities and Companies does not apply to the companies governed by Law 203/1991; do not treat this as a universal repeal of Law 97/1983 for every public-sector entity.
- The law governs holding/subsidiary formation, boards, general assemblies, capital, shares, financial controls, disclosure, governance, arbitration and employee regulations.

## 2. Amendment Law 185/2020

- Law 185/2020 amends Law 203/1991.
- Gazette issue 36 bis (A), 5 September 2020; effective 6 September 2020 according to publication metadata.
- Secondary Gazette-index record:
  https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/05-09-2020/no-185?id=2001148&type=1
- Government Cabinet report confirms the Executive Regulation was amended through PM Decision 948/2021, with 55 amendments approved in May 2021:
  https://www.cabinet.gov.eg/Upload/StaticContent/AttachmentA/15/تقرير%20عن%20تقدم%20أعمال%20برنامج%20الحكومة.pdf
- Key 2020 amendment themes include separation between non-executive board chair and executive managing director, corporate governance, disclosure, board powers, shareholding and transfer of wholly state-owned subsidiary shares.
- Obtain the original Gazette text and map each substituted/added/repealed provision.

## 3. Executive Regulation PM Decision 1590/1991, amended by Decision 948/2021

- Base Executive Regulation: PM Decision 1590/1991, Gazette issue 44, 31 October 1991; effective 1 November 1991.
- Secondary copy with amendments:
  https://manshurat.org/node/11918
- PM Decision 948/2021 amends the Executive Regulation to align it with Law 185/2020.
- The Cabinet report says the 2021 update included 55 amendments and followed a broad corporate restructuring program.
- The official Amiri Press/official Gazette source for Decision 948/2021 and its annexes should be obtained and article-mapped; do not rely solely on the secondary consolidated text.

## 4. Public Sector Authorities and Companies Law 97/1983 — historical/current scope requires careful classification

- Law 97/1983 was the framework for public-sector authorities and companies before the transition to the Public Business Sector Law 203/1991.
- Law 203/1991 provides that its rules do not apply to the holding/subsidiary companies covered by Law 203; this is not necessarily a blanket repeal for every remaining public entity.
- Determine whether each entity is a public authority, economic authority, holding/subsidiary company, public-sector company, public business-sector company or public-law person before selecting the employment/corporate regime.
- Obtain Law 97/1983 and any remaining scope/transition provisions if a current public-sector entity relies on it.

## 5. Public Sector Employees Law 48/1978

- Law 48/1978 issued the public-sector employee system, Gazette issue 29 supplement (b), 20 July 1978; commencement 1 July 1978.
- Secondary full-text:
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11488
- Secondary Gazette record:
  https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/20-07-1978/no-48?id=3525&sub=1693901&type=1
- It repealed Law 61/1971 on public-sector employees.
- It applies to employees of public-sector companies within its scope. Its continuing application must be assessed against the later transformation into holding/subsidiary companies under Law 203/1991, Civil Service Law 81/2016 and any entity-specific laws.
- Do not assume Law 48/1978 governs all State/public-sector employees today.

## 6. Public Authorities Law 61/1963

- Law 61/1963 is the general Public Authorities Law.
- Secondary full-text/PDF lead:
  https://manshurat.org/node/11855
- It is cited in PM Decision 200/2026, Waste Executive Regulation 722/2022 and multiple authority-establishment decisions.
- It is a framework statute for establishing and regulating public authorities, not a substitute for each authority's special establishing law, statute or sectoral regulation.
- Obtain official Gazette text and amendments; collect each authority's establishment decree, board powers, budget, employment regime and supervisory ministry.

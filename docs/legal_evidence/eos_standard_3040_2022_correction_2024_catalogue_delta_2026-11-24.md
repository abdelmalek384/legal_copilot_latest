# EOS catalogue delta — Egyptian Standard 3040/2022, 2024 correction

**Research date:** 24 November 2026  
**Standard:** Egyptian Standard 3040/2022  
**Official EOS catalogue record:** https://www.eos.org.eg/ar/standard/3210  
**Related mandatory instrument:** Trade and Industry Decision 559/2022  
**Related EPR instrument:** PM Decision 662/2025

## 1. What the official EOS catalogue currently displays

The EOS catalogue record identifies:

- Standard number: 3040/2022.
- Displayed title: **أكياس التسوق البلاستيكية (تصويب 2024)**.
- Scope: requirements and sampling/testing methods for single-layer plastic shopping bags, in all shapes and dimensions, made from thermoplastic materials and used to carry products.
- Approval date displayed: 27 September 2022.
- Pages: 11.
- Mandatory status: إلزامى.
- Decision reference: 2022/559.
- Catalogue status: **تعديل كلي** (full amendment).
- Product standard; chemical sector; ICS 55.080.

The catalogue page presents the 2024 correction in the title while retaining 2022/559 as the displayed decision reference and describing the status as a full amendment. This is an important current catalogue delta; it is not itself the full amended standard or the amending Gazette instrument.

## 2. Consequences for the earlier 50-micron lead

The official SIS/EEAA implementation communication says that Decision 559/2022 / Standard 3040 requires manufacturers of single-use plastic bags to produce bags at least 50 microns thick. The EOS record now adds that the standard is displayed as corrected in 2024 and has status “تعديل كلي”.

These records must be held together but not collapsed:
- the 50-micron figure remains an official implementation statement;
- the EOS record establishes the standard's displayed scope/status and a 2024 correction/full-amendment indicator;
- neither record, by itself, supplies the complete technical text or proves that the 50-micron requirement is unchanged in every product category under the corrected 2024 text.

Do not give a technical conformity answer from the 50-micron statement alone. The current authoritative Standard 3040 text and the instrument approving/correcting the 2024 edition are still required.

## 3. Relationship to Decisions 445/2024 and 662/2025

- Decision 559/2022 is the catalogue-linked mandatory instrument for Standard 3040/2022.
- Decision 662/2025 applies EPR to plastic shopping bags produced/imported under Standard 3040.
- Decision 445/2024 lists separate standards for home-compostable carrying bags (8917), woven plastic shopping bags (8918), reusable non-woven plastic shopping bags (8919) and paper shopping bags (8925).
- Do not infer that Decision 445/2024 amended Standard 3040: the EOS catalogue associates 3040 with Decision 559/2022, while Decision 445/2024 is separately associated with standards 8917/8918/8919/8925.

## 4. Acquisition gaps and action

1. Identify the exact EOS decision/circular or Gazette publication that approved the 2024 correction/full amendment to Standard 3040.
2. Obtain the complete 11-page current Standard 3040 by a lawful route; the EOS catalogue offers it for purchase and does not publish its full text.
3. Compare the corrected 2024 text with the 2022 edition and extract all changed clauses, test methods, tolerances, sampling and marking requirements.
4. Confirm whether the 50-micron threshold appears in the corrected text and precisely which bag categories it covers.
5. Check for later corrections, amendments, replacement standards or changes in the mandatory status after the EOS page's current display.
6. Assess reuse rights and obtain qualified Egyptian lawyer review.

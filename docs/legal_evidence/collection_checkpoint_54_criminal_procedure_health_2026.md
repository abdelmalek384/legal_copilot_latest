# Collection checkpoint 54 — 2026 amendments to Criminal Procedure and Universal Health Insurance

## 1. Law 160/2026 — postpones commencement of Criminal Procedure Law 174/2025

- Official Amiri Press full text:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Aalg37zxdcU%3D
- Law 160/2026 amends the promulgation law of Criminal Procedure Law 174/2025.
- Issued 30 September 2026.
- Article 1 replaces the phrase "1 October 2027" with "1 October following the date of publication" in Article 6 of Law 174/2025.
- The Amiri Press source's current replacement wording establishes commencement on **1 October 2027**.
- Article 2 provides commencement of Law 160/2026 on the day after its publication.
- This is a major currentness correction: Criminal Procedure Law 174/2025 is **not yet in force as of 17 November 2026**; its commencement has been postponed to 1 October 2027.
- Until then, the previous Criminal Procedure Law 150/1950 remains the operative general procedural statute, subject to other applicable laws and transitional rules.
- Checkpoint 7's earlier note that Law 174/2025 was published in November 2025 and would commence based on its original commencement clause is superseded by Law 160/2026. Do not answer criminal-procedure questions using Law 174/2025 as current before 1 October 2027.
- Preserve Law 174/2025 as future-effective legislation and build a date-aware version switch.

## 2. Law 155/2026 — amendment to Universal Health Insurance Law 2/2018

- Official Gazette repository record:
  https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/e1cc1496-1861-438c-8a92-9374c5a55d5e
- Official State Information Service announcement confirms presidential signature and publication on 4 August 2026:
  https://sis.gov.eg/ar/الرئاسة/شئون-داخلية/القرارات-الرئاسية/الرئيس-السيسي-ي-وقع-تعديل-بعض-أحكام-قانون-نظام-التأمين-الصحي-الشامل/
- Gazette issue 31 bis (a), 2 August 2026, according to Gazette metadata.
- Secondary full text:
  https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=19430
- Law 155/2026 adds two paragraphs to Article 42 of Universal Health Insurance Law 2/2018:
  - The solidarity contribution under Article 40(9) is classified as tax revenue.
  - Egyptian Tax Authority is responsible for examination, assessment and collection from those subject to it, by the deadline for filing the income-tax return, with ordinary legal collection powers.
  - Proceeds are paid into the State Treasury, which must automatically allocate and remit an equivalent amount to the Universal Health Insurance Authority to support its resources, without deductions.
- Article 2 deletes the former wording in Article 40(9) stating the contribution was not deductible for income-tax purposes and that collection procedures would be set by the Law's Executive Regulation.
- Article 3 requires the Minister of Finance, in coordination with the Universal Health Insurance Authority, to issue implementation decisions within 60 days of commencement.
- Article 4 provides commencement on the day after publication.
- This is a tax-collection and revenue-routing amendment; it does not change the overall coverage or contribution percentages unless other provisions do so.

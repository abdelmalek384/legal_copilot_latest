## 2. Law 70/1964 amendment chain

Created `law70_1964_amendment_chain_and_fee_currentness_2026-11-18.md`. It links the base statute, fee amendments, constitutional effects and annual vehicle valuation schedules.

Confirmed statutory amendment chain:
- Law 6/1991: replaces Articles 21, 25, 26 and 27; amends Article 19 and Article 32; inserts Articles 24 bis, 34 bis and 34 bis(1); repeals Article 20.
- Law 224/1996: replaces Article 24 bis(1) and Article 34 bis.
- Law 225/1996: repeals Article 21(8).
- Law 9/2003: inserts Article 34 bis(2), an additional 25% reduction subject to temporal rules.
- Law 3/2004: inserts Article 34 bis(3), a further one-third reduction subject to temporal rules.
- Law 83/2006: EGP 2,000 registration cap, fixed-fee caps, replaces Article 19; also amends Law 142/1964 Article 30.
- Justice Minister Decision 5424/2006: sets fixed, preservation and relative fee categories; full secondary text and tables located at Egypt Notaries Union; Gazette publication metadata/original issue still outstanding.
- Law 163/2019: adds a Table B item for overseas company experience/balance-sheet certifications at 0.5%, capped at EGP 100,000.
- Law 67/2021: broadens the Table B category from “contracting companies” to “companies”.

The 11-page Law 70/1964 Gazette scan was visually inspected in checkpoint 94. Schedule A/B/C pages are present but not yet fully transcribed.

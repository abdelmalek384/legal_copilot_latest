## What is now established
1. Original Law 4/2018 was published 16 January 2018 in issue 2 bis (ط), effective 17 January 2018.
2. A correction was published 8 March 2018 in issue 10.
3. The consolidated EastLaws page flags Article 3 as the final version after Article 1 of the corrigendum and exposes both the final and original text sections.
4. The FRA-hosted consolidated Law 159/1981 text also includes Law 4/2018 amendment annotations and final text for many inserted provisions:

The exact corrigendum operative text remains outstanding; do not claim primary-source comparison complete.

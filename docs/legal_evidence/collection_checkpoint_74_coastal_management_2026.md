## 2. Committee composition

- Chaired by the Prime Minister or delegate.
- Membership includes ministers responsible for:
  - Defence and Military Production.
  - Health and Population.
  - Transport.
  - Water Resources and Irrigation.
  - Electricity and Renewable Energy.
  - Local Development and Environment.
  - Tourism and Antiquities.
  - Foreign Affairs, International Cooperation and Egyptians Abroad.
  - Agriculture and Land Reclamation.
  - Petroleum and Mineral Resources.
  - Housing, Utilities and New Urban Communities.
  - Investment and Foreign Trade.
  - Planning and Economic Development.
  - Industry.
- Minister of Local Development and Environment serves as rapporteur.
- Ministers may delegate a senior leadership-level official; committee may use technical experts.

## 3. Committee functions reported from Article 2

The reported functions include:
1. Set its internal working rules without conflicting with the Higher Committee for Licensing within the 200-metre coastal beach prohibition zone established by PM Decision 987/2023.
2. Define each ministry's roles/responsibilities under its governing legislation to integrate the three levels of coastal-management governance.
3. Approve national strategies, development plans, programmes and recommendations for coastal/marine/lake areas, after technical-secretariat review.
4. Approve proposals from local integrated coastal-management committees in coastal governorates.
5. Resolve overlapping jurisdiction in coastal areas through binding decisions based on technical-secretariat recommendations.
6. Advise on accession to international/regional conventions and protocols on coastal/marine/lake protection and direct preparation of domestic implementing legislation.
7. Arrange financial resources and funding for integrated coastal-management plans and recommendations.
- These are reported summaries; obtain and compare the official text before treating the exact scope as source-matched.

## 4. Technical secretariat and local committees

- Contemporaneous reports state the decision creates a technical secretariat chaired by the CEO of the Egyptian Environmental Affairs Agency.
- It includes representatives of participating ministries/entities, the Lake Protection and Development Authority, Waste Management Regulatory Authority, Information and Decision Support Center, and EEAA's central coastal/lakes administration.
- It reviews/merges local plans into a national plan, assesses major development projects, and prepares/updates integrated coastal-management plans.
- Local committees are formed in Mediterranean coastal governorates, reportedly including Matrouh, Alexandria, Beheira, Kafr El-Sheikh, Dakahlia, Damietta, Port Said and North Sinai, chaired by the governor.
- Their work includes local implementation plans, development programmes, environmental guidance and risk-map review.

## 5. Existing coastal licensing committee — boundary of authority

- PM Decision 987/2023 created the Higher Committee for Licensing within the coastal beach prohibition zone, reported as extending 200 metres.
- Decision 3536/2026 expressly preserves that committee's jurisdiction; the new national committee should not be interpreted as replacing or overriding it.

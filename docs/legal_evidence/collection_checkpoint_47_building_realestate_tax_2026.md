## 3. Real Estate Tax Law 196/2008 — major 2026 amendment Law 3/2026

- Official Amiri Press text of Law 3/2026:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=IC9djkqa+a4%3D
- Law 3/2026 amends the Real Estate Tax Law 196/2008.
- Issued 2 April 2026; Gazette issue 14 bis, 7 April 2026 (publication metadata to verify against Gazette PDF).
- Replaces terminology throughout the law: "Real Estate Tax Directorate" / "Directorates" with "Real Estate Tax Region" / "Regions".
- Replaces portions of Articles 4, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20 and 21.
- New Article 4(1): annual rental value assessments apply for five years; reassessment procedures must start at least one year and no more than three years before the end of the prior period; the Authority must publish indicative price-map details at least 90 days before the new assessment takes effect.
- New Article 14: taxpayer declarations are filed during the second half of the year preceding the five-year census, and by the end of December for annual census cases including newly established properties and certain changed circumstances.
- New Article 18(1)(d): exemption for the taxpayer's principal private residence where net annual rental value is below EGP 100,000; amount above the threshold is taxable, and the family definition includes taxpayer, spouse and minor children.
- New Article 14 bis provides a tax incentive for timely, complete returns: 25% discount for residential properties and 10% for non-residential properties; an additional early-payment discount may be set by ministerial decision up to 5%.
- New Article 23 last paragraph requires payment of tax and delay charges through electronic payment methods, subject to ministerial organization.
- The law adds Articles 27 last paragraph, 27 bis and 29 bis, including procedures for settlement of specified tax disputes before appeal committees/courts.
- It directs the Minister of Finance to amend the Executive Regulation within six months; existing decisions continue insofar as they do not conflict until the amendment is issued.
- This is a major current 2026 tax amendment. Do not use the 2008 base law alone or rely on outdated EGP 24,000 residence exemption without checking Law 3/2026.

## 4. Architectural heritage and demolition Law 144/2006 — 2026 compensation rules

- PM Decision 2557/2026 official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Enpy6WpQq34%3D
- Issued 20 August 2026; effective the day after publication.
- Establishes working rules for the committee assessing compensation for prohibitions on demolition/addition of buildings/structures with distinctive architectural character, under Law 144/2006.
- It sets application requirements, supporting documents, committee meetings, compensation assessment bases and payment responsibility.
- The decision cites Law 144/2006, Expropriation Law 10/1990, Building Law 119/2008, Public Finance Law 6/2022, Building Reconciliation Law 187/2023 and PM Decision 3279/2021.
- This is a 2026 implementation instrument. Obtain the full Law 144/2006, Executive Regulation, heritage-building lists, PM Decisions 2276/2006 and 243/2019, Ministerial Decision 1205/2024 and all related court decisions.

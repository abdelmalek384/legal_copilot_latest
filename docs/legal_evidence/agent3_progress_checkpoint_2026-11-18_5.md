## New text work since prior checkpoint

### 1. Competition Law 3/2005 and merger-control rules
- ECA official Arabic Law 3/2005 compilation (654 extracted lines) and Executive Regulation 1316/2005 (851 extracted lines) acquired.
- Law 175/2022 merger-control provisions captured: economic concentration, control/material influence, filing thresholds, standstill, substantive review and special FRA-supervised-sector process.
- PM Decision 1120/2024 captured: new Executive Regulation Chapter Nine, Articles 48–62; material influence thresholds, filing dossier, public comment, commitments, fee bands, below-threshold call-in and public-interest exceptions. Gazette issue 14 supplement (أ), 4 April 2024; effective 1 June 2024.

### 2. Consumer Protection Law 181/2018 and Executive Regulation 822/2019
- Combined Gazette text extraction of 1,411 lines, covering Law 181/2018 through Article 76 and PM Decision 822/2019 regulation through Article 45.
- Captured core Arabic text on consumer rights, product safety, material disclosure, Arabic-language invoices, strategic-product hoarding, misleading conduct, 14-day no-reason returns, 30-day defect remedies, instalment/timeshare, repairs/warranties, Agency powers, complaints, inspection, enforcement and penalties.
- Law 20/2024 replaces Article 71; its original 2018 text must remain historical.
- Records: [base law and regulation](agent3_consumer_protection_law181_2018_and_exec_reg822_2019_full_text_audit.md); [Law 20/2024 amendment](agent3_consumer_protection_law181_2018_law20_2024_amendment_arabic_text.md).

### 3. Trade Register — primary source upgrades
- Official ITDA PDF CR_WorkGuide34.pdf acquired; it contains Law 34/1976 (25 articles) and Decision 946/1976 (24 regulation articles), including Gazette metadata and the 1978 Article 4 amendment.
- Official ITDA Gazette reproduction of Decision 41/2020 (Al-Waqa'i issue 56 supplement, 8 March 2020) acquired and all three pages visually inspected. Articles 13 bis/1–3 captured exactly: beneficial-owner register, update/notice, identity data, five-year post-cessation retention and book-integrity rules.
- Decision 106/2018 remains blocked: ITDA lists a distinct 14,898,711-byte PDF as the current Executive Regulation. Web extraction rejects the file due size; local download failed. Do not substitute Decision 946/1976. ITDA's online Work Guide is guidance, not legislation.
- Record: [Trade Register regulation and amendments](agent3_trade_register_exec_reg_946_1976_amendments_2020_arabic_text.md).

### 4. Commercial Code 17/1999 — expanded Arabic text
- Official Tax Authority / Amiri Press second-edition PDF reopened: 3,759 extracted lines / 135 printed pages.
- Added selected operative Arabic text for Articles 1–67, 90–101, 130–138, 163–174, 208–218, 329–341, 404–413, 491–503 and 633–637. Topics: commercial acts and merchant status, commercial books/retention, Commercial Register, business establishment, joint liability, default interest, unfair competition, sale remedies, warehouses/pledges, agency, transport, bank transfers/credit/documentary credits, bills and cheques, historical bankruptcy administration.
- Issuing Article 3 commencement amended by Law 168/2000. Bankruptcy provisions are historical where superseded by Law 11/2018 and Law 11/2021.
- Record: [Commercial Code text audit](agent3_commercial_code_17_1999_official_eta_text_audit_2026-11-18.md).

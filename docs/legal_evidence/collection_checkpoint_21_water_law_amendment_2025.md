# Collection checkpoint 21 — Water Resources Law amendment 167/2025 and Executive Regulation (2026-11-17)

## 1. Water Resources and Irrigation Law 147/2021 — official full text

- Official Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation PDF:
  https://www.mwri.gov.eg/spa/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/قانون-147-لسنة-2021.pdf
- Alternate official Ministry PDF:
  https://www.mwri.gov.eg/eoa/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/قانون-رقم-147-لسنة-2021-بإصدار-قانون-الموارد-المائية-والرى-1-1.pdf
- Gazette: issue 41 (repeated), 16 October 2021.
- PDF opened and searchable full text inspected (890 line references in the web extraction).
- Promulgation Article 2 repeals Irrigation and Drainage Law 12/1984.
- Article 1 defines the ministry, competent officials, water-user associations, groundwater, waterways, water resources and related concepts.
- Articles 2–7 establish public-property categories related to water resources and irrigation, Ministry jurisdiction and constraints on use of public water-resource property.
- Articles 8–14 regulate private works in public water-resource property, licensing, fees, maintenance, inspection, cancellation and removal.
- Article 15 onward governs rights of use over private irrigation/drainage sources and systems.
- The text covers Nile protection areas, public waterways, groundwater wells, flood channels, water distribution, irrigation and drainage associations, licensing, penalties and administrative enforcement.
- The full PDF should be extracted locally and article-segmented, preserving page/article mapping and OCR review. The searchable online text is not yet a local archive or checksum.

## 2. Executive Regulation — PM Decision 81/2023

- Official Gazette PDF:
  https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/21255/OG52_20230104_1123.pdf%3Fsequence%3D1%26isAllowed%3Dy
- Gazette issue 52 bis (هـ), 4 January 2023.
- Decision 81/2023 issues the Executive Regulation of Law 147/2021; effective the day after publication according to Gazette metadata.
- Secondary full-text record:
  https://www.mksegypt.org/ar/decisions/17229
- Prime Minister's official explanatory page describes the Regulation's scope:
  https://www.cabinet.gov.eg/Meeting/Details/10709
- The Regulation covers public water-resource property, permits and documents for activities, technical conditions, private irrigation/drainage use rights, distribution from public canals and groundwater wells, water intakes/outfalls, mechanically/electrically operated pumps, water-user associations, improvement/development of irrigation and drainage systems and licensing new land irrigation/drainage.
- Store Decision 81/2023 as a separate regulation document linked to Law 147/2021.

## 3. Law 167/2025 — amendment to Law 147/2021

- Official Amiri Press text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=K+G2F8Nz2Sw%3D
- Gazette: issue 32 supplement (a), 7 August 2025.
- Official Gazette repository record:
  https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/37bdd550-f6d8-46fe-88e7-288e014d9ce7/full
- Law 167/2025 replaces Article 107 of Law 147/2021.
- The new Article 107 punishes violation of the first or second paragraphs of Article 70 with imprisonment of at least one month and a fine of at least EGP 50,000 and no more than EGP 500,000, or either penalty.
- The new Article 107 text is visible in the official Amiri Press source; the exact Article 70 cross-reference must be mapped to the base law to understand the offence scope.
- A legal review states that it incorporated the new Article 107 from Law 167/2025 but had to rely on two matching unofficial transcriptions because the Gazette image was not available to that reviewer:
  https://abdelazizandpartners.com/legal-library/water-resources-irrigation-147-2021/
- The DSpace Gazette item provides a primary publication locator and PDF attachment, but byte-level comparison and visual review remain outstanding.

## 4. Extracted verbatim Arabic — replacement Article 107

> مادة (107): يعاقب كل من يخالف أحكام أى من الفقرتين الأولى والثانية من المادة (70) من هذا القانون بالحبس مدة لا تقل عن شهر ، وبغرامة لا تقل عن خمسين ألف جنيه ولا تزيد على خمسمائة ألف جنيه ، أو بإحدى هاتين العقوبتين.

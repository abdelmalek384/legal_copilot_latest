## 2. Law 15/2025 — court district and jurisdiction restructuring

- Official Amiri Press full text:
  https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=6eG+%2F7d83vk%3D
- Law 15/2025 amends Law 93/2015 on establishing certain primary courts and changing names/jurisdiction of other primary courts.
- It replaces Article 10 first and second paragraphs:
  - "Assiut Primary Court" is renamed "North Assiut Primary Court" wherever referenced in that law or any other law.
  - North Assiut Primary Court has jurisdiction over specified districts/centres: Assiut Centre, Fath, Abnoub, Manfalut, Qusiya and Dayrut.
  - A new South Assiut Primary Court is established in Assiut City with jurisdiction over First/Second Assiut sections, Abu Tig, Sidfa, Ghanayem, Sahel Selim and Badari.
- Pending cases/requests whose jurisdiction changes are transferred in their existing state unless reserved for judgment; parties are notified.
- Law issued 13 May 2025; effective the day after publication.
- This is a substantive court-organization statute with direct venue/jurisdiction consequences.

## 3. Related court-organization implementing decisions

- Justice Minister Decision 4065/2025 changes references to "South Assiut Primary Court" to "North Assiut Primary Court" wherever appearing in Decisions 7519/2023 and 4727/2024:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=aViaXJZltFQ%3D
- The decision was issued 19 June 2025 and takes effect the day after publication.
- This decision is a follow-on correction/coordination instrument to Law 15/2025; capture it with the underlying 2023/2024 court-location decisions.
- Justice Minister Decision 2980/2026 transfers the venue of the 9th and 10th first-instance criminal circuits from the 10th of Ramadan rehabilitation complex to Belbeis Court Complex, effective 1 July 2026:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=BcFc1e98dOU%3D
- Such venue decisions are administrative court-organization records; they should not be mistaken for amendments to criminal jurisdiction rules.

## 5. Supreme Constitutional Court Law 48/1979 — procedural rules surfaced in judgments

- Official Amiri Press / SCC judgment text cites Article 30 of Law 48/1979:
  - a referral decision or constitutional lawsuit statement must identify the challenged legislative provision, the constitutional provision alleged to be violated and the grounds of conflict.
- Example SCC judgment transcription:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Q27YPsHc%2FmQ%3D
- Another SCC judgment discusses the binding erga omnes authority of SCC judgments under Constitution Article 195 and Articles 48–49 of Law 48/1979:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=YPhEy+px9vg%3D
- These are judicial decision texts/case extracts and must be stored as judgments, not as amendments to Law 48/1979.
- Obtain the official full text of Law 48/1979 and its amendments and current SCC procedural regulations.

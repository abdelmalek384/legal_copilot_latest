## 6. Gazette ingestion classification rules

Every Gazette record should be classified as one of:
1. General normative legislation (law, regulation, general decision).
2. General administrative/organizational act (authority formation, delegations, appointments, territorial jurisdiction).
3. Individual administrative act (naturalization permission, removal, parcel allocation, licence for named person/company).
4. Event/time-limited act (election timetable, opening hours, temporary expenditure control, deadline extension).
5. Publication notice/correction/erratum.
6. Non-binding explanatory/guidance material.

Only category 1 and general category 2 content ordinarily belongs in the shared general legal corpus. Categories 3–5 should be stored separately with scope/date/identity controls and should not be generalized into legal rules. Category 6 must be labelled non-binding.

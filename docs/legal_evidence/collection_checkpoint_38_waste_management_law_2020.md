## 1. Law 202/2020 — scope and institutions
- Article 1 promulgation: applies Waste Management Law without prejudice to Nuclear/Radiological Activities Law 7/2010 and applicable international agreements.
- Article 2: Waste Management Regulatory Authority replaces the former authority established by PM Decision 3005/2015 and assumes its rights/obligations.
- Law establishes the integrated waste-management framework for hazardous, industrial, agricultural, municipal solid, construction/demolition and other waste.
- 86 substantive articles; the secondary transcription says it is reproduced verbatim from the Official Gazette and updated through 2026. Gazette article-by-article verification remains necessary.

## 2. Executive Regulation 722/2022 — selected content
- Promulgation Article 1 applies the attached regulation; Article 2 repeals inconsistent provisions; Article 3 effective the day after Gazette publication.
- Regulation defines waste-management system actors, waste types, licensing, integrated municipal waste management, hazardous waste, collection, transport, sorting, recycling, treatment, disposal and landfill.
- The law/regulation structure includes:
  - Waste generation prevention and reduction.
  - Extended producer responsibility and product stewardship.
  - Licensing/registration of waste-management activities and facilities.
  - Municipal waste planning and local-authority responsibilities.
  - Hazardous waste classification, packaging, storage, transport, treatment and disposal.
  - Construction/demolition waste management.
  - Waste collection/transport permits and manifests.
  - Recovery, recycling and final disposal.
  - Financial resources, fees and incentives.
  - Administrative controls, inspection, enforcement and penalties.
- Full regulation text is available in the secondary transcription; article-level extraction and Gazette comparison are still outstanding.

## 3. Decision 1113/2024 — amendment to Executive Regulation 722/2022
- Gazette issue 14 (supplement), 4 April 2024.
- Replaces Regulation Article 1 item 18 (definition of hazardous-waste landfill) and Article 29.
- New definition of hazardous-waste landfill requires a single/multiple-cell facility with safe design/operation based on waste characteristics and integrated systems for:
  - Double impermeable base liner.
  - Collection/treatment of gas emissions.
  - Collection/treatment of leachate.
  - Rainwater collection.
  - Receiving, classifying and determining location/method for hazardous waste.
  - Leak monitoring.
  - Safe closure of cells.
  - Post-closure management.
- The full replacement text of Article 29 must be transcribed from the Gazette PDF; this checkpoint records the visible first replacement and source locators.

# Law 1/2017 — National Food Safety Authority (NFSA): primary Gazette text acquisition

**Acquisition date:** 24 November 2026  
**Instrument:** Law No. 1 of 2017 promulgating the Law of the National Food Safety Authority  
**Gazette:** Official Gazette, issue 1 bis (C), 10 January 2017  
**Status:** Full Arabic text located in a Gazette reproduction hosted by FAO's FAOLEX legal database; official Amiri Press searchable-text record also located. No page-image comparison or subsequent amendment/currentness consolidation is claimed.

The FAOLEX-hosted PDF reproduces the Official Gazette masthead and identifies issue 1 bis (C), dated 10 January 2017. Its extracted text includes the issuing provisions and Articles 1–19. The Amiri Press search result independently identifies the statute and reproduces its definition section, but the full Amiri Press page could not be opened in this pass. The FAOLEX PDF is therefore the accessible text source used for this capture; do not describe FAO as the publisher of the law.

## 2. Substantive structure captured

- Issuing Articles 1–6: enactment; transfer/consolidation of food-trading control functions; transitional continuation of existing authorities and regulations pending NFSA assumption; staff transfer; executive-regulation authority; commencement on the day after publication.
- Articles 1–3: definitions, establishment/personality/public reporting line and headquarters, and the Authority's purposes and extensive functions.
- Articles 4–16: governance and management structure (Board of Trustees, Board of Directors and executive director), rulemaking, inspections, laboratory arrangements, traceability, risk analysis, recalls and emergency response.
- Article 17: independent budget and financial resources, including inspection/licensing/certificate fees subject to the statutory ceiling and adjustment mechanism.
- Article 18: grievance committees, composition, 15-day grievance period and decision period, expedited cases, and judicial challenge route.
- Article 19: judicial-officer status and coordination requirements.

## 3. High-value retrieval crosswalk

- **Institutional exclusivity / regulatory transition:** issuing Article 2 assigns NFSA the relevant food-trading control functions previously allocated across ministries and public bodies; issuing Article 3 preserves predecessor authority functions and existing rules until the statutory transition occurs.
- **Scope:** Article 1 definitions include food, food trading, food safety, food-contact materials, hazards, traceability, withdrawal and recall.
- **Authority powers:** Article 3 covers mandatory food-safety rules, licensing, inspection, imported/domestic food control, emergency measures, risk analysis, traceability and food withdrawal.
- **Governance:** Articles 5–16 allocate policy, board, executive and administrative powers.
- **Fees:** Article 17 provides the statutory fee framework; do not infer current fee amounts from this provision alone.
- **Appeals:** Article 18 sets the statutory grievance framework; cross-read Executive Regulation 412/2019 Articles 20–24.
- **Enforcement:** Article 19 establishes NFSA judicial-officer arrangements; cross-read Executive Regulation 412/2019 Articles 25–30.

## 1. Law 15/2017 — scope and core rules
- Applies to industrial establishments and shops inside/outside industrial zones, including free/investment zones, subject to Special Economic Zones Law 83/2002 and Sinai Development Law 14/2012.
- Replaces conflicting industrial-licensing rules under Law 453/1954 and repeals Chapter One of Law 21/1958 on organizing/encouraging industry.
- IDA is the competent authority for industrial licensing.
- Two licensing regimes:
  1. Notification-based licensing for low-risk activities.
  2. Prior licensing for high-risk activities involving security, health, safety or environment.
- Article 3: industrial licence is generally indefinite unless Executive Regulation specifies otherwise.
- Article 4: low-risk establishment notifies IDA using prescribed form; same-day receipt of notification constitutes a licence with legal effect.
- Article 5: IDA inspects within 90 days; may allow up to 180 days to regularize, renewable once; maximum period doubled for small/micro projects.
- Articles 8–11: prior licensing for high-risk activities and specified geographic areas; application, documentation and decision deadlines.
- Article 14 establishes Licensing Requirements Committee to set technical, fire-safety, environmental, industrial-safety and occupational-health requirements, classify risk, set building codes and identify non-essential requirements.
- Article 23 permits companies providing industrial licensing services and land-allocation assistance, registered with IDA.
- Article 33 sets maximum fees: EGP 20,000 for prior licence and EGP 5,000 for notification-based licence, with annual increases capped at 5%; small/micro projects receive 50% reduction.
- Article 37 establishes grievance committees chaired by State Council deputy; grievance period 15 days, decision within 15 days (7 days in urgent cases); Administrative Court review thereafter.
- Article 40 criminalizes establishing/operating high-risk establishment without required licence; imprisonment up to one year and fine EGP 100,000–5m or either, plus closure.

## 2. Executive Regulation Decision 1082/2017
- Promulgates detailed implementation rules and forms.
- Defines industrial establishment, notification licence, prior licence, accreditation offices, relevant authorities and risk categories.
- Provides procedures for:
  - Risk classification and activities eligible for notification-based licensing.
  - High-risk activities requiring prior licence.
  - Application forms and required documents.
  - IDA inspection, technical review and decision deadlines.
  - Accreditation offices (مكاتب الاعتماد) and certificates accepted by IDA.
  - Industrial site/land allocation, building and environmental/fire-safety approvals.
  - Licence transfer, amendment, renewal where time-limited, suspension/cancellation and regularization.
  - Grievances, committee procedures and records.
- The official Supreme Court portal entry states the regulation is updated through 27 July 2021; retrieve every amending ministerial decision and compare with the 2017 Gazette.
- IDA official index lists Decision 461/2018 on licensing conditions, Decision 497/2018 on industrial register, Decisions 47/2020 and 45/2021 on heavy-industry licensing/tendering, and other implementing instruments.

# Collection checkpoint 80 — Cabinet Decision 80/2026 allocates 50% of State-land regularization fees

## 1. Official Gazette PDF

- Cabinet Decision 80/2026 concerns purposes and limits for spending 50% of examination and inspection fees collected under State Private Property Law 168/2025.
- Official Ministry of Finance PDF:
  https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/qrar_mjls_alwzrae_rqm_80_lsnt_2026_bshan_aghrad_whdwd_srf_nsbt_50_mn_rswm_alfhs_walmeaynt_almhslt_wfq_a_lahkam_alqanwn_rqm_168_lsnt_202_90762391b4.pdf
- Gazette: Official Gazette issue 41 bis (h), 12 October 2026.
- Signed 12 October 2026; effective the day after publication.

## 2. Article 1 — allocation of 50% of fees

- State Treasury allocates 50% of the total examination and inspection fees collected under Law 168/2025 for the purposes and limits set out in Decision 80/2026.

## 3. Article 2 — distribution rules

The decision allocates the 50% share as follows, with different percentage bases for examination versus inspection fees:

- 10% of total examination/inspection fees to the Military Survey Department.
- 5% of total examination/inspection fees to Armed Forces information systems administration.
- 5% of total examination/inspection fees to the State Land Recovery Committee and its subordinate committees (pricing, monitoring, logistics/travel).
- 20% of total examination/inspection fees as incentives for committee members formed under Articles 5 and 6 of Law 168/2025.
- 10% of inspection fees only to the Egyptian General Survey Authority.
- 5% of examination fees only to committees formed by the Ministry responsible for Local Development/Environment to monitor implementation in governorates.
- 2% of examination fees only to committees formed by the National Center for State Land Use Planning to study regularization applications and identify the competent State authority.
- 3% of examination fees only to cover insured-contract costs and logistics of committees in governorates/State authorities, including travel, notices, advertisements and technical/administrative support.
- Preserve each percentage's exact denominator. Do not add percentages with different fee bases as though they were all percentages of the same pool.

## 4. Article 3 — quarterly performance and spending approval

- State Land Recovery Committee monitors implementation and performance rates every three months.
- It prepares a memorandum to the Minister of Finance describing requirements, expenditure purposes and financial study.
- Ministry of Finance reviews the memorandum and approves spending within the purposes and limits in the decision.

## 5. Connection to Law 168/2025

- Law 168/2025 establishes the hand-occupier regularization process for State private property.
- Executive Regulation PM Decision 200/2026 sets examination/inspection fees, committees, survey, field inspection, pricing and payment.
- Cabinet Decision 80/2026 governs the use of 50% of those collected fees.
- Presidential Decision 480/2026 extends the application period by another six months (checkpoint 73).
- These are separate legal layers:
  - Law 168/2025: substantive authority/eligibility.
  - PM Decision 200/2026: procedural regulation and fees.
  - Cabinet Decision 80/2026: allocation/spending of part of fee revenue.
  - Presidential Decision 480/2026: extension of application period.

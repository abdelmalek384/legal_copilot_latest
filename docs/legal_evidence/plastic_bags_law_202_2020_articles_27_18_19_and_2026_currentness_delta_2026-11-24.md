## 2. Law 202/2020 — Article 27 (Arabic text excerpt)

The publicly reproduced Article 27 establishes the statutory structure:

**مادة (٢٧):**

**(أ)** يكون تصنيع أو استيراد أو تصدير الأكياس البلاستيكية أحادية الاستخدام وفقاً للضوابط والاشتراطات والمواصفات الفنية التي يصدر بها قرار من وزير التجارة والصناعة بالاتفاق مع الوزير المختص، ويجوز أن يتضمن القرار حظر تصنيع أو استيراد أو تصدير الأكياس المشار إليها إذا كان يدخل في مكوناتها مدخلات أو مواد من شأنها الإضرار الجسيم بالبيئة.

**(ب)** لا يجوز أن يكون بيع أو تداول أو تخزين أو التوزيع المجاني أو التخلص من الأكياس البلاستيكية أحادية الاستخدام إلا وفقاً للضوابط والاشتراطات والمواصفات الفنية التي تحددها اللائحة التنفيذية لهذا القانون.

**(ج)** يصدر وزير المالية بعد التنسيق مع الوزير المختص ووزير التجارة والصناعة نظاماً للحوافز المالية والاقتصادية والإعفاءات الضريبية والجمركية لتشجيع استيراد وإنتاج وتصنيع البدائل الآمنة الصديقة للبيئة للأكياس البلاستيكية أحادية الاستخدام، وتحدد اللائحة التنفيذية المعايير والضوابط اللازمة لهذه الحوافز ومواصفات تلك البدائل الآمنة.

This is an excerpt of Article 27 relevant to plastic bags, not a full transcription of the law or a current consolidated version. The complete Article 27 text, including remaining paragraphs and any amendments, must be checked against the Gazette scan before ingestion as a complete instrument text.

## 3. Executive Regulation — Article 18 (official Gazette-hosted text)

The EEAA-hosted Gazette PDF for issue 7 bis (C), 22 February 2022, exposes Article 18. The provision states that sale, circulation, storage, free distribution or disposal of single-use plastic bags is subject to the following requirements:

1. Bags must conform to Egyptian Organization for Standardization and Quality specifications and to the controls issued by the Minister of Trade and Industry in agreement with the competent minister.
2. Free distribution of compliant single-use plastic shopping bags is prohibited unless the Waste Management Regulatory Authority gives written permission.
3. Single-use plastic bags designated for collecting medical waste may not be used for another purpose.
4. Large and medium consumer-goods outlets must use reusable shopping bags, establish incentives for customers to use them, and promote them; competent administrative authorities monitor compliance.
5. Large and medium consumer-goods outlets must establish appropriate systems to collect, circulate and store single-use plastic shopping bags used by customers and safely dispose of them in coordination with the Authority.
6. The Egyptian Organization for Standardization and Quality is required to complete the necessary specifications for single-use and reusable bags, including woven plastic, non-woven porous plastic, woven cotton/linen/natural-fibre cloth, bleached or unbleached paper and biodegradable plastic bags.
7. Factories producing biodegradable bags must apply the required markings, colours and technical specifications to facilitate sorting and avoid harm to recycling.

**Article 19** begins a framework for financial/economic incentives and tax/customs exemptions to encourage safe alternatives. The indexed official PDF extract shows its opening and first criterion, but this note does not transcribe the complete Article 19. Do not represent Article 19 as fully captured.

## 4. Relationship to PM Decision 662/2025

Decision 662/2025 adds a separate extended-producer-responsibility layer for plastic shopping bags produced/imported under Egyptian Standard 3040:

- Article 1 designates those bags as priority products subject to EPR under Article 17 of Law 202/2020.
- Article 2 requires producer/importer establishment registration, quarterly reporting of quantities sold, and payment of EGP 37.5 per kilogram sold in the domestic market when submitting the quarterly statement.
- Articles 3–4 direct the proceeds to the Waste Management Regulatory Authority for safe-disposal measures and require an annual report to the Cabinet.
- Article 5 provides commencement after three months from the day following Gazette publication.

Decision 662/2025 does not, on its face, replace Article 27 of Law 202/2020 or Articles 18–19 of the Executive Regulation. The provisions address overlapping but distinct matters: product specification/manufacture/import/export; sale/distribution/storage/disposal rules; and EPR registration/reporting/payment.

## 5. Later official implementation signal — 50-micron reference

The SIS government communication located in this pass states that the minister directed coordination with the Ministry of Industry regarding Decision 559/2022 and Egyptian Standard 3040, describing it as requiring manufacturers of single-use plastic bags to produce bags with a thickness of at least **50 microns**.

This is a later official government communication, not the text of Decision 559/2022 or the full Egyptian Standard 3040. Record it as an **official implementation statement / specification lead**, not as a complete primary-source technical standard. The underlying Decision 559/2022 and Standard 3040 text must be acquired and checked for scope, test method, tolerances, bag types, exclusions and amendments before the product states a compliance rule.

The same SIS communication refers to Law 202/2020 Articles 27 and 18–19, Decision 662/2025, and the national strategy to reduce single-use plastic bags. The national strategy and ministerial directions are policy/implementation context; they do not amend the statute or decision.

## 6. Important legal and product distinctions

- **Article 27 law scope:** single-use plastic bags, with controls on manufacture/import/export and separate delegated controls on sale/circulation/storage/free distribution/disposal.
- **Executive Regulation Article 18:** operational rules for retail outlets, including the written-permission exception for free distribution, reusable-bag incentives, collection/storage and safe disposal.
- **Executive Regulation Article 19:** incentive framework; full text and implementation instruments not captured here.
- **Decision 559/2022 / Standard 3040:** technical specification lead cited by Decision 662/2025 and later government communication; not acquired in full in this note.
- **Decision 662/2025:** EPR payment/reporting obligation for the defined product scope. Do not conflate the EGP 37.5/kg amount with a penalty, a retail price, or the 50-micron specification.
- **No inferred repeal:** the located later communications do not establish repeal of Articles 27, 18 or 19, Decision 559/2022, or Decision 662/2025.

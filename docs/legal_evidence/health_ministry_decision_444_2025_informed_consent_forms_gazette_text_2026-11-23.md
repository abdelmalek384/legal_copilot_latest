## Why this instrument matters

Decision 444/2025 adopts two attached informed-consent forms for purposes of Medical Liability and Patient Safety Law 13/2025:
1. informed consent for surgical intervention;
2. informed consent for refusal of a medical procedure and leaving the medical facility.

The decision cites, among other instruments, Medical Facilities Law 51/1981, Mental Patient Care Law 71/2009, Organ Transplantation Law 5/2010, Universal Health Insurance Law 2/2018, University Hospitals Law 19/2018, Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020, Clinical Medical Research Law 214/2020, Blood and Plasma Law 8/2021, Egyptian Health Council Law 12/2022, and Medical Liability Law 13/2025.

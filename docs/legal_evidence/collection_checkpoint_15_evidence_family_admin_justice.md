## 4. Ministry of Finance primary source portal: official discovery inventory

- Index reports 936 law records, 522 financial law records and a separate decisions catalogue reported elsewhere as 2,037 records.
- These are catalogue counts, not unique current instruments or full-text collection totals.

- Portal's disclaimer says online texts are guidance and Official Gazette is the legal reference.

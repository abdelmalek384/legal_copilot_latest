# Collection checkpoint 9 — data protection, cybercrime, e-signature and consumer law (2026-11-17)

## 1. Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020 — source lead and dependencies

- Official law number and enactment: Law 151/2020 on Personal Data Protection.
- FRA Decision 141/2023 updated text expressly cites Law 151/2020 in its legal basis, demonstrating a direct regulatory dependency between personal-data protection and fintech outsourcing controls.
- Primary-source locator to establish next: Ministry of Communications / Personal Data Protection Center official legislation and executive regulation. Do not rely on a standalone secondary PDF as current-law proof.
- Required linked instruments: executive regulation, Data Protection Center establishment/competence decisions, cross-border transfer rules, controller/processor licensing/permits, breach notification and sectoral regulator rules.
- Currentness is a high priority because this area directly affects Legal Copilot's client/case data and processing architecture.

## 2. Cybercrime / Information Technology Crimes Law 175/2018

- Secondary full-text discovery lead: https://manshurat.org/node/31487
- Official primary source and executive regulation still need pinning.
- Required related instruments include the Executive Regulation, competent authority decisions, telecom/network obligations and procedural/evidence interactions.
- Keep criminal offences, service-provider obligations, preservation/disclosure rules and electronic evidence requirements in separate indexed concepts.

## 3. Electronic Signature Law 15/2004

- Primary-source discovery should be routed through ITIDA and the Ministry of Communications.
- The law is a foundational dependency for e-signatures, electronic records and trusted service providers.
- Related regulations and ITIDA licensing/technical decisions must be collected separately; don't infer an electronic document is legally signed merely because it is digitally stored.

## 4. Consumer Protection Law 181/2018

- Secondary full-text discovery lead: https://manshurat.org/node/31479
- Primary source: Consumer Protection Agency official legislation index and Gazette copies still required.
- Related instruments to collect: Executive Regulation, e-commerce rules, supplier disclosure, warranty, recall, advertising and price-announcement decisions.
- Map overlap with Consumer Finance Law 18/2020, banking, fintech and e-commerce.

## 5. Financial consumer protection / consumer finance

- FRA official regulatory material links Law 5/2022 fintech with digital identity, digital contracts, digital registers and security controls.
- Consumer Finance Law 18/2020 is a further statutory lead; official FRA or relevant regulator text and executive regulations need to be pinned.
- Do not conflate consumer protection generally with consumer finance sector licensing.

## 2. Law 160/2023 — major amendment to Investment Law 72/2017

- Official Amiri Press full text:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=mP8ZtbLPe4g%3D
- Law 160/2023 replaces the definition of special incentives and amends Articles 9, 11 (first paragraph, item 1), 12 (first paragraph, item 2), 13, 14 (first paragraph), 17, 20 and 34 of Law 72/2017.
- It defines special incentives by reference to Articles 11 and 11 bis.
- Article 9 confirms general incentives apply to all investment projects under the Law, whether established before/after its commencement, except free-zone projects.
- Article 11 (as amended) provides a 50% investment-cost deduction for sector (A) geographic areas most in need of development, based on the investment map and official CAPMAS data.
- Article 12 extends the deadline for establishment of qualifying projects to three years from the Executive Regulation's commencement, with Cabinet power to extend up to nine years in total.
- Article 13 permits additional Cabinet incentives for projects under Articles 11 and 11 bis, including special customs outlets for project imports/exports by agreement with Finance Minister.
- Article 20 allows a single approval for strategic/national projects and public-private infrastructure/energy/roads/transport/ports projects, covering establishment, operation, management, building permits and land allocation; approval may be effective without further administrative action, subject to conditions and enforcement.
- Article 34 allows free-zone projects in specified petroleum, fertilizer, steel, natural gas and energy-intensive industries with Supreme Energy Council approval, subject to statutory conditions.
- This is a substantive amendment, not just an implementing decision.

## 5. Importers Register Law 121/1982 — official Customs Authority full text

- Official Customs Authority PDF:
  https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/eda4175-240b-41b5-93c6-d91eca3a6da6.pdf
- Gazette issue 21, 5 August 1982.
- Article 1 prohibits import of goods for trade unless the natural/legal person is registered in the Importers Register at the competent Ministry.
- Article 2 sets registration conditions for natural persons and legal persons, including commercial-register/tax-card status, nationality/residence/experience, capital, company form and other requirements.
- Law 121/1982 is distinct from Import/Export Law 118/1975 and Customs Law 207/2020; all three must be read together for commercial importers.

## 6. Executive Regulation — Minister of Trade Decision 846/2017

- Customs Authority index lists Minister of Trade and Industry Decision 846/2017 issuing the Executive Regulation of Importers Register Law 121/1982:

## Operative provisions — captured in summary

1. **Article 1:** Subjects the occupations listed in the attached schedule (435 occupations) to skill-measurement tests under the then-applicable Labour Law and implementing ministerial decisions.
2. **Article 2:** Repeals any prior text or decision that conflicts with Decision 111/2023.
3. **Article 3:** Orders publication in Egyptian Proceedings and commencement on the day after publication.

The decision's preamble cites Labour Law 12/2003 and Ministerial Decision 196/2003. It also refers to the 2017 Egyptian Occupational Classification.

## Relationship to Decision 266/2025

Ministerial Decision 266/2025 later establishes a new framework for skill measurement and occupational licensing under Labour Law 14/2025. Its Article 25 repeals prior decisions or provisions that conflict with the new decision.

Accordingly, Decision 111/2023 is retained as **historical legal material**. It must not be represented as the current licensing/skill-measurement rule. The precise extent to which each provision or schedule entry is superseded must be established by a lawyer; the general repeal clause does not justify silently marking every historical row as expressly repealed.

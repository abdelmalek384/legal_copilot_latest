## Substantive scope relevant to Agent 3
- The standards state that they are aligned with the 2025 International Valuation Standards while being adapted to Egypt's legislative, regulatory and economic environment.
- They cover the valuation of real estate and rights in real estate, as well as machinery, equipment and infrastructure used in production and capital assets.
- The official introduction links valuation to investment and financing decisions, risk management, collateral valuation, protection of users, real-estate finance, real-estate investment and REITs, financial leasing, asset-backed finance, project finance, restructuring and acquisitions.
- The standards require attention to valuation scope, purpose and date; asset identification; data reliability; valuation bases and methods; documentation; reporting; conflicts of interest; professional competence and independence.
- The three principal valuation approaches are market, income and cost approaches; the PDF explains methods under each approach.
- The standards contain detailed treatment of real-estate interests and development property, and specific machinery/equipment/infrastructure valuation rules.
- The official FRA announcement says the standards supersede/update the first real-estate valuation edition issued under Decision 39/2015; exact commencement and transitional clauses must be confirmed from the Gazette decision itself.

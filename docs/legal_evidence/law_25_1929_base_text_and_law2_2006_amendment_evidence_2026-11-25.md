## Law 2/2006 — amendment to Article 21(2)
- **Instrument:** Law 2/2006 amending Article 21(2) of Decree-Law 25/1929.
- **Publication:** Official Gazette issue 6 bis, 15 February 2006.
- **Commencement:** Article 2 states the day after publication: 16 February 2006.
- **Sources:** Eastlaws full-text metadata https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/15-02-2006/no-2?id=259518&type=1 ; Ahmed Moussa law firm explainer https://ahmedmossa.com/ar/8-المفقود ; secondary consolidated annotations https://www.diaalwkeel.com/qanwn-rqm-25-lsnh-1929
- **Operative change observed:** Article 1 replaces the second paragraph of Article 21. It treats the missing person as deceased after at least 15 days where the person was proven to be aboard a ship that sank or aircraft that crashed, and after one year if the missing person was a member of the armed forces lost during war operations. Article 2 provides for publication and next-day commencement.
- **Succession:** Law 33/1992 had earlier replaced Articles 21 and 22; Law 2/2006 replaces Article 21(2); Law 140/2017 later replaces Article 21(2)-(3) and Article 22. Preserve these as separate versions, not one undifferentiated current text.
- **Evidence limitation:** no original Gazette issue 6 bis PDF/page image acquired; Law 2 text remains a secondary reproduction and the event EV-0366 is provisional.

## Related judicial currentness lead
Secondary consolidated sources report that the Supreme Constitutional Court declared Article 18 bis 3 of Law 25/1929 unconstitutional in case 5/8 (constitutional), judgment 6 January 1996, published Official Gazette issue 3, 18 January 1996. This must be checked against the official judgment and Gazette publication before any current consolidated text uses or omits that provision.

## 1996 constitutional judgment — exact partial invalidation scope

The official SCC judgment in Case 5/8 (constitutional), dated 6 January 1996, is available at https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/DjangoPortalResources/Rules_Html/592_8_5_1_2.html. It invalidates only two specified portions of Article 18 bis 3 of Law 25/1929, not the whole article. Those portions concern (1) requiring the father to provide suitable housing even when the children have sufficient funds or the custodian has a dwelling, and (2) the time limit tied to the former wife's idda where the marital home is rented. The original Gazette issue 3 page image remains outstanding; see EG-0557 / FT-0233 / EV-0368.

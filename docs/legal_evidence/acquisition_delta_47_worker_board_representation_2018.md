## 1. Law 18/2018 — worker representatives on public-sector/public-business boards

**Secondary full Arabic transcription:** https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12621  
**PDF facsimile metadata:** https://manshurat.org/node/25917  
**Publication:** Law 18/2018, dated 19 March 2018; Official Gazette publication 19 March 2018; effective 20 March 2018. Gazette issue/page should be confirmed from the original image before production ingestion.

The transcription contains six promulgation articles and 13 substantive articles. Key article map:
- Issuance Arts.1–6: scope and relationship with Companies Law 159/1981, National Authority for Military Production Law 6/1984 and Public Business Sector Law 203/1991; transitional extension of former elected members; simultaneous election timing; repeal of Law 73/1973; delegation to the Minister; publication/effectiveness.
- Substantive Arts.1–3: definitions and scope; secret direct universal ballot under judicial supervision; voter exclusions; candidacy eligibility and disqualifications.
- Art.4: worker representation in public-sector unit boards is at least 50% of elected members; public-business sector representation follows Law203/1991; the union committee chair is invited to board meetings without a counted vote unless already a board member.
- Arts.5–8: ministerial election rules; election committee composition; publication of candidate lists and election dates; result notice and three-day judicial challenge.
- Arts.9–13: four-year term; vacancies and replacement/supplementary election; protection against dismissal/suspension absent judicial basis; prohibition on reducing elected seats during a term; elections following establishment, merger or division of an entity.

Short Arabic text anchors from the transcription:
- Art.2: “بالاقتراع السرى العام المباشر ، تحت إشراف قضائى”.
- Art.4: “بنسبة (50%) ... على الأقل من عدد الأعضاء المنتخبين”.
- Art.8: challenge to results within three days of announcement.

**Source status:** complete 13-article Arabic transcription is accessible from a secondary legal source; a small official PDF record is available on Manshurat. No government-hosted full Gazette PDF was acquired in this pass. Counsel must verify applicability to each type of public entity and reconcile it with Law203/1991 and later company-governance amendments.

## 2. Ministerial Decision 37/2018 — official Gazette text acquired

**Full Gazette issue 61 supplement PDF:** https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/108457/36-2018.pdf  
The PDF's own table of contents places Decision 37/2018 at printed p.173, after Decisions 35/2018 (p.3) and 36/2018 (p.20). Issue 61 (supplement) dated 14 March 2018.

- Decision 37/2018 sets nomination/election dates for the 2018–2022 union cycle.
- The four issuing articles provide the election calendar for workplace/professional committees, general unions and federations, including nomination, publication of candidate lists, challenge, election and document deposit dates.
- This is a historical calendar instrument for the 2018–2022 cycle, not a reusable current election timetable. It is useful for the 2018 cycle archive and procedural history only.
- The full Arabic text and schedules are searchable in the Gazette facsimile at printed pp.173–176. Short text anchor: “بشأن مواعيد الترشيح والانتخاب لعضوية مجالس إدارة المنظمات النقابية العمالية للدورة النقابية ٢٠١٨/٢٠٢٢”.
- Status: source-backed Gazette facsimile via ILO/NATLEX; original Egyptian Gazette remains the controlling publication.

## 5. Currentness and cross-law issues

- Law 18/2018 is a standalone statute and is not expressly repealed by Labour Law 14/2025 issuance Article 12 (which repeals Labour Law 12/2003 and Law125/2010). Its present scope must nevertheless be checked against Law203/1991, Law159/1981, subsequent amendments and current company governance rules.
- Decisions 87–89/2018 implement or relate to Law18/2018 and should be tracked under that statute, not automatically treated as repealed merely because Law14/2025 replaced the old Labour Code.
- Decision37/2018 is time-bound to the 2018–2022 union cycle. Later election decisions (including 2022 cycle decisions 45–48/2022) must be checked separately; do not use the 2018 dates.
- Law18/2018 and the union-law framework are related but distinct: employee representation on boards is not equivalent to trade union registration or collective bargaining.
- No claim is made that Law18/2018 or Decisions87–89/2018 are currently operative in every covered entity. Currentness remains to be verified.

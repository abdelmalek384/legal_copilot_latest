- Signed: 19 May 2024.
- Published: الوقائع المصرية، العدد 125، 5 June 2024.
- Effective: day after publication (6 June 2024).
- Full two-article Arabic transcription: https://www.almasdar.com/print.aspx?id=142692
- Corroborating report with preamble and article text: https://www.dostor.org/4727911
- Legal basis stated in preamble: Commercial Register Law 34/1976; Presidential Decision 354/2008 establishing ITDA; Executive Regulation Decision 946/1976; ITDA memorandum dated 25 March 2024.
- Original Gazette PDF/page images not located in this pass. The published article reports the Gazette issue/date; the official ITDA website is the operational issuing authority for commercial-register records.

## المادة الأولى — approved commercial-register documents

«تعتمد نماذج المحررات المرافقة لهذا القرار التى تصدر عن السجل التجارى وهى كالآتى:

1- نموذج 1: صورة مستخرجة من صفحة القيد فى السجل التجارى.

2- نموذج 2: شهادة بيانات من السجل التجارى "بطاقة منشأة".

3- نموذج 3: شهادة سلبية بعدم القيد فى السجل التجارى.»

## Regulatory effect

- Form 1 standardizes the extracted copy of the relevant commercial-register entry page.
- Form 2 standardizes the commercial-register data certificate (“establishment card”).
- Form 3 standardizes a negative certificate confirming that no commercial-register entry exists.
- The approved forms are an annex to the decision. The annex templates themselves were not reproduced in the secondary text and must be acquired from the original Gazette or ITDA's official form archive before ingestion as actual templates.
- Decision 90/2024 operationalizes the evidence documents issued under Law 34/1976 and Executive Regulation 946/1976; it does not replace the underlying registration rules.

# Checkpoint 115 — Minister of Justice Decisions 5207/2026 and 5208/2026: cadastral postponements in five governorates

## Decision 5207/2026 — Qena and Luxor

- Issuer: Minister of Justice.
- Signed/issued: 30 September 2026 (secondary sources consistently report this date).
- Publication: Egyptian Proceedings issue 243, 2 November 2026.
- Official Amiri Press text portal (indexed result; direct open returned HTTP 502): https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=rEOlBjl474g%3D
- Secondary full/near-full transcriptions and contemporaneous reports: Dóstor (2 November 2026), El Watan (2 November 2026), and other press sources.
- Operative effect reported from the text: postpones the commencement of the real-property-register system under Law 142/1964 for specified cadastral sections in Qena and Luxor for one year, 1 October 2026 to 1 October 2027. Areas include Abnud in Qena Centre; specified sections in Deshna and Abu Tesht; and Thomas wa Afia in Esna, Luxor.
- Article 2 provides for acceptance of Article 19 settlement forms in the listed cadastral sections for two months from the date of issue. Article 3 provides publication and operation from 1 October 2026; Article 4 assigns implementation to the head of the Real Estate Publicity and Notarization Sector.
- Original Gazette PDF not acquired in this step; exact cadastral descriptions and full text need comparison with the official issue.

- Operative effect reported: postpones real-property-register commencement for specified cadastral sections in Sohag, Aswan and Damietta for one year. Sohag and Damietta sections are reported to run through 30 September 2027; Aswan includes Edfo Qibli. The decision also opens the Article 19 settlement-form process for two months from date of issue and provides retroactive operation from 1 October 2026.
- The full section-by-section schedule and exact issue text are not transcribed from the original Gazette because the 6.02 MB file is request-only. Do not treat the press summary as the definitive list of cadastral sections.

## Legal and operational significance

- Together, Decisions 5207 and 5208 cover selected cadastral sections in five governorates: Qena, Luxor, Sohag, Aswan and Damietta.
- These are territorial commencement postponements under Law 142/1964, not amendments to the substantive rules of title registration.
- The two-month Article 19 settlement-form period is a distinct period from the one-year postponement. The text says it runs from the date of issue, not the Gazette publication date. A precise final day should not be certified until the original decision wording, date-counting rule and implementing office practice are confirmed.
- The decisions operate from 1 October 2026 although publication occurred in November. This creates a retrospective effective-date issue that should be preserved in the temporal version record, not silently normalized.
- Earlier postponement sequence for Sohag and other areas includes Decisions 5715/2023, 5375/2024 and 5281/2025; exact per-section continuity should be mapped against the preambles and Gazette copies.

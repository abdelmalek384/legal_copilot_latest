## New bond-offering legal chain — Decisions 145/2021 and 136/2026

### FRA Decision 145/2021 — current consolidated bond-offering rules
- Official FRA 9-page consolidated Arabic PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/145-2021-%D9%88%D9%81%D9%82%D8%A7-%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84.pdf
- The PDF is expressly updated through 8 July 2026 and contains the original two decision articles plus eight substantive sections.
- Covers public/private bond offerings; qualified investors; parties to an offering; minimum denomination; currency; deposit/settlement; listing; subscription; underwriting; legal counsel; issuer disclosures; prospectus/information memorandum content; and continuing disclosure during bond life.
- Explicitly includes financial-leasing and factoring companies among institutional qualified investors for private placements.
- Full record: [Decision 145/2021 consolidated text](agent3_fra_decision_145_2021_bond_offering_rules_consolidated_2026_arabic_text.md).

### FRA Decision 136/2026 — bond-offering amendment
- Official two-page Gazette PDF: https://fra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/alamiria_2026_136.pdf
- Gazette issue 168 supplement, 4 August 2026, printed pages 2–3; adopted 8 July 2026; effective 5 August 2026.
- Replaces three clauses in Decision 145/2021: listed-bond private placements must allocate at least 10% of the issue to an additional tranche for persons other than first-tranche subscribers, exempting them from the normal minimum; private-placement subscription windows are at least one week and no more than 30 business days; legal counsel must warrant the securitization portfolio's contracts/collateral have not been previously assigned or used as collateral and are free of adverse third-party rights.
- Full record: [Decision 136/2026 Arabic text](agent3_fra_decision_136_2026_bond_offering_amendment_arabic_text.md).

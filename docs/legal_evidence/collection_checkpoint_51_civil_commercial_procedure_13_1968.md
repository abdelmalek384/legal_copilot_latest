## 1. Law structure and scope
- 523 articles in the current secondary transcription.
- Regulates civil/commercial litigation procedure, jurisdiction, filing, service/notice, evidence references, hearings, judgments, appeals, cassation, provisional measures, execution and enforcement.
- Promulgation repeals Civil and Commercial Procedure Law 77/1949 except specified preserved provisions, and repeals the first book of the Cassation Appeal Cases and Procedures Law, subject to transition.
- Main books cover courts/jurisdiction, proceedings, judgments and appeals, and execution; exact book/chapter map should be parsed from the full text.

## 2. Core procedural provisions inspected
- Article 1: procedural laws apply to pending actions and procedures not completed before commencement, with exceptions for jurisdiction changes after closure of pleadings, deadlines already started and appeal routes for judgments already issued.
- Article 3: no claim, request or defence accepted without personal, direct, existing legal interest; potential interest suffices to prevent imminent harm or preserve evidence; court may reject ex officio and impose procedural fine up to EGP 500 for abuse.
- Article 47: First Instance Court jurisdiction over civil/commercial disputes not assigned to partial courts; its judgment is final where claim value does not exceed statutory threshold; also hears appeals from partial courts and urgent/ancillary/connected applications.
- Article 248: cassation threshold and categories (as amended) must be read with Law 157/2024.
- Article 480: relevant monetary threshold in execution procedures, as amended.
- The statute also regulates service of process, jurisdiction objections, intervention, joinder, expert evidence, judgment reasoning, appeal periods, interlocutory orders, execution titles, garnishment, real-property execution and sale.

## 3. Critical correction — Law 157/2024 raises thresholds, it does NOT lower them
**Important correction to earlier checkpoint 17:** the earlier note described the 2024 threshold substitutions in reverse. The Arabic promulgated text uses "تستبدل عبارة ... بعبارة ..." so the replacement/new threshold is the first quoted amount; the old phrase is the second.

Law 157/2024 replaces the following older phrases with higher thresholds wherever they occur in Articles 41, 42 (paragraph 1), 43, 47 (paragraph 1), 248 and 480:
- EGP 100,000 is replaced by EGP 200,000.
- EGP 15,000 is replaced by EGP 30,000.
- EGP 250,000 is replaced by EGP 500,000.
- The exact original/corrected text should be read directly from Gazette PDF, but the direction of change is upward.
- Transitional rule: cases filed before 1 October 2024 continue before the courts where pending until final judgment, using procedures and appeal routes in force when filed.
- Law 157/2024 repeals inconsistent provisions and is effective 1 October 2024.

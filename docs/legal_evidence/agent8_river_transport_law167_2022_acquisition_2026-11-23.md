## 1. Main statute — Law 167/2022
**Arabic title:** قانون رقم 167 لسنة 2022 بإصدار قانون إعادة تنظيم الهيئة العامة للنقل النهري  
**Gazette:** Official Gazette, issue 46 bis (أ), 19 November 2022.  
**Commencement:** 20 November 2022 (day after publication).  
**Primary source locators:**
- Amiri Press searchable text: https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=NsEFOyRNhoc%3D
- Court of Cassation publication download: https://register.cc.gov.eg/publications/86/download
**Secondary full Arabic transcription:** https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-167-2022 and https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=12893

### Source status and exactness
- The Amiri Press official search result exposes the statute title and issuing provisions, but a direct open timed out in this session.
- The Court of Cassation portal search result identifies its publication as Gazette issue 46 bis (أ), 19 November 2022; direct PDF retrieval timed out.
- The complete 28-provision Arabic text (seven issuing articles plus 21 articles in the attached law) was read in a secondary legal transcription that identifies its source as the Court of Cassation legislation portal and cites the Gazette issue. This is **not** an official-PDF verification and is not represented as an authenticated Gazette transcription.
- Secondary source reports a corrigendum (استدراك) in Official Gazette issue 48 bis (ب), 6 December 2022, correcting three typographical errors in Article 14. Treat as a correction/version event linked to Law 167/2022, not as an amending law. The corrigendum's official page image/text was not acquired directly.

### Article inventory — 21 articles of the attached law
1. Definitions: competent minister, authority, competent agencies, navigation licences, one-way trip, river units, routes, permits, berths, river ports, dismantling, locks, volumetric ton and navigable channels.
2. Legal status, affiliation and headquarters of the River Transport Authority.
3. Statutory objective: development and improved efficiency of inland-water transport.
4. Authority functions: planning, technical specifications, navigation routes, locks, inspections, port/berth licensing, passenger/freight activity licences and company formation.
5. Board composition and appointment.
6. Board meetings, quorum, voting and ministerial approval.
7. Board's superior authority and enumerated powers.
8. Chairperson's management and representation.
9. Deputy chairperson(s).
10. Administrative one-stop licensing unit; division between tourism establishment licences and river navigation/berthing licences.
11. Contracting and allocation/reallocation of state-owned private property.
12. Exclusive board competence over construction, management, operation and maintenance licences for river ports, quays and berths.
13. Technical conditions for vessels carrying specified dangerous, polluting, special-service or tourism cargo; ministerial decision to be published in Egyptian Proceedings.
14. Inspection, licensing, tonnage, berth, port and advisory-service fees. **Version note:** correct Article 14 against the reported 6 December 2022 corrigendum; secondary transcription records corrections to the body-inspection fee wording, engine inspection unit and advisory-service fee ceiling (EGP 3 million, not EGP 2 million).
15. Public-utility concessions for river ports, quays and berths.
16. Concession duration (minimum five years; maximum fifteen years) and approval process.
17. Periodic reports, annual budget and final accounts.
18. Authority revenue sources.
19. Annual budget and special account within the unified treasury account.
20. Public-money status and restrictions on attachment/acquisitive prescription.
21. Administrative attachment for collection of authority dues.

**Provision count:** 28 total complete provision texts read in the secondary transcription (7 issuing articles + 21 attached-law articles). The article inventory above is a structured map, not a verbatim transcription. No official Gazette page images were read; no local authenticated full-text mirror is claimed.

## 2. Related implementing / operational instruments identified

### Minister of Transport Decisions 340, 341 and 342/2024
- Decision 340/2024: رسوم وتكاليف معاينات الوحدات النهرية والموانئ والمراسي.
- Decision 341/2024: اشتراطات تراخيص الموانئ والمراسي.
- Decision 342/2024: تراخيص الوحدات النهرية غير الآلية.
- Reported issuance date: 28 May 2024; publication: Egyptian Proceedings issue 132 (تابع ج), 13 June 2024; commencement reported 14 June 2024 (Decision 342 metadata; the same issue/date is reported for the batch).
- Official government source: Ministry of Investment / GAFI-hosted River Transport Authority licensing guide PDF: https://www.gafi.gov.eg/Arabic/StartaBusiness/InvestmentZones/SiteAssets/Pages/Inland/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%87%D8%B1%D9%89.pdf
- The guide identifies the three decisions and includes their references/attachments as annexes, but the accessible text layer does not reproduce the operative clauses of the decisions. It confirms that Decision 340 is the source for inspection/service charges, Decision 341 for port/berth licensing requirements, and Decision 342 for non-motorized river-unit licensing.
- Secondary corroboration: Al-Masdar reports Decision 340 in Gazette issue 132 (تابع ج), and the same issue for Decisions 341/342: https://www.almasdar.com/143274 ; https://www.almasdar.com/keyword/23605
- EastLaws metadata for Decision 342: https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/minister-transport/13-06-2024/no-342?id=2135355&type=1
- **Not acquired:** complete official Gazette copies, exact operative article bodies and annex page images for the three decisions.

### River Transport Authority administrative Decision 1/2024
- Title: إصدار لائحة المخالفات والجزاءات للموظفين بالهيئة العامة للنقل النهري.
- Publication reported: Egyptian Proceedings issue 124 (تابع), 4 June 2024.
- Secondary-hosted Gazette reproduction: https://cdn.vetogate.com/UploadCache/libfiles/502/8/0x0o/66.pdf
- The preamble cites Law 167/2022, Civil Service Law 81/2016 and prior Authority Decision 4/1983.
- The source reproduces the issuing clause and identifies the scope as employee disciplinary violations/penalties. The full annex/operative articles have not been transcribed or page-image checked.

### River Transport Authority administrative Decisions 255 and 256/2025
- Decision 255/2025: works with annexes to Ministerial Decision 342/2024 on licensing non-motorized river units; secondary report lists nine technical/licensing annexes and berth requirements for tourist vessels. Publication reported in Egyptian Proceedings issue 182 (تابع), 18 August 2025.
- Decision 256/2025: works with board annexes to Ministerial Decision 341/2024 on river-port and berth licences, including a berth/river-port licence form. Publication reported in the same issue/date.
- Secondary sources: https://www.almasdar.com/print.aspx?id=160138 ; https://www.almasdar.com/print.aspx?id=160139
- Decision 255 metadata: https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/minister-transport/18-08-2025/no-255?id=2175349&type=1
- The secondary metadata includes an apparent one-year duration marker for Decision 255; its exact scope and any extension/replacement must be checked. Do not assume the 2025 annexes remain current in November 2026.
- Complete official issue 182 pages and all technical annexes remain outstanding.

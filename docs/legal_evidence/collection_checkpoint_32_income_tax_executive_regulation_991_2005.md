## Promulgation
- Article 1 applies attached Executive Regulation to Income Tax Law 91/2005; for matters not covered, Finance Minister general instructions/circulars may apply under the law.
- Article 2 contains commencement/transition rules for natural and legal persons.
- Article 3 repeals inconsistent provisions.
- Article 4: publication in Al-Waqa'i and commencement the day after publication.

## Selected actual provisions inspected

### Articles 1–8: residence, permanent establishment and tax periods
- Article 1: inheritance of sole proprietorship to one or more heirs treats each heir as an individual taxpayer.
- Article 2: equipment rental is treated as use/right to use for royalty purposes; registered branch may constitute permanent establishment.
- Article 3: natural-person permanent home can be based on presence most of year or a commercial/professional office/factory/workplace in Egypt. Corporate effective management in Egypt can be established by at least two factors: daily management decisions in Egypt; board meetings held in Egypt; at least 50% of board/managers residing in Egypt; or shareholders holding more than half capital/voting rights residing in Egypt. Anti-avoidance rule where entity adopted management location to evade tax.
- Article 4: preparatory/auxiliary activity means activity that does not contribute to income taxable in Egypt.
- Article 5: broker/agent is treated as devoting most time/effort to foreign company if wholly/almost wholly acting for it under conditions differing from independent enterprises.
- Article 6: shorter/longer tax periods may apply in first year, death, departure, cessation, transfer, change of fiscal year and other listed cases.
- Article 7: ETA may authorize change of tax period for legal persons with regular books, material reason and 12-month period.
- Article 8: identifies competent tax office based on income source, activity location, principal office, real estate, large/mid/small taxpayer centres.

### Salary and benefits
- Article 11: values in-kind benefits at market value with special methods for company cars (20% of fuel/insurance/maintenance cost), mobile phones (20% of annual expenses), low-interest loans (7% reference), employer-paid insurance and discounted company shares.
- Article 12: lists excluded income and deductions, including social insurance, private insurance fund contributions, qualifying life/health insurance premiums and collective in-kind benefits; cap for certain insurance deductions is 15% of net income or EGP 10,000, whichever is less, subject to applicable law/version.
- Articles 13–15: salary change and annual settlement, tax withholding on non-primary-employer payments, and non-resident salary rules.
- Article 21: employers must remit amounts withheld under tax rules.

### Business and accounting rules
- Articles 24–26: computation of net profits, equity-method investment conditions and commercial transactions/asset disposals.
- Article 99 bis (1)–(4): legacy manual invoice book requirements and invoice minimum fields, retained pending decision that electronic invoicing is complete. Decision 286/2021's promulgation Article 4 preserves these rules until that ministerial/delegate decision.
- Article 100: notice of cessation of business; includes permanent departure, forced/administrative closure, surrender of business premises and expropriation, unless later income is proved.
- Article 101: taxpayer may request tax status determination on cessation/transfer/permanent departure; authority responds within 90 days if required returns filed.
- Article 109: correction of mathematical errors in returns.
- Articles 110–111: remittance of withholding/collection-at-source amounts and records of payees/withheld amounts.
- Article 113: ETA may not disregard taxpayer's books/records unless it proves by documents that they are incorrect.
- Article 114 bis: sample audit may estimate income/expenses if supporting documents are absent.
- Article 117: definition of material/accounting errors and corrections.
- Article 119: re-examination only where statutory conditions exist; authority must state reasons.
- Articles 125–127: instalment amount/period based on turnover, profits, collateral and payment history; modification where general/personal circumstances prevent compliance; tax payable in specified situations and delay consideration rules.

- The transcription says updated 9 April 2023; it does not include the later Income Tax Law 151/2026 amendments, including Article 18 repeal and changes to Articles 28, 43, 46 bis, 50, 52 and 56 bis.
- The Income Tax Executive Regulation may require new implementing decisions for Law 151/2026 and its corrigendum.
- Do not use older salary deductions, benefit valuations, tax brackets or manual invoicing provisions without checking current statutes and decisions.

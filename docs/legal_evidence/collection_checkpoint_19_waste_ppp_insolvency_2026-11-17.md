## 4. PPP Law 67/2010 — Executive Regulation and amendment Decision 3217/2022

- Official Amiri Press text of PM Decision 3217/2022:
  https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=JRIfZHx3kaU%3D
- It amends the Executive Regulation issued by PM Decision 238/2011 under PPP Law 67/2010.
- The decision replaces numerous provisions and adds Articles 62 bis through 62 bis 7, including:
  - Limited tender/auction routes where only certain entities have the necessary technical competence and financial capacity.
  - Direct contracting where urgent economic interest or social necessity requires rapid execution and public tendering is unsuitable.
  - Private-sector-originated project proposals, approval and listing in the PPP project list.
  - Competitive tendering for private-sector-originated projects with defined preference mechanisms for the project originator, subject to tender terms and caps.
- This is a major implementation amendment. Obtain base Law 67/2010, original Executive Regulation 238/2011 and the complete 3217/2022 Gazette text before consolidating.

## 5. Insolvency Law 11/2018 — new 2026 implementation fee amendment

- PM/Ministerial source discovery:
  - Insolvency Law 11/2018 is cited as the Law Regulating Restructuring, Preventive Composition and Bankruptcy.
  - Ministry of Justice Decision 3715/2026 amends Justice Minister Decision 2208/2019 on fees and deposits for bankruptcy-management experts in Economic Courts:
    https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=hQXViw+FAPU%3D
- Decision 3715/2026 substitutes fee/deposit amounts and percentage bands in Decision 2208/2019; the text includes changing 20,000 to 40,000 EGP, 50,000 to 100,000 EGP, 2% to 3% and 5% to 6% in specified provisions.
- This is not an amendment to the substantive Insolvency Law itself; it is an amendment to the expert-fee decision implementing insolvency administration in Economic Courts.
- Build the chain: Law 11/2018 → Economic Courts Law 120/2008 and amendments → Decision 2208/2019 → Decision 3067/2021 (expert fee rules) → Decision 3715/2026.

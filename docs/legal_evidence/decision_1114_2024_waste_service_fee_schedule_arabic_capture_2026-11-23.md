Decision 1114/2024 sets fees for integrated waste-management services or one of those services for covered built units and vacant land under Law 202/2020. This capture is transcribed/structured from the accessible searchable Amiriya source. Fee units are Egyptian pounds per month.

## Article 2 — captured exemptions

**مادة (٢)**

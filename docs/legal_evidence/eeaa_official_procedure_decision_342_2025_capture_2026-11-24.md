## Official page content captured

### Legal scope / regulated fields

The EEAA page states that, under Article 13 bis of the Environment Executive Regulation, the listed environmental work may not be practised by persons lacking the required licence/accreditation. It lists:

1. إعداد دراسات تقويم المخاطر البيئية الكمية والنوعية.
2. إعداد دراسات تقويم التأثير البيئي.
3. تطبيق نظم إدارة البيئة.
4. إعداد خطط الالتزام البيئي وإصحاح المخالفات.
5. اقتصاديات البيئة والمحاسبة البيئية.
6. إدارة المشروعات الإنتاجية والخدمية والأنشطة الاقتصادية بالمحميات الطبيعية.
7. النمذجة الرياضية لتشتت الملوثات.
8. دراسات التنوع البيولوجي.
9. الاستدامة البيئية.

The page also states that required environmental measurements must be performed by laboratories accredited by local or international accreditation bodies, with an accreditation scope covering the pollutant types measured.

### Individual accreditation experience bands

The page gives these bands:

| Level | Experience stated on page |
|---|---|
| Environmental specialist B | No experience or less than five years |
| Environmental specialist A | Documented experience from five to ten years |
| Environmental consultant B | Documented experience from ten to twenty years |
| Environmental consultant A | More than twenty years |

It also states that an expert-house director must be registered as an environmental consultant. The page describes a three-month decision period after a complete application and a five-year certificate validity period, renewable under the stated conditions.

### General rules reproduced by EEAA

The page reproduces the following rules as general registration procedures:

- Appropriate higher-education qualification for the relevant environmental specialty.
- No more than three applications for three fields as an environmental consultant (A/B).
- An accredited consultant may not be responsible manager of more than one office.
- Applications are assessed against documented experience in the requested field.
- Experience certificates must identify participation in studies submitted to EEAA and the applicant's role; the committee may reject experience it considers unsuitable.
- A master's degree in the requested specialty may count as one year of experience; a doctorate may count as two years maximum.
- A rejected applicant may reapply after curing the rejection reason.
- All workers in an expert house must hold accreditation for the fields in which it works.
- Certain environmental impact assessments, environmental compliance plans and environmental management-system work (including specified category B/C work) may not be submitted by a lone consultant; the page requires an accredited team and disclosure of each participant's role.
- The higher committee/technical secretariat may interview and technically assess applicants; the page states an 80% minimum score.

### Application-document checklists reproduced by EEAA

**Specialist B (نموذج 1):**
- Application addressed to the EEAA chief executive.
- Completed Form 1 and training/experience form.
- Certified graduation certificate and postgraduate certificate, if any.
- Evidence of no government-sector insurance registration.
- Employer no-objection letter for specialist-B accreditation.
- Recent criminal-record certificate addressed to the Ministry of Environment.
- Evidence of relevant EEAA training.

**Specialist A (نموذج 1):**
- Application and completed Form 1.
- Graduation/postgraduate certificates and relevant training certificates.
- Evidence of no government-sector insurance registration.
- Employer no-objection letter.
- Recent criminal-record certificate.
- Evidence of prior work/studies in the requested specialty; EEAA's relevant database is to be consulted.
- Interview/assessment; page states at least 80 points for a positive recommendation.

**Consultant B and A (نموذج 2):**
- Application and completed Form 2.
- Graduation/postgraduate certificates and relevant training certificates, if any.
- Evidence of no government-sector insurance registration.
- Employer no-objection letter.
- Recent criminal-record certificate.
- Evidence of prior work/studies in the requested specialty, checked against EEAA records.
- Interview/assessment; page states at least 80 points for a positive recommendation.

**Consultancy office / expert house (نموذج 3):**
- Application and completed Form 3, including responsible manager identity and registered business name.
- Commercial-register extract showing environmental activity; tax card; company incorporation contract.
- Valid accreditation certificate of the responsible manager.
- Valid certificates for the specialists/consultants used by the office, with full-time/part-time status, written consent and identity copies.
- Declaration that application data are correct.
- Responsible manager declaration that they do not manage another office.
- Where the accredited consultant does not own the office, a formal notarized power of attorney appointing them to manage it.
- Interview for the responsible manager and the listed personnel.

The page additionally requests an electronic CD containing the application papers.

### Fees displayed on the page

- EGP 100 per environmental field as the fee for presentation to the Higher Committee.
- EGP 1,000 per environmental field for issuance of the accreditation certificate after committee approval.

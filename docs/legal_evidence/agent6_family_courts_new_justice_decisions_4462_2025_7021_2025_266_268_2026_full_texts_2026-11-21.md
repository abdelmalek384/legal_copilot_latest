## A. Justice Decision 4462/2025 — Rashid Family Court and settlement office / Family Prosecution relocation

- Egyptian Proceedings issue 188, 25 August 2025, pages 3–4.
- PDF: https://www.cairo24.com/Upload/libfiles/164/4/939.pdf

**Minister of Justice Decision 4462/2025**

Minister of Justice

Having reviewed the Constitution;
Law 46/1972 on Judicial Authority;
Law 1/2000 regulating certain status and litigation procedures in personal-status matters;
Law 10/2004 establishing Family Courts;
Decision 4291/2004 appointing venues for Family Courts and family-dispute settlement offices, dated 9/8/2004;
Decision 4844/2004 forming the Family Affairs Prosecution;
Decision 1077/2009 relocating Rashid Family Court, under North Damanhour Primary Court, and Rashid Family Prosecution, under North Damanhour Prosecution;
the letter of the Chief Judge of North Damanhour Primary Court dated 10/7/2025;
and the letter of the Assistant Minister for the General Authority for Court Buildings and Real Estate Registration dated 10/7/2025.

**Article 1:** Relocates Rashid Family Court and the family-dispute settlement office, under the Rashid Full Court Mission / North Damanhour Primary Court, from their current location at Wakalet El-Basha Street, Rashid, Beheira, to building 14, El-Bahr Street, opposite the ferry and landing, Rashid, Beheira.

**Article 2:** Relocates Rashid Family Prosecution, under North Damanhour Full Prosecution, from Wakalet El-Basha Street to the same new building at 14 El-Bahr Street, opposite the ferry and landing, Rashid, Beheira.

**Article 3:** The competent Justice Ministry and Public Prosecution administrations shall implement.

**Article 4:** Published in Egyptian Proceedings; effective Saturday 2/8/2025.

Issued 10/7/2025. Minister: Counselor Adnan Fنجري.

## C. Justice Decision 266/2026 — Establishment of Port Fouad Partial Court

- Egyptian Proceedings issue 34, 11 February 2026, pages 3–4.
- PDF: https://www.cairo24.com/Upload/libfiles/176/9/889.pdf

**Minister of Justice Decision 266/2026**

Minister of Justice

Having reviewed the Constitution;
Criminal Procedure Law 150/1950 and amendments;
Civil and Commercial Procedure Law 13/1968 and amendments;
Judicial Authority Law 46/1972;
the letter of the Chief Judge of Port Said Primary Court dated 30/12/2025;
and the letter of the Assistant Minister for the General Authority for Court Buildings and Real Estate Registration dated 30/12/2025.

**Article 1:** Establishes a partial court named “Port Fouad Partial Court,” affiliated with Port Said Primary Court, at Al-Mo'tasem Bellah Street, opposite Port Said University Club, First Port Fouad District, Port Said, instead of its current hearing venue at Port Said Primary Court. It hears partial civil cases, misdemeanors, and violations originating in Port Fouad First, Port Fouad Second, and East Tafreea police districts.

**Article 2:** The competent Justice Ministry and Public Prosecution administrations shall implement.

**Article 3:** Published in Egyptian Proceedings; effective Sunday 1/2/2026.

Issued 15/1/2026. Minister: Counselor Adnan Fنجري.

## D. Justice Decision 268/2026 — Establishment of Port Fouad Family Court and settlement office

- Egyptian Proceedings issue 34, 11 February 2026, pages 6–7.
- PDF: https://www.cairo24.com/Upload/libfiles/176/9/889.pdf

**Minister of Justice Decision 268/2026**

Minister of Justice

Having reviewed:
Civil and Commercial Procedure Law 13/1968 and amendments;
Judicial Authority Law 46/1972 and amendments;
Law 1/2000 regulating certain status and litigation procedures in personal-status matters;
Law 10/2004 establishing Family Courts;
Decision 4291/2004 appointing some Family Court and family-dispute settlement venues;
the letter of the Chief Judge of Port Said Primary Court dated 30/12/2025;
the letter of the Assistant Minister for the General Authority for Court Buildings and Real Estate Registration dated 30/12/2025;
and the letter of the Assistant Minister for Specialized Courts dated 15/1/2026.

**Article 1:** Establishes Port Fouad Family Court and a family-dispute settlement office, affiliated with Port Said Primary Court, at Al-Mo'tasem Bellah Street, opposite Port Said University Club, First Port Fouad District, Port Said, instead of the current hearing location at Port Said Primary Court. It handles cases originating from Port Fouad First, Port Fouad Second, and East Tafreea police districts.

**Article 2:** Family Courts shall, on their own initiative and without fees, transfer cases that become within the jurisdiction of the newly established court, in their current procedural state. If a party is absent, the clerk shall notify that party of the transfer decision and require attendance at the appointed hearing. This paragraph does not apply to cases already decided or reserved for judgment; those remain governed by the decisions under which they were issued.

**Article 3:** The competent Justice Ministry and Public Prosecution administrations shall implement.

**Article 4:** Published in Egyptian Proceedings; effective Sunday 1/2/2026.

Issued 15/1/2026. Minister: Counselor Adnan Fنجري.

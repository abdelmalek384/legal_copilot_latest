# Questions for an Egyptian lawyer: source and data rights

**Purpose:** obtain a short, dated written opinion before collecting, indexing, storing, quoting, or commercially using legal materials in Legal Copilot. This checklist is a request for advice, not a statement of Egyptian law.

## Intended product use to assess

- Store and index legal texts and their metadata.
- Show limited excerpts with citations in a paid legal-research product.
- Preserve historical versions and amendment relationships.
- Process confidential client case files only under a separately approved data-processing design.
- Use third-party AI services only after reviewing the service terms, data location, retention, and training settings.

## Questions for counsel

1. **Official legal texts:** Under Law No. 82/2002 and its applicable provisions, which official documents are excluded from copyright protection (laws, regulations, official decisions, Official Gazette and Egyptian Proceedings texts, and court judgments)? Does the answer differ for annotations, headnotes, indexes, summaries, or editorial compilations?
2. **Court decisions:** What rules apply to Court of Cassation judgments and to principles or summaries prepared by a court or another publisher? May personal data be indexed or shown?
3. **Website terms and acquisition:** What is the legal effect of each source's terms of use, anti-automation restrictions, and commercial-use restrictions? Does manual download change the analysis? Which sources and acquisition methods are approved?
4. **Secondary copies and verification:** May a secondary transcription be used after article-by-article comparison with the Official Gazette? What evidence must be retained, and how should unresolved differences be handled?
5. **Compilations and historical versions:** What rights may exist in another party's arrangement, annotations, amendment history, or editorial work? Which parts may Legal Copilot create and protect independently?
6. **Personal data:** What duties arise under Law No. 151/2020 and other applicable rules when processing personal data in published judgments and private case files? What safeguards or restrictions apply to cross-border transfers and external AI processors?
7. **Professional secrecy:** What Bar Association rules and professional duties govern lawyers' use of AI tools? When are client notice, consent, confidentiality terms, or a local-only processing option required?
8. **Excerpts and books:** What quotation or excerpt limits apply to books, commentaries, research, and modern editions of older works? Which uses require a licence?
9. **Product terms and liability:** What user warnings, terms, disclaimer language, correction procedure, and liability allocation are appropriate for a legal-research assistant that may return incomplete or outdated material?
10. **Written outcome:** Please provide a dated and signed opinion that identifies permitted sources, prohibited uses, required licences, safeguards, and any conditions for commercial deployment.

## Evidence to retain

For each source, record the exact title, publisher/authority, URL or acquisition location, access date, applicable terms, permission/licence basis, permitted purpose, attribution requirements, and counsel's decision. Keep the written opinion and any licences with the source-permission register.

Do not treat a source as approved merely because it is publicly accessible, a URL is available, or a copy matches a secondary transcription. Do not automate retrieval where the source prohibits it. A clean text comparison does not establish current legal status, amendment completeness, privacy compliance, or reuse permission.

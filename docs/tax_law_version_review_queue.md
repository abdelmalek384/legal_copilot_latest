# Tax-law version review queue (Egypt)

## Scope

This queue records discovery leads for tax legislation and related ministerial decisions. It is not a consolidated tax code, legal opinion, or statement that a provision is currently in force. The official Tax Authority and Ministry of Finance indexes help identify instruments; each legal text must still be matched to its original Official Gazette or Egyptian Proceedings publication.

## Priority 1 — correction-sensitive 2026 amendments

The official tax authority indexes list Laws 149/2026 (VAT), 150/2026 (Unified Tax Procedures), and 151/2026 (income tax), together with a corrigendum described as correcting amendments to those three laws.

Required before any of these texts is used in legal answers:

1. Acquire the original promulgated law and the corrigendum only after permission for the intended acquisition is established.
2. Record Gazette issue, publication date, page range, original file hash, and any official replacement/correction notices.
3. Compare the corrigendum with each affected law and document exact before/after wording.
4. Determine whether the correction changes cross-references, operative language, scope, or commencement; do not infer its legal effect from the index label alone.
5. Have qualified Egyptian tax counsel review the corrected text and any commencement/transitional questions.
6. Link each searchable chunk to the reviewed legal version; keep earlier text as a historical version rather than silently overwriting it.

## Other 2025–2026 tax instruments in the inventory

- VAT amendment Law 157/2025 and related listed ministerial decisions 417/2025 and 418/2025.
- Simplified tax accounting records Decision 420/2025.
- Taxpayer settlement Law 5/2025 and tax-incentives Law 6/2025.
- Resource-fee Law 148/2026, tax-dispute renewal Law 152/2026 and Decision 450/2026, stamp-tax amendment Law 153/2026, and public-entity profit remittance Law 154/2026.

These are separate instruments with different legal functions. A tax authority index entry is discovery evidence only; it does not by itself prove authenticity of a downloaded file, permission for commercial reuse, exact scope, commencement, or current effect.

## Provenance and permissions

- Source permission is unknown/pending unless supported by documented authorization or reviewed terms.
- Do not scrape, bulk download, OCR, index, or commercially display materials solely because they are publicly accessible.
- Preserve original bytes and SHA-256 only after the acquisition permission gate passes.
- Keep the official publication distinct from a ministry explanatory page, portal index, FAQ, or secondary summary.
- A corrigendum must be represented as a separate versioned instrument and related explicitly to the affected laws.

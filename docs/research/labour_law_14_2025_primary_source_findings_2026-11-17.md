The issue identifies itself as Egyptian Gazette supplement no. 64, dated 18 March 2026. It reproduces the Ministry of Labour decisions and their publication text. The source is a third-party-hosted scan of an official publication, not a direct Ministry-hosted download; use it as a locator/corroboration and prefer the IDSC Gazette item for controlled acquisition.

### Specific facts confirmed from the PDF
- Decision 48/2026: employers with 100 or more female workers at one location must establish or contract for a nursery for workers' children under four; smaller establishments within a 500-metre radius must share arrangements or contract with a licensed nursery. The decision also specifies location, accessibility, licensing, and cost arrangements. Do not use these summaries as a substitute for reading all eight articles.
- Decision 49/2026: grants seven additional paid annual-leave days to workers in listed hazardous/arduous work and remote areas. The PDF enumerates industrial sectors and named remote areas, including North Sinai, South Sinai, Red Sea, Matrouh, New Valley, Toshka, and East Oweinat. It requires periodic review/update of the lists, with a maximum three-year interval.
- Decision 50/2026 begins on Gazette page 13 and concerns child employment/training and prohibited work. The complete article list and commencement clause still need extraction and review.

## 3. Additional official Ministry / IDSC notice for Decision 346/2025

The IDSC newsletter dated 31 December 2025 reproduces the Ministry announcement and operative provisions of Decision 346/2025 on religious holidays for Christian employees subject to the Labour Law. It says the listed Orthodox holidays include Christmas, Epiphany, Palm Sunday, Maundy Thursday and Easter; Catholic and Protestant lists differ. The decision adds these days to the paid holidays under Decision 294/2025 and allows late arrival until 10 a.m. on certain holidays. Article 2 states it takes effect the day after publication in the Egyptian Gazette.

Source:
https://idsc.gov.eg/upload/NewsLetter/AttachmentA/13459/الأربعاء%2031%20ديسمبر%202025-عدد%20رقم%201208-السنة%20الخامسة.html

This is an official government newsletter reproducing an announcement, not the Gazette publication itself. Gazette issue/page and full signed text must still be captured.

## 5. Important temporal and source corrections

1. The Ministry of Finance legislation page itself warns that its electronic text is for guidance and the Official Gazette is the sole legal reference.
2. Law 14/2025 promulgation Art. 11 says implementing decisions are issued separately and existing decisions continue only insofar as they do not conflict until replaced. This makes repeal/supersession tracking a first-class data requirement.
3. The 2025 Gazette supplement bundles include decisions omitted from the secondary 19-item list. The 19-item roundup is therefore incomplete as a comprehensive inventory.
4. DSpace records may contain item-specific licence files. The repository End User Agreement and each item-specific licence must be read before storing, extracting, indexing, embedding, displaying, or commercializing content.
5. Do not mark any new text `verified`; maintain `verified=0` pending a qualified Egyptian lawyer's sign-off.

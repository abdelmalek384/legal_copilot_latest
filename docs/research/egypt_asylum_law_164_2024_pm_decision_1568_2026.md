## Selected regulatory structure from the indexed official text

- Article 1 defines the competent ministry as the Ministry of Interior, the competent body as the Permanent Committee for Refugee Affairs, and terms including refugee, asylum seeker and priority/vulnerable groups.
- The vulnerable-group definition includes persons with disabilities, older persons, pregnant women, unaccompanied children, and victims of trafficking, torture or sexual violence.
- Article 4 allows a person lacking legal capacity to be represented by a guardian/representative; where none exists, the Committee must provide a qualified legal representative without charge for the application process.
- Family applications are possible, but an adult family member is not included without written consent and may request an independent application.
- The regulation provides for registration, initial medical screening, classification, referrals and case-management arrangements.
- Article 14 sets a decision period of up to six months for an applicant who entered lawfully and up to one year for one who entered unlawfully; priority groups receive priority in examination.
- Article 15 permits temporary measures for national-security/public-order reasons, including prior permission for inter-governorate or international travel; the regulation also provides proportionality and grievance safeguards.
- Article 28 concerns issuance of an asylum-seeker registration card.
- Article 35 lists cessation grounds, including voluntary return, resettlement elsewhere, acquisition of Egyptian nationality and other specified changes in protection circumstances.

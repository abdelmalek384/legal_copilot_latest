## 1. Decision 134/2003 — base establishment / committee / training framework

**Issuer:** Minister of Manpower and Migration, Ahmed Ahmed El-Amawy.  
**Signed:** 19 July 2003.  
**Published:** 2 August 2003.  
**Effective:** 3 August 2003 (Manshurat metadata).  
**Manshurat record:** https://manshurat.org/node/12136  
**Full-text reproduction:** https://bwadc.com.eg/ws/ar/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/190201_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%86_%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A8_1.pdf  
**Source class:** 21-article text and tables reproduced inside a public occupational-training handbook; not the Gazette original. The reproduction begins at PDF text lines/pages after a separate Labour Law extract. Verify all page boundaries and table cells against the Gazette.

### Article and schedule map

- **Arts. 1–4:** application scope and functional OSH units. Article 1 uses a 50-worker threshold for the covered classes and distinguishes industrial/non-industrial establishments by Table 1. Article 2 requires a specialist OSH functional unit for establishments within Article 1, with staffing according to Table 2. Article 4 places the safety duty on the employer/delegate in listed establishments with fewer than 50 workers.
- **Art. 3:** broad functions of the OSH unit: design/equipment review, policy, hazard identification, measurement/evaluation, emergency plans, PPE and collective protection, occupational-health data, awareness, training, daily inspections, incident investigation, records, permits-to-work and fire-protection maintenance.
- **Arts. 5–13:** workplace OSH committees, composition and powers, notification of committee membership, monthly meetings and meetings within 48 hours after serious incidents/occupational disease, quorum, records, inspector attendance and central committees for multi-branch enterprises.
- **Arts. 14–17:** specialist/technician qualifications and training obligations/exemptions.
- **Art. 18:** original training-provider framework: Industrial Security Institute and National Centre for basic training; National Centre for advanced/specific/specialized training; maximum three-year interval between basic and advanced training; licensing for eligible colleges/institutes/associations under Article 19.
- **Art. 19:** ministerial committee powers over curricula, training-provider licensing, trainer/trainee standards, assessments and certificates.
- **Arts. 20–21:** repeal of prior conflicting decisions and commencement the day after publication.

### Tables

- **Table 1:** industrial and non-industrial establishment classifications.
- **Table 2:** number of full-time OSH specialists/technicians and non-full-time personnel by establishment type and workers per shift. The scan's extracted text has a split/awkward row at the non-industrial 500-worker continuation. Do not import staffing numbers until the actual page image is manually transcribed and independently checked.
- **Table 3:** training types, approved providers and covered trainees.

### Operational caution

The 50-worker and staffing thresholds above are what the reproduced 2003 text says. They are not a statement of current thresholds under Law 14/2025 or Decision 249/2026. The schedules must be compared with later amendments and the new law before any answer engine uses them.

## 3. Decision 263/2010 and Decision 74/2013 — training curriculum and licensing

**Decision 263/2010 metadata:** signed 5 October 2010; Egyptian Gazette issue 240, 18 October 2010; effective 19 October 2010 (Manshurat metadata).  
**Manshurat record and scan:** https://manshurat.org/node/12186  
**Source class:** 11-page scanned Gazette PDF; the first two page images were visually inspected. Full OCR/transcription and page-by-page QA remain outstanding.

The visible preamble and first operative page describe a committee to set OSH training curricula and programmes, licensing rules/conditions for eligible training providers, and requirements for lecturers and trainees. The later 2013 amendment confirms that Article 5 addresses candidate qualifications and that Article 6 addresses training assessment. The exact complete provisions must be transcribed from the scan.

### Additional page-image transcription (Gazette issue 240, 18 October 2010)

The 11-page scan was visually inspected beyond its cover and first operative page. The following provisions are legible in the page images; treat this as a working transcription pending second-reader review:

- **Art. 1:** forms a committee chaired by the head of the Ministry's central OSH administration, with Ministry, National Centre, Workers' Cultural Institution, employer/worker organisation and specialist-expert representation.
- **Art. 2:** establishes theoretical and practical curricula and programme durations for basic, advanced, management/fire-prevention and practical measurement-device training. The schedule differentiates internal and external delivery and categories such as OSH specialists, technicians and committee members. Capture every row from the image before importing exact durations.
- **Art. 3:** licensing conditions for colleges, institutes, research centres and specialized NGOs to provide specific/specialized OSH training. The applicant must be a legal person; submit legal status, engineering plan, ownership/lease evidence, trainer-manager experience/CVs, training programme and equipment details. Training premises must be permanent/independent and at least 80 m².
- **Art. 4:** lecturer requirements include scientific/technical qualifications and specialized practical experience; the scan states at least eight years of OSH experience for the relevant lecturer categories.
- **Art. 5:** trainee qualifications differ by OSH specialist, technician and committee-member courses. The scan permits some non-matching qualifications under specified conditions, and Decision74/2013 later replaces paragraph 3.
- **Art. 6:** defines course assessment weights by programme type and excludes trainees below 80% attendance from examinations; a successful trainee needs at least 50% of total marks.
- **Art. 7:** general course administration conditions, internal/external course sizes and committee meeting cycle. The visible page gives external group size 10–30 and internal 15–45, with separate minimum for management-level external programmes.
- **Art. 8:** a technical secretariat supports the committee.
- **Art. 9:** expressly repeals Decisions206/2004 and 202/2008, plus conflicting provisions.
- **Art. 10:** publication in the Egyptian Gazette and commencement the following day; signed 5 October 2010.

**Do not rely on the working transcription for exact scoring percentages, every course duration or every licensing attachment until each table cell is manually captured and checked by a second reviewer.** The image pages are the evidence; the summary above is not a substitute for a structured, article-by-article transcription.

**Decision 74/2013:** signed 21 February 2013; Egyptian Gazette issue 72, 27 March 2013; effective the day after publication.  
**Full transcription:** https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?ItemID=83233&NID=102316&Type=6&action=ViewActivePages

- Article 1 replaces paragraph 3 of Article 5 of Decision 263/2010.
- It permits candidates holding qualifications different from those in Article 5(1) to attend basic and advanced specialist/technician training, but the certificate must state that the certificate alone does not exempt the holder from the appropriate qualification required for an OSH specialist/technician position.
- Article 2 repeals conflicting provisions.
- Article 3 provides publication and commencement on the day after publication.

**Practical effect of the amendment:** course attendance/completion is not equivalent to satisfying the underlying professional qualification requirement. Preserve the certificate disclaimer as a separate rule and do not collapse training eligibility into job-qualification eligibility.

## 3.0.1 Decision 31/2004 — separate internal Centre training regulation

**Instrument:** Ministerial Decision31/2004 amending the training regulations of the National Centre for Occupational Safety and Health Studies and Work Environment Protection.  
**Official Ministry PDF locator:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/0vvhscce/2004-31-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84-%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A-%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%87%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A6%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84.pdf  
**Full public reproduction:** https://ahmedmossa.com/ar/الباب-السابع-أجهزة-البحوث-والدراسات  
**Source class:** secondary legal reproduction of 39 articles; official Ministry PDF locator identified but not directly acquired.

### Article map

- **Art. 1:** training programmes, durations, target cohorts and historical fees; the reproduced table references Decision206/2004 for entry requirements.
- **Arts. 2–5:** special programmes, annual planning, programme announcement and class size (15–45).
- **Arts. 6–7:** admission requirements based on qualifications, experience and responsibility.
- **Arts. 8–13:** administrative/technical supervision, six-hour daily maximum, Arabic/English delivery, 80% attendance threshold and examinations.
- **Arts. 14–15:** repeat examination and certificates.
- **Arts. 16–22:** lecturer/trainer selection, technical/administrative supervisors, training materials.
- **Arts. 23–35:** detailed historic remuneration rates for lecturers, practical trainers, supervisors and support staff.
- **Art. 36:** trainee feedback/evaluation.
- **Art. 37:** foreign/Arab/African trainee admission and historical fee schedule.
- **Arts. 38–39:** conflicting text repeal and commencement in training year 2005/2006.

This is a separate internal Centre training regulation, not the same instrument as Decision206/2004's training curricula and provider conditions. Do not assume Decision263/2010 expressly repealed Decision31/2004; its scanned Article9 names Decision206/2004 and a disputed 2008 instrument, not 31/2004. Decision11/2014 later regulates Centre training in 22 articles, but its Article21 only repeals conflicting text. Whether it wholly replaces, partially supersedes or coexists with Decision31/2004 needs clause-by-clause comparison.

## 3.1 Decision 25/2010 and its repeal chain

**Official Ministry-hosted Gazette PDF:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/0kmpptt1/2010-25-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9-18-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-134-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2003.pdf  
**Gazette:** issue 33, 10 February 2010. **Signed:** 3 February 2010.

The Ministry PDF text supplies the complete three-article decision:
- Article 1 substitutes Article 18 of Decision134/2003. It assigns basic, advanced, specific and specialized training to the National Centre; sets a maximum three-year interval between basic and advanced training; and allows licensing of specialized colleges, institutes and NGOs to provide basic/specific/specialized training subject to Article19.
- Article 2 expressly repeals Decision98/2006 and all conflicting prior text.
- Article 3 orders publication and implementation.

A separate public legislative record states that Article5 of Decision232/2010 later repealed Decision25/2010. The official Ministry-hosted 232/2010 PDF must be compared to confirm this clause directly. Decision25/2010 is therefore a historical intermediate rule, not the final Article18 text.

**Year discrepancy:** Decision11/2014's Gazette transcription cites “Decision25/2003” in its preamble, while the official Ministry Gazette PDF establishes a Decision25/2010. Keep the preamble's printed citation and the actual 25/2010 instrument distinct until original page images resolve whether the year is a typographical error or another instrument.

### Article map

- **Arts. 1–2:** programme types, durations and historical training fees; special in-house programmes.
- **Arts. 3–5:** annual programme plan, class-size range (15–40, with exceptions if costs can be covered), evening delivery and seminars.
- **Arts. 6–7:** admission requirements and foreign trainees.
- **Arts. 8–13:** administrative/technical supervision, daily schedule capped at six hours, Arabic delivery with limited English option for specialized technical curricula, tests, 80% attendance prerequisite for examination, certificates and certificate replacement.
- **Arts. 14–16:** lecturer/trainer qualifications, supervisors and delivery of training materials for review.
- **Art. 17:** remuneration schedule for lecturers, supervisors and supporting staff.
- **Arts. 18–20:** trainee feedback, seminar financing and allocation of remaining training budget.
- **Arts. 21–22:** repeal of conflicting text and publication/commencement.

### Unresolved internal citation mismatch

The preamble and Article 6 refer to Decision 263/2010, but the training-programme table repeatedly says eligibility is according to Decision 623/2010. Preserve this as a source discrepancy. Do not silently replace 623 with 263 until the signed official PDF/Gazette scan is checked. The preamble also cites Decision 25/2003 as an Article 18 amendment, whereas the Ministry index lists Decision 25/2010 and public material says Decision 232/2010 repealed 25/2010. These year/number conflicts need original-source resolution.

Historical fees in Article 1 are not current fees and must never be surfaced as today's training prices.

## 6. Consolidated relationship graph (not a currentness conclusion)

1. **134/2003** is the base OSH functional-unit, committee, qualification and training instrument.
2. **25/2010** is the official Gazette amendment to Article18 of 134/2003; Article2 repeals 98/2006; public legislative reference says Article5 of **232/2010** later repealed 25/2010.
3. **232/2010** amended Articles18 and19 of 134/2003.
4. **263/2010** is a separate training-curriculum / training-provider licensing framework; Article9 repeals Decisions206/2004 and a 2008 decision whose number must be resolved (scan appears 202; secondary commentary says 20).
5. **74/2013** amended Article 5(3) of 263/2010.
6. **11/2014** amended the National Centre's internal training regulations and refers to Decisions 134/2003, 25/2003 and 263/2010; it contains an unresolved 623/2010 cross-reference in its training table.
7. **216/2016** later replaced the first paragraph of Article 18 of 134/2003.
8. **249/2026** is a later Law 14/2025-era instrument in the OSH establishment/committee field. The complete relationship between 249/2026 and the old 134/2003 framework—including express repeal, partial replacement, savings and Article 11 transition—still requires full Gazette comparison.

## 7. Law 14/2025 transition checkpoints

- Law 14/2025, Article 261 places basic, advanced, specific and specialized OSH training with the Ministry, National Centre and licensed entities; the Minister is to issue licensing and training rules.
- Article 262 requires licensing for OSH expertise/consultancy.
- Article 263 assigns the National Centre a research-planning role.
- Law 14/2025 Article 11's continuation rule must be applied instrument-by-instrument: only pre-existing decisions that were in force and do not conflict with the new law continue until replacement executive decisions. Do not presume that every old training clause or fee schedule survived.
- The old 134/2003 staffing thresholds, 2014 training fees, admission rules and provider approvals must be checked against the new statute and 2026 decisions before production use.

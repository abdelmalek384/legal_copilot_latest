# Cabinet-Approved Draft — Higher Council for Human Resource Skills (28 October 2026)

Research date: 17 November 2026  
Status: Cabinet approval of a draft Prime Minister decision only. No final signed PM decision number or Official Gazette publication was located in this pass. Do not register as an issued instrument or treat the draft as legally operative.

## Official source

- **Cabinet meeting, 28 October 2026:** https://www.cabinet.gov.eg/Meeting/Details/11912
- **Relevant passage:** Cabinet decision item 6, lines 50–59 of the official meeting report.
- The same meeting approved the draft PM decision for the Training and Qualification Fund, which was subsequently published as PM Decision 3356/2026 on 1 November 2026. The HR Skills Council draft did not appear as a separate decision in the Official Gazette issue 44 bis (A) metadata reviewed.

## Draft structure and powers described by Cabinet

- **Chair:** Minister responsible for labour.
- **Membership:** representatives of several ministries; head of the Central Agency for Organization and Administration or delegate; head of the National Council for Persons with Disabilities or delegate; employer-organization and labour-union representatives; and experts as needed.
- **Main functions:**
  1. Set public policies for human-resource skills development and training/qualification.
  2. Set policies for training and qualifying persons with disabilities, dwarfs and priority groups.
  3. Link education/training outputs to current labour-market needs and future occupations/skills.
  4. Develop a comprehensive national programme for human-resource skills development.
  5. Coordinate with the Higher Council for Planning and Operating Domestic/Overseas Workforce to track labour-market information and demographic indicators and forecast workforce needs.
  6. Coordinate ministries/entities so workforce planning and employment connect with education/training and economic/social development plans.
  7. Coordinate with universities/institutes to provide technical training opportunities for students.
  8. Prepare a national strategy for workforce reskilling, skill development and emerging requirements.
- **Term:** four years.
- **Meetings:** at least once every three months at the chair's invitation.
- **Committees:** may form specialist committees from members/non-members; labour-union and employer-organization representation must be considered.
- **Reporting:** chair submits decisions, recommendations and results to the Prime Minister every three months or when needed.

## Statutory link and status

- Labour Law 14/2025 Article 18 requires a PM decision constituting the Higher Council for Human Resource Skills and defining its powers and work system.
- Cabinet approval is a necessary government step but does not prove the final decision was signed, numbered, published or brought into force.
- As at 17 November 2026, the final instrument number, signed date, Gazette issue and commencement clause remain unknown.

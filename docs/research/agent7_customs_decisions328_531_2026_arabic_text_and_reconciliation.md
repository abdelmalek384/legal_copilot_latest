# Agent 7 — Customs Executive Regulation amendments: Finance Decisions 328 and 531/2026

**Acquisition date:** 23 November 2026  
**Base regulation:** Minister of Finance Decision430/2021, Executive Regulation to Customs Law207/2020.  
**Scope:** two new amendments to separate provisions of the customs regulation. Do not merge them with Decision262/2026, which amended Article232.

## A. Finance Minister Decision 328/2026 — Article95(c) guarantee undertakings

**Gazette metadata:** Egyptian Proceedings issue132 supplement (أ), 21 June 2026.  
**Official Amiri Press record:** https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/d618d107-ac2d-40dd-b1fa-5372805b4271/full  
**Official Customs Authority reproduction:** Procedures Circular16/2026, https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace065890338.pdf  
**Secondary record with effective date:** https://blog.iqrar.ai/news/gnews_gaz_780dc01d7523_0000/  
**Source status:** only the amended clause is captured; original Gazette PDF is request-copy restricted and the decision preamble/Article2 have not been recovered.

### Arabic text captured — amended clause

ورد في منشور مصلحة الجمارك رقم (١٦) لسنة ٢٠٢٦ أن قرار وزير المالية رقم ٣٢٨ لسنة ٢٠٢٦ استبدل بنص البند «ج» من المادة (٩٥) من اللائحة التنفيذية لقانون الجمارك المشار إليها، النص الآتي:

«ج- تعهد يقبله الوزير أو من يفوضه، من إحدى الوزارات أو المصالح الحكومية أو الهيئات العامة أو شركات القطاع العام أو الشركات القابضة لجميع الأنظمة الجمركية، على أن يكون موقعاً من الوزير المختص أو رئيس المصلحة أو رئيس الهيئة أو رئيس الشركة أو من يفوضه كل منهم، وكذلك التعهد الصادر من الاتحاد العام للغرف التجارية بالنسبة لتطبيق الاتفاقية الجمركية المتعلقة بالنقل الدولي للبضائع المشار إليها، على أن يكون موقعاً من رئيس مجلس إدارة الاتحاد.»

### Legal/operational effect

The accepted guarantor pool is expanded beyond the former scope to include ministries, government departments, public authorities, public-sector companies and holding companies, with signature by the competent minister/entity head or delegate. The clause also accepts undertakings from the General Federation of Chambers of Commerce for the international carriage convention, signed by its board chair.

The secondary index reports effective date 22 June 2026 (the day after the reported Gazette date). This must be verified against the restricted Gazette copy. The Customs Authority circular reproduces the amended clause but does not reproduce the entire decision.

**المادة الثانية**

### Cross-source discrepancy — preserve, do not silently normalize

The Gazette page image and Al-Masdar transcription say that Article1 replaces subparagraph **(أ) of Article264**. Customs Authority Procedures Circular16/2026, issued later, describes Decision531 as replacing **item (3) of Article264**, while quoting replacement text beginning with «أ-». The original Gazette image is direct evidence for the wording of the decision itself; the Customs Authority circular is evidence of the administration's later cross-reference. Keep both source statements in the legal data until the original PDF and consolidated Article264 are compared. Do not silently convert one locator into the other.

### Legal/operational effect

The amendment requires a full-content customs declaration with all bill-of-lading particulars entered in the automated system under register 46 K.M. Where the owner or agent seeks to withdraw only part of goods stored in customs warehouses, regulatory/security approvals are limited to that withdrawn part.

## C. Relationship among 2026 amendments to Decision430/2021

| Decision | Gazette date | Regulation provision | Subject |
|---|---|---|---|
| 262/2026 | 24 May 2026 | Article232 | Documents accompanying customs declaration |
| 328/2026 | 21 June 2026 | Article95(c) | Accepted guarantee undertakings |
| 531/2026 | 17 September 2026 | Article264(أ), subject to cross-reference discrepancy | Full-content declaration and partial withdrawal from customs warehouses |

The three decisions amend different provisions and are not interchangeable. A consolidated Decision430/2021 text must preserve the amendment source, date, effective date and exact affected subparagraph.

# Decision 134/2003 — OSH establishment duties, committees, staffing and training

Research date: 17 November 2026  
Instrument: Ministerial Decision 134/2003 determining establishments required to establish occupational safety and health functional units and committees, training bodies and applicable rules.  
Text source: Government-owned water and wastewater holding-company training manual, version 2 dated 1 April 2019, reproducing the decision within a trainee legal-reference guide: https://ghwsc.com.eg/Image_Pdf/TraininEducationDocument/14/153.PDF  
Source class: government-company-hosted training material reproducing the text; not the signed Gazette original. The transcription includes a separate copy of Law 12/2003 before the decision and later Decision 126/2003 after it. It contains OCR/truncation and table-extraction limitations.

## 1. Coverage and establishment thresholds

- **Article 1:** the decision applies to workplaces and establishments of any type or affiliation. The specialist OSH-unit regime applies to the listed establishments with 50 or more workers at one site; separate sites carrying out an establishment activity are treated as establishments when each has 50 or more workers. Branches/sites within the same governorate are aggregated as described in the reproduced text.
- **Schedule 1:** lists industrial and non-industrial activity classes. The reproduced text names construction, land/sea/air transport and communications, media/printing/publishing, tourism/hotels/public venues, loading/unloading/storage, agriculture/veterinary/fishing/forestry, services (including cleaning/security/port services/oil services), mines/quarries/salt works/petroleum, manufacturing, water/sewerage/electricity/gas utilities, nuclear energy, healthcare, hazardous-waste treatment, water resources and natural-gas activities. The exact classification and any excluded categories must be checked against the Gazette schedule.
- **Article 4:** for listed establishments employing fewer than 50 workers, the employer or delegate is responsible for providing OSH/work-environment requirements and precautions; the source does not support treating every small establishment as automatically subject to the same full-time staffing table as a 50+ establishment.

## 2. OSH functional unit and duties

**Article 2** requires a specialized OSH functional unit in establishments covered by Article 1, sized by the establishment's levels/workforce and the attached staffing schedule. The reproduced text is truncated part-way through Article 2 in the GHWSC manual copy; the complete wording and table should be verified against the Gazette.

**Article 3** lists the functional unit's duties. The transcription includes:
- advising on the design of buildings, production/service equipment and risk-control systems;
- defining OSH policies and identifying production processes, raw/ intermediate materials, products, hazardous materials and waste;
- identifying physical, mechanical, biological, chemical and other workplace hazards;
- measuring/evaluating pollutants and risks with portable and fixed equipment;
- analysing potential industrial disasters and accidents, preparing emergency response plans and training/testing their effectiveness;
- contributing to technical specifications for collective and personal protective equipment;
- advising on occupational health/social care, first aid, accident/injury/disease statistics and preventive recommendations;
- worker awareness through seminars, campaigns, posters, bulletins and guidance;
- contributing to training plans and OSH research;
- preparing annual plans and investment needs;
- inspecting work areas on every shift;
- investigating serious accidents, injuries and occupational diseases and preparing technical reports;
- maintaining OSH service/policy records and issuing work permits before hazardous maintenance/repair/other work, with advance notification to the OSH unit;
- notifying the employer/manager and competent authorities immediately when hazards are found;
- maintaining fire/explosion prevention and rescue equipment.

The list above is a structured paraphrase of the government-company-hosted transcription, not a substitute for the complete Arabic text.

## 4. OSH committees (Articles 5–13)

- **Article 5:** establishments with 50 or more workers establish one or more OSH/work-environment committees. The reproduced composition includes the employer/delegate or responsible manager as chair; department heads; civil-defence/fire representative; establishment doctor if present; the lead OSH officer as member/rapporteur; and an equal number of worker representatives selected through the union process described in the text.
- The committee examines working conditions, serious accidents, injuries and occupational diseases; sets preventive rules; reviews OSH structures, investment plans, staffing/equipment, risk measurement, emergency response, technology, hazardous materials and waste. Its decisions are described as binding on the establishment and branches.
- **Article 6:** notify the competent Labour Directorate OSH office of committee members and changes.
- **Article 7:** meet at least monthly and within 48 hours after a serious accident or confirmed occupational-disease injury.
- **Article 8:** quorum is a majority including at least one worker representative and one management representative. If quorum is absent, the meeting is postponed 48 hours after written notice; the subsequent meeting may proceed with any number. Decisions pass by majority; the chair's side prevails on a tie.
- **Article 9:** members submit proposals to the rapporteur in writing at least one week before the meeting.
- **Article 10:** the rapporteur maintains a numbered, page-stamped committee register available to OSH inspectors; minutes are signed by the chair, rapporteur and worker representative.
- **Article 11:** the Directorate is notified of meeting time/place so the OSH inspector can attend at least two meetings annually.
- **Article 12:** after three consecutive unexcused absences, the establishment committee nominates a replacement and notifies the OSH office.
- **Article 13:** establishments with multiple branches may form a central committee at the head office to supervise branch committees; it includes the employer/board chair or delegate, branch committee chairs, branch OSH officers, head-office doctor/fire representative if present, head-office OSH lead and worker representatives; it meets every three months.

## 5. Qualifications and training (Articles 14–19)

- **Article 14:** OSH specialists in Schedule 1/A establishments must hold scientific/practical qualifications aligned with the establishment's work.
- **Article 15:** OSH technicians must hold relevant intermediate or post-intermediate scientific/practical qualifications or equivalent military technical qualifications.
- **Article 16:** technicians in Schedule 1/B establishments may hold the qualifications in Article 15 or relevant intermediate technical qualifications as determined by the competent Labour Directorate manager.
- **Article 17:** establishments train specialists, technicians, committee members and management/production/facilities personnel, including doctors, according to responsibilities and workplace nature. Training includes basic, advanced, specific and specialized levels. The text states exemptions for specified postgraduate-qualified specialists and Ministry/Directorate/Centre inspectors and specialists who have received training from those bodies.
- **Article 18:** the reproduced original wording names the Industrial Safety Institute and National Centre for OSH Studies as basic-training providers; the Centre provides advanced, specific and specialized training. It states a maximum three-year interval between basic and advanced training and permits specified colleges/institutes/associations to deliver specific/specialized training under Article 19 rules.
- **Article 19:** creates a Ministerial committee to set theoretical/practical curricula, license eligible training bodies, define lecturer/trainee qualifications and levels, set examination rules, supervise implementation and approve training certificates.

Decision 232/2010 and Decision 216/2016 later amend Article 18-related provisions; see [the OSH predecessor relationship note](osh_legacy_decisions_134_232_216_2026-11-17.md) and [Decision 11/2014's training rules](decision_11_2014_osh_centre_training_regulations.md).

## 6. Repeal and commencement

- **Article 20:** the reproduced text says previous decisions and any provisions conflicting with Decision 134/2003 are repealed.
- **Article 21:** publication in the Egyptian Gazette and commencement on the day after publication.

The GHWSC guide transcription does not show the Gazette issue/page, signature date or a source hash for Decision 134/2003. Do not infer those particulars from the guide's publication/version date.

## 7. Source discrepancies and limitations

1. **Ministry-index title mismatch:** the Ministry's current legislation index lists a 2003/134 entry as “إقرار حقوق العمال السابق لهم الحصول عليها”, whereas the GHWSC guide, later Decisions 232/2010 and 216/2016, and Decision 11/2014's preamble identify Decision 134/2003 as the OSH functional-unit/training instrument. Treat the index title as a metadata conflict, not proof that the OSH decision does not exist. Obtain the Gazette original to resolve identity and publication particulars.
2. **Preamble year typo:** the GHWSC guide transcription's Decision 134 preamble refers to Labour Law 12/2001, whereas the law is Law 12/2003 and the other public transcription identifies 12/2003. Preserve this as a transcription error lead; do not quote it as correct law.
3. **Article 2 truncation:** the GHWSC manual copy truncates Article 2 before the end. Do not reconstruct the missing wording from memory or an unlicensed copy.
4. **Schedule 2 extraction:** non-industrial staffing rows are incomplete; do not invent missing counts.
5. **Rights:** a separate Dar Almandumah reproduction identifies its source as an Egyptian Tax Association bulletin and expressly limits use to personal use, prohibiting copying/redistribution without permission. That restricted source was not used as a product-ingestion source. The GHWSC training guide's own reuse terms still require review.
6. **Currentness:** this is a pre-Law 14/2025 instrument. Article 11 transition, later amendments (including 25/2003 or 25/2010 discrepancy, 232/2010 and 216/2016), Decision 249/2026 and current OSH rules require clause-level review.

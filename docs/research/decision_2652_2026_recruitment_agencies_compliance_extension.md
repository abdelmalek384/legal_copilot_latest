## Operative text map

**Preamble:** Prime Minister, after reviewing the Constitution and Labour Law 14/2025, and on the Minister of Labour's submission.

**Article 1:** Extends by six months the period for regularizing the status of private employment/recruitment agencies that:
- take the form of a joint-stock company;
- a partnership limited by shares;
- a limited-liability company; or
- a one-person company;

and are licensed to carry out the placement of Egyptians in work inside or outside Egypt under the final paragraph of Article 6 of Labour Law 14/2025.

**Article 2:** Orders publication in the Official Gazette.

The decision is dated 7 September 2026. It contains no separate article expressly specifying a different commencement date or the exact final calendar date of the extended period. Do not calculate a hard deadline for a particular agency without checking the original statutory deadline, its individual licence/transition facts and counsel's interpretation of the extension.

## How this interacts with the existing recruitment-decision corpus

- Decision 272/2025 regulates domestic/overseas recruitment operations, including agency and electronic-platform licensing and compliance controls.
- Decision 2652/2026 is a time-limited transitional extension; it does not itself replace the substantive licensing requirements in Law 14/2025 or Decision 272/2025.
- The second Ministry Q&A booklet (see the Ministry guidance note) discusses licensing, capital, security deposit, application/renewal and electronic-platform obligations. Those explanations are guidance; the statute and signed implementing decisions remain controlling.
- The extension is for already licensed private recruitment agencies of the specified company forms. It should not be treated as a blanket authorization for unlicensed intermediaries, individual brokers or social-media pages.

## Product/data rules

1. Model this as a transitional deadline extension, linked to Labour Law 14/2025 Article 6 and Decision 272/2025, not as a permanent licence rule.
2. Retain the covered legal forms and prior-licence condition explicitly.
3. Do not infer that the extension suspends licence requirements or authorizes collecting placement fees from workers.
4. Do not set a universal expiry date for all agencies without the original transitional start date, individual licence data and legal review.
5. Keep the original decision text, Gazette issue and effective-date evidence attached to any deadline calculation.

## 6. Legal effect and safe handling

Under Article49 of the Supreme Constitutional Court Law48/1979, the general rule is that a provision declared unconstitutional is not applied from the day following publication of the judgment unless the Court specifies another date; tax provisions have special temporal treatment. This is a provisional default only: the operative part of each full judgment controls.

For each case, the product must store:
- Exact invalidated text and paragraph.
- Full case number, judicial year, session date and publication date.
- Gazette issue, date and page.
- Effective date specified by the Court.
- Any provisions that fall by inseparability, only where the Court expressly determines that result.
- Historical cases/transactions to which the ruling applies.
- The remaining valid parts of the same article.

Do not replace a whole article with “unconstitutional” when the public notice identifies only one paragraph.

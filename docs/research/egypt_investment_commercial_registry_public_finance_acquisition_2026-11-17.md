## 1. Investment Guarantees and Incentives Law8/1997 — historical only

Official ETA-hosted PDF: https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-10/law-8-1997.pdf

The 312-line text shows:
- Law8/1997 was promulgated 11 May 1997.
- Article3 gave GAFI implementation responsibility for Law8/1997, Companies Law159/1981, Finance Leasing Law95/1995 and Mortgage Finance Law148/2001.
- Article4 repealed Investment Law230/1989 and specified provisions of other laws.
- Article5 provided for a Prime Minister executive regulation.

**Currentness:** Investment Law72/2017 Article8 expressly repeals Law8/1997. Keep Law8 only for historical projects, transitional rights and grandfathered incentives; do not cite it as current general investment law.

## 2. Investment Law72/2017

Official ETA-hosted PDF: https://portal.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-10/law-72-2017.pdf

The 924-line text identifies Official Gazette issue21 bis (C), 31 May 2017. Article1 applies the law to domestic and foreign investment and recognizes four investment regimes:
1. Internal investment.
2. Investment zones.
3. Technology zones.
4. Free zones.

Other important promulgation provisions:
- Article3 substitutes the expression “Investment Law” for “Investment Guarantees and Incentives Law” in other laws/decisions.
- Articles5–7 contain transitional and dispute-resolution provisions.
- Article8 expressly repeals Law8/1997 and conflicting provisions.
- Article9 requires a Prime Minister executive regulation within 90 days.
- Article10 states commencement on the day following publication: 1 June 2017.

This is the base law text, not a consolidated 2026 version. It must be read with the Executive Regulation and later amendments, sector-specific licensing rules and GAFI decisions.

## 6. Commercial Registry Law34/1976

Official ETA-hosted PDF: https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-10/law-34-1976.pdf

The six-page PDF has 173 extracted lines. It covers trader/company/branch registration, amendments to registration, court notices concerning bankruptcy and company liquidation, renewal and deletion of registrations. Article4 records an amendment by Law98/1996. It provides a five-year registration renewal cycle, subject to the statutory grace/late-fee rules.

This is a useful cross-link between company formation, insolvency judgments and registry practice. Electronic registry rules and all subsequent amendments still need to be integrated.

## 7. Law148/2026 — State Financial Resources Development Fee Law147/1984

Official ETA-hosted Gazette PDF:
https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.148.of_.2026.pdf

- Official Gazette issue30 bis (A), 28 July 2026; one page.
- Article1 replaces two items in Article1 of Law147/1984:
  - Item5: EGP100 on departure from Egypt, with stated exemptions for Egyptian/foreign public passenger and freight truck drivers and workers on regular cross-border lines.
  - Item19: EGP35 per ton of cement produced, payable by factories to the Tax Authority.

## 8. Law154/2026 — transfer of state/public-entity company profits to Treasury

Official ETA-hosted Gazette PDF:
https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.154.of_.2026.pdf

- Official Gazette issue30 bis (A), 28 July 2026; two pages.
- Article1 requires boards of companies whose capital is wholly owned by the State or public legal persons to set aside 5% of distributable net profits after covering carried losses and before any reserve allocation.
- For companies in which the State/public legal persons hold more than 50% but not all capital, the amount is 4% of distributable net profits, capped at the State/public-entity share of those profits.
- The sums are classified as tax revenues and transferred to the Treasury within four months of the financial-year close.
- The Cabinet may temporarily exempt companies for social/economic reasons by decision on the Minister of Finance's proposal.
- Article2 provides commencement on the day after Gazette publication: 29 July 2026.

This is a new statutory public-finance obligation. Do not calculate it for a company without checking ownership percentages, accounting period, carried losses, reserves, treaty/establishment exceptions, Cabinet exemption decisions and later implementation guidance.

## 9. Labour Law12/2003 — historical predecessor

ETA's public legal index links a copy of Law12/2003. Labour Law14/2025 Article12 expressly repeals Law12/2003. Retain it for historical employment periods, claims and transitional provisions only. Do not answer post-commencement labour questions using the 2003 law as the current law.

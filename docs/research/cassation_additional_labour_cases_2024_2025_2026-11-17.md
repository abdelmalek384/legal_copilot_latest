# Additional Court of Cassation Labour Case Leads — 2024–2025

Research date: 17 November 2026. These are case-law leads for controlled review, not approved legal principles for production. Most judgments below applied predecessor legislation (Law 12/2003 or Social Insurance Law 79/1975), not Labour Law 14/2025.

## 1. Appeal 12226/92 — General Assembly, 30 May 2023

- Official Court of Cassation technical-office PDF: https://register.cc.gov.eg/storage/publications/pdfs/Fg9XimjgIHKMIK32A8ydLiMka67w0VykI1LL5Ocu.pdf
- Secondary transcription: https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2023/06/12226-92-30-5-2023.html
- **Statutory regime:** Labour Law 12/2003, Articles 56, 69, 110 and 129.
- **Principle reported:** Article 56 worker duties include preserving work dignity and behaving appropriately. Article 129 permits termination following a final conviction for a felony or custodial sentence in an offence affecting honour, trust or public morals. The General Assembly distinguishes that case from specified allegations which, in themselves, may be considered incompatible with the worker's honour/reputation and work dignity; in those specific circumstances the employer may choose unilateral termination or wait for final conviction, subject to judicial review.
- **Critical limitation:** this is not a general right to dismiss on accusation alone. The holding concerns the specifically described conduct and must be read with the full reasoning, the exact criminal allegations and the judicial-review safeguard. It interprets repealed Law 12/2003 and must not be transplanted to Law 14/2025 without counsel analysis.
- **Date discrepancy:** a secondary blog heading says 30 May 2022, but the official technical-office PDF identifies the hearing as 30 May 2023. Use the official PDF date.

## 2. Appeal 12946/92 — 9 December 2024

- Official Court of Cassation category page: https://cc.gov.eg/category/12
- The official headnote distinguishes wage from a grant/bonus: wage is what is paid to the worker in return for work under the employment contract; a grant is paid for efficiency/effort or on specified occasions generally and continuously/fixedly. Whether a grant forms part of wage depends on the conditions in the headnote and the actual payment arrangement.
- **Status:** official Court portal headnote only in this pass; full judgment, circuit and underlying statutory regime need retrieval.

## 3. Appeal 6130/87 — 11 December 2024

- Official Court of Cassation workers section: https://cc.gov.eg/?p=1998
- Secondary text: https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/09/6130-87-17-12-2024-75-129-844.html

## 5. Appeal 16664/93 — 21 April 2024

- EastLaws unpublished-labour-judgment record: https://www.eastlaws.com/judgments-full-text/ar/egypt/cassation-civil/judicial-year-93/21-04-2024/no-16664?id=1509678&type=1
- **Statutory regime:** Labour Law 12/2003, Article 69(4).
- **Principle in the indexed headnote:** dismissal for absence exceeding 20 intermittent days or 10 consecutive days in the year requires that the absence be without a legitimate reason.
- **Status:** EastLaws search-indexed headnote; its page did not expose full judgment text in this retrieval pass. Do not treat as a current statutory threshold under Law 14/2025.

## 8. Appeal 15513/94 — 2 November 2025

- EastLaws unpublished-labour-judgment record: https://www.eastlaws.com/judgments-full-text/ar/egypt/cassation-civil/judicial-year-94/02-11-2025/no-15513?id=3902911&type=1
- **Statutory regime:** Labour Law 12/2003, Articles 56 and 69.
- **Principle in the indexed headnote:** an assault on an employer/manager can constitute serious misconduct under the specific facts; the judgment also addresses electronic email evidence. It states that electronic correspondence is not inadmissible merely because it is electronic; a party contesting authenticity must pursue the forgery route rather than merely deny the printout. The employer must state the reasons for termination; the worker bears the burden of disproving those reasons in the circumstances described.
- **Status:** search-indexed headnote only; full judgment not visible in this pass. Do not generalize the holding to every critical email or complaint; facts and exact wording matter.

## 9. Appeal 11438/86 — 11 December 2024

- Secondary full transcription: https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/10/11438-86-11-12-2024-75-126-824.html
- **Statutory regime:** Social Insurance Law 79/1975, Articles 52 and 56.
- **Principle:** where the combined permanent partial disability from old and new work injuries reaches at least 35%, the worker may qualify for an injury pension even where earlier injury compensation was paid as a lump sum; calculation uses the monthly wage in the year before the latest injury, under the statutory formula.
- **Status:** secondary full text; official Court publication locator/full official judgment still required. Do not apply the old Law 79/1975 formula without checking Law 148/2019 and current regulations.

## Handling rules

1. The official Court portal and technical-office PDF outrank secondary headnotes. EastLaws records are labelled unpublished and may be access-controlled; do not bypass any access restrictions.
2. Keep all predecessor-law cases tagged historical. Labour Law 12/2003 was repealed when Law 14/2025 took effect; check transitional provisions and the new text before using any earlier principle.
3. Social-insurance cases under Law 79/1975 require a separate currentness review against Law 148/2019.
4. The case register below records source quality and rights status separately. No case has been lawyer-reviewed or cleared for commercial reuse.

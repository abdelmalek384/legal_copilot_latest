# Egyptian Constitution and election law — first primary-source acquisition tranche

**Snapshot:** 17 November 2026  
**Source hub:** National Elections Authority digital legal library — https://www.elections.eg/digital-library-laws

## 1. Constitution of Egypt — 2014 text amended in 2019

- Official 63-page Arabic PDF: https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/Constitution_2014.pdf
- Official Presidency constitution page: https://www.presidency.eg/ar/مصر/الدستور/
- The NEA-hosted PDF identifies the text as the Constitution of Egypt, April 2019, amended following the April 2019 referendum. It also contains the 2014 promulgation material and Gazette references: Official Gazette issue 14 (supplement), 19 January 2014, and Official Gazette issue 16 bis (F), 23 April 2019 for the referendum result.
- The Presidency portal exposes the constitutional structure: State; social/economic/cultural foundations; rights/freedoms/duties; rule of law; legislative/executive/judicial powers; State Council; Supreme Constitutional Court; judicial bodies; legal profession; armed forces/police; National Elections Authority; independent bodies; general/transitional provisions; Senate.

**Acquisition status:** primary government-hosted PDF opened; article-by-article extraction and 2019 amendment redline not yet completed. The Presidency page is a parallel official text presentation. Do not assume this PDF includes later constitutional amendments beyond 2019 without a Gazette search.

## 2. National Elections Authority Law 198/2017

- Official 15-page Arabic PDF: https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/NEAlaw-2017-198.pdf.pdf
- The PDF identifies Gazette issue 30 bis (B), 1 August 2017.
- It contains 37 articles and covers the Authority's definitions, establishment and powers, organs, board composition and term, board competencies and meetings, complaints, final election/referendum results, challenges, executive apparatus, polling/counting, staff duties/conflict rules, finances and transitional provisions.
- The PDF's footnotes identify amendment to Article 12 by Law140/2020.

## 3. Political Rights Law 45/2014

- Official 23-page Arabic PDF: https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/PoliRights-2014-45.pdf.pdf
- Gazette issue 23 (supplement), 5 June 2014.
- The NEA-hosted PDF preserves amendment footnotes:
  - Law92/2015.
  - Law198/2017.
  - Law140/2020.
- The PDF notes that Chapter II was deleted in full by Law198/2017.
- It covers suffrage, voter registration, campaign controls, media coverage, referendum/election procedures, voting and counting, objections and election offences.

This is a consolidated/reprint source copy; each amendment must be compared with the relevant Gazette law and effective date before the text is treated as fully current.

## 4. House of Representatives Law 46/2014

- Official 18-page Arabic PDF: https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/prlaman-46-2014.pdf
- Gazette issue 23 (supplement), 5 June 2014.
- The PDF includes amendment notes for Law140/2020 and Law85/2025. The latter replaces the first paragraph of Article4 and Article5 and changes candidate-list composition. The current official NEA copy therefore reflects an amendment chain beyond the original 2014 text.
- Law85/2025 is also reported as amending Law174/2020 district schedules. The standalone signed Gazette PDF for Law85/2025 has not yet been acquired in this tranche; secondary copies are discovery-only.

## 5. Senate Law 141/2020

- Official 21-page Arabic PDF: https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/Senate-2020-141.pdf.pdf
- Linked directly by the NEA's public legal library.
- A secondary source reports Law84/2025 as amending Law141/2020; the standalone primary Gazette text has not yet been acquired. Do not treat the 2020 PDF as a consolidated current text until Law84/2025 and later amendments are checked.

## 6. House election districts Law 174/2020

- Official 21-page Arabic PDF: https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/senate_2025/174-2020.pdf
- Linked by the NEA public library. The schedules are integral legal data and must be preserved as tables with district, governorate, seat count, constituency and version.
- Law85/2025 is reported to replace the schedules. Until its signed Gazette copy and full replacement schedules are obtained, the 2020 schedules must not be treated as current.

## 7. Law140/2020 — amendments to Political Rights Law and related electoral provisions

- Official 9-page Arabic PDF: https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/PoliRightsAmendments2020-140.pdf
- Gazette issue 26 bis (B), 1 July 2020.
- The PDF is an amendment instrument covering Law45/2014 and related provisions in Law46/2014 and Law198/2017. It should be parsed as a delta against the base laws, not merely indexed as an isolated document.

## 8. Law85/2025 — House and district schedules amendment

- NEA consolidated Law46/2014 PDF contains amendment footnotes for Law85/2025.
- Secondary full text located: https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=13693
- Secondary record identifies Law85/2025, signed 4 June 2025, Official Gazette issue 23 supplement, effective 5 June 2025. It replaces House Law46/2014 Articles4(1), 5 and 10 and the attached schedules to Law174/2020.
- The official standalone Law85/2025 Gazette PDF and full replacement schedules remain outstanding. Use the NEA consolidated House Law only as official confirmation that an amendment is reflected, not as a substitute for the amendment's primary instrument.

## 9. Legal-data extraction requirements

- Constitution: create one record per article, chapter/part, amendment and constitutional judgment citation.
- Political rights / NEA / House / Senate: create one record per article plus each amendment instrument.
- District schedules: structured tables, not OCR-only paragraphs; preserve exact seat totals, constituency boundaries, Arabic labels and source page.
- Store historical versions and date-validity intervals; an election district table must never be overwritten without keeping the previous version.
- Capture election decisions issued by NEA for each election cycle separately from statutes.
- Link every constitutional/election precedent to the exact challenged statutory provision and judgment identifier.

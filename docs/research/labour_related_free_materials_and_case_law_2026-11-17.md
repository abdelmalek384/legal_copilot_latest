## 2. Ministry of Labour legislation index — legacy and related instruments

Official index: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/القوانين-والتشريعات/

The index exposes links for:
- Labour Law 14/2025 (current primary statute).
- Labour Law 12/2003 (repealed by Law 14/2025; historical and transitional use only).
- Law 180/2008 amending Law 12/2003 (historical version chain).
- Trade Union Organizations Law 213/2017 and amendment references.
- Social Insurance and Pensions Law 148/2019 and its executive regulations.
- Mineral Resources Law 198/2014 (adjacent sector-specific legislation).
- Law 125/2010 on priority/privilege of workers' rights (repealed by Law 14/2025; historical and transitional use only).
- Executive regulations under Law 125/2010.
- Ministerial Order 290/2010 relating to implementation instructions for Law 125/2010.
- Older labour decisions, including:
  - 2003/121 — workplace nurseries;
  - 2003/111 — night inspection assignments;
  - 2003/112 — holidays;
  - 2003/113 — preparatory/complementary work;
  - 2003/114 — OSH research/studies;
  - 2003/041 — Egyptian labour recruitment companies abroad.
- Other older instruments concerning collective bargaining, penalties, tourism-service charges, OSH committees and labour inspection.

**Critical transition rule:** Promulgation Art. 11 of Law 14/2025 states that until the new implementing decisions issue, existing decisions continue insofar as they do not conflict with the new law. This means the old decisions are not all simply “repealed” or “still in force” as a block. For each legacy instrument, determine:
1. subject matter and enabling article;
2. whether a new 2025/2026 decision covers the same subject;
3. whether the old text conflicts with Law 14/2025 or a newer decision;
4. whether it was expressly repealed;
5. the exact transition date and any savings clause.

Do not use the old 2003 instruments as current rules without this analysis.

## 3. International Labour Organization datasets and guidance

### NATLEX — legislation
- Egypt labour-code finder: https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/r/natlex/fe/results?clear=2&p2_country_filter=EGY&p2_home_flag=Y&p2_subject_classification_filter=01.02

- Labour Code 12/2003 record EGY-2003-L-64693 shows Law 14/2025 as the repealing text and identifies amendments by Laws 90/2005 and 180/2008.
- The 2025 Law 14 text is available in a NATLEX PDF record: https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/118430/qnwn_lml_ljdyd_14_lsn_2025_m_q.pdf

Use NATLEX for cross-checking version history and identifying international instruments; the Egyptian Gazette remains the controlling source for the domestic Arabic text.

### ILO Child Labour Observatory
- Egypt country profile: https://webapps.ilo.org/clodashboard/country/1A/EGY
- Cross-references Law 14/2025 Article 62, ILO Convention 138 and the previous Ministerial Decree 215/2021 on child employment/training.
- Useful for tracking minimum-age standards and related international obligations. It is an analytical dataset, not a substitute for the Arabic statute or its implementing decision (including Decision 50/2026).

### ILO Employment Protection Legislation Database (EPLex)
- Egypt country detail: https://eplex.ilo.org/en/country-detail/?code=EGY&yr=2025
- Contains structured indicators and commentary for Law 14/2025, including fixed-term contracts, probation, exclusions from coverage, dismissal protections, and statutory article references.
- Treat as a comparative analytical aid. Every proposition should be checked against the current Arabic text and implementing decisions before use.

### ILO Better Work / inspection guidance
- ILO article on Egyptian labour inspectors and OSH: https://www.ilo.org/resource/article/how-egypt%E2%80%99s-labour-inspectors-protect-factories-ground

## 5. New source acquisition checklist

1. Retrieve both Ministry FAQ parts and the Labour Inspection Guide from their official download links.
2. Retrieve the Ministry's 2025/2026 OSH newsletters and classify each as binding rule, technical guidance, or general awareness.
3. Build a legacy-decision status matrix for 2003 decisions 111, 112, 113, 114, 121 and 41, and other relevant legacy decisions.
4. Obtain the full Court of Cassation judgments for the appeal identifiers above, preserving statute-version tags and full citation details.
5. Add ILO Convention records and ratification/status data relevant to minimum age, forced labour, equal remuneration, discrimination, OSH, working time, weekly rest, and collective bargaining.
6. Keep the ILO/EPLex and Ministry Q&A material as secondary/official explanatory evidence, never as a replacement for Gazette text.

## A. Finance Minister Decision 69/2026 — Article14(a) declaration extension

**Publication metadata:** Egyptian Proceedings issue36 supplement, 14 February 2026.  
**Subject:** extends the filing period under Article14(a) of Law196/2008 to 31 March 2026.  
**Sources:**
- Secondary full text: https://www.almasdar.com/167714
- EastLaws record: https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/finance-minister/14-02-2026/no-69?id=5469154&type=1

### Preamble as reproduced by Al-Masdar

وزير المالية  
بعد الاطلاع على قانون الضريبة على العقارات المبنية الصادر بالقانون رقم 196 لسنة 2008 ولائحته التنفيذية؛ وعلى مذكرة رئيس مصلحة الضرائب العقارية رقم (10) بتاريخ 8-1-2026؛ وبناءً على ما تقتضيه المصلحة العامة؛

### Verbatim operative Arabic text

**قرار وزير المالية رقم 69 لسنة 2026**

**المادة الأولى**

تمد فترة تقديم الإقرارات الضريبية المنصوص عليها في البند "أ" من المادة "14" من قانون الضريبة على العقارات المبنية المشار إليه حتى 31-3-2026.

**المادة الثانية**

ينشر هذا القرار في الوقائع المصرية.

### Source and legal-status note

Full operative text and reported preamble are from a secondary reproduction, not an acquired Amiri Press PDF. The decision is historical as to deadline: later Decisions188/2026, 342/2026 and 550/2026 successively moved the Article14(a) deadline to 30 June, 30 September and 31 December 2026. Article14(b)'s annual-census filing rule is distinct and is not amended by these extensions.

### Administrative follow-through

Customs Tariff Circular44/2026 dated 2 August 2026 disseminates the cancellation to customs offices and identifies the cancelled instruments as Decisions190,203 and258/2026. A later Customs Circular46/2026 addresses treatment of fertiliser consignments entered into customs areas under declarations opened before the repeal but not exported by the effective date, and consignments exported after the repeal. Circulars are implementation instructions; they do not replace or amend the ministerial decision.

## 4. Article 11 and currentness workflow

Promulgation Article 11 of Law 14/2025 requires instrument-by-instrument transition analysis. For each historical decision:

1. Capture the complete official text and its Gazette issue/page, date and source hash.
2. Record the enabling provision under the predecessor law and the corresponding provision, if any, in Law 14/2025.
3. Search for express repeal, amendment or replacement.
4. Compare scope and operative clauses with newer decisions (notably 248–252, 260, 264, 266, 267, 272, 279, 49, 75 and 162).
5. Classify each clause separately where a newer instrument replaces only part of the old regime.
6. Keep status as unknown until the primary instruments and commencement provisions are checked. Do not infer repeal merely because a newer decision addresses a similar subject.

## 5. Acquisition and rights status

- The Ministry page is a free public discovery index. That does not establish a licence for bulk collection, commercial reuse, redistribution or embedding.
- A Ministry-hosted PDF is stronger provenance than a third-party transcription, but it still needs integrity checks, Gazette comparison where available, currentness review and counsel approval.
- No text from the new discovery rows above is promoted to the production corpus by this note.
- verified=0, lawyer_reviewed=false and rights_cleared=false remain unchanged unless the corresponding approvals are actually obtained.
- The Ministry index itself can change; retain an access-date snapshot and revisit it during future collection.

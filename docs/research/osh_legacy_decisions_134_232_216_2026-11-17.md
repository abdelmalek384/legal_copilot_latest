# OSH predecessor decisions 134/2003, 232/2010 and 216/2016 — text findings

Research date: 17 November 2026  
Purpose: preserve the source-level relationship between the historic OSH decisions and avoid treating titles as proof of their operative contents.

## 1. Decision 216/2016 — three-article transcription located

**Instrument:** Ministry of Manpower Decision 216/2016 concerning establishments required to create OSH functional units and committees, the bodies responsible for training, and the applicable rules.  
**Issuer:** Minister of Manpower.  
**Signed:** 11 August 2016.  
**Gazette:** Egyptian Gazette, issue 205, year 189, dated 7 September 2016.  
**Commencement stated in Article 3:** the day after publication.  
**Official Ministry PDF locator:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/o00hsjnh/2016-216-تحديد-المنشآت-التي-تلتزم-بإنشاء-أجهزة-ولجان-السلامة-والصحة-المهنية.pdf  
**Accessible transcription:** https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?ItemID=104248&NID=129757&Type=6&action=ViewActivePages  
**Source class:** public third-party transcription reproducing the Gazette heading and all three operative articles; official Ministry PDF URL identified separately but direct download timed out in this pass.

### Article-level extract

- **Preamble:** cites Social Insurance Law 79/1975, Article 227 of Labour Law 12/2003, ratified international/Arab conventions, Presidential Decision 333/2003, maritime trade Decision 8/1990, and Ministerial Decision 134/2003.
- **Article 1:** replaces the first paragraph of Article 18 of Decision 134/2003. The new paragraph names the Occupational Safety and Health Institute and its branches under the Workers' Cultural Institution, and the National Centre for Occupational Safety and Health Studies and its branches, as training providers. It covers basic training for specialists, technicians and OSH committee members, as well as advanced, specific and specialized OSH/work-environment training.
- **Article 2:** repeals any provision conflicting with Decision 216/2016.
- **Article 3:** publication in the Egyptian Gazette and commencement on the day after publication.

### Important scope correction

Although the instrument's title refers broadly to establishments required to form OSH functional units/committees and training rules, the accessible three-article text found in this pass specifically changes the **training-provider paragraph in Article 18 of Decision 134/2003**. Do not attribute a full establishment-threshold or staffing schedule to Decision 216/2016 without locating a separate annex or the full base Decision 134/2003.

## 3. Relationship and timeline

The sources support the following limited sequence:

1. **Decision 134/2003** is the base instrument identified in the preambles of both 232/2010 and 216/2016.
2. **Decision 232/2010** amended Articles 18 and 19 of Decision 134/2003.
3. **Decision 216/2016** later replaced the first paragraph of Article 18 of Decision 134/2003, including the named training institutions and categories of OSH training.
4. **Decision 249/2026** is a later Law 14/2025-era instrument in the same OSH functional-units/committees field. The exact repeal/savings relationship between 134/2003 as amended and 249/2026 still requires article-by-article comparison against the Gazette originals.

Do not describe Decision 216/2016 as simply “repealed by 249/2026” without checking express repeal language, commencement and the continued-operation rule in Enactment Article 11 of Law 14/2025. A newer instrument may replace only part of an older regime.

## 4. Technical standards lead — Decision 211/2003

A Ministry-hosted climate presentation cites Article 208 of Law 12/2003 and Articles 5–6 of Ministerial Decision 211/2003 for workplace thermal conditions, periodic thermal-stress assessment and measurement. The presentation is guidance/context, not the signed decision.

- Ministry presentation: https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/yggf04x5/نشرة-المناخ-مسئوليتى.pdf
- A public professional association PDF and a 2025 labour-law commentary also cite Decision 211/2003 and summarize its Article 3 thermal-risk requirements, including heat assessment and periodic measurements. These are secondary references and must not be used to populate technical limit tables without the actual decision: https://alolabor.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/ALC_48_2022_B05.pdf and https://alolabor.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/ALC_51_2025_B05.pdf

**Correction to avoid a false match:** the Ministry legislation index separately lists **Decision 211/2009** as the financial/administrative regulation for irregular-worker units in mines/quarries. It is not Decision 211/2003 and concerns a different subject.

# Labour Law 14/2025 Executive-Decisions Completeness Gap

Research date: 17 November 2026  
Purpose: prevent the identified-source harvest from being mistaken for a complete inventory of implementing instruments.

## 1. Officially announced decision universe

The Minister of Labour publicly described a package of approximately **87 executive decisions** for Labour Law 14/2025, with **68 to be issued by the Ministry of Labour** and the remainder by the Prime Minister and the Ministries of Justice and Health.

Primary/official Ministry announcements:
- 29 July 2025: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-يشيد-بقراري-وزير-العدل-بشأن-إنشاء-38-محكمة-عمالية-وإطلاق-مكاتب-مساعدة-قانونية-مجانية-للعمال/
- 4 August 2025: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-يوجه-بسرعة-الانتهاء-من-إصدار-87-قرارا-وزاريا-تنفيذيا-للقانون-الجديد/
- 24 August 2025 Ministry report: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-يرأس-الأعلى-للتشاور-الاجتماعي-لمناقشة-القرارات-التنفيذية-للقانون-الجديد/

The 29 July and 24 August Ministry reports describe 87 decisions, including 68 from the Ministry of Labour. Other contemporary reports quote a different Ministry count of 62 (e.g. 7 August interview and 6 September Ahram Business). The difference may reflect a changing count, different definitions or reporting inconsistency; it is unresolved. Preserve both figures and do not silently normalize them.

On 14 November 2026, the Ministry said that it had completed issuing the executive decisions after presentation to the Supreme Council for Social Dialogue:
- Official Ministry report: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-لمديرة-إدارة-الحوكمة-والثلاثية-بمنظمة-العمل-الدولية-مصر-تمضي-بخطى-ثابتة-نحو-بناء-منظومة-عمل-عصرية-ترتكز-على-التشريعات-المتطورة-والحوار-الاجتماعي-والحماية-والامتثال-لمعايير-العمل-الدولية/
- The report does not publish an itemized list or identify decision numbers, dates, Gazette issues or issuers.

## 3. Why the count is not yet reconcilable

1. **Count conflict:** Ministry reports cite 68 Ministry decisions; other contemporaneous reports quote 62. The November 2026 completion statement does not resolve the count.
2. **Instrument type:** some decisions may be general regulations; others may be administrative/organizational decisions, schedules, forms, licensing procedures or cross-ministry instruments.
3. **Publication lag and indexing:** some instruments are in Al-Waqa'i al-Misriyya or the Official Gazette but are not separately indexed in DSpace; some DSpace items are restricted.
4. **Issuer separation:** Ministry of Labour, Prime Minister, Ministry of Justice and Ministry of Health must be inventoried separately.
5. **Later measures:** decisions issued in 2026, including remote-work monthly extensions, wage-council arrangements and recruitment-agency deadline extensions, may be related to Labour Law 14/2025 but not part of the original pre-commencement 87-decision estimate.
6. **Circulars and guidance:** circulars, ministerial instructions, forms, FAQs and manuals should not be counted as formal decisions unless the source identifies them as such.

## 5. Priority acquisition plan to reconcile the 87-decision announcement

### Priority A — obtain the Ministry's actual 68-decision list

1. Request or locate the Ministry's internal/public index of all implementing decisions prepared for the Supreme Council for Social Dialogue.
2. Search the Ministry publications page, Gazette issues, DSpace MELES 2025/2026 collections, Amiri Press searchable text and Ministry press releases by decision number and topic.
3. Record every candidate with issuer, number, signed date, Gazette issue/date, article count, direct source and source tier.
4. Deduplicate decisions repeated in Gazette supplements, circulars and later amendments.

### Priority B — cross-check each statutory delegation

Build a provision-by-provision matrix from the full Law 14/2025 (including the 13 promulgation articles and 298 attached-law articles). For every phrase such as “يصدر الوزير قرارًا”, “يصدر قرار من رئيس مجلس الوزراء”, “بالتنسيق مع وزارة الصحة”, or “وفقًا للضوابط التي يصدر بها قرار”, record:
- delegating article;
- issuing authority;
- subject matter;
- expected instrument type;
- identified instrument number(s);
- status: located / partial / not located / possibly no separate instrument;
- Gazette evidence;
- lawyer review.

Do not treat one decision as satisfying multiple delegations merely because its subject is adjacent.

### Priority C — missing issuer categories

- **Ministry of Justice:** compare the 3 located decisions with the full 87 list; check court formation, territorial venue, case procedure, legal aid and inspector/judicial police designation instruments.
- **Ministry of Health:** identify occupational medical examination, fitness, disease, health-record, occupational-disease schedule and joint medical/OSH instruments, where delegated by the Labour Law.
- **Prime Minister:** distinguish statutory executive decisions from temporary operational orders (e.g. remote work) and general wage-council decisions.
- **Ministry of Labour:** target the currently unlocated half of the announced ministerial count, especially detailed schedules, licensing forms, fee decisions, skill/occupation annexes, foreign-worker controls, non-regular labour, new work patterns, OSH, inspections and social/medical protection.

### Priority D — legal quality

For every located instrument:
- obtain authorized Gazette copy or official Amiri Press indexed text and record the source type;
- compare all articles, annexes, tables and commencement/repeal provisions;
- capture SHA-256 for locally obtained files;
- track amendments, corrigenda and later circulars;
- keep lawyer review and rights clearance false until separately approved.

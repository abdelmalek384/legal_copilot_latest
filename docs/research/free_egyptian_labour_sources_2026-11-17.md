## 2. Case-law sources and leads

### Official Court of Cassation portal
- [Judgment and principle search](https://cc.gov.eg/judgments_search/)
- [Official Court of Cassation home / legal knowledge index](https://cc.gov.eg/?p=57)
- The portal explicitly exposes a civil-jurisdiction category for labour appeals and social-insurance disputes.
- The portal's indexed recent principles include a labour matter dated 2026-08-25 concerning employees of Gas Egypt, termination of service, warning of the employee, and alleged error in applying the law. This is a **discovery lead only**; the complete judgment, appeal number, court reasoning, and source locator must be retrieved before ingestion.
- A homepage-indexed principle also concerns an employer's right to terminate in a specified circumstance involving proof of breach and a final criminal conviction. Treat this as historical case-law material until the complete decision, date, appeal number, and its relationship to Law 14/2025 are established.

### Case-law acquisition procedure
1. Search the official portal's labour appeals section by Arabic issue terms (e.g. إنهاء الخدمة، الفصل، الأجر، الإجازات، إصابة العمل، التأمينات الاجتماعية، الاستقالة).
2. Open each result and capture the full judgment/principle, appeal number, judicial year, hearing date, circuit, publication/source URL, and paragraph/page locator.
3. Distinguish a court's extracted principle from the full judgment. Do not label a short headnote as the full reasoning.
4. Record the date of the dispute and the legislation applicable at that time. Pre-2025 decisions may interpret repealed Law 12/2003 and must not be presented as direct interpretations of Law 14/2025.
5. Check terms before systematic collection or bulk indexing.

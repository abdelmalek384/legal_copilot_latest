## 1. Environment Law4/1994 and amendment chain

Official EEAA law library: https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/laws/55/index

The EEAA page identifies:
- Law4/1994 on Environment, amended by Law9/2009.
- Separate Law105/2015 amending Environment Law4/1994.

- Executive Regulation PM338/1995, amended by PM1741/2005.
- Further executive-regulation amendments: PM1095/2011, PM710/2012, PM964/2015, PM544/2016, PM618/2017, PM1963/2017 and PM2466/2024.

The EEAA explicitly says the Arabic original is the official adopted version; the English version is only an aid to understanding. The complete consolidated Arabic law and regulation, including every amendment and annex, has not yet been transcribed.

### 2024 amendment and 2025 professional licensing

EEAA news dated 30 July 2024 confirms Cabinet approval of amendments to PM338/1995, later identified as PM2466/2024. The EEAA service page explains Article13 bis of the Executive Regulation:
- Environmental risk assessments, EIA studies, environmental management systems and environmental compliance plans are among the regulated professional activities.
- Practitioners must be registered/licensed or hold the relevant accreditation.
- Certificates are valid for five years, subject to renewal and eligibility.
- The page states EGP100 committee presentation fee per specialty and EGP1,000 accreditation certificate fee per specialty.
- Decision342/2025 sets registration/accreditation committee rules and procedures.
- The page states an 80% minimum score for the personal/interview assessment.

These are regulator website summaries. The signed text of PM2466/2024 and Ministerial Decision342/2025 must be acquired and checked before legal advice or operational eligibility decisions.

## 2. Natural Reserves Law102/1983 — full official Arabic PDF

Official EEAA PDF: https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/Uploads/Laws/Files/20221017140256340.pdf

The full six-page Arabic PDF was opened and its text layer inspected.

- Article1 defines a natural reserve as a land area or coastal/inland water area with living organisms (plants, animals, fish) or natural features of cultural, scientific, tourist or aesthetic value, designated by Prime Minister decision on EEAA proposal.
- Article2 prohibits acts or activities that destroy, damage or degrade the natural environment, harm wildlife/marine/plant life or diminish its aesthetic value.
- The article specifically prohibits hunting/capturing/transporting/killing wildlife, taking natural materials such as shells/coral/rocks/soil, damaging plants or geological formations, introducing alien species, and polluting reserve soil/water/air.
- Buildings, installations, roads, vehicles and agricultural/industrial/commercial activity in reserves require permission under the rules set by Prime Minister decision.

EEAA's decision index lists related instruments including PM264/1994, PM2728/2015, Ministerial Decisions204/2019 and202/2019, and EEAA Executive Decision33/2018. These must be connected to the statute and the specific reserve.

## 3. Waste Management Law202/2020 and Executive Regulation PM722/2022

EEAA law index: https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/Laws/56/index  
Official Government Printing Office text for PM722/2022: https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=4dvIKCtGaRc%3D

The GPO page identifies PM722/2022 as issuing the Executive Regulation of Law202/2020. Its opening provisions:
- Article1 applies the attached Executive Regulation.
- Article2 repeals conflicting provisions.
- Article3 provides publication in the Official Gazette and commencement the day after publication.

The regulation's definitions distinguish hazardous waste, hazardous waste subject to conditions, medical waste, waste generators, licences and permits. It includes technical and operational rules for collection, segregation, packaging, storage, transport, treatment, recovery and final disposal.

Official EEAA training material identifies Annexes6–11 and18 as containing detailed healthcare-waste management standards, records and tracking forms. Annex11 covers segregation/collection/transport/storage equipment and standards for hazardous and healthcare waste.

The law contains penalties in Articles65–78. EEAA's 2026 public guidance refers to Article71 as requiring shops/premises to provide dedicated waste containers. The Executive Regulation also contains rules on single-use plastic bags; the 2026 EEAA page refers to Articles18 and19.

## 4. Extended producer responsibility — PM662/2025

EEAA official 2026 update: https://www.eeaa.gov.eg/News/22901/Details

The EEAA page dated 23 February 2026 states that PM662/2025 applies extended producer responsibility to single-use plastic shopping bags. It also refers to Article27 of Law202/2020 and Articles18 and19 of the Executive Regulation. The page notes coordination with the Ministry of Industry and a 50-micron minimum thickness standard for single-use plastic bags, linked to a separate Egyptian Standard Decision559.

EEAA's 2 January 2026 update also describes implementation of PM662/2025 and the need for product-responsibility systems and compliance monitoring.

## 6. Critical identity discrepancy — Law152/2021

One EEAA search-result extract for Executive Decision518/2023 appears to refer to Law152/2021 as a law regulating medical-waste management.

However, the Official Gazette DSpace record identifies Law152/2021 as **the Law on Measures to Confront Epidemics and Health Pandemics**, published in Official Gazette issue47 bis on 29 November 2021, with nine articles:
https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/ea4c9140-f39b-40f4-be26-502aabdf2896

The DSpace title/abstract is a primary Gazette catalogue record. A secondary full-text source also gives the same title. Therefore, do **not** classify Law152/2021 as a medical-waste law on the basis of the EEAA search-result phrase. The discrepancy may be a citation/title error or refer to another instrument. The signed Gazette PDF and the original EEAA Decision518/2023 must be compared to resolve it.

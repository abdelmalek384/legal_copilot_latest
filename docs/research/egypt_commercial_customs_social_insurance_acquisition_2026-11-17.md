# Commercial Code, Customs and Social Insurance — primary-source acquisition tranche

**Snapshot:** 17 November 2026

## 1. Commercial Code — Law17/1999

**Government-published Arabic PDF:** https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-10/law-17-1999.pdf

The PDF text layer contains 3,759 lines. The cover identifies a second edition prepared/reviewed by two lawyers at the General Authority for Government Printing; it is hosted on the official Egyptian Tax Authority domain. It is a government-published reprint, not a certified Gazette scan.

The PDF's own introduction and promulgation provisions establish:
- Law17/1999 was published in Official Gazette issue19 (bis), 17 May 1999.
- Article1 repeals the Commercial Code issued by High Order on 13 November 1883, except Chapter1 of Book2 concerning partnerships, and substitutes the new attached Code.
- Article1 also repeals Penal Code Article337 from the stated future date and repeals conflicting provisions.
- Article3 provides commencement from 1 October 1999, with cheque provisions subject to a delayed commencement.
- The reprint's footnote records that Law168/2000 changed the cheque-related dates.
- The contents cover general trade, trader status, commercial books, commercial registry, business premises, stock exchange, commercial obligations/contracts, technology transfer, sale, commercial pledge, public warehouses, commercial agency, brokerage, transport, banking operations, bills of exchange, promissory notes, cheques, bankruptcy and preventive composition.

The code includes detailed substantive provisions. For example, Articles1–3 address the scope of commercial law, sources/order of application (contract, statutory commercial rules, commercial custom, then Civil Code), and mixed contracts. Articles4–5 enumerate commercial acts and professional activities.

**Critical transition:** Law11/2018 on restructuring, preventive composition and bankruptcy is a later special insolvency statute. The retained 1883 partnership chapter and old bankruptcy portions in the 1999 reprint must not be treated as a single untouched current code. Map the later statute and all amendments before answering insolvency questions.

## 2. Customs Law — Law207/2020

**Official Egyptian Customs Authority compilation:** https://customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/ECAAdminace159396001.pdf

The 70-page PDF text layer was opened. It reproduces the Official Gazette issue45 bis (D), 11 November 2020 and includes the promulgating provisions and Law207/2020 text, with comments/annotations by a named preparer. The cover says “all comments” and “October 2024 version”, so keep this separate from the bare Gazette text.

Key promulgation points in the reproduced Gazette text:
- Article1 introduces the attached Customs Law, subject to international agreements, other statutory exemptions, the special economic zones statute and Investment Law72/2017.
- Article4 calls for an executive regulation.
- Article5 expressly repeals Customs Law66/1963 and Customs Exemptions Law186/1986, and conflicting provisions.
- Article6 provides commencement on the day after publication.
- Gazette issue/date are printed as issue45 bis (D), 11 November 2020.

The law covers customs definitions, Customs Authority powers and staff, customs control, clearance, valuation, transit, temporary admission/refund, warehouses, customs brokers, records, audit, prohibited goods and smuggling.

## 3. Customs Executive Regulation — Finance Decision430/2021

**Official Gazette supplement PDF:** https://www.customs.gov.eg/Upload/ECAAdminace/457fa8a8-216a-4da3-8f23-ac12b71d7d60.pdf

The 4,482-line PDF was opened. It is Egyptian Gazette supplement issue193, 31 August 2021. The instrument:
- Is Decision430/2021 issuing the Executive Regulation of Customs Law207/2020.
- Article1 applies the attached regulation.
- Article2 repeals Finance Decision10/2006 (Customs Executive Regulation) and Finance Decision861/2005 (Customs Exemptions Executive Regulation), subject to the stated exception for decisions identifying customs circles.
- Article3 provides for electronic circulation of Customs Authority forms as determined by the Minister of Finance.
- Article4 provides publication in Egyptian Gazette and commencement on the following day, without prejudice to Finance Decision38/2021.

The 2021 Gazette file itself is a primary source. Its contents include definitions, manifests, cargo inspection, green/yellow/red release routes, transit, valuation, customs declarations, temporary admission and detailed procedural rules.

## 5. Social Insurance — Law148/2019 and 2026 parameters

Official NOSI public library: https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/Pages/NOSIlibrary/NOSIlibrary.aspx

The library search result identifies 168 documents across 34 pages and includes Law148/2019, Civil Service Law81/2016, ministerial decisions and circulars. It is a major source for the social-insurance corpus and should be harvested page-by-page with attachments.

Official NOSI announcement confirming Law148/2019 and commencement:
https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/Pension-Law-1.aspx

The NOSI announcement dated 19 August 2019 states Law148/2019 was published in the Official Gazette and generally commenced 1 January 2020, except Articles111–114 which commenced the day after publication.

### 2026 contribution wage and pension parameters

Official NOSI announcement dated 30 November 2025:
https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2025-11-30.aspx

It announces effective 1 January 2026:
- Minimum insured contribution wage: EGP2,700 (from EGP2,300).
- Maximum insured contribution wage: EGP16,700 (from EGP14,500).
- Minimum new pension: EGP1,755 (from EGP1,495).
- Maximum pension: EGP13,360 (from EGP11,600).

These are annual operational parameters announced by NOSI, not amendments to the text of Law148/2019. Store them as effective-dated values, preserve prior years, and obtain the formal circular/decision if available. Re-check for any later 2026 or 2027 change before use.

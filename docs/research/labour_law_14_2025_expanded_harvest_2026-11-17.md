# Labour Law 14/2025 — expanded source harvest (17 November 2026)

This file extends `free_egyptian_labour_sources_2026-11-17.md`. It is a research index of public metadata and discovery leads. It is **not** a complete or certified list of all instruments, not a rights clearance, and not lawyer-approved legal content.

## A. Gazette issue bundles found

The strongest practical discovery result is that the IDSC DSpace catalogue exposes several *whole Official Gazette supplement bundles* with metadata naming multiple Ministry of Labour decisions. The bundles are much more efficient collection targets than searching each decision independently.

**Caution:** catalogue abstracts can contain transcription errors. In particular, one DSpace record's English abstract appears to mismatch the subjects of Decisions 300 and 301. Do not rely on the abstract for the instrument title; inspect the Gazette PDF itself and each decision's opening page. DSpace records also have item-specific licence files; the general End User Agreement and item licence must be reviewed before reuse.

## D. Court of Cassation — labour case-law collection leads

### Official portal
- Judgment/principle search: https://cc.gov.eg/judgments_search/
- Labour appeals and social-insurance category is explicitly listed under civil principles.
- Search categories include labour appeals, employment disputes and social insurance.
- Portal result dated 2026-08-25: Gas Egypt employees; termination by unilateral will; dismissal; warning of employee; alleged error in applying law. This is a current discovery lead; the full judgment number and reasoning were not captured in this pass.
- Category page: https://cc.gov.eg/category/12 exposes historical labour principles, including:
  - Cassation appeal 5242/94, hearing 2024-12-17: absence from work and entitlement to cash compensation for unused leave (summary appears on official portal).
  - Cassation appeal 10075/89, hearing 2024-12-17: transfer of worker and liability for cash compensation for unused leave attributable to the transferring employer.
  - Cassation appeal 6130/87, hearing 2024-12-17: distinction between professional allowances and grants and when a recurring fixed grant enters wage.
- Official technical-office PDF indexed on the Court of Cassation publication domain includes appeals 17958/89 and 2948/92, hearing 2023-03-22, on procedures for employer request to dismiss worker accused of a felony/misdemeanour under Labour Law 12/2003. This is explicitly **old-law** material.
- Another official technical-office PDF includes appeal 7965/88, hearing 2024-04-24, on labour-court jurisdiction for disputes between Petrojet and its workers under Labour Law 12/2003. Also old-law material.
- The portal's 2026 principles should be checked for full case number, judicial year, date, court, and whether the dispute applies Law 14/2025 or earlier legislation before ingestion.

### Temporal-law warning
Most located Court of Cassation labour principles concern Law 12/2003 or earlier employment regimes. Preserve them as historical precedent with explicit statute-version metadata. They must not be represented as direct judicial interpretation of Law 14/2025 unless the judgment actually interprets the new law.

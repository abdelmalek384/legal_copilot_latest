## 4. Product and legal-evidence handling

For every guide:
1. Store exact title, file URL, source owner, access date, document date/version, author/department and page count.
2. Preserve Arabic wording, units, diagrams, tables and cited article numbers.
3. Label as Ministry technical guidance, not legislation, unless the PDF is itself a Gazette instrument.
4. Trace every legal citation to the cited Law/Decision and verify the relevant version.
5. For numerical values, capture unit, test method, averaging period, duration, population, source standard and revision date.
6. Preserve extraction/OCR errors; do not silently normalize units or repeated table labels.
7. Check copyright, reuse terms and rights clearance before commercial embedding or redistribution.
8. Do not import unreviewed guidance as authoritative legal answer material.

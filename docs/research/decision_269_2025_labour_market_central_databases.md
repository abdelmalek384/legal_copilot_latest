## Four-article map

### Article 1 — purpose

Creates central labour-market databases, supports necessary field studies/research and periodic sectoral/geographic reports on current/future labour-market demand for occupations and skills, and monitors changes.

### Article 2 — mandatory data form and reporting

Adopts the attached data form for creating/updating employment databases and the labour-market information system. Establishments selected by the General Department for Labour-Market Information, Wage and Productivity Studies must complete the form and submit it to that department within **30 days from the request date**.

The Ministry/SIS four-article PDF does not include the attached form; the form is referenced as an annex but has not been acquired in this pass. Do not guess its fields or treat the general employer-information list in the Law itself as an exact transcription of the form.

### Article 3 — procedural manual

The General Department for Labour-Market Information, Wage and Productivity Studies must prepare a procedural manual for labour-market information and occupational classification for use by all field work units. The manual is to be issued within **three months from the decision's effective date**.

## Relationship to Labour Law 14/2025

- The decision implements the labour-market information duties associated with Article 38, which requires covered establishments to provide data/information needed to create or update employment databases and the labour-market information system within 30 days of request.
- It complements Decision 74/2026 on the Central Coordinating Labour Market Observatory and regional observatories. Decision 269 concerns employer-supplied establishment/workforce data and the labour-market information system; Decision 74 creates the central/regional observatory governance and research structure.
- Do not merge the two instruments: they have different issuers, purposes and operative provisions.

## Privacy, data quality and product safeguards

1. The decision requires selected establishments to submit a form within 30 days of request. The actual form is missing; no field-level ingestion should be implemented until the form is obtained.
2. Treat worker-level data (identity, age, nationality, gender, occupation, wages and other potentially identifying fields if present) as personal/confidential data. Use only the fields the official form requires, for the specified labour-market purpose, with lawful access controls, minimization, retention and security.
3. Do not infer that Decision 269 authorizes unrestricted publication of individual worker records. Its stated purpose is aggregated labour-market intelligence, sectoral/geographic reports and workforce planning.
4. Cross-reference Decision 74/2026 Article 7, which requires regional observatories to preserve confidentiality of information sources and share information in accordance with data-protection/confidentiality laws.
5. Preserve provenance and collection date, geography, sector, occupation classification and form version for each dataset.
6. Never use synthetic/demo data as if it were actual official workforce data.

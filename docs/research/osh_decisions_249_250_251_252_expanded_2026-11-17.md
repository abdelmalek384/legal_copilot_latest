## Decision 249/2026 — OSH units and committees

- **Official title:** identifies establishments and branches required to create OSH functional units and committees.
- **Scope lead:** secondary sources report application to establishments with 50 or more workers, with each geographically/administratively separate worksite counted independently if it reaches the threshold. The signed decision must be checked for the exact threshold and risk tiers.
- **Risk classes:** high, medium and limited risk; the decision is reported to require units/committees, safe-driving standards and employer OSH policy/management resources.
- **Employee protection:** the Ministry's official clarification of 27 October 2026 states that existing OSH professionals should not be harmed, excluded, or stripped of acquired legal/professional positions. It recognizes relevant specialist degrees and other graduates with postgraduate OSH qualifications, extended experience and recognized certificates; professional levels include OSH Specialist A and B. The Ministry said circulars would be issued to unify implementation.
- **Official clarification:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/بيان-توضيحي-هام-بشأن-القرار-الوزاري-رقم-249-لسنة-2026-الخاص-بالسلامة-والصحة-المهنية-وزارة-العمل-لن-ي-ضار-أي-عامل-يعمل-بمهنة-أخصائي-السلامة-والصحة-المهنية-ولا-مساس-بالمراكز-القانونية-المكتسبة-وأهمية-المهنة-تستوجب-منتهى-الدقة-في-اختيار-شاغليها/

## Decision 250/2026 — safety standards and hazard controls

- **Official title:** rules/standards and safety limits in workplaces, including precautions to prevent risks.
- **Secondary full text inspected:** https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/10/250-2026.html
- The transcription's contents index shows detailed rules for physical, engineering, electrical, biological, chemical and indirect hazards; confined spaces; work permits; lockout/tagout; delivery motorcycles; violence/harassment/bullying; numerical safety limits and chemical exposure tables; and required equipment/chemical records.
- The chemical exposure tables include hundreds of entries (the index reaches 781 numbered substances/entries). The text also includes detailed numerical tables for noise, vibration, lighting, radiation, magnetic/electric fields, safe approach distances and heat stress.
- This is an unusually large high-risk source: do not ingest numerical exposure limits or chemical thresholds from a secondary transcription without verifying the original Gazette PDF, units, footnotes, measurement conditions and annex versions.

## Decision 251/2026 — licensing OSH expertise and consultancy

- **Official title:** conditions, rules and procedures for licensing OSH expertise and consultancy services.
- **Secondary report:** https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/10/28/حماية-أرواح-العمال-أولوية-وزارة-العمل-تحدد-مهلة-24-ساعة-للإبلاغ-عن-الحوادث-داخل-مواقع-الإنتاج/7590406
- The report describes two licence levels: Consultant A and Consultant B. Consultant A reportedly requires a bachelor's degree in a listed specialist discipline (engineering, pharmacy, human/veterinary medicine, science, applied health sciences, environment/waste management) and at least 20 years of actual OSH experience. Consultant B has separate conditions; other related degrees may be considered by the relevant committee.
- A law-firm explanatory article reports that the decision regulates applications, supporting documents, review, fees, duration and cancellation, and prohibits consultancy practice without a Ministry licence: https://wwlawfirms.com/ar/single-blog/ترخيص-استشاري-السلامة-المهنية-في-مصر

## Decision 252/2026 — licensing and training in OSH

- **Official title:** rules for granting licences and training systems in occupational safety and health and workplace protection.
- **Secondary text lead:** https://www.almasdar.com/175156
- **Further text snippet:** https://nabd.com/s/179622255-6a7de2/وزارة-العمل-تحدد-قواعد-منح-تراخيص-ونظم-التدريب-بمجال-السلامة-والصحة-المهنية
- The opening definitions distinguish the Ministry, competent authority, Law 14/2025, the OSH central administration and training programmes. Programmes include basic, advanced, specific and specialized training for OSH specialists/technicians, members of functional units and OSH committees, prospective members and other applicants.
- The decision implements Labour Law Art. 261, which provides for basic/advanced/specific/specialized training and licensing rules. Art. 262 provides for licensing of OSH expertise/consultancy. Check the statute's maximum fee limits and exemptions against the signed decisions.

## Decision 251/2026 — OSH expert/consultant licensing: expanded substantive extract

- Gazette issue 225 supplement (A), 12 October 2026. DSpace bundle metadata lists the signed decision, but the PDF is restricted/request-copy.
- Secondary article-level reporting: https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/10/28/حماية-أرواح-العمال-أولوية-وزارة-العمل-تحدد-مهلة-24-ساعة-للإبلاغ-عن-الحوادث-داخل-مواقع-الإنتاج/7590406
- Additional detailed secondary summary: https://www.a7walegypt.com/38829
- The decision requires a Ministry licence to practise OSH/environmental-safety expertise and consultancy. It creates Consultant A and Consultant B categories.
- Consultant A: relevant bachelor's degree in engineering, pharmacy, human medicine, veterinary medicine, sciences, applied health sciences, or environment/waste management; practical experience at least 20 years (secondary report states 15 years for relevant PhD holders); approved international OSH certificate; digital transformation/ICDL certificate; Ministry tests; good standing/criminal-record and drug-test requirements.
- Consultant B: same or approved academic fields; practical experience at least 15 years (secondary report states 10 years for relevant PhD holders); approved international certificate and required computer/Ministry tests.
- Application requires a paper/electronic dossier including CV, original qualification, ID, criminal record, experience evidence, professional certificates and drug-test certificate. A central Ministry licensing committee reviews applications; a licensing register and updated electronic database are maintained, with licensed consultants published on the Ministry website.
- Procedure: review within 15 days; notify applicant of outcome within three working days; incomplete documents can be completed; refusal must be reasoned and appealable.
- Licence lasts three years, renewable. Fees: Consultant A EGP 30,000 issue / EGP 25,000 renewal; Consultant B EGP 25,000 issue / EGP 20,000 renewal. Persons with disabilities receive a 50% fee reduction. Renewal requires work history for the previous three years.
- Cancellation grounds include loss of eligibility, false documents/data, serious professional breach, a conviction affecting honour/trust, and positive drug-use findings.
- Status: substantive secondary extraction only. The actual Gazette text and annexes must be obtained and compared before relying on degree/experience reductions, fee schedule, appeal periods or cancellation grounds.

## Decision 249/2026 — expanded official clarification and secondary article extract

- Official Ministry clarification, 27 October 2026: https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/بيان-توضيحي-هام-بشأن-القرار-الوزاري-رقم-249-لسنة-2026-الخاص-بالسلامة-والصحة-المهنية-وزارة-العمل-لن-ي-ضار-أي-عامل-يعمل-بمهنة-أخصائي-السلامة-والصحة-المهنية-ولا-مساس-بالمراكز-القانونية-المكتسبة-وأهمية-المهنة-تستوجب-منتهى-الدقة-في-اختيار-شاغليها/
- Secondary Gazette-text extract: https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/10/28/حماية-أرواح-العمال-أولوية-وزارة-العمل-تحدد-مهلة-24-ساعة-للإبلاغ-عن-الحوادث-داخل-مواقع-الإنتاج/7590406
- The secondary text reports the 50-worker threshold: establishments with 50 or more workers must create the required functional OSH units/committees; each geographically or administratively separate worksite is treated separately if it reaches the threshold, regardless of employment relationship, work pattern or ownership/affiliation.
- Employer/board chair/manager bears responsibility (as applicable) for a safe work environment, an approved OSH policy suited to activity and legal standards, and adequate human, financial and technical resources. The decision differentiates high-, medium- and limited-risk establishments according to the severity/likelihood of fire, explosion, collapse, toxic/radioactive/biological release and other activity-specific hazards.
- The official Ministry clarification states no current OSH specialist is to be harmed or removed merely because of the new rules; previously recognized academic qualifications and acquired legal positions are preserved. It recognizes specialist degrees in medicine, veterinary medicine, pharmacy, engineering, sciences and environment/waste management, and also other university degrees supplemented by specialized postgraduate diplomas, extended practical experience and recognized certificates. It introduces OSH Specialist A/B classification according to qualification, specialization, experience and establishment risk/needs.
- The Ministry says the central OSH administration will issue circulars to unify implementation and regularization procedures.
- **Important:** this is a Ministry clarification plus secondary reporting, not the signed 249 text. Do not derive exact risk thresholds, staffing ratios, committee composition or staffing schedules from this summary. The DSpace Gazette PDF remains restricted/request-copy.

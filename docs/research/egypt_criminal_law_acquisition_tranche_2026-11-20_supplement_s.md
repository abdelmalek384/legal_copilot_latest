# Criminal-law acquisition tranche — supplement S (20 November 2026)

## Scope and acquisition result

This supplement acquires Ministry of Defence medical-fitness instruments implementing Military and National Service Law 127/1980. This is a new sub-chain not duplicated in supplements Q or R:
- Decision 195/2020: replacement medical-fitness framework; complete Official Proceedings issue PDF acquired as a Gazette scan/text source.
- Decision 166/2022: complete four-article amendment text; official Proceedings issue 171 scan/text.
- Decision 53/2019: complete four-article amendment to the former Decision 44/1987; full LAWEG transcription.
- Decision 44/1987: historical baseline, repealed in full by Decision 195/2020.

## 1. Decision 195/2020 — current base medical-fitness decision, as amended

**Title:** قرار القائد العام للقوات المسلحة ووزير الدفاع والإنتاج الحربي رقم 195 لسنة 2020 بشأن شروط اللياقة الطبية للخدمة العسكرية والوطنية بالقوات المسلحة.

**Publication:** Egyptian Proceedings (الوقائع المصرية), issue 257, 15 November 2020. Decision signed 13 October 2020 and effective from its date of issue under Article 7.

**Source status:** Full 60-page Official Proceedings issue PDF scan, hosted by Elwatan (secondary host, but the document itself is the printed Official Proceedings issue). Text extraction spans the decision at pages 3–32. The source contains the complete Arabic medical-condition schedules; repository note records the document and exact source rather than claiming the full schedule has been independently manually proofread.

### Schedule content and QA

The Official Proceedings scan contains the full medical exclusion/fitness schedules. The main Article 2 list is grouped by medical specialties, including ophthalmology, respiratory diseases, vascular surgery, cardiology, general surgery, urology, dermatology, ENT, orthopaedics, neurology/psychiatry and other medical conditions. Articles 3 and 4 set fitness categories B and C. Decision 166/2022 later repealed Article 4 and changed specific Article 2 orthopaedic thresholds.

The extracted PDF text contains OCR defects and some clinically technical terms require visual inspection against the scan before machine use. This supplement does not paraphrase the full list as though it were a verified diagnostic standard.

### Current operative effect

For the specific height/chest criterion in Article 2, ninth (bones), Decision 166/2022 replaced the prior threshold with:
- height below 160 cm; and
- chest circumference below 80 cm on expiration.

It also changed the specified leg curvature threshold to more than 10 cm between the inner malleoli, and repealed Article 4 of Decision 195/2020 (the Level C criteria article). Therefore, the original 155 cm/75 cm and 10–12 cm Level C criteria in the 2020 text must not be presented as current after 4 July 2022.

**Currentness:** Decision 53/2019 amended Decision 44/1987, but Decision 195/2020 Article 5 subsequently repealed Decision 44/1987 in full. Thus Decision 53/2019 is historical and is not a current independent source of medical standards. The later Decision 166/2022 modifies Decision 195/2020.

## 4. Decision 44/1987 — historical base and repeal

**Instrument:** Minister of Defence Decision 44/1987 on medical fitness levels for military and national service. Eastlaws identifies it as published in Proceedings issue 50 bis, 28 February 1987. It was amended by Decisions 203/1989, 205/1993 and 53/2019. Decision 195/2020 Article 5 expressly repealed it in full.

**Source status:** Eastlaws historical full-text record is partially accessible by article and marks the instrument as repealed in full. Complete original signed 1987 scan and the full text of Decisions 203/1989 and 205/1993 remain outstanding. These historical instruments should not be used as current medical standards.

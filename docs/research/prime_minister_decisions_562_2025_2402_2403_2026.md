# Prime Minister Decisions 562/2025, 2402/2026 and 2403/2026 — Social Dialogue and Unorganized-Worker Support

## 1. Decision 562/2025 — earlier Social Dialogue Council formation (superseded)

- **Title:** قرار رئيس مجلس الوزراء رقم 562 لسنة 2025 بشأن إعادة تشكيل المجلس الأعلى للتشاور الاجتماعي في مجال العمل.
- **Issued:** 13 February 2025 (legal-memory index date; exact Gazette publication locator not obtained).
- **Source leads:**
  - Legal-memory gazette index: https://mksegypt.org/public/ar/nashras/28
  - Ministry/State Information Service media archive, meeting record and attached PDF: https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/handle/123456789/97016
  - Official State Information Service human-rights report referencing the decision: https://hrightsstudies.sis.gov.eg/أخبار/محلية/إيمان-كريم-ذوي-الإعاقة-حصلوا-على-نصيب-وافر-من-الحقوق-بقانون-العمل-الجديد/
- The decision reconstituted the Supreme Council for Social Dialogue in the field of work, with balanced representation of government, employers and labour organizations.
- On 5 August 2026, Prime Minister Decision 2402/2026 expressly repealed Decision 562/2025. Keep 562 as historical/superseded, not a current source for Council composition or operation.

## 2. Decision 2402/2026 — Supreme Council for Social Dialogue

- **Title:** قرار رئيس مجلس الوزراء رقم 2402 لسنة 2026 بشأن المجلس الأعلى للتشاور الاجتماعي.
- **Signed:** 5 August 2026.
- **Published:** Official Gazette issue 31 bis (C), 5 August 2026; effective the day after publication (6 August 2026).
- **Official Cabinet meeting approval and draft text:** https://cabinet.gov.eg/Meeting/Details/11900
- **Official Ministry explanation (31 August 2026):** https://sis.gov.eg/ar/المركز-الإعلامي/الأخبار/وزارة-العمل-المجلس-الأعلى-للتشاور-الاجتماعي-يمثل-إحدى-الآليات-الوطنية-الرئيسية-لترسيخ-الحوار-الاجتماعي/
- **Gazette publication report/full secondary summary:** https://almasdar.com/173659
- **Article-level secondary summary:** https://almalnews.com/2132412/تعرف-على-اختصاصات-وتشكيل-المجلس-الأعلى-للتشاور/
- **DSpace issue listing:** Official Gazette 2026 collection: https://dspace.id.com.eg/collections/bb4a4122-9215-48e2-a8a5-b622ffa5eeb6

### Main provisions

- Establishes/reconstitutes the Supreme Council for Social Dialogue under the Ministry responsible for labour, implementing Labour Law Articles 188–190.
- Council chaired by the Minister responsible for labour.
- Government representation includes senior representatives from local development, tourism/antiquities, social solidarity, agriculture, education/technical education, parliamentary affairs, justice, labour, planning/economic development and industry, plus the Central Agency for Organization and Administration.
- Eleven employer-organization representatives and eleven labour-organization representatives; the decision requires representation balance and gender representation of at least one-third for each of the three parties where possible.
- Representatives of the National Council for Women, National Council for Persons with Disabilities, National Council for Childhood and Motherhood and National Council for Human Rights attend without a counted vote.
- Council functions include national tripartite consultation/social-dialogue policy, opinions on draft labour/social-protection/trade-union/industrial-relations legislation, review of international and Arab labour conventions before ratification, legislative/executive gap studies, proposals to prevent collective disputes, employment-support initiatives and labour-economics research.
- Establishes a public electronic website/platform for publishing Council decisions, recommendations and activities.
- Funding includes budget allocations and accepted gifts/grants/donations under the stated two-thirds approval condition. A special account may be maintained under the Unified Treasury Account, with a bank account subject to Finance Ministry approval; surplus can roll over and be invested under the decision's controls.
- The Council chair reports decisions/results to the Prime Minister every three months or when needed.
- Repeals Prime Minister Decision 562/2025.

## 3. Decision 2403/2026 — Unorganized Workers Emergency Aid and Social/Health Services Fund

- **Title:** قرار رئيس مجلس الوزراء رقم 2403 لسنة 2026 بشأن صندوق إعانات الطوارئ والخدمات الاجتماعية والصحية للعمالة غير المنتظمة.
- **Signed:** 5 August 2026.
- **Published:** Official Gazette issue 31 bis (C), 5 August 2026; effective the day after publication (6 August 2026).
- **Official Cabinet meeting approval and draft text:** https://cabinet.gov.eg/Meeting/Details/11900
- **Official government Gazette issue locator:** https://www.almasdar.com/search/query/?page=4&w=الجريدة%20الرسمية
- **Full secondary transcription:** https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=19475
- **Official implementation follow-up:** https://www.nile.eg/وزير-العمل-بدء-الاستعدادات-لتفعيل-دور-صندوق-إعانات-الطوارئ-والخدمات-الاجتماعية-والصحية-للعمالة-غير-المنتظمة

### Sixteen-article map

**Article 1 — purpose and functions**
The Fund operates nationally to support, protect and employ irregular workers, develop skills, protect workers during employment and provide assistance during unemployment. Its functions include:
1. Emergency assistance during general economic crises, disasters, epidemics and temporary work stoppages.
2. Social and health services.
3. Medical expenses and healthcare support.
4. Contributions toward social-insurance payments, in coordination with the Finance and Social Solidarity Ministers and the National Social Insurance Authority, within Fund resources.
5. Supporting/developing employment and placement operations.
6. Training irregular workers and developing technical/vocational skills.
7. Providing necessary work tools to certain groups.
8. Supporting transport, subsistence and accommodation at remote worksites.
9. Supporting OSH compliance and work-environment protection.
10. Cultural/sporting programmes, competitions and recreational activities, subject to resources.
11. Funding national enumeration of irregular workers and databases.
12. Awareness campaigns on labour, insurance and social rights.
13. Digital platforms for Fund services.
14. Development projects to improve irregular workers' conditions or formalize informal-sector workers, subject to national approvals.

**Article 2 — resources**
The full secondary transcription lists:
- 1%–3% of actual wages of irregular workers in construction/building; where actual wages cannot be established, deemed wages up to 20% of the general-contract value, and up to 45% for services/industrial-work operations.
- 1%–3% of actual wages of irregular workers in mines/quarries and similar activities; if actual wages cannot be established, deemed wages up to 15% of the operation value.
- Monthly registration contribution for other irregular-worker categories between EGP 20 and EGP 200.
- 0.5% of the value of agricultural products purchased by the State.
- EGP 100 per notarization/signature-validity/enforceability request for agricultural-land sale contracts.
- Up to EGP 50 per first issue or renewal of a professional driving licence.
- Existing irregular-worker accounts at Labour Directorates and returns on their assets/deposits.
- Donations/grants accepted by the board, symbolic service fees (if any) and investment returns.
- A separate Fund board-chair decision sets contribution/fee categories within statutory caps and payment dates.

**Article 3 — board composition**
Board chaired by the Minister responsible for labour, with senior representatives from local development, social solidarity, agriculture, housing/utilities/urban communities, labour, industry, the National Social Insurance Authority, General Health Insurance Organization and Comprehensive Health Insurance Authority; five employer representatives and five labour-organization representatives; experts may attend without a counted vote.

**Article 4 — board powers**
The board controls Fund affairs and approves policies/plans, annual budget and final accounts, technical organizational decisions, grants/donations, periodic reports, projects, public-awareness programmes, provincial branches and monitoring/evaluation of funded work.

**Article 5 — board term**
Three-year term; reconstitution steps begin during the final 60 days. Vacant seats are refilled by the nominating body within one month.

**Article 6 — delegation and expert support**
The board may delegate matters to its chair or members and use technical, legal, accounting or other experts for studies and administrative/financial systems.

**Article 7 — meetings and allowances**
Board meetings at least once every three months; quorum is a majority; decisions by absolute majority of attending members, with the chair's casting vote on tie. Attendance allowance is EGP 5,000 per meeting for the chair and each member, paid from Fund resources.

**Article 8 — Secretary-General**
Appointment by the Fund chair after board approval. The text specifies Egyptian nationality, appropriate higher qualification, at least ten years' relevant experience, good reputation, fitness, no disqualifying conviction and no final disciplinary dismissal.

**Article 9 — Secretary-General functions**
Financial/administrative supervision, protection of Fund assets, implementation of board decisions, preparation of technical organizational drafts, periodic performance reports and the budget proposal.

**Article 10 — staffing**
Fund roles may be filled by secondment from ministries/government entities or contracts with experts under applicable legal procedures.

**Article 11 — bank account and investments**
Fund has a special account at a commercial bank registered with the Central Bank; disbursement through non-cash payment channels. Surplus rolls over; investments may be made in approved savings vehicles, Treasury bills, banking instruments or related direct investments, with Prime Minister approval for direct investment.

**Article 12 — annual accounts and reporting**
Annual financial statements, estimated budget and year-end report of spending, beneficiaries, balances, disbursements and establishments that paid Fund dues; copy sent to the Central Auditing Organization.

**Article 13 — external auditor**
One or more external auditors appointed by the board from the Ministry of Finance's registered auditors; annual balance-sheet review and periodic reports on financial position and internal controls.

**Article 14 — financial year**
1 July to 30 June, with the first year running from Fund establishment to the end of that financial year.

**Article 15 — public funds**
Fund assets are public funds for Penal Code purposes and subject to Central Auditing Organization oversight.

**Article 16 — publication and commencement**
Published in the Official Gazette and effective the day after publication.

### Legal and product cautions

- The Fund is a support/protection/employment instrument for irregular workers; it is not the same as the Training and Qualification Fund.
- Do not confuse the Fund's EGP 1%–3% contribution/resource rates with a wage deduction from a worker. The decision's contribution base and liable payer must be checked from the signed text and the relevant sector rules.
- The fee/resource rates in Article 2 are transcribed from a secondary full text; verify each figure and its base against the signed Gazette before building financial logic or advising employers.
- The official Cabinet report confirms the decision was approved as part of implementing Labour Law 14/2025, but the full 16-article text used here is secondary. No production import or rights clearance.

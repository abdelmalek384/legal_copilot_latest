# Clinical Medical Research Law 214/2020 and PM Decision 927/2022 — acquisition note

**Snapshot:** 17 November 2026  
**Status:** official GPO text for the Executive Regulation indexed; complete Gazette scan, file hash and lawyer review pending.

## Primary instrument

- Enabling statute: Law 214/2020 regulating clinical medical research.
- Executive instrument: Prime Minister Decision 927/2022 issuing the Executive Regulation.
- Official GPO text: https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Zl+Ntz1HZ5w%3D
- The decision was signed 12 March 2022 and states that it takes effect on the day following publication. The exact Gazette issue/date must be recorded from the scan.

## Selected regulatory content

The official GPO text shows the regulation covers:
- Ethical principles, informed consent, good clinical practice, protection from research-related harm and participant withdrawal.
- Protocol review and approval, with different routes depending on whether a study uses novel drugs, biologics, indications, devices or other interventions.
- Roles of institutional ethics committees, the Supreme Council for Clinical Medical Research Ethics and the Egyptian Drug Authority.
- Clinical-trial phases, evaluation before progression and conditions for placebo use.
- Participant rights, including confidentiality and written informed consent in the participant's mother tongue.
- Principal investigator and sponsor qualifications/duties, adverse-event reporting, record retention, insurance and treatment/compensation.
- Use and storage of human biological samples, including restrictions on future use and storage outside Egypt.

## High-value article-level leads

- **Article 2:** informed consent, ethical/scientific protocol, health safeguards, treatment of research-related side effects and participant withdrawal.
- **Articles 5–9:** protocol review/approval routes and timelines; institutional committee, EDA and Supreme Council responsibilities.
- **Articles 10–15:** oversight, inspection, complaints, committee structure and EDA functions.
- **Articles 16–19:** clinical research phases and placebo.
- **Articles 20–21:** participant rights, confidentiality, informed-consent copies and restrictions on inducements.
- **Articles 22–29:** investigator/sponsor obligations, serious adverse-event notification, record retention and insurance/compensation.
- **Article 30:** restrictions on use, trade, future storage and export of human samples.

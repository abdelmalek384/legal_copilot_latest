## 1. Unified Public Finance Law No. 6 of 2022 and Executive Regulation No. 73 of 2024

### Law No. 6/2022 — original Gazette text

**Government-hosted PDF:** https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.6-2022.pdf  
**Gazette:** Official Gazette issue 5 bis (D), 8 February 2022.  
**Commencement:** promulgating Article 6: «ينشر هذا القانون في الجريدة الرسمية، ويعمل به من اليوم التالي لتاريخ نشره.» Effective 9 February 2022.  
**Source status:** official government-hosted PDF; extracted text 431 lines.

#### Promulgating provisions — verbatim extracts

**المادة الأولى:** the law applies to public finance and, subject to constitutional/legal guarantees for certain independent bodies, covers budgeted state administrative units, local administration units, service public authorities, special units, special funds/accounts and projects funded from them, and economic public authorities. It excludes funds/accounts financed by member subscriptions and accounts expressly excluded by international agreements.

**المادة الثانية (original 2022):** applies the line-item budget system with full program/performance budgeting within four years of commencement. **Important:** this four-year period was amended by Law 18/2024; do not use the original period as current.

**المادة الثالثة:** «يلغى القانون رقم ٥٣ لسنة ١٩٧٣ بشأن الموازنة العامة للدولة، والقانون رقم ١٢٧ لسنة ١٩٨١ بشأن المحاسبة الحكومية، كما يلغى كل حكم يخالف أحكام القانون المرافق.»

**المادة الرابعة:** Minister of Finance to issue the executive regulation within one year; until then, the executive regulations under Laws 53/1973 and 127/1981 continue insofar as not inconsistent with the new law.

**المادة السادسة:** commencement on the day following publication.

#### Selected operative articles — verbatim / near-verbatim extracts

**المادة (٧):** «لا يجوز بعد العمل بهذا القانون إنشاء صناديق وحسابات خاصة إلا بقانون.» The article also regulates allocation of dedicated resources, treatment of special funds/accounts as a single unit at entity level, transfers subject to competent-authority approval after Ministry of Finance opinion, and a separate budget for each fund/account.

**المادة (٨):** budgets are prepared and executed on program/performance basis, aligned with national development plans and strategic objectives; the executive regulation specifies classifications.

**المادة (١٠):** state general budget prepared and executed on cash basis; economic public authorities and National Authority for Military Production use accrual basis.

**المادة (١١):** requires a medium-term budget framework for three following fiscal years.

**المادة (٣٢):** «تلتزم الوزارة بتنفيذ الفتاوى الصادرة عن الجمعية العمومية لقسمى الفتوى والتشريع بمجلس الدولة وغيرها من القرارات الملزمة الصادرة عن الجهات المختصة قانوناً بنظر وتسوية المنازعات الناشئة بين الجهات الإدارية وبعضها البعض...» Other opinions/decisions imposing financial burdens require Ministry of Finance approval before execution.

**المادة (٣٤):** administrative entities may not open accounts in their own name or in the name of their special funds outside the Central Bank without Minister approval, subject to statutory exceptions and controls.

**المادة (٣٥):** before incurring expenditure or concluding a financial contract/agreement, the entity must obtain confirmation that the relevant budget appropriation is legally available and sufficient; prohibits payment without an appropriation/commitment.

**المادة (٥٨):** Ministry financial inspection bodies inspect administrative entities’ accounting units and may access records and inspect stores, safes and collection units, without prejudice to Central Auditing Organization powers.

**المادة (٥٩):** on request of the Public Prosecution or Administrative Prosecution, the Ministry may nominate a financial inspector as technical expert for relevant investigation issues.

**المادة (٦٠):** preserves Administrative Prosecution and disciplinary-court jurisdiction; Ministry has disciplinary authority for its financial/accounting employees in the scope stated, with notification duties.

**المادة (٦١):** requires prompt notification of theft, embezzlement, fire, misappropriation, destruction, negligence and encroachment on public property to the Ministry, Central Auditing Organization and relevant oversight bodies.

### Amendment — Law No. 18 of 2024

**Official Printers authority page (Amiri Press):** https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=7Y3g6Yk5VVQ%3D  
The page text identifies **Law 18/2024**, amending Law 6/2022 and its accompanying law. It substitutes:
- the first paragraph of promulgating Article 2, extending the program/performance budget transition from four years to **no more than six years** from commencement; and
- definitions of “fiscal year”, “Ministry” and “Minister” in Article 1; and
- specified provisions in Articles 23(2), 30(1) and (2), 35 and 74.

**Source status:** primary legislation text on the official Amiri Press site, but page fetch returned HTTP 502 in this research pass. Search-indexed page text was available. Need retrieve a stable Gazette PDF/image and complete all substituted provisions, including Article 74. Do not treat the snippet as the full amendment text.

## 2. Local Administration Law No. 43 of 1979 and Executive Regulation PM Decision No. 707 of 1979

### Primary/near-primary source acquisition

**Original law metadata:** Law 43/1979, published Official Gazette issue 25 (تابع), 21 June 1979; Eastlaws metadata says effective on publication.  
**Full text / regulation combined scan:** https://manshurat.org/file/24908/download?token=U11HRSB2  
The PDF identifies the Local Government Law and Official Gazette issue 29 bis, 25 July 1979, and contains PM Decision 707/1979 plus the Executive Regulation. The page's extraction includes original-era terminology and page-date inconsistencies.  
**Official Ministry of Finance catalogue for PM Decision 707/1979:** https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-5fe86ab67bfc600007cc0063  
**Secondary metadata for Law 43/1979:** https://manshurat.org/node/13261  
**Secondary metadata for Executive Regulation:** https://manshurat.org/node/13282

### Executive Regulation — promulgation and operative extracts

**PM Decision 707/1979, المادة الأولى:** «يعمل باللائحة التنفيذية لقانون نظام الحكم المحلي المرافقة.»

**المادة الثانية:** «ينشر هذا القرار في الجريدة الرسمية، ويعمل به من تاريخ نشره.» Signed 25 July 1979.

**اللائحة، مادة (١):** when dividing the Republic into governorates, centres, cities and villages, natural, demographic, economic and urban conditions are to be considered; large cities may be divided into districts having regard to population and integration of services/production.

**اللائحة، مادة (٢):** creation and territorial definition of governorates by presidential decision on proposal of the Governors Council; creation of centres, cities, districts and villages by governor decision after the approvals specified in the text.

**اللائحة، مادة (٣) (text appearing immediately after general chapter heading):** local units, within state policy and the general plan, establish and administer public utilities within their territory except utilities designated national by PM decision; they also exercise ministerial powers assigned by applicable laws/regulations, while the governorate exercises utility powers not assigned to other local units.

**اللائحة، مادة (٦):** allocates public-health and medical facility functions among governorates, centres/cities, districts and villages.

**اللائحة، مادة (٧):** governorate role in housing, urban planning and municipal utilities; local units’ roles include water and sanitation, road/street works, environmental improvement, cleanliness and supervision of municipal services.

**اللائحة، مادة (٥١):** «يعتبر المحافظ ممثلاً لرئيس الجمهورية بالمحافظة، ويتولى الإشراف على تنفيذ السياسة العامة للدولة...» and assigns the responsibilities listed in the provision.

**اللائحة، مادة (٥٢):** «يتولى المحافظ كافة السلطات والاختصاصات التنفيذية المقررة للوزراء بمقتضى القوانين واللوائح بالنسبة للمرافق العامة التي تنشئها وتديرها وحدات الحكم المحلي بالمحافظة...» including supervision and control of those facilities and personnel.

**اللائحة، مادة (٥٤):** authorizes the governor to delegate specified powers to assistants, secretary-general/deputy, service heads and local unit heads within the text's conditions.

**Warning:** this 1979 regulation is heavily exposed to later constitutional and statutory change, including the 2014 Constitution and its five-year transitional provision (Article 242). Do not treat the original allocation of popular local council powers or terminology as current without mapping subsequent laws, constitutional provisions and implementing decisions. Article 181 Constitution also assigns a specific local council competence dispute to the State Council General Assembly of Fatwa and Legislation.

### Amendment gap

- Law 145/1988 substituted “local administration” terminology for “local government” in Law 43/1979 (secondary metadata; obtain Gazette amendment).
- The current status of elected local councils, local executive powers, fiscal decentralization, and the continued operative portions of Decision 707/1979 require a provision-by-provision review against the Constitution 2014/2019 and subsequent legislation.
- No claim is made that Law 43/1979 or Decision 707/1979 is reproduced here in a fully consolidated current form.

## 3. Administrative Prosecution Law No. 117 of 1958 and internal regulation

**Law text:** https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11722  
**Original Gazette/PDF metadata:** https://manshurat.org/node/7342  
**Executive/internal regulation lead:** Administrative Prosecution internal regulation / disciplinary courts Decision No. 1489 of 1958 (linked from the LawHub record).  
**Source status:** secondary full text; original Gazette PDF is listed at Manshurat but not yet OCR-compared.

### Arabic text and amendment trail

**المادة (١) (text as displayed in the secondary source):** «النيابة الإدارية هيئة قضائية مستقلة تلحق بوزير العدل.» The text then defines the Authority's composition and supervision structure. This article should be checked against the current constitutional framework and amendments before use.

**المادة (٢):** establishes administrative prosecution departments, offices and branches; their number, jurisdiction and seats are determined by Minister of Justice decision on proposal of the Authority President and after the specified opinion process.

The source annotates:
- Law 59/1959 amendment to Article 2 (Official Gazette issue 33 bis (B), 21 Feb 1959);
- Law 12/1981 amendments to paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 1 (Official Gazette issue 10 (تابع ج), 5 Mar 1981);
- Law 12/1989 substitution of Article 1 and Article 2 (Official Gazette issue 16, 20 Apr 1989).

### Later amendment — Law No. 77 of 2019 (official Gazette PDF)

**Official government-hosted Gazette PDF:** https://sis.gov.eg/media/647533/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-77-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2019-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%B6-%D8%A7%D8%AD%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%8575-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-1963.pdf  
**Gazette:** issue 25 bis (B), 26 June 2019. **Commencement:** Article 6 says next day after publication, i.e. 27 June 2019.

**المادة الأولى / مادة (٣٥):** «يعين رئيس هيئة النيابة الإدارية بقرار من رئيس الجمهورية من بين أقدم سبعة من نواب رئيس الهيئة، وذلك لمدة أربع سنوات أو للمدة الباقية حتى بلوغه سن التقاعد أيهما أقرب، ولمرة واحدة طوال مدة عمله.»

**المادة الرابعة / مادة (٨٣) من قانون مجلس الدولة:** replaces first three paragraphs with: «يعين رئيس مجلس الدولة بقرار من رئيس الجمهورية من بين أقدم سبعة من نواب رئيس المجلس، وذلك لمدة أربع سنوات أو للمدة الباقية حتى بلوغه سن التقاعد أيهما أقرب، ولمرة واحدة طوال مدة عمله.»

**المادة السادسة:** «ينشر هذا القانون في الجريدة الرسمية، ويُعمل به من اليوم التالي لتاريخ نشره.»

This official instrument is a cross-statute amendment: it modifies Administrative Prosecution Law 117/1958 and State Council Law 47/1972 (as well as other judicial authority statutes). Its Article 4 amendment to State Council Law Article 83 is important for the leadership-selection chain and should be indexed under both parent laws.

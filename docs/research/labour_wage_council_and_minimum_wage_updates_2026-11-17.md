# National Council for Wages and Minimum-Wage Updates — 17 November 2026

## Prime Minister Decision 3165/2026 — National Council for Wages rules and powers

- **Instrument:** Prime Minister Decision No. 3165/2026 on the operating system and powers of the National Council for Wages (قرار رئيس مجلس الوزراء رقم 3165 لسنة 2026 بشأن نظام عمل المجلس القومي للأجور وتحديد اختصاصاته).
- **Signed:** 12 October 2026.
- **Gazette:** Official Gazette issue 41 bis (و), dated 12 October 2026. News coverage of the Gazette publication appeared on 26 October 2026.
- **Official Amiri Press searchable text:** https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=EreMgz%2FNoYY%3D
- **Full secondary transcription:** https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=19918
- **Official Gazette publication report:** https://business.ahram.org.eg/UI/Front/Inner.aspx?NewsContentID=134866
- **Legal basis:** Labour Law 14/2025, particularly Articles 101–102; also Social Insurance and Pensions Law 148/2019 and Trade Union Law 213/2017.
- Eight articles. The full text is indexed by the Amiri Press search service; the secondary transcription provides a readable article-by-article copy.

### Article map

- **Article 1:** Council may form specialist subcommittees from members or non-members; membership should preserve balanced tripartite representation among government, employers' organizations and trade unions.
- **Article 2:** Council powers include:
  1. Setting national minimum wages across sectors, considering worker/family needs, living costs, balance between parties and productivity.
  2. Reviewing the minimum wage at least annually, or as needed, based on economic/social conditions, inflation, prices and living costs.
  3. Setting the minimum periodic annual increment, not less than 3% of insured wage, and rules for payment under the Labour Law.
  4. Reviewing employer requests to reduce or exempt the periodic annual increment for emergency economic circumstances; decision within 30 days after referral to the Council, under Council criteria and balancing both parties' interests.
  5. Notifying relevant ministries of acceptance/rejection of reduction/exemption requests.
  6. Studying wage structures by sector/profession/activity and proposing solutions, including attention to vulnerable groups and low-wage regions.
  7. Studying international/regional wage standards and recommendations and relevant economic statistics.
  8. Approving publication of periodic detailed reports explaining economic/social data and consultations used in wage reviews.
  9. Developing a monitoring system for economic/social effects of minimum-wage decisions, including employment, inflation, poverty and living standards.
- **Article 3:** Council meets at least every six months or when needed; quorum is a majority; decisions by majority of attending members; chair has casting vote on tie.
- **Article 4:** Planning and Economic Development Minister establishes the technical secretariat, chaired by a Ministry representative not below Director-General level, with four specialists from Planning and Labour.
- **Article 5:** Secretariat monitors economic/social/labour indicators, prepares studies and impact assessments, monitors implementation with the Ministry of Labour, receives and examines employer requests to reduce/exempt the periodic increment, notifies employers of decisions by registered letter or electronic means, prepares draft decisions/agendas/minutes, maintains records, and reports to the Council. It meets at least every three months; chair submits a semiannual report.
- **Article 6:** Council chair sets meeting allowances for Council/subcommittee/secretariat members within legal rules and with Finance Ministry approval.
- **Article 7:** repeals Prime Minister Decision 2659/2020 and conflicting provisions.
- **Article 8:** published in the Official Gazette and effective the day after publication.

### Critical distinction

Article 2(4)–(5) concerns employer requests to reduce or be exempt from the **periodic annual increment** for emergency economic circumstances. It does not itself create an exemption from the national minimum wage. Do not conflate the two.

## National Wages Council approval announced 15 November 2026 — future minimum-wage change, formal decision pending

- **Official State Information Service report:** https://sis.gov.eg/ar/المركز-الإعلامي/الأخبار/رئيس-الوزراء-يترأس-اجتماع-المجلس-القومي-للأجور/
- **Official Cabinet report:** https://www.cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/86511
- **Official MENA report:** https://mena.org.eg/ar/news/المجلس-القومي-للأجور-يوافق-على-رفع-الحد-الأدنى-لأجور-العاملين-بالقطاع-الخاص-لـ8500-جنيه-اعتبارًا-من-أول-يناير-2027
- The Council met under Prime Minister chairmanship on 15 November 2026 and approved:
  - Private-sector monthly minimum wage: **EGP 8,500 from 1 January 2027**.
  - Minimum net hourly wage for workers in work temporary by nature or working part-time: **EGP 34 per hour**. The official announcement does not separately state the effective date for this hourly rate; secondary coverage associates it with the 2027 package, so confirm the final instrument.
  - Minimum periodic annual increment for the private sector: at least **3% of insured wage**, with a minimum of **EGP 350**, effective from each establishment's 2027 financial year according to its accounting year.
- **Legal status as at 17 November 2026:** the official sources reviewed announce Council approval, but no numbered National Wages Council implementing decision, signed text or Official Gazette publication for the EGP 8,500 figure was located. The Minister of Labour was reported as saying a circular would follow to specify implementation mechanisms and controls. Keep this as an announced future measure, not as an already effective November 2026 wage floor.
- Until the new minimum-wage decision takes effect, the previously documented private-sector monthly floor is EGP 7,000 under National Wages Council Decision 15/2025, effective 1 March 2025, subject to any later formal instrument not yet located in this harvest. The 2025 implementation circular is separately recorded in the circular harvest.
- Do not create a production rule for the EGP 8,500 / EGP 34 figures until the formal Council decision and Ministry implementation circular are obtained and reviewed.

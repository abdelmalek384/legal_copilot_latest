# Legacy Labour Decisions — Transitional Effect under Enactment Article 11

Research date: 17 November 2026  
Companion register: data/research/legacy_decision_transition_register_2026-11-17.csv

## 1. Transitional rule

Article 11 of the Enactment Articles to Labour Law 14/2025 provides that the Minister responsible for labour issues the implementing decisions within 90 days from the Law's effective date. **Until those decisions are issued, decisions already in force in the relevant matters continue to apply to the extent that they do not conflict with the new Law and its attached statute.**

The new Law repealed Labour Law 12/2003 and Law 125/2010, but that does not mean every subordinate decision issued under Law 12/2003 vanished automatically on 1 September 2025. Each legacy instrument needs a subject-by-subject conflict, repeal, amendment and savings analysis.

## 2. Official Ministry legacy decision index

- **Official Ministry laws and legislations page:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/القوانين-والتشريعات/
- The indexed page lists a substantial catalogue of decisions issued under Labour Law 12/2003 and related employment/OSH laws, including medical examinations, work injury forms, OSH units/committees, training, apprenticeship, recruitment agencies, foreign-worker permits, women/child work, holidays, remote areas, fines, social services and OSH councils.
- Direct PDF links have been acquired for selected decisions, including Decision 153/2003 (medical/capability examination), Decision 232/2010 (OSH units) and Decision 414/2015 (apprenticeship). Many other items remain index-only and require direct download/copy retrieval.

## 3. Important legacy chains to resolve

### A. Medical fitness and health examinations

- Decision 153/2003 governs pre-employment medical and capability examinations and refers to Health Minister Decision 133/1983.
- Current Law 14/2025 Article 266 requires initial medical and capability examinations and a joint Labour/Health decision setting fitness/health/mental/psychological levels.
- No new joint Article266 decision was located.
- **Currentness hypothesis:** Decision153/2003 may continue under Enactment Article11 if not inconsistent; verify the old standards, subsequent amendments and whether a new joint decision has superseded it.

### B. Work injury forms and OSH units

- Decision 126/2003 addresses work injuries, serious accidents and disease statistics/forms.
- Decision 134/2003 addresses establishments required to create OSH units/committees; Decision232/2010 and Decision216/2016 appear in the official Ministry index as amendments/updates.
- New Law14 instruments 248/2026 (forms/notifications) and 249/2026 (OSH units/committees) are later instruments.
- **Required action:** check express repeal clauses and compare thresholds, annexes, report forms and transition clauses. Do not assume the entire old chain is either wholly valid or wholly repealed.

### C. OSH Council and vital establishments

- PM Decision985/2003 formed the Supreme OSH Council; the Ministry index identifies PM Decision3093/2010 as the later reconstitution/replacement.
- New Law14 Article264 requires a PM decision for the Supreme Council for OSH and Work Environment; no new PM decision was located in this pass.
- PM Decision982/2003 is listed as defining establishments related to national security. It may be relevant to the new Article234 vital-establishments/essential-services list, but the exact text and statutory fit must be confirmed.
- **Required action:** obtain PM Decisions3093/2010 and 982/2003, compare their lists/composition with new Articles234 and 264, and determine whether they continue under Article11.

### D. Workplace regulations, apprenticeship and training

- Decision185/2003 covers model workplace regulations/disciplinary sanctions; Decision162/2026 now creates a 66-article workplace-regulation framework.
- Apprenticeship chain: Decision175/2003 → Decision230/2007 → Decision414/2015 → Decision267/2025. Decision414/2015 is listed as replacing/withdrawing earlier apprenticeship rules; Decision267/2025 is the new Law14 instrument.
- Training-centre chain: Decisions150/2003 and 151/2003 → Decision80/2023 → Decision264/2025. Verify the exact repeal and any unaffected licensing subjects.
- The new OSH-specific training system is Decision252/2026 and must not be confused with general vocational-training centre rules.

### E. Women, child work, holidays and night inspection

- Women: Decision155/2003 and Decisions43/2021 and 44/2021 are predecessors to new Decision75/2026.
- Child work: Decision215/2021 is a predecessor to new Decision50/2026.
- Holidays: Decision112/2003 is a predecessor to Decisions294/2025 and 346/2025.
- Night inspection: Decision111/2003 is a predecessor to Decision320/2025.
- Preparatory/complementary work: Decision113/2003 is a predecessor to Decision292/2025.
- For each pair, check the later instrument's repeal clause and whether old provisions survive for topics not covered by the new decision.

### F. Recruitment, foreign workers and skills

- Recruitment agencies: Decision135/2003 and subsequent amendments → Decision272/2025.
- Foreign-worker permits: Decision136/2003 → Decision146/2019 → Decision279/2025.
- Skills/occupational measurement: Decision196/2003 → Decision111/2023 → Decision266/2025.
- Competent administrative authorities: Decision166/2003 and Decision116/2003 → Decision260/2025.
- **Required action:** compare all eligibility, fee, security, licence, reporting and exemption rules, and preserve historical effective periods.

### G. Fines, social services and funds

- Decisions122/2003, 128/2003 and 127/2014 address the disposition of fine proceeds. New Law14 Article14 changes the statutory allocation; the new allocation instrument has not been located.
- **Decision215/2003** (establishment-level minimum social/cultural services): three operative articles are now captured from a secondary transcription (see [text and preamble anomaly note](../legal_evidence/decision_215_2003_establishment_social_cultural_minimum_services_text.md)). The official Ministry PDF locator is identified but retrieval timed out; no original bytes/hash. The secondary page contains an unrelated licensing-committee preamble fragment that must be checked against Gazette page images. Currentness under Law14 Article272 and the Article11 transition rule remains unverified.
- **Decision217/2003** (national Fund service catalogue): complete three-article Arabic text was captured from a secondary-hosted Gazette reproduction, with an official Ministry PDF locator identified but not downloaded. Crucially, **Article 2 fell** as a consequence of Supreme Constitutional Court Case21/30 (issued 13 Dec 2014; published Gazette issue51 bis(C), 22 Dec 2014). The later court-text reproduction expressly records the fall of Decision217 Article2. Do not use its old service catalogue as operative; Article1/3 were not separately shown repealed in this pass. See [Decision217/2003 text and ruling note](../legal_evidence/decision_217_2003_social_health_cultural_services_full_text_and_2014_ruling.md) and [the separate Case21/30 ruling record](../legal_evidence/scc_case21_30_2014_labour_law12_article223_and_decisions216_217.md).
- **Decision214/2003** (four articles) and **Decision216/2003** (thirteen articles) now have secondary text captures: [Board composition](../legal_evidence/decision_214_2003_fund_board_composition_and_functions_text.md) and [financial/administrative bylaw](../legal_evidence/decision_216_2003_fund_financial_administrative_regulations_text.md). Decision16/2012 Article14 expressly repealed both; SCC Case21/30 (2014) additionally invalidated Decision216 Article2 item1 and Article7.
- **Decision16/2012** now has a complete 15-article secondary text capture ([full text and transition analysis](../legal_evidence/decision_16_2012_social_services_fund_board_full_text.md)). Article14 repeals Decisions214/216; Article2 refers to Decision217/2003 Article2, but that reference does not revive the fallen Article2. Articles8–9 contain the predecessor EGP8 annual minimum and quarterly-payment schedule; assess their survival against Law14 Article273 and Article11 transition. Law14 Articles272–274 provide the new statutory framework; no new PM Article273 Board/financial-administration decision was located.
- Decision162/2019 concerns irregular-worker financial/administrative arrangements; PM Decision2403/2026 reorganizes the Unorganized Workers Emergency Aid/Social/Health Fund. Compare for repeal/savings and Article80 Ministerial rules.

## 4. Do not confuse same-number decisions

The number **982** occurs in at least two unrelated decisions:
- PM Decision982/2003: establishments related to national security (legacy candidate relevant to Article234);
- PM Decision982/2026: temporary Sunday remote-work scheme for April 2026, later extended monthly through September.

Always key records by issuer + number + year + date, never by number alone.

## 5. Status definitions for the legacy register

- **potentially_continued_under_issuance_art11:** no clear successor located; may continue if not inconsistent, but legal currentness not confirmed.
- **newer_instrument_exists_repeal_check:** a later decision covers the same subject, but explicit repeal and surviving scope must be checked.
- **superseded_by_later_pm_decision / repealed_by_414_2015:** an identified later instrument expressly replaces or repeals the earlier one.
- **legacy_candidate_currentness_unverified:** the Ministry index lists the decision, but text and current status remain unverified.

The CSV is a research lead register, not a definitive statement that any legacy instrument remains in force.

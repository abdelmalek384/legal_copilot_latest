# Decisions 187, 194 and 214/2025 — expanded text acquisition

Research date: 17 November 2026  
Scope: three ministerial instruments in the first implementation wave of Labour Law 14/2025. This note separates signed-decision images, official Ministry/DSpace metadata, and secondary text reproductions. It is not a lawyer's opinion or a primary-byte verified corpus.

## 1. Decision 187/2025 — approval of employee resignations

**Arabic subject:** بشأن وضع آليات وضوابط لاعتماد الاستقالات العمالية من جانب مكاتب العمل.  
**Signed / effective:** 14 September 2025 (Article 9, as reproduced).  
**Official Ministry announcement:** 15 September 2025 — https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-ي-صدر-قرار-ا-وزاري-ا-يوضح-ضوابط-وآليات-اعتماد-الإستقالات-العمالية-طبق-ا-لقانون-العمل-الجديد/  
**Official SIS-hosted excerpt:** https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/111571/MOM20251001_1201.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=4  
**Full nine-article secondary transcription:** https://www.youm7.com/story/2025/9/16/تعرف-على-تفاصيل-قرار-وزير-العمل-لضبط-آليات-اعتماد-الاستقالات-العمالية/7122463  
**Source class:** Ministry announcement and SIS excerpt are official government sources; the detailed all-article transcription is a secondary report. The signed Gazette original, issue/page and PDF hash have not been located.

### Article map (secondary transcription)

- **Art. 1 — submission and registration:** resignation is written, signed by the worker or a special attorney, and approved by a Labour Relations Office at the directorate having jurisdiction over the employer's head office or a branch, or by the directorate. The office registers a serial number in the paper/electronic register, stamps and dates it, writes the serial on the resignation and delivers it to the worker. Directorates send monthly statistics to the Ministry's central Labour Relations administration.
- **Art. 2 — evidentiary effect of stamp:** the Labour Relations Office stamp is accepted by the employer and in official transactions; the worker may request the Republic seal.
- **Art. 3 — identity and no employer submission:** the office checks the applicant's national ID. The resignation must identify employer and worker and bear the worker's personal signature; if a special attorney signs, the power-of-attorney details are entered in the resignation and register. Employer or its representative may not submit the resignation.
- **Art. 4 — when service ends:** service ends upon the employer's acceptance or expiry of ten days from submission, as applicable. Worker remains at work until the employer decides or the ten-day period expires. The employer must decide within the statutory period; otherwise it is deemed accepted by operation of law.
- **Art. 5 — withdrawal:** the worker or special attorney may withdraw in writing within ten days after notification that the employer accepted the resignation. Withdrawal must be approved by the same Labour Relations Office; the resignation is then treated as if it never existed.
- **Art. 6 — mutual termination excluded:** the approval rules do not apply to consensual termination (مخالصة / تقايل), whether the contract is fixed or indefinite, provided the agreement states that the worker received all financial entitlements.
- **Art. 7 — free will and settlement:** resignation or mutual-termination agreement must reflect the worker's free will, without coercion or compulsion; employer must settle worker rights and financial entitlements.
- **Art. 8 — service certificate and return of documents:** employer must issue the certificate described in Law 14/2025 Article 175 within 15 days of request and return deposited papers, certificates, tools and clearance documents at termination upon request.
- **Art. 9 — commencement/conflict:** applies from 14 September 2025; competent authorities implement it; conflicting prior text is repealed.

### Product/legal-data mapping

- Link to Labour Law 14/2025 Arts. 167 (resignation mechanics) and 175 (service certificate / return of documents), subject to a direct article-text check against the official Law 14 Gazette PDF.
- Model separate events: worker submission, Labour Relations Office approval/serial, employer acceptance, ten-day statutory deemed acceptance, withdrawal notice and ten-day withdrawal period, settlement of dues, certificate request/15-day delivery.
- Do not infer that a mutually agreed termination is a resignation; Article 6 distinguishes them.
- Do not represent the secondary article map as a verified official Gazette text.

## 2. Decision 194/2025 — temporary regularization of foreign workers in small establishments

**Arabic title:** قرار رقم 194 لسنة 2025 بشأن تسهيل قواعد وإجراءات تصحيح أوضاع العمالة الأجنبية في المنشآت الصغيرة داخل مصر.  
**Signed / effective date shown on image:** 18 September 2025 (Article 6).  
**EastLaws metadata locator:** https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/labor/18-09-2025/no-194?id=4749539&type=1  
**Secondary explanatory report with images:** https://www.parlmany.com/News/2/585659/عمل-الأجانب-فى-مصر-هل-يخضع-عملهم-للقانون-الجديد-وهل  
**Decision page images:**  
- Page 1: https://img.parlmany.com/ArticleImgs/2025/9/27/73687-%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A8-3.jpg  
- Page 2: https://img.parlmany.com/ArticleImgs/2025/9/27/90644-%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A8-4.jpg  
**Source class:** the two images reproduce a signed Ministry decision; EastLaws provides metadata/snippets and Parlmany provides contextual reporting. No Gazette issue/page or official Ministry-hosted PDF was located in this pass.

### Article map (working transcription from the two signed-decision images)

- **Art. 1 — limited initiative:** authorizes the General Administration for Foreign Work Licences, for three months from issuance, to receive applications from foreign nationals of all nationalities for temporary work permits in restaurants, public shops and sole-proprietorship establishments. The permit may be issued for up to one year.
- **Art. 2 — application:** sets the application route and criteria for a foreign national or legal representative to apply during the initiative period. The image should be re-read at high resolution before encoding every required document or eligibility condition; do not supplement from the separate Instruction 20/2025.
- **Art. 3 — temporary exemptions:** exempts covered foreigners from specified ordinary foreign-work-permit and recruitment conditions under Labour Law 14/2025 during the initiative. The signed image also states that covered establishments are exempt during the initiative from the ordinary maximum foreign-worker-to-national-worker ratio. The exact scope must be compared with the signed image and later general Decision 279/2025.
- **Art. 4 — permit duration/fee:** the first year is calculated from the permit's issuance date, not the foreigner's entry date. Permit is issued for one year or less according to the application after payment of EGP 3,000 per year.
- **Art. 5 — cancellation:** cancellation grounds include a conviction in a felony or offence affecting honour/trust/public morals; proof that the permit was obtained on incorrect data; use in a profession or workplace other than that for which it was issued; and objection by competent security authorities for reasons connected with the country's general interest.
- **Art. 6 — commencement/conflict:** effective from issuance on 18 September 2025; conflicting prior provisions are repealed.

### Time/currentness warning

The initiative's application window was three months from 18 September 2025 and is historical. Do not present Decision 194/2025 as an open regularization route in 2026. Decision 279/2025 later establishes a broad foreign-worker permit framework and its Article 39 repeals conflicting prior text; determine the exact effect on Decision 194 only by comparing the signed texts. Do not assume the 2026 extension Decision 2652/2026 (which concerns licensed recruitment agencies' regularization under Law Article 6) extended this separate small-establishment foreign-worker initiative.

## 3. Ministry Instruction 20/2025 — implementation of Decision 194

**Arabic heading visible on page:** تعليمات رقم 20 لسنة 2025 — تراخيص أجانب.  
**Issuer shown:** General Administration for Foreign Work Licences / Ministry of Labour (exact signing authority/date need confirmation).  
**Image:** https://img.parlmany.com/ArticleImgs/2025/9/27/46690-%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A8-6.jpg  
**Source class:** one-page image of a Ministry instruction reproduced by Parlmany; no official Ministry-hosted original or separate publication record acquired.

The instruction directs covered applications to the competent Labour Directorate according to territorial jurisdiction without referral to the central General Administration for Foreign Work Licences, and lists the following supporting documents:
1. Original commercial register or establishment/activity licence for inspection plus a copy.
2. Original establishment tax card plus a copy.
3. Original foreigner's passport plus a copy.
4. Certificate that the foreigner is free of HIV/AIDS.
5. One completed Form 1 for work licences.
6. Six completed copies of Form 3 for work licences.
7. Eight personal photographs of the foreign worker.

The page describes the three-month regularization window as applying to foreign workers in restaurants, public shops and sole-proprietorship establishments. Preserve this as operational implementation evidence, not a separate ministerial decision or a current application route.

## 4. Decision 214/2025 — employment-contract definition, writing and deposit

**Arabic title:** قرار رقم 214 لسنة 2025 بشأن عقد العمل وشروطه وضوابطه.  
**Signed:** 5 October 2025.  
**Published:** Egyptian Gazette / الوقائع المصرية, supplement issue 249, 6 November 2025 (official DSpace catalogue metadata).  
**Effective:** day after publication under Article 9 (7 November 2025, subject to Gazette page verification).  
**Official DSpace item:** https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/8ebcec10-0c45-4fbf-9a56-44ca409dc6c4  
**Official issue 249 bundle metadata:** https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/9922b3e7-f8f3-40c3-b808-a00258c49cb6/full  
**Full nine-article secondary transcription:** https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/01/214-2025-4.html?m=0  
**Source class:** official DSpace catalogue metadata identifies Ministry authorship and Gazette issue; full article text is from a secondary transcription. DSpace original file is listed but rights/access must be checked before acquisition or copying.

### Article map (secondary transcription)

- **Art. 1 — definition:** defines the employment contract as the contract under which a worker undertakes to work for an employer under the employer's management or supervision for remuneration.
- **Art. 2 — four copies:** employer prepares the Arabic written contract in four copies: employer's service file, worker, competent social-insurance office and competent administrative authority.
- **Art. 3 — depositing the fourth copy:** the fourth copy is lodged with the competent Labour Directorate physically or electronically using its official email. The submission comes from the company's approved official email and includes certified commercial/industrial registration and tax card, employer/board-chair ID or passport, company and workplace data, insurance number, number of contracts, a stamped worker schedule in PDF and Excel, and each contract as a separate PDF named for the worker and national ID.
- **Art. 4 — renewals and endings:** the same deposit method applies to renewal of fixed-term contracts or evidence of renewal. The directorate must also be notified of non-renewal or termination for any reason within 15 days of renewal, non-renewal or termination.
- **Art. 5 — transition for pre-law contracts:** no deposit under Article 3 is required for indefinite-term contracts made before the Law's effective date (1 September 2025). Fixed-term contracts made before that date must be deposited on their first renewal.
- **Art. 6 — mandatory particulars:** start date; employer name/workplace address; worker name, qualification, profession/craft, insurance number, residence and identity particulars; nature/type of work; agreed wage, payment method/date and other cash/in-kind benefits.
- **Art. 7 — foreign worker language:** where a foreign worker does not know Arabic, contract is made in Arabic and the worker's language, or Arabic and English; Arabic controls in case of interpretive conflict.
- **Art. 8 — advice and guidance:** Ministry and directorates provide advice, when requested, to distinguish employment from other legal arrangements and assess whether work is performed for an employer or on a self-employed basis, considering performance method, wage periodicity, subordination/supervision and other indicators; this does not prejudice labour-court jurisdiction.
- **Art. 9 — publication/effect:** publication in Egyptian Gazette, commencement the day after publication, implementation by competent authorities and repeal of conflicting prior provisions.

### Important source discrepancy

The official DSpace catalogue identifies the instrument and issue 249. The full secondary transcription's preamble cites Law 14/2025. An earlier scan-derived note in the repository reported the preamble as citing “Law 4/2025”. Preserve this discrepancy until the actual Gazette image is visually inspected; do not silently normalize the citation.

### Product/data implications

- Contract filing should be represented as a workflow with separate copy recipients, fourth-copy submission, company email identity, PDF/Excel schedule, and a 15-day change-notification obligation.
- Model the transition rule separately for pre-1 September 2025 indefinite-term contracts and fixed-term contracts renewed after that date.
- Keep the worker-language rule distinct from the mandatory Arabic contract requirement.
- Do not treat the 15-day notice in Article 4 as a general limitation period for all employment-contract claims.

## 5. Cross-instrument links

- Decision 187/2025 ↔ Law 14/2025 Arts. 167 (resignation mechanics) and 175 (service certificate / return of documents), subject to a direct article-text check against the official Law 14 Gazette PDF.
- Decision 194/2025 ↔ Law 14/2025 Arts. 70–74 (foreign-worker scope, permit, employer reporting, investment-related licensing and repatriation), and later Decision 279/2025 (general work-permit framework).
- Instruction 20/2025 ↔ Decision 194/2025 only; it is implementation guidance, not a freestanding law.
- Decision 214/2025 ↔ Law 14/2025 Arts. 86–89 (employment contract, written form, copies, mandatory particulars) and Article 11 promulgation transition.

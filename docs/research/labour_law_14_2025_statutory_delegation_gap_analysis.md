# Labour Law 14/2025 — Statutory Delegation Gap Analysis

Research date: 17 November 2026  
Primary text: Official Amiri Press searchable Law 14/2025, Gazette issue 18 supplement, 3 May 2025.  
Structured companion: data/research/labour_law_14_2025_delegation_matrix_2026-11-17.csv

## 1. Why this matrix matters

The Minister of Labour announced an implementation package of approximately 87 decisions, including 68 from the Ministry of Labour, while other contemporary reports quoted 62 Ministry decisions. On 14 November 2026 the Ministry said the executive decisions had been completed but did not publish an itemized list.

A title-based harvest cannot establish completeness. The law itself delegates dozens of distinct matters to the Minister of Labour, the Prime Minister, the Minister of Justice, the Minister responsible for health and joint authorities. The matrix therefore maps explicit delegations article-by-article and separates:
- instrument located and text extracted;
- instrument located but Gazette match/annex review pending;
- partial mapping;
- no instrument located;
- Cabinet-approved draft where the final signed instrument has not been found.

## 2. Newly identified critical gaps

### A. Prime Minister decisions not yet located

1. **Article 18 — Higher Council for Human Resource Skills.** Cabinet approved a draft on 28 October 2026 (see [draft extraction](cabinet_draft_human_resource_skills_council_2026-10-28.md)). The official Gazette issue 44 bis (A) lists Decision 3356/2026 for the Training Fund, but no final PM decision establishing the Human Resource Skills Council was found by 17 November. The Council's final constitution, powers, operating rules and executive secretariat remain a specific gap.
2. **Article 32 — Higher Council for Planning and Operating Domestic/Overseas Workforce.** The Law requires a PM decision within six months. No final decision number or Gazette text was located in this pass.
3. **Article 234 — vital establishments and essential services where strike is prohibited.** No PM decision identifying the covered facilities/services was located.
4. **Article 237 — economic closure/reduction committees.** No PM decision was located for the two committees, their composition, powers, representation, procedures and grievance timetable.
5. **Articles 273–274 — Social, Health and Cultural Services Fund.** This is a distinct Fund for workers in public/public-business/private establishments. No separate PM Board/baseline regulation was located. Do not confuse it with:
   - PM Decision 2403/2026: Unorganized Workers Emergency Aid/Social/Health Services Fund; or
   - PM Decision 3356/2026: Training and Qualification Fund.

### B. Ministry of Labour decisions or joint rules not yet located / partially located

1. **Article 14:** allocation and spending of one-third of fines collected under the Law.
2. **Article 80:** Ministerial financial/administrative rules, revenue collection, services and emergency-aid eligibility/payment rules for the Unorganized Workers Fund established by Article 78 and PM Decision 2403/2026.
3. **Article 245:** optional licensing of OSH/work-environment compliance offices (legal form, staffing, licence terms and fee up to EGP 100,000); no decision located. Decision 251/2026 licenses OSH experts/consultants and is not the same category.
4. **Article 254:** Decision 162/2026 partially requires workplace bylaws to define conduct, prohibit harassment/bullying/violence and set complaint/investigation mechanisms; the separate ministerial model code, model complaint forms and standard settlement procedures required by Article 254 remain unlocated.
5. **Article 263:** National OSH Research Centre plans, studies and operating procedures.
6. **Article 265:** governorate-level subcommittees of the Supreme OSH Council.
7. **Article 266:** joint Labour/Health standards for medical fitness and physical, mental and psychological capability examinations.
8. **Article 270:** joint Labour/Health decision setting procedures/standards for medical-treatment, medicine and hospital-accommodation expenses; no decision located.
9. **Article 271:** joint decision defining remote areas, worker-housing standards, food types/quantities and employer contributions; Decision 49/2026 only partially identifies remote areas for extra annual leave and does not establish the full housing/food regime.
10. **Article 272:** minimum social and cultural services for establishments with 50 or more workers.
11. **Article 276:** Decision 320/2025 supplies night/out-of-hours inspection triggers, reporting and statistics; Article 6 still defers the actual allowance amounts/controls to a separate authority decision not located.
12. **Article 121:** Decisions 291/2025 and 292/2025 supply overtime grounds/rates and category-specific caps, but Decision 291 delegates annual sectoral overtime maxima to the Supreme Council for Social Dialogue; that follow-up decision was not located.
13. **Article 100:** no general new-work-pattern decision located. The Ministry was still discussing remote, flexible, freelance and platform-work frameworks on 26 September 2026; PM Decision 982 is only a temporary Sunday scheme and Decision 162 only requires internal bylaw clauses.
14. **Article 3 of PM Decision 3356/2026:** separate Ministerial decision for full exemption from the Training Fund contribution where an establishment trains its workers according to its needs/approved bylaws.
15. **Article 3 of Ministerial Decision 269/2025:** the labour-market information/occupational-classification procedural manual due within three months; the manual was not located.

### C. Ministry of Justice / joint inspector instruments

- **Article 275:** Minister of Justice, in agreement with the Minister responsible for labour, designates officers with judicial-police powers for labour-law enforcement. Decisions 4621, 4693 and 5614/2025 concern court locations/legal aid and do not, by themselves, satisfy Article 275. No specific joint designation decision was located.

## 3. Areas with instruments identified but requiring exact article mapping or Gazette comparison

- **Arts.9, 13, 20, 27, 28, 38, 40–44, 48, 51–52, 71–72, 101–102, 129, 137, 215, 239, 255, 259–262:** corresponding decisions are identified in the register, but each must be checked against its exact delegation, all annexes, commencement clauses and amendments.
- **Arts.23–26:** Decision 264/2025 and OSH-specific Decision 252/2026 address different training regimes; do not treat them as interchangeable.
- **Art.254:** Decision 162/2026 contains workplace-bylaw requirements on conduct and complaint/investigation processes, but it does not yet establish that the separate Article 254 Ministry model code and standard procedure have been issued.
- **Art.269:** the Law itself imposes daily workplace inspection, complaint review and periodic health-check duties; no separate implementing decision is specified in the article, but the required registers/health-insurance procedures need operational source review.
- **Arts.33–37:** Decisions 270 and 271/2025 are only partially cross-mapped to registration/disability data and require exact article-level mapping.
- **Art.53:** Decision 75/2026 is the women's prohibited-work/maternity/OSH instrument; verify the complete Article 3 risk schedule and signed Gazette text.
- **Art.59:** Decision 162/2026 partially addresses workplace rules and flexible/remote work, but a specific women-work system for carers of children with disabilities/dwarfs and the general Article 100 framework remain to be verified.
- **Art.60:** Decision 48/2026 addresses nursery/day-care arrangements; verify the complete thresholds, shared-nursery and cost-alternative controls against its signed Gazette text.
- **Art.124:** Decision 49/2026 identifies hazardous/arduous work and remote areas qualifying for seven additional annual-leave days; verify all listed occupations and the exposure criteria.
- **Arts.117–120:** Decisions 288–290/2025 cover continuous/arduous work, industrial hours and intermittent work; exact provision-to-decision mapping and Gazette schedules remain to be checked.
- **Art.121:** Decision 291/2025 sets overtime grounds/rates, but the annual sectoral overtime maxima delegated to the Supreme Council for Social Dialogue were not located.
- **Art.123:** Decision 292/2025 sets maximum actual/overtime hours for preparatory/complementary, guarding and cleaning work; Gazette schedule comparison remains pending.
- **Art.150:** Decision 299/2025 appears related to individual labour-dispute settlement; confirm the exact delegation and procedure. Decision 300/2025 is the collective-dispute conciliation instrument under Article 215.
- **Women's-work chapter:** Decision 75/2026 is mapped to maternity/night-work protections, but its exact statutory article crosswalk and complete Article 3 hazard schedule still require Gazette verification.
- **Child-work chapter:** Decision 50/2026 is the child-employment instrument; compare all occupation annexes, age bands, hours and medical controls against the Gazette.
- **Arts.259–262:** Decisions 248–252/2026 cover OSH forms, units/committees, standards, consultants and training; original Gazette comparison remains incomplete.

## 5. Legal-data rules

- Never infer that an instrument exists merely because the statute delegates a subject; mark it not located until a source is found.
- Never mark a delegation satisfied by a decision on an adjacent topic without an article-level match.
- Preserve exact issuing authority, statutory article, instrument number, date, Gazette issue, commencement, repeal and amendment history.
- Keep the source tier distinct from legal status: an official indexed text is not the same as a certified Gazette copy; a secondary transcription is not a primary-source match.
- No item in this matrix is lawyer-reviewed or rights-cleared. Do not promote to production legal data until source comparison and Egyptian lawyer sign-off.

## 17 November 2026 follow-up — Prime Minister fund/council delegations (historical snapshot; superseded where noted below)

- **Article18 / Higher Council for Human Resource Skills:** Cabinet approved a *draft* PM decision on 28 October 2026 (official Cabinet meeting 11912: https://cabinet.gov.eg/Meeting/Details/11912). The draft summary specifies a four-year membership term, quarterly meetings, ministerial/employer/worker representation, disability-council representation, skills policy, education/training alignment and a national skills programme. The Official Gazette 2026 collection's visible issues through 12 November (issues 44, 44 bis A, 45 and 46) did not surface a final PM decision for this Council; no final number is assigned. Law Article18 required the PM decision within six months of the Law's commencement. With the general commencement candidate of 1 September 2025, the six-month date is 1 March 2026; the Cabinet draft approval is later. This is a timeline lead for counsel, not a finding about legal validity or compliance.
- **Article20 / Training and Qualification Fund:** PM Decision3356/2026 was published in Official Gazette issue44 bis(A) on 1 November 2026 and takes effect the next day under Article18 of the secondary text. It maps the Fund's 18-article governance and contribution framework. It expressly repeals PM Decision1543/2003 by Article17. The separate Ministerial full-exemption decision under PM Decision3356 Article3 remains unlocated.
- **Articles78–80 / Unorganized Workers Fund:** PM Decision2403/2026 supplies the Fund's 16-article framework. The Ministry's 2 November 2026 report confirms that its second Board meeting was still preparing the Fund's financial/administrative regulations, revenue collection, service rules and detailed emergency-benefit eligibility/amount/duration/suspension/payment rules. The Article80 Ministerial rules and final operational schedules remain unlocated.
- **Articles273–274 / Social, Health and Cultural Services Fund:** remains a distinct statutory Fund. Do not confuse it with Decision2403 (unorganized workers) or Decision3356 (training and qualification); no separate PM Board/baseline regulation was found in this pass.


## 17 November 2026 follow-up — Social, Health and Cultural Services Fund (Law Arts.272–274)

A new historical-instrument chain was recovered:

- **Decision215/2003:** Official Ministry index and direct PDF locator found: https://www.labour.gov.eg/media/yhjp3rzk/2003-215-الحد-الادنى-للخدمات-الاجتماعية-والثقافية-على-مستوى-المنشأة.pdf. Retrieval timed out on 24 Nov 2026; full text, amendments and currentness remain outstanding. Law14 Article272 now applies to establishments with 50+ workers and directs the Minister to set minimum services after consultation with employer/worker organizations. Do not assume the 2003 instrument is fully current.
- **Decision217/2003:** Complete three-article Arabic text captured from a secondary-hosted Gazette reproduction (issue227 supplement(A), 5 Oct 2003, pp.61–62); official Ministry PDF locator found but retrieval timed out. Article2 **fell** under Supreme Constitutional Court Case21/30 (13 Dec 2014; published Gazette issue51 bis(C), 22 Dec 2014); a later court-text reproduction expressly records the fall of Article2. Do not use the old service catalogue as operative. Article1/3 were not separately shown repealed in this pass; Law14 Article274 now lists the current statutory service categories. See [Decision217 text and 2014 ruling](../legal_evidence/decision_217_2003_social_health_cultural_services_full_text_and_2014_ruling.md).
- **Decision214/2003:** old Board-formation decision. Ministerial Decision16/2012 Article14 expressly repeals it.
- **Decision216/2003:** old financial/administrative bylaw. Ministerial Decision16/2012 Article14 expressly repeals it.
- **Decision310/2009:** secondary PDF says it amended Article2(1) of Decision216/2003; because 216/2003 was expressly repealed in 2012, treat 310/2009 as historical derivative pending counsel review.
- **Ministerial Decision16/2012:** full 15-article secondary text is available at https://www.laweg.net/framePlain.aspx?ItemID=76003&NID=93978&Type=6&action=ViewActivePages. It was published in Egyptian Gazette issue43 on 22 February 2012; Article14 repeals 214/2003 and216/2003; Article2 adopts Decision217/2003 services; Articles8–9 set the old contribution at at least EGP8 per worker for establishments with 20+ and quarterly payment.
- **Current gap:** Law14 Article273 now requires a Prime Minister decision to form the Fund Board, specify its powers/system and accounting rules; it also specifies EGP8–16 per worker/year for establishments with 20+ and permits deductions where the employer supplies services or a better benefit. No new PM Article273 decision was found in this pass. The old Ministerial Decision16/2012's composition/payment rules must not be assumed to satisfy the new PM-level delegation.

**Source hierarchy:** Decision16/2012 is a secondary transcription at Laweg, not a retrieved Gazette binary. The Ministry's current legislation index is an official locator for Decisions215/2003 and217/2003 but did not expose their full texts in this pass. No instrument is lawyer-reviewed or promoted to verified.


## 21 November 2026 update — Article 18/20 instruments located and source status improved

### Article 18 — Supreme Council for Human Resource Skills Development

The former “Cabinet-approved draft only” gap is closed at the instrument-identification level. **PM Decision 3561/2026** was published in Official Gazette issue 46 bis (C), 15 November 2026 and took effect 16 November 2026. Official Gazette repository metadata: https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/cb156f2d-a809-4480-ada2-48df74251c68 . The original 274.86 KB PDF is listed but direct access returned request-a-copy/502. Secondary text provides Article 1 membership, Article 2 functions and the commencement rule; see the 18 November acquisition tranche: [Decision 3561/2026 source record](../legal_evidence/acquisition_tranche_finance634_and_hr_skills3561_2026-11-18.md).

Decision 3561/2026 also expressly repeals PM Decision 705/2014 (predecessor National Council for Human Resource Development), according to secondary reproductions: https://www.iqraa.news/233873/ . The legacy transition register now records 705/2014 as repealed by 3561/2026, pending primary Gazette page-image verification. The original 705/2014 remains historical, not current authority.

**Status:** delegation instrument located; Gazette metadata source-matched; full text not yet primary-text-matched; lawyer review=0.

### Article 20 — Training and Qualification Fund

PM Decision 3356/2026 was already identified in the earlier register; this pass upgrades its text acquisition. Full 18-article Gazette facsimile, with printed pages 10–18, is available here: https://lawhub.info/eg/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/lawhub.info-eg-dec-3356-2026-.pdf . Gazette issue 44 bis (A), 1 November 2026; effective 2 November. Article 2 sets the 0.25% minimum-insured-wage contribution for establishments with 30+ workers, with annual per-worker bounds EGP10–30. Article 17 repeals PM Decision1543/2003. Article 3 delegates the full-exemption conditions to a further Minister of Labour decision; that ministerial instrument remains missing. Full acquisition and exact Arabic excerpt: [3356/2026 text acquisition](../legal_evidence/labour_pm_decisions_3356_3561_2026_arabic_text_acquisition_2026-11-21.md).

**Critical reconciliation:** do not assume the historic 1% contribution remains collectible. Reconcile Enactment Article2 of Law14/2025, substantive Article20, Decision3356 Articles2–3 and the historical litigation savings clause; obtain the Article3 ministerial exemption/collection decision.

### Remaining Article 18/20-related gaps

- Article 18: original Gazette PDF and complete Article 1–10 exact transcription for Decision3561/2026; confirm whether any transitional provision preserves selected 705/2014 arrangements pending council formation.
- Article 20: Minister of Labour decision under PM Decision3356 Article3 (full exemption conditions); implementation forms, payment instructions and fund bank/accounting controls.
- Article 18's national council is distinct from the National Council for Wages and the Supreme Council for Workforce Planning and Employment. Preserve separate entity IDs and cross-references.


### Article 21 — Training Fund contribution / exemption

Law14 Article21 itself fixes the 0.25% contribution formula for establishments with 30 or more workers, with a minimum EGP10 and maximum EGP30 per worker annually. PM Decision3356/2026 Article2 repeats the statutory resource rule; it is not the source of the rate. PM Decision3356 Article3 delegates only the full-exemption rules to a separate Minister of Labour decision. That Ministerial exemption decision remains unlocated. The delegation matrix now contains a separate Article21 row to prevent conflating the Article20 Fund-governance delegation with Article21 contribution/exemption rules.

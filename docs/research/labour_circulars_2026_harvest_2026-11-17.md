## Circular No. 9/2026 — paid Eid al-Fitr holiday for private-sector workers

- **Document:** كتاب دوري رقم 9 لسنة 2026 بشأن منح العاملين المخاطبين بأحكام قانون العمل رقم 14 لسنة 2025 إجازة رسمية مدفوعة الأجر بمناسبة عيد الفطر المبارك.
- **Issued/announced:** 12 March 2026.
- **Official DSpace catalogue:** https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/7432d91b-492d-4d5f-b526-fdfe72fcc8af
- **Official SIS Ministry record:** https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/handle/123456789/123176
- **Official Ministry announcement:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/لتوحيد-مواعيد-الإجازات-الرسمية-لكافة-العاملين-بقطاعات-الدولة-المتنوعة-وزير-العمل-من-19-إلى-23-مارس-إجازة-رسمية-مدفوعة-الأجر-للعاملين-بالقطاع-الخاص-بمناسبة-عيد-الفطر-المبارك-تشغيل-العامل-في-الإجازة-جائز-عند-الضرورة-مع-منحه-أجر-ا-مضاعف-ا-أو-ي/
- **SIS source summary:** official Ministry circular dated 12 March 2026, public record; PDF listed as MOM20260312_1105.pdf. DSpace item lists English and Arabic PDF files, but the files are restricted and the download route requires a request-a-copy form.
- **One-time application:** paid official holiday for workers governed by Labour Law 14/2025 from Thursday 19 March through Monday 23 March 2026.
- **Work during the holiday:** the employer may require work if business circumstances require it; the worker receives double the wage for that day or a substitute day, under the Labour Law.
- **Legal basis:** Ministry announcement says the circular implements Labour Law Article 129 and aligns the private-sector holiday with Prime Minister Decree 785/2026 for public-sector employees.
- **Distribution:** the circular was circulated to Labour Directorates to publish at workplaces and production sites and monitor implementation.

**Product/data handling:** This is a date-specific circular, not a recurring annual holiday rule.

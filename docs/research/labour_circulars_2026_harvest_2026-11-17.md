## Circular No. 16/2025 — paid Mawlid holiday for private-sector workers

- DSpace record: https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/4c13c82a-3466-4f1e-9a86-2c69b9997c93
- DSpace metadata says issued 1 September 2025. The circular grants a paid holiday on Thursday 4 September 2025 (12 Rabi' al-Awwal 1447 AH) to employees governed by Labour Law 14/2025 for Mawlid.
- Employer may require work if business necessity requires it; the worker receives double pay in addition to the ordinary wage or another day off in lieu, based on a written request placed in the worker's file.
- Ministry/Directorate heads were instructed to circulate the circular at workplaces and production sites and monitor implementation.
- DSpace file links are listed; access/rights must be checked. This is a one-date historical circular, not a recurring holiday date rule.

## Circular No. 19/2025 — paid Armed Forces Day holiday for private-sector workers

- DSpace full metadata/text: https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/c2de87b6-9f5a-4851-a1e4-fa277cb73d09/full
- Issued 5 October 2025. It implements Labour Law Article 129 and the transitional rule in Article 11 of the enactment provisions. It grants private-sector employees covered by Law 14/2025 a paid holiday on Thursday 9 October 2025 instead of Monday 6 October, aligning with Prime Minister Decree 3445/2025 for public-sector employees.
- Employer may require work if operating conditions require it; the worker is entitled to double wage plus regular wage for the day and another day off in lieu, based on a written request to be kept in the worker's file.
- Ministry central departments and Labour Directorate heads were instructed to disseminate and implement the circular.
- The DSpace record includes Arabic/English PDF files and an item-specific license. This is a historical one-time circular; do not treat it as a standing rule for future 6 October dates.

## 1. Decision 248/2026 — serious accidents, occupational disease and injury notifications/statistics

- **Gazette:** Egyptian Gazette no. 225 supplement (A), 2026-10-12.
- **Text inspected:** https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/10/248-2026.html
- **Official Ministry announcement:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-يصدر-5-قرارات-تنفيذية-لتعزيز-منظومة-السلامة-والصحة-المهنية-وتأمين-بيئة-العمل/
- **Structure:** 10 articles plus forms.
- **Article 2 — serious accident definition:** includes death; expected permanent disability of at least 35%; third-degree or more severe burns; more than one person injured at the same workplace at the same time; fires, collapses, explosions or hazardous-material leaks that stop work for at least one shift; and certain transport/delivery-road accidents causing the listed human losses.
- **Article 3:** applies broadly to workplaces, establishments, branches, land/sea/air workplaces and transport.
- **Article 4:** serious accidents must be notified to the competent authority within 24 hours if inside the establishment/branch/worksite, and 48 hours if outside those boundaries. Occupational disease notification is due within 24 hours after receipt of the health-insurance authority report confirming the occupational disease.
- **Article 5:** employer must provide technical findings from relevant authorities (e.g. Interior/Health) on causes and final human/material losses when available.
- **Article 6:** establishments with 30 or more workers must submit semiannual statistics on ordinary/chronic/occupational diseases, serious accidents and work injuries during the first half of July and January; requested certified copies are due within 15 days.
- **Article 7:** requires paper/electronic registers maintained contemporaneously and supervised by the establishment OSH unit for at least three years; knowingly false statistical information can be actionable even after the submission period.
- **Articles 8–9:** statistical analysis must support risk assessment, prevention, OSH policy and central research/planning.
- **Article 10:** effective the day after Gazette publication (2026-10-13, subject to original Gazette confirmation).

**Implementation data requirements:** incident type, location, date/time, injury/death count, disability estimate, notification deadline (24h/48h), occupational-disease diagnosis report receipt timestamp, form number, authority notified, evidence and follow-up. The product should calculate reminders only after the employer's legal classification and applicable exceptions are confirmed.

## 2. Decision 249/2026 — OSH functional units and committees

- **Gazette:** no. 225 supplement (A), 2026-10-12 (according to Ministry/secondary sources).
- **Title:** identifies establishments/branches required to establish OSH functional units and committees.
- **Legal basis identified in secondary analysis:** Labour Law 14/2025, Art. 259; older Ministerial Decisions 232/2010 and 216/2016 are related predecessor instruments and require a repeal/savings analysis.
- **Sources:** Ministry announcement above; metadata/text listing https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=19705; explanatory record https://www.mohamedabdelfattah.com/library/d/decree-2026-249
- **Subject leads:** risk-tier classification (high/medium/limited); thresholds for establishing OSH units/committees; safe-driving requirements; protection against retaliation for OSH personnel; committee meetings; inspection and recordkeeping.
- **Secondary report states:** committees meet monthly in high/medium risk establishments and every two months in limited-risk establishments; an additional meeting is required within 48 hours of a serious accident or confirmed occupational disease. This must be checked against the full signed text.
- **Status:** title and scope identified; complete text and exact thresholds not yet extracted.

## 3. Decision 250/2026 — workplace safety limits and risk-prevention standards

- **Gazette:** no. 225 supplement (A), 2026-10-12.
- **Text inspected:** https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/10/250-2026.html
- **Title:** rules, standards and safety limits in workplaces, with requirements and precautions to prevent all types of risks.
- **Structure:** extensive instrument with three major parts, numerous articles, quantitative tables and mandatory workplace records. Secondary transcription's contents index places main text around pages 48–182, safety limits/tables around pages 184–360, and records around pages 361–364.
- **Legal/international references in preamble:** Law 14/2025; laws on civil defence, ionizing radiation, agriculture, fire equipment, boilers, mines/quarries, environment, child protection, building, nuclear/radiological activities, food safety, universal health insurance, disability rights, social insurance, public shops, waste management and hotel/tourism establishments; ILO Conventions 115, 139, 148, 155 and 187; Arab Labour Conventions 13/1981, 6/1976, 16/1983 and 19/1998; Maritime Labour Convention 2006; Egyptian Fire Code.
- **Contents map extracted from the transcription:**
  - Part I: definitions/general rules; worksite construction; fire prevention; risk assessment; emergency plans; OSH in agriculture.
  - Part II: physical hazards (heat stress, cold stress, noise, mechanical vibration, lighting, ionizing radiation, laser, non-ionizing/infrared/ultraviolet radiation, explosions/pyrotechnics); engineering hazards (construction, excavation/tunnels, demolition, scaffolding, work at height, machinery, mobile equipment, hot work, hand tools, cranes/hoists, lifting accessories, ladders, platforms, materials handling/storage, mining/quarrying, diving/underwater work, ships/floating facilities); electrical hazards; ergonomics; biological hazards; chemical hazards; indirect hazards (rescue, first aid, medical examinations, hygiene, housekeeping, PPE); confined spaces; work permits; lockout/tagout; delivery motorcycles; workplace violence/harassment/bullying.
  - Part III: numerical safety limits and chemical exposure tables, including heat, workload, clothing adjustment, wind chill, work/rest schedules, continuous/intermittent noise, noise limits by work setting, hand-arm and whole-body vibration, lighting, ionizing radiation, laser, infrared/UV, magnetic/electric fields, safe approach distances, chemical threshold limits, carcinogenicity classes, biological exposure indicators, and threshold quantities of hazardous materials.
  - Registers: equipment/machinery maintenance; lifting equipment inspections/tests; measuring-device calibration; hazardous/toxic chemicals and waste.
- **Examples from text:** noise controls require periodic measurement and documentation, engineering controls, maintenance, exposure-time management and PPE where needed. The transcription's continuous-noise table lists 85 dB(A) for 8 hours, 88 dB(A) for 4 hours, 91 dB(A) for 2 hours, etc.; the exact table and its measurement conditions must be checked against the original before use.
- **Status:** broad full transcription inspected, but no original PDF hash, exact Gazette pages or lawyer review. Do not use numerical exposure limits in answers or compliance automation until the original tables are verified.

## 6. Decision 264/2025 — training operations and levels

- **Decision date:** 2025-12-04.
- **Gazette:** no. 291 supplement, 2025-12-25.
- **Text inspected:** https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2026/01/264-2025.html
- **Official Gazette bundle metadata:** https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/37add437-802d-4848-8a24-8e064db22fcc
- **Structure:** 41 articles.
- **Arts. 1–3:** definitions and scope; all training providers must regularize their status within one year from commencement of Law 14/2025.
- **Arts. 4–8:** eligible legal forms and exceptions; Ministry licence requirement; no training program may be delivered unless included in the licence; electronic training requires a separate Ministry licence and a stated EGP 10,000 fee.
- **Arts. 9–12:** public/private partnerships, restrictions on training-centre names, anti-discrimination and incentives for youth, women and persons with disabilities.
- **Arts. 13–19:** licensing conditions for training centres, documents and premises; Ministry review/inspection; stated EGP 8,000 inspection fee and EGP 15,000 licence fee; three-year licence term; suspension/cancellation grounds.
- **Arts. 20–25:** central curriculum-development committee; training programme approval; annual training plan must be approved at least two months before execution; programme, duration, hours, instructors, participant numbers, skills outcomes and fees must be specified; centre operating rules.
- **Arts. 26–30:** individual trainer licensing; minimum experience of three years; trainer preparation and credentials; three-year licence; stated EGP 3,000 trainer licence fee; suspension/cancellation grounds.
- **Arts. 31–37:** centre duties, insurance for trainers/trainees, OSH PPE, trainee reporting, licensed premises, assessments, certificate issuance and inspection.
- **Arts. 38–39:** electronic data reporting and a national database for training-centre graduates and employment outcomes.
- **Arts. 40–41:** repeal of conflicting earlier rules and commencement day after Gazette publication (2025-12-26, subject to original Gazette confirmation).
- **Important rights note:** the fees and conditions above are extracted from a secondary transcription. Verify the signed original and current fee schedule before any legal or commercial use.

## 7. Why these records remain low-trust

- PDF availability does not equal readable or verified content.
- Several files have no text layer or corrupted OCR.
- The historic sales tax law is not current VAT law.
- The property tax amendment cannot be relied on until dates and article numbers are visually verified.
- Law148/2026 is only a narrow amendment to Law147/1984, not a full replacement fee schedule.
- Keep all records lawyer_reviewed=false and rights_cleared=false.

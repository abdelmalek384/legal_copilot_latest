# Value Added Tax Law149/2026 — full amendment extraction

**Official source:** Egyptian Tax Authority PDF  
https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.149.of_.2026.pdf  
**Publication:** Official Gazette issue30 bis (A), 28 July 2026  
**Length:** 4 pages; 90 extracted lines  
**Commencement:** day following publication — 29 July 2026 (Article5)

## 1. Amendments to VAT Law67/2016

Article1 substitutes:
- Article3, first paragraph.
- Article5, fourth paragraph.
- Article28 bis.
- Article30, first paragraph, item3.

### Article3 — rates
- General rate remains 14% from financial year 2017/2018 onward (the law text restates the historical 13% and 14% rate timeline).
- The rate on machinery, equipment and medical devices used to produce goods or provide services is 5%, except buses and passenger cars.
- Devices used for medical purposes are treated as medical devices for this exception.

### Article5 — transit
VAT is not due on goods in transit and services performed on them, provided transport occurs under Customs Authority supervision according to the Customs Law rules.

### Article28 bis — industrial machinery and medical equipment
- VAT on imported or locally purchased machinery, equipment and medical devices for factories/production units for industrial production is suspended for one year from customs release or local purchase.
- The Tax Authority may extend the suspension for justified reasons, for one or more additional periods, but the total extension may not exceed three years.
- If the machinery/equipment/device is used in industrial production during the applicable period, the suspended tax is exempted.
- The producer may not dispose of the machinery/equipment/device for non-exempt purposes during the five years following exemption without notifying the Tax Authority and paying the tax due according to its condition/value and the tax rate in force on payment date.
- If the period expires without use in industrial production, the tax and additional tax become payable from the original customs release/local purchase date until payment.
- The Executive Regulation is to specify implementing rules and procedures.

### Article30 — refund of credit balance
The amended item3 addresses credit balances outstanding for more than four consecutive tax periods. For taxpayers under Law6/2025 (tax incentives/easing for projects with annual turnover not exceeding EGP20m), more than three months is sufficient.

## 2. Amendments to the exempt goods/services list

Article2 substitutes items 19, 20, 28, 32, 35 and 36:
- Item19: butane gas (cooking gas).
- Item20: natural products, including mining and quarry products in their natural state, excluding crude oil and natural gas.
- Item28: sale and lease of vacant land, agricultural land, buildings and residential units, and sale/lease of non-residential buildings/units, with exceptions for independent business premises and specified religious, charitable, social, educational and health activities as provided in the text.
- Item32: wheelchairs and parts, artificial body parts and components, hearing aids and parts, body-worn/carried/implanted devices and their production inputs for disability/impairment, dialysis devices and components/consumables, incubators, serums, vaccines, blood and derivatives, blood bags and family-planning devices.
- Item35: postal savings fund banking services and financial services provided by the National Postal Authority.
- Item36: non-banking financial services supervised by FRA or CBE.

## 3. New Article22(2)(3) and natural gas schedule entry

Article3 adds to Article22(2) a new item3 covering sales of machinery, equipment and medical devices subject to Article28 bis.

It also adds serial17 to the goods/services tax table:
- Natural gas.
- Collection unit: 1,000 cubic feet.
- Tax amount: EGP20.

Article4 repeals serials8 and10 of the first part of the goods/services table.

## 4. Legal and product implementation cautions

1. This is a 2026 amendment, not a full consolidated VAT Law67/2016. The base law, all prior amendments and Executive Regulation must be integrated.
2. Preserve the 5% rate's precise scope: the statute says machinery/equipment/medical devices used to produce a good or provide a service, with express exclusions for buses/passenger cars. Do not generalize to all medical purchases.
3. Article28 bis is a suspension followed by conditional exemption, not an unconditional immediate exemption. The one-year clock, extensions up to a three-year maximum, use in industrial production and five-year disposal restriction must be modeled separately.
4. The additional tax accrual when the machinery is not used in industrial production is calculated from the original release/purchase date; the exact calculation must be checked against the VAT law and executive regulation.
5. The three-month credit-balance threshold under Law6/2025 applies only to the described eligible small projects; do not apply it to all taxpayers.
6. The amended exempt list must be stored as a versioned list; do not overwrite prior items because historic tax periods may be governed by earlier text.
7. Do not change production tax calculations until an Egyptian tax lawyer/accountant validates the complete consolidated law and regulations.

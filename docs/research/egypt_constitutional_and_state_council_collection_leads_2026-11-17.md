# Egyptian constitutional and administrative judiciary — public collection leads

**Snapshot:** 17 November 2026

## Supreme Constitutional Court

Official portal: https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/newportal

The official portal exposes:
- Free search by legislation and case status.
- Search across constitutional judgments and principles.
- Subject matter decisions, formal decisions, conflict-of-jurisdiction decisions, execution disputes and legislative interpretation decisions.
- Latest decisions and hearing calendar information.
- Constitutional document collections and selected electronic publications.

### Public record located — 3 January 2026

Official court summary: https://www.sccourt.gov.eg/SCC/?a=cp3&id=891

The Court's official summary reports that it rejected a constitutional challenge concerning Penal Code Articles 86, 86 bis, 86 bis A and 88 bis C, together with provisions of Anti-Terrorism Law 94/2015. The published summary frames the constitutional reasoning by reference to Constitution Articles 59, 93 and 237 and Egypt's ratified anti-terrorism instruments, including the Arab Convention for the Suppression of Terrorism, the UN Convention against Transnational Organized Crime, the International Convention for the Suppression of the Financing of Terrorism and the Arab anti-money-laundering/terrorist-financing convention.

**Important limitation:** the public summary retrieved in this pass does not expose the case number or the full judgment. This is registered as an official press-summary lead, not a full judgment or a complete judicial ratio. The exact decision number, parties, operative text, full reasoning and subsequent treatment must be retrieved before it is used as a cited precedent.

The current portal also lists a selected-judgments bulletin for the 7 November 2026 session and further bulletins for 10 October, 9 September, 1 August, 1 July and 6 June 2026. These are collection targets; the underlying decisions have not yet been individually acquired.

## State Council

Official portal: https://esc.gov.eg/

The State Council's public institutional pages identify:
- Supreme Administrative Court.
- Administrative Courts.
- Administrative Judiciary.
- Judicial Section.
- Advisory/Fatwa Section.
- Legislative Section.
- General Assembly of the Fatwa and Legislation Sections.

### Supreme Administrative Court — public principles series

The State Council website's “Higher Courts Collections” index lists principle collections for judicial years:
11, 18, 21, 27, 44, 45, 50 (parts 1 and 2), 51 (parts 1 and 2), 53 (parts 1 and 2), 54, 55–56, 57 (parts 1 and 2), 58, 59 (parts 1 and 2), 60 (parts 1 and 2), and 61 (parts 1 and 2).

Index locator: https://esc.gov.eg/قسم-التشريع-duplicate-1/

This is a bibliographic index of published principle collections. It does not itself establish that every volume is downloadable or openly licensed. Each volume must be opened, its contents and page structure inventoried, and each principle linked to its underlying judgment number/date/circuit where available.

### General Assembly of the Fatwa and Legislation Sections

Official page: https://esc.gov.eg/الجمعية-العمومية-لقسمي-الفتوى-والتشريع/

The public page describes the General Assembly's statutory role under State Council Law 47/1972, including reasoned opinions on important international, constitutional, legislative and other legal matters referred by specified senior state authorities. It also describes its advisory role under the Civil Service Law and other statutes.

Do not treat every State Council opinion as a court judgment or as generally binding precedent. Record the issuing body, statutory basis, requesting authority, addressee, date, legal question and any statutory provision governing its effect.

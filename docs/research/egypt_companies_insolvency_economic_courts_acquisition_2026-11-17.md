# Companies, insolvency and Economic Courts — acquisition tranche

**Snapshot:** 17 November 2026

## 1. Companies Law159/1981 — official government reprint

PDF: https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-10/law-159-1981.pdf

The official ETA-hosted PDF has 993 extracted lines. It identifies Law159/1981, amended by Law3/1998, and includes notes to Law94/2005 and a Supreme Constitutional Court judgment.

### Structure and material observed
- Applies to joint-stock companies, partnerships limited by shares and limited liability companies.
- Promulgation provisions repeal Laws26/1954, 244/1960 and 137/1961 to the extent stated, and conflicting rules.
- It includes formation, founders, company incorporation, capital, boards, shareholder assemblies, share issuance and transfer, accounts, dissolution and liquidation.
- Article14 is marked amended by Law3/1998.
- Article16 is marked amended by Law3/1998.
- Article17 includes a note that the Supreme Constitutional Court in Case25/Judicial Year22, session 2 June 2001, held unconstitutional the requirement for Cabinet approval to establish a company whose purpose includes issuing newspapers.
- The reprint's opening note refers to Law94/2005 and investment-law incentives.

**Critical limitation:** this is not a consolidated 2026 text. The separate Law17/2015 amending Companies Law159/1981 is identified by the Ministry of Finance; later GAFI rules and decisions must also be reconciled. Do not use the old wording in an affected article without checking its amendment history and constitutional invalidation.

## 2. Restructuring and Bankruptcy Law11/2018 — base text lead

- Secondary full text: https://lawhub.info/eg/?p=11442
- Secondary full text record identifies Official Gazette issue7 bis (D), 19 February 2018, and 279 articles.
- The law regulates restructuring, preventive composition and bankruptcy; applies to traders under Commercial Code Law17/1999, subject to statutory exclusions.
- It provides for specialized Bankruptcy Administration within Economic Courts and rules for restructuring, preventive composition, bankruptcy, creditor claims and liquidation.
- It requires use of Civil and Commercial Procedure and Evidence Law where the insolvency law contains no special provision.
- It shifts insolvency-related proceedings to the competent Economic Court subject to transition provisions.

**Status:** secondary transcription only. The signed primary Gazette PDF for Law11/2018 remains to be acquired. Do not label the text source-matched solely because a secondary publisher reproduces it.

## 4. Decision3715/2026 — fees and deposits of Economic Courts bankruptcy experts

Official Government Printing Office text:
https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=hQXViw+FAPU%3D

The GPO page identifies Ministry of Justice Decision3715/2026 amending Decision2208/2019 on fees and deposits of experts of the Bankruptcy Administration at Economic Courts.

The retrieved text shows substitutions to the financial amounts and percentages in Decision2208/2019, including:
- EGP40,000 in place of EGP20,000.
- EGP100,000 in place of EGP50,000.
- A minimum percentage changed from 3% to 2%.
- A maximum percentage changed from 6% to 5%.
- EGP1,000,000 changed to EGP500,000.

# Court of Cassation — public case-law headnotes harvest

**Snapshot:** 17 November 2026  
**Official portal:** https://cc.gov.eg/

## 1. Public/restricted access boundary

The Court of Cassation's official home page publishes selected recent principles/headnotes with docket number, subject, session date and Technical Office year. The homepage contains civil, labour, commercial, economic, personal-status and criminal categories.

The Court's own subscription page states that the following are restricted to subscribers:
- Full civil and criminal judgment texts.
- Advanced search by combined criteria and search within full judgment/holding/headnote.
- The legislative/constitutional database.
- Official PDF downloads of stamped judgments and appeals.
- Expanded session lists and daily Technical Office updates.

The official page states an annual subscription fee of EGP1,080 for Egyptian subscribers, paid in person at the Court of Cassation's Judicial Services Office in Cairo.

**Collection policy:** only collect public homepage headnotes and any other genuinely free official materials. Do not scrape or bypass subscription controls. Full judgments remain an acquisition gap unless lawful access is obtained.

## 3. What these records are — and are not

- They are official Court of Cassation public headnotes, not full reasoned judgments.
- Some headnotes identify a General Assembly principle and express departure from prior rulings; preserve that status because it materially affects precedent weight.
- The case number/date/Technical Office year are transcribed from the public portal. A few records have incomplete dates or need confirmation of docket formatting.
- Do not invent the ratio beyond the headnote text. Retrieve the full judgment lawfully before using detailed facts, procedural history or quotations.
- Some headnotes concern repealed statutes (e.g. Customs Law66/1963 and Social Insurance Law79/1975); retain them for historical periods and transition disputes, not as current text.

## 4. Important handling rules

1. Store the full appeal number, judicial year, hearing date, circuit, technical-office year/page/principle number, publication URL, and whether the source is the official judgment, official headnote, or secondary transcription.
2. A headnote can summarize only one issue from a multi-issue appeal. Capture all holdings and distinguish ratio from background.
3. The 12226/92, 619/92, 7382/92 and 10984/82 records interpret Law 12/2003. Law 14/2025 repealed that statute; these cases remain historical precedent, but their rules must be re-evaluated under the new statutory text and transitional provisions.
4. The 11438/86 decision concerns Social Insurance Law 79/1975. Do not apply its statutory articles to current social-insurance disputes without checking Law 148/2019 and its regulations.
5. The 10075/89 and 5242/94 decisions include leave and termination issues that may be affected by the new law and related legislation; never transplant their formulas without current-law analysis.
6. Do not use any of these records as production legal evidence until the full official source, rights position, and currentness are checked and qualified Egyptian counsel approves the extracted principle.

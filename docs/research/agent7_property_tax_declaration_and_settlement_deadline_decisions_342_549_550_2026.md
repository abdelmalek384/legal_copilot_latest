The preamble is reported to cite Law196/2008 and its amendments, including Law3/2026, and earlier Finance Minister Decisions69, 188 and 340/2026, plus Real Estate Tax Authority memorandum No.428 dated 28 September 2026. The exact full preamble/signature block remains unverified.

### Scope caution

Decision550 extends the period in **Article14(a)**, the five-year census. It does not independently amend the statutory deadline in Article14(b) for annual-census events (new buildings, additions, or material changes). A 30 December/31 December common calendar deadline does not merge the two distinct legal bases.

## 3. Decision 549/2026 — extension of delay-interest exemption and settlement window

- **Issuer:** Minister of Finance.
- **Decision date:** 29 September 2026.
- **Publication:** الوقائع المصرية issue 215 تابع (أ), 29 September 2026.
- **Scope:** extends by six months, to **2 April 2027**, (a) the delay-interest exemption period under Article3 of Law3/2026 and (b) the period for applications to settle real-estate-tax disputes under Articles5 and 6 of Law3/2026.

- **Source quality:** secondary sources reproduce the two operative effects and identify the Gazette issue/date. No full official Gazette transcription or original Amiri Press binary was obtained. The exact Arabic drafting of the two articles is therefore **not quoted as verbatim** below.

### Operative content confirmed from secondary reports (not a verbatim transcription)

- **Article 1:** extends the period for the delay-interest exemption under Law3/2026 until 2 April 2027.
- **Article 2:** extends the period for submitting settlement applications in real-estate-tax disputes pending before courts of all levels and tax appeal committees/records until 2 April 2027.

This is a later and distinct extension instrument. It should be linked to, but not conflated with, Decision396/2026 and Circular3/2026: Decision396 approved forms and Circular3 set internal processing instructions; Decision549 extends statutory application windows.

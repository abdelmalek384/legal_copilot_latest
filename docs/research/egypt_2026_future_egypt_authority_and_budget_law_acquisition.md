# 2026 strategic development authority and public-finance instruments

**Snapshot:** 17 November 2026

## 1. Future of Egypt Sustainable Development Agency — Law147/2026

**Official Government Printing Office full text:**  
https://alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=oHXoEUHb8y0%3D

The GPO text is titled **Law147/2026 issuing the law reorganizing the Future of Egypt Sustainable Development Agency**. It was signed on 26 July 2026. The promulgating Article14 states it takes effect on the day after publication in the Official Gazette; the exact Gazette issue/date/page still needs to be recorded.

The attached law establishes a national agency of a special nature, reporting to the President, with legal personality and technical/financial/administrative independence. The text includes at least 95 attached articles and creates:
- Sustainable development zones for strategic national and economic projects.
- A sovereign wealth fund named “Future of Egypt Sovereign Wealth Fund — Ahramat Al Nile”.
- A service fund named “Future of Egypt Service Fund — Daem”.
- A special governance and financial framework for the agency, zones, funds and subsidiaries.

### Material provisions identified in the GPO text

- Article2: agency is a national entity of a special nature, affiliated with the President, with independent legal personality and head office in Cairo.
- Article3: objectives include food, water and energy security, national economic development, sustainable sovereign wealth and social justice.
- Article8: Board of Directors appointed by presidential decision for four-year terms, renewable.
- Article14: Chairperson exercises ministerial powers for the agency/funds in specified matters, including staffing, foreign expertise subject to security approval and administrative/financial/investment management.
- Articles43–46: development-zone framework, public-service/one-stop service centre, transfer of licensing/approval powers to representatives at the centre, and deemed acceptance if the relevant authority does not object within two weeks (subject to statutory exceptions).
- Articles47 onward: creation and governance of the Ahramat Al Nile sovereign wealth fund.
- Article80: excludes the agency's legal department from Law47/1973 and applies the agency's internal HR rules subject to the law.
- Article87: addresses the treatment of subsidiaries/assets and exclusion from Law170/2025 on state ownership in companies, subject to the statutory wording.
- Article89: provides a special framework for transfers of operating surpluses/returns to the Treasury, including a minimum 10% of annual net operating surplus of a sustainable development zone and 7% of real annual returns on investment allocations to the sovereign fund, subject to the exact article conditions.

This law has broad implications for land allocation, permits, public asset transfers, company governance, public employment, investment, taxes/fees and public accountability. It must be mapped article by article with all cross-referenced statutes.

## 2. State General Budget Law77/2026 — FY2026/2027

**Official GPO text:** https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=SkEkBVRxYno%3D

- Law77/2026 is the State General Budget Law for FY2026/2027.
- It was signed on 7 July 2026.
- Article17 provides that it applies from 1 July 2026.
- Article1 estimates State budget uses at EGP8,186,183,184,000.
- It estimates revenues and receipts from lending/asset sales at EGP4,175,607,998,000.
- The text contains detailed budget schedules and a public-debt ceiling; those tables are part of the legal instrument and must be preserved as structured data, not merely summarized.

This is an annual, time-bound statute. Do not reuse its appropriations or debt ceilings for later fiscal years. The detailed schedules, amendments and Gazette pagination remain to be extracted.

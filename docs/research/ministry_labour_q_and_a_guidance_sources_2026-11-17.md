## 3. Q&A Part 2 — recruitment agencies and placement operations

- The Ministry publications portal lists “Part 2 — Question and Answer in Labour Law 14/2025.”
- **Secondary report on the Ministry booklet:** https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/10/10/كيف-تحمي-نفسك-من-شركات-التوظيف-الوهمية-قانون-العمل-يغلق/7574322
- The report identifies the booklet's preparer as Dr. Sherif Mohamed Nour El-Din, labour attaché and head of the Egyptian labour representation office at the Consulate General in Kuwait, under the Minister's patronage.
- It describes the booklet's coverage of recruitment agencies and employment placement:
  - the categories of entities permitted to place Egyptian workers domestically/abroad;
  - prior licensing and eligibility conditions for private recruitment agencies;
  - capital thresholds reported as EGP 250,000 for domestic-only placement and EGP 500,000 for domestic/overseas placement, with ownership conditions;
  - minimum EGP 1,000,000 security;
  - reported EGP 5,000 licence fee and one-year licence duration;
  - application, renewal, late-renewal and branch requirements;
  - records and employment-contract requirements;
  - electronic recruitment-platform licensing, cybersecurity, encryption, safe data storage, incident reporting and retention of electronic logs.
- These details are a secondary summary of the Ministry booklet. Verify each number and condition against Law 14/2025, Decision 272/2025, Decision 2652/2026 and the signed original text before building a compliance engine.
- The Part 2 direct PDF URL was not independently located in this pass. Do not guess it from the Part 1 path.

## 4. Ministry statement of 14 November 2026 — implementation and enforcement

- **Official Ministry report:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/وزير-العمل-لمديرة-إدارة-الحوكمة-والثلاثية-بمنظمة-العمل-الدولية-مصر-تمضي-بخطى-ثابتة-نحو-بناء-منظومة-عمل-عصرية-ترتكز-على-التشريعات-المتطورة-والحوار-الاجتماعي-والحماية-والامتثال-لمعايير-العمل-الدولية/
- **Date:** 14 November 2026.
- The Minister said the Ministry had completed issuing the executive decisions for the Labour Law after presenting them to the Supreme Council for Social Dialogue. The report does not enumerate the decisions or provide a definitive instrument checklist.
- The Minister also said:
  - the current priority is enforcement, including building labour-inspector capacity and launching geographic/sectoral inspection campaigns;
  - support for labour courts and labour judges, and legal-assistance offices;
  - a Labour Law guide had been issued for establishments participating in the Better Work programme to support compliance with national legislation and international labour standards;
  - OSH implementing decisions had been issued and inspectors were being trained on the new requirements;
  - the Zero Vision project was beginning in railways to promote OSH culture;
  - a domestic-worker bill and operational framework were being prepared with ILO cooperation;
  - a second generation of the national plan against the worst forms of child labour was being prepared.
- **Interpretation limit:** this is a Ministry statement of completion, not a complete published legal inventory. Use it as a cross-check trigger: reconcile every ministerial delegation in Law 14/2025 against the decision register, then obtain the signed Gazette instrument for each. Do not assume the current 42-instrument register is exhaustive merely because the Ministry says the package is complete.

## 5. Better Work guidance and inspection manuals

- The 14 November Ministry report confirms a Labour Law guide for Better Work participating establishments. The actual PDF, issue/version, table of contents and scope were not located in this pass.
- The Ministry has a separate gender-responsive labour-inspection guide, referenced in its institutional reporting and used to train labour/OSH inspectors. It is distinct from the Better Work Labour Law guide and should not be conflated with it.
- The Ministry's OSH publications catalogue lists many technical bulletins; these are valuable for inspector education and hazard recognition but do not automatically have the status of binding legal standards.
- Search for the signed Decisions 248–252/2026 and compare the official technical bulletins to those decisions. A technical leaflet cannot amend a ministerial decision's threshold or annex.

## 6. Acquisition and ingestion rules

1. Obtain original Ministry-hosted PDF files and record the direct URL, retrieval date, SHA-256, page count, file size, publication date and version.
2. Extract text locally; preserve headings, question numbers, article references, tables and page coordinates.
3. Label each answer as **official Ministry guidance**, **secondary reporting of guidance**, or **binding legal text**.
4. Never use a Q&A response to override the statute, Official Gazette, signed ministerial decision or binding court ruling.
5. For numerical values, fees, deadlines, eligibility thresholds, penalties and exceptions, require a separate exact statutory source citation.
6. Confirm whether the Better Work guide is publicly downloadable and what reuse rights attach before storing/redistributing it.
7. Do not ingest individual or confidential source data merely because Decision 74/2026 or a Ministry guide refers to labour-market information.

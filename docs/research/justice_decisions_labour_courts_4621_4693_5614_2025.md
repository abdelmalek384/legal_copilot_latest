# Ministry of Justice Labour-Court Implementation Decisions — 2025

Research date: 17 November 2026  
Instruments: Decisions 4621/2025, 4693/2025 and 5614/2025  
Status: official Amiri Press indexed text plus Gazette publication locators; not lawyer-reviewed; rights not cleared.

## Why these belong in the Law 14/2025 implementation corpus

The Minister of Labour publicly identified the Ministry of Justice as one of the issuing authorities for the executive decisions needed to implement Labour Law 14/2025. Decisions 4621 and 4693 were specifically cited in the Ministry's 29 July 2025 announcement as part of readiness for the new labour courts and free legal-aid offices. Decision 5614 later supplements the court-location arrangement.

## 1. Justice Decision 4621/2025 — locations of labour courts

- **Title:** قرار وزير العدل رقم 4621 لسنة 2025 بتعيين مقار المحاكم العمالية بدوائر اختصاص المحاكم الابتدائية.
- **Signed:** 22 July 2025.
- **Published:** Egyptian Gazette issue 188, 25 August 2025.
- **Effective:** 1 October 2025.
- **Official Amiri Press indexed text:** https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=1K6IwYRW0Sg%3D
- **Secondary full locator/list:** https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/justice/25-08-2025/no-4621?id=2175209&type=1

### Operative map

- **Article 1:** designates the location of each labour court within the jurisdiction of each primary court. The published schedule lists 38 labour-court locations, including Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, Benha, Tanta, Zagazig, Mansoura, Damanhour, Kafr El-Sheikh, Damietta, Ismailia, North/South Sinai, Port Said, Fayoum, Beni Suef, Minya, Assiut, New Valley, Sohag, Qena, Luxor, Red Sea and Aswan.
- **Article 2:** directs the competent Ministry of Justice departments to implement.
- **Article 3:** publication and commencement from 1 October 2025.

The detailed 38-location table should be retained as a separate structured venue dataset with source-page coordinates, not as free-text legal prose only.

## 2. Justice Decision 4693/2025 — labour legal-assistance offices

- **Title:** قرار وزير العدل رقم 4693 لسنة 2025 بإنشاء وتنظيم عمل مكاتب المساعدة القانونية العمالية بالمحاكم الابتدائية.
- **Signed:** 27 July 2025.
- **Published:** Egyptian Gazette issue 193, 31 August 2025.
- **Effective:** from publication.
- **Official Amiri Press indexed text:** https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Jx8EYU41ZXI%3D
- **Secondary full text:** https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/moj-decree-4693-2025
- **Additional secondary article:** https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8124067

### Eight-article map

- **Article 1:** establishes a labour legal-assistance office at each labour court and at every other place where a labour court sits within the jurisdiction of the relevant primary court. Each office receives a suitable location. Services are optional and free of charge.
- **Article 2:** each office consists of a head and a sufficient number of court employees experienced in managing case files; appointment is by decision of the Assistant Minister of Justice for Specialized Courts, on nomination by the relevant primary-court president.
- **Articles 3–5:** define the office's functions, management/administrative supervision and support. The accessible secondary text identifies assistance to litigants in preparing labour claims, procedural guidance and support in the filing/processing of labour cases; verify every enumerated service against the signed text.
- **Article 6:** each office sends the General Department for Labour Courts, by no later than the fifth day of each month, statistical statements for the previous month. They include the number and subject of assistance requests, receipt date, actions taken and outcome of each dispute.
- **Article 7:** directs the competent Ministry of Justice departments to implement and repeals conflicting decisions.
- **Article 8:** publication and effectiveness from publication.

**Implementation significance:** this decision creates a free, optional access-to-justice service at labour-court venues. Do not describe it as mandatory legal representation or as a substitute for counsel.

## 3. Justice Decision 5614/2025 — additional local hearing venues

- **Title:** قرار وزير العدل رقم 5614 لسنة 2025 بتعيين مقار لنظر الدعاوى العمالية داخل اختصاص المحاكم الجزئية ببعض المحاكم الابتدائية.
- **Signed:** 24 September 2025.
- **Published:** Egyptian Gazette issue 244 (original), 1 November 2025.
- **Effective:** 1 October 2025 (as stated in Article 3, despite publication being later; preserve the exact clause and seek counsel review on retrospective operation).
- **Official Amiri Press indexed text:** https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=7FYalJBNQ2s%3D
- **Publication locator:** https://egyptianlaws.com/?p=191015
- **Secondary full text:** https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2025/11/5614-2025.html

### Three-article map

- **Preamble:** cites Labour Law 14/2025, Decision 4621/2025 on labour-court locations and Decision 4693/2025 on labour legal-assistance offices, and requests from the presidents of South Giza and South Damanhour primary courts.
- **Article 1:** designates two additional local hearing venues:
  1. South Giza Labour Court: Saf Court building, with jurisdiction over Saf and Atfih.
  2. South Damanhour Labour Court: Rahmaniya Court building, with jurisdiction over Rahmaniya, Shubrakhit and Abu al-Matamir.
- **Article 2:** directs the competent Ministry of Justice departments to implement.
- **Article 3:** publication in Al-Waqa'i al-Misriyya and effectiveness from 1 October 2025.

**Date warning:** the decision was signed 24 September and published 1 November but says it applies from 1 October. Preserve both publication and stated effective date; do not silently normalize or assume retroactivity is legally uncontroversial.

## Source and implementation safeguards

1. These are Justice Ministry instruments, not Ministry of Labour ministerial decisions. Keep issuer metadata accurate.
2. Their primary function is judicial infrastructure and venue/access-to-justice administration; they do not change substantive worker/employer rights.
3. Venue lists must be kept current against later amendments and court administrative orders.
4. Legal-assistance offices provide free optional services; avoid implying that a litigant is required to use them.
5. No lawyer review or rights clearance; no production import performed.

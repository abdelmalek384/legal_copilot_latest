## Relationship to Labour Law 14/2025

- Labour Law Article 20 provides for the Training and Qualification Fund, its national activities and services, and delegates its Board formation, powers, financial treatment, branches, bylaws and revenue-collection system to a Prime Minister decision.
- Decision 3356/2026 reorganizes the Fund and expressly repeals Prime Minister Decision 1543/2003.
- This is the **Training and Qualification Fund**, distinct from the Unorganized Workers Emergency Aid/Social/Health Services Fund governed by PM Decision 2403/2026.

## Article-by-article map

### Article 1 — national remit and services

The Fund operates nationally according to labour-market needs and the requirements of sectoral employer organizations established by law. Services include:
1. Funding human-resource skills development and vocational/technical training through creation and development of training centres, programme preparation and delivery, and skills/competency guides.
2. Funding development projects to build skills and align education/training outputs with current and future labour-market needs, in coordination with sectoral skills councils.
3. Establishing conditions and executive rules to regulate funding operations.
4. Coordinating with relevant entities on vocational-training plans to optimize available financial resources.
5. Research, studies, seminars, conferences, exhibitions and publications about training programmes, and publicizing them to prospective trainees.
6. Media programmes to explain Fund activities and promote vocational training, industrial apprenticeship and other training systems.
7. Encouraging relevant entities to develop their own resources to finance internal training.
8. Monitoring and evaluating all Fund-financed work.

### Article 2 — Fund resources

- Annual contribution equal to **0.25% of the minimum insured wage** at establishments in the public sector, public-business sector and private sector employing **30 workers or more**.
- Minimum contribution: **EGP 10 per worker**; maximum: **EGP 30 per worker** per year.
- The establishment bears and pays the contribution annually, in return for the services referred to in Labour Law Article 20.
- Additional resources: accepted subsidies, donations and grants; investment returns under the Board's rules and applicable law.

### Article 3 — payment and exemption

- Establishments pay the Article 2 contribution in quarterly instalments at the end of each three-month period using non-cash payment channels.
- A decision by the Minister responsible for labour sets the rules/conditions for **full exemption** from the contribution where the establishment trains its workers according to its own needs or approved bylaws.
- The final implementing ministerial exemption decision has not yet been identified in this harvest. Do not assume self-training automatically creates exemption before the required ministerial rules are obtained.

### Article 4 — Board composition

Board chaired by the Minister responsible for labour, with senior representatives from:
- Transport;
- Education and Technical Education;
- Social Solidarity;
- Labour;
- Planning and Economic Development;
- Industry (Productivity and Vocational Training Authority).

It also includes three employer-organization members and three labour-organization members, nominated by their organizations with proportional representation and most-representative-organization principles. Experts may attend without a counted vote.

### Article 5 — Board powers

The Board controls Fund affairs and may:
- set general policies and plans and monitor implementation;
- approve the annual budget and final accounts;
- issue administrative, technical and financial organizational decisions;
- approve the Fund's organizational structure;
- accept grants, gifts and donations under law;
- review periodic activity, performance and financial reports;
- approve programmes entrusted by relevant ministries/entities;
- consider matters submitted by the chair or members.

### Article 6 — Board term

Three-year membership; reconstitution procedures begin during the final 60 days. A vacant nominating body must nominate a replacement within one month.

### Article 7 — delegation and expert support

The Board may delegate matters to its chair or one or more members and may use technical, legal, accounting and other experts to prepare studies and propose administrative/financial systems.

### Article 8 — meetings and allowances

- Meetings at least once every three months.
- Quorum: majority of members.
- Decisions by absolute majority of attending members; chair has casting vote on tie.
- Attendance allowance: **EGP 5,000 per meeting** for each of the chair and members, paid from Fund resources.

### Article 9 — Secretary-General

The Fund has a Secretary-General nominated by the Board chair and appointed by a chair decision after Board approval. Eligibility includes Egyptian nationality, suitable higher qualification, at least ten years' relevant experience, good reputation, health fitness, no disqualifying conviction and no final disciplinary dismissal.

### Article 10 — Secretary-General functions

Financial/administrative supervision, protection of Fund assets and rights, implementation of Board decisions, preparation of organizational drafts, periodic reports, identification of performance obstacles and preparation of the annual budget/final accounts.

### Article 11 — staffing

Fund jobs may be filled by secondment from ministries/government entities or by contracting with experts under applicable legal procedures.

### Article 12 — provincial branches

Branches are established at Labour Directorates. They implement the Labour Law provisions related to the Fund, Decision 3356 and Board decisions. Each branch keeps separate paper/electronic registers and books for Fund activities.

### Article 13 — bank account and investment

- Special account at a Central Bank-approved commercial bank.
- Non-cash disbursements.
- Surplus rolls over; exceptional cross-year expenses require Board approval.
- Permitted investments include Central Bank savings vehicles, Treasury bills, banking instruments and related direct investments after Prime Minister approval.

### Article 14 — accounts and financial year

Annual financial statements and estimated budget; year-end report on expenditure, beneficiaries, balances, amounts paid and establishments paying dues; report copy sent to the Central Auditing Organization. Financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June.

### Article 15 — external audit

One or more external auditors appointed by the Board from the registered auditors' roll. Auditor reviews annual accounts and accounting/legal compliance and submits periodic reports on financial position and internal controls.

### Article 16 — public funds

Fund assets are public funds, particularly for Penal Code purposes, and are subject to Central Auditing Organization oversight.

### Article 17 — repeal

Repeals Prime Minister Decision 1543/2003 on formation and operation of the Fund Board.

### Article 18 — publication and commencement

Published in the Official Gazette and effective the day after publication. Signed at the Cabinet on 1 November 2026.

## Compliance and product implications

1. The contribution applies to establishments in the listed public/public-business/private sectors with 30 or more workers; do not apply it to every employer or every worker without checking the exact statutory scope.
2. The annual amount is 0.25% of the minimum insured wage, subject to EGP 10 minimum and EGP 30 maximum per worker. Confirm the applicable minimum insured wage and the method of counting covered workers before calculation.
3. The employer pays the contribution; it is not described as a wage deduction from the employee.
4. Payment is quarterly in arrears/at each three-month end under Article 3; exact payment calendar and collection mechanism may require Board or Ministry instructions.
5. Full exemption depends on a separate Ministerial decision where the establishment trains workers according to its needs or approved bylaws. That exemption decision is still missing from the current register.
6. Do not confuse this Fund with the irregular-workers emergency/social/health Fund under Decision 2403/2026.
7. Decision 3356 repeals PM Decision 1543/2003; retain the old decision as historical and do not apply its former rules after the new decision takes effect.
8. Financial figures and contribution mechanics must be compared against the Gazette before coding or giving legal advice.

# Egyptian Criminal Procedure Law — current commencement status after Law 160/2026

**Research snapshot:** 17 November 2026  
**Scope:** commencement date and statutory transition only; not a full article-by-article review.

## Primary-source findings

### Law 174/2025 — new Code of Criminal Procedure

- Official Ministry of Justice-hosted copy of Official Gazette issue 45 bis (D), 12 November 2025: https://moj.gov.eg/backend/api/documents/download/20F3150C-29F1-487E-A0F0-FCCE10CB207B
- The promulgating law is Law 174/2025 and includes the new Criminal Procedure Code.
- Article 4 provides for repeal of Criminal Procedure Law 150/1950 and Law 140/2014 concerning surrender of accused persons and transfer of convicted persons, as part of the new law's commencement framework.
- The original commencement clause was subsequently amended. Do not use the original 1 October 2026 commencement date without the later amending law.

### Law 160/2026 — primary official text and Gazette metadata

- Official Government Printing Office full text: https://www.alamiria.com/Sec/TashTxt?id=Aalg37zxdcU%3D
- Official DSpace Gazette item: https://dspace.id.com.eg/items/1b1e2a48-59d9-4307-b215-1ac3a07b4ee3
- Gazette issue: Official Gazette 39 bis (B), dated 30 September 2026.
- The official text identifies Law 160/2026 as amending Law 174/2025.
- Article 1 substitutes the date “1 October 2027” for the phrase “the first of October following its publication” in Article 6 of Law 174/2025.
- Article 2 provides publication in the Official Gazette and commencement on the day following publication.
- It was promulgated at the Presidency on 30 September 2026.

**Result:** the new Code of Criminal Procedure under Law 174/2025 is scheduled to enter into force on **1 October 2027**, not 1 October 2026. This date is supported by the official Government Printing Office text of Law 160/2026 and the Official Gazette catalogue record.

### Article-version control

- Target provision: Law 174/2025, promulgating Article 6.
- Previous wording: «ينشر هذا القانون فى الجريدة الرسمية، ويعمل به اعتبارًا من الأول من أكتوبر التالى لتاريخ نشره».
- Replacement wording after Law 160/2026: «ينشر هذا القانون فى الجريدة الرسمية، ويعمل به اعتبارًا من الأول من أكتوبر لعام 2027».
- Law 160/2026 itself commenced 1 October 2026, the day after Gazette publication on 30 September 2026.
- The replacement date is a fixed date, not a rolling “October following publication” formula. The code remains enacted but not in force until 1 October 2027, unless a later amendment changes it.

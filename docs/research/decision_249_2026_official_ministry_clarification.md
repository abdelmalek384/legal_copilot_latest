# Official Ministry clarification — Decision 249/2026

**Official Ministry announcement date:** 2026-10-27  
**Instrument:** Ministerial Decision 249/2026 on establishments/branches required to establish OSH functional units and committees.  
**Official source:** https://www.labour.gov.eg/ar/الأخبار/بيان-توضيحي-هام-بشأن-القرار-الوزاري-رقم-249-لسنة-2026-الخاص-بالسلامة-والصحة-المهنية-وزارة-العمل-لن-ي-ضار-أي-عامل-يعمل-بمهنة-أخصائي-السلامة-والصحة-المهنية-ولا-مساس-بالمراكز-القانونية-المكتسبة-وأهمية-المهنة-تستوجب-منتهى-الدقة-في-اختيار-شاغليها/

## Ministry's express clarifications

The Ministry says:
- Applying Decision 249 is not intended to harm existing OSH workers, exclude experienced practitioners, or disregard previously recognized academic qualifications.
- Existing acquired legal/professional positions are to be preserved.
- The decision aims to regulate the profession, raise qualification/specialization standards and improve workforce capacity.
- Relevant qualifications include medicine, veterinary medicine, pharmacy, engineering, science, environment and waste management, and comparable fields.
- Other university graduates may be considered where they have completed specialist postgraduate OSH study, accumulated extended practical experience, and obtained recognized certificates authorizing work in the field, subject to the rules for each professional level.
- Professional classifications include “OSH Specialist A” and “OSH Specialist B”; different classifications are intended to align scientific specialization and skill with establishment activity/risk, not to negate experience.
- The Ministry's central OSH administration was instructed to issue circulars to unify interpretation and clarify implementation/regularization procedures.

## Critical caution

A third-party explanatory source reports a threshold of 50 or more workers for some OSH-unit obligations. The Ministry clarification above does not itself establish that threshold or the complete risk-tier matrix. Do not encode thresholds, job classifications, qualifications or grandfathering rules from secondary summaries alone. Obtain the signed Decision 249/2026 and the promised Ministry circulars, then verify each category and transitional rule.

## Product/data implications

- Store Decision 249/2026 and the 27 October clarification as separate linked instruments (implements / official clarification of).
- Preserve effective dates and the distinction between new qualifications and protected pre-existing qualifications.
- Do not automatically mark an existing OSH professional non-compliant based only on new qualification requirements.
- Model professional categories and risk tiers only after full-text verification.
- Require Egyptian OSH/legal reviewer approval before converting the clarification into compliance rules.

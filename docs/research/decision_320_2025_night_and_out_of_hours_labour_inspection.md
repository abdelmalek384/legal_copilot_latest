## Article-by-article extraction

### Article 1 — definitions

- **Night inspection:** inspection carried out between sunset and sunrise.
- **Inspection outside official working hours:** inspection assigned by the competent administrative authority outside the official inspection working hours for the relevant inspectors.

### Article 2 — when night/out-of-hours inspection may be assigned

Without prejudice to inspectors' right to enter establishments freely at any time, day or night, without prior permission or notice, the competent administrative authority may assign Labour and OSH inspectors to night/out-of-hours inspection in the following cases:
1. Establishments operating three shifts.
2. Establishments whose nature requires night work.
3. Establishments employing children/adolescents or women.
4. Inspection of rest periods, night closing times, weekly closing and weekly rest.
5. Establishments carrying out seasonal work/industries.
6. Where there is imminent danger to workers' health or safety requiring an inspector to attend at night/outside official hours.
7. Inspection of meals served at night.
8. Where an inspection begun during working hours continues beyond official working hours.
9. Other night/out-of-hours inspections assigned to the relevant inspection employees.

### Article 3 — inspection reports

Inspectors assigned to night/out-of-hours work must prepare reports on the inspection results for presentation to their superiors on the following day, within pre-prepared routes/itineraries for the assigned inspection tasks.

### Article 4 — employer cooperation

Employers or their representatives must facilitate the work of assigned inspectors, enable them to carry out their duties, and provide the documents and data needed for inspection and monitoring compliance with the Law and implementing decisions.

### Article 5 — statistical reporting

The competent Labour Inspection and OSH inspection departments, Labour Directorates, zones and offices must prepare monthly, semiannual and annual statistics concerning night/out-of-hours inspections.

### Article 6 — financial allowances

Inspectors assigned to night/out-of-hours inspection receive financial allowances. The competent authority issues a separate decision determining the allowance amounts and payment controls.

**Open follow-up:** the separate allowance-setting decision has not been identified in the current harvest. Decision 320 itself does not specify the amounts. Do not invent or encode a payment value.

### Article 7 — publication and commencement

Published in Al-Waqa'i al-Misriyya; effective from the day after publication.

## Relationship to Labour Law 14/2025

This decision implements the final paragraph of **Article 276**, which directs the Minister responsible for labour to set rules/procedures for assigning night and out-of-hours inspection and the allowances due to inspectors.

It does not remove inspectors' general entry powers or make night inspection conditional on an employer's consent. Article 2 expressly preserves free entry at any time without prior permission/notice.

## Compliance implications

- Employers must maintain readiness for lawful inspection outside normal hours; no prior notice is required for entry under the Law/Decision.
- Three-shift and inherently night-operating establishments are express triggers, but the list is not limited to them.
- Employers should preserve relevant shift/rest, closure, meal and attendance records and cooperate with inspection.
- The decision creates reporting/statistical duties for inspection authorities; it does not itself prescribe a universal employer night-inspection fee.
- The allowance amount is delegated to a separate authority decision and remains unresolved.

## Decision 294/2025 — fully paid holidays

A PDF served from the Egyptian State Information Service's media repository is available:
https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/117216/MOM20251226_0805.pdf?sequence=4

The indexed text includes the statutory holiday list, the rule that listed holidays are fully paid and not deducted from annual leave, compensation or substitute leave when a worker is required to work, additional religious leave for non-Muslim workers, and a final provision stating commencement on the day after publication.

A secondary full-text page identifies the decision date as 4 December 2025 and publication in Gazette supplement issue 291 on 25 December 2025, with commencement reported as 26 December 2025:
https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/labor/25-12-2025/no-294?id=5008509&type=1

**What this resolves:** there is now a public government-hosted PDF lead in addition to Gazette metadata and secondary transcription.

**Next action:** inspect the PDF's title page and all pages to confirm it is the complete Decision 294/2025 text, compare against Gazette supplement 291, record exact page pinpoints and hash the retained file. Do not assume the government-hosted PDF is itself the Gazette original without verifying its document metadata.

## Global controls

- A hosted PDF or a source's indexed text is not proof of present-day legal force.
- A public download does not establish a commercial reuse licence.
- Do not record a SHA-256 unless calculated from the exact bytes actually downloaded and retained.
- Record the retrieval date, host, publisher/issuing authority, and artifact type separately.
- Keep every affected card at `search_eligible: false` until primary-text comparison, currentness, reuse and qualified review gates are satisfied.

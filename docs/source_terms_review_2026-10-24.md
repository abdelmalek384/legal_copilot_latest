# Source Terms Review — 2026-10-24

## Result

This review is a terms-evidence update, not a grant of permission. The register deliberately keeps collection and commercial reuse permissions unknown for WIPO Lex and ILO NATLEX legal-text records because each service may point to third-party national legal material.

## Reviewed published policies

### WIPO / WIPO Lex

- Terms page: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/terms-of-use/index
- Reviewed: 2026-10-24
- The WIPO terms page says WIPO-owned online publications and content are generally issued under CC BY 4.0 unless more restrictive terms apply. It expressly says the open-access policy does not apply to third-party content, for which rights must be cleared with the right holder.
- Decision: keep collection and commercial reuse as unknown for WIPO Lex legal-text records. A general WIPO content policy does not establish rights in every national law, judgment, image, or third-party record exposed by WIPO Lex. Confirm service-specific terms and item-level rights before storing, extracting, indexing, or displaying full text.
- Do not use the WIPO emblem or imply WIPO endorsement without separate written permission.

### ILO / NATLEX

- Rights and permissions: https://www.ilo.org/rights-and-permissions
- Reviewed: 2026-10-24
- The ILO policy says ILO publications published on or after 3 May 2023 are generally CC BY 4.0 unless a work indicates otherwise; older publications do not automatically benefit. ILO databases/datasets and accompanying referential metadata published on or after that date are also generally CC BY 4.0. Third-party components must be cleared separately.
- Decision: keep collection and commercial reuse as unknown for NATLEX-linked national legal-text copies. The policy may be relevant to ILO-owned database metadata, but it does not prove that the ILO owns or licenses the underlying Egyptian legal text. Check the exact record and service-specific terms before reuse.

## Egyptian primary-source portals

The following are still unresolved in this review: Amiri Press, Presidency, Ministry of Justice, Government Services Authority, Egyptian Tax Authority, and Ministry of Finance. Public access or a located official page is not evidence of permission for automated collection, OCR, storage, indexing, commercial excerpt display, or redistribution. The Amiri Press portal timed out in this review and the Presidency page returned access denied to the research client; neither outcome is evidence of a legal restriction or a licence.

**Decision for all unresolved Egyptian portals:** retain unknown permissions, do not scrape or download, and seek written authorization or a documented official API/export licence for the intended uses.

## Court of Cassation

The existing register records the published terms at https://cc.gov.eg/terms and keeps automated/bulk collection blocked and commercial reuse subject to prior written permission. This review does not change that state.

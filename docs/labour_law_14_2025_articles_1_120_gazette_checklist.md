## Completion definitions

For each article, record:
1. **Text match:** compare the complete article against the exact Gazette text, including headings, paragraphs, numbering, punctuation, amounts, dates, exceptions and cross-references. Record differences verbatim in a separate discrepancy log.
2. **Visual check:** inspect the rendered Gazette page image for OCR omissions, broken characters, columns, tables, footnotes and page continuity.
3. **Pinpoint:** record printed Gazette page and zero-based PDF page index from the actual page, not from inferred page sequences.
4. **Currentness:** identify later amendments, repeal/supersession, implementing decisions and transitional provisions that affect the article; distinguish an implementing instrument from an amendment.
5. **Counsel:** record qualified Egyptian-lawyer review separately. Do not set this field from automated tests or source matching.
